 # A bound on 2^(s0U k) in terms of k²

The lemma `two_pow_s0U_le`: for k ≥ 1, 2^(s0U k) ≤ 4k², so 2^s < 4k² for every s < s0U k. 

### `two_pow_s0U_le`

 2^(s0U k) ≤ 4k².
