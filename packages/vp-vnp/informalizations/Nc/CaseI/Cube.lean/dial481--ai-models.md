# The points u/2, u ∈ {-1,0,1}^k, of the cube


### `bsign`

 The sign 1 or -1 of a bit. 

### `half`

 The place of u in {-1,0,1}^k: the point u/2 of ℝ^k. 

### `dist_half_neg`

 Opposite vertices of the cube have the distance √k.
