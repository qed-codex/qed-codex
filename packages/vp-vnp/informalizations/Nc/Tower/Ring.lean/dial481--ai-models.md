# The rings F₂[s]/(s^m)


### `R`

 The ring F₂[s]/(s^m). 

### `mkR`

 The class of a polynomial. 

### `liftR`

 Homomorphisms from the ring. 

### `cst`

 The constants. 

### `s`

 The nilpotent element. 

### `red`

 Reduction modulo s^m. 

### `exists_of_red_eq_zero`

 The kernel of the reduction. 

### `ev`

 The value at s = 0. 

### `eq_cst_ev`

 In F₂[s]/(s) every element is a constant. 

### `t`

 The unit t = 1 + s.
