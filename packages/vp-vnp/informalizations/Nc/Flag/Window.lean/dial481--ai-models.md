# Windows of the unitriangular group acting on a complete CAT(0) space


### `win_win`

 A smaller window. 

### `win_comm`

 Disjoint windows commute. 

### `wh`

 The homomorphism of a window (trivial if it doesn't fit). 

### `W`

 The action of a window. 

### `e`

 The displacement by a generator.
