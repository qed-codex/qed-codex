# Binary numbers, the first bit is the highest


### `bv`

 The value of a word of bits. 

### `bin`

 The word of L bits of a number. 

### `bv_succ`

 The successor of a word. 

### `exists_cut`

 A word that has a successor ends with 0 1 ⋯ 1.
