## Generation of a special unitary group by elementary matrices

[Theorem ALG.0495](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.SA.locGen/)

Let $k$ be a field with $2\ne0$, let $K$ be a reduced commutative $k$-algebra of dimension two, and let $\sigma:K\to K$ be a $k$-algebra involution whose fixed ring is $k$. Let $J\in M_N(K)$ satisfy $J^t=\sigma(J)$ and $\det J\in K^\times$. Write $$h(x,y)=x^tJ\sigma(y),\qquad \operatorname{SU}(J)=\{g:\det g=1,\ g^tJ\sigma(g)=J\}.$$ Suppose $e,f\in K^N$ satisfy $h(e,e)=h(f,f)=0$ and $h(e,f)=1$. For $u\in K^N$, $c\in K$ with $$h(u,e)=h(u,f)=0,\qquad c+\sigma(c)+h(u,u)=0,$$ put $$E_e(u,c)=I+u(J\sigma(e))^t-e(J\sigma(u))^t+c\,e(J\sigma(e))^t,$$ and define $E_f(u,c)$ by exchanging $e,f$. Then $\operatorname{SU}(J)$ is exactly the multiplicative monoid generated by all these matrices. The generation uses finite products.
