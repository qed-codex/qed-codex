## Holomorphic functions are locally analytic

[Theorem AG.1427](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.osgood/)

Let $e\ge0$, let $U\subset\mathbb C^e$ be open, and let $f:\mathbb C^e\to\mathbb C$ be complex differentiable on $U$. Then $f$ is analytic in a neighborhood of each point of $U$.
