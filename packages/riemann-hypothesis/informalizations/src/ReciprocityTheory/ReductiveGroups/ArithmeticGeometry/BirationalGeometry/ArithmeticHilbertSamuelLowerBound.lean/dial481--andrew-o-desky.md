## Arithmetic intersection systems in homogeneous coordinates

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D006](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.IsIntersectionSystem/)

**Coordinates and metrics.** Let $B$ be a finite set of blocks, each with a finite coordinate set $I_b$, and put $J=\coprod_{b\in B}I_b$ and $R=\mathbb Z[X_{b,i}]$. A polynomial is multihomogeneous of degree $a\in\mathbb N^B$ if every monomial in its support has degree $a_b$ in block $b$. An ideal is multihomogeneous if it is generated by such polynomials, with their degrees allowed to vary.

An ideal is *relevant* if no entire coordinate block belongs to it, and *horizontal* if its intersection with $\mathbb Z$ is zero. An *arithmetic variety of dimension $n$ in these coordinates* is a relevant horizontal multihomogeneous prime $P\subset R$, with $B\ne\varnothing$ and $$\dim(R/P)=n+|B|.$$ For a coordinate tuple $y\in\Omega^J$ in a field $\Omega$, its homogeneous coordinate ideal is $$P_y=\ker\bigl(R\longrightarrow\Omega[T_b:b\in B],\quad X_{b,i}\longmapsto y_{b,i}T_b\bigr).$$ The complex cone of an ideal $P$ is $$\mathcal C_P=\{z\in\mathbb C^J:\text{each block of }z\text{ is nonzero and }f(z)=0\text{ for every }f\in P\}.$$

A *Hermitian line datum* $A=(a,h)$ consists of $a\in\mathbb Z^B$ and a function $h:\mathbb C^J\to\mathbb R$ positive whenever every block is nonzero, satisfying $$h((c_bz_{b,i})_{b,i})=\prod_b|c_b|^{a_b}h(z)\qquad(c_b\in\mathbb C^\times).$$ It is smooth if $h$ is real $C^\infty$ on the nonzero-block cone. Tensor product adds multidegrees and multiplies metric functions. For $s\in R$, put $\log\|s\|_A(z)=\log(|s(z)|/h(z))$ on that cone, with $\log0=0$.

**Divisors and geometric degrees.** For an ideal $V$ and a polynomial $s$, let $\mathcal D(V,s)$ be the relevant minimal primes over $V+(s)$. For $W\in\mathcal D(V,s)$, put $$m(V,s;W)=\operatorname{length}_{R_W}\bigl((R/(V+(s)))_W\bigr).$$ An infinite length is assigned the value zero. For every relevant ideal $V$, fix a coordinate $i_b(V)$ in each block with $X_{b,i_b(V)}\notin V$; this choice depends only on the coordinate system and on $V$. Define integers $d_n(V;a_1,\ldots,a_n)$ recursively by $$d_0(V)=\operatorname{length}_{\mathbb Z}\!\left(R/(V+(X_{b,i_b(V)}-1:b\in B))\right),$$ $$d_n(V;a_1,\ldots,a_n)=\sum_b(a_n)_b \sum_{W\in\mathcal D(V,X_{b,i_b(V)})} m(V,X_{b,i_b(V)};W)d_{n-1}(W;a_1,\ldots,a_{n-1})\quad(n\ge1).$$ The same infinite-length convention applies in $d_0$, and $d_n=0$ when $V$ is not relevant. For a nonhorizontal prime $W$, write $p_W$ for the positive generator of $W\cap\mathbb Z$.

**The logarithmic intersection integral.** The following defines a real number $\mathcal I_P(L_1,\ldots,L_e;g)$ for smooth line data $L_j$ with metric functions $h_j$ and a real function $g$ on the cone. Write $\operatorname{Seg}(z)=(\prod_bz_{b,i_b})_{(i_b)\in\prod_bI_b}$ for the Segre coordinates. If there is a complex-linear map $\mathbb C^{\prod_bI_b}\to\mathbb C^{e+1}$ that is nonzero at every $\operatorname{Seg}(z)$ with $z\in\mathcal C_P$, fix one such map $\Phi$; this choice depends only on the coordinate system, on $P$ and on $e$.

For $y\in\mathbb C^e$, consider all $w=\operatorname{Seg}(z)$ with $z\in\mathcal C_P$ and $\Phi w=(1,y)$. A local sheet above $(y,w)$ is a holomorphic map $\gamma$ from a neighborhood of $y$ into $\mathcal C_P$, with $\operatorname{Seg}(\gamma(y))=w$ and $\Phi\operatorname{Seg}(\gamma(t))=(1,t)$ near $y$. For each pair $(y,w)$ above which a local sheet exists, fix one. Its contribution is $$g(\gamma(y))\,e!\left(\frac2\pi\right)^e \operatorname{Re}\operatorname{MD}\bigl(H_1(y),\ldots,H_e(y)\bigr),\qquad (H_j)_{uv}=\frac{\partial^2(\log h_j\circ\gamma)}{\partial z_u\partial\bar z_v},$$ where the mixed discriminant is $$\operatorname{MD}(A_1,\ldots,A_e)=\frac1{e!} \sum_{S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,e\}}(-1)^{e-|S|}\det\left(\sum_{j\in S}A_j\right).$$ A pair admitting no local sheet contributes zero. Sum these contributions over $w$ and integrate over $y\in\mathbb C^e$ with Lebesgue measure. This is $\mathcal I_P(L_1,\ldots,L_e;g)$. If no map $\Phi$ as above exists, its value is zero. A sum with infinitely many nonzero terms and a nonintegrable Lebesgue integral are assigned the value zero. For $e=0$, determinants and mixed discriminants of empty matrices are $1$.

**Basic intersection systems.** An intersection system assigns a real number $I_P(L_1,\ldots,L_n)$ to every finite coordinate system, every ideal $P$, every $n\ge0$ and every ordered list of $n$ Hermitian line data. It is *basic* if the following hold for all coordinate systems.

1. If $P$ is an arithmetic variety of dimension $n$ and the line data are smooth, $I_P$ is unchanged by permuting the list.
2. For an arithmetic variety $P$ of dimension $n+1$ and smooth $L_1,\ldots,L_n,A,A'$, one has $$I_P(L_1,\ldots,L_n,A\otimes A')=I_P(L_1,\ldots,L_n,A)+I_P(L_1,\ldots,L_n,A').$$
3. If $P$ has dimension $n+1$, all line data are smooth, and $s\notin P$ is multihomogeneous of the nonnegative multidegree of $A$, then $$I_P(L_1,\ldots,L_n,A)= \sum_{W\in\mathcal D(P,s)}m(P,s;W) \begin{cases} I_W(L_1,\ldots,L_n),&W\text{ horizontal},\\ d_n(W;a_1,\ldots,a_n)\log p_W,&W\text{ nonhorizontal} \end{cases} -\mathcal I_P(L_1,\ldots,L_n;\log\|s\|_A),$$ where $a_j$ is the multidegree of $L_j$.

**Coordinate projections and full systems.** Between block systems $(B,I)$ and $(B',I')$, a block map consists of injections $\kappa:B\to B'$ and $v:J\to J'$ carrying block $b$ bijectively onto the entire block $\kappa(b)$. It induces $R\to R'$ by $X_j\mapsto X'_{v(j)}$. The image ideal of $P'\subset R'$ is its inverse image $P\subset R$. Pullback of $(a,h)$ has degree $a_b$ on block $\kappa(b)$, degree zero on other blocks, and metric $h'(z')=h(z'\circ v)$.

A basic system is *full* if it also satisfies the following three laws.

- **Multilinearity.** Replacing any one factor by a tensor product adds the two corresponding intersections, for every arithmetic variety of the matching dimension and every smooth choice of factors.
- **Projection formula.** For a tuple $y'\in\Omega^{J'}$ in a characteristic-zero field, suppose $P'=P_{y'}$ and its image ideal $P=P_{y'\circ v}$ are arithmetic varieties of dimension $d$. For smooth $L_1,\ldots,L_d$ and their pullbacks $L'_j$, $$I_{P'}(L'_1,\ldots,L'_d)=[F(y'):F(y'\circ v)]I_P(L_1,\ldots,L_d).$$ Here $F(y)$ is the subfield generated over $\mathbb Q$ by all same-block coordinate ratios with nonzero denominator. The displayed degree is the vector-space dimension when finite, and zero otherwise.
- **Contraction.** Suppose $P'$ is an arithmetic variety of dimension $d+r$, its image ideal is an arithmetic variety of dimension $d_0<d$, and $L'_1,\ldots,L'_d$ are pullbacks of smooth line data on the image. For arbitrary smooth line data $N_1,\ldots,N_r$ on the source, $$I_{P'}(L'_1,\ldots,L'_d,N_1,\ldots,N_r)=0.$$

The *full intersection-system existence hypothesis* asserts that a full system exists.

## Small sections and positivity in homogeneous coordinates

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D017](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Birational/)

Use the finite block set $B$, coordinate sets $I_b$, ring $R=\mathbb Z[X_{b,i}]$, complex cones $\mathcal C_P$, Hermitian line data $A=(a,h)$ and integers $d_n$ of ([Arithmetic intersection systems in homogeneous coordinates](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.IsIntersectionSystem/)).

**Positivity.** For ideals $P,P_0\subseteq R$, a vector $w\in\mathbb C^{\coprod_b I_b}$ is tangent to its cone at $z$ if $$\sum_{b,i}\frac{\partial f}{\partial X_{b,i}}(z)w_{b,i}=0\qquad(f\in P_0).$$ The datum $A$ is *semipositive relative to $P_0$ on $P$* if $h$ is smooth on the entire nonzero-block cone and, for every $z\in\mathcal C_P$ and every such tangent vector $w$, $$\frac14\left(D^2\log h(z)[w,w]+D^2\log h(z)[iw,iw]\right)\ge0,$$ where $D^2$ is the real second derivative. It is *invariant under conjugation* if $h(\bar z)=h(z)$ for every ambient tuple $z$.

The datum $A$ and its multidegree $a$ are called *nef on the fibres of $P$* if $d_1(W;a)\ge0$ for every relevant multihomogeneous prime $W\supseteq P$ in positive characteristic with $\dim(R/W)=1+|B|$.

**Chart lattices.** Let $y\in\Omega^{\coprod_b I_b}$ for a field $\Omega$, and put $P=P_y$. The coordinates are *birational* if every element of $\Omega$ is $g(y)/f(y)$ for integral multihomogeneous polynomials $g,f$ of the same multidegree, with $f(y)\ne0$.

For each choice $c=(c_b\in I_b)$ with all $y_{b,c_b}\ne0$, define $$R_c=\mathbb Z[y_{b,i}/y_{b,c_b}]\subseteq\Omega,$$ and let $R_c^\nu$ be its integral closure in $\Omega$. For $n\in\mathbb N$, put $$\Lambda_n(y,a)=\left\{s\in\Omega:s\prod_b y_{b,c_b}^{-na_b}\in R_c\text{ for every such }c\right\},$$ $$\Lambda_n^\nu(y,a)=\left\{s\in\Omega:s\prod_b y_{b,c_b}^{-na_b}\in R_c^\nu\text{ for every such }c\right\}.$$

**Smallness and counts.** An element $s\in\Omega$ is *small in degree $n$ for $A$* if, for every $d_g,d_f\in\mathbb N^B$ and every pair of integral polynomials $g,f$ multihomogeneous of degrees $d_g,d_f$ with $d_g=d_f+na$ and $g(y)=s f(y)$, one has $$|g(z)|\le |f(z)|h(z)^n\qquad(z\in\mathcal C_P).$$ These tests include pairs with vanishing evaluations of $f$. Let $\mathcal B_n(y,A)$ and $\mathcal B_n^\nu(y,A)$ be the small elements in $\Lambda_n(y,a)$ and $\Lambda_n^\nu(y,a)$, respectively. Define $$\widehat h_n(y,A)=\log\operatorname{card}_0\mathcal B_n(y,A),\qquad \widehat h_n^\nu(y,A)=\log\operatorname{card}_0\mathcal B_n^\nu(y,A),$$ where $\operatorname{card}_0$ is ordinary cardinality for finite sets and zero for infinite sets, and $\log0=0$.

For an integer $D\ge0$, define the real numbers $$V_D(y,A)=\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{\widehat h_n(y,A)}{n^{D+1}/(D+1)!},\qquad V_D^\nu(y,A)=\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{\widehat h_n^\nu(y,A)}{n^{D+1}/(D+1)!}.$$ Real division by zero is zero. Here the real limsup of a sequence $t_n$ means $$\inf\{b\in\mathbb R:\text{there is }N\text{ such that }t_n\le b\text{ for all }n\ge N\},$$ with value zero if this set is empty or unbounded below.

For a nonnegative multidegree $a\in\mathbb N^B$, the *monomial bound* means $$\left|\prod_{b,i}z_{b,i}^{\mu_{b,i}}\right|\le h(z)$$ for every $z\in\mathcal C_P$ and every array $\mu_{b,i}\in\mathbb N$ with $\sum_i\mu_{b,i}=a_b$ for every block $b$.
