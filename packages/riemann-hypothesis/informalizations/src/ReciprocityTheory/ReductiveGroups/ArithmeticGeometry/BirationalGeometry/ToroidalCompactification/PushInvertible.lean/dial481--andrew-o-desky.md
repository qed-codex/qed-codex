## Unitary toroidal models and boundary charts

[Definition / hypothesis AG.D102](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.MumfordChart.HasChart/)

**Moduli data.** Use the CM-host and polarized-lattice definitions in the conventions of ([An indefinite polarized CM lattice splits by signature](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.hermitianLatticeSignatureSplit_of_indefinite/)). Write $E$ for the CM field, $(p,q)$ for the signature, and $r,w\in\mathcal O_E$ and $r_0\in\mathbb C$ for the chosen elements with $r^2=r_0^2=-d_0$ and $2w=1+r$. Put $\Psi=\{\tau:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C:\tau(r)=r_0\}$.

A level consists of an odd integer $M\ge3$, an integer $A\ge1$ and an ideal $\mathfrak b\subset\mathcal O_E$, generated by its elements in $\mathbb Z[w]$, such that $$\mathfrak b\overline{\mathfrak b}=(M),\qquad \mathfrak b+\overline{\mathfrak b}=\mathcal O_E.$$ Put $M'=\operatorname{lcm}(M,A)$ and $K_0=\mathbb Q(r_0,e^{2\pi i/A})\subset\mathbb C$. The moduli scheme is a smooth quasiprojective $K_0$-scheme of dimension $[E:\mathbb Q]pq/2$. Use the moduli objects, both marked level maps, their determinant condition and their torsion compatibility conditions in ([Polarized modules and unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)). It represents these objects over reduced schemes and satisfies the infinitesimal uniqueness in ([Polarized modules and unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)). The number field $K\subset\mathbb C$ contains $K_0$, every image of $E$ in $\mathbb C$, and $e^{2\pi i/M'}$; $X$ is the base change to $K$.

**Models and the determinant Hodge presentation.** For the coefficient-field base change $X/K$ of this moduli scheme, a model is a normal projective flat $K$-scheme $Y$ with a dense open immersion $j:X\hookrightarrow Y$ and a semiabelian family $G/Y$ extending the universal family. The extension and its identification over $X$ preserve the $\mathcal O_E$-action, symmetric rigidified cubical polarization, conjugate-adjointness and compatibility with $w$ of the universal family. The abelian locus of $G$ is exactly $j(X)$. Write $D=Y\setminus j(X)$ with its reduced closed-subscheme structure. An effective Cartier divisor $B$ on $Y$ has ideal $\mathcal I_B$ and associated line bundle $\mathcal O_Y(B)$.

The determinant Hodge presentation $\lambda$ is defined as follows. Use all affine integral opens of $Y$ carrying a section of the polarization line that is nonzero somewhere on every fibre of $G$, and carrying a finite tangent basis and $\mathcal O_E$-action matrices compatible with arbitrary base change. On an overlap, if the second tangent basis is obtained from the first by a matrix $C$, the transition unit for $\lambda$ is $(\det C)^{-1}$. The condition that $\lambda$ is a line bundle requires these opens to cover $Y$; the transitions then define the dual of the determinant tangent bundle. The twist used with $B$ and an integer $k\ge0$ is $$\lambda^{\otimes k}\otimes\mathcal O_Y(-B).$$

**Boundary charts.** Boundary chart data at a point $x\in D$ consist of a field $k_x$, integers $m,e\ge0$, and $$R=k_x[[t_1,\ldots,t_m,z_1,\ldots,z_e]],\qquad \xi:\operatorname{Spec}R\longrightarrow Y$$ taking the closed point to $x$. The stalk map $u:\mathcal O_{Y,x}\to R$ is flat, satisfies $u(\mathfrak m_x)R=\mathfrak m_R$, and induces a finite residue-field extension. The boundary ideal maps to $(\prod_i t_i)$, and a prime maps to $D$ exactly when it contains this product. There is a semiabelian pullback of $G$ with a positive convergent polarized degeneration datum and a polarized uniformization, in the senses defined in ([Siegel compactification with polarized boundary charts](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.SiegJoin.siegelToroidalPolCharts/)). Write $C/R$ for its abelian part, $L$ for its polarization line, $X_0$ for its character group, $c:X_0\to C(R)$ for its additive map, and $\psi(y)\in L_{c(y)}^{-1}$ for its quadratic period section. The group $X_0$ carries an $\mathcal O_E$-module structure, and $C$ and the generic abelian scheme carry $\mathcal O_E$-actions with conjugation as Rosati adjoint. Require $c(ay)=[a]c(y)$ and balanced periods: under every normalized Rosati identification $u_a$ of the two polarization biextensions, $$\tau(ay,z)u_a(c(y),c(z))=\tau(y,\bar a z).$$ The generic-fibre identification and the formal quotient maps are $\mathcal O_E$-linear; on the torus, $[a]$ acts on characters by $y\mapsto\bar a y$. The uniformization line equals the polarization line of the pulled-back semiabelian family. With $w=(1+r)/2$, require a normalized trivialization $v$ on $C$ of $$L_{x+[w]x}\otimes L_x^{-2}\otimes L_{[w]x}^{-1}\otimes L_0^2$$ such that, for every $y\in X_0$, $\tau(y,wy)v(c(y))=\psi(y)\eta$, where $\eta$ is the rigidification at zero. There are symmetric balanced integral forms $\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_m$ on $X_0$ whose values are divisible by $M'$. For every $y\in X_0$, the quadratic period section $\psi(y)$ has coordinate, relative to a line-bundle trivialization over $R$, $$u_y\prod_i t_i^{n_i(y)},\qquad u_y\in R^\times,\quad n_i(y)\in\mathbb N,\quad 2n_i(y)=\gamma_i(y,y).$$

An ordinary boundary chart requires these data and that $\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_m$ extend to a $\mathbb Z$-basis of the group of symmetric balanced integral forms on $X_0$ divisible by $M'$.

A polarized boundary chart for $(\phi,B,d)$ requires the boundary chart data, the rank bound $\operatorname{rk}_{\mathcal O_E}X_0\le\min(p,q)$, and a cone $\delta_1,\ldots,\delta_m$ for $\phi$ in ([Polarizing fan conventions for toroidal compactifications](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.LocM.InvFun/)), with $$\gamma_i=M'\delta_i.$$ There must be integers $a_i\ge0$ satisfying $$a_i=d\phi(\gamma_i),\qquad \mathcal I_B R=\left(\prod_i t_i^{a_i}\right).$$

## Polarized modules and unitary moduli

[Definition / hypothesis AG.D144](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)

Use a CM field $E$ and the arithmetic notation of ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)). Independently choose a prime $d_{\rm mod}\equiv7\pmod{16}$, elements $\rho,w\in\mathcal O_E$ and $\rho_\infty\in\mathbb C$ with $\rho^2=-d_{\rm mod}$, $2w=1+\rho$ and $\rho_\infty^2=-d_{\rm mod}$. Let $\Psi=\{\tau:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C:\tau(\rho)=\rho_\infty\}$ and let a bar denote CM conjugation, including its restriction to $\mathcal O_E$. Assume $\mathcal O_E/\mathcal O_{E^+}$ is unramified. An integral polarized module $\Lambda$ and its signature $(p_{\rm sig},q_{\rm sig})$ are required at each natural stage of the moduli family.

Hypothesis (integral data and moduli). At each stage the integral module is finite projective over $\mathcal O_E$ and has a signature, with the following conditions imposed using that stage's module and signature. At the selected stage, its alternating form $E_\Lambda:\Lambda\times\Lambda\to\mathbb Z$ induces a bijection $\Lambda\to\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(\Lambda,\mathbb Z)$ and satisfies $$E_\Lambda(ax,y)=E_\Lambda(x,\bar a y).$$ Its $\mathcal O_E$-rank is $p_{\rm sig}+q_{\rm sig}$. On $\Lambda_\mathbb R=\mathbb R\otimes_\mathbb Z\Lambda$ there is a real-linear $J$ with $J^2=-1$, $E_\Lambda(Jx,Jy)=E_\Lambda(x,y)$ and $E_\Lambda(Jx,x)>0$ for $x\ne0$. For the action $A_a$ of $a\in\mathcal O_E$ on $\Lambda_\mathbb R$, put $$T(a)=\sum_{\tau\in\Psi}\{p_{\rm sig}\tau(a)+q_{\rm sig}\bar\tau(a)\}.$$ Require $JA_a=A_aJ$, $\operatorname{tr}_{\mathbb R}A_a=2\Re T(a)$ and $\operatorname{tr}_{\mathbb R}(JA_a)=-2\Im T(a)$. These conditions define every integral polarized module of a specified signature used here.

The moduli problem parametrizes proper commutative semiabelian families with $\mathcal O_E$-action and a relatively ample rigidified symmetric cubical line bundle $\mathscr P$. The polarization has trivial translation kernel: if $t_x^*\mathscr P\otimes\mathscr P^{-1}$ is locally trivial over the base, then $x=0$. Write $\mathscr B(x,y)=\mathscr P_{x+y}\otimes\mathscr P_x^{-1}\otimes\mathscr P_y^{-1}\otimes\mathscr P_0$ for the bilinear bundle associated with $\mathscr P$. Require $\mathscr B(ax,y)\simeq\mathscr B(x,\bar a y)$ and $\mathscr P_x\simeq\mathscr B(x,wx)$. At every algebraically closed point the tangent multiplicity is $p_{\rm sig}$ at embeddings in $\Psi$ and $q_{\rm sig}$ at the others. There are compatible $\mathcal O_E$-linear identifications $\Lambda/n\Lambda\simeq A_y[n]$ for all positive integers $n$, where $A_y$ is the abelian variety at the point, with compatible primitive roots of unity, under which the Weil pairing is the root raised to $E_\Lambda(a,b)$. The root at the auxiliary level $A$ is the chosen $\zeta_A=\exp(2\pi\mathrm i/A)$. The two additive level maps agree with these identifications at levels $M$ and $A$; the first agreement is required on $\bar{\mathfrak b}\Lambda$ and its kernel contains $\mathfrak b\Lambda$, while the auxiliary map kills $A\Lambda$.

The moduli schemes are smooth and quasi-projective of dimension $\lfloor[E:\mathbb Q]/2\rfloor p_{\rm sig}q_{\rm sig}$ over $\mathbb Q(\rho_\infty,\zeta_A)$, where $\zeta_n=\exp(2\pi\mathrm i/n)$. They carry universal objects, and every such object over a reduced scheme is induced by a unique map to the moduli scheme. If an object over the dual numbers is induced both by a point and by its constant reduction, that point is constant.

## Coefficient fields and deeper moduli levels

[Definition / hypothesis AG.D143](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Level/)

Use the polarized modules, moduli schemes and notation in ([Polarized modules and unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)). The original level is a triple $(M,\mathfrak b,A)$. Let $K_0\subset\mathbb C$ be the original coefficient number field, containing every image of $E$, and identify the coefficient ring of the automorphic forms with $\mathcal O_{K_0}$. For a fixed stage choose a finite extension $K_0\subseteq K\subset\mathbb C$ and a moduli scheme $X/K$ at a deeper level, together with its map to the original moduli scheme and a Cartesian map of universal semiabelian families. Write $e_{\rm lev}\ge0$ for the level exponent and $o\in\mathbb R$ for the field-size bound.

Hypothesis (level and coefficient fields). Let the deeper level be $(M_2,\mathfrak b_2,A_2)$. The level multiplier is $15^{e_{\rm lev}}$, $$M_2=M15^{e_{\rm lev}},\quad (15^{e_{\rm lev}},M)=1,\quad \mathfrak b_2=\mathfrak b I,\quad I+\bar I=\mathcal O_E,\quad A_2=1,$$ and level divisibility means $M\mid M_2$, $\mathfrak b_2\subseteq\mathfrak b$ and $A\mid A_2$. At the original level require $M$ odd and at least three, $A>0$, $\mathfrak b\bar{\mathfrak b}=(M)$, $\mathfrak b+\bar{\mathfrak b}=\mathcal O_E$, and $\mathfrak b$ generated by its elements in $\mathbb Z[w]$; impose the same conditions on the deeper triple. The field $K_0$ contains $\rho_\infty$ and the chosen primitive $\operatorname{lcm}(M,A)$th root $\zeta_{\operatorname{lcm}(M,A)}$; $K$ contains $\rho_\infty$, every image of $E$ and $\zeta_{M_2}$. The map of universal semiabelian families is Cartesian and preserves the $\mathcal O_E$-action and cubical polarization data. It carries the $15^{e_{\rm lev}}$th powers of the deeper $M_2$-level sections to the original $M$-level sections, and carries the $(A_2/A)$th powers of the auxiliary sections to the original ones. Every framed point over an algebraically closed compatible coefficient extension lifts through this map, preserving its underlying point, tangent frame, polarization line bundle, rigidification and generating section. The chosen weight-zero scalar section of the deeper model is regular and nowhere zero on these framed points. Every automorphism of $\mathbb C$ fixing $K_0$ maps $K$ into itself, and $$\log[K:\mathbb Q]+\frac{\log|\operatorname{disc}K|}{[K:\mathbb Q]}\le o.$$ For each irreducible component $Z$, put $m_\tau=p_{\rm sig}$ for $\tau\in\Psi$ and $m_\tau=q_{\rm sig}$ otherwise. Require that $K$ be algebraically closed in $$K(Z)(t_{\tau,i,j}:\tau:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C,\ 0\le i<j<m_\tau),$$ where $K(Z)$ is the function field of $Z$ and the $t_{\tau,i,j}$ are independent variables.

A framed point means a moduli point, a basis of each tangent eigenspace and a generator of the polarization's section module. Over a commutative coefficient algebra $R$, a triangular change fixes the moduli point and replaces its tangent bases, for every embedding $\tau$ and $0\le i<m_\tau$, by $$u'_{\tau,i}=\sum_{j=0}^i g_{\tau,ij}u_{\tau,j},\qquad g_{\tau,ij}\in R,\quad g_{\tau,ii}\in R^\times.$$ Using the induced identification of the polarization line bundles, it replaces the generating section $s$ by $a s$ for a unit $a\in R^\times$.

The auxiliary theta data include the following arbitrary operation. For every commutative $K$-algebra $R$, framed point $y$ over $R$, integer $h$, section $s$ of $\mathscr P_y^{\otimes h}$ on the family over $y$, and finitely supported multiindex $\alpha:\{(\tau,i):0\le i<m_\tau\}\to\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$, choose a value $N_y(h,s,\alpha)\in R$. No linearity or naturality condition is imposed on this operation here. There is also a function $\phi_\delta:(\Lambda/\mathfrak b_2\Lambda)^0\to K$; its domain is the singleton consisting of the empty tuple. For any coefficient algebra over the level's base field, every positive integer is a unit, the distinguished square root of $-d_{\rm mod}$ is the image of $\rho_\infty$, and the distinguished primitive $A$th root is the image of $\exp(2\pi\mathrm i/A)$.

## Flag models and logarithmic Kodaira–Spencer maps

[Definition / hypothesis AG.D141](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CompHS.TorFlag/)

Let $K$ be a commutative ring, let $X$ be a $K$-scheme, and let $\pi:G\to X$ be a semiabelian scheme with an $\mathcal O_E$-action for a CM number field $E$. Write $\bar a$ for CM conjugation. Fix a line bundle $\mathscr P$ on $G$ and an open subset $V\subseteq X$. Present $\mathscr P$ on an open cover with transition units $g_{ij}$, using the convention that local section coefficients satisfy $s_i=g_{ij}s_j$.

**Logarithmic derivations and tangent frames.** For an affine open $U\subseteq X$, put $A=\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_X)$ and let $I_U\subseteq A$ be the ideal of the reduced closed subset $(X\setminus V)\cap U$. Let $$\mathscr D_U=\{D\in\operatorname{Der}_K(A,A):D(I_U)\subseteq I_U\}.$$ An invariant tangent vector over $U$ is an $A[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$-point of $G$, over $U$, whose reduction is the identity section. For every finite $A$-basis $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ of these vectors, define the action matrices $C(a)$ by $$[a]_*v_i=\sum_jC(a)_{ij}v_j.$$ Their balanced symmetric matrices form the $A$-module $$\mathscr B_C=\{\Theta\in M_n(A):\Theta^{\mathsf T}=\Theta,\ C(a)^{\mathsf T}\Theta=\Theta C(\bar a)\ \text{for all }a\in\mathcal O_E\}.$$

For any $A$-algebra $S$ and point $g\in G(S)$ over $U$, translate the scalar extension of $v_a$ by $g$. If $f$ is regular on an open containing the image of $g$, write $(v_af)(g)$ for the coefficient of $\epsilon$ in its value at this translated dual-number point.

**The local Kodaira–Spencer identity.** For $D\in\mathscr D_U$ and any matrix $\Theta\in M_n(A)$, require the following condition. There is an open cover $(W_i)$ of $\pi^{-1}(U\cap V)$ refining the chosen trivializations of $\mathscr P$, and there are absolute derivations over $\mathbb Z$, denoted $d_i$, of regular functions on $W_i$ and all its smaller opens, commuting with restriction. They lift $D$: for every $r\in A$, $$d_i(\pi^*r)=\pi^*(Dr)\quad\text{on }W_i.$$ On every open $O\subseteq W_i\cap W_j$, for every regular function $f$ on $O$, every commutative $A$-algebra $S$ with structure map $\varphi:A\to S$, and every $S$-point $g$ over the point of $U$ specified by $\varphi$, with image in $O$, require $$((d_i-d_j)f)(g)=\sum_{a,b=1}^n\varphi(\Theta_{ab})\, (v_bg_{ij})(g)\,g_{ij}(g)^{-1}\,(v_af)(g).$$ Here $g_{ij}$ is the transition unit of the two selected line-bundle trivializations, restricted to the overlap. Its value at $g$ is a unit of $S$.

The *logarithmic Kodaira–Spencer condition* requires, for every affine $U$ and every finite tangent basis as above, an $A$-linear isomorphism $$\kappa_U:\mathscr D_U\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathscr B_C$$ such that, for every $D\in\mathscr D_U$ and every $\Theta\in M_n(A)$, the local Kodaira–Spencer identity holds if and only if $\Theta=\kappa_U(D)$. The open cover and local lifts in that identity may depend on $D$ and $\Theta$. The basis length $n=0$ is allowed.

**Compactified flag models.** For this construction, take $K$ to be the coefficient number field.

Assume ([Polarized modules and unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)), ([Coefficient fields and deeper moduli levels](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Level/)), and ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)). Let $B/K$ be a normal, flat, projective model with a dense open immersion $X\hookrightarrow B$. The universal semiabelian family extends over $B$, retaining its action, rigidification, cubical polarization and bilinear-bundle relations; its abelian locus is exactly $X$. Require $B$ to be smooth of dimension $\lfloor[E:\mathbb Q]/2\rfloor p_{\rm sig}q_{\rm sig}$ and $B\setminus X$ to be a simple normal crossings divisor.

A flag model here is a proper morphism $\mathcal F\to B$, with $\mathcal F$ normal and flat over $K$. It carries an open atlas of tangent frames: the frame matrices have left inverses and, after changing base tangent charts, differ by upper triangular matrices on overlaps. The base charts are affine with integral-domain coordinate rings, carry tangent bases and the matrices of the $\mathcal O_E$-action compatibly with arbitrary base change, and carry polarization sections generating all sections over every smaller base open and nonzero on each fiber. For every embedding $\tau$, let $\mathscr F_{\tau,i}$ be the span of its first $i$ frame vectors. The successive quotients $\mathscr L_{\tau,i}=\mathscr F_{\tau,i+1}/\mathscr F_{\tau,i}$ define the graded line bundles. The open flag locus is dense. On the open moduli locus, for every embedding $\tau$, the span of the full $\tau$-flag is exactly the $\tau$-eigenspace of the tangent space. Every framed moduli point over every coefficient algebra lifts uniquely to $\mathcal F$ with those filtration steps, compatibly with every base change. Over every algebraically closed field, only finitely many flag points above a fixed base point have the same filtration steps.
