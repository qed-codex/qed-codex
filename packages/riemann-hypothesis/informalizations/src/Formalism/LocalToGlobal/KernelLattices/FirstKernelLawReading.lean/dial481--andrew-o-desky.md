## Geometric theta-lift data

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D187](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.GL3Theta.LiftObj/)

Hypothesis (geometric theta-lift datum). Use ([Column chains and period pairings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Member/)), ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)), and ([Ideal characters and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TaylorLogMetric.Tor/)). Fix a source and one of its twisted chains $C$, with rank $N_i$, class set $C_i$, frame domain $Y_i$, determinant functions $d_i(y,\tau)$, determinant phases $\rho_i(c),\rho'_i(c')$, and uniformization operators $U^C_{i,c,c'}$. Write $D_{\det}$ for its determinant group and $U$ for the prescribed subgroup.

Choose characters $\mu_i:D_{\det}\to\mathbb C^\times$ for every natural $i$, full self-dual lattices $L_i^0\subset E^{N_i}$, full self-dual lattices $L_{i,c}\subset E^{N_i}$ for $c\in C_i$, and determinant labels $t_{i,c}\in D_{\det}$. Require $$x\in L_i^0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x_k\text{ is integral over }\mathbb Z\text{ for every }k,\qquad [t_{i,c}]=\operatorname{relDet}(L_i^0,L_{i,c}).$$ Define characters $$r_i(t)=\mu_{i+1}(t)^{-1}\chi_D(t)^{-\lfloor N_{i+1}/2\rfloor},\qquad r'_i(t)=\mu_{i+1}(t)\chi_D(t)^{\lfloor N_i/2\rfloor}.$$ For $i<m$ require $\rho_i(c)=r_i(t_{i,c})$ and $\rho'_i(c')=r'_i(t_{i+1,c'})$.

Choose maps $f_i:Y_i\to\prod_{\tau\in\mathcal T}M_{N_i}(\mathbb C)$ for all natural $i$. For $i\le m$ each map is injective, its image consists exactly of tuples of invertible matrices, and $$d_i(y,\tau)=\det f_i(y)_\tau.$$ Choose $c^D_\tau\in\mathbb C$ and a number field $M\subset\mathbb C$ containing every $e_\tau(x)$ for $x\in E$. For each $\tau$ require $c^D_\tau\in M$ and $$\{a\in M:a e_\tau^{\rm coord}\in\operatorname{span}_{\mathcal O_M}\{(e_\nu(x))_\nu:x\in A_0\}\}=c^D_\tau\mathcal O_M.$$ Here $e_\tau^{\rm coord}$ is the coordinate unit vector. Finally choose a finite subset $S\subset\mathbb N$ and functions $$\Theta_{i,c,c'}:\mathbb C^{\mathcal T\times\{0,\ldots,N_{i+1}-1\}\times\{0,\ldots,N_i-1\}}\longrightarrow\mathbb C$$ for every $i,c,c'$. The datum itself imposes no analytic condition on these functions.

The polynomial uniformization law at $(i,\nu)$ is the following additional condition. Let $\mathcal P_i=\mathbb C[X_{\tau,a,b}:a,b<N_i]$ and $\mathrm{ev}_i(Q)(y)=Q(f_i(y))$. Fix a complex-linear map $R_i:(Y_i\to\mathbb C)\to\mathcal P_i$ with $R_i\mathrm{ev}_i=1$ whenever such a map exists, and an arbitrary fixed map otherwise. For a function $\Theta$, let $\mathcal J_\Theta:\mathcal P_i\to\mathcal P_{i+1}$ first substitute $$X_{\tau,a,b}\longmapsto\sum_{s<N_{i+1}}W_{\tau,s,b}Z_{\tau,s,a},$$ where $W_{\tau,s,b}=0$ for $b\ge N_{i+1}$, and then replace each $Z$-monomial $Z^\alpha$ by $(\prod_{\tau,s,a}(c^D_\tau)^{\alpha_{\tau sa}})\partial^\alpha\Theta(0)$. Set $U_\Theta=\mathrm{ev}_{i+1}\mathcal J_\Theta R_i$. Require $$U^C_{i,c,c'}(\mathrm{ev}_iQ)=U_{\Theta_{i,c,c'}}(\mathrm{ev}_iQ)$$ for every $c,c'$ and every polynomial $Q$ satisfying $$Q((X_\tau\beta_\tau)_\tau)=\left(\prod_{\tau,a}(\beta_\tau)_{aa}^{\nu_{\tau a}}\right)Q(X)$$ for all upper triangular matrices $\beta_\tau$ with nonzero diagonal. The full polynomial uniformization law requires this identity for every $Q\in\mathcal P_i$. The weight used for a source $z$ is $\nu_{\tau a}=\lfloor\nu^{\rm row}_{z,i+1,\tau,a}\rfloor_+$, the nonnegative natural floor of the row weight defined in ([Rational kernel entries and derivative minors](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.KerEnt/)).

## Kernel-reading chains and theta transforms

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D193](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Ker.KerRead/)

Hypothesis (kernel-reading chain). Use ([Arithmetic field models for kernel comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Col.Fields/)), ([Character indices and local kernel constants](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Col.LocCst/)), ([Local Fourier coefficients of kernel comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Col.Loc.om/)), ([Finite theta transforms of period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Ker.thC/)), and ([Column chains and period pairings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Member/)). Write $E'$ for the model field, $C_i$ for the chain classes and $m=m_2+2$. Choose a finite set $S'$ of primes of $E'$, a natural number $\ell$, modules $N_{i,c}\subset(E')^{N_i}$, functions $\Xi_{i,c,c'}:\operatorname{Hom}_{E'}((E')^{N_i},(E')^{N_{i+1}})\to\mathbb C$, and subsets $P_i\subseteq C_i$ for every natural $i$.

For a model word $v$, require $S'=T(v)\cup B$ and $\ell=T_v(\prod_{P\in B}NP)^{A+B_0}$. There are decorated points with extra $Q$-frames $p_{i,c}$ for $i\le m_2+1$, indices $\alpha_{i,c}\in I_i$ for $i\le m_2$, auxiliary cosets $Y_c$ at stage $m_2+1$, and a partial map $d:C_m\dashrightarrow\{\text{terminal row points}\}$. These are all objects defined in ([Decorated lattices and auxiliary kernel characters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Col.Deco/)) and ([Local Fourier coefficients of kernel comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Col.Loc.om/)). Require $$N_{i,c}=N(p_{i,c})\quad(i\le m_2+1),$$ and require $Y_c$ to be one of the specified cosets for $N_{m_2+1,c}$. For all relevant classes and homomorphisms $x$, $$\Xi_{i,c,c'}(x)=\sigma_0(X_i(\alpha_{i,c},\alpha_{i+1,c'};p_{i,c},p_{i+1,c'};x))\quad(i<m_2),$$ $$\Xi_{m_2,c,c'}(x)=\sigma_0(X^{(1)}(\alpha_{m_2,c};p_{m_2,c},p_{m_2+1,c'},Y_{c'};x)).$$ If $d(c')$ is defined, then $$N_{m,c'}=N(\widetilde {d(c')}),\qquad\Xi_{m_2+1,c,c'}(x)=\sigma_0(X^{(2)}(p_{m_2+1,c},Y_c,d(c');x)).$$ Require $P_i=C_i$ for $i\le m_2+1$, and $c'\in P_m$ if and only if $d(c')$ is defined.

Let $\mathcal A$ be the ambient theta datum of ([Arithmetic field models for kernel comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Col.Fields/)). For each adjacent pair, apply the transform of ([Finite theta transforms of period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Ker.thC/)) to $\Xi_{i,c,c'}$ and $e_{N_i,N_{i+1},N_{i,c},N_{i+1,c'}}$, with prime set $S'$ and integer $\ell$. Transport its coordinates by the bijection $t:\mathcal T'\simeq\mathcal T$ to obtain a complex function $\Theta^{\rm read}_{i,c,c'}$ on the member's matrix space.

The integral reading condition at $(i,S_0)$, for a finite set $S_0\subset\mathbb N$, a torus datum $T$ and function family $e^T$ of ([Ideal characters and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TaylorLogMetric.Tor/)), means the following additional conditions. The ambient scale is $h=\operatorname{area}(L_T)^{-1}$, where $L_T$ is the period subgroup of $T$, $\ell$ is positive and supported on $S_0$, and for every $c,c'$ the transported ambient base function is exactly $e^T_{N_i,N_{i+1},sN_{i,c},sN_{i+1,c'}}$. The transport of every point of the model period subgroup $L$ lies in $\mathcal L_T(sN_{i,c},sN_{i+1,c'})$. The form and subgroup $(H,L)$ satisfy the integral quadratic condition of ([Finite theta transforms of period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Ker.thC/)), and $$[L:L\cap L_-]\ne0,\qquad [L_+:L_+\cap L\cap L_-]\ne0,\qquad[L:L\cap L_+]\ne0.$$ Every value of $\Xi_{i,c,c'}$ is integral off $S_0$ in the sense of ([Finite theta transforms of period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Ker.thC/)). The integral reading condition is an additional predicate, not part of the preceding chain hypothesis.

## Compatible kernel realizations

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D181](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Ker.KerEntTied/)

Hypothesis (compatible kernel realization). Fix the same member, divisor data, twisted chains and integral comparisons as in ([Integral and theta compatibility for families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Extra/)). Choose the ideal, scalar, character and determinant-to-ideal map of ([Ideal characters and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TaylorLogMetric.Tor/)), with $\mathfrak c$ exactly the member's prescribed ideal $\mathfrak c_0$. Choose ([Standard period bases for kernel theta functions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Ker.IsoKG/)) for this ideal and scalar, and ([Arithmetic field models for kernel comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Col.Fields/)). Use its field isomorphism $s:E'\simeq E$, embedding $\sigma_0:K'\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ and bijection of embedding labels $t$.

The two model generator primes are the member's $g_0,g_1$. Choose a map from member sources $z$ to model words $v(z)$ such that $T_{v(z)}=T_z$. Choose a set $S\subseteq\mathbb N$, a map from member twists $\mu$ to auxiliary tuples $\xi_\mu$ relative to $S$ in ([Decorated lattices and auxiliary kernel characters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Col.Deco/)), and a map from model $F'$-primes to model $E'$-primes. For every natural $p$ and every $\mu$, require $$p\in Q_\mu\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad p\text{ prime and some }P\in Q(\xi_\mu,s_{\rm pl})\text{ contains }p.$$ Here $Q_\mu$ is the member's prescribed divisor-prime set, and $s_{\rm pl}$ is the chosen map of prime sets, distinct from the field isomorphism $s$.

Choose local constants and character indices for each $\mu$, and one shared natural $m_2$ and shared global character datum, satisfying ([Character indices and local kernel constants](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Col.LocCst/)). Its complex coefficient embedding is $\sigma_0$, and its scalar $x_0\in E'$ satisfies $s(x_0)=\xi_0$, the member's prescribed scalar. Choose one vector $c^D_\tau\in\mathbb C$ indexed by the member's embeddings. These choices prescribe the ambient datum and the natural functions by ([Arithmetic field models for kernel comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Col.Fields/)) and ([Standard period bases for kernel theta functions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Ker.IsoKG/)).

For each $z,\mu$, choose ([Geometric theta-lift data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.GL3Theta.LiftObj/)) on its twisted chain and data satisfying ([Kernel-reading chains and theta transforms](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Ker.KerRead/)) with model word $v(z)$, tuple $\xi_\mu$, its local constants and character indices. Require that the discriminant-generator vector of every geometric datum be the same $c^D$, its finite set be $$\{T_z,g_0,g_1\}\cup Q_\mu,$$ each of its class lattices be exactly $sN_{i,c}$, and each complex theta function be exactly $\Theta^{\rm read}_{i,c,c'}$. Use the row weights defined in ([Rational kernel entries and derivative minors](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.KerEnt/)). At every $i<m$, require the polynomial uniformization law of ([Geometric theta-lift data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.GL3Theta.LiftObj/)) for weight $\lfloor\nu^{\rm row}_{z,i+1,\tau,a}\rfloor_+$.

Choose ([Rational kernel entries and derivative minors](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.KerEnt/)) for these same divisor data and twisted integral forms. For every level-free $(z,\mu,w)$, choose ([Finite-kernel basis comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.GL3Columns.KFTie/)) and impose its scalar comparison law. For every eligible $(z,w)$ of ([Integral comparisons for theta chains](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LatCmp/)), impose ([Local frames for kernel-entry series](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ThAtServed/)). In particular, each original local comparison embedding is the kernel-entry embedding $\lambda_w$.
