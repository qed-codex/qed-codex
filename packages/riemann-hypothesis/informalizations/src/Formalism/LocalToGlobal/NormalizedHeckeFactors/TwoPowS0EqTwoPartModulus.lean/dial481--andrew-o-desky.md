## Local Hecke algebras and normalized blocks

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D225](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.NormalizedDataCompatibility/)

Use ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)), ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)), ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)) and ([Automorphic realizations and normalized Hecke operators](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.NGalTiedAlt/)). Let $K$ be the coefficient field, $w$ a coefficient prime and $j$ a natural stage. Let $\mu_w^{\rm tw}$ be the delta-times-twist tuple, denoted $\nu_w$ in ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)), whose Hecke character is $\delta_{\ell_w}\psi_w$ in ([Hecke characters in recursive realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Host.CharFrame/)). Write $V_{j,w}=V_{\mu_w^{\rm tw},j}$ for the rational theta-input space and $O_a$ for the local coefficient ring of a record $a$.

Hypothesis (integral presentations). Map every generator of the specified integral theta-output module at stage $j(a)$ to the local output module $B_a$. These images span $B_a$ over $O_a$. If $b_g$ are their images, require $$\sum_g c_gb_g=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad(c_g)_g\in\operatorname{span}_{O_a}\{(s_a(r_g))_g:r\text{ is an original integral generator relation}\}.$$ Here $s_a:O\to O_a$ is the coefficient map characterized by equality with the fixed $K\to\operatorname{Frac}(O_a)$ after passage to fractions.

Hypothesis (local Hecke blocks). For every $j,w$ choose a coefficient DVR $A_{j,w}$, an embedding $e_{j,w}:K\hookrightarrow L_{j,w}=\operatorname{Frac}(A_{j,w})$ inducing $w$, and an $L_{j,w}$-space $U_{j,w}$ with a semilinear map $I_{j,w}:V_{j,w}\to U_{j,w}$. The images of every rational basis form an $L_{j,w}$-basis. Inducing $w$ means $$v_w(x)\ge0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad e_{j,w}(x)\in A_{j,w}.$$ Choose a finite auxiliary set $Q_{j,w}$, rational Hecke operators $H_{P,k}$ on $V_{j,w}$, and operators $\widetilde H_{P,k}$ on $U_{j,w}$ with $\widetilde H_{P,k}I_{j,w}=I_{j,w}H_{P,k}$. A generating Hecke index $(P,k)$ means that $P$ is split in the CM extension, lies over neither the residue prime nor a generator prime nor the selected opposite delta prime, is outside $Q_{j,w}$, and $1\le k\le n_V$. At selected stages and every generating Hecke index, the rational operators realize all normalized split Hecke operators of ([Automorphic realizations and normalized Hecke operators](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.NGalTiedAlt/)). For every natural $j$, every $w$ and every generating Hecke index, $\widetilde H_{P,k}$ is split over $L_{j,w}$ through its generalized eigenspaces. The set $Q_{j,w}$ contains every prime above the same $F$-prime as any of its members and is closed under CM conjugation.

Let $\mathscr T_{j,w}$ be the $A_{j,w}$-subalgebra of $\operatorname{End}_{L_{j,w}}(U_{j,w})$ generated by these operators. For an $A_{j,w}$-algebra character $\psi:\mathscr T_{j,w}\to A_{j,w}$, put $$W(\psi)=\bigcap_{T\in\mathscr T_{j,w}}\bigcup_{N\ge0}\ker(T-\psi(T))^N,\qquad \mathfrak m(\psi)=\ker(\bar\psi).$$ For an ideal $\mathfrak m\subseteq\mathscr T_{j,w}$, let $U[\mathfrak m]$ be the sum of all $W(\psi)$ with $\mathfrak m(\psi)=\mathfrak m$, and $U[\ne\mathfrak m]$ the sum of those with different residual kernel.

For every record $a$ with $j(a)=j$ and $w(a)=w$, require $Q_a=Q_{j,w}$ and choose a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_a\subseteq\mathscr T_{j,w}$. These ideals are distinct for distinct such records. At selected stages and nontrivial tuples, every maximal ideal is obtained in this way. Choose compatible isomorphisms $$\phi_a:O_a\simeq A_{j,w},\qquad \Phi_a:\operatorname{Frac}(O_a)\simeq L_{j,w},\qquad \Phi_a|_{O_a}=\phi_a,$$ with $\Phi_a\circ\iota_a=e_{j,w}$ on $K$.

There is a $\Phi_a$-semilinear injection from the local rational theta-input space into $U_{j,w}$, with image exactly $U[\mathfrak m_a]$. On every rational input $x$, its value after the specified local projection differs from $I_{j,w}(x)$ by an element of $U[\ne\mathfrak m_a]$.

The recorded eigenvalue labels $X_a$ are in bijection with those $A_{j,w}$-algebra characters $\psi$ having residual kernel $\mathfrak m_a$ and $W(\psi)\ne0$. Under this bijection, $$\phi_a(h_{a,P,k}(x))=\psi_x(\widetilde H_{P,k})$$ at every generating Hecke index. Finally choose one complex embedding $A_{j,w}\to\mathbb C$ for every $j,w$; its composition with each $\phi_a$ is the recorded complex embedding of $O_a$.
