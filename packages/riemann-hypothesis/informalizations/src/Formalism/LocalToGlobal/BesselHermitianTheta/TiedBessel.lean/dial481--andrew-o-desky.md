## Bessel flags and their period integrals

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D215](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Theta.BesDat/)

Use ([Hermitian theta spaces and pairings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ThetaX/)), ([Automorphic theta functions and their measures](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Theta.ThetaTied/)) and ([Adelic oscillator actions and theta integrals](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Theta.WeilTied/)). Write $G_A,G_B$ for the adelic unitary groups, $h_B$ for the Hermitian form and $\psi$ for the specified adelic additive character.

Hypothesis (Bessel flags). For every $E$-linear $i:A\to B$ with odd natural-number difference $\max(0,\dim B-\dim A)$ and every selected positive label $p\in\mathcal P(i)$, choose $\rho\in\mathbb N$ with $\dim A+2\rho+1=\dim B$, independent vectors $e_0,\ldots,e_{\rho-1}$ forming an isotropic subspace orthogonal to $i(A)$, and a vector $z$ orthogonal to $i(A)$ and every $e_j$, with $h_B(z,z)\ne0$. Choose nonzero scalars $c_j\in E$ and a finite adelic $y\in G_B$; require $y=1$. For every $A,B,i,p$, also choose a real $b(i,p)>0$ with $b(i,p)=1$.

Let $N_e$ be the subgroup generated by unitary $n$ satisfying $(n-1)e_i\in\operatorname{span}_{A_E}(e_j:j<i)$ and $(n-1)x\in\operatorname{span}_{A_E}(e_j)$ for every rational $x$ orthogonal to all $e_j$, where $A_E=\mathbb A_F\otimes_F E$. Its rational subgroup is $N_e\cap U(B)(F)$. Define the scalar $\chi_e(n)$ by choosing coefficients $a_{ij},b_j\in A_E$ satisfying $$(n-1)e_i=\sum_j a_{ij}e_j,\qquad(n-1)z=\sum_jb_je_j,$$ and setting $$\chi_e(n)=\psi\left(\operatorname{Tr}_{A_E/\mathbb A_F}\left(\sum_{i+1<\rho}c_i a_{i+1,i}+\sum_{i+1=\rho}c_i b_i\right)\right).$$ If no such value occurs, set $\chi_e(n)=1$; if one occurs, fix a choice of that value. This definition applies to every $n\in G_B$.

Hypothesis (Bessel integral comparison). Suppose $\dim A\le\dim B$, the rank difference is odd, $A$ has correct Gram signatures, $i$ is isometric, and $p$ equals one of the specified symmetric labels $p'_u\in\mathcal P(X_0\to Y_0)$ after identifying its domain, codomain and map. For every compatible Haar measure $\nu_A$ and rational fundamental domain $\mathcal F_A$, every Haar measure $\nu_N$ on $N_e$ and rational fundamental domain $\mathcal F_N$ of mass one, and every map $s:G_A\to G_B$ acting as $h$ on $i(A)$ and as the identity on its orthogonal complement, require $$B_{i,p,s_F}(\phi,F)=b(i,p)p(A)\int_{\mathcal F_A}\int_{\mathcal F_N}\overline{e_A\phi(h)}e_BF(ns(h)y)\overline{\chi_e(n)}\,d\nu_N(n)\,d\nu_A(h).$$ This holds for every Fourier--Jacobi input $s_F\in\mathcal J(i)$, every $F\in\mathcal C(B)$ and every $\phi\in\mathcal A(A)$ provided either $A$ has no nonzero isotropic subspace or $\phi\in\mathcal C(A)$. The data $e,z,c,y$ in this formula are those chosen for the indicated $i,p$.
