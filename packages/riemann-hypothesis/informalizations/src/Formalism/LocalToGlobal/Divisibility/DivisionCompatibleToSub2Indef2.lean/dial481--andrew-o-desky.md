## Divisor rows and additive-character comparisons

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D218](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.DIV.DivisionCompatibilityDataThreeSided/)

Use ([Arithmetic families with local and theta data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/family-arithmetic-data.html)), ([Row-side families of Hermitian spaces](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Rows.RowSideFamily/)), ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)), ([Divisor realizations in arithmetic families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DivX/)) and ([Hermitian row lattices and their rational models](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DivData/)). Write $K$ for the coefficient field, $E/F$ for the CM extension, $T$ for the tuples and $t_0$ for the distinguished tuple. Let $S_0,S_1$ be the specified coordinate realizations at the base and sharp levels. Denote their row dimensions by $d_0,d_1$, their row lattices by $R_0,R_1$, their archimedean frame matrices by $X_{E,0,v},X_{E,1,v}$, and their lifted dimensions, integral modules and theta matrices by $d_{W,j},M_j,\theta_{j,t}$. Let $L_{j,t}$ be the matrix including selected lifted coordinates into all lifted coordinates, and $\pi_{j,t}$ its specified projection back. All these are fixed data, not additional existence conclusions.

Hypothesis (divisor row comparisons). Choose a group isomorphism $e_G:G\simeq G_{\rm gen}$ to the product of generator-place matrix groups in ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), a tuple map $\tau:T\to\mathcal T$, and $K$-linear isomorphisms $$e_R:K^{d_1}\simeq R_K,\qquad e_R^0:K^{d_0}\simeq R_K^0.$$ Here $R_K$ and $R_K^0$ are the rational sharp and base Hermitian row spaces. Choose semilinear maps from all sharp and base row coordinates to their respective complex ambient row spaces at $\tau(t)$. For every $j,t$ choose a $K$-linear isomorphism $e_{j,t}:K^{n_{j,t}}\simeq V_{\tau(t),j+1}$ to the rational theta-input space, and a semilinear map $J_j:K^{d_{W,j}}\to M_{j+1}^{\mathbb C}$ to the theta-output space. Choose a map $u\mapsto w(u)$ from family source words to coordinate-column words, and $K$-linear isomorphisms $e_{N,u}:K^{a_{w(u)}}\simeq N_{u,K}$ to the rational source spaces.

The first two isomorphisms identify the integral row lattices and every archimedean Hermitian form: $$e_R(R_1)=R,\qquad e_R^0(R_0)=R^0,$$ $$Q_v(e_Rx,e_Ry)=\langle X_{E,1,v}^{-1}x,X_{E,1,v}^{-1}y\rangle,\qquad Q_v^0(e_R^0x,e_R^0y)=\langle X_{E,0,v}^{-1}x,X_{E,0,v}^{-1}y\rangle.$$ For every $j,t$, the selected complex input space is exactly $$Z_{\tau(t),j+1}=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{J^{\rm in}_{\tau(t),j+1}(e_{j,t}(L_{j,t}\mathbf e_a)):a\},$$ and the theta maps satisfy $$\theta_{\tau(t),j+1}^{P}\bigl(J^{\rm in}_{\tau(t),j+1}(e_{j,t}x)\bigr)=J_j(\theta_{j,t}\pi_{j,t}x).$$ The source-to-row pairings satisfy $$B_u(e_{N,u}x,e_Ry)=B^{\rm col}_{w(u)}(x,y).$$

Impose the following three numerical identities for every coordinate stage $0\le i<m$, tuple $t$ and coefficient prime $w$. The family indices at stage $i+1$ equal the coordinate indices at stage $i$: $$I_{\tau(t),i+1,w}=[M_i:\theta_{i,t}(T_{i,t})]_w,\qquad J_{\tau(t),i+1,w}=[T_{i,t}:T_{i,t}^{\#}]_w,$$ where $T_{i,t}^{\#}$ is the dual for the coordinate Hermitian form. The correction is zero off the prescribed twisted set and otherwise equals $$\eta_{i+1,w}=\frac{1}{e_w}\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O_{K,w}}\bigl((T_{i,\mu(w)})_w/((T_{i,\mu(w)})_w\cap(C_{i,\mu(w)})_w)\bigr).$$ Here $T_{i,t}$ and $C_{i,t}$ are respectively the selected theta-input and cut numerator lattices obtained from the base-level row coordinates. The supplied family indices are those in ([Local error functions and weight normalizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ErrC/)). Relative local index means $$[A:B]_w=\operatorname{length}(A_w/(A_w\cap B_w))-\operatorname{length}(B_w/(A_w\cap B_w)).$$ In these numerical identities transport $w$ through the specified identification of coefficient primes before evaluating the coordinate data.

Hypothesis (additive characters and finite levels). There exist a finite set $P_g$ of primes of $E$, a bijection $\lambda:\coprod_{a=0,1}\mathcal P_a\simeq P_g$ from the generator-place labels, field isomorphisms $r_v:k_{\lambda(v)}\simeq\mathbb F_{g_a}$ for $v\in\mathcal P_a$, additive characters $\psi_P:k_P\to K^\times$ for all primes $P$ of $E$, and a group isomorphism $$e_C:G\simeq\prod_{P\in P_g}\mathrm{GL}_r(k_P).$$ The prime $\lambda(v)$ is the prescribed prime of the family label $v$. Applying $r_v$ entrywise to $e_C(g)_{\lambda(v)}$ gives $e_G(g)_v$. Every $\psi_P$ for $P\in P_g$ is nontrivial. If $\bar\xi_0$ denotes the reduction of the fixed integral scalar $\xi_0$, then $$\jmath(\psi_{\lambda(v)}(c))=\exp(2\pi\mathrm i b/g_a)\quad\text{whenever }\bar\xi_0c=b\text{ in }k_{\lambda(v)},\ b\in\mathbb Z.$$

For a residue field $k$, let $U_r(k)$ be the upper unitriangular group, with character $u\mapsto\psi(\sum_{i=0}^{r-2}u_{i,i+1})$. Let $U_{r,\rho,k}^{\sharp}(k_P)$ consist of those $u\in U_r(k_P)$ with $u_{i,\rho+k}=0$ for $\rho\le i<\rho+k$. Its character is $u\mapsto\psi_P(\sum_{(i,l)\in A}u_{i,l})$, where $$A=\{(i,i+1):i+1\ne\rho,\ i+1\ne\rho+k\}\cup\{(\rho-1,\rho+k)\}$$ with only indices in $\{0,\ldots,r-1\}$ retained. Require that $e_C$ identifies $P_0,\psi_0$ with the product of the groups $U_r(k_P)$ and these characters. Define a permutation $s$ of the indices by $s(i)=k+i$ for $i<\rho$, $s(i)=i-\rho$ for $\rho\le i<\rho+k$, and $s(i)=i$ otherwise, where $\rho=r_S$. Reindexing $e_C(g)_{s(i),s(l)}$ identifies $P_1,\psi_1$ with the product of the groups $U_{r,\rho,k}^{\sharp}(k_P)$ and their displayed characters.

Hypothesis (rational theta output). Write $\mathcal H_j$ for the rational Hermitian theta-output space in ([Divisor realizations in arithmetic families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DivX/)) and $W_j^K$ for the rational cusp-form realization in ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)). At a coefficient prime $w$, let $\mu_w$ be the selected tuple and $\mu_w^{\rm tw}$ the delta-times-twist tuple whose Hecke character is $\delta_{\ell_w}\psi_w$ in ([Hecke characters in recursive realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Host.CharFrame/)). There are $K$-linear maps $d_j:\mathcal H_j\to W_j^K$ for every natural $j$. For each selected $i$, if $y$ is the coordinate vector obtained from $x\in\mathcal H_{i+1}$ by the inverse of the specified output-coordinate isomorphism, then $$f_i(d_{i+1}x)=\sum_a y_a b_{i,a},$$ where $f_i$ is the fixed comparison with the rational realization and $b_{i,a}$ is its specified base-level rational basis.

For every coefficient prime $w$ and natural $j$ there is a semilinear map $$E_{w,j}:\mathcal H_j\longrightarrow K_w'\otimes_{O_{i(w)}}\mathcal F_{i(w),j,\epsilon(w)}.$$ Write $O'_w$ for the coefficient DVR with fraction field $K'_w$. For $1\le j\le m$ and $\mu_w\ne\mu_0$, the images under $E_{w,j}$ of the specified rational basis are independent over $K_w'$. The $O'_w$-span of the images of the supplied integral lattice in $\mathcal H_j$ is finitely generated, is contained in the scalar extension of the selected finite-group idempotent image of the $\psi_U$-eigenspace, and spans that image over $K'_w$. Under these same restrictions on $j,w$, if $x$ belongs to the selected rational theta-input subspace at $\mu_w^{\rm tw}$ and $J_j^{\mathcal H}y=\theta_{\mu_w^{\rm tw},j}^{P}(J_{\mu_w^{\rm tw},j}^{\rm in}x)$, then $$E_{w,j}y=\theta_{w,j}^{P}(e_{w,j}^{\rm in}x).$$ The rational cusp-form structure has a $K_s$-basis which remains a $K$-basis after scalar extension at every selected stage. For $1\le j\le m$, if $d_jx=\sum_{a=1}^n c_a f_a$ with $c_a\in K$ and $f_a$ in its $K_s$-form, require $$E_{w,j}x=\sum_{a=1}^n\iota_w(c_a)\otimes\mathcal E_{i(w),j,\epsilon(w)}^{-1}(1\otimes f_a).$$ For every $a\in K_s$ and theta form $f$, require also $$\iota_w(a)\otimes f=1\otimes(\iota_{i(w),\epsilon(w)}(a)f).$$ The coefficient rings, embeddings, twist index $i(w)$, side $\epsilon(w)$, input comparisons and theta maps are those of ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)), ([Twist period lattices and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NChoice/)), and ([Integral and theta compatibility for families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Extra/)). The identities just stated apply to every natural $j$ where their quantifier does not explicitly restrict $j$ to the selected range.
