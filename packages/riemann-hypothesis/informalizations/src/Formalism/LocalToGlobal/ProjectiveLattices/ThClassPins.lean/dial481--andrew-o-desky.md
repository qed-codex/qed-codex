## Geometric theta class evaluations

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D216](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.OMPins/)

Use ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)) for the one-step coefficient realizations, their finite class sets, class lattices, coefficient maps, deeper moduli and scalar rules. Use ([Polarized modules and unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)), ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)), ([Adelic frames for unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.Core/)), ([Canonical derivative charts for theta sections](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.IsNab/)), ([Analytic realizations of rational Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.BaseW/)) and ([One-map and geometric comparison laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)). Write $K$ for the coefficient field, $K_s$ for the original moduli field, $K'\subseteq\mathbb C$ for the one-step coefficient field, $\sigma':K'\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ for its inclusion, and $\mathcal C_t$ for the finite class set. All section evaluations below are the scalar-rule evaluations defined in ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)).

Here is the theta-class evaluation used in the comparison. For an $\mathcal O_E$-module $L\subset E^n$, put $L^- =L\cap\mathcal O_E^n$ and $$z_L(N)=\#(L/N\mathcal O_E^n)^{p+q},\qquad i_L(N)=[L^-:N\mathcal O_E^n]^{p+q}.$$ The indices mean the index of the indicated subgroup in the ambient module. At a framed point $y$ over a coefficient algebra $S$, a short theta value for a finite coefficient $w$, polynomial $P$ and denominator $N$ is an element $v\in S$ with the following property. After a faithfully flat coefficient extension $S\to S'$ and the induced framed point $y'$, choose tuples $z_i\in A_{y'}^n$, indexed by $1\le i\le z_L(N)$, that remain distinct after every residue-field extension. They are killed by $N^2$. For every matrix $\mu$ carrying $L$ into $\mathcal O_E^n$ and every integral matrix $(c_{ab})$ equal to $N\mu$, require $\sum_b[c_{ab}]z_{i,b}=0$ for every $a$.

For every finite-level tuple $q\in(\Lambda/\mathfrak b\Lambda)^n$, choose $t_{q,b}$ with $[N]t_{q,b}$ equal to its level point and killed by some power of $\mathfrak b$. Starting with the generating polarization section, pull it through the normalized multiplication-by-$N$ isomorphism and then apply symmetric theta-group lifts at $z_{i,b}$ and $t_{q,b}$. Denote the resulting section of $\mathscr L^{N^2}$ by $F_{q,i,b}$. The multiplication isomorphism is normalized by the polarization's zero rigidification; the symmetric lifts have parameter $N^2$. Using the chosen derivative operation $\nabla$ from ([Canonical derivative charts for theta sections](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.IsNab/)), require $$v=\frac{1}{i_L(N)\,\#(\Lambda/\mathfrak b\Lambda)^n\,N^{\deg P}}\sum_q w(q)\sum_i\sum_{\alpha\in\operatorname{supp}P}P_\alpha\prod_{b=1}^n\nabla_{y'}(N^2,F_{q,i,b},\alpha_b)$$ after scalar extension to $S'$. The multiindex $\alpha_b$ is the part of $\alpha$ belonging to column $b$. This is an existence relation; no uniqueness is assumed in its definition.

For the one-step deeper moduli datum at tuple $t$, let $\delta_t$ be its nonvanishing scalar section and $\varepsilon_t:K'\simeq K'_t$ its fixed coefficient-field identification. Say that a scalar rule $f$ is the theta class associated with $x=(x_c)_{c\in\mathcal C_t}$ when the following holds over every algebraically closed compatible coefficient field and every deeper framed point $y_2$ over an original framed point $y$. There are short theta values $v_c$ formed using the deeper derivative operation, $\varepsilon_t(w_{t,c})$, the lattice $L_{t,c}$, denominator $N_{t,c}$ and polynomial $\varepsilon_t(\operatorname{io}(x_c))$, such that $$f(y)=\delta_t(y_2)\sum_{c\in\mathcal C_t}\frac{\operatorname{vol}_t\,\operatorname{ep}_{t,c}}{\gamma_{t,c}}v_c.$$ Being a deeper framed point over $y$ means equality of the underlying moduli point under the prescribed map, equality of the transported tangent frame, and an isomorphism of the pulled-back polarization line bundle preserving its rigidification and its generating section.

Hypothesis (one-map comparison). Let $\mathcal C$ be the original domain labels and let $c_\eta$ and $\mathrm{ph}$ be complex numbers. Require $|c_\eta|=|\mathrm{ph}|=1$. There is a single $z\in Z(G)$ such that, for every $t$, there is a root of unity $\zeta_t$ in the distinguished complex image of $K$ with the following property. For every placed input $x$, its theta class has a representative in the specified algebraic cusp-form space. Every section $f$ of the indicated weight and scalar degree representing that theta class satisfies $$\mathrm{ph}\,\theta_t^{\rm ad}(x)(q)=\zeta_t\,c_{\delta(q)}\,\ell_j\,\Phi_j^\circ(A_j(z)f)(q)$$ at every class $q$ in the adelic quotient by the rational unitary group. Here $\delta(q)$ is the component chosen by the adelic frame map, $\ell_j$ is the prescribed Lie factor, and $\Phi_j^\circ$ is adelization before multiplying by either the domain scalar or the Lie factor. The central element is shared by all tuples and inputs.

The one-map comparison also requires distinguished-embedding equivariance and rationality. Put $\Phi_{j,c}^\circ(f)(h)=c_{\delta(h)}\Phi_j^\circ(f)(h)$. For every $g\in G$ and every compatible finite-level lift $k_g$, the action $A_j(g)$ preserves the specified cusp space and $$\Phi_{j,c}^\circ(A_j(g)f)(h)=\left(\prod_{P\in P_g}\chi_{j+1,P}(\det e_G(g)_P)\right)\Phi_{j,c}^\circ(f)(hk_g).$$ For every rational base-tuple coefficient vector $x$, there are finitely many $a_s\in K$ and algebraic cusp forms $f_s\in C_j$ such that $$\mathrm{ph}\,\operatorname{ev}_{v_j}(\theta_{j,t_0}(\iota_{j,t_0}x))(h)=\sum_s\jmath(a_s)\,\ell_j\,\Phi_{j,c}^\circ(f_s)(h)$$ for every adelic $h$.

Require also that the image of $K$ is contained in $K'$. Every cusp form over $K'$ of every nonnegative weight and scalar degree is a finite $K'$-linear combination, in evaluations at all compatible framed points, of cusp forms over $K_s$ of the same weight. Every such scalar-extension expression for sections is preserved by each level operator $A_j(g)$.

Hypothesis (pinned one-step realizations). At each selected stage the distinguished one-step field $K'$ is exactly the complex image of $K$. The following conditions hold, with the integral embedding $\iota$ and base frames of ([Scalar normalizations and adelic period identifications](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.LawsJ/)).

The embedding $K\to K'$ followed by $\sigma'$ is $\jmath$. The coefficient source has identity Gram matrix and standard integral lattice. Its chosen extremal tuple has the conditional normalization of ([One-map and geometric comparison laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)). There is an arithmetic-to-analytic family comparison in the sense of ([Analytic realizations of rational Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.BaseW/)) for which the coefficient map $c_0'$ is scalar extension of the specified coefficient map: for every polynomial $F$ over $K$, its coefficientwise image $F'$ over $K'$ satisfies $c_0'(F')=c_0(F)$.

For every $g\in G$ choose a finite-level lift $k_g$ of its residue action. For every $a\in\bar{\mathfrak b}\Lambda$, require $$\iota(\operatorname{lev}(g)a)-k_g\iota(a)\in M\widehat L_W.$$ At every tuple there are finite adelic representatives $h_c$ and a finite-index subgroup $J'$ of the prescribed coefficient level group $J_t$. They give all rational double classes modulo $J'$, their stabilizer orders are exactly $\gamma_{t,c}$ and $\operatorname{vol}_t=[J_t:J']^{-1}$. The bad integer is nonzero, is divisible by every generator or tuple auxiliary prime, and each $h_c$ has split support away from it. Its global lattice is exactly the chosen $L^h_{t,c}$. Every rational coefficient form has a tuple of polynomial values at these representatives. The placed inputs are exactly such tuples, and their analytic theta values are the prescribed $\theta_t^{\rm ad}$.

For every nonzero ideal $\mathfrak a$ coprime to the bad integer, $$\sigma'(B_t(\mathfrak a))=(\mu_{0,j}\xi_t\eta^{-1})(\mathfrak a)\sqrt{N\mathfrak a}.$$ The determinant character $\xi_t'$ agrees with $\xi_t$ on every compatible determinant root. Put $$U(x)=\beta_V^{-1}G_V^{-1}\overline{(\iota x)}\,G_W\beta_W,$$ Its rows index coefficient vectors and its columns index $W$. There is a split-support idempotent $e$ for $P_g\cup Q_t$ such that $eU(x)$ is integral for every integral tuple $x$, and its finite coefficient is $$\sigma'(\phi_t([x]))=\Omega_g(\operatorname{red}(eU(x)))\,\Omega_{Q_t}(\operatorname{red}(eU(x))).$$ Here $\Omega_g=1$ exactly when, at every generator prime, the first $r$ rows have identity leading $r\times r$ block and the remaining rows in those columns are zero; otherwise it is zero. The factor $\Omega_{Q_t}$ is zero unless the first $r$ columns below row $r$ vanish and the leading $r\times r$ determinant is a unit at every $P\in Q_t$; when this holds it is $\prod_{P\in Q_t}\xi_{t,P}(\det U_P)^{-1}$. The original ideal $\mathfrak b$ equals $\prod_{P\in P_g}P$, the tuple auxiliary ideal is contained in every $P\in Q_t$, and all these primes avoid $Q_SqN(\mathfrak c_{E_0})$.

Both original and extended coefficient level actions are total and single valued up to the identity framed-point comparison, obey identity and multiplication, commute with every coefficient base change, and preserve each upper-triangular frame change. Each move is induced by a moduli isomorphism and Cartesian semiabelian comparison preserving the action, polarization and rigidification, and acts by the recorded level endomorphisms on the two level structures; it transports the tangent frame and the generating section.

Every bad integer is positive and divisible by $2,Q_Sq,d,g_0g_1$. Every $L_{t,c}$ is self-dual for the standard Hermitian form. It is commensurable with $\mathcal O_E^n$ using denominators coprime to both the bad integer and the level $M_2$. The denominator $N_{t,c}$ is odd, divisible by $M_2$, and $N_{t,c}\mathcal O_E^n\subseteq L_{t,c}\cap\mathcal O_E^n$. Its stabilizer order is positive. There is a nonzero integral denominator whose prime divisors are all non-self-conjugate, multiplying each of $L_{t,c}$ and $\mathcal O_E^n$ into the other. The lattices satisfy $L_{t,c}=\overline{L^h_{t,c}}$.

The correction coefficient is $\operatorname{ep}_{t,c}=\operatorname{epF}_t(L^h_{t,c})$. If a self-dual lattice $L$ has the same denominator and split-support conditions and an ideal $\mathfrak a$ satisfies $\mathfrak a+\bar{\mathfrak a}=\mathcal O_E$ and $\det(L)\bar{\mathfrak a}=\mathfrak a$, then some $u\in\mathbb Q_{>0}$ satisfies $$u^2[L:L\cap\mathcal O_E^n]^{p+q}=N\mathfrak a,\qquad \operatorname{epF}_t(L)=B_t(\mathfrak a)^{-1}u.$$ Here $p+q$ is the moduli dimension.

Require $p+q\le n$. The coefficient map $\operatorname{io}$ is injective onto the span of harmonic polynomials of the chosen weight: they are killed by every contraction between conjugate embedding rows, are invariant under adding an earlier row to a later row, and have the prescribed degree in each row. Its transformation under a unitary coefficient matrix is a scalar multiple of the conjugate-matrix substitution on polynomials. The specified highest vector is the tensor of the fixed extremal polynomials. It belongs primitively to the standard coefficient lattice: a scalar multiple of it is integral only when that scalar is integral. Its image is the product of the initial principal minors at CM-type embeddings and final principal minors at conjugate embeddings, raised to successive differences of the row weights. Every harmonic polynomial with integral coefficients is the image of an integral coefficient vector.

Each $w_{t,c}$ is the same finite Fourier transform of $\phi_t$, transported by $\varepsilon_t$: $$\varepsilon_t(w_{t,c}(q))=\sum_x\varepsilon_t(\phi_t(x))\,\zeta_{M_2}^{\sum_b E(x_b,c_{\rm sep}q_b)}.$$ Here $x$ runs over the quotient by $\bar{\mathfrak b}_2$, $q$ over the quotient by $\mathfrak b_2$, and $c_{\rm sep}\in\bar{\mathfrak b}_2$ is the chosen element congruent to one modulo $\mathfrak b_2$. The Fourier sum has the source's finitely supported sum convention.

The deeper level satisfies $M_2=M\,p_t$, $(p_t,M)=1$, $\mathfrak b_2=\mathfrak b\mathfrak I_t$ and $\mathfrak I_t+\bar{\mathfrak I}_t=\mathcal O_E$, as well as the original divisibility of auxiliary levels. The moduli map preserves the $p_t$th powers of its level points and the corresponding auxiliary-level powers. Every original framed point over every compatible algebraically closed coefficient field has a deeper lift, and $\delta_t$ is a nonvanishing section of weight and scalar degree zero. The deeper auxiliary level is one. Its derivative operation satisfies ([Canonical derivative charts for theta sections](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.IsNab/)).

Finally, one root of unity $c_0\in\mathbb C$ works for every tuple. At each tuple, at the chosen coefficient embedding, impose the complex uniformization and lattice/period identities stated in ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)) and ([Adelic frames for unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.Core/)), with the chosen integral embedding and base frame. Whenever a rational isometry $\gamma$ identifies the component lattice selected by $h$ with $L_W(\gamma h)$ and satisfies the prescribed congruence modulo $M_2$ on $\bar{\mathfrak b}_2\Lambda$, require $$\delta_t(\delta_2(h))=c_0\,\xi_t'(\det(\gamma h)).$$ Every deeper component has Gram matrix $-G_W^{\mathsf T}$ and alternating multiplier $-\xi_0$ after the fixed rank identification.

Hypothesis (one-map rows). At every selected stage the distinguished one-step realization admits the one-map comparison for some domain scalars and phase. In addition, suppose that all tuple auxiliary primes over a fixed rational prime contract to the same prime of $E_0$ and all avoid $Q_SqN(\mathfrak c_{E_0})$. Then there exists a one-step coefficient realization for the full tuple set, whose coefficient field contains the image of $K$, satisfying all the pinned conditions above and the one-map comparison with the fixed domain scalars and specified phase of ([One-map and geometric comparison laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)).
