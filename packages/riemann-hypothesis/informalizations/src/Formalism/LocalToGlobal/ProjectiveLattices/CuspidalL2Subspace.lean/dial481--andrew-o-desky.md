## Adelic frames for unitary moduli

[Definition / hypothesis AG.D139](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.Core/)

Definition (geometric core). Let $E/F$ be a CM extension with its usual conjugation, let $E_0\subset E$ be an imaginary quadratic subfield, let $r$ be a natural number, and let $O$ be a commutative ring. Choose the moduli-host data of ([Polarized modules and unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)), a common original level and coefficient number field $K_0\subset\mathbb C$ as in ([Coefficient fields and deeper moduli levels](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Level/)), and a ring isomorphism $\mathcal O_{K_0}\simeq O$. At every natural stage $j$ choose an integral polarized module with its own signature and a moduli scheme $X_j$ at that common level.

For every $j$ and every embedding $\sigma:K_0\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$, choose the original Hermitian-lattice domain data and maps to framed points defined in ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)), with a finite label set $C_{j,\sigma}$. Write $\Psi_{j,\sigma,c}$ for the CM type of its domain with label $c$. Choose a Hermitian space $V_{j,\sigma}$ with recorded lattice data as defined in ([Hermitian spaces in the theta constructions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.CMData/)). Put $G_V=U(V)(\mathbb A_F)$. Choose maps $$\delta_{j,\sigma}:G_{V_{j,\sigma}}\longrightarrow\coprod_{c\in C_{j,\sigma}}\operatorname{Mat}_r(\mathbb C)^{\Psi_{j,\sigma,c}},\qquad \mu_{j,\sigma}:G_{V_{j,\sigma}}\longrightarrow\mathbb C,$$ and a subgroup $K_{j,\sigma}\subseteq G_{V_{j,\sigma}}$. Finally choose an ordered pair of finite places of $E_0$. These choices form a geometric core. The maps $\delta_{j,\sigma}$ have arbitrary matrix values; the uniformization conditions and the adelic frame and lattice conditions below are additional hypotheses.

For the following hypotheses, assume ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), ([Polarized modules and unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)), ([Coefficient fields and deeper moduli levels](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Level/)), and ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)). At the chosen stage let $V$ be the Hermitian $E$-space of dimension $r=p_{\rm sig}+q_{\rm sig}$, with a self-dual reference lattice $L_0$, a fixed basis and Gram matrix $G_V$. Its form $h_V$ is conjugate-linear in its first argument. The conditions below concern the deeper moduli scheme at the chosen stage.

Hypothesis (adelic frames and lattices). Let $F=E^+$ be the maximal real subfield and $G(\mathbb A_F)$ the adelic unitary group of $V$. The primes $g_0,g_1$ are the distinct generator primes in ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)). For $N=g_0g_1 15^{e_{\rm lev}}$, the finite-level subgroup consists exactly of the adeles with trivial infinite component whose finite action satisfies $(k-1)\widehat L_0\subseteq N\widehat L_0$. At each permitted $\sigma$ there is a map $$\delta:G(\mathbb A_F)\longrightarrow\coprod_c\{\text{frames of }\mathcal D_c\}.$$ Left multiplication by a rational isometry, or right multiplication by a member of the finite level subgroup, changes $\delta(g)$ by an element of $\Gamma_c$ and preserves $c$. There is a base frame in each domain in the image of $\delta$. Right multiplication by an adelic isometry with trivial finite component changes the frame, modulo $\Gamma_c$, by its infinite matrix conjugated by this base frame. Every image frame belongs to its domain; every domain frame occurs modulo $\Gamma_c$ and a triangular change. If two adelic images differ by $\Gamma_c$, the adeles differ by left rational multiplication and right finite-level multiplication.

The domain Gram matrices and alternating multipliers satisfy $$G_c=-G_V^T,\quad \xi_c=-\xi_0,\quad |\tau(\xi_c)|=|\phi_0(\xi_0)|,$$ where $\xi_0$ and $\phi_0:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ are fixed, and the absolute-value identity holds for every embedding $\tau:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$. Every $\Delta_{\tau,i}(\delta(g))$ has absolute value one. Use the ideals $\mathfrak c$ and $\mathfrak b_0$ fixed in ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)). Put $\mathfrak a=(\mathfrak c\mathcal O_E)\mathfrak b_0^{-1}$ and $\xi_0=-1/(\operatorname{N}\mathfrak c\,s_d)$, where $\operatorname{N}\mathfrak c$ is the ideal norm. For an adele $h$ define $L(h)$ to be the $\mathcal O_E$-span of $av$ with $a\in\mathfrak a$ and $v\in E^r$ whose finite-adelic image belongs to $h\widehat L_0$; define $\widehat L(h)$ as the closure of $\mathfrak a h\widehat L_0$. There is an $\mathcal O_E$-linear injection $\iota:\Lambda\to E^r$ whose image is $L(1)$, and a sign $\epsilon\in\{1,-1\}$ such that $$E_\Lambda(a,b)=\epsilon\operatorname{Tr}_{E/\mathbb Q}\{\xi_0 h_V(\iota a,\iota b)\}.$$ At every permitted $\sigma$ choose frames $x_0(\tau)$ whose Hermitian Gram matrices are the diagonal matrices of signature signs. The period identifications above can be chosen so that, for every adelic $h$, some rational isometry $\gamma$ identifies the domain lattice selected by $\delta(h)$ with $L(\gamma h)$. On $\bar{\mathfrak b}_2\Lambda$, the corresponding finite-adelic vectors differ from the image of $\iota$ under $\gamma h$ by $M_2$ times $\widehat L(\gamma h)$. At infinity the selected frame is the infinite matrix of $\gamma h$ times $x_0$. For every relevant embedding $\tau:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$, the chosen extension $\theta:\mathbb A_{F,\infty}\otimes_F E\to\mathbb C$ satisfies $\theta(1\otimes x)=\tau(x)$ for every $x\in E$, and $a\mapsto\theta(a\otimes1)$ is continuous on $\mathbb A_{F,\infty}$.

## Integral evaluation of moduli sections

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D198](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.LawsM.evalWD/)

Hypothesis (integral evaluation). Use the original moduli schemes, coefficient field $K_0$, polarized modules and framed points in ([Canonical integral modules for original moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.LawsM.pins/)). The following conditions hold at every selected stage $1\le j\le m$. Write $m_\tau=p_j$ on the chosen CM type and $q_j$ on its conjugate. A reduced finite-type presentation means a ring $K_0[z_1,\ldots,z_s]/I$ with $I$ radical.

A scalar rule assigns a value to every framed point over every reduced finite-type presentation. It is natural when extension of scalars between such presentations carries values to values. Require the following independence of presentation: if a framed point over an arbitrary $K_0$-algebra is obtained by scalar extension from two presented framed points, every natural scalar rule has the same image in that algebra from both presentations. Evaluate a scalar rule at an arbitrary framed point by a chosen such presentation when one exists, and by zero otherwise. This is the evaluation convention of ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)).

A complete coefficient DVR here means a characteristic-zero complete DVR $R$, with fraction field $L$; its residue field can be infinite. Given an embedding $\iota:K_0\hookrightarrow L$, an integral framed point is a framed point $y$ over $L$ that has a reduced finite-type presentation and the following integral realization. There are a semiabelian datum $G/R$, a semiabelian datum $G'/L$, and Cartesian maps $G'\to G$ and $G'\to A_y$ respecting the action, cubical polarization and rigidification, with the meaning of such maps in ([Polarized modules and unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)). For each $\tau$, the tangent $\tau$-eigenspace of $G/R$ has an $R$-basis of length $m_\tau$, and its image after extension to $L$ is the given tangent basis of $y$. An eigentangent vector means that $a\in\mathcal O_E$ acts through its eigenvalue whenever that eigenvalue lies in $R$. There is a section $\theta$ of the polarization of $G/R$ generating its full section module freely over $R$. Its pullback to $G'$ identifies with the generating section of $y$ under the rigidified line-bundle isomorphisms. The semiabelian data have all the conditions of ([Polarized modules and unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)); no level structure on $G/R$ is required by this definition.

For every algebraic section $f$ of nonnegative graded exponents $a_{\tau,i}$ and scalar exponent $n$, and any two integral framed points over the same $R,L,\iota$ related by a triangular change of frame in ([Coefficient fields and deeper moduli levels](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Level/)), require $$v_{R,e}(f(y))=v_{R,e}(f(y'))\qquad(e\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}),$$ where $v_R$ is the integer DVR valuation and $$v_{R,e}(z)=\begin{cases}+\infty,&z=0,\\v_R(z)/e,&z\ne0.\end{cases}$$ Real division by zero is zero. Algebraic sections have the meaning in ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)).

For every finite prime $\lambda$ of $K_0$ there are such $R,L,\iota$, an integer $e>0$ and an integral framed point $y$ with finite residue field, for which $$v_R(\iota(a))=e\,v_\lambda(a)\qquad(a\in K_0).$$ The integer valuations of zero in this last identity use the total convention $v_R(0)=v_\lambda(0)=0$; the extended evaluation valuation $v_{R,e}(0)$ remains $+\infty$.

## Original moduli uniformizations and cusp norms

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D196](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.LawsM/)

Hypothesis (original moduli and cusp norms). Use the original moduli family, field $K_0$, Hermitian spaces $W_j$, integral modules and levels in ([Canonical integral modules for original moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.LawsM.pins/)), and impose ([Integral evaluation of moduli sections](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.LawsM.evalWD/)). The following conditions concern every stage $1\le j\le m$; conditions involving $\sigma$ concern every embedding $\sigma:K_0\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ with $\sigma(\rho_\infty)=\rho_\infty$.

The original domains and framed-point maps satisfy the complex-uniformization conditions in ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)), with original scheme $X_j/K_0$, signature $(p_j,q_j)=(k+j-1,m-j)$ and level $(M,\mathfrak b,A)$ in place of the deeper data. Thus these conditions include surjectivity and uniqueness modulo the arithmetic and triangular groups, pullback of algebraic sections, period families and their integral-module isomorphisms, holomorphic period lifting, the displayed Appell–Humbert and normalized theta integral identities, descent of every image point from a reduced finite-type presentation, and triviality of the induced automorphy characters for the weights below.

The original adelic space at $(j,\sigma)$ is exactly $W_j$. Write $G_j=U(W_j)(\mathbb A_F)$, $G_j(F)$ for its rational subgroup, $\widehat L_{0,j}$ for its completed reference lattice, $\delta_{j,\sigma}$ for its map to domain frames, and $K_{j,\sigma}$ for its chosen subgroup. Require $$K_{j,\sigma}=\{k\in G_j:k_\infty=1,\ (k_f-1)\widehat L_{0,j}\subseteq g_0g_1\widehat L_{0,j}\}.$$ There are continuous extensions of the domain CM embeddings to $\mathbb A_{F,\infty}\otimes_F E$, in the sense of ([Adelic frames for unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.Core/)). With these extensions, the dictionary $\delta_{j,\sigma}$ has the following six properties: left rational multiplication and right $K_{j,\sigma}$-multiplication change its frame by an element of the domain arithmetic group and preserve the label; there is a base frame in each domain occurring in its image, with right infinite multiplication given, up to arithmetic action, by the infinite matrix conjugated by that base frame; every image belongs to its domain; every domain frame occurs modulo arithmetic and triangular action; and images equivalent by arithmetic action come from adeles differing by left rational and right $K_{j,\sigma}$ multiplication. Each domain's alternating multiplier satisfies $$|\tau(\xi_c)|=|\phi_0(\xi_0)|\qquad(\tau:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C).$$

The chosen scalar function $\mu_{j,\sigma}:G_j\to\mathbb C$ is continuous and satisfies $$\mu_{j,\sigma}(gg')=\mu_{j,\sigma}(g)\mu_{j,\sigma}(g'),\quad|\mu_{j,\sigma}(g)|=1,\quad\mu_{j,\sigma}(\gamma)=1\quad(\gamma\in G_j(F)).$$ Every leading Hermitian minor of every frame $\delta_{j,\sigma}(g)$ has absolute value one. The quotient measure $\nu_j$ specified in the conventions has finite, strictly positive total mass; write $\nu_j^1=\nu_j/\nu_j(G_j(F)\backslash G_j)$.

A weight consists of natural numbers $a_{\tau,i}$ for every complex embedding $\tau$ of $E$ and $i\ge0$, nonincreasing in $i$, and an even natural number $n$. For a triangular change whose tangent diagonal entries are $t_{\tau,i}$ and whose generating-section scale is $t_0$, an algebraic section of weight $(a,n)$ transforms by $$t_0^n\prod_\tau\prod_{0\le i<m_\tau}t_{\tau,i}^{a_{\tau,i}}.$$ Here $m_\tau=p_j$ on the CM type and $q_j$ on its conjugate. Scalar rules, naturality and their evaluation at arbitrary algebras have the meaning in ([Integral evaluation of moduli sections](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.LawsM.evalWD/)). Write $\mathcal S_j(a,n)$ for these algebraic sections. On every good embedding, pull back a section to domain frames and extend it by zero off the domains. Denote this collection of functions by $R_\sigma f$, with domain weight $\lambda^U$ given in ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)). Define $$\mathcal C_j(a,n)=\{f\in\mathcal S_j(a,n):R_\sigma f\text{ lies in the complex span of holomorphic cusp functions of weight }\lambda^U\text{ for every good }\sigma\}.$$ The holomorphic cusp condition, its weighted pointwise norm and the vanishing of its constant Fourier–Jacobi coefficients are defined in ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)). Require $\mathcal C_j(a,n)$ to be finite-dimensional over $K_0$.

The real supremum norm of $R_\sigma f$ is the supremum of its weighted pointwise norms, with $+\infty$ sent to zero. If this norm is zero for $f\in\mathcal C_j(a,n)$, require $f=0$. Choose once a representative $s(q)\in G_j$ of every left rational coset. Require $$q\longmapsto\mu_{j,\sigma}(s(q))\,(R_\sigma f)(\delta_{j,\sigma}(s(q)))$$ to be square-integrable for $\nu_j^1$ whenever $f\in\mathcal C_j(a,n)$. If its $L^2$ norm is zero, require $f=0$. Hypothesis (multiplication of cusp sections). Require $$\mathcal C_j(a,n)\,\mathcal C_j(a',n')\subseteq\mathcal C_j(a+a',n+n')$$ for every pair of weights.
