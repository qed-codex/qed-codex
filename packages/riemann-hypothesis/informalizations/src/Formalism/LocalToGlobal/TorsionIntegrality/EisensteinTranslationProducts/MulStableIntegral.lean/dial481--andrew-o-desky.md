## The Weierstrass sigma addition formula

[Theorem ANT.0224](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Th1.F.sigmaAdd/)

Let $\omega_1,\omega_2\in\mathbb C$ be linearly independent over $\mathbb R$, and put $\Lambda=\mathbb Z\omega_1+\mathbb Z\omega_2$. Use the functions $\sigma_\Lambda$, $S$ and the operators $U_x$ defined in ([Sigma functions and translations on a period lattice](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Th1.F.wSigma/)). Let $\wp_\Lambda$ be the Weierstrass elliptic function of $\Lambda$.

For $z,w\in\mathbb C\setminus\Lambda$, $$\sigma_\Lambda(z+w)\sigma_\Lambda(z-w)=-\sigma_\Lambda(z)^2\sigma_\Lambda(w)^2\bigl(\wp_\Lambda(z)-\wp_\Lambda(w)\bigr).$$

Write $\sigma^*=S$ and $\wp=\wp_\Lambda$. For $P,Q,z\in\mathbb C$ satisfying $Q\notin\Lambda$ and $z+P\notin\Lambda$, $$U_{P+Q}\sigma^*(z)\,U_{P-Q}\sigma^*(z)=\bigl(U_Q\sigma^*(0)\bigr)^2\bigl(\wp(Q)-\wp(z+P)\bigr)\bigl(U_P\sigma^*(z)\bigr)^2.$$

## A product formula for translation by a half-period

[Theorem AV.0708](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Th1.F.halfShift/)

Let $\Lambda\subset\mathbb C$ be the lattice generated by two real-linearly independent complex periods. Let $\wp_\Lambda$ and $g_2(\Lambda)$ be the Weierstrass function and invariant defined in ([Sigma functions and translations on a period lattice](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Th1.F.wSigma/)). For $t,z\in\mathbb C$ satisfying $$t\notin\Lambda,\qquad 2t\in\Lambda,\qquad z\notin\Lambda,\qquad z+t\notin\Lambda,$$ then $$\bigl(\wp_\Lambda(z+t)-\wp_\Lambda(t)\bigr) \bigl(\wp_\Lambda(z)-\wp_\Lambda(t)\bigr) =3\wp_\Lambda(t)^2-\frac{g_2(\Lambda)}4.$$

## Scaling identities for elliptic lattice functions

[Theorem ANT.0553](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Th1.F.homogeneity/)

Let $\omega_1,\omega_2\in\mathbb C$ be linearly independent over $\mathbb R$, and put $\Lambda=\mathbb Z\omega_1+\mathbb Z\omega_2$. Let $c\in\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$ and put $\Lambda'=c\Lambda$, with ordered periods $c\omega_1,c\omega_2$.

Use the functions and constants of ([Sigma functions and translations on a period lattice](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Th1.F.wSigma/)) for these two ordered period pairs. Subscripts indicate the lattice; for either lattice, write $\sigma^*=S$. For every $z\in\mathbb C$, $$z\in\Lambda'\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad c^{-1}z\in\Lambda.$$ For all $z,t\in\mathbb C$, $$\zeta_{\Lambda'}(cz)=c^{-1}\zeta_\Lambda(z),\quad\sigma_{\Lambda'}(cz)=c\sigma_\Lambda(z),\quad\wp_{\Lambda'}(cz)=c^{-2}\wp_\Lambda(z),$$ $$\eta_{\Lambda'}(cz)=c^{-1}\eta_\Lambda(z),\quad A_{\Lambda'}=|c|^2 A_\Lambda,\quad s_2(\Lambda')=c^{-2}s_2(\Lambda),$$ $$\sigma^*_{\Lambda'}(cz)=c\sigma^*_\Lambda(z),\qquad\theta_{0,\Lambda'}(ct,cz)=\theta_{0,\Lambda}(t,z),$$ $$g_2(\Lambda')=c^{-4}g_2(\Lambda),\qquad g_3(\Lambda')=c^{-6}g_3(\Lambda).$$

## A Weierstrass function identity for an endomorphism

[Theorem AV.1072](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Th1.F.isogenyP/)

Let $\Lambda\subset\mathbb C$ be the lattice generated by two real-linearly independent complex periods, and let $\wp_\Lambda$ be its Weierstrass function, defined in ([Sigma functions and translations on a period lattice](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Th1.F.wSigma/)). Suppose $\alpha\in\mathbb C$ satisfies $\alpha\ne0$ and $\alpha\Lambda\subseteq\Lambda$. Let $S\subset\mathbb C$ be a finite set of representatives for the nonzero classes in $\alpha^{-1}\Lambda/\Lambda$. For every $z\in\mathbb C$ with $\alpha z\notin\Lambda$, $$\alpha^2\wp_\Lambda(\alpha z)=\wp_\Lambda(z)+\sum_{P\in S}\bigl(\wp_\Lambda(z+P)-\wp_\Lambda(P)\bigr).$$
