## A Weierstrass sum over an endomorphism kernel

[Theorem ANT.0862](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Th1.B.KSum.kappa_sum_holds/)

Let $\Lambda\subset\mathbb C$ be the lattice generated by two real-linearly independent complex periods. Let $\wp_\Lambda$ and $g_2(\Lambda)$ be the Weierstrass function and invariant defined in ([Sigma functions and translations on a period lattice](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Th1.F.wSigma/)). Let $\alpha\in\mathbb C^\times$ satisfy $\alpha\Lambda\subseteq\Lambda$, and put $H=\{z\in\mathbb C:\alpha z\in\Lambda\}$. Let $S\subset H\setminus\Lambda$ be a finite set meeting every nonzero coset in $H/\Lambda$ in exactly one point. Then $$\sum_{P\in S}\left(3\wp_\Lambda(P)^2-\frac{g_2(\Lambda)}4\right) =\frac{\alpha^4-1}{20}\,g_2(\Lambda).$$
