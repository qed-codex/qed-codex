## Weierstrass series and good CM models

[Definition / hypothesis PHT.D252](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Th1.CM.cW/)

Let $K$ be a field of characteristic zero and let $g_2,g_3\in K$. Define $$c_2=\frac{g_2}{20},\qquad c_3=\frac{g_3}{28},\qquad c_k=\frac{3}{(2k+1)(k-3)}\sum_{m=2}^{k-2}c_mc_{k-m}\quad(k\ge4),$$ and the formal power series $$U_{g_2,g_3}(T)=T\frac{1+\sum_{k\ge2}c_kT^{2k}}{1-\sum_{k\ge2}(k-1)c_kT^{2k}}\in K[[T]].$$ Write $U=U_{g_2,g_3}$ when $g_2,g_3$ are fixed. The denominator has constant term $1$. The series $U$ has zero constant term and linear coefficient $1$; write $\log_U$ for its inverse under formal composition.

For a prime $\ell$, let $\mathbb C_\ell$ be the completed algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q_\ell$, with $|\ell|_\ell=\ell^{-1}$. Let $K^{\mathrm{ur}}_\ell\subseteq\mathbb C_\ell$ be the topological closure of the subfield generated by all roots of unity of order prime to $\ell$.

Let $\Lambda\subset\mathbb C$ be a rank-two period lattice, with Weierstrass invariants $g_2(\Lambda)$ and $g_3(\Lambda)$, and let $\iota:\mathbb C\simeq\mathbb C_\ell$ be a field isomorphism. A **good model** of $(\Lambda,\iota)$ at $\ell$ is a pair $g_2,g_3\in K^{\mathrm{ur}}_\ell$ such that $$|g_2|_\ell\le1,\qquad |g_3|_\ell\le1,\qquad |g_2^3-27g_3^2|_\ell=1,$$ and, for some $\lambda\in\mathbb C_\ell^\times$, $$g_2=\lambda^4\iota(g_2(\Lambda)),\qquad g_3=\lambda^6\iota(g_3(\Lambda)).$$

For a nonnegative integer $d$, put $\omega_d=(1+i\sqrt d)/2$. Stability of $\Lambda$ under $\omega_d$ means $\omega_d\Lambda\subseteq\Lambda$.
