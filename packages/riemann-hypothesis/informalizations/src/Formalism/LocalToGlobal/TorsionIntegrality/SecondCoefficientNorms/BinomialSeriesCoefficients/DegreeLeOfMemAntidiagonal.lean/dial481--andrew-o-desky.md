## Integrality of a formal square root with constant term one

[Theorem ALG.0731](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Th1.C.IntegralMvPolynomialLemmas.intMv_of_sq/)

Let $K$ be a field with $2\ne0$, let $R\subseteq K$ be a subring containing $1/2$, and let $I$ be any set of variables. If $g\in K[[X_i:i\in I]]$ has constant term $1$ and $g^2$ has all its coefficients in $R$, then every coefficient of $g$ lies in $R$.
