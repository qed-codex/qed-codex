## Algebraic Weierstrass coordinates at torsion arguments

[Theorem AV.0997](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.KerInert.AlgP.pTorsAlg_of/)

Let $\Lambda\subset\mathbb C$ be a period lattice whose invariants $g_2(\Lambda)$ and $g_3(\Lambda)$ are algebraic over $\mathbb Q$. If $u\notin\Lambda$ and $Nu\in\Lambda$ for some integer $N\ge1$, then $$\wp_\Lambda(u),\quad\wp'_\Lambda(u)\in\overline{\mathbb Q}.$$
