## Weil actions and prescribed theta test vectors

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D227](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.WeilFull.IsWeilFull/)

**Theta functions.** Use the CM extension $E/F$, conjugation, distinguished imaginary element $\xi_0$ and Hermitian spaces in ([Hermitian spaces in the theta constructions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.CMData/)). Let $\psi:(\mathbb A_F,+)\to\mathbb C^\times$ be a group homomorphism. For spaces $S,T$ with Gram matrices $G_S,G_T$, put $W=M_{\dim T,\dim S}(\mathbb A_F\otimes_FE)$ and $$B(x,y)=\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb A_F\otimes_FE/\mathbb A_F}\{\xi_0\operatorname{tr}(G_S^{-1}\bar y^tG_Tx)\},\qquad H(u)f(x)=\psi(B(x,u)/2)f(x+u).$$ Rational matrices are embedded diagonally.

A theta function is a function $f:W\to\mathbb C$ such that $$f(\gamma+x)=\psi(-B(\gamma,x)/2)f(x)\quad(\gamma\in M_{\dim T,\dim S}(E)),$$ which is continuous, fixed by $H(u)$ for every finite $u$ in some open additive subgroup, and smooth after restriction to every continuous additive map $\mathbb R^a\to W$ with zero finite component, for every natural $a$ and every translate of that map.

**Character conditions.** Let $\Sigma$ be the selected CM type. A compatible family consists of ring homomorphisms $\theta_\tau:\mathbb A_{F,\infty}\otimes_FE\to\mathbb C$ for $\tau\in\Sigma$ such that $$\theta_\tau(1\otimes x)=\tau(x)\quad(x\in E),\qquad a\longmapsto\theta_\tau(a\otimes1)\text{ is continuous on }\mathbb A_{F,\infty}.$$ Call $\psi$ standard if it is continuous, unitary, trivial on $F$ and nontrivial, compatible families exist, and for every compatible family $$\psi_\infty(a)=\exp\left(-2\pi\mathrm i\sum_{\tau\in\Sigma}\theta_\tau(a\otimes1)\right)\quad(a\in\mathbb A_{F,\infty}).$$ Here $\psi_\infty$ is the restriction to adeles with zero finite component.

For a character $\chi:(\mathbb A_F\otimes_FE)^\times\to\mathbb S^1$ and a natural number $n$, the prescribed restriction in degree $n$ means that there exists a nontrivial continuous character $\eta:\mathbb A_F^\times/F^\times\to\mathbb S^1$ trivial on norms from $(\mathbb A_F\otimes_FE)^\times$, and that $\chi(a)=\eta(a)^n$ for every such $\eta$ and every $a\in\mathbb A_F^\times$. The character conditions on $\psi,\chi_S,\chi_T$ are that $\psi$ is standard, both splitting characters are continuous and trivial on $E^\times$, and they have the prescribed restrictions in degrees $\dim S,\dim T$, respectively.

**Weil representations.** For characters satisfying these conditions, a Weil representation is a representation $\omega$ of $U(S)(\mathbb A_F)\times U(T)(\mathbb A_F)$ on all complex functions on $W$. It preserves theta functions and satisfies, on that space, $$\omega(g,h)H(u)=H(hug^{-1})\omega(g,h).$$ For every theta function $f$ and rational $g,h$, one has $(\omega(g,h)f)(0)=f(0)$. For every theta function $f$ and $x$, the map $(g,h)\mapsto(\omega(g,h)f)(x)$ is continuous. For every additive Haar measure $\nu$ and every set $\mathcal F$ measurable in its completion whose translates by rational matrices cover $W$ almost everywhere and are pairwise almost everywhere disjoint, require $$\int_{\mathcal F}\|\omega(g,h)f\|^2\,d\nu=\int_{\mathcal F}\|f\|^2\,d\nu,$$ with equality also when the nonnegative integrals are infinite.

At a split finite place choose one of the two conjugate place idempotents $e,\bar e$. If $g,h$ are supported there, and units $a,b$ satisfy $a=e\det g+1-e$ and $b=e\det h+1-e$, some real $c>0$ has the following property. Every complex linear functional $\ell$ invariant under $H(u)$ on theta functions for all $u=\bar e u$ satisfies $$\ell(\omega(g,h)f)=c\,\chi_T(a)^{-1}\chi_S(b)\ell(f)$$ for every theta function $f$.

Hypothesis (additional local and archimedean conditions). Let $\omega$ be a Weil representation. Impose the following conditions.

At a finite place of $F$ of odd residue characteristic admitting no split place idempotent over it, suppose the two dimensions are different and at least one is even. For every pair of self-dual integral lattices and every pair of adelic frames for them, if both splitting characters are unramified at that place, the frame stabilizers supported at that place fix every theta function which is a lattice vacuum there. A lattice vacuum for an additive lattice $\Lambda\subset W$ means $$H(2u)f=f\quad(u\in\Lambda),\qquad H(u)f=f\quad(u\in\Lambda,\ u=eu\text{ for a split place idempotent }e).$$ Self-dual means a full $\mathcal O_E$-lattice equal to its dual for its Hermitian form. The tensor lattice at a set $A$ of finite places of $F$ is the additive subgroup generated by $$\alpha\otimes\left(a\,t\,\bar s^tG_S\right),$$ where $s,t$ belong to the two chosen integral lattices, $a$ belongs to the fixed coefficient ideal of ([Hermitian theta spaces and pairings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ThetaX/)), and $\alpha$ is a finite integral adele supported in $A$.

The real infinitesimal identity is required conditionally on the existence, for every compatible family $\theta$, every size, every infinite place and every complex matrix, of a continuous additive adelic line with that matrix at the chosen infinite place, zero at the other infinite places and zero finite part. Fix any such family, Cartan involutions $s_S,s_T$ and signature frames $B_S,B_T$: each $s$ squares to $1$, preserves its Gram form, and makes $Gs$ positive definite; choose natural cutoffs $p_S,p_T$, with no separate bound on them. Each $B$ has Gram matrix $\operatorname{diag}(\epsilon_a)$, where $\epsilon_a=1$ for $a<p$ and $-1$ otherwise, and satisfies $sB=B\operatorname{diag}(\epsilon_a)$. For generators $X_S,X_T$ in the two real unitary Lie algebras at $\tau$, write $$Jz=\mathrm i s_Tzs_S,\quad Az=X_Tz-zX_S,\quad e_{ac}=B_TE_{ac}B_S^{-1}.$$ Let $c_{ac}(z)$ be the $(a,c)$ entry of $B_T^{-1}zB_S$ when $a<p_T\Longleftrightarrow c<p_S$, and its complex conjugate otherwise. For matrix-index pairs $k,l$ set $$M_{kl}=c_k((Ae_l-JAJ e_l)/2),\qquad N_{kl}=c_k((Ae_l+JAJ e_l)/2).$$ With $z_k$ the polynomial variables at $\tau$, define $$Q(P)=\sum_{k,l}\left(M_{kl}z_k\partial_lP+\tfrac12N_{kl}z_kz_lP-\tfrac12\bar N_{kl}\partial_k\partial_lP\right).$$ If $\chi_S,\chi_T$ have infinite exponents $\alpha_S,\alpha_T$ at $\tau$, meaning their values on that single complex factor are $(z/|z|)^{2\alpha_S},(z/|z|)^{2\alpha_T}$ with integral $2\alpha_S,2\alpha_T$, then every theta Fock vacuum $f$ and polynomial $P$ satisfy the pointwise derivative identity $$d\omega(X_S,X_T)\Phi_f(P)=\Phi_f\left(Q(P)+\{\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}M+\alpha_S\operatorname{tr}X_T-\alpha_T\operatorname{tr}X_S\}P\right).$$ Here $\Phi_f(P)$ is obtained by applying $P$ in the normalized creation operators to $f$. The derivative defining a Heisenberg infinitesimal action is taken along the specified additive line; its annihilation and creation operators in direction $z$ are $D_z+\mathrm iD_{Jz}$ and $D_z-\mathrm iD_{Jz}$. The creation operators are divided by $2\sqrt{2\pi|\tau(\xi_0)|}$. A Fock vacuum is annihilated by every $D_z+\mathrm iD_{Jz}$. A simultaneous vacuum is a theta function, a lattice vacuum for all finite places, and a Fock vacuum at every infinite place; normalized means that covolume-one Haar fundamental domains exist and its squared norm is $1$ on every such domain.

The prescribed finite test vectors are obtained from these normalized vacua by the following local-model convention. On a selected split half $eW$, a test function $\varphi$ has bounded integral support if some integer $a>0$ makes it periodic under $a\Lambda\cap eW$ and its support lies in $a^{-1}\Lambda\cap eW$. There exists a complex linear map $\mathcal T$ from all functions on $W$ to all functions on $W$ which, on these test functions, takes translation by $x\in eW$ to $H(x)$ and multiplication by $\psi(B(\cdot,y))$, $y\in\bar eW$, to $H(y)$, and takes the characteristic function of $\Lambda\cap eW$ to the chosen vacuum. The specified vector is $\mathcal T\varphi$ for every such $\mathcal T$.

For a chain step the half $e$ is the sum of split selecting idempotents at its generator and auxiliary places, with $e\bar e=0$. The selecting idempotents, finite levels, characters and block numbering have their exact definitions in ([Finite levels and character matching](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.LevCh.Is/)). Put $a=\dim S$ and write $N=\beta_T^{-1}x_f\beta_S$. The test function is zero unless $x=ex$ and every entry of $N$ is integral. Reduce $N$ entrywise at each selected place. At every generator place require the lower-left block below the first $a$ rows and within the first $a$ columns to vanish, the top $a\times a$ block to be invertible and block upper triangular for the specified numbering, and its initial $r\times r$ block tuple to lie in the selected finite level subgroup. Multiply the level character, all specified later-block characters, and the inverse step character of the determinant of that full $a\times a$ block. At every auxiliary place require the lower-left block below the first $r$ rows and within the first $r$ columns to vanish and the top $r\times r$ block to be invertible; multiply its auxiliary determinant character. A failed condition gives zero.

For an input-to-target vector use $\bar e$ and the framed Hermitian adjoint $$N^*=\beta_S^{-1}G_S^{-1}\bar x_f^{\,T}G_T\beta_T.$$ The test function is zero unless $x=\bar e x$ and all entries of $N^*$ are integral. At every generator place its top $\dim T$ square block is the identity and the lower-left block below it vanishes. At every auxiliary place that top square block is invertible and the lower-left block below it vanishes; multiply the inverse auxiliary character of its determinant. Nonintegral entries have residue zero in these definitions, and all values outside the stated conditions are zero.
