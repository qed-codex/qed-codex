## Analytic theta intertwiners and Weil actions

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D156](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.IsChainW/)

Use the CM extension $E/F$, tuple set $T$, distinguished tuple $t_0$, Hermitian spaces $Y_k,X,V_j,W_j$, finite-dimensional Hilbert realizations, measures and specified linear maps of ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)). Smooth tensor products mean the algebraic tensor products of the indicated smooth-vector spaces; their inclusions in the Hilbert tensor products are used in all weak derivative identities. A lowest-type tensor is a tensor of vectors in the designated lowest compact types. Write $\operatorname{pt}$ for the linear map taking a polynomial to its theta test function by the normalized creation operators applied to the chosen vacuum.

The following conditions define the admissible linear maps used in the hypothesis. An admissible $I_k$ is a nonzero bilinear map from polynomials and smooth tensors of $Y_{k+1}$ to smooth tensors of $Y_k$, satisfying the intertwiner conditions below for every tuple $t$. An admissible terminal $I_X$ satisfies those conditions from $X$ to $Y_{m-1}$ for every $t$.

For fixed $t,k$ with $k+1<m$, a downward integral map $\mathcal I_{t,k}$ is admissible when its integrands are integrable and $$\int_{\mathcal F_{t,k+1}}\theta_{t,k}(g,h;\operatorname{pt}_{t,k}\phi)\,f(v)(h)\,d\nu_{t,k+1}(h)=(\mathcal I_{t,k}f)(I_k(\phi,v))(g)$$ for every coefficient-valued automorphic form $f$, polynomial $\phi$, smooth tensor $v$ and $g$. At the terminal step the domain is the range of the terminal coefficient pairing applied to the $X$-realization, the target consists of coefficient-valued forms on $Y_{m-1}$, and $I_k$ is replaced by $I_X$.

A base theta map $\Theta_t$ is admissible when its function realization is the composite of the chosen downward integral maps from the terminal coefficient pairing, converted to the $Y_0$ multilinear model and followed by its initial central-idempotent projection in ([Finite levels and character matching](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.LevCh.Is/)). A lowest-type pairing $B_j$ is admissible when it satisfies the pairing conditions below for every $t$.

A map $T_{j,t}$ from the input Hilbert realization to the full smooth multilinear function model of $W_j$ is admissible when its image lies in the full prescribed automorphic multiplicity subspace of ([Analytic realizations of rational Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.BaseW/)), and there is a linear map $T^P$ on the range of the coefficient-valued realization such that, on every lowest-type tensor $v$, $$T_{j,t}(x)(v)=T^P(\operatorname{ext}x)(v),\qquad T^P(f)(e)(g)=\int_{\mathcal F_{j,t}}\theta_{j,t}(h,g;\operatorname{pt}_{j,t}(B_j(e,f(h))))\,d\nu_{j,t}(h).$$ Every integrand must be integrable for every $f,e,g$. A final Hilbert-valued map $\theta_{j,t}$ is admissible when its function realization is the already chosen full smooth map $T_{j,t}$ followed by the stage and embedding central-idempotent projection in ([Finite levels and character matching](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.LevCh.Is/)).

The intertwiner conditions used above are the following. Write $M,M'$ for the source and target discrete-series models, $\omega$ for the oscillator action, $\operatorname{pt}$ for the polynomial-to-test-function map, and $I(\phi,v)\in\operatorname{Sm}(M)$ for the intertwiner. Require $I\ne0$. For every $\phi$ and tensor-product coefficient functional $\ell_w$ on the Hilbert tensor product, there is a real $C$ such that $$|\ell_w(I(\phi,\bigotimes_\tau v_\tau))|\le C\prod_\tau\|v_\tau\|.$$ If $g$ belongs to the real unitary group at $\tau$ and $\operatorname{pt}\phi'=\omega(g,1)\operatorname{pt}\phi$, then $I(\phi',v)=gI(\phi,v)$ in the Hilbert tensor realization. If $X$ is in that unitary Lie algebra and $\operatorname{pt}\phi'$ is the derivative at zero of $\omega(\exp(tX),1)\operatorname{pt}\phi$, then $I(\phi',v)$ is the weak derivative of $\exp(tX)I(\phi,v)$: equality of derivatives is required after every $\ell_w$. On the other unitary factor require $I(\phi',v')=I(\phi,v)$ whenever $\operatorname{pt}\phi'=\omega(1,h)\operatorname{pt}\phi$ and $v'=hv$. Infinitesimally, if $\phi'$ and $v'$ are the corresponding derivatives under $X$, require $I(\phi',v)+I(\phi,v')=0$.

The pairing conditions are equivariance under the infinite unitary group of $V_j$, equivariance under each maximal compact group of the $W_j$ model, and normalization. Explicitly, $$\omega(u,1)\operatorname{pt}(B_j(x,w))=\operatorname{pt}(B_j(x,\rho(u)w)).$$ If $v_\tau'=\sigma_\tau(k)v_\tau$ in the lowest-type model, then applying $\omega(1,f_\tau^{-1}kf_\tau)$ to $\operatorname{pt}(B_j(\bigotimes v,w))$ replaces $v_\tau$ by $v_\tau'$. There exist lowest-type vectors $v_\tau$ of norm one and a coefficient vector $w$ of norm one with $$B_j(\bigotimes_\tau v_\tau,w)=\Delta_j,$$ where $\Delta_j$ is the fixed determinant polynomial of the prescribed signature and weights.

Hypothesis (normalization of analytic theta maps). Use the definitions in the Theta functions and Weil representations sections of ([Weil actions and prescribed theta test vectors](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.WeilFull.IsWeilFull/)), together with its definitions of normalized vacua and prescribed finite test vectors. The specified test functions at every polynomial are theta functions. For each $t$ and $k+1<m$, the selected oscillator action for $(Y_k,Y_{k+1})$ is a Weil representation and the selected finite test vector is the prescribed chain-step vector. The terminal choices have these properties for $(Y_{m-1},X)$. For every $j,t$, the oscillator action for $(V_j,W_j)$ is a Weil representation and its finite test vector is the prescribed input-to-target vector.

Each chosen $I_k$, terminal $I_X$, downward integral map, terminal downward integral map, base theta map, lowest-type pairing $B_j$, map $T_{j,t}$, and final map $\theta_{j,t}$ satisfies its admissibility conditions whenever at least one map of the same type does, keeping all other selected maps fixed. If no such map exists, the selected map is zero. The conditions on $I_k$ are imposed only for $k+1<m$; the terminal, base, pairing and lifted conditions have their full displayed tuple and stage ranges.

The polynomial used to normalize the extremal-vector pairing is defined as follows. At map index $0\le j<m$, corresponding to arithmetic stage $j+1$, and CM embedding $\rho$, let $(p_\rho,q_\rho)$ be the signature of the target Hermitian space, and let $n_{V,j}$ be the rank of the input Hermitian space. Define the weight list $\lambda_\rho$ by concatenating the $k-1$ weights $V_{\rho,i}$ and the $m$ weights $-B_{\rho,a}$ of ([Interlacing weight arrays](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.HCParam/)), and set it to zero beyond its range. For $0\le i<p_\rho$ and $0\le a<q_\rho$, respectively, put $$\alpha_i=\left\lfloor\lambda_\rho(i)+E_{\rho,j}-(p_\rho-i-1)-(t+1)/2\right\rfloor_+,$$ $$\beta_a=\left\lfloor-\{E_{\rho,j}+\lambda_\rho(p_\rho+q_\rho-a-1)+q_\rho-a+(t-1)/2\}\right\rfloor_+.$$ Here $E_{\rho,j}$ is the distinguished interlacing exponent in ([Interlacing weight arrays](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.HCParam/)). Let $Z_\rho$ be an $r\times n_{V,j}$ matrix of independent variables, with any out-of-range entry interpreted as zero. Write $M^+_{\rho,a}$ for its minor in the first $a$ rows and first $a$ columns, and $M^-_{\rho,a}$ for its minor in rows $p_\rho,\ldots,p_\rho+a-1$ and the last $a$ columns, with row indices starting at zero. With $\alpha_{p_\rho}=\beta_{q_\rho}=0$, the required polynomial is $$\Delta_j^{\rm pair}=\prod_\rho\left\{\prod_{i=0}^{p_\rho-1}(M^+_{\rho,i+1})^{(\alpha_i-\alpha_{i+1})_+}\prod_{a=0}^{q_\rho-1}(M^-_{\rho,a+1})^{(\beta_a-\beta_{a+1})_+}\right\}.$$ This polynomial is distinct from the nonnegative integer spacing parameter $\Delta$.

## Finite levels and character matching

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D154](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.LevCh.Is/)

Hypothesis (finite levels and character matching). Use ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)) and ([Hecke characters in recursive realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Host.CharFrame/)). Write $\mu_j^H$, $\eta^H$ for the designated Hecke characters, $\xi_t^H$ for the tuple twists, and $\mu_j,\eta,\xi_t$ for the circle-valued adelic characters. Write $P_g,Q_t$ for the generator and auxiliary places, $\chi_{j,P},\xi_{t,P}$ for their residue characters, and $t_0$ for the base tuple.

A circle-valued adelic character matches a Hecke character when it is continuous, trivial on $E^\times$, and has the following local agreement. For every $E$-place $P$, every adele idempotent $e$ selecting $P$, and every $a\in E^\times$, evaluate it on $1+e(a-1)$; the result is the Hecke character's local value at $P$ on $a$. A selecting idempotent satisfies $e^2=e$, lies over one finite $F$-place idempotent $\epsilon$, and either $e\bar e=0$, $e+\bar e=\epsilon$, or $e=\epsilon$; for nonzero $a\in\mathcal O_E$ require $$a\in P\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad e a^{-1}\text{ has nonintegral finite part}.$$ The base idempotent $\epsilon$ has value one at that $F$-place and zero elsewhere, including infinity.

Require $\mu_j$ to match $\mu_j^H$ for $1\le j\le m$, $\eta$ to match $\eta^H$, and every $\xi_t$ to match $\xi_t^H$. The map $t\mapsto\xi_t^H$ is injective, $\xi_{t_0}^H=1$, and every $\xi_t^H$ has finite positive order, has the following trivial restriction to $F$, and is tame at every finite place: for every finite $F$-place $v$ and every $a\in F^\times$, the product of its local values at $P\mid v$ on $a$ is one, and the analogous product at the infinite places over every infinite $F$-place is one. Here tame means trivial on $1+P\mathcal O_{E_P}$. Its ramified places are exactly $Q_t\cup\bar Q_t$.

The generator set $P_g$ consists exactly of the places dividing one of the two chosen quadratic generator primes extended to $E$. At every $P\in Q_t$, its residue-field cardinality is a rational prime $q_P\ne g_0,g_1$, and $q_P\notin P^2$. No two places of $Q_t$ are conjugate, including a place with itself. No $P\in Q_t$ contains the integer $\operatorname{cond}N\,T$ or any of the row integers $T_u$ from ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)). For each $p\in\{p_\delta,p'_{\delta}\}$ there is one quadratic prime $\mathfrak p$ such that every auxiliary place over $p$, for every tuple, contracts to $\mathfrak p$.

For $P\in P_g$ the inverse residue character $\chi_{j,P}^{-1}$ is the restriction of $\mu_j^H$; for $P\in Q_t$ the residue character $\xi_{t,P}$ is the restriction of $\xi_t^H$. Restriction means equality on every nonzero residue class lifted by $a\in\mathcal O_E\setminus\{0\}$ with that nonzero residue.

For every $j<m$, the finite frame $\beta_{Y,j}$ is a frame of its chosen lattice $\Lambda_{Y,j}$, which is self-dual and satisfies, for some $c\ge0$, $$(g_0g_1)^cL_{0,Y_j}\subseteq\Lambda_{Y,j},\qquad(g_0g_1)^c\Lambda_{Y,j}\subseteq L_{0,Y_j}.$$ The frames of $X'$, every $V_j$ and every $W_j$ frame exactly their self-dual reference lattices. A frame $\beta$ means that its completed lattice is $\beta\widehat{\mathcal O_E}^{,n}$.

The finite-group identification is $e_G:G\simeq\prod_{P\in P_g}\mathrm{GL}_r(k_P)$. For the class comparisons on $X'$ and $V_j$ in ([Rational class presentations and Hecke comparison](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Bridge.FamReal/)), require that the larger-level reduction, followed by $e_G$, is the upper-left $r\times r$ residue reduction in these frames. There is also an isomorphism from $G$ to the arithmetic member's product indexed by its two generator-place index sets, a bijection of that disjoint index set with $P_g$, and field isomorphisms $\mathbb F_{g_a}\simeq k_P$. They identify the embedded central-idempotent coefficients and all component group matrices after base change, and each indexed $P$ divides the corresponding quadratic generator prime.

For every $P\in P_g$ choose a nontrivial additive character $\psi_P:k_P\to\mathbb C^\times$. The first selected finite level is the product of upper unitriangular groups, with character $$u\longmapsto\prod_{P\in P_g}\psi_P\left(\sum_{a=0}^{r-2}u_{a,a+1}\right).$$ Put $\rho=r_S$. On the upper unitriangular group impose the additional zero entries $u_{a,\rho+k}=0$ for $\rho\le a<\rho+k$. Give it the character with edges $$\{(a,a+1):a+1\ne\rho,\rho+k\}\ \cup\ \{(\rho-1,\rho+k)\}.$$ An edge outside the coordinate range contributes nothing. The character is the product of $\psi_P$ applied to the sum of these entries. After the coordinate permutation $$\pi(a)=\begin{cases}k+a,&a<\rho,\\a-\rho,&\rho\le a<\rho+k,\\a,&a\ge\rho+k,\end{cases}$$ the second selected finite level and character equal this group and character. Finally, the rational target character at stage $j$ has embedded value $$\jmath(\chi_j^W(g))=\prod_{P\in P_g}\chi_{j,P}(\det(e_G(g)_P)).$$

## Analytic realizations of rational Hecke families

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D155](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.BaseW/)

Hypothesis (analytic realizations of rational Hecke families). Use ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)), with coefficient field $K$, embedding $\jmath$, tuple set $T$ and base tuple $t_0$. Impose ([Finite levels and character matching](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.LevCh.Is/)), ([Rational class presentations and Hecke comparison](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Bridge.FamReal/)), and ([Polynomial coefficient representations and integral lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Bridge.CoefMapTie/)). Use the specified groups, characters, ranks, linear maps and class-sum families of those hypotheses. The stage count and rank parameters of the rational family equal the arithmetic $m,r,t$, and its distinguished infinite place is that of $\jmath$. The following conditions hold for every indicated tuple and stage.

The split-place map $\operatorname{pl}$ is injective and its norm parameter is $q(P)=|k_{\operatorname{pl}(P)}|$. The fixed finite excluded set $S_b$ contains $P_g$. A split label is good at tuple $t$ exactly when its place and its conjugate are distinct and both avoid $S_b\cup Q_t$. Every split place whose two conjugates avoid $S_b$ occurs in the index map. At every place of $P_g\cup Q_t$, $K$ contains a primitive root of order the residue-field cardinality. Every degree-zero rational Hecke operator is the identity, and operators of degree above the Hermitian rank are zero, on both right and input families. The scalar twisting values in ([Class sums and finite Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.DIV.CSD/)) satisfy $$\jmath(\mu_{j,t}(P))=(\mu_j^H\xi_t^H)(\operatorname{pl}(P)),\qquad \jmath(\eta(P))=\eta^H(\operatorname{pl}(P))\,q(P)^{-1/2},$$ for $P$ good at $t$ in the first identity and good at $t_0$ in the second. A Hecke-character value here is its local value at the fixed choice of uniformizer in $E^\times$, as in ([Hecke characters in recursive realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Host.CharFrame/)).

The chosen infinite maps extend the designated CM embeddings continuously: $\vartheta_\tau(1\otimes a)=\tau(a)$ and $b\mapsto\vartheta_\tau(b\otimes1)$ is continuous. Every input space $V_j$ is positive definite. Each complex coefficient representation on $X'$ and $V_j$ is trivial on finite adeles, unitary, strongly continuous, nonzero and irreducible: the only invariant complex linear subspaces are zero and the whole space.

The chosen bilinear pairing $p_X:C_X\times\mathscr S(\mathscr D_X)\to\mathbb C$ is nonzero and invariant under simultaneous action on either factor at each real unitary place. Explicitly, if $u\in U(X')_\tau$ and a smooth vector $v_\tau'$ equals $u v_\tau$, then $$p_X\left(\rho_{C_X}(u)w,\ v_1\otimes\cdots\otimes v_\tau'\otimes\cdots\right) =p_X\left(w,\bigotimes_\rho v_\rho\right).$$ The adelic $u$ in this formula has the specified matrix at $\tau$, identity matrices at all other infinite places and trivial finite part.

Every map $H_{X,t}\to\{G_{X',f}\to C_X\}$ and $H_{V,j,t}\to\{G_{V_j,f}\to C_{V,j}\}$ is injective, has image the full equivariant function space $\mathcal A(\rho,J,\chi)$ in ([Rational class presentations and Hecke comparison](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Bridge.FamReal/)), and preserves its stabilizer-weighted representative pairing. The rational realization maps $\iota_t,\iota_{j,t}$ have function realizations exactly equal to the class-presentation extensions $\mathcal E$ there.

Use the multiplicity spaces and pairings defined in the conventions below. The map from $H_E$ is an isometric injection onto $$\mathscr M(\mathscr D_{Y,0},J_0,\chi_0)\cap\ker(\mathsf e_0-1),$$ where $\mathsf e_0$ is the central-idempotent action in ([Finite levels and character matching](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.LevCh.Is/)), with coefficients embedded by $\jmath$. Its pairing is $I_{\nu_0,\mathcal F_0}$. For every $j$ and every embedding $\sigma:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$, the map from $H_{W,j,\sigma}$ is an isometric injection onto the full space $$\mathscr M(\mathscr D_{W,j,\sigma},J_{W,j},\chi_{W,j}).$$ Its pairing is $d_{j,\sigma}I_{\nu_{W,j},\mathcal F_{W,j}}$, where $d_{j,\sigma}$ is the product of the two compact Weyl dimensions at each CM place, with weight transported to $\sigma$ as in the conventions below. The $G$-actions on $H_E$ and all $H_{W,j,\sigma}$ intertwine these injections with the finite-level actions $A_{e_G(g)}$ on the function variable.

Every chosen measure and set is a Haar measure and a rational fundamental domain with the exact local normalization in the conventions below. Use density normalization for $\nu_0$, every $\nu_{W,j}$, and $\nu_{Y,t,k}$ when $k+1<m$. Use probability normalization for $\nu_{X,t}$, every $\nu_{V,j,t}$ and $\nu_{Y,t,m-1}$. The finite level in each normalization is its full specified level in ([Finite levels and character matching](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.LevCh.Is/)). At every CM place, the chosen matrix on $V_j$ is orthonormal for its Hermitian Gram matrix. At the distinguished coefficient embedding the positive signs in the $W_j$ discrete-series model occur first, in exactly the recorded number.

The inert-place map is injective, and an inert label is good at $t$ exactly when its prime is fixed by CM conjugation and avoids $S_b\cup Q_t$. Every conjugation-fixed prime outside $S_b$ occurs. At every good inert label and admissible list type, the right and every input rational operator have all seven finite-double-coset comparison conditions in ([Rational class presentations and Hecke comparison](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Bridge.FamReal/)). The finite $F$-place set $S_F$ is exactly the contractions of places of $S_b$.

Finally impose Hypothesis (normalization of analytic theta maps) in ([Analytic theta intertwiners and Weil actions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.IsChainW/)) on the chosen chain and one-step maps. In particular their conditional selection laws retain their zero alternatives when no admissible map exists. The spectral scalar and tuple conditions are stated separately in ([Spectral normalizations of analytic realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat3.IsScal/)).
