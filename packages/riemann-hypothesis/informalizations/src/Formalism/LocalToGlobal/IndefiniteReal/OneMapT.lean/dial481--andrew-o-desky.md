## Analytic and rational realization data

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D204](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)

Use ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), ([Hermitian spaces in the theta constructions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.CMData/)), ([Interlacing weight arrays](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.HCParam/)) and ([Class sums and finite Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.DIV.CSD/)). Write $K$ for the coefficient field, $E/F$ for the CM extension, $T$ for the class-sum twist set, $G$ for its finite group and $v_0$ for its distinguished infinite place. Let $\jmath:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ be the fixed embedding and require $v_0$ to be its infinite place. Put $Y_j=Y_{\min(j,m)}$, and use $V_{i+1},W_{i+1}$ for $0\le i<m$. For a Hermitian space $U$, let $G_U=U(U)(\mathbb A_F)$ and let $G_{U,f}$ be its finite-component image. The dimensions are $\dim X'=r+mt$, $\dim Y_j=r+jt$ for $j\le m$, $\dim W_{i+1}=r$ and $\dim V_{i+1}=r+t$.

Hypothesis (analytic data). Choose the character frame of ([Hecke characters in recursive realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Host.CharFrame/)). For each $t\in T$ choose an additional Hecke character $\xi_t^H$ of $E$. Choose circle-valued characters $\mu_j$ of the ideles of $E$ for every $j\ge0$, a circle-valued character $\eta$, and a circle-valued character $\xi_t$ for every $t$. Choose a finite set $P_g$ of finite $E$-places, auxiliary finite sets $Q_t$ of finite $E$-places, residue-field characters $\chi_{j,P}:k_P^\times\to\mathbb C^\times$ for all $j\ge0,P$, and $\xi_{t,P}:k_P^\times\to\mathbb C^\times$ for every $t,P$. Choose $\mathcal O_E$-submodules in the recorded coordinates of every $Y_j,X',V_{i+1},W_{i+1}$, and invertible finite-adelic frame matrices $\beta_{Y,j},\beta_X,\beta_{V,i},\beta_{W,i}$ of their respective ranks.

Choose a group isomorphism $G\simeq\prod_{P\in P_g}\mathrm{GL}_r(k_P)$, maps from both split and inert Hecke-place labels to finite $E$-places, finite sets $S_b$ of finite $E$-places and $S_F$ of finite $F$-places, ring homomorphisms $\vartheta_\tau:\mathbb A_{F,\infty}\otimes_FE\to\mathbb C$ for every CM label $\tau$, and an additive character $\psi:\mathbb A_F\to\mathbb C$.

Choose discrete-series models $\mathscr D_{Y,j}$, $\mathscr D_X$, and $\mathscr D_{W,i,\sigma}$ at every embedding $\sigma:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$. Their signed parameters are respectively the negatives of the prescribed parameters of $Y_j$, the negative terminal parameter, and the transported holomorphic parameter of $W_{i+1}$. A discrete-series model with signed parameter $S_\tau$ means the following data and conditions at every $\tau$. There is a complex inner-product space $H_\tau$, continuous complex-linear operators $\pi_\tau(g)$ for all complex matrices $g$ of the Hermitian-space rank, strictly decreasing real numbers $\lambda_a$ that are rational and whose signed set $\{(\lambda_a,\epsilon_a)\}$ equals $S_\tau$, and an invertible complex matrix $f_\tau$ with $$f_\tau^*\operatorname{diag}((-1)^{1-\epsilon_a})f_\tau=\operatorname{Gram}(U)_\tau.$$ The space $H_\tau$ is complete and nonzero. Restricted to the unitary group of the displayed sign matrix, $\pi_\tau$ is a strongly continuous unitary representation with no proper nonzero closed invariant subspace, all matrix coefficients are square-integrable for its fixed Haar measure, and it contains an irreducible finite-dimensional representation of the maximal compact subgroup with highest weight $$\lambda+\rho_n-\rho_c.$$ Among every occurring maximal-compact highest weight $\mu$, this weight minimizes $\|\mu+2\rho_c\|^2$. Here $\rho_c$ and $\rho_n$ are the half-sums of the positive compact and noncompact roots for the ordered signed coordinates. The action in the recorded Hermitian coordinates is $g\mapsto\pi_\tau(f_\tau g f_\tau^{-1})$.

Let $H_\tau^{\rm sm}$ be the complex span of the smooth vectors for that action. Write $\mathscr S(\mathscr D)=\bigotimes_\tau H_\tau^{\rm sm}$, and $\mathscr L(\mathscr D)$ for the tensor product of the sums of all maximal-compact subrepresentations with the specified lowest highest weight. The associated multilinear function space consists of complex multilinear maps from $\prod_\tau H_\tau^{\rm sm}$ to functions $G_U\to\mathbb C$. Its automorphic multiplicity subspaces, level characters and Petersson pairings are the ones explicitly defined in ([Analytic realizations of rational Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.BaseW/)).

Choose finite-dimensional complex inner-product spaces $C_X,C_{V,i}$ with representations of $G_{X'},G_{V_{i+1}}$, respectively. Choose a bilinear pairing $p_X:C_X\times\mathscr S(\mathscr D_X)\to\mathbb C$. At every twist choose finite-dimensional complex inner-product spaces $H_{X,t},H_{V,i,t}$ with complex-linear maps into functions $G_{X',f}\to C_X$, respectively $G_{V_{i+1},f}\to C_{V,i}$. Choose finite-dimensional complex inner-product spaces $H_E,H_{W,i,\sigma}$ with complex-linear maps into the corresponding multilinear function spaces of $\mathscr D_{Y,0}$ and $\mathscr D_{W,i,\sigma}$. Choose representations of $G$ on $H_E$ and every $H_{W,i,\sigma}$. These model maps have no injectivity or image condition until the laws in ([Analytic realizations of rational Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.BaseW/)) are imposed.

Choose semilinear maps, relative to $\jmath$, from every right rational class-sum space to $H_{X,t}$, and from every lifted rational class-sum space to $H_{V,i,t}$. Choose also the double-coset coefficient realizations in ([Analytic realizations of rational Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.BaseW/)). For every $t,j$ choose a measure $\nu_{Y,t,j}$ and subset $\mathcal F_{Y,t,j}$ of $G_{Y_j}$; choose $\nu_0,\mathcal F_0$ on $G_{Y_0}$, $\nu_{X,t},\mathcal F_{X,t}$ on $G_{X'}$, $\nu_{V,i,t},\mathcal F_{V,i,t}$ on $G_{V_{i+1}}$, and $\nu_{W,i},\mathcal F_{W,i}$ on $G_{W_{i+1}}$.

For $U,V$, put $\mathcal M(U,V)=\operatorname{Mat}_{\dim V\times\dim U}(\mathbb A_F\otimes_FE)$ and $\mathcal P(U,V)=\mathbb C[X_{\tau,a,b}:a<\dim V,b<\dim U]$. For every $t$ choose representations of $G_{Y_j}\times G_{Y_{j+1}}$, $G_{Y_{m-1}}\times G_{X'}$ and $G_{V_{i+1}}\times G_{W_{i+1}}$ on the complex functions on the respective $\mathcal M$. Choose one complex function on each such matrix space independent of $t$, and another for each $t$. Choose bilinear contractions $$\mathcal P(Y_j,Y_{j+1})\times\mathscr S(\mathscr D_{Y,j+1})\to\mathscr S(\mathscr D_{Y,j}),\qquad \mathcal P(Y_{m-1},X')\times\mathscr S(\mathscr D_X)\to\mathscr S(\mathscr D_{Y,m-1}).$$ Choose linear maps between the corresponding level-and-character multiplicity spaces at adjacent $Y$ stages, from the image of $p_X$ on $H_{X,t}$ to the terminal $Y$ multiplicity space, and $\Theta_t:H_{X,t}\to H_E$. At every $i,\tau$ choose a complex square matrix of size $\dim V_{i+1}$. Choose bilinear maps $\mathscr L(\mathscr D_{W,i,\sigma_0})\times C_{V,i}\to\mathcal P(V_{i+1},W_{i+1})$, linear maps from $H_{V,i,t}$ to the multilinear functions of $\mathscr D_{W,i,\sigma_0}$, and linear maps $H_{V,i,t}\to H_{W,i,\sigma_0}$. Here $\sigma_0$ is $\jmath$ or its conjugate according to the distinguished infinite place, with $\jmath$ chosen when its place is $v_0$.

Hypothesis (rational forms).

**Rational forms for the lifts.** Choose a number field $K_s$ embedded in $K$. A rational-form datum on a $K$-space $W_L$ and complex models $W_\sigma$ consists of a $K_s$-subspace $W_s\subset W_L$, sets $Y_{\mathfrak p}$ at all finite $K_s$-places, functions $n_{\mathfrak p,y}:W_s\to\mathbb R_{\ge0}$ with $$n_{\mathfrak p,y}(0)=0,\quad n_{\mathfrak p,y}(f+g)\le\max(n_{\mathfrak p,y}(f),n_{\mathfrak p,y}(g)),\quad n_{\mathfrak p,y}(af)=|a|_{\mathfrak p}n_{\mathfrak p,y}(f),$$ a $K$-linear group representation $\rho$, a character $\chi$ into $K^\times$, and $\sigma$-semilinear maps $j_\sigma:W_L\to W_\sigma$. All scalar actions and restrictions are compatible. Put $M_s=\{f\in W_s:n_{\mathfrak p,y}(f)\le1\text{ for all }\mathfrak p,y\}$. For every $i<m$ choose a dimension $n_i$ and such data on $K^{n_i}$ with complex models $H_{W,i,\sigma}$. Choose a selected twist $\mu(w)$ for every finite $K$-place, a finite set of twisted places, a real constant $\kappa$, real scalars $\lambda_{i,t,\pi,v}$ and $\lambda'_{i,t,\pi',v}$ for the split and full spectral labels, real errors $e_{w,i}$, and complex scalars $\phi_i$.

**Rational forms at the geometric stages.** Choose also a rational-form datum of the same kind for every natural stage $j$ on a $K$-space $W_{L,j}$ with models $W_{j,\sigma}$, and a group $G_s$ identified with the member's finite general-linear group. Its rationality field embeds in the distinguished coefficient subfield $K_0\subset K$ compatibly with its inclusion in $K$. At each $1\le j\le m$ identify these data with the analytic rational-form datum: the identification consists of a compatible rationality-field isomorphism, a $K$-linear coordinate isomorphism, a group isomorphism, and isometric complex-linear model isomorphisms at every $\sigma$. They identify $W_s$ and $M_s$, intertwine $\rho$ and $\chi$, and commute with every $j_\sigma$. Their group maps agree after mapping to the member's finite group. The group action preserves $W_s$, and $\chi$ takes values in $K_0$.

For every twist index, side and $1\le j\le m$, choose a fraction-field-linear isomorphism from the member's theta-expansion space to the scalar extension of $W_{s,j}$. Its integral module on the latter side is the span of $1\otimes M_{s,j}$. A nonzero coefficient-ring element multiplies either integral module into the other under the isomorphism, in both directions. The isomorphism intertwines the finite-group actions, with the rational action multiplied by the inverse character. The coefficient rings and member expansion spaces are defined in ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)).

Hypothesis (one-step geometric data). For every selected stage choose a coefficient extension $K'\subset\mathbb C$ of the moduli coefficient field, an embedding $K\hookrightarrow K'$, and an identification of the geometric Hermitian space at its induced embedding with $W_{i+1}$. Its algebraic coefficient module is the tensor product of the polynomial highest-weight modules with the stage weights specified in ([Polynomial coefficient representations and integral lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Bridge.CoefMapTie/)). Choose a semilinear map from that module to $C_{V,i}$, and an $\mathcal O_E$-linear endomorphism of the geometric lattice for each $g\in G$.

For each auxiliary twist choose a natural number $p_\delta$, an ideal $I\subset\mathcal O_E$, a second level, its polarized moduli space and coefficient field, equality of that coefficient field with $K'$, a morphism to the initial moduli scheme, and a morphism of the universal semiabelian schemes above it. Choose an operation assigning a scalar to every coefficient-algebra-valued framed point, integer power of the polarization line bundle, section of that power over the point, and monomial in the tangent coordinates. Choose a regular frame function $c_\delta$ and a $K'$-valued function on tuples of the second finite level quotient. The moduli, line-bundle and frame conventions are those of ([Polarized modules and unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)) and ([Flag models and logarithmic Kodaira–Spencer maps](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CompHS.TorFlag/)).

For each auxiliary twist choose a finite class set $C_t$, a natural bad bound, $\mathcal O_E$-submodules $L_{t,c},\Lambda_{t,c}\subset E^{\dim V_{i+1}}$, natural numbers $N_{t,c},\gamma_{t,c}$, scalars $\operatorname{vol}_t,\varepsilon_{t,c}\in K'$, and $K'$-valued functions $B_t$ on ideals and $\varepsilon_t$ on $\mathcal O_E$-submodules of $E^{\dim V_{i+1}}$. The notation $\Lambda_{t,c}$ alone imposes no fullness condition. Choose a $K'$-linear map from the algebraic coefficient module to the polynomial ring with variables $(\tau,a,b)$, $a$ indexing a tangent basis at $\tau$ and $b<\dim V_{i+1}$, and a vector in that coefficient module. Choose a $K'$-valued function on tuples of the enlarged finite level quotient and, for each class, a function on tuples of the ordinary finite level quotient.

Choose complex uniformizations of all these second-level moduli spaces as in ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)), maps from $G_{W_{i+1}}$ to their components with analytic frames, characters of the finite $E$-ideles into $\mathbb C^\times$, complex-valued functions of coefficient-module-valued class functions and rational unitary automorphisms, and predicates on those class functions. The finite level quotient modules are those of ([Finite levels and character matching](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.LevCh.Is/)). Their matching conditions are exactly ([One-map and geometric comparison laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)).

## One-map and geometric comparison laws

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D223](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)

Use ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)) and ([Scalar normalizations and adelic period identifications](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.LawsJ/)). Write $K$ for the coefficient field, $K_s$ for the coefficient field of the original moduli, $\jmath:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ for the distinguished embedding, and $\sigma_s=\sigma|_{K_s}$. An allowed $\sigma_s$ is an embedding preserving the specified complex square root. At stage $j+1$, let $C_j$ be the $K_s$-space of algebraic cusp forms of the chosen nonnegative weight and scalar degree, $W_j^{K_s}\subseteq K^{n_j}$ its analytic rational form, $H_{j,\sigma}$ its complex Hilbert realization, and $J_{j,\sigma}:K^{n_j}\to H_{j,\sigma}$ its specified semilinear map. Let $\Phi_{j,\sigma_s}(f)$ be the algebraic form's adelic function, including its prescribed Lie factor and domain scalars.

Hypothesis (algebraic and analytic comparison). The map $K_s\to K$ on algebraic integers is the prescribed coefficient-ring map. There is a $K_s$-linear isomorphism $e_j:C_j\simeq W_j^{K_s}$ for every $j$. The space $W_j^{K_s}$ has a finite $K_s$-basis that is also a $K$-basis of $K^{n_j}$. The positive and negative highest-weight entries of the algebraic weight equal the prescribed coefficient-representation weights, and its scalar degree is $n_V$.

For every finite prime $\lambda$ of $K_s$ there is a bijection between the analytic norm-test points and the moduli norm-test points. Under this bijection their seminorms agree on $e_jf$ and $f$. The original moduli choices satisfy ([Scalar normalizations and adelic period identifications](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.LawsJ/)).

At each allowed $\sigma_s$, the prescribed unitary Hecke character $\chi_{j,\sigma_s}$ is trivial on the embedded $F$-ideles and has infinite exponent $-e_{j,\sigma,\tau}$ at every infinite factor $\tau$, where $e_{j,\sigma,\tau}$ is the prescribed character weight transported through $\sigma$. For every idele $x$ with trivial infinite component there exists $a\in K_s$ such that $$\mu_{0,j+1}(x)=\jmath(a),\qquad \chi_{j,\sigma_s}(x)=\sigma_s(a).$$ If $\sigma_s$ is the distinguished embedding, require $\chi_{j,\sigma_s}=\mu_{0,j+1}$.

Choose, for every $j,\sigma$, a tuple $v_{j,\sigma}$ of unit lowest-type vectors of the discrete-series model. Each is an extremal weight vector: on a diagonal compact torus with angles $(\alpha_a)$ it transforms by $\exp(\mathrm i\sum_a\mu_a\alpha_a)$, for the model's fixed lowest compact weight in its prescribed reversed-negative order. At the distinguished embedding impose this additional conditional normalization: if any such extremal tuple pairs with a unit coefficient vector to $\Delta_j$, the chosen tuple does too. Thus there are lowest-type vectors representing the chosen tuple and a unit coefficient vector $w$ with $B_j(\bigotimes v,w)=\Delta_j$ whenever that normalization is realizable.

At every allowed $\sigma_s$, the fixed moduli base frame equals the model's orthonormal frame, with its columns put in the prescribed positive/negative order. Impose the following conditional comparison. If there exists a $\sigma$-semilinear map $L:K^{n_j}\to H_{j,\sigma}$ satisfying $$\operatorname{ev}_{v_{j,\sigma}}(L(e_j f))=\Phi_{j,\sigma_s}(f)\quad(f\in C_j),$$ then the specified map $J_{j,\sigma}$ satisfies the same equation. If there is no such $L$, require $J_{j,\sigma}=0$. Every equality here is required for every identification of the moduli Hermitian space with $W_j$ of the indicated type.

At a disallowed $\sigma_s$, use instead the complex conjugate of the algebraic adelic function at the conjugate coefficient embedding. If some unit tuple $v$ and some $\sigma_s$-semilinear map from $C_j$ into $H_{j,\sigma}$ evaluate to that conjugate function, then there is a unit tuple $v$ such that the map induced by $J_{j,\sigma}e_j$ evaluates to it. If no such unit tuple and semilinear map exist, require $J_{j,\sigma}=0$.

Hypothesis (geometric finite-group action). Let $O_j$ be the one-step coefficient realization in ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)) and let $A_j(g)$ be the induced operator on algebraic scalar rules. If all $A_j(g)$ preserve $C_j$, require $$e_j(A_j(g)f)=\rho_j(g)e_j(f)\quad(g\in G,\ f\in C_j).$$ If this preservation fails, every $\rho_j(g)$ is the identity. The implication has one antecedent asserting preservation for all $g$.

For allowed $\sigma_s$ and any chosen domain scalars $c$, define the equivariance condition as follows. For every $g\in G$, every finite-level lift $k_g$ of its specified residue matrix, and every $f\in C_j$, the form $A_j(g)f$ lies in $C_j$ and there is $a\in K_s$ with $$\jmath(a)=\prod_{P\in P_g}\chi_{j+1,P}(\det e_G(g)_P),\qquad \Phi_{j,\sigma_s,c}(A_j(g)f)(h)=\sigma_s(a)\Phi_{j,\sigma_s,c}(f)(hk_g)\quad(h\in G_j(\mathbb A_F)).$$ If some domain scalars of modulus one satisfy this condition, the fixed domain scalars $c_{j,\sigma_s}$ satisfy it.

Use the one-map comparison defined in ([Geometric theta class evaluations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.OMPins/)). If the distinguished one-step coefficient realization admits some domain scalars and phase satisfying that comparison, then it admits the fixed domain scalars with some phase. Under the same antecedent it satisfies the comparison with both the fixed domain scalars and the specified phase $\mathrm{ph}_j$.

## Geometric theta class evaluations

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D216](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.OMPins/)

Use ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)) for the one-step coefficient realizations, their finite class sets, class lattices, coefficient maps, deeper moduli and scalar rules. Use ([Polarized modules and unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)), ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)), ([Adelic frames for unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.Core/)), ([Canonical derivative charts for theta sections](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.IsNab/)), ([Analytic realizations of rational Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.BaseW/)) and ([One-map and geometric comparison laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)). Write $K$ for the coefficient field, $K_s$ for the original moduli field, $K'\subseteq\mathbb C$ for the one-step coefficient field, $\sigma':K'\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ for its inclusion, and $\mathcal C_t$ for the finite class set. All section evaluations below are the scalar-rule evaluations defined in ([Complex uniformizations and period lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.FD.Data/)).

Here is the theta-class evaluation used in the comparison. For an $\mathcal O_E$-module $L\subset E^n$, put $L^- =L\cap\mathcal O_E^n$ and $$z_L(N)=\#(L/N\mathcal O_E^n)^{p+q},\qquad i_L(N)=[L^-:N\mathcal O_E^n]^{p+q}.$$ The indices mean the index of the indicated subgroup in the ambient module. At a framed point $y$ over a coefficient algebra $S$, a short theta value for a finite coefficient $w$, polynomial $P$ and denominator $N$ is an element $v\in S$ with the following property. After a faithfully flat coefficient extension $S\to S'$ and the induced framed point $y'$, choose tuples $z_i\in A_{y'}^n$, indexed by $1\le i\le z_L(N)$, that remain distinct after every residue-field extension. They are killed by $N^2$. For every matrix $\mu$ carrying $L$ into $\mathcal O_E^n$ and every integral matrix $(c_{ab})$ equal to $N\mu$, require $\sum_b[c_{ab}]z_{i,b}=0$ for every $a$.

For every finite-level tuple $q\in(\Lambda/\mathfrak b\Lambda)^n$, choose $t_{q,b}$ with $[N]t_{q,b}$ equal to its level point and killed by some power of $\mathfrak b$. Starting with the generating polarization section, pull it through the normalized multiplication-by-$N$ isomorphism and then apply symmetric theta-group lifts at $z_{i,b}$ and $t_{q,b}$. Denote the resulting section of $\mathscr L^{N^2}$ by $F_{q,i,b}$. The multiplication isomorphism is normalized by the polarization's zero rigidification; the symmetric lifts have parameter $N^2$. Using the chosen derivative operation $\nabla$ from ([Canonical derivative charts for theta sections](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.IsNab/)), require $$v=\frac{1}{i_L(N)\,\#(\Lambda/\mathfrak b\Lambda)^n\,N^{\deg P}}\sum_q w(q)\sum_i\sum_{\alpha\in\operatorname{supp}P}P_\alpha\prod_{b=1}^n\nabla_{y'}(N^2,F_{q,i,b},\alpha_b)$$ after scalar extension to $S'$. The multiindex $\alpha_b$ is the part of $\alpha$ belonging to column $b$. This is an existence relation; no uniqueness is assumed in its definition.

For the one-step deeper moduli datum at tuple $t$, let $\delta_t$ be its nonvanishing scalar section and $\varepsilon_t:K'\simeq K'_t$ its fixed coefficient-field identification. Say that a scalar rule $f$ is the theta class associated with $x=(x_c)_{c\in\mathcal C_t}$ when the following holds over every algebraically closed compatible coefficient field and every deeper framed point $y_2$ over an original framed point $y$. There are short theta values $v_c$ formed using the deeper derivative operation, $\varepsilon_t(w_{t,c})$, the lattice $L_{t,c}$, denominator $N_{t,c}$ and polynomial $\varepsilon_t(\operatorname{io}(x_c))$, such that $$f(y)=\delta_t(y_2)\sum_{c\in\mathcal C_t}\frac{\operatorname{vol}_t\,\operatorname{ep}_{t,c}}{\gamma_{t,c}}v_c.$$ Being a deeper framed point over $y$ means equality of the underlying moduli point under the prescribed map, equality of the transported tangent frame, and an isomorphism of the pulled-back polarization line bundle preserving its rigidification and its generating section.

Hypothesis (one-map comparison). Let $\mathcal C$ be the original domain labels and let $c_\eta$ and $\mathrm{ph}$ be complex numbers. Require $|c_\eta|=|\mathrm{ph}|=1$. There is a single $z\in Z(G)$ such that, for every $t$, there is a root of unity $\zeta_t$ in the distinguished complex image of $K$ with the following property. For every placed input $x$, its theta class has a representative in the specified algebraic cusp-form space. Every section $f$ of the indicated weight and scalar degree representing that theta class satisfies $$\mathrm{ph}\,\theta_t^{\rm ad}(x)(q)=\zeta_t\,c_{\delta(q)}\,\ell_j\,\Phi_j^\circ(A_j(z)f)(q)$$ at every class $q$ in the adelic quotient by the rational unitary group. Here $\delta(q)$ is the component chosen by the adelic frame map, $\ell_j$ is the prescribed Lie factor, and $\Phi_j^\circ$ is adelization before multiplying by either the domain scalar or the Lie factor. The central element is shared by all tuples and inputs.

The one-map comparison also requires distinguished-embedding equivariance and rationality. Put $\Phi_{j,c}^\circ(f)(h)=c_{\delta(h)}\Phi_j^\circ(f)(h)$. For every $g\in G$ and every compatible finite-level lift $k_g$, the action $A_j(g)$ preserves the specified cusp space and $$\Phi_{j,c}^\circ(A_j(g)f)(h)=\left(\prod_{P\in P_g}\chi_{j+1,P}(\det e_G(g)_P)\right)\Phi_{j,c}^\circ(f)(hk_g).$$ For every rational base-tuple coefficient vector $x$, there are finitely many $a_s\in K$ and algebraic cusp forms $f_s\in C_j$ such that $$\mathrm{ph}\,\operatorname{ev}_{v_j}(\theta_{j,t_0}(\iota_{j,t_0}x))(h)=\sum_s\jmath(a_s)\,\ell_j\,\Phi_{j,c}^\circ(f_s)(h)$$ for every adelic $h$.

Require also that the image of $K$ is contained in $K'$. Every cusp form over $K'$ of every nonnegative weight and scalar degree is a finite $K'$-linear combination, in evaluations at all compatible framed points, of cusp forms over $K_s$ of the same weight. Every such scalar-extension expression for sections is preserved by each level operator $A_j(g)$.

Hypothesis (pinned one-step realizations). At each selected stage the distinguished one-step field $K'$ is exactly the complex image of $K$. The following conditions hold, with the integral embedding $\iota$ and base frames of ([Scalar normalizations and adelic period identifications](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.LawsJ/)).
