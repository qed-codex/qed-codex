## Group and rational comparison compatibility

[Definition / hypothesis PHT.D176](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.G.GroupCompatibilityAll/)

Hypothesis (group and rational comparison compatibility). Use ([Local group completions and stabilizers](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Herm.GLevTie/)), ([Local polynomial lattice settings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.GAtP/)), ([Integral level lattices and their class representatives](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ColLevel/)), and ([Local lattice, orbit-count and gain conditions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.LinkG_at/)). For each source word $u$, choose a Hermitian integral level $L_u^h$ and the stage-zero coordinate and value identifications in ([Coordinate lattices and adelic column values](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Forms.AtTTied/)), whose class level is denoted $L_u^f$. Require the completion and stabilizer identifications for $L_u^h$ at every coefficient place $w$ and every admissible local datum there. The rational form values and finite-level character below belong to $L_u^f$; the local representatives and the final constructed datum belong to $L_u^h$. In this statement $p$ is the residue characteristic of $w$, $K$ is the coefficient number field, $e:K\hookrightarrow K_w$ is the specified comparison embedding, and $\iota_\infty:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ is the specified complex embedding.

Choose a single $B\in\mathrm{GL}_k(E)$ such that $$\bar B^{\mathsf T}hB=1,\qquad x\in L_0\ \Longleftrightarrow\ B^{-1}x\in\mathcal O_E^k.$$ Here $L_0$ is the specified initial self-dual coordinate lattice. At every local datum the frames $f_{\mathfrak p}$ in ([Local group completions and stabilizers](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Herm.GLevTie/)) equal $\operatorname{pr}_1s_{\mathfrak p}(B)$.

Write $\rho:(v,s)\mapsto\tau$ for the specified bijection from local factors and their Frobenius positions to the selected complex embeddings. Put $$t_\tau=\iota_E\circ\sigma_\tau:E\longrightarrow K,\qquad i_v=e_v^{-1}\circ\operatorname{pr}_1s_{\mathfrak p_v}|_E.$$ For every $v$, $0\le s<n_v$ and $a\in E$, require $$e\bigl(t_{\rho(v,s)}(a)\bigr)=\iota_v\sigma_v^s(i_v(a))\quad(s\text{ even}),\qquad e\bigl(t_{\rho(v,s)}(\bar a)\bigr)=\iota_v\sigma_v^s(i_v(a))\quad(s\text{ odd}).$$ For every vector $x$ in the initial rational form space, every local component $i$ and every tuple $L$ of local submodules, choose a polynomial $q_{x,i,L}$ over $K$ in the matrix blocks $(v,s)$ of ([Local polynomial lattice settings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.GAtP/)). For lattice tuples, coefficient extension by $e$ takes $q_{x,i,L}$ to the specified local comparison value of $x$.

The polynomials must recover the complex form values as follows. Let $g_v\in\mathrm{GL}_k(Q_v)$ satisfy $L_v=g_vW_v^k$. Let $c$ be an initial global class, $\gamma\in G(F)$ and $\kappa$ in its initial finite level, and suppose that the finite adele $a=\gamma y_c\kappa$ has the components $$a_{\mathfrak q}=(y_i)_{\mathfrak q}\quad(\mathfrak q\nmid p),\qquad \operatorname{pr}_1s_{\mathfrak p_v}(a)=\operatorname{pr}_1s_{\mathfrak p_v}(B)\,e_v(g_v)\,\operatorname{pr}_1s_{\mathfrak p_v}(B)^{-1}.$$ For any invertible complex frames $z_\tau,z'_\tau$ with $z_\tau=\tau(\gamma)z'_\tau$, require $$\mathcal E_B\bigl(\iota_\infty(q_{x,i,L});z\bigr)=\chi(\kappa)F_x(c,z').$$ The value $F_x(c,z')$ is the initial complex form value, after the specified stage-zero frame identification; $\chi$ is its initial finite-level character. To define the expression on the left, let $\lambda^*_{v,s}$ be the even row $\lambda_{v,s}$ and the negative reversed odd row, and let $b_{v,s}$ be its last entry. At $\tau=\rho(v,s)$ set $$Z_{v,s}=\begin{cases}\tau(B)^{-1}z_\tau,&s\text{ even},\\ \bigl((\tau(B)^{-1}z_\tau)^{-1}\bigr)^{\mathsf T}\text{ with columns reversed},&s\text{ odd}. \end{cases}$$ Then $\mathcal E_B(Q;z)=(\prod_{v,s}\det Z_{v,s}^{\,b_{v,s}})Q((Z_{v,s})_{v,s})$.

For $i$, $\eta\in\Gamma_i$ and an initial-level element $\kappa$ such that $\kappa_{\mathfrak p}=1$ over $p$ and $\kappa_{\mathfrak q}=(y_i^{-1}\gamma_i(\eta)y_i)_{\mathfrak q}$ away from $p$, require an $r\in K$ with $$\iota_\infty(r)=\chi(\kappa),\qquad \chi_i(\eta)e(r)=1\text{ in }K_w.$$

Two further requirements hold for every $u,w$ and admissible local datum. First, for every factor $v$, use the following fixed reference rings with $n=n_v$: $$W_n=W(\mathbb F_{p^n}),\quad Q_n=\operatorname{Frac}W_n,\quad O_n=W(\mathbb F_{p^{2n}})[T]/(T^2-p),\quad K_n=\operatorname{Frac}O_n.$$ The inclusion $j_n:W_n\to O_n$ is induced by the chosen quadratic extension of finite fields; write $\omega=T$ and $\iota_n:Q_n\to K_n$ for the extended inclusion. In a fixed algebraic closure of $K_n$, choose an embedding $\tau_{w,n}:K\to\overline K_n$ inducing the coefficient place $w$. Put $K_{w,n}=K_n(\tau_{w,n}(K))$ and let $O_{w,n}$ be the integral closure of $O_n$ in $K_{w,n}$. There must be ring isomorphisms $$W_n\simeq W_v,\quad Q_n\simeq Q_v,\quad O_{w,n}\simeq O_w,\quad K_{w,n}\simeq K_w$$ commuting with both fraction-field maps, carrying the canonical map $Q_n\to K_{w,n}$ to $\iota_v$, the canonical map $W_n\to O_{w,n}$ to $j_v$, and $\omega$ to the distinguished $w_0\in O_w$. The reference integral closure is finite over $O_n$, complete for its maximal ideal, and has fraction field $K_{w,n}$; its two inclusion maps commute.

Second, the chosen local datum must equal a datum constructed from the integral level as follows. There are common $W,Q,O,K'$ and maps $\iota,\sigma,j,w_0$ satisfying the single-factor ring and Frobenius conditions of ([Local polynomial lattice settings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.GAtP/)), in particular $p\ge5$ and even $n\ge2$, integers $a,r,m,n,e_0\ge0$ with $m+1=k$, dominant rows $\lambda_{v,s}$ of length $k$ for $v<a$, $s<n$, and common-completion identifications satisfying the additional level condition of ([Local group completions and stabilizers](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Herm.GLevTie/)). Choose a character $\psi:U^{(p)}\to O^\times$. The constructed datum has these coefficient rings, $a$ identical local rings $W\subset Q$, Frobenius $\sigma$, length $n$ in every factor, the specified $r,\lambda$, and exponent $e_0$. Its component set is exactly $G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A_{F,f})/U^{(p)}$, with the fixed chosen representatives $y_i$ whose components over $p$ are one. Its group at $i$ is $$\Gamma_i=G(F)\cap y_iU^{(p)}y_i^{-1},$$ acting at $v$ by $f_{\mathfrak p_v}^{-1}\operatorname{pr}_1s_{\mathfrak p_v}(\gamma)f_{\mathfrak p_v}$ transported through $e_v^{-1}$, with character $\gamma\mapsto\psi(y_i^{-1}\gamma y_i)$. Equality here includes the component set, groups, characters, local maps, weights, ranks and exponent.
