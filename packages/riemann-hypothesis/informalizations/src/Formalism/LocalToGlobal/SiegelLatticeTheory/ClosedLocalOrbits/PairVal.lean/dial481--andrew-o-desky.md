## Operators and coefficients of geometric moment terms

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D206](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.TermOp.aTerm/)

Hypothesis (operators of geometric moment terms). Use the terms, flags, lattices and cell labels of ([Geometric moment terms for spectral realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.TermDef.Term/)), and the adelic groups, coordinate maps, measures, column representatives, finite representations $\pi_u$ and coefficient functions $W_u$ of ([Spectral column and period realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.HidaSetting.HidaSetting/)). The moment datum of ([Moment data for the family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.S.Draft.SData/)) is identified, by the isometries in ([Spectral column and period realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.HidaSetting.HidaSetting/)), with the following construction.

For a row lattice $\Lambda$ put $$w_u(\Lambda)=|\operatorname{Aut}(\Lambda,F_u;X_u^\perp/X_u)|^{-1},\qquad M_u=\sum_{\Lambda\in\mathcal R_u}w_u(\Lambda).$$ The automorphism set here consists of the maps on $X_u^\perp/X_u$ induced by lattice-preserving Hermitian isometries which preserve every $X_{u,j}$ and fix $z_u$. Cardinalities use their natural value, with infinite cardinality sent to zero; division is total.

Take the Hilbert direct sum $C_u=\bigoplus_\rho\pi_u$ over the chosen finite column representatives. Let $\operatorname{sh}_u$ send a column functional to the Riesz vector of its evaluation at each representative. This is conjugate linear. Give the functional space the inner product $$\langle T,T'\rangle=\langle\operatorname{sh}_uT',\operatorname{sh}_uT\rangle.$$ The injectivity and surjectivity of these maps are assumptions of ([Spectral column and period realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.HidaSetting.HidaSetting/)). The common moment space is $\mathscr H_0=\operatorname{im}(R_eR_\varphi)$ inside the square-integrable automorphic space of ([Spectral column and period realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.HidaSetting.HidaSetting/)), and its column maps are the chosen linear Poincaré summation maps into $\mathscr H_0$. Its two volume functions are the supplied positive nilpotent and stabilizer volumes. Its signature-measure constants are the chosen positive constants identifying the signature Haar measures with the coordinate measures in ([Period, measure and twist comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Ties/)).

For an adelic lift $a$ represented by $(x,\gamma)$, let $N_a$ consist of infinite components of rational pairs $(n_\infty,n_f)$ with rational isometry $n\in N_\Lambda(F_u)$ and $\gamma^{-1}n\gamma\in N_{\Lambda'}(F_{u'})$. Choose a measurable fundamental domain $\Omega_a$ for its left translates on the infinite unitary group when one exists; choose the empty set otherwise. A fundamental domain here meets every orbit and has the literal uniqueness condition: if $y,ny\in\Omega_a$ with $n\in N_a$, then $n=1$. Define $$O_a=\int_{\Omega_a}|W_u(y)\rangle\langle W_{u'}(x_\infty^{-1}y)|\,d\mu_\infty(y),$$ where $|v\rangle\langle w|$ sends $\xi$ to $\langle w,\xi\rangle v$ and the integral is the Bochner integral of continuous linear maps. The defining Bochner integral has value zero when the integrand is not integrable on the chosen set.

For column representatives $h_\rho,h_{\rho'}$, with the designated finite frame shifts $x_u,x_{u'}$, put $$k_a(\rho,\rho')=(h_\rho x_u)^{-1}x_f(h_{\rho'}x_{u'}),\qquad c_u=\frac{\operatorname{vol}(K_2)\,\mu_{N,u}(F_{N,u})\,\mu_{H,u}(H_u)}{\dim\pi_u},\qquad b_{u,u'}=\frac{\operatorname{vol}(K_2)}{\sqrt{c_uc_{u'}}}.$$ Measures in $c_u$ are converted from extended nonnegative reals to real numbers. The $(\rho,\rho')$ block of the operator of $a$ is $$b_{u,u'}\chi_2(k_a(\rho,\rho'))O_a$$ if the two column lattices are $\Lambda,\Lambda'$ and $k_a(\rho,\rho')\in K_2$, and is zero otherwise. Sum these blocks to obtain $A_a:C_{u'}\to C_u$.

The rational-term operator is the finite sum of these operators over all adelic lifts of the term, transported by $\operatorname{sh}_u^{-1}A_a\operatorname{sh}_{u'}$. Finiteness of each fibre is required in ([Spectral column and period realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.HidaSetting.HidaSetting/)).

The vector attached to a lift has only one potentially nonzero component, indexed by its ordered pair of lattices, in the Hilbert direct sum of Hilbert–Schmidt operator spaces. That component is $$\sqrt{w_u(\Lambda)w_{u'}(\Lambda')}\,b_{u,u'}\chi_N(k_a(\rho_\Lambda,\rho_{\Lambda'}))O_a.$$ Here $\rho_\Lambda$ is the distinguished column representative for $\Lambda$. The character $\chi_N$ is $$\chi_N(k)=\psi_E^f\left(g^{-1}\sum_{a=0}^{s-1}h_A(f_a^0,k n_a^0)\right),$$ with $n_a^0=e_{a+1}^0$ for $a+1<s$ and $n_{s-1}^0=z^0$. The vectors $e_a^0,f_a^0,z^0$ are the chosen finite test vectors, and $h_A$ and $\psi_E^f$ are the finite adelic Hermitian form and additive character, in ([Spectral column and period realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.HidaSetting.HidaSetting/)).

A lift's row label is its two lattices together with the tuple of $k$ by $k$ matrices whose $(i,j)$ entry at residue place $v$ is $\operatorname{red}_v(h_A(b_i',k_a b_j))$, where $b_i',b_j$ are the chosen finite test vectors. The reduction is the chosen reduction on the integral finite-adeles and has value zero outside that subring. Use the identity matrix for a resulting block whose determinant is not a unit. A pair of rational terms is allowed exactly when some lifts have the same row label. Its pair value is $$p(t,t')=\sum_{\substack{a\mapsto t,\ a'\mapsto t'\\\operatorname{row}(a)=\operatorname{row}(a')}}\langle v_{a'},v_a\rangle.$$ Its mass is $w_u(\Lambda)/M_u$ for the lattice of its first term. To define the remaining two pair attributes, choose the source's fixed such lifts and put $\beta=\gamma'\gamma^{-1}$.

For the resulting flags $F,F',F''$, let $U'_E$ and $U''_E$ be the $E$-spans of the two modules $U$ in ([Geometric moment terms for spectral realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.TermDef.Term/)). Let $H'=(U'_E)^\perp$, $H''=(U''_E)^\perp$ and $$T=\{(x,y)\in H'\times H'':x-y\in X(F),\ \beta x-y\in X(F'')\}.$$ The integer attribute is the natural subtraction $s_0=k-\dim_E\operatorname{pr}_1T$. For the scalar predicate put $$Y_1=U'_E\cap(X(F)+Ez)^\perp,\quad Y'_1=U'_E\cap(X(F')+Ez')^\perp,$$ and define $Y_2,Y'_2$ with $F''$ in place of $F'$. For every $y_1\in Y_1,y'_1\in Y'_1,y_2\in Y_2,y'_2\in Y'_2$ with $y_1-y_2\in X(F)$ and $y'_2-\beta y'_1\in X(F'')$, and every $a,b\in E$, require the equivalence $$y_1-y'_1-az-bz'\in X(F)+X(F')\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad y_2-y'_2-az-bz''\in X(F)+X(F'').$$ This is the scalar predicate itself; it imposes no extra equation $s_0=1$. Independence of these pair attributes from the chosen lifts is required in ([Spectral column and period realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.HidaSetting.HidaSetting/)).

To specify the scalar coefficient of a term, a normalized representative at $w$ means a representative flag with the identity admissibility witness and $Z^\circ=L C(w)R$ for lower and upper unitriangular $L,R$. Its phase is $$\epsilon(L,R)=\exp\left(-\frac{2\pi\mathrm i}{g}\operatorname{Tr}_{E/\mathbb Q}\left\{\sum_{a=0}^{s-1}L_{a+1,a}+\sum_{a=0}^{s-1}R_{a,a+1}\right\}\right).$$ Choose such a representative and factors when they exist; otherwise its phase is zero.

Here is the local normalization needed for the volume factor. At a place $p$ of $F$, put $g_p=g_i$ if its residue characteristic is one of $g_0,g_1$, and $g_p=1$ otherwise. With the chosen local scalar $\delta_p$, define $d_{p,a}=((\delta_p\iota_E(g_p/g))^{s-a})^{-1}$ and $$Y_p(Z)_{ab}=\bar d_{p,a}\iota_E(Z_{ab})d_{p,b}.$$ At a nonsplit local model set $\mathcal Z_p(Z)=Y_p(Z)$. At a split model with chosen $E_p\simeq F_p\times F_p$ and uniformizer $q_p$, define $$\mathcal Z_p(Z)_{ab}=\left(\frac{Y_p(Z)_{ab}^{(1)}}{q_p},-\frac{Y_p(Z)_{ba}^{(2)}}{q_p}\right)-\mathbf1_{a=b=s}\left(-\frac{T_u}{q_p},\frac{T_{u'}}{q_p}\right),$$ interpreting the ordered pair through that split isomorphism.

Let $I_w$ be the subgroup generated by all lower unitriangular $A\in\mathrm{GL}_{s+1}(E)$ admitting an upper unitriangular $B$ with $A C(w)B=C(w)$ and the following property. At every $p$ there are lower and upper unitriangular matrices $l_p,r_p$, with entries in the local model ring $R_p$, for which $$\mathcal Z_p\bigl((A Z^\circ B)^\circ\bigr)=l_p\mathcal Z_p(Z)r_p\quad\text{for every }Z.$$ Here $R_p$ is the local integral ring at a nonsplit model; in a split model it corresponds to the pairs $(a,b)\in\mathcal O_{F_p}^2$ with $a\equiv b\pmod{q_p}$. For a normalized representative $F',L,R$, let $J_{t,F',L}$ be the subgroup generated by matrices $L^{-1}U^*L$ for which a common element $n\in N_\Lambda(F)\cap N_\Lambda(F')$ acts on the appended frame of $F$ by the matrix $U$.

For relevant generic $t$ admitting some normalized representative at some cell, choose one and set $$v(t)=\frac{[I_w:I_w\cap J_{t,F',L}]}{[J_{t,F',L}:I_w\cap J_{t,F',L}]}.$$ Each infinite index has natural value zero. In every other case set $v(t)=1$. If $t$ is at some cell, choose such a cell and set $c_t$ equal to its phase times $v(t)$; if it is at no cell set $c_t=0$.

Finally, for each lattice $\Lambda$ count the orbits of admissible lifted flags $F'$ with nonzero $G$, identity admissibility witness, genericity, and exact Gram matrix $Z$. The acting group is the subgroup of $N_\Lambda(F)$ fixing every $e_a$ and $z$. Convert an infinite orbit count to zero, and call the resulting natural count $n_\Lambda(Z)$. The mean-count function is $$N_{u,u'}(w)=\frac{\sum_{\Lambda\in\mathcal R_u}w_u(\Lambda)n_\Lambda(C(w)^\circ)}{M_u}.$$ All identifications of term attributes, operators, pair values, masses, scalar coefficients and mean counts are the ones required by ([Spectral column and period realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.HidaSetting.HidaSetting/)).
