## Geometric moment terms for spectral realizations

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D207](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.TermDef.Term/)

Hypothesis (geometric moment terms). The term sets and geometric predicates used in ([Spectral column and period realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.HidaSetting.HidaSetting/)) are identified with the following constructions. Their operators and pair values are specified in ([Operators and coefficients of geometric moment terms](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.TermOp.aTerm/)). Use the initial Hermitian space $V=Y_0$ over $E/F$, its form $h$, source words $u$ and source primes $T_u$ from ([Hermitian spaces in the theta constructions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.CMData/)). Put $s=m/2-1$ and $g=g_0g_1$.

A flag of norm $T$ consists of linearly independent vectors $e_0,\ldots,e_{s-1}$ and a vector $z$, with $h(e_a,e_b)=0$, $h(e_a,z)=0$, and $h(z,z)=-T$. Write $X_j=\langle e_a:a<j\rangle_E$, $X=X_s$, and append $z$ to obtain its frame $v_0,\ldots,v_s$. A lifted flag also has a chosen vector $\widetilde z$; the lift condition on a lattice $\Lambda$ is $z\in\Lambda$ and $\widetilde z=z$. A base flag is regarded as lifted by setting $\widetilde z=z$.

For a lifted flag $F$, put $X_\Lambda(F)=\Lambda\cap X(F)$, $L(F)=\Lambda\cap X(F)^\perp$ and $L^+(F)=(L(F)+X(F))\cap X(F)^\perp$. The fixed-lattice convention at $F$ is the following. At every prime $P$ of $E$, some nonzero scalars $c_a$ give $$X_\Lambda(F)_P=\left(\sum_a\mathcal O_Ec_ae_a\right)_P,$$ with $c_a=1$ for all $a$ if $P\mid g$. Also $cz\in L^+(F)$ implies $c\in\mathcal O_E$. For a row lattice require a full unimodular lattice in the genus of the fixed lattice, this convention, the lift condition, and $$X_\Lambda(F)=\sum_{a=0}^{s-1}\mathfrak d^{-(s-a)}e_a,$$ where $\mathfrak d$ is the extension to $E$ of the different of $F/\mathbb Q$.

For flags $F,F'$ define their Gram matrix $Z_{ab}=h(v_a,v_b')$, let $D_j$ be its leading $j$ by $j$ minor, and put $$G(F,F')=D_{s+1}\bar D_{s+1}-TT'D_s\bar D_s,\qquad Z^\circ_{ab}=(-1)^{s-b}Z_{ab}.$$ An admissible lifted flag $F'$ satisfies the lift condition on $\Lambda$ and has an upper unitriangular matrix $\xi\in M_{s+1}(\mathcal O_E)$ for which every difference $v_b'-\sum_av_a\xi_{ab}$ belongs to $(g\Lambda)_P$ at every $P\mid g$. It also satisfies the fixed-lattice convention just given and $X_\Lambda(F')=\sum_a\mathfrak d^{-(s-a)}e_a'$.

Let $N_\Lambda(F)$ be the lattice-preserving Hermitian isometries $n$ which satisfy $(n-1)X_{j+1}\subseteq X_j$ for all $j\ge0$ and $(n-1)X^\perp\subseteq X$. Two admissible lifted flags $F_1,F_2$ are related when some $n\in N_\Lambda(F)$ and $n'\in N_\Lambda(F_1)$ take every frame vector of $F_1$ and its chosen $\widetilde z_1$ to those of $F_2$ by $nn'$. Use the equivalence relation generated by these relations. A rational geometric term consists of a row lattice $\Lambda$ in the chosen finite row set and one such class of admissible lifted flags of norm $T_{u'}$, relative to the base flag at $u$.

The scalar $G$ of a term is computed at the source's fixed representative of this quotient. The remaining predicates below mean that there exists a representative with the stated property. The distinguished small-cell predicate is $$e_a'-e_a\in X_a\quad(0\le a<s),\qquad z'-z\in X.$$ The identity predicate means that some $n\in N_\Lambda(F)$ takes every $e_a$ to $e_a'$ and has $nz-z'\in X$. The equal-frame predicate means that some such $n$ takes every appended frame vector to its partner exactly.

For relevance, let $\mathfrak n_F$ be the infinitesimal Hermitian isometries $Y$ which lower every $X_j$ to $X_{j-1}$, $1\le j\le s$, and send $X^\perp$ into $X$. Write $$\ell_F(Y)=\sum_{a=0}^{s-1}\operatorname{coef}_{e_a}(Yv_{a+1}),$$ where the coefficient is the unique coordinate in $X$ when the vector lies in $X$, and zero otherwise. The flags are relevant when $\operatorname{Tr}_{E/F}\ell_F(Y)=\operatorname{Tr}_{E/F}\ell_{F'}(Y)$ for every $Y\in\mathfrak n_F\cap\mathfrak n_{F'}$.

Here are the integral predicates. Put $$U=X_\Lambda(F)+\mathcal O_E\widetilde z+X_\Lambda(F')+\mathcal O_E\widetilde z',\qquad B=\Lambda\cap\langle U\rangle_E,$$ and, for an admissibility witness $\xi$, put $$U_\xi^+=X_\Lambda(F)+\mathcal O_E\widetilde z+\sum_b\mathcal O_Eg^{-1}\left(v_b'-\sum_av_a\xi_{ab}\right).$$ Generic means that one admissibility witness satisfies $B_P=(U_\xi^+)_P$ at every $P\mid g$ and $B_P=U_P$ at every $P\nmid g$. The local nongeneric predicate at a place $p$ of $F$ means that for some $P\mid p$ these equalities fail: at $P\mid g$ they fail for every admissibility witness, and at $P\nmid g$ one has $B_P\ne U_P$.

A cell label $w$ is a point of size $s+1$ in ([Moment data for the family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.S.Draft.SData/)), with matrix $C(w)$. A term is at $w$ when some representative has $G\ne0$ and $$Z^\circ=L C(w)R$$ for lower and upper unitriangular matrices $L,R$ over $E$. For every term, its designated point is a fixed occurring cell label when the term is relevant and such a label exists; in all other cases it is the source's fixed label whose cell matrix has zero value under the displayed determinant expression for $G$. This fallback does not assert that the term itself has $G=0$.

An adelic geometric lift of a rational term additionally chooses a second row lattice $\Lambda'$ in the row set at $u'$ and a class of pairs $(x,\gamma)$. Here $x$ is an adelic unitary element and $\gamma$ a rational Hermitian isometry taking $\Lambda'$ to $\Lambda$, with $\gamma F_{u'}$ admissible. In the coordinates and local signature frames of ([Spectral column and period realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.HidaSetting.HidaSetting/)), the infinite and finite matrices of $x$ are the images of the same rational unitary matrix representing $\gamma$. Pairs are identified by $\gamma\mapsto n\gamma n'$ for $n\in N_\Lambda(F_u)$ and $n'\in N_{\Lambda'}(F_{u'})$, using the relation generated by these transformations. The rational term underneath is the class of $\gamma F_{u'}$. Fixed representatives of both quotient constructions are part of these definitions; assertions that their derived values are independent of representatives are stated separately in ([Spectral column and period realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.HidaSetting.HidaSetting/)).
