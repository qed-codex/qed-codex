## Normalised Haar measures on the adelic unitary groups of Hermitian spaces over the CM field, and the volume function of a datum

[Definition / hypothesis ANT.D269](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.StandardLattice.IsVolMeas/)

Let $D$ be a datum, as in ([Datum of the construction and objects over it](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.EX.OB.Obj/)), with its CM field $E$, the maximal real subfield $F$ and $f=[F:\mathbb Q]$, as in ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)). Conjugation on $E$ is written $x\mapsto\bar x$. The datum has an index set $\mathcal T$ of cardinality $f$, identified with the selected CM type in ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)); write $\tau_\rho\colon E\to\mathbb C$ for the embedding attached to $\rho\in\mathcal T$. The datum also contains a positive real-valued function $\operatorname{vol}$ on Hermitian spaces with recorded lattice data over $E$, and Haar measures $\mu^{\mathrm{cd}}_{P,Q}$ on the groups $U(P,Q)$ with the compact-dual normalization of ([Formal degree with compact-dual normalization](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.RgFd.B.formalDegreeCompactDual/)). Here $$U(P,Q)=\{b\in\mathrm{GL}_{P+Q}(\mathbb C):b^*J_{P,Q}\,b=J_{P,Q}\},\qquad J_{P,Q}=\operatorname{diag}(1_P,-1_Q).$$

**Hermitian spaces.** Let $W$ be a Hermitian space with recorded lattice data over $E$, in the sense of ([Hermitian spaces in the theta constructions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.CMData/)): it has a rank $d$, a basis $(v_i)_{0\le i<d}$, a self-dual $\mathcal O_E$-lattice $L_0$ and, for every embedding $\tau\colon E\to\mathbb C$, a recorded signature. Write $G_W=(h(v_i,v_j))_{i,j}$ for its Gram matrix. We say that the recorded signatures of $W$ are *correct* if, for every $\tau$, the recorded signature at $\tau$ is the signature of the complex Hermitian matrix $\tau(G_W)$, and that $W$ *has signature $(P,Q)$* if its recorded signatures are correct and equal to $(P,Q)$ at every embedding of $E$ into $\mathbb C$.

**The adelic unitary group.** Let $\mathbb A_F=F_\infty\times\mathbb A_{F,f}$ be the adele ring of $F$, with $F_\infty=\prod_{v\mid\infty}F_v$. The *adelic unitary group* of $W$ is $$H_W(\mathbb A_F)=\{g\in\mathrm{GL}_d(\mathbb A_F\otimes_FE):\bar g^{\,T}G_W\,g=G_W\},$$ where conjugation acts on the factor $E$; it is a topological group, with its Borel sets. For $g\in H_W(\mathbb A_F)$ write $g_\infty\in\mathrm{GL}_d(F_\infty\otimes_FE)$ and $g_f\in\mathrm{GL}_d(\mathbb A_{F,f}\otimes_FE)$ for its two components. The subgroup $H_W(F)$ of *rational points* is the image of $\{g\in\mathrm{GL}_d(E):\bar g^{\,T}G_Wg=G_W\}$ under the map induced by $E\to\mathbb A_F\otimes_FE$. It acts on $H_W(\mathbb A_F)$ by left multiplication.

Let $\widehat{\mathcal O}_F\subset\mathbb A_{F,f}$ be the set of finite adeles that are integral at every finite place. The *adelic lattice* $\widehat L_0\subset(\mathbb A_{F,f}\otimes_FE)^d$ is the subgroup generated by the vectors $a\otimes x$ with $a\in\widehat{\mathcal O}_F$ and $x\in E^d$ the coordinate vector, in the basis $(v_i)$, of an element of $L_0$. Its stabiliser is $$K_W=\{g\in H_W(\mathbb A_F):g_f\,\widehat L_0=\widehat L_0\}.$$

**Normalised measures.** For $\rho\in\mathcal T$, an *extension of $\tau_\rho$* is a ring homomorphism $\theta\colon F_\infty\otimes_FE\to\mathbb C$ with $\theta(1\otimes x)=\tau_\rho(x)$ for $x\in E$ and with $a\mapsto\theta(a\otimes1)$ continuous on $F_\infty$. Let $P,Q\ge0$. A measure $\mu$ on $H_W(\mathbb A_F)$ is *normalised for $(P,Q)$* if the following hold.

1. $\mu$ is a Haar measure.
2. Every $\tau_\rho$ has an extension. For every choice of extensions $(\theta_\rho)_{\rho\in\mathcal T}$, there are complex matrices $A_\rho$ with $P+Q$ rows and $d$ columns such that $A_\rho^*J_{P,Q}A_\rho=\tau_\rho(G_W)$ for every $\rho$.
3. For every choice of $(\theta_\rho)$ and $(A_\rho)$ as in 2 and all measurable sets $B_\rho\subseteq U(P,Q)$, $$\mu\bigl\{g\in K_W:\text{for every }\rho\text{ there is }b\in B_\rho\text{ with }b\,A_\rho=A_\rho\,\theta_\rho(g_\infty)\bigr\}=\prod_{\rho\in\mathcal T}\mu^{\mathrm{cd}}_{P,Q}(B_\rho).$$

**The volume function gives the measures of fundamental domains.** This is the following condition on the datum. For all $P,Q\ge0$ and every Hermitian space $W$ with recorded lattice data over $E$ that has signature $(P,Q)$:

- there is a measure on $H_W(\mathbb A_F)$ normalised for $(P,Q)$;
- for every such measure $\mu$ there is a fundamental domain for the action of $H_W(F)$ on $H_W(\mathbb A_F)$ with respect to $\mu$, and every such fundamental domain $\mathcal F$ has $\mu(\mathcal F)=\operatorname{vol}(W)$.
