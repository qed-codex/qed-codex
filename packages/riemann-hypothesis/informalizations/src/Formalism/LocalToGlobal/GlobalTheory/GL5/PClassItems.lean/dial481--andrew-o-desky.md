## Finiteness of the definite unitary class set

[Theorem ANT.1244](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.GlobalTheoryTheory.GenusMassVolumes.class_number_finite/)

Choose an arithmetic datum satisfying ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)), with CM extension $E/F$. Let $W$ be a Hermitian space with recorded lattice data in the generic definition of ([Hermitian spaces in the theta constructions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.CMData/)), with every recorded signature equal to the actual signature of its Gram matrix, and suppose its signature at every complex embedding is $(\dim_EW,0)$. Let $L_0$ be the recorded standard integral lattice in $W$. Put $\widehat{\mathcal O}_F=\prod_{v<\infty}\mathcal O_{F_v}$ and let $\widehat L_0$ be the additive subgroup of $W\otimes_F\mathbb A_{F,f}$ generated by $x\otimes a$ with $x\in L_0$ and $a\in\widehat{\mathcal O}_F$. Set $$K_{L_0}=\{g\in U(W)(\mathbb A_F):g_f\widehat L_0=\widehat L_0\}.$$ There are $n\ge0$ and $g_1,\ldots,g_n\in U(W)(\mathbb A_F)$ such that $$U(W)(\mathbb A_F)=\bigcup_{i=1}^n U(W)(F)g_iK_{L_0}.$$ If $\gamma g_i k=\gamma'g_jk'$ with $\gamma,\gamma'\in U(W)(F)$ and $k,k'\in K_{L_0}$, then $i=j$.
