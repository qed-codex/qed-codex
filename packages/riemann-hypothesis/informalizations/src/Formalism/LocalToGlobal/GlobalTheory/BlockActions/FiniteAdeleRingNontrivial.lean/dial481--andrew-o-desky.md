## Integral adelic membership detects a global lattice

[Theorem ANT.0677](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.GlobalTheoryTheory.Br.latLocGlob/)

Let $E/F$ be an extension of number fields, $n\ge0$, and $\Lambda\subset E^n$ a finitely generated $\mathcal O_E$-submodule. Set $$A=E\otimes_F\mathbb A_{F,f},\qquad S=\text{the subring of }A\text{ generated by } \mathcal O_E\otimes1\text{ and }1\otimes\widehat{\mathcal O}_F.$$ Let $\Lambda_S$ be the $S$-submodule of $A^n$ generated by the image of $\Lambda$. If $x\in E^n$ has image in $\Lambda_S$, then $x\in\Lambda$.
