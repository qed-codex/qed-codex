## Coordinate lattices and adelic column values

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D169](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Forms.AtTTied/)

Use ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), ([Hermitian spaces in the theta constructions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.CMData/)) and ([Integral forms on column representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.MemberForms/)). Write $K$ for the coefficient field, $\jmath:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ for its embedding, $E/F$ for the CM extension, $E_0\subset E$ for the fixed quadratic subfield, and $\tau:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ for its CM labels. The finite coefficient places $w$ have valuations $\operatorname{val}_w$ and rational residual characteristics $\ell_w$. Put $N_i=k+i(t+1)$. All class lattices below are expressed in the fixed frame of $X_i$, with Gram matrix $G_i$. A dual lattice means the $\mathcal O_E$-span of the vectors pairing integrally with the given lattice.

The following conventions define the coordinate conditions used at a stage, or separately on each branch of classes. A branch is a fibre of a specified map from the class set to a finite set. At an endpoint use the whole class set as one branch. For local coefficient-lattice conditions let $\mathcal W$ be the specified set of permitted coefficient places. In the global integral-lattice version, $\mathcal W$ is the set of all coefficient places.

For a weakly decreasing integer weight $\lambda\in\mathbb Z^n$, put $d=\lambda_{n-1}$, with $d=0$ for $n=0$, and $\nu_a=\lambda_a-d$. Let $W_R(\lambda)=\Delta_\nu(R)$ and $H_\lambda=v_\nu$ in ([Polynomial Weyl modules and row substitutions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.GradedWeylDivisibility.dualWeyl/)), with both the number of rows and the number of columns equal to $n$. Across CM labels take products of these one-block polynomials. Denote their complex span by $\mathcal W_\lambda$. Their $K$-rational span is the additive subgroup generated by $\jmath(a)\prod_\tau P_\tau$, with $a\in K$ and $P_\tau\in W_{\mathbb Z}(\lambda_\tau)$. The $w$-integral span restricts $a$ to $\operatorname{val}_w(a)\ge0$.

For arbitrary complex matrix tuples $M$, invertible frame tuples $y$, a polynomial $P$ and integer shifts $d_\tau$, set $$F_{d,M,P}(y)=\left(\prod_\tau\det(M_\tau y_\tau)^{d_\tau}\right)P((M_\tau y_\tau)_\tau),$$ $$\mathcal N_\lambda(P,Q)=\frac{[P,\overline Q]_{\rm fac}}{\prod_\tau[H_{\lambda_\tau},\overline{H_{\lambda_\tau}}]_{\rm fac}},$$ using ([Bilinear factorial pairing of polynomials](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.G.Model.TS.fockR/)); bars on polynomials mean coefficientwise complex conjugation. This pairing is linear in its first argument. Integer powers use $0^0=1$ and $0^a=0$ for every nonzero integer $a$. A frame called orthonormal here satisfies $y_\tau^*G_\tau y_\tau=I$ or $-I$, independently at each $\tau$. A matrix $B\in\mathrm{GL}_n(E)$ is an $\ell$-local basis of a lattice $L$ if, for every $x\in E^n$, some positive integer $a$ prime to $\ell$ has $ax\in L$ if and only if some such integer makes every coordinate of $aB^{-1}x$ integral. The integer may differ between the two sides.

Hypothesis (coordinate levels and classes). Choose a full finitely generated integral lattice $\Lambda\subset E^n$ with $$T\Lambda^\#\subset\Lambda,\qquad[\Lambda^\#: \Lambda]=T^{2f}.$$ At every finite $F$-place choose a unitary lattice stabilizer subgroup $U_v$, equal to the full stabilizer away from $T$ and a specified set of bad places. At $v\mid T$ choose a module $Q_v$ over the integral local $E$-algebra, with underlying additive group of cardinality $T$, and a nonzero linear map $f_v:(\Lambda^\#)_v\to Q_v$. It kills $T(\Lambda^\#)_v$ and $\Lambda_v$. Require $U_v$ to be exactly the unitary lattice stabilizer fixing this map. Choose a compact adelic subgroup $U$, open in the unitary finite adelic group, whose elements are precisely the unitary matrices with components in $U_v$. Choose a character $\chi:U\to\mathbb C^\times$ trivial on elements with identity components at every bad place.

Choose representatives $y_c\in U(G)(\mathbb A_f)$ bijecting the prescribed classes with $U(G)(F)\backslash U(G)(\mathbb A_f)/U$. Their prescribed coordinate lattices satisfy $\widehat L_c=y_c\widehat\Lambda$. Embed every prescribed class stabilizer injectively into $U(G)(F)$, with image exactly $U(G)(F)\cap y_cUy_c^{-1}$. The same conditions define a branch presentation by restricting $c$ to that branch.

An equivariant adelic function $g(x,y)$ obeys $$g(\gamma x\kappa,y)=\chi(\kappa)g(x,y'),\qquad y=\gamma_\infty y',$$ for rational unitary $\gamma$ and $\kappa\in U$. Its weight-$\lambda$ value at every $x$ must be $F_{d,1,P}$ for $P\in\mathcal W_\lambda$, with $d_\tau=\lambda_{\tau,n-1}$. The corresponding class form space consists of restrictions at the $y_c$.

Hypothesis (split levels). At each $v\mid g_a$, $a=0,1$, choose a splitting $E\otimes_FF_v\simeq F_v\times F_v$ that interchanges the two factors under conjugation and sends $1\otimes b$ to $(b,b)$. Let $\phi_{a,v}$ be its first component. Choose $\pi_{g,a}\in\mathcal O_E$ with $\pi_{g,a}\bar\pi_{g,a}=g_a$ and $|\phi_{a,v}(\bar\pi_{g,a})|_v<1$. In the arithmetic column applications both $\pi_{g,a}$ lie in $E_0$. Choose a nontrivial additive character $\psi_{a,v}$ of the residue field $\kappa_v$ and multiplicative characters $\chi_{a,v,j}$ for every $j\ge0$.

Choose frames $B_{a,v}$ with $\phi_{a,v}(\Lambda_v)=B_{a,v}\mathcal O_v^n$. Reduce entries of $B_{a,v}^{-1}\phi_{a,v}(x)B_{a,v}$ modulo $v$, sending a nonintegral entry to zero. Two forms of level specification will be used. In the general form choose $H_{a,v}\subset\mathrm{GL}_n(\kappa_v)$, characters $\lambda_{a,v}:H_{a,v}\to\mathbb C^\times$, and maps $r_{a,v}:U\to H_{a,v}$ equal to this reduction. For every unitary lattice stabilizer $x$, require $$x\in U_v\iff\operatorname{red}(B_{a,v}^{-1}\phi_{a,v}(x)B_{a,v})\in H_{a,v},\qquad \chi(\kappa)=\prod_{a=0}^1\prod_{v\mid g_a}\lambda_{a,v}(r_{a,v}(\kappa))^{-1},\quad |\chi(\kappa)|=1.$$ In the explicit form, parameters $a_0,t,b,s,c$ and a Boolean $z$ specify the reduced matrix $M$. Put $b_0(x)=x$ for $x<a_0$ and $b_0(x)=a_0+\lfloor(x-a_0)/t\rfloor$ otherwise. Require $M_{xy}=0$ when $b_0(y)<b_0(x)$, $M_{xx}=1$ for $x<a_0$, and column $c$ to be the corresponding standard column if it exists. If $z$ is true, the final row and column are those of $I$. The local character is $$L_{a_0,t,b,s}(M)=\psi_{a,v}\left(\sum_{x+1=y<a_0,\ y\ne s}M_{xy}\right)\prod_{j=0}^{b-1}\chi_{a,v,j}(\det M_{[a_0+jt,a_0+(j+1)t),[a_0+jt,a_0+(j+1)t)}).$$ Entries outside the matrix are zero. Use these factors in the same inverse-product formula for $\chi$, and again require $|\chi|=1$.

Hypothesis (prime-$T$ residues and local reductions). At a source $u$, identify its finite-place labels with all $F$-places dividing $T_u$. Choose $\pi_u\in\mathcal O_E\cap E_0$ with $$\pi_u\bar\pi_u=T_u,\qquad \pi_u\equiv1\pmod{Q_Sqd(g_0g_1)^2}.$$ Choose surjective maps $r_v:\mathcal O_E\to k_v$ killing $\pi_u$, nonzero on $\bar\pi_u$, and with $r_v(a)=0$ for $a\in\mathcal O_F$ exactly when $a$ belongs to the designated $F$-prime. Write $P_v=\ker r_v$. At each coefficient place $w$ with $\ell_w=T_u$, that prime membership is equivalent to positive $w$-valuation under the recorded embedding indexed by $\tau(w,v)$. At every CM label the embedded $\pi_u$, if the recorded flag $\epsilon_w$ is true, or $\bar\pi_u$ otherwise, has positive $w$-valuation.

For every class put $K_{v,c}=L_c+P_vL_c^\#$. Choose surjective additive maps $p_{v,c}:L_c^\#\to V_{v,c}$ and $p^k_{v,c}:K_{v,c}\to V^k_{v,c}$, semilinear for $r_v$, with kernels $P_vL_c^\#$ and $P_vK_{v,c}$. The recorded finite functional satisfies $f_c(p(z))=0$ exactly for $z\in K_{v,c}$. If $f_c(p(z))=1$, then $p^k(T_uz)$ is the recorded vector $t_e$ whenever $T_uz\in K_{v,c}$. Choose additive equivalences $Q_v\simeq k_v$, separately for each branch when present. Whenever $y_{c,v}^{-1}z\in(\Lambda^\#)_v$, they send $f_v(y_{c,v}^{-1}z)$ to $f_c(p(z))$.

Hypothesis (coordinate values and local operators). Identify the recorded value set at each chosen stage with the invertible frame tuples. There is $C\in\mathrm{GL}_n(E)$ such that $C^*C=G_i$, the reference lattice is $C^{-1}\mathcal O_E^n$, and the recorded determinant factor at $y,\tau$ is $\tau(\det C)\det(eY(y)_\tau)$. Put $\widetilde\lambda_i=\lambda_i-\lfloor i(t+1)/2\rfloor$ and use its determinant shift $d_i$ in the functions $F$ below.

The recorded finite form space is exactly the class form space above for the shifted weight $\widetilde\lambda_i$, on every branch. Its rational coefficient image consists exactly of these forms with classwise polynomials in the $K$-rational span. For every permitted coefficient place $w$, membership of a rational vector in the recorded local coefficient lattice is equivalent to the following condition: for every class $c$ and every $\ell_w$-local basis $B$ of $L_c^\#$, its class function is $F_{d_i,B_\infty^{-1},P}\circ eY$ with $P$ in the $w$-integral span. For an endpoint's global integral lattice this criterion is imposed at every $w$.

At every class and orthonormal frame $o$, require $$\operatorname{ipV}_c(F_{d_i,o^{-1},P}\circ eY,F_{d_i,o^{-1},Q}\circ eY)=\mathcal N_{\lambda_i}(Q,P).$$ For every embedding $s:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$, rational vector $x$ represented at each class by a $K$-polynomial $P_c$, and orthonormal frame $o$ for the $s$-conjugate Gram matrices, require $$\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{maj}_s(x,x)=\sum_c\operatorname{Re}(\operatorname{wt}_c)\prod_\tau|\det o_\tau|^{2d_i(\tau)}\operatorname{Re}\mathcal N_{\lambda_i}\bigl(P_c^s((o_\tau Z_\tau)_\tau),P_c^s((o_\tau Z_\tau)_\tau)\bigr).$$ Here the recorded rational Hermitian form and its positive majorant are those of ([Integral forms on column representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.MemberForms/)).

For raising, take $z\in L_c^\#$ with $f_c(p(z))=1$ and let $c'$ be its recorded raised class. There are rational unitary $\gamma$, unitary finite $\eta$ supported at $v$, and $\kappa\in U$ such that $$\gamma y_{c'}=y_c\eta\kappa,\qquad(\gamma L_{c'})_{P_v}=(L_c^\#)_{P_v},\qquad(\gamma L_{c'}^\#)_{P_v}=(L_c^\#+\mathcal O_ET_u^{-1}z)_{P_v}.$$ Whenever $T_u\gamma x\in L_c^\#$, require $f_{c'}(p(x))=f_c(p(T_u\gamma x))$. For every function $h$ and values $s,s'$ with $eY(s)=\gamma_\infty eY(s')$, the recorded raising operator has value $\chi(\kappa)^{-1}h(s')$ at $s$.

For lowering, take a nonzero finite dual functional $g$ and its recorded lowered class $c'$. Choose $\gamma,\eta,\kappa$ with the same adelic relation, require $(\gamma L_{c'}^\#)_{P_v}=(L_c)_{P_v}$ and $f_{c'}(p(x))=g(p^k(\gamma x))$ whenever $\gamma x\in K_{v,c}$, and require the same $\chi(\kappa)^{-1}h(s')$ function-action formula. In a branch presentation both raised and lowered classes must remain in the branch.

For a residue-field unit $a$, an integer $b$ reducing to $a$, and a unitary adele $\delta$ supported at $v$ that stabilizes $\Lambda_v$ and its dual and satisfies $b f_v(\delta_vx)=f_v(x)$, dilation preserves the branch and sends $[y_c]$ to $[y_c\delta]$. On restrictions of every equivariant adelic function its recorded operator is evaluation at $y_c\delta$.

Finally, when $\ell_w=T_u$ and $\epsilon_w$ is true, membership in the recorded subspace $B_{w,v}$ is equivalent to this integral criterion: for every class and every $T_u$-local basis $B$ of $L_c^\#$ and $B'$ of $K_{v,c}$, the class function is $F_{d_i,M,P}\circ eY$ for a $w$-integral polynomial $P$, where $M_\tau=(B'_\tau)^{-1}$ at $\tau(w,v)$ and $M_\tau=B_\tau^{-1}$ at every other CM label.
