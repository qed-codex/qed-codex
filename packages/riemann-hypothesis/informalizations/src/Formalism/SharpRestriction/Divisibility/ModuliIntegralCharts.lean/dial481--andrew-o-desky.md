## Extensions of integral charts and degree filtrations

[Definition / hypothesis PHT.D178](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.ModuliIntegralCharts.ExtA/)

Hypothesis (extensions of integral charts). Use ([Integral charts and theta representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.ModuliIntegralCharts.CF/)), retaining its notation. For every model $Z\in\mathcal M$, choose a set $\mathcal F_Z$ and a map $\operatorname{Fl}_Z:\mathcal F_Z\to\mathcal M$. Choose $f_m\in\mathcal F_{X_m}$ with $Y=\operatorname{Fl}_{X_m}(f_m)$, sets $\mathcal I_Z$, maps $\operatorname{Bl}_Z:\mathcal I_Z\to\mathcal M$, a predicate $\operatorname{Pow}$ on $\mathcal I_Z$ and a predicate $\operatorname{Prin}(I,\chi)$ for $\chi\in\mathcal C(Z,v,e)$. Choose sets $\mathcal D_{Z,Z'}$ containing at most one element, maps $\mathcal D_{Z,Z'}\to\mathcal M$, sets $\mathcal V_b$ for $b\in\{*\}\sqcup\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$, and maps $\mathcal V_b\to\mathcal I_{Z_b}$, where $Z_*=X_m$ and $Z_n=X_n$.

For every $Z$ choose a set $Q_Z$ with a relation $\rightsquigarrow$ and predicates $\operatorname{Ab}_Q,\operatorname{Q},\operatorname{Gen}_Q$. Require $\operatorname{Gen}_Q(q)\Rightarrow\operatorname{Ab}_Q(q)$. Choose $g_Z:G_Z\to Q_Z$ such that $$\operatorname{Ab}(\eta)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \operatorname{Ab}_Q(g_Z\eta),\qquad\neg\operatorname{Q}(g_Z\eta).$$ Require $G_Z$ finite and nonempty. Choose $r_Z:V\to Q_Z$ such that $\rho_V(v)>0\Longleftrightarrow\neg\operatorname{Ab}_Q(r_Zv)$, and maps $a_Z:G_Z\to V$ with $r_Za_Z=g_Z$. For each chart $\chi\in\mathcal C(Z,v,e)$ choose points $q_M(\chi),q_U(\chi)\in Q_Z$ with $$q_U(\chi)\rightsquigarrow q_M(\chi),\qquad\operatorname{Q}(q_U(\chi)).$$ The centered predicate implies $q_M(\chi)=r_Z(v)$; the exact abelian predicate implies $\neg\operatorname{Ab}_Q(q_U(\chi))$. Choose sets $R_{v,e}$ and maps $\mathcal C(Z,v,e)\to R_{v,e}$.

Call $y$ good when $\Lambda_y=0$. Choose $k_Z:V\to P_Z$ with $k_Z(v)$ good if and only if $\rho_V(v)=0$, and $y_Z:G_Z\to P_Z$ with $y_Z(\eta)$ good if and only if $\operatorname{Ab}(\eta)$. For $e\in E_v$ choose $v_e>0$. Choose a predicate $\operatorname{Ord}(\chi,A,s,a)$ for chart $\chi$, $A\in\mathcal S_Z$, $s\in S(A)$ and $a\in\mathbb R$.

For $A,B\in\mathcal S_Z$ choose $\operatorname{Hom}(A,B)\in\mathcal S_Z$, invariant whenever both arguments are invariant, maps $$\operatorname{Iso}(A,B)\to S(\operatorname{Hom}(A,B)),\quad\varphi\mapsto s_\varphi,\qquad \operatorname{Iso}(A,B)\to\operatorname{Iso}(B,A),\quad\varphi\mapsto\varphi^{-},$$ with $\lVert s_\varphi\rVert_y=p^{-v_\varphi(y)}$ and $v_{\varphi^-}(y)=-v_\varphi(y)$.

For $y\in P_Y$ choose a $C_y$-vector space $V_y$, an additive subgroup $L_y$, subspaces $E_{y,\sigma}$, and subspaces $W_{y,\sigma,i}\subseteq E_{y,\sigma}$ for $\sigma\in\Sigma\times\{0,1\}$ and $i\ge0$. Choose additive subgroups $T_{y,\sigma,i}$ with $$T_{y,\sigma,i}=W_{y,\sigma,i}\cap L_y.$$ Choose real-valued functions $N(\ell,s,y,(A_{\sigma,i}))$ of integer arrays $\ell$, sections $s\in S(L(\ell))$ and families of additive subgroups of $V_y$, such that $$\lVert s\rVert_{{\rm ref},y}=N(\ell,s,y,(W_{y,\sigma,i}\cap(E_{y,\sigma}\cap L_y))),\qquad \lVert s\rVert_y=N(\ell,s,y,(T_{y,\sigma,i})).$$

For every $y\in P_Z$, require $\Omega_y^0$ to be stable under scalars of norm at most one. Choose a $C_y$-vector space $T_y^C$, a homomorphism $i_y:T_y\to T_y^C$ whose image spans, and a linear map $H_y:T_y^C\to\Omega_y$ with $H_yi_y=\operatorname{HT}_y$. Choose a $C_y$-bilinear form on $T_y^C$, a subspace $\mathfrak g_y^1\subseteq T_y^C$, and endomorphisms $e_{y,\tau}$ for $\tau\in\Sigma$.

For $n\ge0$, choose a set $\mathcal H_n$, homomorphisms $h_y:T_y\to\Omega_y$ for $h\in\mathcal H_n$, $y\in P_{X_n}$, and an index $I_h\in\mathcal I_{X_n}$. For $n\ge3$ require $$\operatorname{Bl}_{\rm HT}(Z',X_n)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \exists h\in\mathcal H_n:\ Z'=\operatorname{Bl}_{X_n}(I_h),\quad h_y(t)\in\Omega_y^0,\quad h_y(t)-\operatorname{HT}_y(t)\in p^n\Omega_y^0\ \ (y,t\text{ arbitrary}).$$ Choose additive subgroups $W_{h,y,\tau}\subseteq T_y^C$, choices $f_Z^H\in\mathcal F_Z$, and objects $\Omega_Z\in\mathcal S_Z$. Choose subgroups $\Omega_y^P\subseteq\Omega_y$ for all $y\in P_Z$, such that for $n\ge3$ and $\operatorname{Bl}_{\rm HT}(Z',X_n)$, $$\Omega_y^P=\Omega_y^0\cap\bigl(\langle\operatorname{HT}_y(T_y)\rangle_{\mathcal O_{C_y}}+p^n\Omega_y^0\bigr)\qquad(y\in P_{Z'}).$$ Here $\langle S\rangle_{\mathcal O_C}$ is the additive subgroup generated by all $as$ with $|a|\le1$, $s\in S$; no topological closure is used.

For every $Z$, choose another set $B_Z$. Each $b\in B_Z$ has a field $C_b$ satisfying the complete valued-field conditions in ([Integral charts and theta representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.ModuliIntegralCharts.CF/)), a $C_b$-vector space $\Omega_b$, subgroups $\Omega_b^0,\Omega_b^P,\Omega_{b,\tau}$, and endomorphisms $e_{b,\tau}$ for $\tau\in\Sigma$.

Choose sets $S_Z$ with a boundary predicate, a set $\mathcal F$, maps $h_Z:S_Z\to\mathcal F$, a set $\mathcal L$ with a relation $\operatorname{Mem}\subseteq\mathcal F\times\mathcal L$ and dimensions $d_L\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$, a set $A$ and a map $a\mapsto L_a\in\mathcal L$. Require $\operatorname{Boundary}(Z)$ to mean exactly that there is a finite $A_0\subseteq A$ such that every boundary point $x\in S_Z$ satisfies $\operatorname{Mem}(h_Zx,L_a)$ for some $a\in A_0$, and $$d_{L_a}+f(r-1)=fr(r-1)/2\qquad(a\in A_0).$$

Choose curve sets $\mathcal R_Z^S,\mathcal R^{\mathbb Q}$ with integer graded-degree functions $d_i(C,\tau)$ and $d_i^{\mathbb Q}(C,\tau)$ for $i\ge0$. The two nef predicates of ([Integral charts and theta representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.ModuliIntegralCharts.CF/)) satisfy $$\operatorname{Nef}_{\rm HT}(Z,\ell)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \forall C\in\mathcal R_Z^S,\quad-\sum_\tau\sum_{i=1}^r\ell_{\tau i}d_i(C,\tau)\ge0,$$ $$\operatorname{Nef}_{\mathbb Q}(\ell)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \forall C\in\mathcal R^{\mathbb Q},\quad-\sum_\tau\sum_{i=1}^r\ell_{\tau i}d_i^{\mathbb Q}(C,\tau)\ge0.$$ On the chart curve set $\mathcal R$ choose integer degrees $d_i^D(C,\tau)$, requiring $$d_{\rm dR}(C,\ell)=-\sum_\tau\sum_{i=1}^r\ell_{\tau i}d_i^D(C,\tau),$$ $$d_{\rm Lie}(C,\tau,0)=\sum_{i=1}^jd_i^D(C,\tau),\qquad d_{\rm Lie}(C,\tau,1)=-\sum_{i=j+1}^rd_i^D(C,\tau).$$ When $E$ is unramified at $p$, require $d_e(C)=-\sum_\tau(d_{\rm Lie}(C,\tau,0)+d_{\rm Lie}(C,\tau,1))$. Finally, $$\sum_{a=1}^id_a^D(C,\tau)\le P(C)\quad(1\le i\le j),\qquad -\sum_{a=i+1}^rd_a^D(C,\tau)\le P(C)\quad(j\le i\le r).$$

## Integral charts and theta representations

[Definition / hypothesis PHT.D179](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.ModuliIntegralCharts.CF/)

Hypothesis (integral charts and theta representations). Use ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), with CM field $E$, selected embedding set $\Sigma$ and $f=|\Sigma|$. Fix natural numbers $r,j,p$. Choose a set $\mathcal M$, distinguished elements $X_m,Y\in\mathcal M$, elements $X_n\in\mathcal M$ for every $n\ge0$, and sets $P_Z$ for $Z\in\mathcal M$. For each $y\in P_Z$ choose a complete algebraically closed nontrivially normed ultrametric field $C_y$ of characteristic zero with $\lVert p\rVert=p^{-1}$, and a finite free abelian group $\Lambda_y$ with an $\mathcal O_E$-module structure. Choose a symmetric $\mathbb Z$-bilinear form $\beta_y:\Lambda_y\times\Lambda_y\to\mathbb R$, satisfying $$\beta_y(ca,b)=\beta_y(a,\bar c b).$$ Its Gram matrix in some finite integral basis is positive definite over $\mathbb R$. Require an integer $\rho_y\ge0$ with $$\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}\Lambda_y=2f\rho_y,\qquad 1\le j<r\ \Longrightarrow\ \rho_y\le j,\quad\rho_y+j\le r.$$

For each $Z$, choose a set $G_Z$ with a predicate $\operatorname{Ab}$, a set $\mathcal S_Z$ with two predicates $\operatorname{Inv},\operatorname{LF}$, and for $A\in\mathcal S_Z$ an abelian section group $S(A)$, a subgroup $H(A)\subseteq S(A)$, and functions $\lVert s\rVert_y\ge0$. Require $s\in H(A)\Rightarrow\lVert s\rVert_y\le1$ for every $y\in P_Z$. For $A,B\in\mathcal S_Z$, choose a set $\operatorname{Iso}(A,B)$ and real functions $v_\varphi(y)$ for its elements. For every integer array $\ell:\Sigma\times\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\to\mathbb Z$, choose an invariant $L(\ell)\in\mathcal S_Y$ and a second real function $\lVert\cdot\rVert_{\rm ref}$ on $S(L(\ell))\times P_Y$.

Choose a set $V$, a map $\rho_V:V\to\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$, sets $E_v$ for $v\in V$, and sets $\mathcal C(Z,v,e)$ for $Z\in\mathcal M$, $e\in E_v$. Choose five predicates on each $\mathcal C(Z,v,e)$, labelled centered, open, exact abelian, unit condition and constant condition. Choose a set $\Phi$, real functions $\phi(y)$ for $\phi\in\Phi$, and a predicate $\operatorname{Div}(A,s,a,\phi)$ for $s\in S(A)$ and $a\in\mathbb Q$. Require $\phi(y)=0$ whenever $\Lambda_y$ is zero. Choose a set $\mathcal T$, sets $\mathcal X_t$ for $t\in\mathcal T$, and real functions $F_{\ell,s}(t,\chi)$ for $s\in S(L(\ell))$ and $\chi\in\mathcal X_t$.

Choose a set $\mathcal U$. For $U\in\mathcal U$, choose sets $\mathcal B_U,\mathcal B_U^{\mathbb Q}$, a restriction map $q:\mathcal B_U\to\mathcal B_U^{\mathbb Q}$, an element $P_U\in\mathcal B_U^{\mathbb Q}$, and predicates symmetric and rigid on $\mathcal B_U$. For $y\in P_{X_m}$ choose sets $\mathcal B_{y,U},\mathcal P_{y,U}$, restriction maps $\mathcal B_U\to\mathcal B_{y,U}$ and pullback maps $\mathcal P_{y,U}\to\mathcal B_{y,U}$, and a principal predicate on $\mathcal P_{y,U}$. Choose real functions $\psi_y:\Lambda_y\to\mathbb R$, elements $L_{\rm pp},L_c\in\mathcal S_{X_m}$ for every $c\ge0$, and indices $U_a\in\mathcal U$ for $a\ge0$.

For each $y\in P_{X_m}$, $U\in\mathcal U$ and $n\ge0$, choose a finite abelian group $A_{y,U,n}$ killed by $n$. For $n>0$ require $$|A_{y,U_a,n}|=n^{2fra}.$$ Choose a group $\Theta_{y,U,n}$, a surjection $\pi:\Theta_{y,U,n}\to A_{y,U,n}$, and an injective homomorphism $s:C_y^\times\to\Theta_{y,U,n}$ with central image and $\ker\pi=\operatorname{im}s$. Here the abelian group in the target of $\pi$ is written multiplicatively. Choose an involutive automorphism $\delta$ of $\Theta_{y,U,n}$ with $$\pi(\delta g)=\pi(g)^{-1},\qquad\delta(s(a))=s(a).$$ Choose a $C_y$-vector space $V_{y,U,n}$, a homomorphism $\Theta_{y,U,n}\to\operatorname{End}_{C_y}(V_{y,U,n})$ under composition, and a distinguished $\vartheta_{y,U,n}$, with $s(a)$ acting as scalar $a$. For each $D\ge0$, choose a $C_y$-vector space $J_{y,U,n,D}$, an additive subgroup $J^0_{y,U,n,D}$, and a linear map $\operatorname{jet}:V_{y,U,n}\to J_{y,U,n,D}$.

For $y\in P_{X_m}$ and $\sigma\in\Sigma\times\{0,1\}$ choose abelian groups $\mathfrak g_{y,\sigma},\mathfrak b_{y,\sigma},\mathfrak t_{y,\sigma}$, homomorphisms $\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak b$ and $\mathfrak t\to\mathfrak g$, and arbitrary $C_y$-valued functions on $\mathfrak g\times\mathfrak g$ and $\mathfrak b\times\mathfrak b$. Choose sets $\mathcal H_y,\mathcal Q_y$ and two evaluation maps $\mathcal H_y\times\mathcal Q_y\to C_y$.

For every $Z$ and $y\in P_Z$, choose an abelian group $T_y$, a $C_y$-vector space $\Omega_y$, an additive subgroup $\Omega_y^0$, and a homomorphism $\operatorname{HT}_y:T_y\to\Omega_y$. Choose a relation $\operatorname{Bl}_{\rm HT}$ on $\mathcal M\times\mathcal M$, objects $\Omega_{Z,b,\tau}\in\mathcal S_Z$ for $b\in\{0,1\}$ and $\tau\in\Sigma$, sets $\mathcal E_Z$ with predicates $\operatorname{Exact}(e,a)$ for $a\ge0$, and elements $e_{Z,\tau}\in\mathcal E_Z$. Choose predicates $$\operatorname{Nef}_{\rm HT}(Z,\ell),\quad\operatorname{Nef}_{\mathbb Q}(\ell),\quad\operatorname{Desc}(Z,\mu),\quad\operatorname{Boundary}(Z),$$ for real arrays $\ell:\Sigma\times\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\to\mathbb R$ and integer arrays $\mu$ of the same shape. Finally choose sets $\mathcal B_n$ with predicates on $\mathcal B_n$ and on $\mathcal B_n\times\mathcal B_{n+1}$, a curve set $\mathcal R$, integer functions $d_e,P$ on $\mathcal R$ with $P\ge0$, integer functions $d_{\rm Lie}(C,\tau,b)$, and real functions $d_{\rm dR}(C,\ell)$. The predicates and operations in this hypothesis have exactly the displayed signatures and conditions.
