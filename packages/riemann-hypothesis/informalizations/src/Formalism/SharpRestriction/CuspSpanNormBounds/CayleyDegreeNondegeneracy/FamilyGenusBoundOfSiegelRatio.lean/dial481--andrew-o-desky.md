## Counting classes of Hermitian planes with bounded orthogonal vectors

[Theorem ARAK.0726](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.BridgeLemma%E2%82%82.plane_class_count_holds/)

Let $K$ be a totally complex number field with an involution $*$ inducing complex conjugation at every embedding. Suppose that, for each maximal ideal $\mathfrak p\subset\mathcal O_K$, some $\theta\in\mathcal O_K$ has $\theta-\theta^*\notin\mathfrak p$. Let $V$ be a $K$-vector space with compatible $\mathcal O_K$-scalars and Hermitian form $h$, and suppose $L\subset V$ has an orthonormal $\mathcal O_K$-basis of two vectors which is also a $K$-basis of $V$.

Let $\mathcal S$ be a finite set of lattices representing each isometry class in the genus of $L$ exactly once. Let $B_1,B_2\in\mathbb R$. Suppose every $M\in\mathcal S$ is integral for $h$ and contains nonzero orthogonal vectors $x,y$ such that, for every embedding $\tau:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$, $$|\tau(h(x,x))|\le B_1,\qquad|\tau(h(y,y))|\le B_2.$$ Writing $d=[K:\mathbb Q]$, one has $$|\mathcal S|\le(1+2B_1)^{2d}(1+2B_2)^{2d}(B_1B_2)^{4d}.$$

## A bounded orthogonal vector in a self-dual Hermitian plane

[Theorem ARAK.0701](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.BridgeLemma%E2%82%82.second_orthogonal_vector_holds/)

Let $K$ be a totally complex number field with an involution $a\mapsto\bar a$ such that every embedding $\phi:K\to\mathbb C$ satisfies $\phi(\bar a)=\overline{\phi(a)}$. Assume that for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak p\subset\mathcal O_K$, there is $\theta\in\mathcal O_K$ with $\theta-\bar\theta\notin\mathfrak p$. Let $V$ be a $K$-vector space with its compatible $\mathcal O_K$-module structure, and $h$ a Hermitian form. Suppose there is an $\mathcal O_K$-lattice $L$ generated by a $K$-basis $e_1,e_2$ with $h(e_i,e_j)=\delta_{ij}$. For every full $\mathcal O_K$-lattice $M\subset V$ with $h(M,M)\subset\mathcal O_K$ and $M^\vee=M$, and every $0\ne x\in M$, there is $0\ne y\in M$ such that $$h(x,y)=0,\qquad |\phi(h(y,y))|\le|\phi(h(x,x))|\,\operatorname{rd}(K) \quad(\phi:K\to\mathbb C).$$
