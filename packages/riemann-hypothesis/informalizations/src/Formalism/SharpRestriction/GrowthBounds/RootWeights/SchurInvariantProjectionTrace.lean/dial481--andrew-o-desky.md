## The invariant projection on endomorphisms is normalized trace

[Theorem REP.0930](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.GLWeightTheory.schur_invariant_projection_trace/)

Fix the source's global arithmetic datum $D$ as described below. Assume complete reducibility for every finite-dimensional representation over a characteristic-zero field given by operators $E_{ab}$ satisfying the $\mathfrak{gl}_n$ commutator relations and decomposing into simultaneous integral weight spaces for the $E_{aa}$.

Let $K$ have characteristic zero, $L$ be finite-dimensional over $K$, and $(E_{ab})_{1\le a,b\le n}$ be such a representation. Let $v_0\ne0$ be a highest-weight vector of weight $\lambda\in\mathbb Z^n$: $$E_{aa}v_0=\lambda_av_0,\qquad E_{ab}v_0=0\quad(a<b).$$ Suppose the vectors obtained by applying all words in the $E_{ab}$ to $v_0$ span $L$. On $\operatorname{End}_K(L)$ use the action $T\mapsto E_{ab}T-TE_{ab}$. Let $P$ be a $K$-linear map on this endomorphism space that is the identity on the subspace generated by highest-weight vectors of weight zero and their translates, and zero on every such subspace of nonzero highest weight. Then for every $T\in\operatorname{End}_K(L)$, $$(\dim_KL)P(T)=\operatorname{tr}(T)\operatorname{id}_L.$$
