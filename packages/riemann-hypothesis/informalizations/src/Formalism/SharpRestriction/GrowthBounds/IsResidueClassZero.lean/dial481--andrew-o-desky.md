## Local weight extensions and lattice chains

[Definition / hypothesis PHT.D145](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.GatTies/)

Hypothesis (local weight extensions and lattices). Use the field, rank and source-prime data of ([Local congruence tables](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TwistedPlaces.AdmT/)) and the coefficient places of ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)). A permitted pair $(u,w)$ has residue prime $p=p_w\ge5$, lies in the inert prime class, has even order of the $A$-coordinate of every arithmetic Frobenius at every prime of $E$ above $p$, satisfies $p\notin\mathcal P_{\rm lev}$ and $p\nmid T_u$. For each permitted pair take the initial local polynomial lattice setting of ([Local polynomial lattice settings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.GAtP/)). Write $O_w,K_w$ for its coefficient DVR and fraction field, $v\in\{0,\ldots,c_{\rm cyc}-1\}$ for its local cycles, $n_v$ for their lengths, and $\lambda^0_{v,s}$ for the base dominant integer row at position $s$.

For every permitted pair there is a bijection $$\iota_{u,w}:\coprod_{0\le v<c_{\rm cyc}}\{0,\ldots,n_v-1\}\longrightarrow\Sigma,$$ where $\Sigma$ is the CM type. The base row has length $k$. Every $n_v$ equals the order of the $A$-coordinate of every arithmetic Frobenius $\sigma$ at a prime of $E$ above $p$. If $g(\tau)$ is the Galois label of $\tau\in\Sigma$ relative to the chosen base embedding, then $$g(\iota_{u,w}(v,s+1))=g(\iota_{u,w}(v,s))\,g(\sigma)\,c,$$ where cyclic addition is modulo $n_v$ and $c$ is complex conjugation. At each position, $\lambda^0_{v,s}$ equals the integral coefficient highest-weight row at the initial stage of ([Integral forms on column representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.MemberForms/)) under this bijection.

At every cycle choose a subset $T_v\subset\{0,\ldots,n_v-1\}$ and integer functions $a_v,b_v$ on its positions, a natural bound $c_{\rm loc}$, natural numbers $t_{\rm loc},m_{\rm loc}$, and integers $w_{v,j,s}$ for all natural stages $j$. At $s\in T_v$ the endpoints of $\lambda^0_{v,s}$ are $a_v(s),-b_v(s)$ and every interior entry has absolute value at most $c_{\rm loc}$. At $s\notin T_v$, every entry has that bound. Require $$w_{v,1,s}\ge\max\{c_{\rm loc},-\lambda^0_{v,s,k-1}\},\qquad w_{v,j,s}\le w_{v,j+1,s}\quad(j\ge1).$$ For each $j\ge0$ append each of $-w_{v,i,s}$, $1\le i\le j$, exactly $t_{\rm loc}+1$ times to $\lambda^0_{v,s}$. Every resulting row $\lambda^j_{v,s}$ is weakly decreasing.

For each positive stage $j$, choose a finite component set $C_j$ and for every component a level for the rows $\lambda^j_{v,s}$, with the complete meaning of a level in ([Local polynomial lattice settings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.GAtP/)). The initial component set and level are already fixed by ([Local polynomial lattice settings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.GAtP/)). Let $\mathcal F_j$ be the $K_w$-vector space of functions on $C_j$ and tuples of local lattices, with values in the tensor polynomial representation for $\lambda^j$. Its integral module $\mathcal I_j$ is the product over $C_j$ of the specified local integral modules. Choose $K_w$-linear maps $$K_j:\mathcal F_j\longrightarrow\mathcal F_{j+1}\qquad(j\ge0),$$ and an $O_w$-submodule $V^{\rm ram}_{u,w}\subset\mathcal F_0$. Choose also a function $v_{u,w}:A\to\{0,\ldots,c_{\rm cyc}-1\}$.

For family members above the starting scale, identify these local data with ([Orbit weights and local loss definitions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.Consts/)) as follows. Put $p_0=k^2x^{26}$, $q_j=\{k+(2j-1)(t+1)\}/2$, and let $\mathfrak w_j$, $h_p$, $r_o$, $\mathcal R_p$ be the functions, Frobenius step, orbit-test integer and representatives in ([Orbit weights and local loss definitions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.Consts/)). Require $$m_{\rm loc}=m,\qquad t_{\rm loc}=t,\qquad c_{\rm loc}\le4S.$$ The local edge-rank parameter of ([Local polynomial lattice settings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.GAtP/)) is $r_o$ if $p<p_0$, and $1$ otherwise. The restriction of $v_{u,w}$ to $\mathcal R_p$ is injective. A position corresponding under $\iota_{u,w}$ to an embedding with label $(-1,1,h)$ lies outside $T_v$, and $$w_{v,j,s}=\mathfrak w_j(1,h)-q_j\qquad(1\le j\le m).$$ For every $v_0\in\mathcal R_p$, require $n_{v_{u,w}(v_0)}=\operatorname{ord}(h_p)$. At each position $s$ of this cycle, the embedding label is $(1,1,v_0+sh_p)$, and $$s\in T_{v_{u,w}(v_0)}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad v_0+sh_p\in T.$$ At selected positions, $a_{v_{u,w}(v_0)}(s)=a_{v_0+sh_p}$ and $b_{v_{u,w}(v_0)}(s)=b_{v_0+sh_p}$. At every position of this cycle, $$w_{v_{u,w}(v_0),j,s}=\mathfrak w_j(0,v_0+sh_p)-q_j\qquad(1\le j\le m).$$

## Orbit weights and local loss definitions

[Definition / hypothesis PHT.D148](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.Consts/)

Hypothesis (orbit weights). Use the arithmetic fields, ranks, scale and weight data of ([Local congruence tables](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TwistedPlaces.AdmT/)) and the local quantities of ([Local size and support data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.SizeData%E2%82%82/)). Write $\mathcal I,\mathcal S_{\rm sig}$ for the inert and signal prime classes of ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)). All natural-number arguments below include zero; logarithms, division, natural ceiling and natural supremum use the total conventions in ([Local congruence tables](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TwistedPlaces.AdmT/)) and ([Local lattice, orbit-count and gain conditions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.LinkG_at/)).

Choose fifteen nonnegative real constants, in this order, $$g_\Lambda,g_\rho,g_{k,II},g_{t,II},g_{D,II},g_{s,II},g'_{k,II},g_{D,I},g_{\kappa,1},g_{\kappa,2},g_{k,I},d_1,d_2,g_{P,1},g_N.$$ Choose a function $\beta:A\times A\to A$ satisfying $$\forall h,t\in A\quad\exists i\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}:\quad\beta(h,t)+ih=t,\qquad\beta(h,t+h)=\beta(h,t).$$ For every natural stage $j$ choose $\omega_j:\{0,1\}\times A\to\mathbb R$. Let $T_2^*=\{sa_0+a_*:s\in T_2\}$, using the cyclic generator $a_0$ and member shift $a_*$ of ([Local congruence tables](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TwistedPlaces.AdmT/)). Require, for every $j$, $$\omega_j(0,h)\in[b'/x,b'/x+\eta]\quad(h\in T),\qquad\omega_j(1,h)\in[b,b+\eta+1]\quad(h\in T_2^*),$$ and $\omega_j(\epsilon,h)\in[w_0,w_0+\eta]$ at all remaining pairs. Extend $a_h,b_h$ by zero off $T$, and put $$\mathfrak w_j(\epsilon,h)=\mathbf1_{\{\epsilon=0\}}b_h+\omega_j(\epsilon,h),\qquad q_j=\{k+(2j-1)(t+1)\}/2,\qquad W_j=\mathfrak w_j-q_j.$$ For members above the family starting scale, impose the following identifications. For $1\le j\le m$, $\mathfrak w_j(0,h)$ and $\mathfrak w_j(1,h)$ equal the Hermitian weight $E_{\tau,j-1}$ at the embeddings labeled respectively $(1,1,h)$ and $(-1,1,h)$.

For every natural $p$, choose an arithmetic Frobenius label $g_p\in\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb Q)$ at a prime above $p$ if one exists, with an arbitrary label otherwise, and let $h_p\in A$ be its $A$-coordinate. Define $$P_p(h,e)=\left(\mathbf1_{\{p\in\mathcal S_{\rm sig},\ e\text{ odd}\}},h+eh_p\right).$$ At every coefficient place $w$ with residue prime $p$, for all natural $j,e$ and $h\in T$, require the partner weight of ([Local size and support data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.SizeData%E2%82%82/)) to satisfy $$w_{j,h,p,e}=\mathfrak w_j(P_p(h,e)).$$ Choose a natural function $e(p)$ and a real function $\kappa^I(p,n',T',a',b')$ on the inputs of ([Local loss from polynomial lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.KapIDef/)). For family members above the starting scale, require $\kappa^I$ to equal the infimum specified in ([Local loss from polynomial lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.KapIDef/)). The prescribed equation for $e(p)$ and its comparison with the local lattice exponent are part of ([Integral and theta compatibility for families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Extra/)).

Here are the sets and real quantities needed to identify the local losses and gains. Put $$d_p=\begin{cases}2h_p,&p\in\mathcal S_{\rm sig},\\h_p,&p\notin\mathcal S_{\rm sig},\end{cases}\qquad n_p=\begin{cases}2\operatorname{ord}(2h_p),&p\in\mathcal S_{\rm sig},\\\operatorname{ord}(h_p),&p\notin\mathcal S_{\rm sig},\end{cases}$$ where $\operatorname{ord}$ denotes additive order. Let $$\mathcal R_p=\{\beta(d_p,h):h\in A\},\quad T_p(v)=\{h\in T:\beta(d_p,h)=v\},\quad N_p(v)=|T_p(v)|.$$ An element $h$ has even position when $h=\beta(d_p,h)+ih_p$ for some even $0\le i<n_p$. Let $\mathcal R_p^{\rm good}$ consist of those representatives for which the distinguished positions $\{0\le i<\operatorname{ord}(h_p):v+ih_p\in T\}$ pass the orbit test in ([Local lattice, orbit-count and gain conditions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.LinkG_at/)). Put $c_G=4S$, $p_0=k^2x^{26}$, $N_*=2n\delta_1$, and $$\Lambda_G=g_\Lambda N_1^2\log(xb),\qquad\rho_B=\frac{4N_*x^\varepsilon\Lambda_G+g_\rho g_N\log x\,\Lambda_G}{k}.$$ A candidate prime is a prime $p<X'$ in $\mathcal I$ with $\operatorname{ord}(h_p)\ge x^{\varepsilon/4}$ and, if $p\ge p_0$, no positive integer $a$ satisfying $X'(1-\rho_B)\le p^a\le X'(1+\rho_B)$. Define $$\kappa_{p,v}=\begin{cases}\min\{1,g_{k,II}|T_{p,v}|r_o^2kx^{-26}\},&p<p_0,\\ \kappa^I(p,\operatorname{ord}(h_p),T_{p,v},a_{p,v},b_{p,v}),&p\ge p_0, \end{cases}$$ where $T_{p,v}=\{0\le i<\operatorname{ord}(h_p):v+ih_p\in T\}$ and $a_{p,v},b_{p,v}$ are the restrictions along that orbit. Put $$\Sigma_p=m\sum_{v\in\mathcal R_p^{\rm good}}\kappa_{p,v},\qquad \mathcal V_p=\begin{cases}\mathcal R_p^{\rm good},&p\text{ is a candidate and }\Sigma_p\le x^{-\varepsilon},\\\varnothing,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}$$

For all $p,j,v$, let $A_h=a_h-2c_G$, $C_j(h,e)=W_j(P_p(h,e))+c_G$, and $$D_{p,v}=d_1n_pN^2\log_p(2a)+d_2N_p(v)(\log_p(2a))^2.$$ In the next odd-power sum, exclude an exponent $e$ precisely when $P_p(h,e)=(0,h')$ for a distinct $h'\in T$ and $e$ is the least positive exponent reaching $(0,h')$. Define $$g^0_{p,j,v}=\sum_{h\in T_p(v)}\sum_{\substack{e\ge0\text{ odd}\\e\text{ not excluded}}}\left(\frac{A_h}{p^e}-C_j(h,e)\right)_+-D_{p,v},$$ $$g^U_{p,j,v}=\frac p{p^2-1}\sum_{h\in T_p(v)}A_h-\sum_{\substack{h\in T_p(v)\\h\text{ has no even position}}}\sum_{e\ge0\text{ odd}}\min\left\{\frac{A_h}{p^e},C_j(h,e)\right\}-D_{p,v}.$$ Here $(z)_+=\max(z,0)$; infinite sums use the value zero if they are not summable. Define the projection loss by $$R_{p,j,v}=\begin{cases} \displaystyle\sum_{\substack{h\in T_p(v)\\h\text{ has no even position}}}(W_j(0,h)-b_h)+g_{P,1}n_p(w_0+\eta+c_G+N),&p\ge p_0,\\ r_o\{N_p(v)(2b'+c_G)+n_p(w_0+\eta+c_G+N)\},&p<p_0, \end{cases}$$ and put $$S_p(v)=\left\lceil\sum_{j=1}^m\max\{0,g^U_{p,j,v}-R_{p,j,v}-\max(g^0_{p,j,v},0)\}\right\rceil.$$ This ceiling is an integer, with no truncation at zero beyond the maximum already displayed.

For $N^+_p=40n_p\delta_1$, set $$D^{II}_p=\left\lceil g_{D,II}N^+_p(N_1^3+r_o^5+k^2)\log_p a+2r_on_pc_G+r_oN^+_p(2b'+2c_G)\right\rceil_+,$$ $$t^{II}_{p,v}=g_{t,II}r_oN_p(v)\left\{\frac{\Lambda_Ga}{pk}+ap^{-L_1(p)}+2b'+2c_G+kp^{G'}\right\}+2r_on_pc_G,$$ where $L_1(p)=\lceil\log_p((kx^{26})^4)\rceil_+$. For $p\ge p_0$, let $D^I_p$ be $D_I$ from ([Local loss from polynomial lattices](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.KapIDef/)) with dimension input $n_p$, and let $t^I_{p,v}$ be $t_I$ with dimension input $\operatorname{ord}(h_p)$ and the actual restricted orbit weights. Put $D_p=D_p^{II}$ below $p_0$, and $D_p=D_p^I$ otherwise; define $t_{p,v}$ by the same cases. Then require at every coefficient place $w$ above $p$, $$\tau_w=\begin{cases}0,&\mathcal V_p=\varnothing,\\e(p)+m\displaystyle\sum_{v\in\mathcal V_p}(\lceil t_{p,v}\rceil_++D_p+1),&\mathcal V_p\ne\varnothing,\end{cases}$$ $$\iota_w=\begin{cases}0,&\mathcal V_p=\varnothing,\\2\Sigma_p\displaystyle\max_{v\in\mathcal V_p}S_p(v),&\mathcal V_p\ne\varnothing.\end{cases}$$ Require $T_w=T$ at signal primes and at $p\ge X'$, and otherwise $$T_w=\{h\in T:\beta(d_p,h)\in\mathcal V_p\}.$$ At supported places in either $\mathcal I$ or $\mathcal S_{\rm sig}$, for $1\le j\le m$, require $\gamma_{j,w}=0$ if $(p\in\mathcal I\text{ and }h_p=0)$ or $p>X''$; otherwise require $$\gamma_{j,w}=\sum_{v\in\mathcal V_p}\max\{g^U_{p,j,v}-R_{p,j,v},\max(g^0_{p,j,v},0)\}+\sum_{v\in\mathcal R_p\setminus\mathcal V_p}\max(g^0_{p,j,v},0).$$ Also require $\mathcal G=\Lambda_G$.

For each family member above its starting scale, require $\rho_B\le x^{-2}$ and impose all the preceding identifications at every coefficient place. Choose a family constant $C_r\ge1$. The discarded-prime set $\mathcal D$ of ([Local size and support data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.SizeData%E2%82%82/)) consists, among primes, exactly of those $p$ different from every source prime $T_u$ for which at least one of the following holds: $$p<X'(1+C_r\rho_B),\quad p\in\mathcal I,\quad\left[\operatorname{ord}(h_p)<x^{\varepsilon/4}\ \text{or}\ (p\ge p_0\text{ and }\exists a\ge1:\ |p^a-X'|\le\rho_BX')\ \text{or}\ (p\text{ candidate and }\Sigma_p>x^{-\varepsilon})\right];$$ $$p>X'';\qquad p\notin\mathcal I\cup\mathcal S_{\rm sig};\qquad p\in\mathcal I,\ \operatorname{ord}(h_p)\text{ odd},\ h_p\ne0.$$ The first display is one conjunction; the other three displayed conditions are alternatives to it. The global sharing and fixed normalizations of the constants are imposed in ([Fixed constants in an arithmetic family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.parConsts/)).
