## Integral twisting conditions for arithmetic families

[Definition / hypothesis GC.D125](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TwIn/)

Use ([Arithmetic families with local and theta data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/family-arithmetic-data.html)), ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)), ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)), and ([Twist period lattices and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NChoice/)) for the same family. In particular $E$ is the CM field, $\mathfrak c$ its chosen quadratic-field ideal, $g_0,g_1,p_\Delta,p'_\Delta$ its generator and excluded primes, $G_E$ its absolute Galois group and $T_u$ its source primes. The local theta spaces, actions, finite-group idempotents, Hecke operators, spectral labels and period maps in this hypothesis are the ones in ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)).

For each finite place $w$ of the coefficient field, use the choices in ([Twist period lattices and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NChoice/)): a twist index $i_w$, a side $\beta_w\in\{+,-\}$, a finite set $Q_w$, an integer $k_w$ and a character $$\psi'_w:R_{i_w,Q_w}\longrightarrow O_{i_w,k_w}^{\times}.$$ Here $R_{i,Q}$ is the specified finite anticyclotomic quotient of $G_E$ in ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)), with its surjection $\operatorname{Art}_{i,Q}:G_E\to R_{i,Q}$. Define the effective character $$\Psi_w=\begin{cases}\psi'_w,&\beta_w=+,\\(\psi'_w)^{-1},&\beta_w=-.\end{cases}$$ The selected theta-input sets $\mathcal D^0_{i,j,b}$ are those of ([Twist period lattices and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NChoice/)).

Hypothesis (integral twisting). There is a threshold $x_{\rm tw}$ such that, at every member with $x\ge x_{\rm tw}$ and $x>x_1$, there are natural numbers $N_0(i)$ and finite subsets $S_{i,j,b}$ of the theta-input sets, for all twist indices $i$, integers $j\ge0$ and sides $b\in\{+,-\}$, satisfying the conditions below. These choices are made once per member. The requirements at a nonbase coefficient place $w$ use $i=i_w$, $Q=Q_w$, $k'=k_w$ and $\Psi=\Psi_w$. At conjugate nonbase places, $i,Q,k'$ and $\Psi$ agree; when $w\ne\bar w$, the sides $\beta_w$ and $\beta_{\bar w}$ are opposite.

Write $\ell=\ell_i$ and $\psi=\Psi\circ\operatorname{Art}_{i,Q}$. This character has open kernel, satisfies $\psi(cgc^{-1})=\psi(g)^{-1}$ for the chosen complex conjugation $c$, takes values in the subgroup generated by the designated primitive $\ell^{k'}$th root of unity, is ramified exactly at $Q$, and is trivial on every decomposition group above $\ell$. Require $k'\ge1$. Every $P\in Q$ satisfies $P\ne\bar P$ and has residue degree and ramification index one over $\mathbb Q$. Require $Q$ to avoid $S_{0,i}$, the levels of every input in $S_{i,j,b}$ for $1\le j\le m$ and both sides, and all places above the nonzero integers in $$\{3,5,g_0,g_1,p_\Delta,p'_\Delta,q_0\}\cup\{T_u:u\in\mathcal U\}\cup\{p\text{ prime}:p\mid\operatorname{Norm}\mathfrak c\}.$$ Here $\mathcal U$ is the finite source-index set. Let $r_0$ be the least prime not dividing $Q_Sqd(p_\Delta-1)(p'_\Delta-1)\operatorname{Norm}\mathfrak c$, and let $q_0$ be the least prime congruent to $1$ modulo $r_0Q_Sqd$ not dividing $\operatorname{Norm}\mathfrak c$; either least value is zero if its set is empty. The set $S_{0,i}$ and the coefficient rings $O_i^0,O_i,O_{i,k'}$ are the choices in ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)). In particular $S_{0,i}$ contains all places above $\ell,g_0,g_1$ and $p_{D,i}$, where $p_{D,i}=p'_\Delta$ if $\ell=p_\Delta$ and $p_{D,i}=p_\Delta$ otherwise.

For $1\le j\le m$ and each side $b\in\{+,-\}$, let $F_{i,j,b}$ and $e_{i,j,b}$ be the vector space and finite-group idempotent in ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)). Write $U$ for its chosen subgroup and $\xi_{i,b}$ for its character $\psi_{i,b}$. The selected space is $$V_{i,j,b}=e_{i,j,b}\{v\in F_{i,j,b}:uv=\xi_{i,b}(u)v\text{ for every }u\in U\}.$$ The theta vectors of $\mathcal D^0_{i,j,b}$ transform by $\xi_{i,b}$. Let $H_{i,j,b}$ be the $O_i$-span of their theta vectors, let $L_{i,j,b}=e_{i,j,b}H_{i,j,b}$, and write $a_{i,j,b}=|G_{\rm fin}|e_{i,j,b}$ for the idempotent multiplied by the designated finite-group order. Require $a_{i,j,b}H_{i,j,b}\subset V_{i,j,b}$ with full fraction-field span, and require $S_{i,j,b}\subseteq\mathcal D^0_{i,j,b}$ with the vectors $a_{i,j,b}\theta_d$ for $d\in S_{i,j,b}$ forming an $O_i$-basis of $a_{i,j,b}H_{i,j,b}$.

Put $\Psi_+=\Psi$, $\Psi_-=\Psi^{-1}$, and $\psi_b=\Psi_b\circ\operatorname{Art}_{i,Q}$ for either side $b$. For $d\in\mathcal D^0_{i,j,b}$, let $\Theta_{d,k',Q,\psi_b}$ be its specialization subset of $O_{i,k'}\otimes_{O_i}F_{i,j,b}$, as in ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)). Let $\mathcal M^{\rm per}_{i,j,b,k',Q,\psi_b}$ and $\theta^{\rm per}$ be the integral period module and its theta map in ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)). Define $$L_{i,j,b}(\psi_b)=e_{i,j,b}\operatorname{span}_{O_{i,k'}} \left(\bigcup_{d\in\mathcal D^0_{i,j,b}}\Theta_{d,k',Q,\psi_b}\right),\qquad P_{i,j,b}(\psi_b)=e_{i,j,b}\theta^{\rm per} (\mathcal M^{\rm per}_{i,j,b,k',Q,\psi_b}),$$ where $e_{i,j,b}$ acts after extension of scalars. Require $L_{i,j,b}(\psi_b)\subset P_{i,j,b}(\psi_b)$ and require $L_{i,j,b}(\psi_b)$ to be a lattice in $O_{i,k'}\otimes_{O_i}V_{i,j,b}$: it is finitely generated, lies in that space, and contains a nonzero multiple of each vector in it. There are nonnegative real numbers $\epsilon_{j,b}$ with $$\sum_{j=1}^m\sum_{b\in\{+,-\}}\epsilon_{j,b}\le\ell^{-2}$$ such that, for every finite injective DVR extension $O_{i,k'}\to R$ and every lattice $M$ in $R\otimes_{O_i}V_{i,j,b}$, $$s_\ell(M,R L_{i,j,b}(\psi_b))\le s_\ell(M,R L_{i,j,b})+\epsilon_{j,b}.$$ Here $RL$ denotes the image after extending scalars and $$s_\ell(M,N)=\operatorname{len}_\ell(M/(M\cap N))-\operatorname{len}_\ell(N/(M\cap N)),\qquad \operatorname{len}_\ell(A)=\frac{\operatorname{length}_R(A)_{\rm nat}}{v_R(\ell)_{\rm nat}},$$ where the natural-number value sends infinity to zero, and division by zero has value zero.

Let $\mathcal S_i$ be the finite set of spectral labels $s$ for which a nonzero vector in the image of the same finite-group idempotent has the specified Hecke eigenvalues for every operator indexed by $1\le h\le r$ and $P\notin S_{0,i}$ with $P\ne\bar P$. Write $\rho_s$ for the attached rank-$r$ representation and $\chi_s=\chi_{i,j(s),b(s)}$ for its rank-one normalizing character, with the stage $j(s)$ and side $b(s)$ belonging to $s$. On the torsion module $(\operatorname{Frac}O_{i,k'}/O_{i,k'})^r$ take the action $\rho_s\chi_s^{-1}\psi_{b(s)}^{-1}$, where $b(s)$ is its selected side. Let $\operatorname{Sel}_s(\psi_{b(s)})$ be the subgroup of $H^1(E,-)$ with finite Bloch–Kato local condition at places above $\ell$, no restriction at $Q$, at the generator places and at places above $p_{D,i}$ away from $\ell$, and unramified condition at every other finite place. The finite local condition means the span of reductions of finite period-ring cocycles for $\rho_s\chi_s^{-1}$ under the fixed local comparison maps. Normalize its extended length by $v_{O_{i,k'}}(\ell)$ and call it $\mathcal L_s$. There are nonnegative $\epsilon_s$ with $$\sum_{s\in\mathcal S_i}\epsilon_s\le\ell^{-2},\qquad \mathcal L_s\le\nu_\ell+\epsilon_s\quad(s\in\mathcal S_i).$$ Here $$\nu_\ell=\inf\{a\in\mathbb N:\exists g\in G_E,\ \ell^{a+1}\nmid\epsilon_\ell(g)^{tr}-1\text{ in }\mathbb Z_\ell\},$$ with natural infimum zero for an empty set and $\epsilon_\ell$ the cyclotomic character. The normalized Selmer length retains infinity. Use the local period rings in ([Local period rings and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)) and the fixed local coefficient embeddings and decomposition-group identifications. More explicitly, at $v\mid\ell$ transport $\rho_s\chi_s^{-1}$ to a representation $R_{iv,s}$ on a finite local field $L_{iv}^r$. A finite cocycle is a continuous function $c$ satisfying $c(gh)=c(g)+R_{iv,s}(g)c(h)$ and $c(g)\otimes1=gX-X$ for some $X\in L_{iv}^r\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}B_{\rm cris}$. The chosen isomorphism $O_i^0\simeq\mathcal O_{L_{iv}}$ identifies $L_{iv}$ with $\operatorname{Frac}(O_i^0)$. Map the coordinates through this identification into $\operatorname{Frac}(O_{i,k'})$, restrict along the chosen isomorphism $D_v\simeq G_{E_v}$, and reduce modulo $O_{i,k'}$; the $O_{i,k'}$-span of the resulting classes is the finite local subgroup used above. Only reductions that are cocycles for the twisted torsion action are included. For a coefficient DVR $R$, put $v_\ell^{\rm int}(u)=\widetilde v_R(u)/\widetilde v_R(\ell)$, where $\widetilde v_R$ is its nonnegative integer valuation with infinity sent to zero. For $z\in\operatorname{Frac}(R)$ use its fixed fraction presentation $z=n_z/d_z$, $d_z\ne0$, and put $v_\ell(z)=v_\ell^{\rm int}(n_z)-v_\ell^{\rm int}(d_z)$. For nonzero $z$ this is the valuation normalized by $v_\ell(\ell)=1$.

For each abelian theta label $\lambda$, let $c_{\lambda,\delta}(Q)$ be the specified theta coefficients on $R_{i,Q}$. Map these coefficients from $\operatorname{Frac}(O_i)$ to $\operatorname{Frac}(O_{i,k'})$ when evaluating the sum. Put $$\Theta_\lambda(Q,\Psi)=\sum_\delta c_{\lambda,\delta}(Q)\Psi_{b(\lambda)}(\delta),\qquad c_{0,\lambda}=v_\ell\left(\sum_\delta c_{\lambda,\delta}(\varnothing)\right).$$ With the same $N_0(i)$ at every stage and side, require $$\Theta_\lambda(Q,\Psi)\ne0,\qquad -N_0(i)\le v_\ell(\Theta_\lambda(Q,\Psi))\le c_{0,\lambda}+\ell^{-2}$$ for every $\lambda$. Finally require $$t\sum_{P\in Q}\tau_\ell(\psi,P)\le\ell^{-2},\qquad \tau_\ell(\psi,P)=\inf_{\substack{g\in I_P\\\psi(g)\ne1}}v_\ell(\psi(g)-1).$$ The real infimum of an empty set is assigned the value $0$. These conditions hold for the selected twist at every nonbase finite place.

## Conditions on twisting, Galois data and cutting a datum

[Definition / hypothesis AGR.D036](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.GalC.GaloisPreconditions/)

This entry states the outer form of three hypotheses used in "A divisibility bound for a family from twisting and Galois conditions". A datum and a family are as in ([Data, families of data, classes of families, and properties of all sufficiently constrained families of a class](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.Closed/)). At each member above the starting scale, use the coefficient choices, period maps and selected theta-input sets in ([Twist period lattices and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NChoice/)).

**Twisting condition.** Require ([Integral twisting conditions for arithmetic families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TwIn/)), with the same selected theta-input sets and choices at the coefficient places.

**Conditions on a datum and its choice.** The first condition is a conjunction of 18 clauses. Their full constituent predicates are not expanded. Their outer forms include: a record of five conditions, three themselves records of respectively 10, 4 and 6 conditions, plus a condition at every local index and a rank equality for every size datum; a conjunction of three conditions; a second conjunction of three conditions; and a three-part statement about kernels, images and a sum of multiplicities. Another clause requires, for each relevant residual representation, irreducibility after scalar extension and nonvanishing of a specified characteristic polynomial at a specified scalar away from the two distinguished primes. Two clauses give the multisets of weights at a pair of embeddings as $\{p-\tfrac12\}$ and $\{-(p-\tfrac12)\}$, for $p$ in an attached multiset, and the weights of another object as singletons $\{-a-\tfrac12\}$ and $\{a+\tfrac12\}$, respectively. The multiset, integer and objects are not expanded.

Further clauses require an unramified condition away from the two distinguished primes; at specified places above either of them, the polynomial of every indicated wild-inertia element is $(X-1)^{n_V}$; an implication between two conditions on spherical base change; a condition for each used tuple and every $1\le j\le m$; and uniqueness of a local index when two indices have the same row and place and a good object occurs at both. The remaining clauses assert conjunctions and universally quantified conditions on residual eigenvalues, coefficients, choices, triples, twists and good objects; their predicates are not expanded. The choice-compatibility clause requires the character and embedding compatibilities in ([Twist period lattices and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NChoice/)) for those same coefficient choices.

**Four further conditions on a datum.** These are a conjunction of four assertions. The first imposes, at each of two distinguished primes and each relevant place, equations identifying linear polynomials with $X-u$ or $X-u^{-1}$, where $u$ is one of a specified unit and its inverse. The second asserts existence of a record containing finite-group idempotents, characters of inertia, actions on modules and compatibility equations, and satisfying two more conditions: a polynomial factorisation and inverse-root relation under a squarefreeness hypothesis, and an implication carrying a scalar action to a Hecke eigenspace. The third is a polynomial annihilation condition for a potentially crystalline representation, under its stated local hypotheses. The fourth says that nonzero action of a specified idempotent on an indicated parameter space forces its polynomial label to equal the parameter's label. The objects, remaining hypotheses and constructions in these four assertions are not expanded.

**Cutting assertion.** For every datum and choice satisfying both preceding conditions, there is a cut with the following properties. A cut selects local indices and a subset of labels at each local index, nonempty whenever that index is selected. It selects exactly the indices for which the set of good labels is nonempty, contains every good label, and among labels in a specified set contains only good labels. At each selected index there is an endomorphism commuting with all the Hecke operators, acting by a specified finite-group cardinality on every good-label eigenspace and by zero on every eigenspace with label outside the selected subset. For every selected index, selected label, matching object, and place above either distinguished prime, a conjunction of three local conditions holds; these conditions are not expanded.

The cut gives a new datum and transports the choice; neither construction is expanded. The new datum and transported choice satisfy the four further conditions above and a record of norm conditions. That record has a core of 15 clauses and seven additional clauses. Among its core clauses are the first three clauses of the 18 conditions, the residual, weight, local, base-change and used-tuple conditions listed above, a three-part conclusion whose last part is the kernel, image and multiplicity statement above, and two further requirements: a condition for every good pair and a second condition on such pairs at the distinguished primes, including equality of two weight lists and distinctness of constituents; and, for every size datum, an implication from an admissibility condition to a conjunction of six conditions. The seven additional clauses include the uniqueness, coefficient, choice, triple, twist and good-object conditions above and another conjunction of four conditions. The internal predicates and quantifiers of these additional requirements are not expanded.
