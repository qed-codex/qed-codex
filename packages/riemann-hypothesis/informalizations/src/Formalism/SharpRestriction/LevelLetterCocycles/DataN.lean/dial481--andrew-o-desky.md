## Identifications for arithmetic family members

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D133](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)

Hypothesis (member identifications). Choose data satisfying ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)), ([Hermitian spaces in the theta constructions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.CMData/)), ([Moment data for the family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.S.Draft.SData/)), ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)), ([Local parameter systems for the family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NAut/)), ([Finite types and idempotents for the family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NTypes/)), ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)), ([Local period rings and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)), and ([Local formal theta series](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ThetaJet/)). The following conditions identify these choices with the columns and Hermitian lattices used in the argument.

The coefficient field $K$ is a totally complex Galois number field with an involution carried by $\jmath:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ to complex conjugation. There is an embedding $E\hookrightarrow K$ commuting with conjugation and composing with $\jmath$ to $\phi_0:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$. Choose an intermediate field $K_0\subseteq K$ containing $E$. The image of $O\to K$ is integral over $\mathbb Z$ and contains $\mathcal O_E$. The abstract coefficient places correspond bijectively to nonzero prime ideals of $\mathcal O_K$; their valuations on nonzero elements and ramification indices are the normalized ideal valuations and indices over $\mathbb Z$, and $$v_{\bar w}(x)=v_w(\bar x).$$ Identify $\mathcal T$ bijectively with the selected CM type. The source set $\mathcal W$ is finite with a chosen $z_0$. Each source $z$ has the rational arrays $U^z,V^z,B^z,E^z$ satisfying ([Interlacing weight arrays](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.HCParam/)); at $z_0$ these equal the common arrays. The $V,B,E$ rows and the first and last $U$ entries are independent of $z$.

For each source choose the data of ([Column chains and period pairings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Member/)), with its own weight array, and the compatible data of ([Integral forms on column representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.MemberForms/)). Write $T_z,\Gamma_z,D_z^*,T_z^*$ for its prime and comparison parameters. Require $T_z\in\mathcal P_{\rm lev}$, $T_z\notin\{p_\Delta,p'_\Delta\}$, and choose $\pi_z\in\mathcal O_E\cap E_0$ and $c_z\in E^\times$ satisfying $$\pi_z\bar\pi_z=T_z,\quad\pi_z\equiv1\pmod{Q_Sqd(g_0g_1)^2},\quad c_z\bar c_zT_z=T_{z_0},$$ $$T_z^*\le T_z\le(1+1/(fk))T_z^*,\qquad\Gamma_z=\Gamma_{z_0},\qquad D_z^*=D_*.$$ The prime in ([Hermitian spaces in the theta constructions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.CMData/)) is $T_{z_0}$. For every source, stage and class, choose a full $\mathcal O_E$-lattice in $X_j$. Choose a positive real-valued function on all Hermitian spaces with recorded lattice data, and positive numbers $d_\tau$ for $\tau\in\mathcal T$.

Write $U_z^{\rm ram}$ and $P_z^{\rm per}$ for the initial ramified space and final period space of the source's columns. Choose complex-linear isomorphisms $$e_z:U_z\xrightarrow\sim U_z^{\rm ram},\qquad e_z^{\rm per}:P_z^{\rm per}\xrightarrow\sim A_{\mu_0}.$$ The pairing $P_{z,\mu_0}$ equals the column-period pairing after these isomorphisms. The functions $B_z:U_z\times H\to\mathbb C$ are complex-bilinear. For every source and twist $\mu$, choose a column chain with the same rational weight array, equal to the original chain at $\mu_0$, an isomorphism from the original initial column-function space to its twisted version, period data for the twisted chain, and an isomorphism of its final period space with $A_\mu$. For $\mu\ne\mu_0$, the pairing $P_{z,\mu}$ equals the resulting twisted column-period pairing under these identifications.

Choose the source and target lattice data of ([Hermitian row lattices and their rational models](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DivData/)). Their archimedean frame matrices are invertible. Their total-dimension and dimension-defect parameters equal $D_{\rm all},\delta$ of ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)). There are $\jmath$-semilinear maps from their rational spaces to $H$ and $U_z$ carrying the specified rational bases to complex bases. The target map preserves the norm at the chosen complex embedding. There is also an injective $K$-linear map from each rational source space onto the rational ramified subspace of its stage-zero column space, preserving all archimedean norms and agreeing after complex realization with $e_z$. The map $e_z$ identifies the inner product on $U_z$ with the stabilizer-weighted column form.

Choose a commutative group $D_{\det}$, a subgroup $D^0$, characters $\widetilde\mu_i,\chi:D_{\det}\to\mathbb C^\times$ for every $i\ge0$, and labels $d_{z,i,c}\in D_{\det}$ for all source classes. Require $\chi|_{D^0}=1$. Choose a homomorphism $e_1:\{a\in E^\times:a\bar a=1\}\to D_{\det}$ with $$\chi(e_1(a))=\prod_{\tau\in\Sigma}\tau(a)^{-1}.$$ For every $N$ and pair of full self-dual lattices in standard $E^N$, choose $\delta_N(L,L')\in D_{\det}/D^0$ with $$\delta_N(L,L)=1,\quad\delta_N(L,L')\delta_N(L',L'')=\delta_N(L,L''),\quad \delta_N(L,\gamma L)=[e_1(\det\gamma)]$$ for every standard unitary $\gamma\in\mathrm U_N(E)$. The two chain phases satisfy $$\rho_{z,i}(c)=\widetilde\mu_{i+1}(d_{z,i,c})^{-1}\chi(d_{z,i,c})^{-\lfloor N_{i+1}/2\rfloor},$$ $$\rho'_{z,i}(c)=\widetilde\mu_{i+1}(d_{z,i+1,c})\chi(d_{z,i+1,c})^{\lfloor N_i/2\rfloor}.$$

For the moment data, the optional split identification at a finite place of $F$ is supplied exactly when its residue characteristic is $g_0$ or $g_1$. Require $\dim H_{\rm mom}=D_{\rm all}$. There are complex-linear isomorphisms $C_z\simeq U_z^0$ identifying their inner products with the stage-zero column forms, a bijection $\mathcal P_F\simeq\{\text{finite places of }F\}$, and a conjugate-linear isometry $H\to H_{\rm mom}$.

For every local coefficient index $i$ and every place $v\mid\ell_i$, choose a coefficient comparison of ([Local period rings and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)). Denote its two finite extensions of $\mathbb Q_{\ell_i}$ by $K_{iv},L_{iv}\subseteq\overline{\mathbb Q}_{\ell_i}$, its embedding by $j_{iv}:\Omega\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathbb Q}_{\ell_i}$, its group isomorphism by $e_{iv}:D_v\xrightarrow\sim G_{K_{iv}}$, and its coefficient-ring isomorphism by $b_{iv}:O_i\xrightarrow\sim\mathcal O_{L_{iv}}$. Require $$x\in\mathcal O_{\Omega,v}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad |j_{iv}(x)|_{\ell_i}\le1\qquad(x\in\Omega),$$ where $\mathcal O_{\Omega,v}$ is the chosen valuation ring above $v$. Require $L_{iv}$ to contain every root of unity in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_{\ell_i}$ whose order divides $\operatorname{lcm}(\ell_i,Q_Sqd)$. The coefficient embedding $\iota_i:\operatorname{Frac}(O_i)\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathbb Q}_{\ell_i}$ in ([Local parameter systems for the family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NAut/)) agrees with $b_{iv}$ on $O_i$.

For every twist index $a$ and place $v\mid\ell_a$, also choose a coefficient comparison for $D_v$ and $O_a^0$, whose embedding $j_{av}$ satisfies $$x\in\mathcal O_{\Omega,v}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad |j_{av}(x)|_{\ell_a}\le1\qquad(x\in\Omega).$$

The local predicates and weight multisets must agree with these comparisons. Fix $i$, $v\mid\ell_i$, a natural number $a$ and a continuous representation $\rho:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_a(O_i)$. Let $\rho^\iota$ be obtained by applying $\iota_i$ to its matrices, and let $V_{\rho,v}$ be its restriction to $D_v$, transported through $e_{iv}$ and $b_{iv}$ to an $L_{iv}$-representation of $G_{K_{iv}}$. For the predicates of ([Local parameter systems for the family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NAut/)), require $$\mathsf C_{i,v}(\rho^\iota)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ V_{\rho,v}\text{ is crystalline},\qquad \mathsf{DR}_{i,v}(\rho^\iota)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ V_{\rho,v}\text{ is de Rham},$$ $$\mathsf{PC}_{i,v}(\rho^\iota)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ V_{\rho,v}\text{ is potentially crystalline},$$ with the period conditions defined in ([Local period rings and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)).

Write $c_{iv}:E\hookrightarrow K_{iv}$ for the restriction of $j_{iv}$. For every $\mathbb Q_{\ell_i}$-embedding $\tau':K_{iv}\hookrightarrow L_{iv}$, put $\tau=\tau'\circ c_{iv}$, viewed as an embedding into $\overline{\mathbb Q}_{\ell_i}$. If $V_{\rho,v}$ is de Rham, require, for every integer $h$, $$h\in J_{i,\tau}(\rho^\iota)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad F_{\tau'}^{h+1}(V_{\rho,v})\ne F_{\tau'}^h(V_{\rho,v}),$$ $$\operatorname{mult}_{J_{i,\tau}(\rho^\iota)}(h) =\dim_{L_{iv}}F_{\tau'}^h(V_{\rho,v})- \dim_{L_{iv}}F_{\tau'}^{h+1}(V_{\rho,v}).$$ Here $F_{\tau'}^h$ is the filtered de Rham space in ([Local period rings and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)), and $\operatorname{mult}$ counts an integer's occurrences in a multiset.

Require the same three equivalences and both weight identities for every finite extension $L'\subseteq\overline{\mathbb Q}_{\ell_i}$ containing $L_{iv}$ and every continuous $\rho:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_a(L')$. In this case use the natural inclusion of $L'$ in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_{\ell_i}$, transport the restriction of $\rho$ through $e_{iv}$, and allow every $\mathbb Q_{\ell_i}$-embedding $\tau':K_{iv}\hookrightarrow L'$. The dimension differences are taken over $L'$.

The idempotents on $A_\mu,T_{\mu,j}$ equal the actions of $e^{\rm sel}$ in ([Finite types and idempotents for the family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NTypes/)). The rational coefficient function $e_0$ in ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)), embedded by $K_0\subset K\xrightarrow{\jmath}\mathbb C$, is the coefficient function of $e^{\rm sel}$. For $1\le j\le m$, require $\eta_{j,w}=\sum_{i\in I(j,w)}\eta_i$, using the local congruence quantities of ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)).

Choose Haar measures with the compact-dual normalization, including its reference Lebesgue convention, in ([Formal degree with compact-dual normalization](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.RgFd.B.formalDegreeCompactDual/)). In ([Local formal theta series](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ThetaJet/)), each situation selects a source, classes at stages $j_a-1,j_a$ and a coefficient place of residue characteristic $\ell_a$; a situation in $I$ also selects a map $\mathbb Z/d_a\mathbb Z\to\mathcal T$, and one in $I_1$ selects one element of $\mathcal T$. Choose positive reals $\Omega_{\tau,\sigma}$ and $\theta_{1,\sigma}$ for every $\tau\in\mathcal T$ and complex embedding $\sigma:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$.

For every source $z$ and finite coefficient place $w$, put $p=\operatorname{char}(\mathcal O_K/\mathfrak p_w)$. Suppose $p\ge5$, $p$ belongs to the inert prime class, $p\notin\mathcal P_{\rm lev}$ and $p\nmid T_z$. Suppose also that, for every prime ideal of $\mathcal O_E$ above $p$ and every arithmetic Frobenius at that prime, the $A$-coordinate has even order. For each such pair $(z,w)$, choose data satisfying ([Local polynomial lattice settings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.GAtP/)), with prime $p$. The rank $d_{z,w}$ of its integral equivariant-function module must satisfy $d_{z,w}=\dim_\mathbb C U_z^0$.

For $0\le j\le m$, require the signature of $Y_j$ at every complex embedding to equal $$\left(r+jt-\left\lfloor\frac{m-j}{2}\right\rfloor,\ \left\lfloor\frac{m-j}{2}\right\rfloor\right).$$

## Integral theta expansions and local parameters

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D138](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)

Hypothesis (integral theta expansions). Use $E,\Omega,G_E,K_0$, the twist indices $a$, residue primes $\ell_a$, rings $O_a^0,O'_a,O'_{a,h}$, finite place sets $S_{0,a}$, finite label sets $\mathcal V_u$, integral modules $B_{a,j,b}$ and operators $H_{a,j,b,k,v}$ of ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)). Write $K'_a=\operatorname{Frac}(O'_a)$, $K'_{a,h}=\operatorname{Frac}(O'_{a,h})$ and $$G_{\rm fin}=\prod_{u=0}^1\prod_{v\in\mathcal V_u}\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb F_{g_u}).$$ Throughout, $h,M$ range over nonnegative integers and $Q$ over finite sets of finite places of $E$.

For every $a$, integer $j\ge0$ and side $b\in\{+,-\}$, choose a finite-dimensional $K'_a$-vector space $F_{a,j,b}$, with the restricted $O'_a$-module structure, and a representation of $G_{\rm fin}$ on this space. Choose a subgroup $U\subseteq G_{\rm fin}$ and characters $\psi_{a,b}:U\to(K'_a)^\times$. Choose a function $e_0:G_{\rm fin}\to K_0$ and central idempotents $e_{a,b}\in K'_a[G_{\rm fin}]$ whose coefficients are the images of $e_0$ under $K_0\to\operatorname{Frac}(O_a^0)\to K'_a$, using the embedding selected by $b$.

Choose submodules $B^{\rm sup}_{a,j,b}\subseteq F_{a,j,b}$, sets $\mathcal D_{a,j,b}$, elements $\theta_d\in F_{a,j,b}$ and finite sets $L_d$ of finite places of $E$ for $d\in\mathcal D_{a,j,b}$. For every $h,Q$ and group homomorphism $\psi:G_E\to(O'_{a,h})^\times$, choose subsets $$\Theta_{d,h,Q,\psi}\subseteq O'_{a,h}\otimes_{O'_a}F_{a,j,b},\qquad \Theta_{d,h,\varnothing,1}=\{1\otimes\theta_d\}.$$ For every $Q$ and natural number $M$, put $\Delta_{a,Q,M}=(\mathbb Z/\ell_a^M\mathbb Z)^Q$. Choose elements $$\theta^U_{d,Q,M}\in F_{a,j,b}\otimes_{O'_a}O'_a[\Delta_{a,Q,M}],$$ where the bracket denotes the group algebra of the additive finite group. Choose $O'_a$-linear maps $$T_\psi:F_{a,j,b}\to O'_{a,h}\otimes_{O'_a}F_{a,j,b},\quad T_1(f)=1\otimes f,$$ $$T_{Q,M}:F_{a,j,b}\to F_{a,j,b}\otimes_{O'_a}O'_a[\Delta_{a,Q,M}].$$

Choose homomorphisms $\delta_{a,Q,M}:G_E\to\Delta_{a,Q,M}$ with open kernel, unramified outside $Q$, zero on every decomposition group above $\ell_a$, and with $\delta(cgc^{-1})=-\delta(g)$ for the chosen conjugation $c$. If $Q'\subseteq Q$ and both sets are invariant under conjugation, require restriction to the $Q'$ coordinates to give $\delta_{a,Q',M}$. For every conjugate pair $v,v'\in Q$, require a sign $\epsilon\in\{1,-1\}$ such that $\delta_{a,Q,M}(g)_{v'}=\epsilon\delta_{a,Q,M}(g)_v$ for all $g$.

For every $a,j,b,h,Q,\psi$, choose a finite-dimensional $K'_{a,h}$-vector space $V^{\rm per}_{a,j,b,h,Q,\psi}$, with the restricted $O'_{a,h}$-module structure and a finitely generated submodule $M^{\rm per}_{a,j,b,h,Q,\psi}$ spanning it. Choose $O'_{a,h}$-linear maps $$\theta^{\rm per}:V^{\rm per}_{a,j,b,h,Q,\psi}\to O'_{a,h}\otimes_{O'_a}F_{a,j,b}.$$ Choose $K'_a$-linear endomorphisms $H^F_{a,j,b,k,v}$ for every nonnegative integer $k$ and finite place $v$, injective $O'_a$-linear maps $\iota_B:B_{a,j,b}\to F_{a,j,b}$, and $K'_a$-linear idempotents $e^H_{a,j,b}$ on $F_{a,j,b}$.

Hypothesis (evaluation and theta functions). For each $a$, choose an embedding $\iota_a:K'_a\hookrightarrow\mathbb C_{\ell_a}$ with $|\iota_a(x)|\le1$ exactly for $x\in O'_a$. Choose an integer $a_a$ equal to one if $\ell_a\in\{g_0,g_1\}$ and zero otherwise. For each $a,j$, choose a set $P_{a,j}$ and injective additive evaluation maps $$\operatorname{ev}_{a,j,b}:F_{a,j,b}\longrightarrow\mathbb C_{\ell_a}^{P_{a,j}},\qquad \operatorname{ev}(xf)(y)=\iota_a(x)\operatorname{ev}(f)(y)\quad(x\in K'_a).$$ Choose a finitely generated free abelian group $L_{a,j}$ with an $\mathcal O_E$-module structure, a finite variable set $I_{a,j}$, a set $C_{a,j}$ and coefficients $c_{\kappa,u,v}\in O'_a$ for $\kappa\in C_{a,j}$, $u,v\in I_{a,j}$. The specified subset of harmonic polynomials is exactly $$\mathcal H_{a,j}=\left\{P\in O'_a[X_u:u\in I_{a,j}]: \sum_{u,v}c_{\kappa,u,v}\partial_u\partial_v P=0\text{ for every }\kappa\in C_{a,j}\right\}.$$ Choose functions $$\vartheta_{a,j}(\phi,P):P_{a,j}\to\mathbb C_{\ell_a} \quad(\phi:L_{a,j}\to K'_a,\ P\in O'_a[X_u:u\in I_{a,j}]),$$ additive in both arguments, $K'_a$-linear in $\phi$ through $\iota_a$, and $O'_a$-linear in $P$ through $\iota_a$. Each expansion index $d\in\mathcal D_{a,j,b}$ has a function $\phi_d:L_{a,j}\to K'_a$ and a polynomial $P_d\in O'_a[X_u:u\in I_{a,j}]$.

Choose maps $r_{a,j}:P_{a,j}\to G_E$. For each finite place $v$, choose a group $K_{a,j,v}$ acting on the right on $P_{a,j}$, and a map $r_{a,j,v}:P_{a,j}\to D_v$. Let $A$ be any commutative group. For every anticyclotomic homomorphism $\chi:G_E\to A$ with open kernel, require $$\chi(r(y\cdot k))\chi(r_v(y))=\chi(r(y))\chi(r_v(y\cdot k))\quad(k\in K_{a,j,v}).$$ For every homomorphism $\chi:G_E\to A$ with open kernel, every $v'\notin\{v,\bar v\}$ and $k\in K_{a,j,v}$, require $\chi(r_{v'}(y\cdot k))=\chi(r_{v'}(y))$.

For every $a,j,k,v$, choose a finite set $C_{a,j,k,v}$, maps $m_c:P_{a,j}\to P_{a,j}$ and functions $u_{b,c}:P_{a,j}\to\mathbb C_{\ell_a}$. For $1\le k\le r$, $v\ne\bar v$ and $v\notin S_{0,a}$, require $$\operatorname{ev}_{a,j,b}(H^F_{a,j,b,k,v}f)(y)= \sum_{c\in C_{a,j,k,v}}u_{b,c}(y)\operatorname{ev}_{a,j,b}(f)(m_c(y)).$$ Choose a subfield $K_a^0\subseteq K'_a$ and additive subgroups $F^0_{a,j,b}\subseteq F_{a,j,b}$ stable under $K_a^0$, whose $K'_a$-span is $F_{a,j,b}$. Require $$B^{\rm sup}_{a,j,b}=\operatorname{span}_{O'_a} \{f\in F^0_{a,j,b}:|\operatorname{ev}_{a,j,b}(f)(y)|\le1\text{ for every }y\in P_{a,j}\}.$$

## Galois representations and coefficient twists

[Definition / hypothesis AGR.D131](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)

Hypothesis (Galois representations and coefficient twists). Use the fields, primes and ranks of ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)) and the spaces and coefficient field $K$ of ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)). Choose an algebraic closure $\Omega$ of $E$ and a valuation ring of $\Omega$ above each finite place of $E$. Write $G_E=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/E)$ and $D_v,I_v$ for the decomposition and inertia groups for these choices. Write $\bar v$ for the complex-conjugate prime and $Nv$ for the residue cardinality. A homomorphism is unramified at $v$ when it is trivial on the inertia group for every valuation of $\Omega$ above $v$. It is ramified exactly at a set $Q$ when, for every finite prime $v$ and every valuation above $v$, its inertia image is nontrivial if and only if $v\in Q$.

For each prime $\ell$, choose the finite set of places above $\ell$, a valuation ring of $\Omega$ above each of them, and the factorization of the cyclotomic character through the Galois group unramified outside those places. Use the localization lengths $\lambda_\ell(E,c)$ defined in ([Window condition: a bound on the Tate-Shafarevich groups of the twists Q\_l/Z\_l(-k) over auxiliary abelian fields](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Top.VWin/)), with these choices. The nonnegative rational numbers $h_{\ell,c}$ in ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)) satisfy $$\lambda_\ell(E,c)=n<\infty\quad\Longrightarrow\quad h_{\ell,c}=n \qquad(1\le c<t,\ n\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}).$$

Choose a set of local coefficient indices $i$. Each has a prime $\ell_i$, a stage $1\le j_i\le m$ and a coefficient place $w_i$ of residue characteristic $\ell_i$. Require $\mu_i=\nu_{w_i}$ and $\mu_{w_i}\ne\mu_0$. The sets $I(j,w)=\{i:j_i=j,w_i=w\}$ are finite for every $j\ge0$ and coefficient place $w$. At each $i$, choose a complete DVR $O_i$ finite over $\mathbb Z_{\ell_i}$, a finite nonempty set $X_i$, a local subalgebra $\mathbb T_i\subseteq O_i^{X_i}$ separating the points of $X_i$, a finite free $O_i$-module $M_i$, and an injective $O_i$-algebra homomorphism $\mathbb T_i\to\operatorname{End}_{O_i}(M_i)$. Put $K_i=\operatorname{Frac}(O_i)$ and $V_i=K_i\otimes_{O_i}M_i$. For $x\in X_i$, define $$V_{i,x}=\{z\in V_i:a z=a(x)z\text{ for every }a\in\mathbb T_i\},\qquad m_{i,x}=\dim_{K_i}V_{i,x},\qquad \Lambda_i=\operatorname{im}(M_i\longrightarrow V_i).$$ Require every $V_{i,x}$ to be nonzero. Choose two subsets $Y_i,Z_i\subseteq X_i$.

For an $O_i$-module $M$, let $\operatorname{length}^0_{O_i}(M)$ be its module length when finite, and zero when its length is infinite. Put $$\operatorname{len}_{\ell_i}(M)= \frac{\operatorname{length}^0_{O_i}(M)}{\operatorname{length}^0_{O_i}(O_i/\ell_iO_i)}\in\mathbb Q,$$ with division by zero assigned the value zero. Define $$V_i^Y=\sum_{x\in Y_i}V_{i,x},\qquad V_i^{Y^c}=\sum_{x\in X_i\setminus Y_i}V_{i,x},$$ $$\eta_i=\operatorname{len}_{\ell_i}\!\left( \frac{V_i^Y\cap(\Lambda_i+V_i^{Y^c})}{\Lambda_i\cap V_i^Y}\right).$$ The intersections and quotient are taken as $O_i$-modules. For $x\in X_i$, let $\mathfrak c_{i,x}\subseteq O_i$ be the ideal generated by all $a(x)$ with $a\in\mathbb T_i$ vanishing on $X_i\setminus Z_i$, and put $$c_{i,x}=\operatorname{len}_{\ell_i}(O_i/\mathfrak c_{i,x}).$$

The topology of $O_i$ is its maximal-ideal adic topology. Choose an embedding $\jmath_i:K\hookrightarrow K_i$ satisfying $$\jmath_i(x)\in O_i\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad v_{w_i}(x)\ge0.$$ There is a $\jmath_i$-semilinear map $$q_i:T^K_{\nu_{w_i},j_i}\longrightarrow K_i\otimes_{O_i}M_i$$ whose $K_i$-span is the whole target. For the finite set $\mathcal G_{\nu_{w_i},j_i}$ of integral generators and the complex realization $j_{\nu_{w_i},j_i}$ in ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)), require $$\operatorname{span}_{O_i}\{q_i(x):j_{\nu_{w_i},j_i}(x)\in\mathcal G_{\nu_{w_i},j_i}\}=\Lambda_i.$$

Continuity of coefficient characters means continuity of their values in the coefficient ring, and continuity of matrix representations means continuity of their entries. Choose continuous characters $\xi_{i,c}:G_E\to O_i^\times$ for all integers $c\ge0$, with $$\xi_{i,c}=\xi_{i,1}\epsilon_{\ell_i}^{-(c-1)}\quad(1\le c\le t),\qquad u_i=\begin{cases}v_2(Q_S),&\ell_i=2,\\\max\{1,v_{\ell_i}(Q_S)\},&\ell_i\ne2.\end{cases}$$ Here $\epsilon_\ell$ is the cyclotomic character, mapped into $O_i^\times$. Choose finite sets $S_i,Q_i$ of places of $E$, with $S_i\cap Q_i=\varnothing$, containing no place above $\ell_i$, and with every member distinct from its complex-conjugate place. Put $$p_{D,i}=\begin{cases}p'_\Delta,&\ell_i=p_\Delta,\\p_\Delta,&\ell_i\ne p_\Delta,\end{cases}\qquad S_i=\{v:v\mid g_0g_1p_{D,i},\ v\nmid\ell_i\}.$$ For every finite place $v$, choose $\gamma_{i,v}\in I_v$; if $v\in S_i\cup Q_i$, its image generates tame inertia topologically. More precisely, inertia is contained in the closure of the subgroup generated by $\gamma_{i,v}$ and the elements $\sigma$ for which $|\sigma y/y-1|_v<1$ for every $y\in\Omega^\times$. At every $v\in Q_i$, $\xi_{i,1}$ is nontrivial on inertia.

Choose Hecke elements $T_{i,v,h}\in\mathbb T_i$ for all finite $v$ and integers $h\ge0$. The elements with $v\ne\bar v$, $v\nmid\ell_i$, $v\notin S_i\cup Q_i$ and $1\le h\le n_V$ generate $\mathbb T_i$ as an $O_i$-algebra. Choose a finite free $O_i$-module $B_i$. The primes $g_0,g_1$ split completely in $E$. For $a=0,1$, choose a set $\mathcal V_a$ of size $f$, monic degree-$r$ polynomials $F_{a,\pi,v}\in\mathbb F_{g_a}[X]$ for every type label $\pi$ and $v\in\mathcal V_a$, and units $\chi_{a,v,j}\in\mathbb F_{g_a}^\times$ for $0\le j<m$. Require $$d_G=\prod_{a=0}^1|\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb F_{g_a})|^f.$$

For the character-twist data, take $K$ to be a number field with a specified field involution $x\mapsto\bar x$ and a specified subfield $K_0\subseteq K$. The character-twist data have a separate index set $\mathcal I_{\rm tw}$. Choose an automorphism $c$ of $\Omega$ inducing complex conjugation under some embedding $\Omega\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ and carrying $E$ into itself. A homomorphism $\psi:G_E\to A$ to a commutative group is called anticyclotomic here when $\psi(cgc^{-1})=\psi(g)^{-1}$ for every $g$.

For $a\in\mathcal I_{\rm tw}$ choose a prime $\ell_a$, a DVR $O_a^0$ finite over $\mathbb Z_{\ell_a}$ with finite residue field and its adic topology, and two embeddings $\jmath_a^+,\jmath_a^-:K_0\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Frac}(O_a^0)$ such that $\jmath_a^-(x)=\jmath_a^+(\bar x)$ whenever $x,\bar x\in K_0$. Choose an injective local extension $O_a^0\to O'_a$ of DVRs, where $O'_a$ is complete with its adic topology, has algebraically closed residue field and has $\ell_a$ in its maximal ideal. For each integer $h\ge0$, choose an injective finite DVR extension $O'_a\to O'_{a,h}$ and a primitive $\ell_a^h$th root $\zeta_{a,h}\in O'_{a,h}$. Require every element of $\operatorname{Frac}(O'_{a,h})$ to be $P(\zeta_{a,h})/d$ for some $P\in O'_a[X]$ and $d\in O'_a\setminus\{0\}$.

Let $D_a$ be all places above $p'_\Delta$ if $\ell_a=p_\Delta$, and all places above $p_\Delta$ otherwise. Choose an anticyclotomic character $\delta_a:G_E\to(O_a^0)^\times$ with open kernel, ramified exactly at $D_a$, whose inertia image at every place of $D_a$ has order $15$. Require $Nv\equiv1\pmod {15}$ at those places. Choose continuous characters $\chi_{a,j,b}:G_E\to(O_a^0)^\times$ for all $j\ge0$, $b\in\{+,-\}$, unramified outside places above $\ell_a,g_0,g_1$ and $D_a$, equal on inertia at $D_a$ to $\delta_a$ for $b=+$ and to $\delta_a^{-1}$ for $b=-$, and satisfying $\chi_{a,j,b}^{g_u-1}=1$ on inertia above $g_u\ne\ell_a$.

Choose a finite set $\mathcal S_a$ of labels, a stage $j(s)\in\{1,\ldots,m\}$ and side $b(s)$ for each label, and continuous representations $\rho_{a,s}:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_r(O_a^0)$. Choose a finite set $S_{0,a}$ containing all places above $\ell_a,g_0,g_1$ and $D_a$. For every $j\ge0$ and side $b$, choose a finite free $O'_a$-module $B_{a,j,b}$, a submodule $\Theta_{a,j,b}$, $O'_a$-linear endomorphisms $H_{a,j,b,h,v}$ of $B_{a,j,b}$ for every $h\ge0$ and finite $v$, with $H_{a,j,b,0,v}=1$, and scalars $h_{a,s,h,v}\in O'_a$. For every integer $h\ge0$, finite set $Q$ of places of $E$ and group homomorphism $\psi:G_E\to(O'_{a,h})^\times$, choose a submodule $$\Theta_{a,j,b,h,Q,\psi}\subseteq O'_{a,h}\otimes_{O'_a}B_{a,j,b},\qquad \Theta_{a,j,b,h,\varnothing,1}=O'_{a,h}\otimes\Theta_{a,j,b}.$$

Choose another finite label set $\mathcal L_a$. Each $\lambda\in\mathcal L_a$ has a stage $j(\lambda)\in\{1,\ldots,m\}$, a side $b(\lambda)$, an integer $s_\lambda$, a continuous anticyclotomic character $\lambda_a:G_E\to(O'_a)^\times$ unramified outside $S_{0,a}$, values $X_{\lambda,v}\in O'_a$ and $q_{\lambda,v}\in\operatorname{Frac}(O'_a)$. For inertia elements $\sigma$ at $v\in D_a$, require $\lambda_a(\sigma)\delta_a(\sigma)^{-1}$ if $b(\lambda)=+$, or $\lambda_a(\sigma)\delta_a(\sigma)$ if $b(\lambda)=-$, to have order dividing a power of $Nv$. Away from $S_{0,a}$, for every arithmetic Frobenius element at the chosen valuation above $v$, require $$X_{\lambda,v}=\lambda_a(\operatorname{Frob}_v),\qquad q_{\lambda,v}^2=(Nv)^{-s_\lambda}.$$ For every finite place $v$, require $q_{\lambda,v}=q_{\lambda,\bar v}$.

For every finite place set $Q$, choose a finite abelian $\ell_a$-primary group $R_{a,Q}$ and an anticyclotomic surjection $\alpha_{a,Q}:G_E\to R_{a,Q}$ with open kernel, unramified outside $Q$. If $Q$ avoids places above $\ell_a$, every homomorphism $\varphi:G_E\to A$ to a finite abelian $\ell_a$-primary group, with open kernel, anticyclotomic and unramified outside $Q$, factors through $\alpha_{a,Q}$. For $Q'\subseteq Q$, choose a homomorphism $R_{a,Q}\to R_{a,Q'}$ carrying $\alpha_{a,Q}$ to $\alpha_{a,Q'}$. Choose functions $\theta_{a,Q,\lambda}:R_{a,Q}\to\operatorname{Frac}(O'_a)$.

Finally associate to each coefficient index $i$ a twist index $a(i)$ with $\ell_{a(i)}=\ell_i$, a side $b_i$, and a homomorphism $O_{a(i)}^0\to O_i$. If $x\in K_0$ has image $y\in O_{a(i)}^0$ under the embedding selected by $b_i$, require its image in $K_i$ to equal the image of $y$ under this homomorphism. Choose an anticyclotomic character $\psi_i:G_E\to O_i^\times$ with open kernel, every value of $\ell_i$-power order, ramified exactly at $Q_i$, trivial on all decomposition groups above $\ell_i$, and satisfying $$\xi_{i,1}=\chi_{a(i),j_i,b_i}\psi_i$$ after applying $O_{a(i)}^0\to O_i$ to the first factor.

## Systems of Galois representations over the eigensystems at a local coefficient index: conditions, Selmer group and local terms

[Definition / hypothesis GC.D276](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TruncatedSeriesCountingIdentities.admitted/)

This page fixes the vocabulary of ([Bound for the congruence valuation of an eigensystem by a Selmer group, the localization kernels and the class number](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.integralityFromMetric_of_designConditionAll/)). A *system* at a local coefficient index is a family of $\ell$-adic representations of $G_E$, one for each eigensystem at that index, together with a second family of smaller rank, one chosen eigensystem $\pi$, a partition of the places above $\ell$ into two kinds, and three numerical parameters. The page states nineteen conditions on a system, then defines the Selmer group and the local quantities that occur in the bound.

**Setting.** Data that satisfy Hypothesis (member identifications) of ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)) are fixed. We use the notation of ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)) and ([Local period rings and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)): the CM field $E$ with its complex conjugation $v\mapsto\bar v$ on places, its algebraic closure $\Omega$, the group $G_E=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/E)$ and the decomposition and inertia groups $D_v\supseteq I_v$ of the chosen valuation ring of $\Omega$ above each finite place $v$; the primes $g_0,g_1,p_\Delta,p'_\Delta$ and the integer $Q=Q_Sqd$; the integers $r$, $t$ and $n_V=r+t$; and, for a local coefficient index $i$, the prime $\ell_i$, the discrete valuation ring $O_i$ with fraction field $K_i$, the finite set $X_i$ of eigensystems, the algebra $\mathbb T_i\subseteq O_i^{X_i}$ with its elements $T_{i,v,h}$, the subset $Z_i\subseteq X_i$, the characters $\xi_{i,c}:G_E\to O_i^\times$, the finite sets $S_i$ and $Q_i$ of places, the prime $p_{D,i}$, the elements $\gamma_{i,v}\in I_v$, the exponent $u_i$ and the normalized length $\operatorname{len}_{\ell_i}$. For a place $w$ of $E$ above $\ell_i$, the member has a coefficient comparison $(K_{iw},L_{iw},j_{iw},e_{iw},b_{iw})$, in which $e_{iw}:D_w\to G_{K_{iw}}$ and $b_{iw}:O_i\to\mathcal O_{L_{iw}}$ are isomorphisms; and for the prime $\ell_i$ it has abstract local period data, with rings $B$ and $\Delta$ and cyclotomic character $\chi_{\rm cyc}$.

Throughout the page a local coefficient index $i$ is fixed, and $\ell=\ell_i$. Let $\Sigma_\ell$ be the set of places of $E$ above $\ell$, let $\epsilon:G_E\to\mathbb Z_\ell^\times\to O_i^\times$ be the $\ell$-adic cyclotomic character, let $\mathfrak m_i$ be the maximal ideal of $O_i$ and $\mathbb F_i=O_i/\mathfrak m_i$. By ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)), $\xi_{i,c}=\xi_{i,1}\epsilon^{1-c}$ for $1\le c\le t$. Put $$\zeta_{i,v}=\xi_{i,1}(\gamma_{i,v})\in O_i^\times\quad(v\in S_i\cup Q_i),\qquad\Delta_i=\sum_{0<k<t}\bigl(u_i+v_\ell(k)\bigr),$$ where $v_\ell(k)$ is the exponent of $\ell$ in $k$.

**Systems.** A *system at $i$* is a tuple $(R,\varrho,\pi,\mathcal P',\mathcal P'',\lambda,e',c_B)$ in which

- $R=(R_x)_{x\in X_i}$ and $\varrho=(\varrho_x)_{x\in X_i}$ are families of group homomorphisms $R_x:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_{n_V}(O_i)$ and $\varrho_x:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_r(O_i)$;
- $\pi\in X_i$;
- $\mathcal P'$ and $\mathcal P''$ are disjoint subsets of $\Sigma_\ell$ with $\mathcal P'\cup\mathcal P''=\Sigma_\ell$; their elements are the places *of the first kind* and *of the second kind*;
- $\lambda=(\lambda_w)_{w\in\mathcal P'}$ is a family of rational numbers, $e'=(e'_w)_{w\in\mathcal P''}$ is a family of nonnegative integers, and $c_B$ is a nonnegative integer.

For a system we write, for $g\in G_E$ and $x\in X_i$, $$W_\pi(g)=\xi_{i,1}(g)^{-1}\varrho_\pi(g),\qquad W_\pi^*(g)=\epsilon(g)\,{}^t W_\pi(g^{-1}),\qquad R_x^\flat(g)=\xi_{i,1}(g)^{-1}R_x(g),\qquad R_x^*(g)=\xi_{i,1}(g)\,\epsilon(g)\,{}^tR_x(g^{-1}).$$ These are homomorphisms from $G_E$ to $\mathrm{GL}_r(O_i)$ and to $\mathrm{GL}_{n_V}(O_i)$: the twist of $\varrho_\pi$ by $\xi_{i,1}^{-1}$, its Tate dual, the twist of $R_x$ by $\xi_{i,1}^{-1}$, and the Tate dual of that twist.

**Local representations.** Let $w\in\Sigma_\ell$, and let $L'$ be a finite extension of $L_{iw}$ inside $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, with ring of integers $\mathcal O_{L'}$. Put $G=G_{K_{iw}}$ and $I=e_{iw}(I_w)\subseteq G$. An *embedding* is a $\mathbb Q_\ell$-embedding $\tau:K_{iw}\to L'$; there are $[K_{iw}:\mathbb Q_\ell]$ of them, because $L_{iw}$ contains the image of every embedding of $K_{iw}$ in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. By the conditions of a member, $L'$ contains every root of unity of $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ whose order divides $\operatorname{lcm}(\ell,Q)$.

A *representation* is a finite-dimensional $L'$-vector space $V$, with its topology as a finite-dimensional vector space over $L'$, and an $L'$-linear action of $G$ such that $g\mapsto gv$ is continuous for every $v\in V$. A *lattice* of $V$ is a finitely generated $\mathcal O_{L'}$-submodule $T$ that spans $V$ over $L'$ and is stable under $G$. A *morphism of lattices* $f:(V_1,T_1)\to(V_2,T_2)$ is an $L'$-linear map $f:V_1\to V_2$ that commutes with $G$ and satisfies $f(T_1)\subseteq T_2$; it is *onto* if $f(T_1)=T_2$. Crystalline and de Rham representations, the spaces $D_{{\rm dR},\tau}(V)$ and $F_\tau^h(V)$, the jumps of $V$ at an embedding $\tau$ and their multiplicities are those of ([Local period rings and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)), formed with the abstract local period data of the member for $\ell$.

For a homomorphism $M:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_d(O_i)$, let $M_{w,L'}$ be the representation on $(L')^d$ on which $g\in G$ acts by the matrix $b_{iw}\bigl(M(e_{iw}^{-1}(g))\bigr)$, with standard lattice $\mathcal O_{L'}^d$. A character $G_E\to O_i^\times$ is the case $d=1$. For an integer $e$, let $L'(-e)$ be the representation on $L'$ on which $g$ acts by multiplication by $\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)^{-e}$, with lattice $\mathcal O_{L'}(-e)=\mathcal O_{L'}$; and let $L'(1)$ be the representation on which $g$ acts by $\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)$.

**Cocycles.** Let $V$ be a representation and $T$ a lattice of $V$. A *cocycle* of $V$ is a continuous map $a:G\to V$ with $a(gh)=a(g)+g\,a(h)$; a *coboundary* is a map $g\mapsto gv-v$ with $v\in V$. A cocycle $a$ is *finite* if there is $z\in V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}B$ with $$a(g)\otimes1=gz-z\qquad(g\in G),$$ where $G$ acts on $V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}B$ by $g(v\otimes b)=gv\otimes gb$. Let $Z^1_f(V)$ be the $L'$-vector space of finite cocycles and $B^1(V)$ the space of coboundaries, and put $$H^0(G,V)=V^G,\qquad H^1_f(G,V)=Z^1_f(V)/B^1(V).$$ Let $Z^1(T)$ be the $\mathcal O_{L'}$-module of the cocycles of $V$ with values in $T$, let $Z^1_f(T)=Z^1(T)\cap Z^1_f(V)$, and let $B^1(T)$ be the module of the maps $g\mapsto gx-x$ with $x\in T$.

The quotient $V/T$ is given the discrete topology and the induced action of $G$. Let $Z^1(V/T)$ be the $\mathcal O_{L'}$-module of the locally constant maps $c:G\to V/T$ with $c(gh)=c(g)+g\,c(h)$, let $B^1(V/T)$ be the module of the maps $g\mapsto gx-x$ with $x\in V/T$, and $H^1(G,V/T)=Z^1(V/T)/B^1(V/T)$. A map $c:G\to V/T$ is *finite* if there are $a\in Z^1_f(V)$ and $x\in V/T$ with $$c(g)=\bigl(a(g)\bmod T\bigr)+gx-x\qquad(g\in G).$$ Let $H^1_f(G,V/T)\subseteq H^1(G,V/T)$ be the submodule of the classes of the finite elements of $Z^1(V/T)$, and let $H^1_{\rm ur}(G,V/T)$ be the submodule of the classes of the $c\in Z^1(V/T)$ for which there is $x\in V/T$ with $c(g)=gx-x$ for all $g\in I$.

**Conditions on the representations of $G_E$.** The following twelve conditions are imposed on a system at $i$. Conditions 6 and 9 to 12 involve only the data of the member at $i$.

1. *Hecke polynomials.* For every $x\in X_i$, the map $R_x$ is continuous, every $G_E$-stable $K_i$-subspace of $K_i^{n_V}$ (for the action through $R_x$) has a $G_E$-stable complement, and, for every finite place $w$ of $E$ with $w\ne\bar w$, $w\nmid\ell$ and $w\notin S_i\cup Q_i$, every valuation ring of $\Omega$ lying over $w$ and every arithmetic Frobenius element $\sigma$ at that valuation ring, the characteristic polynomial of $R_x(\sigma)$ is $$X^{n_V}+\sum_{h=1}^{n_V}(-1)^h(\mathrm Nw)^{h(h-1)/2}\,T_{i,w,h}(x)\,X^{n_V-h}.$$
2. *Characteristic polynomials in $\mathbb T_i$.* For every $g\in G_E$ and every $k\ge0$, the function that sends $x\in X_i$ to the coefficient of $X^k$ in $\det(1-X\,R_x(g))$ belongs to $\mathbb T_i$.
3. *Constituents and residual representations.* (a) For every $x$, the space $K_i^{n_V}$ with the action through $R_x$ is a direct sum of nonzero $G_E$-stable subspaces $U_1,\dots,U_s$ such that the image of $G_E$ spans $\operatorname{End}_{K_i}(U_j)$ over $K_i$ for every $j$, and such that $U_j$ and $U_{j'}$ are not isomorphic as representations of $G_E$ for $j\ne j'$. (b) For every $x$ and every $g\in G_E$, the characteristic polynomials of the reductions modulo $\mathfrak m_i$ satisfy $$\det\bigl(X-\overline{R_x(g)}\bigr)=\det\bigl(X-\overline{\varrho_\pi(g)}\bigr)\prod_{c=1}^{t}\bigl(X-\overline{\xi_{i,c}(g)}\bigr).$$ (c) The matrices $\overline{\varrho_\pi(g)}$, $g\in G_E$, span $\mathrm M_r(\mathbb F_i)$ over $\mathbb F_i$.
4. *Unramified places.* For every $x$ and every finite place $v$ of $E$ with $v\nmid\ell$ and $v\notin S_i\cup Q_i$, the homomorphism $R_x$ is trivial on $I_v$.
5. *Inertia at the places of $S_i$ and $Q_i$.* Let $w\in S_i\cup Q_i$. (a) If $w\in Q_i$ or $w$ lies above $p_{D,i}$, then for every $x$, $$\bigl(R_x(\gamma)-1\bigr)\bigl(R_x(\gamma)-\xi_{i,1}(\gamma)\bigr)=0\qquad(\gamma\in I_w),$$ and there is $P_x\in\mathrm{GL}_{n_V}(K_i)$ such that, for every $\gamma\in I_w$, the matrix $P_x^{-1}R_x(\gamma)P_x$ is diagonal with its first $r$ diagonal entries equal to $1$ and its last $t$ equal to $\xi_{i,1}(\gamma)$. (b) If $w$ lies above $g_0$ or $g_1$, there is a polynomial $P\in\mathbb T_i[X]$ such that $P(\zeta_{i,w})$ is a unit of $\mathbb T_i$ and, for every $x$, $$P^{(x)}\bigl(R_x(\gamma_{i,w})\bigr)\,\bigl(R_x(\gamma)-\xi_{i,1}(\gamma)\bigr)=0\qquad(\gamma\in I_w),$$ where $P^{(x)}\in O_i[X]$ is the polynomial whose coefficients are the values at $x$ of the coefficients of $P$.
6. *The characters at the places of $S_i$ and $Q_i$.* For every $v\in S_i\cup Q_i$: the element $\gamma_{i,v}$ generates tame inertia at $v$ topologically, as ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)) requires; $\xi_{i,c}$ and $\xi_{i,1}$ agree on $I_v$ for $1\le c\le t$; if $v\in Q_i$, then $\zeta_{i,v}\ne1$; and if $v\in S_i$ lies above $p_{D,i}$, then $\zeta_{i,v}-1$ is a unit of $O_i$.
7. *Lines on which $G_E$ acts through $\xi_{i,1}$.* If $x\in X_i$ and there is a nonzero $u\in K_i^{n_V}$ with $R_x(g)u=\xi_{i,1}(g)u$ for all $g\in G_E$, then $x\in Z_i$.
8. *The representation at $\pi$.* There is a $K_i$-linear isomorphism $K_i^{n_V}\to K_i^{r}\times K_i^{t}$ that carries $R_\pi(g)$ to $\varrho_\pi(g)\times\operatorname{diag}\bigl(\xi_{i,1}(g),\dots,\xi_{i,t}(g)\bigr)$ for every $g\in G_E$.
9. *Values of the cyclotomic character on inertia.* There is $w\in\Sigma_\ell$ such that for every $z\in\mathbb Z_\ell$ there is $g\in I_w$ whose image under the cyclotomic character $G_E\to\mathbb Z_\ell^\times$ is $1+\ell^{u_i}z$.
10. *Everywhere unramified homomorphisms.* Let $\mathcal U_i$ be the $O_i$-module of the locally constant group homomorphisms $G_E\to K_i/O_i$ that are trivial on $I_v$ for every finite place $v$ of $E$, and let $h_E$ be the class number of $E$. Then $\mathcal U_i$ has finite length and $\operatorname{len}_\ell(\mathcal U_i)\le v_\ell(h_E)$.
11. *Local degrees.* For every $w\in\Sigma_\ell$, $[K_{iw}:\mathbb Q_\ell]$ is the degree $[E_w:\mathbb Q_\ell]$ of the completion of $E$ at $w$. (The isomorphism $b_{iw}$ is a homomorphism of $\mathbb Z_\ell$-algebras and a homeomorphism; this is part of the definition of a coefficient comparison.)
12. *Cyclotomic completions.* For every $w\in\Sigma_\ell$, the field $K_{iw}$ is contained in the subfield of $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ generated over $\mathbb Q_\ell$ by the roots of unity whose order divides $\operatorname{lcm}(\ell,Q)$.

**Conditions at the places above $\ell$.** The remaining seven conditions are imposed for every $w\in\Sigma_\ell$ in the indicated set and every finite extension $L'$ of $L_{iw}$ inside $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, with $G$, $I$, embeddings, representations and lattices as above.

13. *Crystalline representations and their jumps* ($w\in\Sigma_\ell$). (a) For every $x$, the representations $(R_x)_{w,L'}$ and $(R_x^\flat)_{w,L'}$ are crystalline. (b) For every embedding $\tau$ and every integer $a$, the multiplicity of $a$ as a jump of $(R_x^\flat)_{w,L'}$ at $\tau$ is the same for all $x\in X_i$ and is at most $1$. (c) For $1\le c\le t$ and every embedding $\tau$, the representation $(\xi_{i,c}\xi_{i,1}^{-1})_{w,L'}$ has exactly one jump at $\tau$, the integer $c-1$. (d) For every $x$ and every embedding $\tau$, the integer $-1$ is not a jump of $(R_x^\flat)_{w,L'}$ at $\tau$. (e) $H^0\bigl(G,(W_\pi)_{w,L'}\bigr)=0$ and $H^0\bigl(G,(W_\pi^*)_{w,L'}\bigr)=0$.
14. *Classes of extensions cut out of crystalline representations* ($w\in\Sigma_\ell$). Let $J_\tau$ be the set of jumps of $(R_\pi^\flat)_{w,L'}$ at the embedding $\tau$. (a) Let $V$ be a crystalline representation all of whose jumps at $\tau$ lie in $J_\tau$, for every $\tau$; let $T$ be a lattice of $V$, let $p:V\to L'$ be an $L'$-linear map with $p(gv)=p(v)$ for all $g\in G$ and $p(T)\subseteq\mathcal O_{L'}$, and let $e_0\in T$ with $p(e_0)=1$. Let $0\le e<t$, and let $\varphi:V\to L'(-e)/\mathcal O_{L'}(-e)$ be an $\mathcal O_{L'}$-linear map with $\varphi(gy)=g\varphi(y)$ for all $g\in G$ and all $y\in T$ with $p(y)=0$. Then the map $g\mapsto\ell^{c_B}\varphi(ge_0-e_0)$ from $G$ to $L'(-e)/\mathcal O_{L'}(-e)$ is finite. (b) For $0<e<t$, $H^1_f\bigl(G,L'(-e)/\mathcal O_{L'}(-e)\bigr)=0$. (c) $H^1_f\bigl(G,L'/\mathcal O_{L'}\bigr)=H^1_{\rm ur}\bigl(G,L'/\mathcal O_{L'}\bigr)$, for the trivial action. (d) For $0\le e<t$, with $H=H^1\bigl(G,L'(-e)/\mathcal O_{L'}(-e)\bigr)$ and $H_f$ its submodule $H^1_f$, $$\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O_{L'}}\bigl(\{y\in H:\ell^{c_B}y\in H_f\}/H_f\bigr)\le\bigl([K_{iw}:\mathbb Q_\ell]+1\bigr)\,c_B\,\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O_{L'}}\bigl(\mathcal O_{L'}/\ell\mathcal O_{L'}\bigr).$$
15. *Places of the first kind* ($w\in\mathcal P'$). Let $(V_1,T_1)$ be a representation with a lattice such that $V_1$ is isomorphic to a subrepresentation of a finite direct sum of representations $(R_x^*)_{w,L'}$, $x\in X_i$, with repetitions allowed. Let $f:(V_1,T_1)\to\bigl((W_\pi^*)_{w,L'},\mathcal O_{L'}^r\bigr)$ be a morphism of lattices that is onto. Then the $\mathcal O_{L'}$-module $$Z^1_f(\mathcal O_{L'}^r)\big/\bigl(f\circ Z^1_f(T_1)+B^1(\mathcal O_{L'}^r)\bigr),$$ formed for the lattice $\mathcal O_{L'}^r$ of $(W_\pi^*)_{w,L'}$, has finite length, at most $\lambda_w\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O_{L'}}(\mathcal O_{L'}/\ell\mathcal O_{L'})$.
16. *Places of the second kind* ($w\in\mathcal P''$). Let $(V_1,T_1)$ and $(V_2,T_2)$ be representations with lattices such that each of $V_1$ and $V_2$ is isomorphic to a quotient of a subrepresentation of a finite direct sum of representations $(R_x^\flat)_{w,L'}$, $x\in X_i$, with repetitions allowed. Let $f:(V_1,T_1)\to(V_2,T_2)$ be a morphism of lattices that is onto. Then $$\ell^{e'_w}\,Z^1_f(T_2)\subseteq f\circ Z^1_f(T_1)+B^1(T_2).$$
17. *Operations on crystalline representations* ($w\in\Sigma_\ell$). (a) Let $0\to V_0\to V_1\to V_2\to0$ be an exact sequence of representations. If $V_1$ is crystalline, then $V_0$ and $V_2$ are crystalline, and for every embedding $\tau$ and every integer $a$ the multiplicity of $a$ at $\tau$ for $V_1$ is the sum of those for $V_0$ and $V_2$. If the sequence has a $G$-equivariant $L'$-linear splitting and $V_0$, $V_2$ are crystalline, then $V_1$ is crystalline. (b) Let $\chi:G\to(L')^\times$ be a homomorphism such that the one-dimensional representation $L'(\chi)$ on which $g$ acts by $\chi(g)$ is crystalline, let $V$ be a crystalline representation, and let $V'$ be a representation with an $L'$-linear isomorphism $\theta:V\to V'$ such that $g\,\theta(v)=\chi(g)\,\theta(gv)$. Then $V'$ is crystalline, and for every embedding $\tau$, every jump $b$ of $L'(\chi)$ at $\tau$ and every integer $a$, the multiplicity of $a+b$ at $\tau$ for $V'$ is the multiplicity of $a$ at $\tau$ for $V$. (c) Let $V$, $V'$ be representations of the same dimension with an $L'$-bilinear pairing $P:V\times V'\to L'$ that is nondegenerate in both variables and satisfies $P(gx,gy)=\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)P(x,y)$. If $V$ is crystalline, then $V'$ is crystalline. (d) Every crystalline representation is de Rham.
18. *Dimension formula and integral duality* ($w\in\Sigma_\ell$). (a) There is an isomorphism of $\mathcal O_{L'}$-modules $H^2\bigl(G,L'(1)/\mathcal O_{L'}\bigr)\simeq L'/\mathcal O_{L'}$; here $H^2$ is formed with locally constant cochains $G\times G\to L'(1)/\mathcal O_{L'}$. (b) For every crystalline representation $V$, $$\dim_{L'}H^1_f(G,V)=\dim_{L'}H^0(G,V)+\sum_\tau\bigl(\dim_{L'}D_{{\rm dR},\tau}(V)-\dim_{L'}F_\tau^0(V)\bigr),$$ the sum being over the embeddings. (c) Let $V$, $V'$ and $P$ be as in 17(c), with $V$ de Rham, and let $T$, $T'$ be lattices of $V$, $V'$ with $T'=\{y\in V':P(x,y)\in\mathcal O_{L'}\text{ for all }x\in T\}$. For $d\in Z^1(T)$ and $c\in Z^1(V'/T')$, call the pair $(d,c)$ *orthogonal* if there is a locally constant map $\phi:G\to L'/\mathcal O_{L'}$ with $$P\bigl(d(g),g\,\tilde c(h)\bigr)\bmod\mathcal O_{L'}=\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)\phi(h)-\phi(gh)+\phi(g)\qquad(g,h\in G),$$ where $\tilde c(h)\in V'$ is a representative of $c(h)$; the left side does not depend on the representative. Then an element $c\in Z^1(V'/T')$ is finite if and only if $(d,c)$ is orthogonal for every $d\in Z^1_f(T)$, and an element $d\in Z^1(T)$ is finite if and only if $(d,c)$ is orthogonal for every finite $c\in Z^1(V'/T')$.
19. *Integral second cohomology* ($w\in\Sigma_\ell$). Let $Z^2$ be the $\mathcal O_{L'}$-module of the continuous maps $z:G\times G\to L'$ with values in $\mathcal O_{L'}$ that satisfy $$\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)z(h,k)-z(gh,k)+z(g,hk)-z(g,h)=0\qquad(g,h,k\in G),$$ and let $B^2$ be the module of the maps $(g,h)\mapsto\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)\phi(h)-\phi(gh)+\phi(g)$ with $\phi:G\to L'$ continuous with values in $\mathcal O_{L'}$. Then $Z^2/B^2\simeq\mathcal O_{L'}$ as $\mathcal O_{L'}$-modules.

A system at $i$ *satisfies the conditions of this page* if conditions 1 to 19 hold.

**The Selmer group.** Let $(R,\varrho,\pi,\mathcal P',\mathcal P'',\lambda,e',c_B)$ be a system at $i$. Let $A_\pi=(K_i/O_i)^r$, with the discrete topology and with $g\in G_E$ acting by the matrix $W_\pi(g)$, and let $H^1(G_E,A_\pi)$ be the group of continuous cocycles $G_E\to A_\pi$ modulo coboundaries; similarly for the subgroups $D_v$ and $I_v$. For a place $v\in\Sigma_\ell$, the isomorphism $b_{iv}$ extends to an isomorphism $K_i\to L_{iv}$ and induces an isomorphism $\bar b_{iv}:A_\pi\to L_{iv}^r/\mathcal O_{L_{iv}}^r$. The *Selmer group* $\operatorname{Sel}(W_\pi)$ is the $O_i$-module of the classes in $H^1(G_E,A_\pi)$ whose restriction to $D_v$ satisfies the following condition for every finite place $v$ of $E$.

- If $v\in\Sigma_\ell$: the restriction is the class of a cocycle $c:D_v\to A_\pi$ for which the map $g\mapsto\bar b_{iv}\bigl(c(e_{iv}^{-1}(g))\bigr)$ on $G_{K_{iv}}$ is finite, for the representation $(W_\pi)_{v,L_{iv}}$ and its lattice $\mathcal O_{L_{iv}}^r$.
- If $v\in S_i\cup Q_i$: no condition.
- Otherwise: the restriction to $I_v$ is zero in $H^1(I_v,A_\pi)$.

Put $s(\pi)=\operatorname{len}_\ell\bigl(\operatorname{Sel}(W_\pi)\bigr)$.

**The local terms.** For $w\in\mathcal P''$ put $$j_w=\dim_{L_{iw}}H^1_f\bigl(G_{K_{iw}},(W_\pi)_{w,L_{iw}}\bigr),\qquad h_w=\operatorname{len}_\ell\bigl(H^0(D_w,A_\pi)\bigr),$$ $$\lambda''_w=e'_w+j_w\,(e'_w+\Delta_i)+2\Delta_i+h_w.$$ For an integer $e$, let $(K_i/O_i)(-e)$ be the module $K_i/O_i$ on which $g\in G_E$ acts by $\epsilon(g)^{-e}$, and put $$h''=\max\bigl\{\operatorname{len}_\ell\bigl(H^0(D_w,(K_i/O_i)(-e))\bigr):w\in\mathcal P'',\ 0<e<t\bigr\},$$ with $h''=0$ if $\mathcal P''$ is empty.
