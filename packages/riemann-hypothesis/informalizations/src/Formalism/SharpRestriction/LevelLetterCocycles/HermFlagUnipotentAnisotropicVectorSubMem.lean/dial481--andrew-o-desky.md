## Division by a product of two distinguished linear factors

[Theorem PHT.0789](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.CrystallineLiftingInputs.truncatedSeriesData.wdiv/)

Let $O$ be a commutative local ring, complete for its maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, and let $\ell\in\mathbb N$ have image in $\mathfrak m$. For every $M,N\ge0$ and $F\in O[[T]]$, there exist $Q\in O[[T]]$ and $R\in O[T]$ such that $$F=T^M(T+\ell)^NQ+R,\qquad\deg R<M+N.$$
