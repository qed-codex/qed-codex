## Fixed-data congruence-defect bound

[Theorem GC.0106](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/defect.html)

A uniform primewise estimate and a polynomial comparison error imply a uniform eventual bound for the weighted congruence defects. This numerical corollary retains the sum of cohomology lengths, weighted by logarithms of primes, as an explicit term in the bound.

Fix a CM field $E$ of degree $2f$, a nonnegative integer $t$, and $m\in\mathbb N$. For each $a\in\mathbb N$ and rational prime $\ell$, let $S_\ell$ be the set of places of $E$ above $\ell$, let $G_{E,S_\ell}$ be the Galois group of the maximal extension of $E$ unramified outside $S_\ell$, and put $$\mathcal S_{\ell,a}(E)=\ker\!\left[H^1(G_{E,S_\ell},\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell(-a))\longrightarrow\prod_{v\mid\ell}H^1(E_v,\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell(-a))\right].$$ Here $E_v$ is the completion at $v$, and $(-a)$ denotes the Tate twist. Let $s_{\ell,a}\in\mathbb Q_{\ge0}$ be specified values, equal to $\operatorname{length}_{\mathbb Z_\ell}\mathcal S_{\ell,a}(E)$ whenever this length is finite and $1\le a<t$. Define $V:\mathbb N\to\mathbb R_{\ge0}$ by $$V(n)=\begin{cases}\displaystyle\log n\sum_{1\le a<t}s_{n,a},&n\text{ prime},\\0,&n\text{ not prime}.\end{cases}$$

Let $K$ be a number field and $\mathcal V$ a finite set of its finite places. For $v\in\mathcal V$, write $\mathfrak p_v$ for its prime ideal, $p_v$ for its residue characteristic, and $e_v$ for its ramification index over $p_v$. Choose labels $\mu_v$ and $\mu_0$ in a common set. For each $j\in\mathbb N$, choose a nonnegative integer $d_j$ and nonnegative rational values $\eta_j(v)$. Define $$a_v=\frac{\log\operatorname{Norm}(\mathfrak p_v)}{[K:\mathbb Q]}e_v,\qquad \eta_j=\sum_{\substack{v\in\mathcal V\\\mu_v\ne\mu_0}}a_v\eta_j(v).$$ Let $x,L,b,N_c\in\mathbb R$ be the scale parameters and congruence budget. For $j\in\mathbb N$ and $C\in\mathbb R$, the fixed-data congruence condition is the implication $$\sum_{n=0}^\infty V(n)\text{ converges}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \eta_j\le d_j\left[C\{fmbL^2+(\log x)^2N_c\}+\sum_{n=0}^\infty V(n)\right].$$ Consider a family with a set of members $\mathcal F(x)$ for each real $x>x_f$, where $x_f>1$ is fixed. All the fixed data may depend on the member. Fix $\varepsilon>0$ and set $L=x^\varepsilon$, $b=e^L$. Assume $f,m\ge1$, $N_c\ge0$ and $$\sum_{\substack{v\in\mathcal V\\\mu_v\ne\mu_0,\ p_v=\ell}}a_v\le\log\ell\qquad(\ell\text{ prime}).$$ Fix constants $C_1\ge1$ and $C_a>0$, and put $C_N=2000(|C_a|+1)^3$. Assume that there is one threshold $x_*$ such that, for every $x\ge x_*$ with $x>x_f$ and every member for which $\mathcal S_{\ell,a}(E)$ has finite length for all primes $\ell$ and $1\le a<t$, the following choices can be made simultaneously. Choose a positive integer $H$, nonnegative real numbers $\eta'_j(v)$ for $1\le j\le m$ and $v\in\mathcal V$, nonnegative real numbers $\nu_\ell,\Lambda_\ell$ for all primes $\ell$, and $\mathcal N\ge0$. All these choices may depend on the member; $H,\nu_\ell,\Lambda_\ell,\mathcal N$ are independent of $j$.

For a prime $\ell$ and positive integer $A$, write $v_\ell(A)$ for the exponent of $\ell$ in $A$. Require the comparison and polynomial bound $$\eta_j(v)\le\eta'_j(v)+d_jv_{p_v}(H)\qquad(1\le j\le m,\ \mu_v\ne\mu_0).$$ $$\log H\le C_1x^{C_1}L.$$ Put $h_E=|\operatorname{Cl}(E)|$, the class number, $W_\ell=\sum_{1\le a<t}s_{\ell,a}$ and $$\eta'_j=\sum_{\substack{v\in\mathcal V\\\mu_v\ne\mu_0}}a_v\eta'_j(v),\qquad \eta'_{j,\ell}=\sum_{\substack{v\in\mathcal V\\\mu_v\ne\mu_0,\ p_v=\ell}}a_v\eta'_j(v).$$ For every $1\le j\le m$ and every prime $\ell$ occurring among these coefficient places, require $$\eta'_{j,\ell}\le d_j\log\ell\{\nu_\ell+2\ell^{-2}+\Lambda_\ell+W_\ell+v_\ell(h_E)+v_\ell(H)\}.$$ For every finite set $\mathcal Q$ of rational primes, require $$\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal Q}\nu_\ell\log\ell\le\mathcal N,$$ $$6+\mathcal N+\log h_E+\log H+\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal Q}\log\ell\,\Lambda_\ell\le C_NfmbL^2.$$ Under these hypotheses there exist $C,x_0\in\mathbb R$, independent of the member and $j$, such that for every $x\ge x_0$, every member with the stated finite cohomology lengths, and every $1\le j\le m$, the fixed-data congruence condition holds. One may take $C=C_N+C_1$.

## Bound for the congruence valuation of an eigensystem by a Selmer group, the localization kernels and the class number

[Theorem GC.1.1771](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.integralityFromMetric_of_designConditionAll/)

Data that satisfy Hypothesis (member identifications) of ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)) are fixed, with the notation of ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)). For a local coefficient index $i$, with prime $\ell_i$, this notation includes the eigenspaces $V_{i,x}$ of the eigensystems $x\in X_i$ in $V_i=K_i\otimes_{O_i}M_i$, their dimensions $m_{i,x}$, the subset $Z_i\subseteq X_i$, the normalized length $\operatorname{len}_{\ell_i}$, and the *congruence valuation* $$c_{i,x}=\operatorname{len}_{\ell_i}\bigl(O_i/\mathfrak c_{i,x}\bigr),$$ where $\mathfrak c_{i,x}$ is the ideal of $O_i$ generated by the values $a(x)$ of the elements $a\in\mathbb T_i$ that vanish at every point of $X_i\setminus Z_i$. For a subset $Z'\subseteq X_i$, let $\eta_i(Z')$ be the projection defect of $Z'$, defined in the conventions.

**1. The defect and the congruence valuations.** Let $i$ be a local coefficient index, $Z'$ a subset of $Z_i$ and $A$ a nonzero integer. Let $\alpha$ be an $O_i$-linear endomorphism of $M_i$ that commutes with the action of $\mathbb T_i$ and whose $K_i$-linear extension to $V_i$ is multiplication by $A$ on $V_{i,x}$ for $x\in Z'$ and is zero on $V_{i,x}$ for $x\in Z_i\setminus Z'$. Then $$\eta_i(Z')\le\sum_{\pi\in Z'}m_{i,\pi}\,\bigl(c_{i,\pi}+v_{\ell_i}(A)\bigr).$$

**2. The congruence valuation and Selmer groups.** Assume the following five hypotheses.

- (a) Hypothesis (existence of global Artin maps) of ([Global Artin map of a number field](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.HeckeGal.IsGlobalArtin/)).
- (b) Assertion (12) of ([Local and global cohomological hypotheses](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Mem.LiteralAutomorphy.LitCoh/)).
- (c) The assertion of ([Local Tate duality](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/CompanionTheory.LocalTateOrthogonality.Gcoh.top_tate/)), for every prime $p$, every finite extension of $\mathbb Q_p$ inside an algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q_p$ and every coefficient module of that page.
- (d) The assertion of ([Local Euler characteristic](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/CompanionTheory.LocalTateOrthogonality.Gcoh.top_euler/)), for the same data.
- (e) Hypothesis (existence of congruence objects) of ([Congruence objects of a system of Galois representations at an eigensystem](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.GoodCongruenceObjects/)), for the fixed data.

Let $i$ be a local coefficient index whose prime $\ell=\ell_i$ is different from $g_0$ and $g_1$, and let $(R,\varrho,\pi,\mathcal P',\mathcal P'',\lambda,e',c_B)$ be a system at $i$ that satisfies the conditions of ([Systems of Galois representations over the eigensystems at a local coefficient index: conditions, Selmer group and local terms](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TruncatedSeriesCountingIdentities.admitted/)), with $\pi\in Z_i$. Let $\operatorname{Sel}(W_\pi)$, $s(\pi)$, $\lambda''_w$, $h''$, $\Delta_i$ and $\zeta_{i,v}$ be the Selmer group and the local terms that the cited page attaches to the system. Suppose that $\operatorname{Sel}(W_\pi)$ has finite length over $O_i$ and that the localization kernels $\mathcal S_{\ell,e}(E)$ of ([Window condition: a bound on the Tate-Shafarevich groups of the twists Q\_l/Z\_l(-k) over auxiliary abelian fields](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Top.VWin/)) have finite length $\lambda_\ell(E,e)$ for $1\le e<t$. Then $$\begin{aligned} c_{i,\pi}\ \le{}&\ s(\pi)+\sum_{1\le e<t}\lambda_\ell(E,e)+v_\ell(h_E)+\sum_{w\in\mathcal P'}\lambda_w+\sum_{w\in\mathcal P''}\lambda''_w\\ &+t\,c_B\sum_{w\in\mathcal P'}\bigl([E_w:\mathbb Q_\ell]+2\bigr)+4\,n_V\bigl(|\mathcal P''|+1\bigr)\Delta_i+t\,|\mathcal P''|\,h''+t\sum_{v\in Q_i}\operatorname{len}_\ell\bigl(O_i/(\zeta_{i,v}-1)O_i\bigr). \end{aligned}$$ Here $h_E$ is the class number of $E$, and $\mathcal P'$, $\mathcal P''$ are the sets of the places of $E$ above $\ell$ of the first and of the second kind for the system.

## Systems of Galois representations over the eigensystems at a local coefficient index: conditions, Selmer group and local terms

[Definition / hypothesis GC.D276](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TruncatedSeriesCountingIdentities.admitted/)

This page fixes the vocabulary of ([Bound for the congruence valuation of an eigensystem by a Selmer group, the localization kernels and the class number](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.integralityFromMetric_of_designConditionAll/)). A *system* at a local coefficient index is a family of $\ell$-adic representations of $G_E$, one for each eigensystem at that index, together with a second family of smaller rank, one chosen eigensystem $\pi$, a partition of the places above $\ell$ into two kinds, and three numerical parameters. The page states nineteen conditions on a system, then defines the Selmer group and the local quantities that occur in the bound.

**Setting.** Data that satisfy Hypothesis (member identifications) of ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)) are fixed. We use the notation of ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)) and ([Local period rings and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)): the CM field $E$ with its complex conjugation $v\mapsto\bar v$ on places, its algebraic closure $\Omega$, the group $G_E=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/E)$ and the decomposition and inertia groups $D_v\supseteq I_v$ of the chosen valuation ring of $\Omega$ above each finite place $v$; the primes $g_0,g_1,p_\Delta,p'_\Delta$ and the integer $Q=Q_Sqd$; the integers $r$, $t$ and $n_V=r+t$; and, for a local coefficient index $i$, the prime $\ell_i$, the discrete valuation ring $O_i$ with fraction field $K_i$, the finite set $X_i$ of eigensystems, the algebra $\mathbb T_i\subseteq O_i^{X_i}$ with its elements $T_{i,v,h}$, the subset $Z_i\subseteq X_i$, the characters $\xi_{i,c}:G_E\to O_i^\times$, the finite sets $S_i$ and $Q_i$ of places, the prime $p_{D,i}$, the elements $\gamma_{i,v}\in I_v$, the exponent $u_i$ and the normalized length $\operatorname{len}_{\ell_i}$. For a place $w$ of $E$ above $\ell_i$, the member has a coefficient comparison $(K_{iw},L_{iw},j_{iw},e_{iw},b_{iw})$, in which $e_{iw}:D_w\to G_{K_{iw}}$ and $b_{iw}:O_i\to\mathcal O_{L_{iw}}$ are isomorphisms; and for the prime $\ell_i$ it has abstract local period data, with rings $B$ and $\Delta$ and cyclotomic character $\chi_{\rm cyc}$.

Throughout the page a local coefficient index $i$ is fixed, and $\ell=\ell_i$. Let $\Sigma_\ell$ be the set of places of $E$ above $\ell$, let $\epsilon:G_E\to\mathbb Z_\ell^\times\to O_i^\times$ be the $\ell$-adic cyclotomic character, let $\mathfrak m_i$ be the maximal ideal of $O_i$ and $\mathbb F_i=O_i/\mathfrak m_i$. By ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)), $\xi_{i,c}=\xi_{i,1}\epsilon^{1-c}$ for $1\le c\le t$. Put $$\zeta_{i,v}=\xi_{i,1}(\gamma_{i,v})\in O_i^\times\quad(v\in S_i\cup Q_i),\qquad\Delta_i=\sum_{0<k<t}\bigl(u_i+v_\ell(k)\bigr),$$ where $v_\ell(k)$ is the exponent of $\ell$ in $k$.

**Systems.** A *system at $i$* is a tuple $(R,\varrho,\pi,\mathcal P',\mathcal P'',\lambda,e',c_B)$ in which

- $R=(R_x)_{x\in X_i}$ and $\varrho=(\varrho_x)_{x\in X_i}$ are families of group homomorphisms $R_x:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_{n_V}(O_i)$ and $\varrho_x:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_r(O_i)$;
- $\pi\in X_i$;
- $\mathcal P'$ and $\mathcal P''$ are disjoint subsets of $\Sigma_\ell$ with $\mathcal P'\cup\mathcal P''=\Sigma_\ell$; their elements are the places *of the first kind* and *of the second kind*;
- $\lambda=(\lambda_w)_{w\in\mathcal P'}$ is a family of rational numbers, $e'=(e'_w)_{w\in\mathcal P''}$ is a family of nonnegative integers, and $c_B$ is a nonnegative integer.

For a system we write, for $g\in G_E$ and $x\in X_i$, $$W_\pi(g)=\xi_{i,1}(g)^{-1}\varrho_\pi(g),\qquad W_\pi^*(g)=\epsilon(g)\,{}^t W_\pi(g^{-1}),\qquad R_x^\flat(g)=\xi_{i,1}(g)^{-1}R_x(g),\qquad R_x^*(g)=\xi_{i,1}(g)\,\epsilon(g)\,{}^tR_x(g^{-1}).$$ These are homomorphisms from $G_E$ to $\mathrm{GL}_r(O_i)$ and to $\mathrm{GL}_{n_V}(O_i)$: the twist of $\varrho_\pi$ by $\xi_{i,1}^{-1}$, its Tate dual, the twist of $R_x$ by $\xi_{i,1}^{-1}$, and the Tate dual of that twist.

**Local representations.** Let $w\in\Sigma_\ell$, and let $L'$ be a finite extension of $L_{iw}$ inside $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, with ring of integers $\mathcal O_{L'}$. Put $G=G_{K_{iw}}$ and $I=e_{iw}(I_w)\subseteq G$. An *embedding* is a $\mathbb Q_\ell$-embedding $\tau:K_{iw}\to L'$; there are $[K_{iw}:\mathbb Q_\ell]$ of them, because $L_{iw}$ contains the image of every embedding of $K_{iw}$ in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. By the conditions of a member, $L'$ contains every root of unity of $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ whose order divides $\operatorname{lcm}(\ell,Q)$.

A *representation* is a finite-dimensional $L'$-vector space $V$, with its topology as a finite-dimensional vector space over $L'$, and an $L'$-linear action of $G$ such that $g\mapsto gv$ is continuous for every $v\in V$. A *lattice* of $V$ is a finitely generated $\mathcal O_{L'}$-submodule $T$ that spans $V$ over $L'$ and is stable under $G$. A *morphism of lattices* $f:(V_1,T_1)\to(V_2,T_2)$ is an $L'$-linear map $f:V_1\to V_2$ that commutes with $G$ and satisfies $f(T_1)\subseteq T_2$; it is *onto* if $f(T_1)=T_2$. Crystalline and de Rham representations, the spaces $D_{{\rm dR},\tau}(V)$ and $F_\tau^h(V)$, the jumps of $V$ at an embedding $\tau$ and their multiplicities are those of ([Local period rings and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)), formed with the abstract local period data of the member for $\ell$.

For a homomorphism $M:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_d(O_i)$, let $M_{w,L'}$ be the representation on $(L')^d$ on which $g\in G$ acts by the matrix $b_{iw}\bigl(M(e_{iw}^{-1}(g))\bigr)$, with standard lattice $\mathcal O_{L'}^d$. A character $G_E\to O_i^\times$ is the case $d=1$. For an integer $e$, let $L'(-e)$ be the representation on $L'$ on which $g$ acts by multiplication by $\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)^{-e}$, with lattice $\mathcal O_{L'}(-e)=\mathcal O_{L'}$; and let $L'(1)$ be the representation on which $g$ acts by $\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)$.

**Cocycles.** Let $V$ be a representation and $T$ a lattice of $V$. A *cocycle* of $V$ is a continuous map $a:G\to V$ with $a(gh)=a(g)+g\,a(h)$; a *coboundary* is a map $g\mapsto gv-v$ with $v\in V$. A cocycle $a$ is *finite* if there is $z\in V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}B$ with $$a(g)\otimes1=gz-z\qquad(g\in G),$$ where $G$ acts on $V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}B$ by $g(v\otimes b)=gv\otimes gb$. Let $Z^1_f(V)$ be the $L'$-vector space of finite cocycles and $B^1(V)$ the space of coboundaries, and put $$H^0(G,V)=V^G,\qquad H^1_f(G,V)=Z^1_f(V)/B^1(V).$$ Let $Z^1(T)$ be the $\mathcal O_{L'}$-module of the cocycles of $V$ with values in $T$, let $Z^1_f(T)=Z^1(T)\cap Z^1_f(V)$, and let $B^1(T)$ be the module of the maps $g\mapsto gx-x$ with $x\in T$.

The quotient $V/T$ is given the discrete topology and the induced action of $G$. Let $Z^1(V/T)$ be the $\mathcal O_{L'}$-module of the locally constant maps $c:G\to V/T$ with $c(gh)=c(g)+g\,c(h)$, let $B^1(V/T)$ be the module of the maps $g\mapsto gx-x$ with $x\in V/T$, and $H^1(G,V/T)=Z^1(V/T)/B^1(V/T)$. A map $c:G\to V/T$ is *finite* if there are $a\in Z^1_f(V)$ and $x\in V/T$ with $$c(g)=\bigl(a(g)\bmod T\bigr)+gx-x\qquad(g\in G).$$ Let $H^1_f(G,V/T)\subseteq H^1(G,V/T)$ be the submodule of the classes of the finite elements of $Z^1(V/T)$, and let $H^1_{\rm ur}(G,V/T)$ be the submodule of the classes of the $c\in Z^1(V/T)$ for which there is $x\in V/T$ with $c(g)=gx-x$ for all $g\in I$.

**Conditions on the representations of $G_E$.** The following twelve conditions are imposed on a system at $i$. Conditions 6 and 9 to 12 involve only the data of the member at $i$.

1. *Hecke polynomials.* For every $x\in X_i$, the map $R_x$ is continuous, every $G_E$-stable $K_i$-subspace of $K_i^{n_V}$ (for the action through $R_x$) has a $G_E$-stable complement, and, for every finite place $w$ of $E$ with $w\ne\bar w$, $w\nmid\ell$ and $w\notin S_i\cup Q_i$, every valuation ring of $\Omega$ lying over $w$ and every arithmetic Frobenius element $\sigma$ at that valuation ring, the characteristic polynomial of $R_x(\sigma)$ is $$X^{n_V}+\sum_{h=1}^{n_V}(-1)^h(\mathrm Nw)^{h(h-1)/2}\,T_{i,w,h}(x)\,X^{n_V-h}.$$
2. *Characteristic polynomials in $\mathbb T_i$.* For every $g\in G_E$ and every $k\ge0$, the function that sends $x\in X_i$ to the coefficient of $X^k$ in $\det(1-X\,R_x(g))$ belongs to $\mathbb T_i$.
3. *Constituents and residual representations.* (a) For every $x$, the space $K_i^{n_V}$ with the action through $R_x$ is a direct sum of nonzero $G_E$-stable subspaces $U_1,\dots,U_s$ such that the image of $G_E$ spans $\operatorname{End}_{K_i}(U_j)$ over $K_i$ for every $j$, and such that $U_j$ and $U_{j'}$ are not isomorphic as representations of $G_E$ for $j\ne j'$. (b) For every $x$ and every $g\in G_E$, the characteristic polynomials of the reductions modulo $\mathfrak m_i$ satisfy $$\det\bigl(X-\overline{R_x(g)}\bigr)=\det\bigl(X-\overline{\varrho_\pi(g)}\bigr)\prod_{c=1}^{t}\bigl(X-\overline{\xi_{i,c}(g)}\bigr).$$ (c) The matrices $\overline{\varrho_\pi(g)}$, $g\in G_E$, span $\mathrm M_r(\mathbb F_i)$ over $\mathbb F_i$.
4. *Unramified places.* For every $x$ and every finite place $v$ of $E$ with $v\nmid\ell$ and $v\notin S_i\cup Q_i$, the homomorphism $R_x$ is trivial on $I_v$.
5. *Inertia at the places of $S_i$ and $Q_i$.* Let $w\in S_i\cup Q_i$. (a) If $w\in Q_i$ or $w$ lies above $p_{D,i}$, then for every $x$, $$\bigl(R_x(\gamma)-1\bigr)\bigl(R_x(\gamma)-\xi_{i,1}(\gamma)\bigr)=0\qquad(\gamma\in I_w),$$ and there is $P_x\in\mathrm{GL}_{n_V}(K_i)$ such that, for every $\gamma\in I_w$, the matrix $P_x^{-1}R_x(\gamma)P_x$ is diagonal with its first $r$ diagonal entries equal to $1$ and its last $t$ equal to $\xi_{i,1}(\gamma)$. (b) If $w$ lies above $g_0$ or $g_1$, there is a polynomial $P\in\mathbb T_i[X]$ such that $P(\zeta_{i,w})$ is a unit of $\mathbb T_i$ and, for every $x$, $$P^{(x)}\bigl(R_x(\gamma_{i,w})\bigr)\,\bigl(R_x(\gamma)-\xi_{i,1}(\gamma)\bigr)=0\qquad(\gamma\in I_w),$$ where $P^{(x)}\in O_i[X]$ is the polynomial whose coefficients are the values at $x$ of the coefficients of $P$.
6. *The characters at the places of $S_i$ and $Q_i$.* For every $v\in S_i\cup Q_i$: the element $\gamma_{i,v}$ generates tame inertia at $v$ topologically, as ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)) requires; $\xi_{i,c}$ and $\xi_{i,1}$ agree on $I_v$ for $1\le c\le t$; if $v\in Q_i$, then $\zeta_{i,v}\ne1$; and if $v\in S_i$ lies above $p_{D,i}$, then $\zeta_{i,v}-1$ is a unit of $O_i$.
7. *Lines on which $G_E$ acts through $\xi_{i,1}$.* If $x\in X_i$ and there is a nonzero $u\in K_i^{n_V}$ with $R_x(g)u=\xi_{i,1}(g)u$ for all $g\in G_E$, then $x\in Z_i$.
8. *The representation at $\pi$.* There is a $K_i$-linear isomorphism $K_i^{n_V}\to K_i^{r}\times K_i^{t}$ that carries $R_\pi(g)$ to $\varrho_\pi(g)\times\operatorname{diag}\bigl(\xi_{i,1}(g),\dots,\xi_{i,t}(g)\bigr)$ for every $g\in G_E$.
9. *Values of the cyclotomic character on inertia.* There is $w\in\Sigma_\ell$ such that for every $z\in\mathbb Z_\ell$ there is $g\in I_w$ whose image under the cyclotomic character $G_E\to\mathbb Z_\ell^\times$ is $1+\ell^{u_i}z$.
10. *Everywhere unramified homomorphisms.* Let $\mathcal U_i$ be the $O_i$-module of the locally constant group homomorphisms $G_E\to K_i/O_i$ that are trivial on $I_v$ for every finite place $v$ of $E$, and let $h_E$ be the class number of $E$. Then $\mathcal U_i$ has finite length and $\operatorname{len}_\ell(\mathcal U_i)\le v_\ell(h_E)$.
11. *Local degrees.* For every $w\in\Sigma_\ell$, $[K_{iw}:\mathbb Q_\ell]$ is the degree $[E_w:\mathbb Q_\ell]$ of the completion of $E$ at $w$. (The isomorphism $b_{iw}$ is a homomorphism of $\mathbb Z_\ell$-algebras and a homeomorphism; this is part of the definition of a coefficient comparison.)
12. *Cyclotomic completions.* For every $w\in\Sigma_\ell$, the field $K_{iw}$ is contained in the subfield of $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ generated over $\mathbb Q_\ell$ by the roots of unity whose order divides $\operatorname{lcm}(\ell,Q)$.

**Conditions at the places above $\ell$.** The remaining seven conditions are imposed for every $w\in\Sigma_\ell$ in the indicated set and every finite extension $L'$ of $L_{iw}$ inside $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, with $G$, $I$, embeddings, representations and lattices as above.

13. *Crystalline representations and their jumps* ($w\in\Sigma_\ell$). (a) For every $x$, the representations $(R_x)_{w,L'}$ and $(R_x^\flat)_{w,L'}$ are crystalline. (b) For every embedding $\tau$ and every integer $a$, the multiplicity of $a$ as a jump of $(R_x^\flat)_{w,L'}$ at $\tau$ is the same for all $x\in X_i$ and is at most $1$. (c) For $1\le c\le t$ and every embedding $\tau$, the representation $(\xi_{i,c}\xi_{i,1}^{-1})_{w,L'}$ has exactly one jump at $\tau$, the integer $c-1$. (d) For every $x$ and every embedding $\tau$, the integer $-1$ is not a jump of $(R_x^\flat)_{w,L'}$ at $\tau$. (e) $H^0\bigl(G,(W_\pi)_{w,L'}\bigr)=0$ and $H^0\bigl(G,(W_\pi^*)_{w,L'}\bigr)=0$.
14. *Classes of extensions cut out of crystalline representations* ($w\in\Sigma_\ell$). Let $J_\tau$ be the set of jumps of $(R_\pi^\flat)_{w,L'}$ at the embedding $\tau$. (a) Let $V$ be a crystalline representation all of whose jumps at $\tau$ lie in $J_\tau$, for every $\tau$; let $T$ be a lattice of $V$, let $p:V\to L'$ be an $L'$-linear map with $p(gv)=p(v)$ for all $g\in G$ and $p(T)\subseteq\mathcal O_{L'}$, and let $e_0\in T$ with $p(e_0)=1$. Let $0\le e<t$, and let $\varphi:V\to L'(-e)/\mathcal O_{L'}(-e)$ be an $\mathcal O_{L'}$-linear map with $\varphi(gy)=g\varphi(y)$ for all $g\in G$ and all $y\in T$ with $p(y)=0$. Then the map $g\mapsto\ell^{c_B}\varphi(ge_0-e_0)$ from $G$ to $L'(-e)/\mathcal O_{L'}(-e)$ is finite. (b) For $0<e<t$, $H^1_f\bigl(G,L'(-e)/\mathcal O_{L'}(-e)\bigr)=0$. (c) $H^1_f\bigl(G,L'/\mathcal O_{L'}\bigr)=H^1_{\rm ur}\bigl(G,L'/\mathcal O_{L'}\bigr)$, for the trivial action. (d) For $0\le e<t$, with $H=H^1\bigl(G,L'(-e)/\mathcal O_{L'}(-e)\bigr)$ and $H_f$ its submodule $H^1_f$, $$\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O_{L'}}\bigl(\{y\in H:\ell^{c_B}y\in H_f\}/H_f\bigr)\le\bigl([K_{iw}:\mathbb Q_\ell]+1\bigr)\,c_B\,\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O_{L'}}\bigl(\mathcal O_{L'}/\ell\mathcal O_{L'}\bigr).$$
15. *Places of the first kind* ($w\in\mathcal P'$). Let $(V_1,T_1)$ be a representation with a lattice such that $V_1$ is isomorphic to a subrepresentation of a finite direct sum of representations $(R_x^*)_{w,L'}$, $x\in X_i$, with repetitions allowed. Let $f:(V_1,T_1)\to\bigl((W_\pi^*)_{w,L'},\mathcal O_{L'}^r\bigr)$ be a morphism of lattices that is onto. Then the $\mathcal O_{L'}$-module $$Z^1_f(\mathcal O_{L'}^r)\big/\bigl(f\circ Z^1_f(T_1)+B^1(\mathcal O_{L'}^r)\bigr),$$ formed for the lattice $\mathcal O_{L'}^r$ of $(W_\pi^*)_{w,L'}$, has finite length, at most $\lambda_w\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O_{L'}}(\mathcal O_{L'}/\ell\mathcal O_{L'})$.
16. *Places of the second kind* ($w\in\mathcal P''$). Let $(V_1,T_1)$ and $(V_2,T_2)$ be representations with lattices such that each of $V_1$ and $V_2$ is isomorphic to a quotient of a subrepresentation of a finite direct sum of representations $(R_x^\flat)_{w,L'}$, $x\in X_i$, with repetitions allowed. Let $f:(V_1,T_1)\to(V_2,T_2)$ be a morphism of lattices that is onto. Then $$\ell^{e'_w}\,Z^1_f(T_2)\subseteq f\circ Z^1_f(T_1)+B^1(T_2).$$
17. *Operations on crystalline representations* ($w\in\Sigma_\ell$). (a) Let $0\to V_0\to V_1\to V_2\to0$ be an exact sequence of representations. If $V_1$ is crystalline, then $V_0$ and $V_2$ are crystalline, and for every embedding $\tau$ and every integer $a$ the multiplicity of $a$ at $\tau$ for $V_1$ is the sum of those for $V_0$ and $V_2$. If the sequence has a $G$-equivariant $L'$-linear splitting and $V_0$, $V_2$ are crystalline, then $V_1$ is crystalline. (b) Let $\chi:G\to(L')^\times$ be a homomorphism such that the one-dimensional representation $L'(\chi)$ on which $g$ acts by $\chi(g)$ is crystalline, let $V$ be a crystalline representation, and let $V'$ be a representation with an $L'$-linear isomorphism $\theta:V\to V'$ such that $g\,\theta(v)=\chi(g)\,\theta(gv)$. Then $V'$ is crystalline, and for every embedding $\tau$, every jump $b$ of $L'(\chi)$ at $\tau$ and every integer $a$, the multiplicity of $a+b$ at $\tau$ for $V'$ is the multiplicity of $a$ at $\tau$ for $V$. (c) Let $V$, $V'$ be representations of the same dimension with an $L'$-bilinear pairing $P:V\times V'\to L'$ that is nondegenerate in both variables and satisfies $P(gx,gy)=\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)P(x,y)$. If $V$ is crystalline, then $V'$ is crystalline. (d) Every crystalline representation is de Rham.
18. *Dimension formula and integral duality* ($w\in\Sigma_\ell$). (a) There is an isomorphism of $\mathcal O_{L'}$-modules $H^2\bigl(G,L'(1)/\mathcal O_{L'}\bigr)\simeq L'/\mathcal O_{L'}$; here $H^2$ is formed with locally constant cochains $G\times G\to L'(1)/\mathcal O_{L'}$. (b) For every crystalline representation $V$, $$\dim_{L'}H^1_f(G,V)=\dim_{L'}H^0(G,V)+\sum_\tau\bigl(\dim_{L'}D_{{\rm dR},\tau}(V)-\dim_{L'}F_\tau^0(V)\bigr),$$ the sum being over the embeddings. (c) Let $V$, $V'$ and $P$ be as in 17(c), with $V$ de Rham, and let $T$, $T'$ be lattices of $V$, $V'$ with $T'=\{y\in V':P(x,y)\in\mathcal O_{L'}\text{ for all }x\in T\}$. For $d\in Z^1(T)$ and $c\in Z^1(V'/T')$, call the pair $(d,c)$ *orthogonal* if there is a locally constant map $\phi:G\to L'/\mathcal O_{L'}$ with $$P\bigl(d(g),g\,\tilde c(h)\bigr)\bmod\mathcal O_{L'}=\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)\phi(h)-\phi(gh)+\phi(g)\qquad(g,h\in G),$$ where $\tilde c(h)\in V'$ is a representative of $c(h)$; the left side does not depend on the representative. Then an element $c\in Z^1(V'/T')$ is finite if and only if $(d,c)$ is orthogonal for every $d\in Z^1_f(T)$, and an element $d\in Z^1(T)$ is finite if and only if $(d,c)$ is orthogonal for every finite $c\in Z^1(V'/T')$.
19. *Integral second cohomology* ($w\in\Sigma_\ell$). Let $Z^2$ be the $\mathcal O_{L'}$-module of the continuous maps $z:G\times G\to L'$ with values in $\mathcal O_{L'}$ that satisfy $$\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)z(h,k)-z(gh,k)+z(g,hk)-z(g,h)=0\qquad(g,h,k\in G),$$ and let $B^2$ be the module of the maps $(g,h)\mapsto\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)\phi(h)-\phi(gh)+\phi(g)$ with $\phi:G\to L'$ continuous with values in $\mathcal O_{L'}$. Then $Z^2/B^2\simeq\mathcal O_{L'}$ as $\mathcal O_{L'}$-modules.

A system at $i$ *satisfies the conditions of this page* if conditions 1 to 19 hold.

**The Selmer group.** Let $(R,\varrho,\pi,\mathcal P',\mathcal P'',\lambda,e',c_B)$ be a system at $i$. Let $A_\pi=(K_i/O_i)^r$, with the discrete topology and with $g\in G_E$ acting by the matrix $W_\pi(g)$, and let $H^1(G_E,A_\pi)$ be the group of continuous cocycles $G_E\to A_\pi$ modulo coboundaries; similarly for the subgroups $D_v$ and $I_v$. For a place $v\in\Sigma_\ell$, the isomorphism $b_{iv}$ extends to an isomorphism $K_i\to L_{iv}$ and induces an isomorphism $\bar b_{iv}:A_\pi\to L_{iv}^r/\mathcal O_{L_{iv}}^r$. The *Selmer group* $\operatorname{Sel}(W_\pi)$ is the $O_i$-module of the classes in $H^1(G_E,A_\pi)$ whose restriction to $D_v$ satisfies the following condition for every finite place $v$ of $E$.

- If $v\in\Sigma_\ell$: the restriction is the class of a cocycle $c:D_v\to A_\pi$ for which the map $g\mapsto\bar b_{iv}\bigl(c(e_{iv}^{-1}(g))\bigr)$ on $G_{K_{iv}}$ is finite, for the representation $(W_\pi)_{v,L_{iv}}$ and its lattice $\mathcal O_{L_{iv}}^r$.
- If $v\in S_i\cup Q_i$: no condition.
- Otherwise: the restriction to $I_v$ is zero in $H^1(I_v,A_\pi)$.

Put $s(\pi)=\operatorname{len}_\ell\bigl(\operatorname{Sel}(W_\pi)\bigr)$.

**The local terms.** For $w\in\mathcal P''$ put $$j_w=\dim_{L_{iw}}H^1_f\bigl(G_{K_{iw}},(W_\pi)_{w,L_{iw}}\bigr),\qquad h_w=\operatorname{len}_\ell\bigl(H^0(D_w,A_\pi)\bigr),$$ $$\lambda''_w=e'_w+j_w\,(e'_w+\Delta_i)+2\Delta_i+h_w.$$ For an integer $e$, let $(K_i/O_i)(-e)$ be the module $K_i/O_i$ on which $g\in G_E$ acts by $\epsilon(g)^{-e}$, and put $$h''=\max\bigl\{\operatorname{len}_\ell\bigl(H^0(D_w,(K_i/O_i)(-e))\bigr):w\in\mathcal P'',\ 0<e<t\bigr\},$$ with $h''=0$ if $\mathcal P''$ is empty.
