## Shared data in an arithmetic family

[Definition / hypothesis AGR.D235](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/family-shared-data.html)

Hypothesis (shared family data). Fix $\varepsilon\in\mathbb R$. There is a real starting scale $x_1>1$, a set $I_x$ of members for every $x\in\mathbb R$, and a set $\mathcal W\subset\mathbb R$ with $$\forall y\in\mathbb R\quad\exists x\in\mathcal W:\ y\le x,\qquad I_x\ne\varnothing\ (x\in\mathcal W),\qquad I_0\ne\varnothing.$$ Each member has the full member datum in ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)), including the fields and ranks in ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), and the local size datum in ([Local size and support data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.SizeData%E2%82%82/)). The scale and integer weights use the notation in ([Local congruence tables](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TwistedPlaces.AdmT/)). In particular its internal scale $x_{\rm int}$ is greater than one even when the external index $x$ is zero. Require $x_{\rm int}=x$ only for $x>x_1$. Every member's exponent is $\varepsilon$, and every member's interval parameter is the same real number $J$.

At a fixed $x$, the full labelled group $A$, the homomorphism $\vartheta$, the distinguished element $a_0$, the integers $e,n,k,m,t$, the numerically labelled set $T$, the integer set $T_2$, the primes $q,d$, the modulus $Q_S$, the root discriminant $\operatorname{rd}_E$, the two generator primes $g_0,g_1$, and both excluded primes $p_\Delta,p'_\Delta$ agree between members. The fields themselves need not be identified. To state the Artin comparison, define a function on all natural numbers by $$\alpha_{\rm num}(p)=\begin{cases} (\operatorname{pr}_q\alpha(p),\operatorname{pr}_d\alpha(p),\operatorname{label}(\operatorname{pr}_A\alpha(p))),&(p,Q_Sqd)=1,\\ (1,1,0),&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases}$$ Here $\operatorname{label}:A\to\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$ is its prescribed numerical labelling. Require $\alpha_{\rm num}$ to agree between all members at $x$.

There is a real $C_o$ such that, for every $x>x_1$ and every member, $$o\le C_o,\qquad Q_S\le C_on.$$ The constants $C_\Gamma,C_c,C_t$ and $C_2$ in the weights, scale bounds and selected-set data are independent of both $x$ and the member. Thus each member uses the same period-normalization constant $C_\Gamma$, cloud-gap constant $C_c$, rank exponent $C_t$ and second-set constant $C_2$.

For every $x$ there is a set $U_x$ and, for each member, a bijection from its finite set of source indices to $U_x$. If source indices in two members correspond to the same element of $U_x$, their binary sequences $\epsilon_z(l)$ agree for every $l\ge0$, with the sequences extended by zero for $l\ge k-2$.

For $x>x_1$ put $\delta_1=x^{-\varepsilon/8}$ and let $b',\mathcal G$ be the member's scale and weight-comparison quantities in ([Local congruence tables](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TwistedPlaces.AdmT/)). There is a real function $b_{\rm top}(x)$ satisfying, for every member, $$b_{\rm top}(x)=b'+\frac{2n\delta_1x^\varepsilon\mathcal G b'}k,\qquad b'+\frac{2n\delta_1x^{2\varepsilon}b'}k\le b_{\rm top}(x),\qquad b_v\le b_{\rm top}(x)\quad(v\in T).$$ For every member $i_0\in I_x$ and every numerical label $a<n$, there is a member $i\in I_x$ whose distinguished shift $a_*$ has label $a$ and whose two integer weight functions $a_v,b_v$ agree with those of $i_0$ at every matching numerical label $v\in T$.

The following additional equalities concern the orbit data in ([Orbit weights and local loss definitions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.Consts/)). At each fixed $x$, the width $\eta$ and cloud bound $S$ agree between members. Every Frobenius step $h_p$ has the same numerical label. Whenever $h,t$ and $h',t'$ in two members have matching numerical labels, the chosen orbit representatives $\beta(h,t)$ and $\beta(h',t')$ have matching labels. For every nonnegative stage $j$ and matching labels $t,t'$, the offsets $\omega_j(0,t)$ and $\omega'_j(0,t')$ agree. The entire local loss function $\kappa^I(p,n',B,\mathbf a,\mathbf b)$ and the entire exponent function $e_{\rm loc}(p)$ agree between members at $x$.

## Arithmetic families with local and theta data

[Definition / hypothesis AGR.D110](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/family-arithmetic-data.html)

Hypothesis (arithmetic family). Fix $0<\varepsilon<1/52$. Choose the real starting scale $x_1>1$, member sets $I_x$, unbounded set of occupied scales $\mathcal W$, source-index identifications, interval parameter and common constants of ([Shared data in an arithmetic family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/family-shared-data.html)).

At every real external scale $x$ and every member $i\in I_x$, choose all the member spaces, maps, forms, lattices, characters and local coefficient data in ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)). Choose the scale datum and local quantities in ([Local size and support data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.SizeData%E2%82%82/)), the orbit data in ([Orbit weights and local loss definitions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.Consts/)), the local polynomial lattices and chains in ([Local weight extensions and lattice chains](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.GatTies/)), and the comparisons in ([Period, measure and twist comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Ties/)). Choose also the divisor and intersection data in ([Divisor realizations in arithmetic families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DivX/)). These choices are made for every member, including the required member at external scale zero. An internal scale is greater than one; it equals $x$ only for $x>x_1$.

The scale domains of these requirements are as follows. At every scale retain the basic choices and defining conditions for each member, the local size datum, the orbit-representative and offset functions, the local dominant-row and lattice-chain data, and the divisor datum. Impose ([Period, measure and twist comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Ties/)) at every scale. The five comparisons in the first part of ([Local weight extensions and lattice chains](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.GatTies/))—base rank, Frobenius cycle lengths, the cycle-position bijection, its Frobenius step, and its initial weight row—also hold at every scale. The parameter-choice inequalities, support saturation, empty-orbit length condition, local loss infimum, local exponent equation, partner-weight and gain formulas, remaining orbit/lattice identifications, adjusted lattices and integral/theta comparisons are required only for $x>x_1$.

For every $x>x_1$ and every member require all the parameter choices in Hypothesis (weights and parameter choices) of ([Local congruence tables](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TwistedPlaces.AdmT/)), all the precision conditions in ([Theta parameters and precision inequalities](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ParamsT/)), and all the size bounds in ([Numerical size conditions for family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.SizesAt/)). The two ordered parameter lists and every global normalization are those of ([Fixed constants in an arithmetic family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.parConsts/)). The fifteen orbit constants and the exponent/error constants are shared by all members at all scales, with exactly the prescribed nonnegativity and fixed values.

For every $x>x_1$ and every member impose ([Integral and theta compatibility for families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Extra/)) and ([Adjusted source lattices and explicit heights](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.FamP.nuDef/)). The data appearing there are the same source spaces, type-indexed coefficient spaces, local polynomial lattices, twist-period modules, theta maps, pairings, divisors and constants chosen above. In particular the local coefficient support contains every nonbase place and every place with residue prime at most $X''$; an empty selected orbit set gives zero local source length away from its source prime; the partner weights, losses, corrections and lattice chains are the computed ones in ([Orbit weights and local loss definitions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.Consts/)) and ([Local weight extensions and lattice chains](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.GatTies/)). Require $\rho_B\le x^{-2}$ and use exactly the discarded-prime criterion in ([Orbit weights and local loss definitions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.Consts/)).

All further equalities between members at a fixed scale, including the numerical Frobenius steps, orbit representatives, offsets on orientation $0$, local loss functions and local exponents, are exactly those in ([Shared data in an arithmetic family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/family-shared-data.html)). The full adjusted source lattices, unshifted source lattices, rational row lattice and explicit source heights are fixed by ([Adjusted source lattices and explicit heights](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.FamP.nuDef/)).
