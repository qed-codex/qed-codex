## Rational forms along the column chain of a word: lattices, twisted chains, the terminal map and valuations at a place

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D270](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.MemberForms.PRat/)

This entry fixes notation for the forms with coefficients in the coefficient field that lie along the column chain of a word, and defines four notions used to state integrality: the terminal map over the coefficient field, the valuation of a vector at a place relative to a lattice, the local lattice at the prime of the word, and the local integrality of the period pairing.

**Setting.** Let $D$ be a datum and $L$ size data for $D$, in the sense of ([Data, families of data, classes of families, and properties of all sufficiently constrained families of a class](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.Closed/)). As there, $\Lambda$ is the coefficient field, with its embedding $\jmath\colon\Lambda\to\mathbb C$, and $W$ is the set of words; the words are the source indices of the entries cited below. For a finite place $w$ of $\Lambda$ write $\mathfrak p_w$ for its prime ideal, $p_w$ for its residue characteristic, $e_w$ for its ramification index over $\mathbb Q$ and $v_w$ for its valuation, normalised by $v_w(\Lambda^\times)=\mathbb Z$. Put $$\Lambda_{(w)}=\{a\in\Lambda:v_w(a)\ge0\},$$ and for an $\mathcal O_\Lambda$-submodule $A$ of a $\Lambda$-vector space write $A_{(w)}$ for the $\Lambda_{(w)}$-module generated by $A$. The tuples $\mu$, the base tuple $\mu_0$, the tuple $\mu_w$ of a place, the spaces $A_\mu$ with their integral spans $\widetilde M_\mu$ and idempotents $e_\mu$, the spaces $U_u$ and the pairings $P_{u,\mu}\colon U_u\times A_\mu\to\mathbb C$ are those of ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)). A tuple is *used* if it is $\mu_0$ or equals $\mu_w$ for some finite place $w$.

**The chain of a word.** Fix a word $u$. Its column chain, prime-level data and period data are those of ([Column chains and period pairings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Member/)): the class sets $C_i$, the finite groups $G_{i,c}$, the function spaces $\mathscr V_i=\mathbb C^{C_i\times Y_i}$, the maps $K_i\colon\mathscr V_i\to\mathscr V_{i+1}$ with composite $K^{(m)}=K_{m-1}\cdots K_0$, the prime $T_u$ with its label set $\mathcal V_T$, the space $U^{\rm ram}\subseteq\mathscr V_0$, the maps $\operatorname{ev}\colon\mathscr V_m\to Z_T$ and $l\colon Z_T\to R_T$ and the pairing $q\colon R_T\times P_T\to\mathbb C$. We write $D_\Gamma(u)$ for the positive integer $D_G$ of the chain. The forms on the chain are those of ([Integral forms on column representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.MemberForms/)): the subspaces $F_i\subseteq\mathscr V_i$ with their forms $q_i$, the Hermitian lattices $\mathcal L_i$ over $\Lambda$ with their $\jmath$-semilinear maps $j_i$ into $\mathscr V_i$, the set $C_P$ with the map $\tau_P\colon C_P\to C_m$ and the homomorphisms $G^P_c\to G_{m,\tau_P(c)}$, the subspace $F_P\subseteq R_T$ with its form $q_P$, the Hermitian lattice $\mathcal L_P$ with its map $j_P$ into $R_T$, the integer rows $\lambda_{i,\tau}$ and $\sigma_\tau$, the non-negative reals $b_\tau$, and, for a place $w$ with $p_w=T_u$, the bijection $v\mapsto\tau_{w,v}$ from $\mathcal V_T$ to $\mathcal T$ and the truth value attached to $w$, which we call the *flag* of $w$. For a Hermitian lattice $\mathcal L$ over $\Lambda$, in the sense of ([Rational Hermitian lattices with specified frames](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.HLat/)), we write $\mathcal L_\Lambda$ for its $\Lambda$-vector space, $\mathcal L^{\rm int}$ for its $\mathcal O_\Lambda$-submodule and $\|x\|_{\mathcal L,v}$ for the norm of $x\in\mathcal L_\Lambda$ at an infinite place $v$ of $\Lambda$. The maps $j_i$ and $j_P$ are injective.

**Twisted chains.** For every tuple $\mu$ the datum contains a second column chain for $u$ with the same weight arrays, equal to the chain of $u$ when $\mu=\mu_0$, with period data of its own and with a complex-linear isomorphism $\epsilon_{u,\mu}\colon\mathscr V_0\to\mathscr V_0^{\mu}$ of initial function spaces ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)). Prime-level data and forms for these chains, with prime $T_u$, are part of the choices of ([Integral comparisons for theta chains](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LatCmp/)); a family supplies them at every member with $x>x_1$. We call these the *twisted chain* and the *twisted forms* of $(u,\mu)$ and mark their objects with the superscript $\mu$: $K^{(m),\mu}$, $\mathcal L_i^{\mu}$, $j_i^{\mu}$, $\mathcal L_P^{\mu}$, $j_P^{\mu}$, $\operatorname{ev}^\mu$, $l^\mu$, $q^{\mu}$, $P_T^\mu$. The datum contains complex-linear isomorphisms $$\vartheta_u\colon U_u\to U^{\rm ram},\qquad\varepsilon_{u}\colon P_T\to A_{\mu_0},\qquad\varepsilon_{u,\mu}\colon P_T^{\mu}\to A_\mu ,$$ such that, for $\phi\in U_u$, $$P_{u,\mu_0}(\phi,F)=q\bigl(l(\operatorname{ev}(K^{(m)}\vartheta_u\phi)),\varepsilon_u^{-1}F\bigr)\quad(F\in A_{\mu_0}),\qquad P_{u,\mu}(\phi,F)=q^{\mu}\bigl(l^\mu(\operatorname{ev}^\mu(K^{(m),\mu}\epsilon_{u,\mu}\vartheta_u\phi)),\varepsilon_{u,\mu}^{-1}F\bigr)\quad(\mu\ne\mu_0,\ F\in A_\mu).$$

**The stabiliser condition.** The *stabiliser condition* for $D$ says: for every word $u'$, every tuple $\mu$, every $i\le m$ and every class $c$ of the twisted chain of $(u',\mu)$, the order of the group $G^{\mu}_{i,c}$ divides the integer $D_G$ of that chain, and that integer equals $D_\Gamma(u_0)$ for the base word $u_0$.

**The terminal map.** The *terminal map* of $u$ is $\operatorname{pt}_u=l\circ\operatorname{ev}\colon\mathscr V_m\to R_T$. It is *defined over $\Lambda$* if for every $x\in(\mathcal L_m)_\Lambda$ there is $y\in(\mathcal L_P)_\Lambda$ with $j_P(y)=\operatorname{pt}_u(j_m(x))$. Then $y$ is unique and $x\mapsto y$ is a $\Lambda$-linear map $$\operatorname{pt}_u^\Lambda\colon(\mathcal L_m)_\Lambda\to(\mathcal L_P)_\Lambda .$$ The terminal map is *integral* if it is defined over $\Lambda$ and $\operatorname{pt}_u^\Lambda(\mathcal L_m^{\rm int})\subseteq\mathcal L_P^{\rm int}$. The same definitions apply to the twisted forms of $(u,\mu)$, with $\operatorname{pt}_{u,\mu}=l^\mu\circ\operatorname{ev}^\mu$.

**Valuation relative to a lattice.** Let $\mathcal L$ be a Hermitian lattice over $\Lambda$, $w$ a finite place and $s$ a real number. A vector $y\in\mathcal L_\Lambda$ *has valuation at least $s$ at $w$ relative to $\mathcal L$* if $y=az$ with $z\in\mathcal L^{\rm int}_{(w)}$ and $a\in\Lambda$ such that $a=0$ or $v_w(a)\ge e_ws$. For a number $y\in\Lambda$ we say that $y$ *has valuation at least $s$ at $w$ in ramification units* if $y=0$ or $v_w(y)\ge e_ws$.

**The local lattice at the prime of the word.** Let $w$ be a finite place with $p_w=T_u$. For $v\in\mathcal V_T$ and $i\ge0$ let $\mathbf U_{i,v}$ be the endomorphism of $\mathscr V_i$ given by $$(\mathbf U_{i,v}\phi)_c=\sum_{x\in L^+_{i,v,c},\ f_{i,v,c}(x)=1}U_{i,v,c,x}\bigl(\phi_{u_{i,v,c}(x)}\bigr),$$ in the notation of ([Integral forms on column representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.MemberForms/)). Let $\mathcal V_{u,w}$ be the set of $v\in\mathcal V_T$ such that $\tau_{w,v}$ lies in the set $\Sigma_{\rm top}$ of top embeddings if the flag of $w$ is true, and the empty set if it is false. Put $\beta_\tau=-\lambda_{0,\tau,k-1}$, the negative of the last entry of the row $\lambda_{0,\tau}$. The *local lattice of $u$ at $w$* is the $\Lambda_{(w)}$-module $N^{T}_{u,w}$ of all $y\in(\mathcal L_0)_\Lambda$ for which there is $$x\in(\mathcal L_0^{\rm int})_{(w)}\quad\text{with}\quad j_0(x)\in U^{\rm ram}\quad\text{and}\quad j_0(y)=T_u^{\,m\sum_{v\in\mathcal V_{u,w}}\beta_{\tau_{w,v}}}\Bigl(\prod_{v\in\mathcal V_{u,w}}\mathbf U_{0,v}\Bigr)^{m}j_0(x).$$ The product of the operators $\mathbf U_{0,v}$ is the composite taken in a fixed order of $\mathcal V_{u,w}$. The number $E_T(u,w)$ is the one of ([Local error functions and weight normalizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ErrC/)).

**The source lattices.** The source lattice $N_u$ of $u$, its $\Lambda$-space $(N_u)_\Lambda$, the injection $\nu_u\colon(N_u)_\Lambda\to(\mathcal L_0)_\Lambda$, the lattice $M_u=\nu_u^{-1}(\mathcal L_0^{\rm int})$ and the unshifted source lattice $\widetilde N_u\subseteq(N_u)_\Lambda$ are those of ([Adjusted source lattices and explicit heights](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.FamP.nuDef/)); $j^N_u\colon(N_u)_\Lambda\to U_u$ is the $\jmath$-semilinear map of ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)), and $\vartheta_u(j^N_u(x))=j_0(\nu_u(x))$ for $x\in(N_u)_\Lambda$. The real numbers $\gamma_{j,w}$, $F_{j,w}$, $E_{u,j,w}$ and the set $\mathcal D$ of discarded primes are those of ([Local size and support data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.SizeData%E2%82%82/)), and $$s_{u,w}=\sum_{j=1}^{m}\bigl(\mathbf 1_{p_w\notin\mathcal D}\,\gamma_{j,w}+F_{j,w}-E_{u,j,w}\bigr).$$

**Local integrality of the period pairing.** The pair $(D,L)$ satisfies the *local integrality of the period pairing* if for every word $u$, every finite place $w$ of $\Lambda$, every $\varphi\in\widetilde N_u$ and every $F\in\widetilde M_{\mu_w}$ in the image of $e_{\mu_w}$ there is $y\in\Lambda$ with $$\jmath(y)=P_{u,\mu_w}\bigl(j^N_u(\varphi),F\bigr)\qquad\text{and}\qquad y=0\ \text{ or }\ v_w(y)\ge\lfloor e_ws_{u,w}\rfloor .$$

## Local error functions and weight normalizations

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D118](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ErrC/)

Hypothesis (local errors). Use ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), ([Integral level lattices and their class representatives](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ColLevel/)), ([Integral forms on column representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.MemberForms/)), ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)), ([Adjusted source lattices and explicit heights](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.FamP.nuDef/)), ([Orbit weights and local loss definitions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.G.Consts/)), and ([Fixed constants in an arithmetic family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.parConsts/)). Write $E_0=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})\subset E$, $K$ for the coefficient field, $z$ for a source, $T_z$ for its prime, $w$ for a finite place of $K$, $p=p_w$ and $e_w=v_w(p)$. Let $\Sigma$ be the CM type, $N=N_m$ and $\eta,S$ the weight-width parameters.

Choose real constants $c_6,c_{\rm dig},g_{\rm err}$ with $c_6\ge12$, $c_{\rm dig}\ge3$, $g_{\rm err}\ge10$. There are integral elements $\pi_0,\pi_1,\pi_z\in\mathcal O_E\cap E_0$ such that $$\pi_a\bar\pi_a=g_a\quad(a=0,1),\qquad \pi_z\bar\pi_z=T_z,\qquad \pi_z\equiv1\pmod{Q_Sqd(g_0g_1)^2\mathcal O_E}.$$ For each source $z$, its finite set of selected $T_z$-level labels embeds injectively into the places of $F$ dividing $T_z$; denote the place belonging to a label $v$ by $v_z$.

At every place of $F$ over $g_a$, the first component of $\bar\pi_a$ in the selected splitting of ([Integral level lattices and their class representatives](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ColLevel/)) has absolute value less than one. At every selected $v_z\mid T_z$, the scalar $\pi_z$ annihilates the quotient module $Q_{v_z}$. For $p_w=T_z$, the Boolean flag $b_{z,w}$ equals one exactly when $$v_w(\tau_K(\pi_z))>0\qquad\text{for every }\tau\in\Sigma,$$ where $\tau_K:E\to K$ is the lift of $\tau$ specified by ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)). When $p_w=T_z$, the embedding $\tau_{w,v}$ assigned to a selected label identifies its place by $$x\in\mathfrak p_{v_z}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad v_w(\tau_{w,v,K}(x))>0\qquad(x\in\mathcal O_F).$$ Every stabilizer order at every class of every twisted chain through stage $m$ divides that chain's integer $D_\Gamma$, and these integers all equal the distinguished initial value $D_\Gamma$.

The supplied weight blocks have the following prescribed values. With $p_j=k+j-1$, $q_j=m-j$, and $a^+_{j,\tau,i}$ defined in ([Adjusted source lattices and explicit heights](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.FamP.nuDef/)), put $$a^-_{j,\tau,c}=B_{\tau,j+c}-e_{\tau,j-1}-\frac{t+1+2c}{2}\quad(0\le c<q_j).$$ For every $1\le j\le m$, the large block is $(a^+_{j,\tau,i})_{0\le i<p_j}$ and the small block is $(-a^-_{j,\tau,c})_{0\le c<q_j}$. Thus the prescribed real expressions equal the supplied integer blocks. Put $$d_{\rm Fock}(j)=\sum_{\tau\in\Sigma}\left(\sum_{i<p_j}|a^+_{j,\tau,i}|+\sum_{c<q_j}|a^-_{j,\tau,c}|\right),\qquad F_G(j)=-d_{\rm Fock}(j)/2.$$

Here are the forced corrections. On the finite set $\{0,1\}\times A$, write $w_j(\beta,h)$ for the Hermitian weight at its corresponding embedding. If $h_p$ is the Frobenius step, define $$\rho_p(\beta,h)=(\beta+\mathbf1_{p\in\mathcal S},h+h_p),\qquad n_p=\begin{cases}2\operatorname{ord}(2h_p),&p\in\mathcal S,\\\operatorname{ord}(h_p),&p\notin\mathcal S,\end{cases}$$ where the first coordinate is modulo two and $\mathcal S$ is the signal prime class. For a real array $(D_s)_{s\in\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z}$, let $$\mathcal F(p,n,D)=\frac p{p^2-1}\sum_sD_s-\sup\left\{\sum_s\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}x_{s,i}:\begin{array}{l}x_{s,i}\ge0,\ x_{s,i}=0\text{ unless }i<n\text{ is odd},\\\sum_{i<n}p^ix_{s,i}+\sum_{i<n}x_{s-i,i}\le D_s\ (s\in\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)\end{array}\right\}.$$ Set $$F_{\rm orb}(j,p,z')=\mathcal F\bigl(p,n_p,((k-1)w_j(\rho_p^sz'))_{s\in\mathbb Z/n_p\mathbb Z}\bigr),$$ $$F_I(j,p)=\begin{cases}\displaystyle\sum_{\tau\in\Sigma}\frac{(k-1)w_j(\tau)}{p^2-1},&p\in\mathcal I,\ h_p=0,\\\displaystyle\frac1{n_p}\sum_{z'\in\{0,1\}\times A}F_{\rm orb}(j,p,z'),&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}$$ $$F_d(j)=-\frac{k-1}{2}\sum_{h\in A}\min\{w_j(0,h),w_j(1,h)\}.$$ For every $1\le j\le m$ and every finite $w$, require $$F_{j,w}=\begin{cases}F_G(j),&p\in\{g_0,g_1\},\\F_d(j),&p\text{ is in the class ramified at }d,\\F_I(j,p),&p\in\mathcal I\cup\mathcal S,\ p\le X'',\\0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}$$ The alternatives are taken in this order. The sets $\mathcal I,\mathcal S$ and the ramified class are those in ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)).

Define the following quantities for the error itself. The arrays $\nu^z_{j,\tau,i}$ are those in ([Adjusted source lattices and explicit heights](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.FamP.nuDef/)). Write $$u^z_{j,\tau}=\sum_{i=0}^{N_{j-1}-1}\nu^z_{j,\tau,i},\qquad V_{a,z,j,w}=\frac1{e_w}\sum_{\tau\in\Sigma}u^z_{j,\tau}v_w(\tau_K(\bar\pi_a)).$$ If $\lambda^z_{i,\tau,l}$ is the integer coefficient weight in ([Integral forms on column representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.MemberForms/)), let $$W^z_\tau=\max_{\substack{0\le i\le m\\0\le l<N_i}}\begin{cases}0,&l=0,\ \tau\in\Sigma_{\rm top},\\|\lambda^z_{i,\tau,l}-\lfloor i(t+1)/2\rfloor|,&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases}$$ The maximum uses the finite nonempty set of displayed indices. Put $$E_G(a,z,j,w)=F_G(j)+V_{a,z,j,w}+g_{\rm err}f(N^2+m^2\eta),$$ $$E_T(z,w)=b_{z,w}\left(2\sum_{\tau\in\Sigma}W^z_\tau+fN+|T|(t+1)(m+1)/4\right).$$ For $h\in A$, set $$c^z_{j,h}=u^z_{j,(0,h)}-\mathbf1_{h\in T}(\nu^z_{j,(0,h),0}-\nu^z_{j,(0,h),1})+\mathbf1_{h\in T}\nu^z_{j,(1,h),0},$$ $$E_d(j)=F_d(j)+\tfrac12\max_z\sum_{h\in A}c^z_{j,h},\qquad E_C(j,p)=c_{\rm dig}fN^2\log_pa+\frac{\sum_\tau w_j(\tau)}{p^2-1}.$$ The source set is nonempty, so the maximum over $z$ is defined.

Let $$A_p=\{(0,h):h\in A,\ p\notin\mathcal S\},\quad B_p=\{(\beta,h):p\in\mathcal S\text{ or }\beta=1\},\quad T_p=B_p\cap(\{0\}\times T).$$ Let $\mathcal C$ be the set of inert-class primes for which $h_p\ne0$ has odd order. Define $$E_X(j,p)=\mathbf1_{p\in\mathcal C}\left(\frac{k-1}{p^3}\sum_{z'\in B_p}w_j(z')+c_6fN^2\log_pa\right),$$ $$\begin{aligned}E_I(j,p)={}&\max\left(0,\frac1{n_p}\sum_{z'\in A_p}F_{\rm orb}(j,p,z')\right)+\frac p{p^2-1}|T_p|(b+\eta+1+3S)\\ &+\frac{c_6}{p}\sum_{z'\in B_p}w_j(z')+|B_p|N_{j-1}N_j\{\log_p(4n_pNa)+1\}\\ &+g_{\rm dl1}|B_p|N^2\log_p(2a)+g_{\rm dl2}|T_p|\{\log_p(2a)\}^2+E_X(j,p). \end{aligned}$$ The constants $g_{\rm dl1},g_{\rm dl2}$ are the selected-prime constants in ([Fixed constants in an arithmetic family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.parConsts/)). Finally, for every source $z$, $1\le j\le m$ and finite $w$, require $$E_{z,j,w}=\begin{cases}E_T(z,w),&p=T_z,\\v_p(D_\Gamma)+E_G(a,z,j,w),&p\ne T_z,\ p=g_a,\\v_p(D_\Gamma)+E_d(j),&p\ne T_z,g_0,g_1,\ p\text{ ramified at }d,\\v_p(D_\Gamma)+E_C(j,p),&p\text{ in neither previous case},\ p\in\mathcal I,\ h_p=0,\ p\le X'',\\v_p(D_\Gamma)+E_I(j,p),&p\text{ in neither previous case},\ p\in\mathcal I\cup\mathcal S,\ p\le X'',\\v_p(D_\Gamma),&\text{otherwise}. \end{cases}$$ All real logarithms, division, finite-dimensional traces and real suprema use their total values; in particular $\log0=0$ and $a/0=0$.

Hypothesis (theta indices and scalars). For every $\mu$, let $T_{\mu,j}$ be the $\mathcal O_K$-span of the rational vectors whose complex images belong to the specified integral theta-input set. Its Hermitian dual inside $KT_{\mu,j}$ is $$T_{\mu,j}^\#=\{x\in KT_{\mu,j}:\langle a,x\rangle\in\mathcal O_K\text{ for every }a\in T_{\mu,j}\}.$$ The local index of two integral submodules $A,B$ is $\operatorname{length}(A_w/(A_w\cap B_w))-\operatorname{length}(B_w/(A_w\cap B_w))$, taking each extended module length to its natural-number value, zero when infinite. For every twist $\mu$, finite place $w$ and stage $1\le j\le m$, the supplied local quantities $I_{\mu,j,w}$ and $J_{\mu,j,w}$ are exactly these local lattice indices of $(M_j,\theta_{\mu,j}T_{\mu,j})$ and $(T_{\mu,j},T_{\mu,j}^\#)$, respectively. These modules, their rational realizations and their theta maps are those of ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)) and ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)). For every nonzero $\pi$-isotypic subspace $M_{j,\pi}$ of $M_j$, require $$\lambda_{\mu,j,\pi}=\frac{\Re\operatorname{tr}(\theta_{\mu,j}\theta_{\mu,j}^*\operatorname{pr}_{M_{j,\pi}})}{\dim_{\mathbb C}M_{j,\pi}}\qquad(1\le j\le m).$$
