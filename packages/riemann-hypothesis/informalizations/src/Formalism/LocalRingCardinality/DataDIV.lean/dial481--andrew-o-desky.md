## Divisor and metric operations for arithmetic families

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D116](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DV.Geo/)

Hypothesis (divisor and metric operations). Let $K$ be the coefficient number field with its involution in ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)). Choose an index set $\mathcal Y$. For every $Y\in\mathcal Y$, choose a nonnegative integer $d_Y$, a field $F_Y$ with a $K$-algebra structure, an additive abelian group $D_Y$, and a predicate $\operatorname{Eff}_Y$ on $D_Y$ such that $$\operatorname{Eff}_Y(0),\qquad \operatorname{Eff}_Y(A),\operatorname{Eff}_Y(B)\Longrightarrow\operatorname{Eff}_Y(A+B).$$ Choose finite-dimensional $K$-subspaces $H_Y(A)\subseteq F_Y$, for $A\in D_Y$, satisfying $$H_Y(A)H_Y(B)\subseteq H_Y(A+B),\qquad \operatorname{Eff}_Y(B-A)\Longrightarrow H_Y(A)\subseteq H_Y(B).$$ Choose a distinguished $K_Y\in D_Y$, a set $C_Y$, predicates $\operatorname{Proper}_Y$ on $C_Y$ and $\operatorname{Sub}_Y$ on $C_Y\times C_Y$, a map $\operatorname{Supp}_Y:D_Y\to C_Y$, and a map $D_Y^{d_Y}\to\mathbb Z$.

Choose predicates $\operatorname{Nef}_Y,\operatorname{Big}_Y$ on $D_Y$, predicates $\operatorname{Free}_Y(A,S),\operatorname{Bir}_Y(A,S)$ for $A\in D_Y$ and $S\subseteq F_Y$, and predicates $\operatorname{NoContr}_Y(A,S,C)$ and $\operatorname{Vanish}_Y(A,f,C)$ for $C\in C_Y$ and $f\in F_Y$. Choose a function $h_Y:\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\times D_Y\to\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$. These predicates and maps are specified data; the displayed requirements on effectivity and $H_Y$ are their only conditions in this hypothesis.

For every $\sigma:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$, choose a measurable space $P_{Y,\sigma}$ with measure $\operatorname{vol}_{Y,\sigma}$, a commutative complex algebra $F_{Y,\sigma}$, and a ring homomorphism $c_{Y,\sigma}:F_Y\to F_{Y,\sigma}$ satisfying $$c_{Y,\sigma}(af)=\sigma(a)c_{Y,\sigma}(f).$$ For every $A\in D_Y$ choose a set $\mathcal M_{Y,\sigma}(A)$, with maps $$\mathcal M(A)\times\mathcal M(B)\to\mathcal M(A+B),\quad(h,h')\mapsto h+h',\qquad \mathcal M(A)\to\mathcal M(nA),\quad h\mapsto h^{[n]}\quad(n\ge0).$$ For every finite index set $I$ and family $s\in F_{Y,\sigma}^I$, choose $h_{\rm FS}(A,s)\in\mathcal M_{Y,\sigma}(A)$. There are two real-valued evaluation functions $p(A,h,f,y)$ and $p_K(A,h,f,y)$, for $h\in\mathcal M(A)$, $f\in F_{Y,\sigma}$, $y\in P_{Y,\sigma}$. There are predicates $\operatorname{Smooth}(A,h)$ and $\operatorname{Psh}(A,h,u)$ for real functions $u$ on $P_{Y,\sigma}$, predicates $\operatorname{Zero}(A,s,U)$ for $s\in F_{Y,\sigma}^{d_Y}$ and $U\subseteq P_{Y,\sigma}$, and $\operatorname{Agree}(A,f,g,U)$ for $f,g\in F_{Y,\sigma}$. There is a real function $$\operatorname{ResNorm}(A,h,B,h',s,f,y)\qquad(s\in F_{Y,\sigma}^{d_Y}),$$ and a predicate $\operatorname{Reg}(A,f,U)$. The indices $Y,\sigma$ are understood in all these functions.

Choose a set $\mathcal A_Y$. A divisor $A$, rational Hermitian datum $W$ over $K$, and $K$-linear map $i:W_K\to F_Y$ determine $\operatorname{of}(A,W,i)\in\mathcal A_Y$. Here a rational Hermitian datum means the basis, archimedean matrices and integral submodule in ([Hermitian row lattices and their rational models](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DivData/)). There is an operation $$\operatorname{tw}(L,c_\infty,c_f)\in\mathcal A_Y$$ for $L\in\mathcal A_Y$, arbitrary real functions on the infinite places and finite places of $K$, respectively. Choose two real functions $a_{\rm top},a_{\rm vol}$ and a predicate $\operatorname{ANef}$ on $\mathcal A_Y$. Choose real functions $s_\infty(L,v,n,f)$ and $s_f(L,w,n,f)$ for $n\ge0$, $f\in F_Y$ and infinite or finite places.

The following are the required identities. If $W$ has basis $(b_i)$ and matrix $X_v$, put $s_l=\sum_i(X_v)_{il}c_{Y,v}(i(b_i))$. Then $$s_\infty(\operatorname{of}(A,W,i),v,n,f)=\sup_{y\in P_{Y,v}}\sqrt{p(nA,h_{\rm FS}(A,(s_l))^{[n]},c_{Y,v}(f),y)}.$$ Whenever $c_f$ has finite support, require $$s_\infty(\operatorname{tw}(L,c_\infty,c_f),v,n,f)=e^{-nc_\infty(v)}s_\infty(L,v,n,f),$$ $$s_f(\operatorname{tw}(L,c_\infty,c_f),w,n,f)=e^{-nc_f(w)}s_f(L,w,n,f).$$ Real square roots and suprema use their total real values.

Finally let $K'/K$ be any number-field extension, $n\ge0$, $s=(s_1,\ldots,s_n)\in F_Y^n$, and $B\in M_{d_Y,n}(\mathcal O_{K'})$. Choose a predicate $\operatorname{Cut}(C;K',s,B)$ for $C\in C_Y$, a real function $\operatorname{CutHt}(L;K',s,B)$ for $L\in\mathcal A_Y$, and a natural-valued function $\operatorname{CutOrd}(K',s,B;A,f)$ for $A\in D_Y,f\in F_Y$. For every rational Hermitian datum $W$ over $K$ and linear map $i:W_K\to F_Y$, choose a predicate $\operatorname{ResFull}(K',s,B;W,i,L)$ and a real function $\operatorname{ResDeg}(K',s,B;W,i,L)$. These choices are required for every $Y$; they have no further identities in this hypothesis.

## Divisor realizations in arithmetic families

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D117](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DivX/)

Hypothesis (divisor realizations). Use ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), ([Hermitian spaces in the theta constructions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.CMData/)), ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)), ([Hermitian row lattices and their rational models](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DivData/)), ([Divisor and metric operations for arithmetic families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DV.Geo/)), ([Flag intersection and reduction data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DV.ShimX/)), and ([Local error functions and weight normalizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ErrC/)). Write $K$ for the coefficient number field, $E/F$ for the CM extension, $\Sigma$ for the selected CM type, and $\mu$ for a twist index with distinguished value $\mu_0$.

Choose divisor and metric operations over $K$ in the sense of ([Divisor and metric operations for arithmetic families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DV.Geo/)). Choose a level-index set $\mathcal K$, with a finite set of natural numbers $S_\kappa$ for each $\kappa\in\mathcal K$. For every specified Hermitian $E$-space $V$ and $\kappa$, choose a datum satisfying Hypothesis (flag degree data) in ([Flag intersection and reduction data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DV.ShimX/)), denoted $\mathscr F(V,\kappa)$. Its level set is $S_\kappa$. When $\dim_E V\ge2$, its rank parameter is $\dim_E V$.

For every prime $p$, choose a finite nonempty set $P_p$ with a bijection $P_p\simeq\{w:p_w=p\}$. Choose nonnegative weights $t_{p,a}$ of sum one, with the exact normalization $$t_{p,a}=\frac{e_wv_p(N\mathfrak p_w)}{[K:\mathbb Q]}\quad\text{when }a\leftrightarrow w.$$ Let $\mathbb K_p=\operatorname{Frac}W(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$. Choose $\iota_{p,a}:\mathbb K_p\to\mathbb C$. Define $$\Sigma_{p,a}=\{\tau:E\to\mathbb K_p:\iota_{p,a}\tau\in\Sigma\}.$$ For every $V,\kappa,p,a$, choose a set $\mathcal M(V,\kappa,p,a)$ and an assignment from it to the reduction intersection data in ([Flag intersection and reduction data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DV.ShimX/)), with rank $$r_\tau=\begin{cases}j,&\tau\in\Sigma_{p,a},\\N-j,&\tau\notin\Sigma_{p,a},\end{cases}$$ where $N,j$ are the parameters of $\mathscr F(V,\kappa)$. For each assigned model $z$, choose a curve-index set $C(V,\kappa,p,a,z)$ and a real function on $C(V,\kappa,p,a,z)\times\Sigma\times\{0,\ldots,N-1\}$.

Choose nonnegative numbers $d_{p,\tau}$ for every nonnegative integer $p$ and $\tau\in\Sigma$. If $p$ does not divide $|\operatorname{disc}E|$, require $d_{p,\tau}=0$ and require $\phi_p^F(y,x)=\phi_p^H(y,x)$ for every point $y$ and coordinate $x$ of every $\mathscr F(V,\kappa)$. For every $V,\kappa,p,a$, choose a distinguished model $z_{V,\kappa,p,a}$. There is a bijection $$C_p^H(\mathscr F(V,\kappa))\simeq\coprod_{a\in P_p}C(V,\kappa,p,a,z_{V,\kappa,p,a})$$ that identifies the degree function on its left with the chosen degree function on its right.

For every nonnegative $j$, choose a rational Hermitian lattice $\mathcal M_j$ over $K$, with nonsingular archimedean frames, finitely generated integral module spanning its rational space, and a semilinear map $J_j:(\mathcal M_j)_K\to M_j$ taking its specified rational basis to a complex basis. For $1\le j\le m$, require $$x\in\mathcal M_j\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad J_jx\text{ is an }\mathcal O_K\text{-linear combination of the specified integral generators of }M_j.$$ Every such generator lies in the range of $J_j$, and its norm at the distinguished embedding is $\|J_jx\|$.

For each $\mu$, choose a finite set $Q_\mu\subseteq\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$, with $Q_{\mu_0}=\varnothing$, $p_w\notin Q_{\mu_w}$, and $g_0,g_1\notin Q_\mu$ whenever $\mu=\mu_0$ or $\mu=\mu_w$ for some $w$. Choose a finite index set $I_\mu$ and integers $q_{\mu,v}>1$. For these used indices $\mu$, every $q_{\mu,v}$ must be a prime belonging to $Q_\mu$. For all $\mu,v$ and nonnegative $i$, choose nonzero complex numbers $\nu_{\mu,v,i}$ of absolute value one, complex numbers $Z_{\mu,v,i}$, a complex number $Z'_{\mu,v}$, and a nonnegative integer $d_{\mu,v,i}$.

For every nonnegative $j$, choose a finite set $C_j$, real functions $h_{j,c,\tau}$, objects $Y_{j,c}\in\mathcal Y$, and elements $B_{j,c}\in C_{Y_{j,c}}$ in the notation of ([Divisor and metric operations for arithmetic families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DV.Geo/)). There are additive maps $$\ell_{j,c}:\mathbb Z^{\Sigma\times\{0,\ldots,r-1\}}\to D_{Y_{j,c}},$$ and $K$-linear maps $i_{j,c}:(\mathcal M_j)_K\to F_{Y_{j,c}}$ with image in $H_{Y_{j,c}}(\ell_{j,c}(\kappa_j))$. Here $r=k+m-1$, $n_V=r+t$, $p_j=k+j-1$, $q_j=\max(0,m-j)$, and, using the supplied integer blocks $a^{\rm big},a^{\rm small}$ from ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)), $$\kappa_j(\tau,i)=\begin{cases}a^{\rm big}_{j,\tau,i}+\lfloor n_V/2\rfloor,&i<p_j,\\a^{\rm small}_{j,\tau,i-p_j}-\lfloor n_V/2\rfloor,&i\ge p_j,\ i-p_j<q_j,\\0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}$$ This requirement on the image holds at every nonnegative $j$.

Choose real functions $\ell\lambda_{j,\pi}(\sigma)$ for every nonnegative $j$, finite type $\pi$ and embedding $\sigma:K\to\mathbb C$, with $$\ell\lambda_{j,\pi}(\iota_K)=\log\lambda_{\mu_0,j,\pi},$$ where $\iota_K$ is the distinguished embedding. Choose a function $\kappa_R(\sigma)>0$ on these embeddings satisfying $\kappa_R(\bar\sigma)=\kappa_R(\sigma)$. Finally choose sets $\mathcal T,\mathcal T_{\rm red}$ with two real functions on $\mathcal T$ and one real function on $\mathcal T_{\rm red}$. These last sets and functions, and all the numerical functions above without displayed constraints, are part of the specified data.

## Flag intersection and reduction data

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D119](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DV.ShimX/)

Hypothesis (flag degree data). Let $\Sigma$ be the finite selected embedding set in ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)). Choose integers $N,j$ with $1\le j<N$, a finite set $S_{\rm lev}$ of primes, a finite nonempty set $C$, and a real number $v$. Put $$R=\Sigma\times\{0,\ldots,N-1\},\qquad d=|\Sigma|N(N-1)/2,$$ $$T=(\{0,1\}\times R)\sqcup(\{0,1\}\times\mathbb Z_{\ge0}).$$ For a variable set $I$, write $\mathbb R[I]$ for its polynomial algebra. Choose linear functions $$c:\mathbb R[R]\to\mathbb R,\quad a_{S,u}:\mathbb R[R]\to\mathbb R,\quad b_{S,u,p}:\mathbb R[T]\to\mathbb R$$ for every finite set $S\subseteq\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$, real $u$ and nonnegative $p$. Require $c(P)=0$ on homogeneous polynomials of degree different from $d$, and $a_{S,u}(P)=0$ on homogeneous polynomials of degree different from $d+1$. Let $P^{(1)},P^{(0)}$ denote the variable inclusions of $P\in\mathbb R[R]$ in $\mathbb R[T]$ using the Boolean coordinates one and zero, respectively. Require $$b_{S,u,p}(P^{(1)})=a_{S\cup\{p\},u}(P),\qquad b_{S,u,p}(P^{(0)})=a_{S\setminus\{p\},u}(P).$$

For every nonnegative $p$, choose sets $C_p^H,C_p^D$ with real-valued functions $d_p^H:C_p^H\times R\to\mathbb R$, $d_p^D:C_p^D\times R\to\mathbb R$, and a set $F_p$ with a predicate on $F_p$. Choose a set $P$ and three real-valued functions $\phi_p^H,\phi_p^D,\phi_p^F$ on $P\times R$. Choose a set $X$ with functions $r,h_-,h_+,n_{\rm con},n_{\rm curv}:X\to\mathbb R$ and $c_X:X\to C$, such that $$r(x)\ge e,\qquad0<h_-(x)\le h_+(x).$$ Choose a function $d_\infty:\Sigma\times X\to\mathbb R$, a map $c_P:P\to C$, functions $d_p^T,d_p^H:\Sigma\times P\to\mathbb R$, and $h_T:\mathbb R\times C\times\Sigma\to\mathbb R$. All subscripts $p$ here range over nonnegative integers, including zero.

There is a set $\mathcal B$ with real functions $a,a_c,g,g_c,v_{\rm ar}$, where $a_c,g_c$ also depend on $c\in C$, and with three predicates $P_1,P_2,P_3$ on $\mathcal B$. Require $$a(L)=\sum_{c\in C}a_c(L),\qquad g(L)=\sum_{c\in C}g_c(L).$$ Choose a map $\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^{\Sigma\times\{0,\ldots,N-1\}}\times\mathbb Q\to\mathcal B$. The sets, predicates and numerical operations in these paragraphs are specified data; no interpretation beyond these signatures and identities is imposed by this hypothesis.

Hypothesis (intersection algebra). Choose a set $\mathcal L$, a commutative $\mathbb Q$-algebra $A$, a map $c_1:\mathcal L\to A$, a rational vector space $D$, a linear map $c_D:D\to A$, a bilinear function $\langle\ ,\ \rangle:D\times A\to\mathbb Q$, a distinguished $P_D\in D$ with $c_D(P_D)=0$, and a nonnegative integer $d_A$. A pair of length $r$ means arrays $x,x'\in\mathcal L^r$ and $D_1,\ldots,D_r\in D$ satisfying $c_1(x'_i)=c_1(x_i)-c_D(D_i)$ for every $i$.

Hypothesis (reduction intersection data). For a prime $p$, let $\mathbb K_p=\operatorname{Frac}W(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$ and let $\mathcal E_p$ be the set of embeddings $E\to\mathbb K_p$. Given a rank function $r_\tau\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$ on $\mathcal E_p$, choose an intersection algebra, integers $c>0$ and $l\ge0$, pairs $L_\tau$ of length $r_\tau$ and $\Omega_\tau$ of length $\max(0,N-r_\tau)$, and two pairs $L_W,\Omega_W$ of length $fN$. Here $f=|\Sigma|$. Let $\bar\tau$ denote composition with CM conjugation. There must be bijections of indices such that $$c_1((\Omega_\tau)_i)=-c_1((L_{\bar\tau})_{\alpha(i)}),\qquad c_1((\Omega_W)_i)=-c_1((L_W)_{\beta(i)}).$$ These formulas concern the first, unprimed arrays of the pairs. Require $d_A=d+1$ with $d$ as in Hypothesis (flag degree data).
