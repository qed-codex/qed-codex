## Prime-to-characteristic primitive roots in Witt vectors

[Theorem PHT.0418](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.FG.witt_teichmuller_root/)

Let $\ell$ be prime and $d\ge1$ an integer. The Witt-vector ring $W_\ell(\overline{\mathbb F}_\ell)$ contains a primitive root of unity of order $\ell^d-1$.
