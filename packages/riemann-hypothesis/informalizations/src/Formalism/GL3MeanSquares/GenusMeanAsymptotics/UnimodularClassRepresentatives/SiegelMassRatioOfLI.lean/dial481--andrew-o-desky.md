## A common Hermitian mass constant in every positive rank

[Theorem ANT.0084](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HermitianLattices.Ext.L3.massConst_all/)

Assume the binary norm-representation and local incidence identities stated in the conventions. Let $K$ be a totally complex number field with involution $a\mapsto\bar a$ inducing complex conjugation at every embedding. Assume that for every maximal ideal $P\subset\mathcal O_K$ some $\theta\in\mathcal O_K$ has $\theta-\bar\theta\notin P$. Let $F$ be a number field embedded in $K$ with image exactly the fixed field of this involution. Put $f=|\Sigma_\infty(K)|$ and $v=|\operatorname{disc}K|^{1/(2f)}/2$. Define $$R_K(j)=\frac{((j-1)!(v/\pi)^j)^f}{\sqrt{(2v)^f}}\prod_P E_j(P),$$ where $P$ ranges over maximal ideals of $\mathcal O_K$ and $$E_j(P)=\begin{cases}(1-(-1)^j(NP)^{-j/2})^{-1},&\bar P=P,\\(1-(NP)^{-j})^{-1/2},&\bar P\ne P.\end{cases}$$ There is $c_1>0$ such that for every $m\ge1$, every $m$-dimensional $K$-space with compatible $\mathcal O_K$-action, every symmetric totally positive definite Hermitian form $h$, and every full integral $\mathcal O_K$-lattice $L$, $$\operatorname{mass}(h,L)=c_1\left(\prod_{j=2}^mR_K(j)\right)\prod_{\mathfrak p}\lambda_{\mathfrak p}(h,L).$$ The mass is the sum of reciprocal automorphism-group orders over the genus. To specify the local factors, let $\eta_{\mathfrak p}=-1$ when $\mathfrak p\mathcal O_K$ is prime and $+1$ otherwise, and let $d_{\mathfrak p}(L)$ be the largest $a\ge0$ such that the ideal generated by Gram determinants of $m$ vectors in $L$ lies in $(\mathfrak p\mathcal O_K)^a$. Let $\alpha_{\mathfrak p}(L)$ be the limit of the number of endomorphisms of $L/\mathfrak p^kL$ preserving its form modulo $\mathfrak p^k$, divided by $(N\mathfrak p)^{km^2}$. Then $$\lambda_{\mathfrak p}(h,L)=\frac{(N\mathfrak p)^{m d_{\mathfrak p}(L)}}{\alpha_{\mathfrak p}(L)}\prod_{j=1}^m(1-\eta_{\mathfrak p}^{\,j}(N\mathfrak p)^{-j}).$$ Products and divisions use the source's total conventions, including division by zero giving zero.
