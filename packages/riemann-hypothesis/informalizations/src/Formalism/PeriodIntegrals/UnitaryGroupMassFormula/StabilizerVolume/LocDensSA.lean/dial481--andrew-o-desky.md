## Additive approximation in the finite adeles

[Theorem ANT.0969](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PI.VolStabS.sAf_holds/)

Let $F$ be a number field and $U\subseteq\mathbb A_{F,f}$ an open additive subgroup. For every $x\in\mathbb A_{F,f}$ there is $a\in F$ such that $$x-a\in U,$$ where $F$ is embedded diagonally in its ring of finite adeles.
