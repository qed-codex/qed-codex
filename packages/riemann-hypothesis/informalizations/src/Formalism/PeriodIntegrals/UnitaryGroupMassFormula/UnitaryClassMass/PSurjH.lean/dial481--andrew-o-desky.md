## Commensurability of two number-field lattices

[Theorem ALG.0889](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PI.UnitaryGroupMassFormula.UJClassMass.cofinStep/)

Let $E$ be a number field, $V$ a finite-dimensional $E$-vector space, and $L,N\subseteq V$ finitely generated $\mathcal O_E$-submodules spanning $V$ over $E$. There is a nonzero integer $D$ such that $$DL\subseteq N,\qquad DN\subseteq L.$$
