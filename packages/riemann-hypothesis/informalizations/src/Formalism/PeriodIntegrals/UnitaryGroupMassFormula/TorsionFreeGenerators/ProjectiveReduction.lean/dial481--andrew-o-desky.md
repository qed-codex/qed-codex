## Torsion-free finite modules over a Dedekind domain are projective

[Theorem ALG.1051](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PI.FGTFG.projective_reduction/)

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain and let $M$ be a finitely generated torsion-free $R$-module. Then $M$ is projective over $R$.
