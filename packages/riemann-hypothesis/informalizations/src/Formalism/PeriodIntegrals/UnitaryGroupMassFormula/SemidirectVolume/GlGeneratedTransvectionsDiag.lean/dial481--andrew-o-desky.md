## General linear generators over a ring of stable range one

[Theorem ALG.1034](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PI.VolSemiDirect.W.Cj.gl_generated_transvections_diag/)

Let $A$ be a commutative ring with the following stable-range-one property: $$ax+by=1\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{there is }t\in A\text{ with }a+tb\in A^\times.$$ For every $k\ge0$, $\operatorname{GL}_k(A)$ is generated by its invertible diagonal matrices and the transvections $$I+cE_{ij}\qquad(i\ne j,\ c\in A).$$
