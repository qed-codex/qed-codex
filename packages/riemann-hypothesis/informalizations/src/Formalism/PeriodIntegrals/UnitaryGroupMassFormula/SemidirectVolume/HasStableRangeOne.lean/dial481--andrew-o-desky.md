## Stable range one for the finite adele ring

[Theorem ALG.1053](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PI.VolSemiDirect.W.SR.stable_rank_one_finite_adeles/)

Let $F$ be a number field and $\mathbb A_{F,f}$ its ring of finite adeles. For any $a,b,x,y\in\mathbb A_{F,f}$ satisfying $ax+by=1$, there is $t\in\mathbb A_{F,f}$ such that $a+tb$ is a unit of $\mathbb A_{F,f}$.
