## Approximation in a unitary group at finitely many places

[Theorem ANT.0540](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/unitary_approximation_finite/)

Let $F,E$ be number fields with $E$ an $F$-algebra. Let $\sigma:E\to E$ be a nontrivial $F$-linear involution and $J\in M_n(E)$ nonsingular with $\sigma(J)^t=J$. Write $$U(J)(A)=\{g\in\operatorname{GL}_n(E\otimes_FA):(\sigma\otimes1)(g)^tJg=J\}.$$ Let $T$ be a finite set of finite places of $F$, let $g_v\in U(J)(F_v)$ for $v\in T$, and let $0\ne D\in\mathcal O_F$. There is $\gamma\in U(J)(F)$ such that, for every $v\in T$ and every matrix entry, $$(g_v-\gamma)_{ij}\in D R_v,$$ where $R_v\subset E\otimes_FF_v$ is the subring generated by $\mathcal O_E\otimes1$ and $1\otimes\mathcal O_{F_v}$.
