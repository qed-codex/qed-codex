## Diagonalization of a matrix over the p-adic integers

[Theorem PHT.0446](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.GM.Top.smithZp/)

Let $p$ be prime, $n\ge0$, and $M\in M_n(\mathbb Z_p)$. There are $U,V\in M_n(\mathbb Z_p)$ with unit determinants and $d_1,\ldots,d_n\in\mathbb Z_p$ such that $$M=U\operatorname{diag}(d_1,\ldots,d_n)V.$$
