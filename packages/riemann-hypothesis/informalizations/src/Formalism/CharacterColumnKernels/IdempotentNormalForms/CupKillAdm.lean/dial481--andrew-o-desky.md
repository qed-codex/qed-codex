## Finite presentation over a left Noetherian ring

[Theorem ALG.0724](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.NG.finPres/)

Let $R$ be a left Noetherian ring, let $M$ be a left $R$-module, and let $n_1,\ldots,n_g$ generate $M$. There exist $m\ge0$ and $P\in M_{m,g}(R)$ such that $$M\simeq_R R^g/\langle P_{1,*},\ldots,P_{m,*}\rangle_R,$$ where the denominator is the left $R$-submodule generated by the rows of $P$.
