## An infinite fibre of a nowhere immersive holomorphic map

[Theorem AG.1430](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GroundTheory.Heights%E2%82%82.Heights.CriticalFibreInfinite.proof/)

Let $k,d\ge0$, let $U\subset\mathbb C^k$ be nonempty and open, and let $f:\mathbb C^k\to\mathbb C^d$ be holomorphic on $U$. Suppose the complex derivative $df_t:\mathbb C^k\to\mathbb C^d$ fails to be injective at every $t\in U$. Then there is $y\in\mathbb C^d$ such that $$\{t\in U:f(t)=y\}$$ is infinite.
