## The local ring at a point of a normal affine curve

[Theorem AG.0394](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GroundTheory.Heights%E2%82%82.Heights.NormalCurvePointDVR.proof/)

Let $I\subseteq\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_N]$ be prime, and assume the quotient $A=\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_N]/I$ is integrally closed and has Krull dimension one. For every $w\in\mathbb C^N$ vanishing on $I$, the local ring of $\operatorname{Spec}A$ at $w$ is a domain and a discrete valuation ring.
