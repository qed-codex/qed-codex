## Noetherianity of holomorphic germ rings

[Theorem AG.1568](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GroundTheory.Heights%E2%82%82.Heights.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.ComplexSets.AnalyticGermsNoetherian.proof/)

Assume Weierstrass division for holomorphic germs and the following coordinate property: every nonzero holomorphic germ at the origin becomes a germ of finite order on the last coordinate axis after an invertible complex-linear change of coordinates. Then, for every finite set $I$ and every $w\in\mathbb C^I$, the local ring $\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^I,w}$ of holomorphic germs at $w$ is Noetherian.
