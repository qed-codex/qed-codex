## Dimensions of components of a prime fibre

[Theorem AG.1454](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GroundTheory.Heights%E2%82%82.Heights.FibreCompDimZ.full/)

Let $R=\mathbb Z[x_i:i\in I]$ with $I$ finite, and let $P\subset R$ be prime with $P\cap\mathbb Z=(0)$. Suppose $\dim(R/P)=d+1$, where $d\ge0$, and let $p$ be a rational prime. Every prime $Q$ minimal over $P+(p)$ satisfies $$\dim(R/Q)=d.$$
