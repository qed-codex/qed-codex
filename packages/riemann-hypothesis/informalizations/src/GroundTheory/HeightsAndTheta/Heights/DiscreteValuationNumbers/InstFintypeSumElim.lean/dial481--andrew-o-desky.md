## Coordinate models and compatible chart lattices

[Definition / hypothesis AG.D016](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GroundTheory.Heights%E2%82%82.Heights.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.NumbersDVR.Bundles.IsModelOver/)

**Coordinate models.** Let $O$ be a commutative ring and $\Omega$ a field with an $O$-algebra structure. Let $K$ be a finite set and let $I_k$ be finite sets. For coordinates $x=(x_{ki})\in\Omega^{\coprod_k I_k}$, put $$P_x=\ker\bigl(O[X_{ki}]\longrightarrow\Omega[T_k:k\in K],\quad X_{ki}\longmapsto x_{ki}T_k\bigr).$$ Give $X_{ki}$ degree $e_k\in\mathbb N^K$, the $k$th standard basis vector. A polynomial is multihomogeneous if all its monomials have the same degree, and an ideal is multihomogeneous if it is generated by such polynomials, whose degrees may vary. An ideal is relevant if each block contains a variable outside it.

When $O$ is a discrete valuation domain and $d\in\mathbb N$, the coordinates satisfy the *dimension-$d$ model conditions* if $K$ is nonempty, $P_x$ is a relevant multihomogeneous prime, its inverse image in $O$ is zero, and $$\dim\bigl(O[X_{ki}]/P_x\bigr)=d+1+|K|.$$

For a chart choice $c\in\prod_k I_k$, write $$m_c(x)=\prod_kx_{k,c_k},\qquad R_c(x)=O[m_t(x)/m_c(x):t\in\prod_kI_k]\subseteq\Omega,$$ where the generated algebra contains the image of $O$. Division by zero in $\Omega$ is assigned the value zero. A nonzero chart means a choice $c$ with $m_c(x)\ne0$.

The *field-generation condition* means that the smallest subfield of $\Omega$ containing the image of $O$ and all within-block ratios $x_{ki}/x_{kj}$ is $\Omega$.

Additional coordinates $z=(z_{bi})$ have a finite block set $K'$ and finite coordinate sets $I'_b$. Their join $(x,z)$ has the disjoint union of the old and new blocks. If $c'$ is a chart choice for the join, $c'|_K$ is its restriction to the old blocks. The chart products and rings of the join are defined by the same formulas.

**Compatible chart lattices and scalar spans.** Let $V$ be an $\Omega$-vector space. A family of additive subgroups $(\Gamma_c\subseteq V)_{c\in\prod_kI_k}$ is compatible if, on every nonzero chart, $\Gamma_c$ is stable under $R_c(x)$, and if, for every pair of nonzero charts $c,t$ and every $v\in\Gamma_c$, there is an integer $a\ge0$ such that $$(m_c(x)/m_t(x))^a v\in\Gamma_t.$$ For an $O$-subalgebra $R\subseteq\Omega$ and a subset $S\subseteq V$, $RS$ denotes the additive subgroup of finite sums $\sum_i a_iv_i$ with $a_i\in R$ and $v_i\in S$. All these spans are taken inside $V$.

**Local freeness inside the ambient vector space.** Let $R\subseteq\Omega$ be an $O$-subalgebra, let $L\subseteq V$ be an additive subgroup and let $r\in\mathbb N$. For $f\in R$, define the following subsets of the ambient field and vector space: $$R[f^{-1}]_\Omega=\{a\in\Omega:\exists n\in\mathbb N,\ f^na\in R\},\qquad L[f^{-1}]_V=\{v\in V:\exists n\in\mathbb N,\ f^nv\in L\}.$$ Say that $L$ is locally free of rank $r$ inside $V$ if it is stable under $R$ and there are finitely many $f_1,\ldots,f_q,c_1,\ldots,c_q\in R$ with $\sum_{\ell=1}^q c_\ell f_\ell=1$ such that, for every $\ell$, there is an $\Omega$-basis $(b_{\ell,1},\ldots,b_{\ell,r})$ of $V$ satisfying $$L[f_\ell^{-1}]_V=\left\{\sum_{i=1}^r a_i b_{\ell,i}:a_i\in R[f_\ell^{-1}]_\Omega\right\}.$$ Natural powers include $0^0=1$. These displayed definitions apply also when $f=0$ and when $r=0$.
