## A unitary isometry from equal Gram matrices

[Theorem ALG.0242](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GroundTheory.Heights%E2%82%82.Heights.FiniteCovolume.GramDetermines.full/)

Let $E/F$ be a quadratic extension of number fields, with $F$ totally real and $E$ totally complex. Let $V$ be an $E$-vector space with a nonsingular Hermitian form $h$, conjugate-linear in its first argument. Let $n\ge0$ and $e_1,\ldots,e_n,e'_1,\ldots,e'_n\in V$. Suppose each of the additive subgroups generated by the two families is finitely generated, is stable under multiplication by $\mathcal O_E$, and spans $V$ over $E$. If $$h(e_i,e_j)=h(e'_i,e'_j)\qquad(1\le i,j\le n),$$ there is an $E$-linear automorphism $\gamma$ preserving $h$ with $\gamma(e_i)=e'_i$ for every $i$.

## A volume lower bound from the shortest integer combination

[Theorem ARAK.0608](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GroundTheory.Heights%E2%82%82.Heights.FiniteCovolume.MinkowskiVolumeForm.full/)

Let $V$ be a real inner-product space of finite dimension $d\ge0$, let $o$ be an orientation of $V$, and let $b_1,\ldots,b_d\in V$. Suppose $\lambda>0$ and $$\left\|\sum_{i=1}^d z_i b_i\right\|\ge\lambda\qquad(0\ne z\in\mathbb Z^d).$$ Then $$\left(\frac{\lambda}{\sqrt d}\right)^d\le\bigl|\operatorname{vol}_o(b_1,\ldots,b_d)\bigr|,$$ where $\operatorname{vol}_o$ is the Euclidean volume form of $o$.

## An index bound from weighted archimedean lengths

[Theorem ARAK.0627](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GroundTheory.Heights%E2%82%82.Heights.FiniteCovolume.WeightedIndexBound.full/)

Let $E$ be a totally complex number field of degree $d$, and let $c_w>0$ for every infinite place $w$ of $E$. Let $A\subset E$ be a finitely generated additive subgroup containing $\mathcal O_E$. Assume that every $0\ne a\in A$ satisfies $$\sum_{w\mid\infty}c_w\le\sum_{w\mid\infty}c_w|\sigma_w(a)|^2,$$ where $\sigma_w:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ is a representative of $w$. If there are $d$ elements $a_1,\ldots,a_d\in\mathcal O_E$, linearly independent over $\mathbb Q$, with $|\sigma(a_i)|\le r$ for every embedding $\sigma:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ and every $i$, then $$[A:\mathcal O_E]\le(r\sqrt d)^d.$$ Here $r\in\mathbb R$ is any number satisfying the displayed bound.
