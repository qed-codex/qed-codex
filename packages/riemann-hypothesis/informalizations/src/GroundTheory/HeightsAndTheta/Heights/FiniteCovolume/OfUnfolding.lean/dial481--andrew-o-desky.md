## A bounded basis from a shortest-vector and covolume bound

[Theorem ARAK.0461](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GroundTheory.Heights%E2%82%82.Heights.FiniteCovolume.ReducedBasisBound.full/)

For every $d\ge0$, $t>0$, and $c\in\mathbb R$, there is $C\in\mathbb R$ such that every full discrete lattice $L\subset\mathbb R^d$ satisfying $$\|x\|\ge t\quad(0\ne x\in L),\qquad \operatorname{covol}(L)\le c$$ has a $\mathbb Z$-basis $b_1,\ldots,b_d$ with $\|b_i\|\le C$ for every $i$. Norms and covolumes are Euclidean.
