## A prime of codimension one avoiding a given element

[Theorem AG.0423](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GroundTheory.Heights%E2%82%82.Heights.Vertical.CodimOnePrimeAvoid.full/)

Let $k$ be a field and $B$ a finitely generated integral $k$-algebra with $\dim B\ge1$. For every nonzero $h\in B$, there is a prime ideal $\mathfrak p\subset B$ such that $$h\notin\mathfrak p,\qquad \dim(B/\mathfrak p)+1=\dim B.$$ Here $\dim$ is Krull dimension.
