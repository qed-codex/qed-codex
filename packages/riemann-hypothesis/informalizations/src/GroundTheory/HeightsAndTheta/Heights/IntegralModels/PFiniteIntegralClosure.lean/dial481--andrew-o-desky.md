## Finiteness of integral closure in a finite field extension

[Theorem ALG.1386](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GroundTheory.Heights%E2%82%82.Heights.Model.FiniteIntegralClosure.proof/)

Let $A$ be an integral domain finitely generated as a $\mathbb Z$-algebra, let $K$ be its fraction field, and let $L/K$ be a finite field extension. Give $L$ the induced $A$-algebra structure, compatible with $A\to K\to L$. Then the integral closure of $A$ in $L$ is a finite $A$-module.
