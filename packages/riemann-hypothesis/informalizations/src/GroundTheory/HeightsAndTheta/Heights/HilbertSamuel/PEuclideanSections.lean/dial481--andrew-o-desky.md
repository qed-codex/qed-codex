## Euclidean section lattices in every degree

[Theorem ARAK.1677](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GroundTheory.Heights%E2%82%82.Heights.HilbertSamuel.EuclideanSections.proof/)

Assume the source's four section-lattice principles: a normed lattice construction with contractive multiplication and an asymptotic bound along multiples of one positive integer; nonzero small sections in every degree; the degree comparison for an injective norm-nonincreasing map of lattices; and uniform bounded-norm additive generators. Fix finite sets $K,I_k$, a field $\Omega$ of characteristic zero, coordinates $y=(y_{ki})$ in $\Omega$, an integer $D\ge1$, a Hermitian line datum $\overline A$ of multidegree $a\in\mathbb Z^K$, a multihomogeneous ideal $P_0$, and an intersection system $\mathcal I$ satisfying all the source's intersection axioms. Assume the coordinate ideal $P$ of $y$ defines an arithmetic variety of dimension $D+1$, and every element of $\Omega$ is a ratio of equal-multidegree integral homogeneous polynomials evaluated at $y$. Assume $P_0\subset P$, the metric of $\overline A$ is smooth and semipositive along $P_0$ at points of $P$, its generic degree is positive, it is invariant under conjugation, and it has finitely many integral sections in degree one, of norm at most one, generating on the prescribed affine charts.

Let $L_n$ be the integral section group: $s\in\Omega$ belongs to $L_n$ if, on every chart obtained by choosing nonzero coordinates $y_{k,c_k}$, the element $s\prod_k y_{k,c_k}^{-na_k}$ belongs to the $\mathbb Z$-algebra generated by the ratios $y_{ki}/y_{k,c_k}$. There are integers $r_n\ge0$ and injective additive maps $\phi_n:L_n\to\mathbb R^{r_n}$ whose images $\Lambda_n$ are discrete full lattices, such that $\|\phi_n(s)\|\le c$ whenever $c\ge0$ and $s$ has prescribed Hermitian section norm at most $c$. There is $C\in\mathbb R$ with $r_n\le C(n+1)^D$, and for every $\varepsilon>0$, for all sufficiently large $n$, $$-\log\operatorname{covol}(\Lambda_n)\le\bigl(\mathcal I(P;\overline A^{D+1})+\varepsilon\bigr)\frac{n^{D+1}}{(D+1)!}.$$ The section norm bound means $|g(z)|\le c|h(z)|A(z)^n$ for every homogeneous identity $g(y)=s h(y)$ of degree difference $na$ and every complex point $z$ of $P$, where $A(z)$ is the metric function.
