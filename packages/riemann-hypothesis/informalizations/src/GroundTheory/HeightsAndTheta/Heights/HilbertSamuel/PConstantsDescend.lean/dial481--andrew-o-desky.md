## Descent of integral constants after clearing one denominator

[Theorem ALG.1493](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GroundTheory.Heights%E2%82%82.Heights.HilbertSamuel.ConstantsDescend.proof/)

Let $\Omega$ be a field of characteristic zero and let $R,T\subset\Omega$ be $\mathbb Z$-subalgebras. Assume $R$ is finitely generated as a $\mathbb Z$-algebra and $T$ is finite as a $\mathbb Z$-module. Let $RT$ be the subalgebra generated by $R$ and $T$. Suppose that any two unital ring homomorphisms $RT\to\mathbb C$ agreeing on $R$ also agree on $T$.

There exists an integer $m\ge1$ such that $mt\in R$ for every $t\in T$.
