## A dimension step for a proper enlargement of a prime ideal

[Theorem ALG.0733](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Bl.FirstBlockLemmaB.goal1/)

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, let $P\subseteq Q$ be ideals with $P$ prime, and suppose $x\in Q\setminus P$ for some $x\in R$. Then $$\dim(R/Q)+1\le\dim(R/P).$$
