## How the five are derived

All five proofs are assembled in `MainTheorems.lean`, and all of them start from `allChi`.

1. **`allChi`** is the result `UpperTheory.FinalTheorems.everyCharacter`. Its statement, `UpperTheory.EveryCharacter`, is word for word the third statement above, so the third theorem is this result under another name. [allChi](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Supplements/Kernels/MainTheorems.lean#L7) · [EveryCharacter](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/EveryCharacter.lean#L13-L14)
2. **`rhOfAll`** gives the first two theorems. It takes $N=1$, where the L-function is $ζ$, and obtains $ζ(s)≠0$ for $\mathrm{Re}\,s>1/2$ and $s≠1$. Then `rh_of_right` treats a zero with $\mathrm{Re}\,s<1/2$. Such a zero is not $s=0$, because $ζ(0)=-1/2$. The functional equation writes $ζ(s)$ as $ζ(1-s)$ times $2$, a power of $2π$, the value $Γ(1-s)$ and a cosine. The first three factors do not vanish. The value $ζ(1-s)$ does not vanish, because $1-s$ lies to the right of $1/2$ and differs from $1$. The cosine vanishes only when $s$ is an even integer. In this half-plane that leaves $s=0$, already ruled out, or a trivial zero, which the statement excludes. [rhOfAll](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Supplements/Kernels/MainTheorems.lean#L9) · [From all characters to $ζ$](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/GammaFactorNonvanishing/CritLine.lean#L29-L32) · [rh_of_right](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/GammaFactorNonvanishing/RhOfRight.lean#L5-L62) · [Functional equation](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/0df444a360eaa60ab8c11dca51a86af692955474/Mathlib/NumberTheory/LSeries/RiemannZeta.lean#L176-L180)
3. **`claimAll`** bundles three statements, and `Main.lean` takes the middle one for the fourth theorem. `Claim.strip` proves it. For a zero with $\mathrm{Re}\,s<1/2$ it first passes to the primitive character $ψ$ that induces $χ$. The two L-functions differ by finitely many factors $1-ψ(p)p^{-s}$, and these do not vanish when $\mathrm{Re}\,s>0$. The functional equation of the completed L-function then gives a zero of $L(ψ^{-1},\cdot)$ at $1-s$, where the real part exceeds $1/2$, and that contradicts `allChi`. A zero with $\mathrm{Re}\,s>1/2$ contradicts `allChi` directly. `Main.lean` never selects the third component, a second derivation of the zeta statement.
