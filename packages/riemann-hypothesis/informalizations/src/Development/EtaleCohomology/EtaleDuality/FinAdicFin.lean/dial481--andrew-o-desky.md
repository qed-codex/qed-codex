## Exactness of restriction of scalars for étale sheaves

[Theorem AG.0403](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/LateralTheory.Fin.AdicFin.etale_torsion_cohomology_restriction_hom_exact/)

For every homomorphism $\varphi:R\to S$ of commutative rings, the restriction-of-scalars functor from sheaves of $S$-modules to sheaves of $R$-modules on the category of schemes with the étale topology preserves finite limits and finite colimits.
