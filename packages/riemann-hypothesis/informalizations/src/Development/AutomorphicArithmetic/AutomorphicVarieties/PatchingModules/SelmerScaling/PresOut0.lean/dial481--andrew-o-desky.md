## A maximal-dimensional prime in a complete local algebra

[Theorem ALG.1023](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.Pa.presOut0/)

Let $O$ be a complete discrete valuation ring and $B$ a commutative Noetherian local $O$-algebra, complete for its maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_B$. Assume $O\to B$ is local and induces an isomorphism on residue fields. Let $d\ge0$ and $x_1,\ldots,x_d\in\mathfrak m_B$ satisfy $$\mathfrak m_B\subseteq(x_1,\ldots,x_d)+\mathfrak m_OB+\mathfrak m_B^2.$$ For any prime ideals $Z,Q\subset B$, if $\dim(B/Z)\ge d+1$, then $Z\subseteq Q$.
