## Finite topological generation under open subgroups and quotients

[Theorem ALG.0504](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.AlgebraTools.TopFG.subgroup_quot/)

Let $G$ be a compact topological group containing a finitely generated dense subgroup. Every open subgroup of $G$ also contains a finitely generated dense subgroup. If $Z$ is a group equipped with a topology and $f:G\twoheadrightarrow Z$ is a continuous surjective homomorphism, then $Z$ contains a finitely generated dense subgroup.
