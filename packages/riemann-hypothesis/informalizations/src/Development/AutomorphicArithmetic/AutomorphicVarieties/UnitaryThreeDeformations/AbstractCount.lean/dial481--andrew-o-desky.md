## A Selmer-length bound from finitely many deformation points

[Theorem GC.0050](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.UnitaryThreeInertia.abstract_count/)

Assume ([Cotangent length bounded by valuations at graded orbits](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.Alg.M.main_holds/)) and the universal module-length bound by a cotangent length stated in the conventions. Let $\ell$ be prime and let $O$ be a complete discrete valuation ring of characteristic zero with finite residue field, finite over $\mathbb Z_\ell$, and faithfully embedded in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ compatibly with that algebra structure. Let $G,\Gamma$ be profinite groups and let $F$ be a deformation datum with a map $G\to\Gamma$, finitely many decomposition and inertia subgroups, characters $\eta:\Gamma\to\{\pm1\}$ and $\Psi:\Gamma\to O^\times$, and distinguished complex-conjugation and local elements. Assume its full structural, local-vanishing, dimension-count, nonpositive-weight torsion-vanishing, unramified-character and weight-$-2$ Selmer-vanishing conditions.

Fix $g_1\in\Gamma$ with $\eta(g_1)=1$ and $\Psi(g_1)^2-1\in O^\times$. Let $R$ be a complete noetherian local $O$-algebra with the same residue field and local structure map. Let $u:\Gamma\to\mathcal G_3(R)$ be the universal normalized deformation for this datum, where $\mathcal G_3(A)=\mathrm{GL}_3(A)\rtimes\{\pm1\}$ uses the specified inverse-transpose involution. Assume its weight-zero submodule is finite over $O$. Let $\phi\in\Gamma$ and let $\mathcal P$ be any predicate on $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$-valued representations. Suppose every irreducible-on-$\ker\eta$ specialization through the algebra generated by the weight submodules of $u$ satisfies $\mathcal P$. Suppose also that every characteristic-zero normalized point $r$ irreducible on $\ker\eta$ and satisfying $\mathcal P$ is infinitesimally rigid for the specified local conditions and has $$\operatorname{tr}r(\phi)\ne\operatorname{tr}r_0(\phi),$$ where $r_0$ is the fixed reference representation.

There are finitely many such characteristic-zero points $r_1,\ldots,r_t$, pairwise nonisomorphic on $\ker\eta$, and an integer $V\ge0$ such that for every $0\ne a\in O$, $$\operatorname{length}_O\mathrm{Ш}_F(a)\le V\le2e_O\sum_{i=1}^t v_\ell\bigl(\operatorname{tr}r_i(\phi)-\operatorname{tr}r_0(\phi)\bigr),\qquad e_O=\operatorname{length}_O(O/\ell O).$$ Here $v_\ell(\ell)=1$, and $\mathrm{Ш}_F(a)$ is the span in $H^1(\Gamma,(O/aO)(\Psi^{-1}))$ of classes unramified at the designated subgroups away from the exceptional set and locally coboundaries at that set.
