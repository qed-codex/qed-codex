## A cotangent-length bound from traces at deformation points

[Theorem AGR.1166](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.U3.model_upper/)

Let $\ell\ne2$ be prime and let $\mathcal O\subset\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ be a discrete valuation ring containing $\mathbb Z_\ell$ and finite over it. Fix the source's normalized local deformation datum: topological groups $\Gamma,H$, a continuous homomorphism $H\to\Gamma$, characters $\eta:\Gamma\to\mathbb Z^\times$, $\Psi:\Gamma\to\mathcal O^\times$, a subset of $\Gamma$ specifying vanishing constraints, the universal local deformation ring and constraint ideal, and marked elements $c,g_1\in\Gamma$. Let $S$ be the resulting normalized global deformation ring, $r$ its rank-three universal representation with multiplier $\eta$, and $M$ a graded model of $S$ over $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, with base point $x_0$ and weight-$(-1)$ cotangent length $L\in\mathbb N\cup\{\infty\}$. Give each matrix entry $r(g)_{ij}$ weight $j-i$, for $0\le i,j<3$. Assume ([Cotangent length bounded by valuations at graded orbits](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.Alg.M.main_holds/)), finite generation over $\mathcal O$ of the algebra generated by all products of matrix entries with total weight zero, and $$c^2=1,\quad\eta(c)=-1,\quad\Psi(c)=1,\quad\eta(g_1)=1,\quad\Psi(g_1)^2-1\in\mathcal O^\times.$$ Assume the point-rigidity package at $x_0$. A local point means an $\mathcal O$-algebra map $f:S\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ taking every element of the maximal ideal to an element of absolute value less than one. Let $\mathcal P$ be the local points for which the specialized representation is neither upper nor lower block triangular in the prescribed three-dimensional decomposition. Choose $\sigma\in\Gamma$ with $\eta(\sigma)=1$, and suppose $$a(f)=\operatorname{tr}r_f(\sigma)-\bigl(\Psi(\sigma)+1+\Psi(\sigma)^{-1}\bigr)\ne0\qquad(f\in\mathcal P).$$ Then $L<\infty$ and there is a finite $T\subset\mathcal P$ meeting every orbit under the prescribed one-dimensional diagonal conjugation torus, with no two distinct points in the same orbit, such that $$\frac{L}{e_{\mathcal O}}\le2\sum_{f\in T}v_\ell(a(f)),\qquad e_{\mathcal O}=\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O}(\mathcal O/\ell\mathcal O),\quad v_\ell(x)=-\log_\ell|x|_\ell.$$
