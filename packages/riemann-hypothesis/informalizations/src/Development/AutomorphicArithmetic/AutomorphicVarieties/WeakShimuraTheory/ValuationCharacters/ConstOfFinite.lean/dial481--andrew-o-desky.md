## Chains of components in a power series quotient

[Theorem ALG.0526](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.Ws.Ch.chain/)

Let $O$ be a complete discrete valuation ring and let $R$ be a nonzero commutative $O$-algebra. Suppose there is a surjective $O$-algebra map $$\pi:O[[X_1,\ldots,X_a]]\twoheadrightarrow R$$ whose kernel is generated by $b$ specified elements. Let $D\ge0$ satisfy $b+D\le a$. Any two minimal prime ideals $P,Q$ of $R$ can be joined by a finite chain of minimal prime ideals $$P=P_0,P_1,\ldots,P_t=Q$$ such that $$\dim R/(P_j+P_{j+1})\ge D\qquad(0\le j<t).$$
