## A normalized integral modular form of level one

[Theorem ANT.0476](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.Sd.levelOne/)

For every $N\ge1$ and every even integer $k\ge0$ with $k\ne2$, there is a modular form $f\in M_k(\Gamma_1(N))$ invariant under the weight-$k$ slash action of every $\gamma\in\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb Z)$, whose Fourier expansion at infinity satisfies $$f=\sum_{n\ge0}a_nq^n,\qquad a_0=1,\qquad a_n\in\mathbb Z\quad(n\ge0).$$
