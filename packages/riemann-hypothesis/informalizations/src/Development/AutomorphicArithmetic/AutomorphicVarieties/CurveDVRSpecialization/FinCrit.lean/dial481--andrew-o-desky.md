## A valuation-ring criterion for finiteness of the weight-zero part

[Theorem ALG.1198](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.Pr.finCrit/)

Let $\mathcal O$ be a complete discrete valuation domain with finite residue field. Let $R$ be a complete Noetherian local $\mathcal O$-algebra, with local structure map and residue field generated by the image of $\mathcal O$. Suppose there is a surjective $\mathcal O$-algebra map $$\pi:\mathcal O[[x_i:i\in I]]\twoheadrightarrow R,$$ where $I$ is finite, each $x_i$ has integer weight $w_i$, and $\ker\pi$ is stable under projection to each weight component. Put $z_i=\pi(x_i)$ and assume $z_i\in\mathfrak m_R$. Suppose that for every complete discrete valuation domain $A$ with finite residue field, local $\mathcal O$-algebra structure, and local $\mathcal O$-algebra homomorphism $f:R\to A$ annihilating $\mathfrak m_{\mathcal O}$, one has $$f(z_i)=0\ (w_i=0),\qquad \bigl(\forall i,\ w_i<0\Rightarrow f(z_i)=0\bigr)\ \text{or}\ \bigl(\forall i,\ w_i>0\Rightarrow f(z_i)=0\bigr).$$ Then the image in $R$ of the weight-zero formal power series is a finite $\mathcal O$-module.
