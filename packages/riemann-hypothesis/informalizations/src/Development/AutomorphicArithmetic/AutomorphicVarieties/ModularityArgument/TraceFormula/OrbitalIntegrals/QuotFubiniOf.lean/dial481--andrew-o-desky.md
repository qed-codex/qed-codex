## Closedness and commutativity of a regular twisted centralizer

[Theorem REP.0494](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.Z.Tf.Orb.twCent_of/)

Let $k$ be a nonarchimedean local field of characteristic zero and let $E/k$ be a finite Galois extension of nonarchimedean local fields with compatible valuations. Suppose $\operatorname{Gal}(E/k)$ is generated by $\sigma$. For $n\ge0$ and $\delta\in\operatorname{GL}_n(E)$, define $$N_\sigma(\delta)=\delta\,\sigma(\delta)\cdots\sigma^{[E:k]-1}(\delta),\qquad C_{\delta,\sigma}=\{g\in\operatorname{GL}_n(E):g^{-1}\delta\sigma(g)=\delta\}.$$ If the characteristic polynomial of $N_\sigma(\delta)$ is separable, then $C_{\delta,\sigma}$ is closed in $\operatorname{GL}_n(E)$ and any two of its elements commute. The automorphism acts entrywise on matrices.
