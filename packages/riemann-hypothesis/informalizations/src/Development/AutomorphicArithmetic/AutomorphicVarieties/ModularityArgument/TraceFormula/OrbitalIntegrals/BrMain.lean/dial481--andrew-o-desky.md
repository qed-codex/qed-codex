## The twisted centralizer of a norm from the base field

[Theorem ALG.1273](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.Z.Tf.Orb.twCentImg_holds/)

Let $E/k$ be a finite Galois extension with cyclic Galois group generated by $\sigma$, and put $d=[E:k]$. Let $n\ge0$, $\delta\in\operatorname{GL}_n(E)$, and $\gamma\in\operatorname{GL}_n(k)$. Assume that the characteristic polynomial of $\gamma$ is separable and $$N_\sigma(\delta)=\delta\,\sigma(\delta)\cdots\sigma^{d-1}(\delta)=\gamma,$$ where matrices over $k$ are viewed over $E$. Then $N_\sigma(\delta)$ has separable characteristic polynomial, and $$\{g\in\operatorname{GL}_n(E):g\delta\sigma(g)^{-1}=\delta\}=\{h\in\operatorname{GL}_n(k):h\gamma=\gamma h\},$$ with the right-hand subgroup included in $\operatorname{GL}_n(E)$.
