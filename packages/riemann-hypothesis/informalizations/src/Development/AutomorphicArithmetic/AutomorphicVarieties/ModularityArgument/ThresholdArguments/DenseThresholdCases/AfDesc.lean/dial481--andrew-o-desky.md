## Hypothesis list D: fifteen assumed statements on cuspidal automorphic representations of general linear groups, base change, descent to unitary groups and Galois representations

[Definition / hypothesis AGR.D047](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.Af.HypothesesH'/)

Require the following fifteen assertions.

**Vocabulary.** Let $F$ be a number field, $\mathbb{A}_F$ its adeles and $\mathbb{A}_F^\infty$ the finite adeles.

- A *cuspidal representation* of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb{A}_F)$ is a nonzero space $\pi$ of smooth functions on $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb{A}_F)$ that consists of cusp forms, is stable under the operators of the finite adelic group, of the maximal compact subgroup at infinity and of the Lie algebra, and has no proper nonzero stable subspace. A *cusp form* is a smooth function that is left invariant under $\mathrm{GL}_a(F)$, fixed by an open subgroup of the finite adelic group, finite under the maximal compact subgroup at infinity and under an algebra $Z$ of operators (not expanded), of moderate growth, and whose integrals over the rational unipotent radicals of the standard maximal parabolic subgroups vanish. The project has two versions, for totally real and for totally complex $F$; the first was opened and the second is taken to be its analogue.
- $\pi$ *has infinitesimal character $m$ at* an infinite place or complex embedding $\tau$, for a multiset $m$ of $a$ complex numbers, if certain operators $c_\tau(u)$, $u\in\mathbb{C}$, act on $\pi$ by the scalars $\prod_{z\in m}(u+z)$ (the operators were not expanded).
- $\pi$ *has Satake parameters $B$ at* a finite prime $w$, for a multiset $B$ of $a$ nonzero complex numbers, if a condition on the local representation of $\pi$ at $w$ holds (not expanded); it includes that $\pi$ is unramified at $w$ if the condition is the usual one.
- $\pi$ is *regular algebraic* if its infinitesimal characters are the exponents of an infinity type that is regular algebraic in the project's sense (not expanded). A pair of data (Satake parameters, infinitesimal characters) is *regular* if at every embedding the infinitesimal character has distinct elements, all in $\tfrac{a-1}{2}+\mathbb{Z}$.
- A *Hecke character* is the project's notion (not expanded), with a value $\eta(v)$ at the primes $v$ where it is unramified.
- For an extension $E/F$, a cuspidal representation $\Pi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb{A}_E)$ is a *weak base change* of $\pi$ if for almost all primes $w$ of $E$, with $v$ the prime below and $f$ the residue degree, $\pi$ has Satake parameters $B$ at $v$ and $\Pi$ has Satake parameters $\{b^f:b\in B\}$ at $w$, for some $B$.

**The statements.**

**(1)** *A collection of statements from the literature in four groups.* The assumption stated in ([Four groups of assumptions for the window bound](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Mem.LiteralAutomorphy.LiteratureRankNHypothesis/)).

**(2)** *Cohomological representations.* Let $E$ be totally complex, $a\ge1$, $\pi$ a cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb{A}_E)$ and $\Xi$ an algebraic representation of the project's kind (not expanded). Suppose that at every embedding $\tau$ the representation $\pi$ has an infinitesimal character $m$ such that $-m$ stands in a relation to $\Xi$ at $\tau$ (not expanded). Then $\pi$ is cohomological for $\Xi$: a cohomology attached to $\pi\otimes\Xi$ is nonzero (not expanded). If the relation says that $-m$ is the infinitesimal character of $\Xi$, this has the form of Clozel's theorem that regular algebraic cuspidal representations are cohomological.

**(3)** *Conjugation by automorphisms of $\mathbb{C}$.* Let $E$ be totally complex, $\pi$ a regular algebraic cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb{A}_E)$ and $\sigma$ a field automorphism of $\mathbb{C}$. There is a cuspidal representation $\pi'$ whose finite component is the conjugate by $\sigma$ of the finite component of $\pi$ (both notions not expanded), and which has at $\sigma\circ\varphi$ every infinitesimal character that $\pi$ has at $\varphi$.

**(4)** *Cuspidal cohomology is bounded by cohomology.* Let $E$ be totally complex, $U$ a compact open subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb{A}_E^\infty)$, $\Xi$ an algebraic representation, and $S$ a finite set of pairwise non-isomorphic cuspidal representations, each cohomological for $\Xi$ and with a nonzero vector fixed by $U$. Then the cardinality of $S$ is at most the dimension of a cohomology space attached to $U$ and $\Xi$ (not expanded).

**(5)** *Galois representations for $\mathrm{GL}_2$ over totally real fields.* Let $F$ be totally real with algebraic closure $\bar F$, $l$ a prime, $\iota\colon\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_l\cong\mathbb{C}$, and $\pi$ a regular algebraic cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A}_F)$ that is self-dual up to twist by a Hecke character $\chi$ with $\chi_v(-1)$ independent of the infinite place $v$. There are a continuous semisimple $r\colon\operatorname{Gal}(\bar F/F)\to\mathrm{GL}_2(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_l)$, an integer $w$ and multisets $HT_\tau$ of integers for $\tau\colon F\to\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_l$ such that: - at every prime $v\nmid l$ where $\pi$ has Satake parameters, the eigenvalues of Frobenius under $\iota\circ r$ are given by those parameters in a normalisation that was not expanded; - $HT_\tau=\{\tfrac12-z\}$ for $z$ in the infinitesimal character of $\pi$ at $\iota\circ\tau$, and $HT_\tau$ is invariant under $n\mapsto w-n$; - at every prime where the local component of $\pi$ satisfies a condition, a compatibility of $r$ with that component and a Hodge-Tate condition hold; a condition holds on the Weil-Deligne representations at the primes not dividing $l$ where $\pi$ satisfies a second condition, which includes the primes with Satake parameters; and at every prime where the local component is spherical the restriction of $r$ to a decomposition group is crystalline in the project's sense (none of these conditions was expanded).

**(6)** *Strong multiplicity one.* Let $F$ be totally real. Two cuspidal representations of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb{A}_F)$ that have the same Satake parameters at almost all primes are isomorphic.

**(7)** *The contragredient.* Let $F$ be totally real. Every cuspidal representation $\pi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb{A}_F)$ carries a nondegenerate bilinear form $B$ with $B(\pi(k)\varphi,\pi({}^tk^{-1})\psi)=B(\varphi,\psi)$ for $k$ in the finite adelic group, invariant under the maximal compact subgroup at infinity, and with $B(X\varphi,\psi)=B(\varphi,{}^tX\psi)$ for $X$ in the Lie algebra. That is, the contragredient of $\pi$ is $\pi$ composed with transpose-inverse.

**(8)** *Admissibility.* Let $F$ be totally real, $\pi$ a cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb{A}_F)$, $U$ an open subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb{A}_F^\infty)$ and $\xi$ a finite-dimensional matrix representation of the maximal compact subgroup at infinity. Then a finite-dimensional subspace of $\pi$ contains the image of every equivariant map from $\xi$ to $\pi$ whose image is fixed by $U$.

**(9)** *Quadratic base change for $\mathrm{GL}_2$.* Let $F$ be totally real, $E$ a totally complex quadratic extension, and $\pi$ a regular algebraic cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A}_F)$. Either $\pi\cong\pi\otimes\eta$ for a Hecke character $\eta$ of $F$ that at almost all primes takes the value $1$ or $-1$ according as the prime splits in $E$ or not, or there is a cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A}_E)$ that is a weak base change of $\pi$ and has at each embedding $\tau$ of $E$ the infinitesimal characters of $\pi$ at $\tau|_F$.

**(10)** *The representation of a newform.* Let $k\ge2$, let $f$ be a newform of weight $k$, level $\Gamma_1(M)$ and a Dirichlet character modulo $M$ (normalised, an eigenform of the Hecke operators at the primes not dividing $M$, and not coming from a proper divisor of $M$ in the sense that the project states), and let $j\in\mathbb{Z}$. There is a cuspidal representation $\pi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}})$ with infinitesimal character $\{\tfrac12+j,\ \tfrac32-k+j\}$ such that at every prime $p\nmid M$ it has Satake parameters $B$ with $\prod_{b}(X-p^{\,j}\tilde b)$ equal to the Hecke polynomial of $f$ at $p$, where $\tilde b$ runs over a normalisation of $B$ (the normalisation and the Hecke polynomial were not expanded).

**(11)** *Descent to a unitary group.* Let $E$ be a CM field unramified at all finite primes over its maximal totally real subfield $E^+$, $p$ a prime, and $S$ a finite set of primes of $E^+$ that split in $E$ and include all primes above $p$. Let $2\le n\le4$ and let $\pi$ be a regular algebraic cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E)$ whose dual is its complex conjugate (not expanded) and which has Satake parameters at every prime not above $S$. Then there is an irreducible automorphic representation $\sigma$ of the adelic group of a unitary group in $n$ variables attached to $E$ (the group and the notion of automorphic form on it were not expanded), unramified at every non-split prime, such that at almost all split primes $w$ the component of $\sigma$ along $\mathrm{GL}_n(E_w)$ has the Satake parameters of $\pi$ at $w$.

**(12)** *Cyclic base change of prime degree.* Let $K$ be totally complex, $M/K$ a Galois extension of prime degree, and $n\ge1$. Call a Hecke character $\eta$ of $K$ a *norm character* if $\eta\ne1$, $\eta^{[M:K]}=1$ and $\eta(v)^f=1$ for almost all primes $v$, $f$ being the residue degree in $M$. Three statements. (i) Every regular cuspidal representation $\pi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_K)$ either satisfies $B\cdot\eta(v)=B$ for its Satake parameters $B$ at almost all $v$, for some norm character $\eta$, or has a weak base change to $M$ that is cuspidal and has at each embedding $\tau$ of $M$ the infinitesimal characters of $\pi$ at $\tau|_K$. (ii) Every regular cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_M)$ that has, at almost all primes, the same Satake parameters at any two primes above the same prime of $K$ is a weak base change of a cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_K)$, with matching infinitesimal characters. (iii) If two cuspidal representations of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_K)$ have the same cuspidal weak base change, then for some $\eta$ that is trivial or a norm character their Satake parameters at almost all $v$ differ by the factor $\eta(v)$. In this item "cuspidal representation" allows the base field to be totally real or totally complex. It has the form of the theorem of Arthur and Clozel.

**(13)** *A disjunction of two descent statements.* At least one of the following holds. (a) For every imaginary quadratic field $M$, every regular cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A}_M)$ whose infinitesimal character at each embedding has the form $\{a,a+k\}$ with $k\ge2$ an integer, and which has the same Satake parameters at the two primes above $p$ for almost all $p$, comes from a cuspidal representation $\pi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}})$: at almost all primes of $M$ of degree one the Satake parameters agree with those of $\pi$ below, and the infinitesimal characters match. (b) For every CM field $L$ abelian over $\mathbb{Q}$, prime $l$, $\iota\colon\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_l\cong\mathbb{C}$ and continuous $W\colon\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})\to\mathrm{GL}_2(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_l)$ whose determinant satisfies a weight condition (not expanded) and whose restriction to $L$ is irreducible: if there is a character of $\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q})$ with values $\pm1$, trivial on $\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}/L)$ and equal to $-1$ on a complex conjugation, and if the restriction of $W$ to $L$ is attached to a regular algebraic cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{A}_L)$ (not expanded), then $W$ is automorphic over $\mathbb{Q}$ in the project's sense (not expanded).

**(14)** *Totally definite quaternion algebras.* For every totally real number field $F$ of even degree there is a central division algebra $D$ of dimension 4 over $F$ such that $\mathbb{R}\otimes_{F,\tau}D$ has no zero divisors for every real embedding $\tau$ and $\mathbb{A}_F^\infty\otimes_FD\cong M_2(\mathbb{A}_F^\infty)$. Read down to standard objects.

**(15)** *Cusp forms with a central eigenvalue decompose.* Let $F$ be totally real. Every cusp form on $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb{A}_F)$ that is an eigenvector of one distinguished element of the Lie algebra (not expanded) lies in the sum of the cuspidal representations of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb{A}_F)$.
