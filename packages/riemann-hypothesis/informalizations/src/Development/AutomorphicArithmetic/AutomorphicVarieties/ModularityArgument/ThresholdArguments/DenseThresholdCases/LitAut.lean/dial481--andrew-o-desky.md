## Four groups of assumptions for the window bound

[Definition / hypothesis GC.D071](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Mem.LiteralAutomorphy.LiteratureRankNHypothesis/)

The assumption is a conjunction of four groups, containing respectively 18, 15, 12 and 11 statements. The following identifies the statements read here; the remaining individual statements in the first, second and fourth groups are **not expanded**. Their conjunction is still part of the assumption.

1. Five of the 18 statements are items (14), (16), (29), (33) and (28) of ([Hypothesis list A: sixty assumed statements on representations of p-adic and real groups, automorphic forms, Galois representations and class field theory](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.UnitaryGroupThirdPlaceHypotheses/)). They concern finite-length properties, products of irreducible representations, a polynomial ring of representation classes, irreducible quotients and characters of finite general linear groups. The other 13 statements are not expanded.
2. Among the 15 statements are items (9), (27), (21), (20), (1), (24) and (30) of ([Hypothesis list A: sixty assumed statements on representations of p-adic and real groups, automorphic forms, Galois representations and class field theory](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.UnitaryGroupThirdPlaceHypotheses/)). These include decay and finiteness conditions on automorphic functions, conditions on their parameter multisets, a cohomological condition, reciprocity and the existence of ray class fields. The other eight statements are not expanded.
3. Require the twelve assertions in ([Local and global cohomological hypotheses](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Mem.LiteralAutomorphy.LitCoh/)).
4. Two of the 11 statements are items (15) and (14.5) of ([Hypothesis list C: sixty assumed statements on resolution of singularities, arithmetic intersection theory, positivity of line bundles, toroidal compactifications, abelian schemes and p-divisible groups](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CompanionTheory.Hypotheses%E2%82%82.ResolutionCharZero/)). A third is item (54)(e) of ([Hypothesis list A: sixty assumed statements on representations of p-adic and real groups, automorphic forms, Galois representations and class field theory](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.UnitaryGroupThirdPlaceHypotheses/)). Of the remaining eight, three are conjunctions of two, three and two statements, respectively, whose constituents are not expanded. Another asserts the existence of a sign and, for every complex abelian scheme with the stated smoothness and line-bundle conditions and every suitable uniformisation, an additive map from its period lattice into its de Rham cohomology, spanning it and compatible with endomorphisms and pairings; the predicates and full compatibility conditions are not expanded. Another asserts the existence of a sign and, for a smooth family of abelian schemes over a complex algebra and a holomorphic family of points, local uniformisations, lattice identifications and period maps with further properties not expanded. Another quantifies over an imaginary quadratic field, an Artin map, an automorphism of the complex numbers, an idele, an elliptic curve and a uniformisation, and asserts a transport condition not expanded. Another asserts, for an ideal lattice over an imaginary quadratic field, that the field generated by its elliptic $j$-invariant is the Hilbert class field, together with degree, equality and orbit assertions about the invariants; the lattice predicates are not expanded. The last asserts a functional equation relating two functions associated with ideal classes of a totally real field and the different, away from the indicated gamma-function poles; the defining predicate for the functions is not expanded.
