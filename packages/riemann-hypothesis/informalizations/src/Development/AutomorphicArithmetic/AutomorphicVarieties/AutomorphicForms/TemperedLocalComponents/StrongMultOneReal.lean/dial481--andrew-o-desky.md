## Strong multiplicity one over a totally real field

[Theorem AGR.1297](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.Af.strongMultOneReal/)

Let $F$ be a totally real number field and $a\ge1$. Let $\pi$ and $\pi'$ be cuspidal automorphic representations of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb A_F)$ realized as nonzero irreducible subspaces of smooth cuspidal automorphic functions with rational invariance, open finite level, finite-dimensional orbits under the maximal compact at infinity and the center of the enveloping algebra, and polynomial growth in $1+\|g\|+\|g^{-1}\|$ after every fixed adelic translate; the subspaces are stable under finite adelic translations, the maximal compact at infinity and the real Lie algebra. Suppose that at all but finitely many finite places $w$ there is a common Satake parameter for $\pi_w$ and $\pi'_w$. Then $$\pi\simeq\pi'.$$
