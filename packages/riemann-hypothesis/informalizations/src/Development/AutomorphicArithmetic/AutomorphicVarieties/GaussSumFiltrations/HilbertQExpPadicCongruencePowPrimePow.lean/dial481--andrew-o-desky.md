## Prime-power improvement of a Hilbert Fourier coefficient congruence

[Theorem ANT.1027](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.hilbertQExp_padicCongruence_pow_prime_pow/)

Let $F$ be a totally real number field, $p$ a prime, and $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_p\simeq\mathbb C$ a field isomorphism. For a real $r$, say a function $h$ on $\mathfrak H^{[F:\mathbb Q]}$ has coefficients bounded by $r$ if it admits an expansion as specified below. If $f-1$ has coefficients bounded by $p^{-1}$, then for every $N,m\ge0$, $$f^{p^Nm}-1\quad\text{has coefficients bounded by }p^{-(N+1)}.$$
