## Homology stability for general linear groups over a Dedekind domain

[Theorem ALG.0646](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.KB.sStab/)

Let $R$ be a Dedekind domain and let $k,n,n'\ge0$ satisfy $$n\le n',\qquad 2(k+1)+4\le n.$$ The usual inclusion $\operatorname{GL}_n(R)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{GL}_{n'}(R)$ induces an isomorphism on group homology in degree $k+1$ with real trivial coefficients.
