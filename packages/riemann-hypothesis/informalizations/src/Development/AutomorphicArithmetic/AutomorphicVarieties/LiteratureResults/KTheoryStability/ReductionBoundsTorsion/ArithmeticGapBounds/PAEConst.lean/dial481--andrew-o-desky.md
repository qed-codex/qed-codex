## A measurable function invariant under a dense subgroup is constant almost everywhere

[Theorem REP.0593](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.KB.SpectralGap.aeConst_holds/)

Let $G$ be a locally compact, second countable Hausdorff topological group, equipped with its Borel sigma algebra and a left Haar measure $\mu$. Let $A\subseteq G$ be a dense subgroup. If $u:G\to\mathbb R$ is measurable and $$u(gx)=u(x)\quad\text{for }\mu\text{-almost every }x\text{, for each }g\in A,$$ then there is $c\in\mathbb R$ with $u(x)=c$ for $\mu$-almost every $x$.
