## A unit avoiding all bounded positive archimedean products

[Theorem ANT.0230](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.KB.SplitAffineSpectral.unit/)

Let $L$ be a totally complex number field and let $0\le m<r_2(L)$ be an integer. There is $u\in\mathcal O_L^\times$ such that for every family of integers $a_w\ge0$, indexed by infinite places of $L$, with $$0<\sum_w a_w\le m,$$ one has $$\prod_w |u|_w^{a_w}\ne1.$$
