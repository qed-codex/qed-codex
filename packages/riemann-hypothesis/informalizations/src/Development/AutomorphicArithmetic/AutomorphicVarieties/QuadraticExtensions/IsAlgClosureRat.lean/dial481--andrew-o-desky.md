## Uniform generators in a family of two-adic towers

[Theorem GC.1298](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.Two.towerTwo/)

Assume ([Class-group ranks in cyclotomic towers](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.V.FW.ferreroWashington/)). Fix an integer $m\ge1$ and a subgroup $H\subseteq(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times$, and let $M=\mathbb Q(\mu_m)^H$. There is an integer $R\ge1$, depending only on $(m,H)$, with the following property. Let $q,d$ be primes satisfying $$q\equiv3\pmod8,\qquad d\equiv7\pmod{16},\qquad\gcd(qd,m)=1,$$ and let $L$ be a number-field realization of the compositum $M(i,\sqrt{-q},\sqrt{-d})$.

Fix an algebraic closure $\Omega$ of $L$, and let $U=\ker(\chi_2:G_L\to\mathbb Z_2^\times)$, the Galois group over $L(\mu_{2^\infty})$. For $N\in\{1,2\}$ let $D_N$ be the $\mathbb Z_2$-module of locally constant homomorphisms $\varphi:U\to\mathbb Q_2/\mathbb Z_2$ such that $\varphi$ vanishes on $U\cap I_v$ for every valuation place $v$ of $\Omega$, and on $U\cap G_v$ for every such place with $v(N)<1$ in multiplicative valuation notation. Here $I_v$ and $G_v$ are its inertia and decomposition groups over $L$. Put $$X_N(L)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z_2}(D_N,\mathbb Q_2/\mathbb Z_2).$$ Both $X_1(L)$ and $X_2(L)$ are generated by at most $R$ elements over $\mathbb Z_2$. Moreover $L$ is totally complex and contains a primitive fourth root of unity.
