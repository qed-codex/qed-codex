## Window condition: a bound on the Tate-Shafarevich groups of the twists Q\_l/Z\_l(-k) over auxiliary abelian fields

[Definition / hypothesis GC.D104](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Top.VWin/)

The window condition asserts a bound for the localization kernels of the twists $\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell(-r)$ over the abelian number fields defined below. The bound is asserted for a real parameter $x$ in an interval $[Y,Y^2]$, which is the window of the name, and for pairs of primes $(q,d)$ that satisfy the three conditions on residues, sizes and divisibility stated below.

**Localization kernels.** Let $E$ be a number field, $\overline E$ an algebraic closure of $E$ and $\ell$ a prime. Let $S_\ell$ be the set of finite places of $E$ above $\ell$. Let $G_{E,S_\ell}$ be the quotient of $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline E/E)$ by the closed normal subgroup generated by the inertia groups of all places of $\overline E$ lying over finite places of $E$ outside $S_\ell$; it is the Galois group of the maximal extension of $E$ unramified at every finite place outside $S_\ell$. The $\ell$-adic cyclotomic character $\chi_\ell:\operatorname{Gal}(\overline E/E)\to\mathbb Z_\ell^\times$ factors through $G_{E,S_\ell}$. For $r\in\mathbb Z$ let $\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell(-r)$ be the discrete group $\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell$ on which $g\in G_{E,S_\ell}$ acts as multiplication by $\chi_\ell(g)^{-r}$. For each place $v\in S_\ell$ choose a place of $\overline E$ above $v$, with decomposition group $D_v\subset\operatorname{Gal}(\overline E/E)$. The *localization kernel* is $$\mathcal S_{\ell,r}(E)=\ker\Big(H^1\big(G_{E,S_\ell},\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell(-r)\big)\longrightarrow\prod_{v\in S_\ell}H^1\big(D_v,\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell(-r)\big)\Big);$$ it is the Tate–Shafarevich group of the twist $\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell(-r)$ with respect to the places above $\ell$. Here $H^1(G,M)$ is the group of continuous functions $f:G\to M$ satisfying $f(gh)=f(g)+g f(h)$, modulo the coboundaries $f(g)=g m-m$ for $m\in M$. The localization map is restriction along $D_v\to\operatorname{Gal}(\overline E/E)\to G_{E,S_\ell}$. Let $\lambda_\ell(E,r)=\operatorname{length}_{\mathbb Z_\ell}\mathcal S_{\ell,r}(E)\in\{0,1,2,\dots\}\cup\{\infty\}$ be the length of $\mathcal S_{\ell,r}(E)$ as a $\mathbb Z_\ell$-module; when it is finite, $\mathcal S_{\ell,r}(E)$ is finite of order $\ell^{\lambda_\ell(E,r)}$. The notation suppresses the choices of the algebraic closure and of the places above the places in $S_\ell$.

**The bound $\mathcal B(E,t,\beta)$.** Let $E$ be a number field, $t\ge0$ an integer and $\beta$ a real number. The statement $\mathcal B(E,t,\beta)$ is the following. For every algebraic closure $\overline E$ of $E$ and every choice, for each prime $\ell$, of a place of $\overline E$ above each $v\in S_\ell$, the lengths $\lambda_\ell(E,r)$ formed with $\overline E$ and these places are finite for all primes $\ell$ and all integers $r$ with $0<r<t$, and, for every finite set $\mathcal P$ of primes, $$\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal P}\log\ell\sum_{1\le r<t}\lambda_\ell(E,r)\le\beta.$$ Equivalently, for every such $\overline E$ and every such choice of places, the groups $\mathcal S_{\ell,r}(E)$ with $\ell$ prime and $1\le r<t$ are all finite and $$\prod_\ell\ \prod_{1\le r<t}\#\mathcal S_{\ell,r}(E)\le\exp(\beta),$$ where the product over primes is the supremum of the finite partial products. Empty sums and products are $0$ and $1$, respectively.

**The fields.** Let $\psi$ be a primitive Dirichlet character, of conductor $N_\psi\ge 1$, let $e\ge 0$ be an integer and let $q$, $d$ be odd primes. A number field $E$ is *attached to* $(\psi,e,q,d)$ if there are an integer $M\ge 1$ divisible by $2^{e+2}$, $N_\psi$, $q$ and $d$ and an embedding of $E$ in $\mathbb Q(\zeta_M)$ whose image is the fixed field of the subgroup of $(\mathbb Z/M\mathbb Z)^\times=\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\zeta_M)/\mathbb Q)$ formed by the classes $u$ with $$u\equiv\pm1\pmod{2^{e+2}},\qquad\psi(u)=1,\qquad\Big(\frac uq\Big)=1,\qquad\Big(\frac ud\Big)=1 .$$

**The conditions on the primes.** Let $\varepsilon>0$ and $U\ge1$ be real numbers, and let $q$ and $d$ be primes. The following three conditions on $q$ and $d$ depend on $\varepsilon$ and $U$.

1. $q\equiv 3\pmod 8$ and $\tfrac12U^{\varepsilon/5}<\log q\le U^{\varepsilon/5}$.
2. $d\equiv 7\pmod{16}$ and $\tfrac12U^{\varepsilon/5}(\log U)^2<\log d\le U^{\varepsilon/5}(\log U)^2$.
3. $q$ divides $d+1$, and every odd prime $p\le U^{\varepsilon/5}$ divides $d+1$.

**Definition (window condition).** For every primitive Dirichlet character $\psi$, every real $\varepsilon$ with $0<\varepsilon<\tfrac1{52}$ and every function $\nu:\mathbb R\to\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$ with $$x^{2+8\varepsilon}\le 2^{\nu(x)}\le 2x^{2+8\varepsilon}\qquad\text{for all }x\ge 1,$$ there are real numbers $J\ge1$ and $X_0\ge1$ such that, for every $X\ge X_0$ and all primes $q$ and $d$ that satisfy conditions 1 to 3 with $U=X^{1/J}$, there is a real number $Y$ satisfying $$X^{1/J}\le Y,\qquad Y^2\le X,$$ such that, for every $x\in[Y,Y^2]$ and every number field $E$ attached to $(\psi,\nu(x),q,d)$, one has $$\mathcal B\big(E,\lfloor x^{401}\rfloor,\exp(x^{\varepsilon}/2)\big).$$
