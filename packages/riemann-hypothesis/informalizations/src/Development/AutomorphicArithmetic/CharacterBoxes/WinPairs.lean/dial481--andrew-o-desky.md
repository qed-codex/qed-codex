## Simultaneous auxiliary-prime and cohomological windows

[Theorem ANT.1564](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Top.winPairs/)

Two interval existence hypotheses give the same auxiliary primes for analytic estimates and bounds for localization kernels. Fix a primitive Dirichlet character $\psi$ of conductor $M\ge1$. Write $\mathbb N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ and $\lfloor u\rfloor_+=\max(0,\lfloor u\rfloor)$. All logarithms are natural.

For $e\in\mathbb N$ and odd primes $q,d$, let $\mathcal E(\psi,e,q,d)$ consist of the number fields $E$ for which there are an integer $N\ge1$, a primitive $N$th root of unity $\zeta_N$, and an embedding $\iota:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb Q(\zeta_N)$, with $2^{e+2},M,q,d$ dividing $N$, such that $$\tau_a\circ\iota=\iota\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a\equiv\pm1\pmod{2^{e+2}},\quad\psi(a)=1,\quad \left(\frac aq\right)=\left(\frac ad\right)=1$$ for every $a\in\mathbb N$ coprime to $N$. Here $\tau_a(\zeta_N)=\zeta_N^a$, and the parenthesized symbols are Legendre symbols.

For a number field $E$, an algebraic closure $\Omega/E$ and a rational prime $\ell$, let $G_E=\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/E)$ and let $S_\ell$ be the finite places of $E$ above $\ell$. Let $G_{E,S_\ell}$ be the quotient of $G_E$ by the closed normal subgroup generated by inertia groups at finite places outside $S_\ell$. Choose, for each $v\in S_\ell$, a place of $\Omega$ above $v$, and write $D_v\subset G_E$ for its decomposition group. Also choose a homomorphism $\kappa_\ell:G_{E,S_\ell}\to\mathbb Z_\ell^\times$ whose pullback to $G_E$ is the $\ell$-adic cyclotomic character. For an integer $a\ge1$, let $V_\ell(a)=\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell$ with the discrete topology and action $\sigma m=\kappa_\ell(\sigma)^{-a}m$, and let $H^1$ denote continuous Galois cohomology. Define $$h_\ell(a)=\operatorname{length}_{\mathbb Z_\ell} \ker\!\left[H^1(G_{E,S_\ell},V_\ell(a))\longrightarrow \prod_{v\in S_\ell}H^1(D_v,V_\ell(a))\right].$$ The maps on cohomology are restrictions along $D_v\to G_{E,S_\ell}$. For real $C,\epsilon$ and $x>0$, write $\mathcal B_C(\epsilon,x;e,q,d)$ for the following assertion: for every $E\in\mathcal E(\psi,e,q,d)$, every $\Omega/E$ and every collection of these choices, all $h_\ell(a)$ with $\ell$ prime and $1\le a<\lfloor x^C\rfloor_+$ are finite, and for every finite set $S$ of rational primes, $$\sum_{\ell\in S}(\log\ell)\sum_{1\le a<\lfloor x^C\rfloor_+}h_\ell(a) \le\sqrt{\exp(x^\epsilon)}.$$

Say that $q,d$ satisfy the prime conditions at real parameters $\epsilon$ and $Y>0$ if they are primes with $$q\equiv3\pmod8,\qquad d\equiv7\pmod{16},$$ $$\tfrac12Y^{\epsilon/5}<\log q\le Y^{\epsilon/5},\qquad \tfrac12Y^{\epsilon/5}(\log Y)^2<\log d\le Y^{\epsilon/5}(\log Y)^2,$$ and $q\mid d+1$, while every odd prime $p\le Y^{\epsilon/5}$ divides $d+1$.

To specify the analytic estimates, for $t>0$ and a nonprincipal primitive Dirichlet character $\chi$ put $$D_\chi=\frac{L'(1,\chi)}{L(1,\chi)},\qquad g_\chi(t)=\sum_{1\le n\le\lfloor t\rfloor_+}\Lambda(n)\chi(n)\max(t/n-1,0)+tD_\chi,$$ where $\Lambda$ is the von Mangoldt function. For an integer $m\ge1$, real $\epsilon,X$ and $Y>0$, let $\mathcal P_m(\epsilon,Y,X;q,d)$ mean the prime conditions at $(\epsilon,Y)$, coprimality $(m,qd)=1$, and the following two assertions.

For every Dirichlet character $\xi$ modulo $m$, every $(i,j)\in\{0,1\}^2\setminus\{(0,0)\}$, and every primitive Dirichlet character $\chi$ of any conductor satisfying $$\chi(a)=\xi(a)\left(\frac aq\right)^i\left(\frac ad\right)^j \qquad(a\in\mathbb N,\ (a,2mqd)=1),$$ one has $$|g_\chi(t)|\le t^{1/2+\epsilon/2},\qquad |D_\chi|\le4\log t \qquad(Y\le t\le X).$$ For every real primitive Dirichlet character $\rho$ of conductor dividing $8m$, every $(i,j)\in\{0,1\}^2\setminus\{(0,0)\}$, and every primitive Dirichlet character $\chi$ of any conductor satisfying $$\chi(a)=\rho(a)\left(\frac aq\right)^i\left(\frac ad\right)^j \qquad(a\in\mathbb N,\ (a,2mqd)=1),$$ one has $$L(s,\chi)\ne0\qquad\left(s\in\mathbb C,\quad \operatorname{Re}s>1-\frac1{400},\quad |\operatorname{Im}s|\le8m\exp(Y^{\epsilon/5})\exp\!\bigl(jY^{\epsilon/5}(\log Y)^2\bigr)\right).$$ Here real means that all values of $\rho$ are real. The primitive characters $\chi$ in these two assertions are nonprincipal: the Chinese remainder theorem allows $a$ to vary modulo $q$ when $i=1$, or modulo $d$ when $i=0$ and $j=1$, while $a\equiv1$ modulo $8m$ and the other auxiliary prime. The displayed character value then takes both signs.

Assume the following two assertions. Increasing a threshold preserves either assertion, so we take the thresholds at least $1$.

(i) For every $0<\epsilon<1/52$ and every function $\nu:\mathbb R\to\mathbb N$ satisfying $$x^{2+8\epsilon}\le2^{\nu(x)}\le2x^{2+8\epsilon}\qquad(x\ge1),$$ there are real numbers $J\ge1$ and $X_0\ge1$ such that, for every $X\ge X_0$ and every prime pair satisfying the prime conditions at $(\epsilon,X^{1/J})$, there is a real $W$ with $$X^{1/J}\le W,\qquad W^2\le X,\qquad \mathcal B_{401}(\epsilon,r;\nu(r),q,d)\quad(W\le r\le W^2).$$ This is the interval conclusion of ([Bound for localization kernels](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.One.vwin_of_row/)), assumed here for the fixed character $\psi$.

(ii) For every integer $m\ge1$, every $0<\epsilon<1/52$ and every real $A\ge1$, there is a real $Y_1\ge1$ such that every $Y\ge Y_1$ admits primes $q,d$ with $\mathcal P_m(\epsilon,Y,Y^A;q,d)$. This is ([Auxiliary primes with controlled twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/QH.pairEx/)).

Then for every $0<\epsilon<1/52$ and every real $c\ge1$ there is a real $J\ge1$ such that, for every $y\in\mathbb R$, some real $Z\ge\max\{1,y\}$ has the following property: for each $x\in[Z,Z^{3/2}]$ there are $Y\in\mathbb R$ and $q,d,e\in\mathbb N$ satisfying $$x^{1/J}\le Y\le x,\qquad\mathcal P_M(\epsilon,Y,x;q,d),\qquad x^{2+8\epsilon}\le c2^e\le2x^{2+8\epsilon},\qquad \mathcal B_{400}(\epsilon,x;e,q,d).$$
