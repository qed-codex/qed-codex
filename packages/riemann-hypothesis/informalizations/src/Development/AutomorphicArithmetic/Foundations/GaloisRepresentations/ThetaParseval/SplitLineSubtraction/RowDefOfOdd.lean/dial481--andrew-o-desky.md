## Local conditions on an l-adic representation of the Galois group of a number field

[Definition / hypothesis PHT.D054](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.RoadWGeo.ESAt/)

Throughout, $E$ is a number field, $\bar E$ an algebraic closure of $E$, $G_E=\operatorname{Aut}(\bar E/E)$ with its profinite topology, $\ell$ a prime, $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ an algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q_\ell$ with its $\ell$-adic absolute value $|\cdot|_\ell$, and $\iota\colon\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\to\mathbb C$ an isomorphism of fields. A *representation* is a homomorphism $\rho\colon G_E\to GL_n(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ for an integer $n\ge0$; no continuity is assumed unless stated. For a character $\chi\colon G_E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell^\times$, the twist $\chi\rho$ is the representation $\sigma\mapsto\chi(\sigma)\rho(\sigma)$. This page defines conditions on a representation $\rho$ at a finite place $w$ of $E$: one condition on Frobenius elements, and conditions at the places above $\ell$ that are formulated with the period rings of $p$-adic Hodge theory.

**Places of $\bar E$, Frobenius elements, the Weil group.** Let $w$ be a finite place of $E$, and let $N(w)$ be the cardinality of its residue field. A *place of $\bar E$ above $w$* is a valuation subring $A$ of $\bar E$ whose maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_A$ meets $\mathcal O_E$ in the prime ideal of $w$. Its *decomposition group* $D_A$ is the stabilizer of $A$ in $G_E$, and its *inertia group* $I_A$ consists of the $\sigma\in D_A$ with $\sigma x-x\in\mathfrak m_A$ for all $x\in A$. A *Frobenius element at $A$* is a $\sigma\in D_A$ with $\sigma x-x^{N(w)}\in\mathfrak m_A$ for all $x\in A$. The *Weil group* $W_A$ consists of the $\sigma\in D_A$ for which there are integers $s,t\ge0$ with $$(\sigma x)^{N(w)^t}-x^{N(w)^s}\in\mathfrak m_A\qquad\text{for all }x\in A;$$ the *degree* of such a $\sigma$ is $\deg\sigma=s-t$. A Frobenius element at $A$ has degree $1$.

**Unramified at $w$, inverse Frobenius killed by a polynomial.** Let $R$ be a finite multiset of complex numbers. We say that *$\rho$ is unramified at $w$ and $\prod_{r\in R}(X-r)$ kills its inverse Frobenius* if, for every place $A$ of $\bar E$ above $w$, $\rho$ is trivial on $I_A$ and $$\prod_{r\in R}\bigl(\rho(\sigma)^{-1}-\iota^{-1}(r)\bigr)=0$$ for every Frobenius element $\sigma$ at $A$. The displayed equation says that the polynomial $\prod_{r\in R}(X-\iota^{-1}(r))\in\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell[X]$, which $\iota$ maps to $\prod_{r\in R}(X-r)$, vanishes at the matrix $\rho(\sigma)^{-1}$.

**Local data at a place above $\ell$.** Let $A$ be a place of $\bar E$ above $w$. *Local data at $A$* consist of local period data for the prime $\ell$, a commutative $\mathbb Z_\ell$-algebra $O$ with a topology, and a coefficient comparison $(K_w,L_w,j,e,o)$ for $E$, $\bar E$, the group $D_A$ and $O$ that induces $A$. These notions are defined in ([Local period rings and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)); we recall the parts that this page uses. The local period data include two rings that carry the structures of Fontaine's rings $B_{\rm cris}$ and $B_{\rm dR}$. In the coefficient comparison, $K_w$ and $L_w$ are subfields of $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ of finite degree over $\mathbb Q_\ell$, and $L_w$ contains the image of every $\mathbb Q_\ell$-embedding of $K_w$ in $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$; $j\colon\bar E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ is a homomorphism of fields that maps $E$ onto a dense subset of $K_w$; $e\colon D_A\to\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell/K_w)$ is an isomorphism of topological groups with $e(g)(j(x))=j(g(x))$ for all $g\in D_A$ and $x\in\bar E$; and $o$ is an isomorphism of topological $\mathbb Z_\ell$-algebras from $O$ to the ring of integers of $L_w$. The comparison *induces $A$* if $$A=\{x\in\bar E:|j(x)|_\ell\le1\}.$$ Local data at $A$ exist only when $w$ lies above $\ell$. Every condition below that involves local data is required for every place $A$ of $\bar E$ above $w$ and all local data at $A$. An embedding $\tau\colon E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ *extends to $K_w$* if $\tau=\tau'\circ j|_E$ for a $\mathbb Q_\ell$-embedding $\tau'\colon K_w\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$.

**Crystalline and de Rham restrictions, Hodge–Tate multisets.** Let local data at $A$ be given, and put $r=\rho|_{D_A}$. ([Local period rings and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)) defines when $r$ is *crystalline*, when $r$ is *de Rham*, and when $r$ *has Hodge–Tate weights $J$ at $\tau$*, for an embedding $\tau\colon E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ and a multiset $J$ of integers. Each of these properties is the existence of a finite coefficient model of $r$ with the property: a subfield $L'\supseteq L_w$ of $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ of finite degree over $\mathbb Q_\ell$ such that $r$ has values in $GL_n(L')$, which makes $(L')^n$ a representation of $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell/K_w)$ through $e$. A *Hodge–Tate multiset of $r$ at $\tau$* is a multiset $J$ of integers such that $r$ has Hodge–Tate weights $J$ at $\tau$: there are a finite coefficient model of $r$ that is de Rham and a $\mathbb Q_\ell$-embedding $\tau'\colon K_w\to L'$ with $\tau=\tau'\circ j|_E$ such that every integer $h$ occurs in $J$ with the multiplicity of $h$ as a jump at $\tau'$ of the filtration on the module $D_{\rm dR}$ of the model. In particular, a Hodge–Tate multiset of $r$ at $\tau$ exists only if $\tau$ extends to $K_w$. We say that $\rho$ is *crystalline at $w$* if $\rho|_{D_A}$ is crystalline for every place $A$ of $\bar E$ above $w$ and all local data at $A$.

**Hodge–Tate multisets containing a prescribed set.** Let $Q$ be a set of pairs $(\tau_\infty,h)$ with $\tau_\infty\colon E\to\mathbb C$ an embedding and $h\in\mathbb Z$. We say that *the Hodge–Tate multisets of $\rho$ at $w$ contain $Q$* if, for every place $A$ of $\bar E$ above $w$, all local data at $A$ and every embedding $\tau\colon E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ that extends to $K_w$, there is a Hodge–Tate multiset $J$ of $\rho|_{D_A}$ at $\tau$ that contains every integer $h$ with $(\iota\circ\tau,h)\in Q$.

**The Weil–Deligne representation.** Let local data at $A$ be given, and put $r=\rho|_{D_A}$. We write $\operatorname{WD}(r)$ for the class $\operatorname{WD}_{w,\iota}(r)$ of Frobenius-semisimplified Weil–Deligne representations that ([Local period rings and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)) associates with $r$, formed with the isomorphism $\iota$. Its members are representations of the Weil group $W_A$, with the degree defined above, on finite-dimensional complex vector spaces, each with a monodromy operator. The *characteristic polynomial of $g\in W_A$ on $\operatorname{WD}(r)$* is the characteristic polynomial of the action of $g$ on the vector space of a member of the class.

**Crystalline at $w$ with Frobenius eigenvalues in $R$.** Let $R$ be a finite multiset of complex numbers. We say that *$\rho$ is crystalline at $w$ with Frobenius eigenvalues in $R$* if, for every place $A$ of $\bar E$ above $w$ and all local data at $A$, the representation $\rho|_{D_A}$ is crystalline and there are an element $g\in W_A$ of degree $-1$ and an integer $m\ge0$ such that the characteristic polynomial of $g$ on $\operatorname{WD}(\rho|_{D_A})$ divides $\prod_{r\in R}(X-r)^m$.

**Class of a representation at $w$.** Let $S$ be a finite multiset of complex numbers, and let $J$ be a function that assigns a multiset $J(\tau)$ of integers to every embedding $\tau\colon E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$. We say that *$\rho$ has class $(S,J)$ at $w$* if both of the following hold.

1. If $w$ does not lie above $\ell$: for every place $A$ of $\bar E$ above $w$, $\rho$ is trivial on $I_A$, and for every Frobenius element $\sigma$ at $A$ the characteristic polynomial of $\rho(\sigma)^{-1}$ is $\prod_{z\in S}(X-\iota^{-1}(z))$.
2. For every place $A$ of $\bar E$ above $w$ and all local data at $A$: $\rho|_{D_A}$ is crystalline and de Rham; for every embedding $\tau\colon E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ that extends to $K_w$, the multiset $J(\tau)$ is a Hodge–Tate multiset of $\rho|_{D_A}$ at $\tau$; and every $g\in W_A$ has characteristic polynomial $\prod_{z\in S}(X-z^{-\deg g})$ on $\operatorname{WD}(\rho|_{D_A})$.

**Class of a character at $w$.** Let $\chi\colon G_E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell^\times$ be a character, $c\in\mathbb C$, and $m$ a function from the embeddings $E\to\mathbb C$ to $\mathbb Z$. We say that *$\chi$ has class $(c,m)$ at $w$* if $\chi$, regarded as a representation $G_E\to GL_1(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$, has class $(S,J)$ at $w$ for the multiset $S=\{c\}$ and the function $J(\tau)=\{m(\iota\circ\tau)\}$. Explicitly, both of the following hold.

1. If $w$ does not lie above $\ell$: for every place $A$ of $\bar E$ above $w$, $\chi$ is trivial on $I_A$ and $\iota(\chi(\sigma)^{-1})=c$ for every Frobenius element $\sigma$ at $A$.
2. For every place $A$ of $\bar E$ above $w$ and all local data at $A$: $\chi|_{D_A}$ is crystalline and de Rham; for every embedding $\tau\colon E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ that extends to $K_w$, the one-element multiset $\{m(\iota\circ\tau)\}$ is a Hodge–Tate multiset of $\chi|_{D_A}$ at $\tau$; and every $g\in W_A$ has characteristic polynomial $X-c^{-\deg g}$ on $\operatorname{WD}(\chi|_{D_A})$.
