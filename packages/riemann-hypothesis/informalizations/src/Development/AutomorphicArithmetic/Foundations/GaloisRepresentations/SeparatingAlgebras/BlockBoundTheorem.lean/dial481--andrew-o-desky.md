## A norm bound from the rank of a finite group convolution matrix

[Theorem ALG.0258](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.groupDet_holds/)

Let $k$ be a normed field with an ultrametric norm, let $\ell$ be a prime, and let $H$ be a finite group of order a power of $\ell$. Let $a:H\to k$ satisfy $\|a(h)\|\le1$ for all $h$, and let $r\ge0$ be an integer. If the matrix $$M_{h,g}=a(hg^{-1})\qquad(h,g\in H)$$ has rank at most $r$, then $$\left\|\sum_{h\in H}a(h)\right\|^{|H|}\le\|\ell\cdot1_k\|^{\max(|H|-r,0)}.$$

## A dimension bound from a reproducing finite-support operator

[Theorem AGR.0323](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.countCore_holds/)

Let $E$ be a CM field, $F$ its maximal real subfield, and $U/F$ the standard definite unitary group of rank $a\ge0$. Choose a dominant integral weight at each infinite place of $E$ and its highest-weight module $W$ for $U_\infty=U(F\otimes_\mathbb Q\mathbb R)$. There is $M\ge0$, depending on $E,a,W$, with the following property.

Let $C\le U(\mathbb A_F)$ be any subgroup and $(p_x)_{x\in X}$ a finite family such that $$U(\mathbb A_F)=\bigcup_{x\in X}U(F)p_xU_\infty C.$$ Let $h:U(\mathbb A_F)\to\mathbb C$ have finite support. Assume that, for $x\in X$, $g,g'\in\operatorname{supp}h$, $\gamma\in U(F)$, $u\in U_\infty$, and $c\in C$, $$p_xg'=\gamma p_xguc\quad\Longrightarrow\quad g=g'.$$ Let $V$ be a linear subspace of the weight-$W$ automorphic multiplicity space, whose values are right $C$-invariant, and suppose $\sum_gh(g)R(g)$ acts as the identity on every value of every element of $V$. Then $$\dim_{\mathbb C}V<\infty,\qquad\dim_{\mathbb C}V\le M|X|\sum_g|h(g)|^2.$$

## A negligible spectral part has sublinear dimension along a level tower

[Theorem AGR.0339](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.count_holds/)

Let $E$ be a CM field, $F$ its maximal real subfield, and $U/F$ the standard definite unitary group of rank $a\ge0$. Choose a prime $w_0$ of $E$ split over $F$, a compact open subgroup $K$ of the finite adelic subgroup away from the place below $w_0$, and compact open subgroups $J_n\le\operatorname{GL}_a(E_{w_0})$. Assume $J_{n+1}\le J_n$, every $J_n$ is normal in $J_0$, and every identity neighbourhood eventually contains $J_n$. Write $K_n$ for the subgroup generated by $K$ and the subgroup at $w_0$ mapping into $J_n$.

Choose a dominant integral weight at each infinite place of $E$, and let $W$ be its finite-dimensional highest-weight module for $U(F\otimes_\mathbb Q\mathbb R)$. Let $\mathcal B$ be any property of irreducible automorphic subspaces, defined in the conventions below, of functions on $U(\mathbb A_F)$, satisfying the condition below. Let $V_n$ be the algebraic sum of their weight-$W$, $K_n$-fixed multiplicity spaces for subspaces with property $\mathcal B$. For every $\varepsilon>0$, for all sufficiently large $n$, $V_n$ is finite-dimensional and $$\dim_{\mathbb C}V_n\le\varepsilon[J_0:J_n].$$

## A bound for the part of a spectrum failing local polynomial conditions

[Theorem AGR.1265](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.countGen_holds/)

Let $E$ be a CM field, $F$ its maximal real subfield, and $U_a=U(I_a)$ the unitary group of the identity Hermitian form of rank $a\ge0$. Choose a finite prime $w_0$ of $E$ split over $F$. Let $K\subseteq U_a(\mathbb A_F)$ be a compact subgroup supported at finite places away from $w_0$ and its conjugate, open within that away-from-$w_0$ finite-adelic subgroup. Let $J_n\subseteq\operatorname{GL}_a(E_{w_0})$ be a decreasing sequence of compact open subgroups, normal in $J_0$, and eventually contained in every identity neighborhood.

Choose an algebraic archimedean weight $W$, a finite set $S$ of primes of $E$ split over $F$ and distinct from $w_0$ and its conjugate, and nonzero polynomials $\Phi_w\in\mathbb C[X_1,\ldots,X_a]$ for $w\in S$. Assume $K$ induces $\operatorname{GL}_a(\mathcal O_{E_w})$ on the local subgroup at each $w\in S$. Let $K_n$ be the subgroup generated by $K$ and the local subgroup at $w_0$ mapping into $J_n$.

Let $B_n$ be the sum, inside the weight-$W$, $K_n$-fixed automorphic forms, of the parts belonging to irreducible automorphic subrepresentations for which the following fails: at every $w\in S$, every ordered Satake tuple $z\in(\mathbb C^\times)^a$ of the local component satisfies $\Phi_w(z)\ne0$. For some $n$, $B_n$ is finite-dimensional and $$2\dim_{\mathbb C}B_n\le[J_0:J_n].$$

## An approximation to the constant function on a finite p-group

[Theorem REP.0298](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.algC_holds/)

Let $\ell$ be prime, $H$ a finite $\ell$-group, and $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$ a field isomorphism. Let $P\subseteq\mathbb C^H$ be a complex linear subspace invariant under every right translation $c(h)\mapsto c(hg)$. If $$|H|\le2\dim_{\mathbb C}P,$$ there is $c\in P$ such that $$\left|\iota^{-1}(1-c(h))\right|_\ell^2\le|\ell|_\ell\qquad(h\in H).$$

## A symmetric Laurent polynomial equal to one on a polynomial zero set

[Theorem ALG.0303](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.small_holds/)

Let $n\ge0$, $\Phi\in\mathbb C[X_1,\ldots,X_n]\setminus\{0\}$, and $\delta>0$. There is a symmetric Laurent polynomial $$p\in\mathbb C[Z_1^{\pm1},\ldots,Z_n^{\pm1}]^{S_n}$$ such that $$\Phi(z)=0\ \Longrightarrow\ p(z)=1\qquad(z\in(\mathbb C^\times)^n),$$ and $$\int_{(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)^n}|p(e^{2\pi i\theta_1},\ldots,e^{2\pi i\theta_n})|^2\,d\theta\le\delta.$$

## A unitriangular basis for a representation of a prime group

[Theorem REP.0527](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.flag_holds/)

Let $\ell$ be prime, let $H$ be a group in which every element has order a power of $\ell$, and let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb F_\ell$. For every representation $\rho:H\to\operatorname{GL}_{\mathbb F_\ell}(V)$ there is a basis $e_1,\ldots,e_d$ such that $$\rho(h)e_j-e_j\in\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_\ell}\{e_1,\ldots,e_{j-1}\}\qquad(h\in H,\ 1\le j\le d).$$

## A fixed vector modulo a proper invariant subspace

[Theorem ALG.0890](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.fixVec_holds/)

Let $\ell$ be prime, $H$ an $\ell$-group, $V$ a finite set equipped with an $\mathbb F_\ell$-vector space structure, and $\rho:H\to\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb F_\ell}(V)$ a representation. If $U\subsetneq V$ is invariant, there is $v\notin U$ such that $$\rho(h)v-v\in U\qquad(h\in H).$$

## Approximating automorphic forms using a finite prime-group cover

[Theorem AGR.0916](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.stepCore_holds/)

Let $E$ be a CM field, $G=U(1_a)$ its definite adelic unitary group, $\ell$ prime, $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$, and $W$ an algebraic weight. Let $C,C'\subseteq G(\mathbb A_{E^+})$ be subgroups with $C'\subseteq C$ and $C$ contained in the finite-adelic subgroup. Let $H$ be a finite $\ell$-group and $\lambda:C\twoheadrightarrow H$ have kernel $C'$. Suppose $q:G(\mathbb A_{E^+})\to H$ satisfies $$q(gx)=q(g)\quad(x\in G(E^+_\infty)),\qquad q(gc)=q(g)\lambda(c)\quad(c\in C),$$ and every function $g\mapsto\phi(q(g))$, with $\phi:H\to\mathbb C$, is an automorphic function in the sense of ([A negligible spectral part has sublinear dimension along a level tower](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.count_holds/)).

Let $P$ be a property of irreducible automorphic subrepresentations in the sense of ([A negligible spectral part has sublinear dimension along a level tower](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.count_holds/)). Denote by $A(W,C',\neg P)$ the sum of their weight-$W$, $C'$-fixed parts for subrepresentations failing $P$. Suppose this space is finite-dimensional and $$2\dim_\mathbb C A(W,C',\neg P)\le|H|.$$ For every $C$-fixed algebraic automorphic form $f$ of weight $W$, there are $F\in A(W,C',P)$ and $e:G(\mathbb A_{E^+})\to\mathbb C$ such that $$f(v,g)-F(v,g)=e(g)f(v,g),\qquad |\iota^{-1}(e(g))|_\ell^2\le|\ell|_\ell$$ for every weight vector $v$ and every $g$.

## Approximating a form while avoiding specified Satake zero sets

[Theorem AGR.1177](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.step_holds/)

Let $E$ be a CM field, $E^+$ its maximal real subfield, $\ell$ a prime, and $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$. Let $a\ge1$ and $U$ be the adelic unitary group of the identity Hermitian form on $E^a$. Choose a split place $w_0\mid\ell$ of $E$. Let $K\subset U(\mathbb A_{E^+})$ be a compact subgroup with trivial infinite component and trivial components at every place of $E$ over $w_0|_{E^+}$, open relative to that subgroup of adelic elements. Fix an algebraic weight $W$, a finite set $S$ of split places of $E$ lying over places of $E^+$ different from $w_0|_{E^+}$, and nonzero polynomials $\Phi_w\in\mathbb C[X_1,\ldots,X_a]$ for $w\in S$. Assume the intersection of $K$ with the local subgroup at $w$ is exactly $\operatorname{GL}_a(\mathcal O_{E_w})$ under the split local identification, for each $w\in S$.

For every $K$-fixed algebraic automorphic form $f$ of weight $W$, there exist a form $G$ and a function $e:U(\mathbb A_{E^+})\to\mathbb C$ such that $$f(v,g)-G(v,g)=e(g)f(v,g),\qquad |\iota^{-1}(e(g))|_\ell^2\le|\ell|_\ell$$ for every coefficient vector $v$ of $W$ and every $g$. The form $G$ belongs to the sum of the $K$-fixed weight-$W$ parts of automorphic subrepresentations $\pi$ with the following property: for each $w\in S$ and every ordered nonzero Satake tuple $z$ of a local component of $\pi$ at $w$, $$\Phi_w(z)\ne0.$$

## Finite operators realizing symmetric Satake polynomials

[Theorem REP.1223](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.locOp_holds/)

Let $E$ be a number field, $w$ a finite place, $a\ge0$, $G=\mathrm{GL}_a(E_w)$, and $K=\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathcal O_{E_w})$. There is $C\in\mathbb R$ such that for every symmetric complex Laurent polynomial $p$ in $a$ variables there is a finitely supported $h:G\to\mathbb C$ with at most one support element in each right $K$-coset and $$\sum_{g\in\operatorname{supp}h}|h(g)|^2\le C\int_{(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)^a}|p(e^{2\pi i\theta_1},\ldots,e^{2\pi i\theta_a})|^2\,d\theta.$$ For every smooth irreducible complex representation $\sigma$ with Satake multiset $\{z_1,\ldots,z_a\}$, $z_i\in\mathbb C^\times$, and every $v\in\sigma^K$, $$\sum_g h(g)\sigma(g)v=p(z_1,\ldots,z_a)v.$$ The torus integral uses normalized product Haar measure.

## Approximation by automorphic summands with a nontrivial local type

[Theorem AGR.1336](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.tyStep_holds/)

Let $E$ be a CM field, $\ell$ prime, and $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$ a field isomorphism. Fix rank $a\ge0$, the definite adelic unitary group $G=U(1_a)(\mathbb A_{E^+})$, a finite place $w_0$ split over $E^+$, a level subgroup $K$ away from $w_0$, and an algebraic weight representation $W$. Let $J\subseteq\operatorname{GL}_a(E_{w_0})$ be compact open and torsion free. Write $J^{\mathrm{loc}}\subseteq G$ for its local embedding. Let $m\ge0$ and let $\theta:J\to(\mathbb Z/\ell^m\mathbb Z,+)$ be a homomorphism.

For every algebraic automorphic form $f$ of weight $W$ fixed by the subgroup generated by $K$ and $J^{\mathrm{loc}}$, there is a form $F$ in the sum of the $K$-fixed weight-$W$ parts of automorphic summands having a nonzero vector $\phi$ with $$R(k)\phi=\phi\quad(k\in K),\qquad R(j)\phi=\chi(\theta(j))\phi\quad(j\in J^{\mathrm{loc}}),$$ for some nontrivial character $\chi:(\mathbb Z/\ell^m\mathbb Z,+)\to\mathbb C^{\times}$. There is a function $e:G\to\mathbb C$ such that $$|\iota^{-1}(e(g))|_\ell\le|\ell|_\ell^m,\qquad f(v,g)-F(v,g)=e(g)f(v,g)\quad(v\in W,\ g\in G).$$

## Finite quotients of open local matrix groups have open kernels

[Theorem REP.1616](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Gen.kerOpen_holds/)

Let $E$ be a number field, $w$ a finite place, $a\ge0$, and $J\subseteq\operatorname{GL}_a(E_w)$ an open subgroup. For any finite group $A$ and any group homomorphism $\theta:J\to A$, there is an open set $U\subseteq\operatorname{GL}_a(E_w)$ with $1\in U$ such that $$x\in J\cap U\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\theta(x)=1.$$
