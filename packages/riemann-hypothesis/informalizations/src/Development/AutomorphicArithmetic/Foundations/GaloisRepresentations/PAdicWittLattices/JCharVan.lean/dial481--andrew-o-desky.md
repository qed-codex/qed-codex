## Vanishing of integral etale cohomology at the characteristic prime

[Theorem AG.0281](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.G3.jCharVan/)

Let $\ell$ be prime, $k$ a field of characteristic $\ell$, and $X$ any scheme equipped with a morphism $X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$. For every $n,i\in\mathbb Z$, $$H^n_{\mathrm{et}}(X,\mathbb Z_\ell(i))=0.$$
