## A fixed-point trace formula for finite étale correspondences

[Definition / hypothesis AG.D044](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.LocalSmoothExtension.HolomorphicLefschetzFormula/)

The *fixed-point trace formula for finite étale correspondences* is the following assertion. It computes the alternating sum of the traces of a correspondence on the cohomology of the sheaves of differential forms of a smooth projective complex scheme as a sum of local terms at the fixed points of the correspondence.

Let $Z$ be a smooth projective scheme over $\mathbb C$ of pure dimension $d\ge0$, let $Y$ be a scheme over $\mathbb C$, and let $c_1,c_2\colon Y\to Z$ be finite étale morphisms of schemes over $\mathbb C$. Suppose that the set $$F=\{y\in Y(\mathbb C):c_1(y)=c_2(y)\}$$ is finite. For every $y\in F$, let $A_y\in M_d(\mathbb C)$ be a matrix with $\det(I_d-A_y)\ne0$ that satisfies the following coordinate condition at $y$.

*Coordinate condition at $y$.* There are affine open subsets $W\subseteq Z$ and $V\subseteq Y$ with $y\in V$, $c_1(V)\subseteq W$ and $c_2(V)\subseteq W$, regular functions $t_1,\ldots,t_d\in\mathcal O_Z(W)$, and a complex-linear map $$\phi\colon\Omega^1_{\mathcal O_Y(V)/\mathbb C}\longrightarrow\mathbb C^d$$ with the following four properties, in which $e_1,\ldots,e_d$ is the standard basis of $\mathbb C^d$, $h(y)$ is the value of a regular function $h$ at the complex point $y$, and $\mathrm dh$ is the Kähler differential of $h$.

1. $\phi(h\omega)=h(y)\phi(\omega)$ for all $h\in\mathcal O_Y(V)$ and $\omega\in\Omega^1_{\mathcal O_Y(V)/\mathbb C}$.
2. $\phi\bigl(\mathrm d(c_1^*t_l)\bigr)=e_l$ for $1\le l\le d$.
3. $\phi\bigl(\mathrm d(c_2^*t_k)\bigr)=\sum_{l=1}^d(A_y)_{kl}\,e_l$ for $1\le k\le d$.
4. Every $\omega$ with $\phi(\omega)=0$ is a finite sum $\sum_ih_i\omega_i$ with $h_i\in\mathcal O_Y(V)$, $h_i(y)=0$ and $\omega_i\in\Omega^1_{\mathcal O_Y(V)/\mathbb C}$.

By properties 1 and 4 the map $\phi$ induces an injective linear map from the cotangent space of $Y$ at $y$, the quotient of $\Omega^1_{\mathcal O_Y(V)/\mathbb C}$ by the submodule generated by the products $h\omega$ with $h(y)=0$, to $\mathbb C^d$, and by property 2 this map is bijective. Thus the differentials of the functions $c_1^*t_l$ form a basis of the cotangent space at $y$, and row $k$ of $A_y$ consists of the coordinates of the differential of $c_2^*t_k$ in this basis.

Then $H^q(Z,\Omega^p_{Z/\mathbb C})$ is a finite-dimensional complex vector space for all integers $p,q\ge0$, and for every integer $p\ge0$, $$\sum_{q=0}^{d}(-1)^q\operatorname{tr}\!\left(\operatorname{Tr}_{c_1}\circ c_2^*\mid H^q(Z,\Omega^p_{Z/\mathbb C})\right) =\sum_{y\in F}\frac{[T^p]\det(I_d+TA_y)}{\det(I_d-A_y)},$$ where $[T^p]$ denotes the coefficient of $T^p$ in a polynomial in the variable $T$.
