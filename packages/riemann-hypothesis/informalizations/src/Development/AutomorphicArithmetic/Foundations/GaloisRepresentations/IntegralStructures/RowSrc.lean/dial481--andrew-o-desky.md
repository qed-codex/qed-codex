## Galois representations of polarized cusp forms

[Theorem AGR.1.0011](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Int.galoisLocalGlobalStrong_recParameter/)

Let $E$ be a CM field with complex conjugation $c$ and maximal totally real subfield $E^+$, let $\overline E$ be an algebraic closure of $E$, and put $G_E=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline E/E)$. Let $a\ge1$ be an integer, $\ell$ a prime and $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\to\mathbb C$ an isomorphism of fields. Let $\pi$ be a cuspidal automorphic representation of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb A_E)$ that is regular algebraic, and let $\chi$ be a Hecke character of $E^+$ such that the pair $(\pi,\chi)$ is polarized.

Then there exist a continuous semisimple homomorphism $$\rho:G_E\longrightarrow\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell),$$ an integer $w$ and, for every embedding $\tau:E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, a multiset $J_\tau$ of integers, such that the following three assertions hold.

**1. Weights.** For every embedding $\tau:E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, $$J_\tau=\bigl\{\tfrac{a-1}2-z:\ z\in\operatorname{Inf}_{\iota\circ\tau}(\pi)\bigr\},\qquad J_{\tau\circ c}=\{w-h:\ h\in J_\tau\}.$$

**2. Finite places not above $\ell$.** Let $v$ be a finite place of $E$ not above $\ell$, and let $A$ be a valuation ring of $\overline E$ lying over $v$. Write $k=E_v$, let $q_v$ be the number of elements of the residue field of $v$, and let $W_k\supset I_k\supset P_k$ be the Weil group of $A$, its inertia subgroup and its wild inertia subgroup. Let $\operatorname{rec}_v$ be a map from the isomorphism classes of irreducible smooth complex representations of the groups $\mathrm{GL}_n(k)$, $n\ge0$, to the isomorphism classes of Weil–Deligne representations of $W_k$ which, together with some local Artin map for $(E,v,A)$, has properties 1 to 12 of ([Hypothesis: existence of a reciprocity map for GL\_n over the completions of number fields, with a restricted list of properties](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.RecTExists/)). Let $\pi_v$ be a local component of $\pi$ at $v$, and put $$x_v=\operatorname{WD}_{A,\iota}(\rho),\qquad y_v=\operatorname{rec}_v\bigl(\pi_v\otimes|\det|^{(1-a)/2}\bigr).$$ Then the following three comparisons hold.

- *Comparison 1.* $\dim x_v=\dim y_v$.
- *Comparison 2.* If $N_{y_v}=0$ and $R_{y_v}(ghg^{-1}h^{-1})=1$ for all $g,h\in W_k$, then $x_v=y_v$.
- *Comparison 3.* Suppose that there is a continuous isomorphism of fields $\mathbb Q_p\to k$ for a prime $p$, and that $a=1$ or $p>16a^2$. Suppose that $N_{y_v}=0$ and that $R_{y_v}$ is trivial on $P_k$. Suppose also that, for every $\gamma\in I_k$ such that the subgroup generated by $\gamma$ and $P_k$ is dense in $I_k$, every root of $\det(T-R_{y_v}(\gamma))$ of multiplicity at least two is a $(q_v-1)$-th root of unity. Then $N_{x_v}=0$ and $$\det(T-R_{x_v}(g))=\det(T-R_{y_v}(g))\qquad(g\in I_k).$$

**3. Finite places above $\ell$.** Let $v$ be a finite place of $E$ above $\ell$, let $A$ be a valuation ring of $\overline E$ lying over $v$, and let $D_A\subset G_E$ be its decomposition group; define $k$, $q_v$ and $W_k\supset I_k\supset P_k$ as in assertion 2. Let local period data for $\ell$ be given, let $O$ be a commutative $\mathbb Z_\ell$-algebra equipped with a topology, and let $(K,L,j,e,o)$ be a coefficient comparison for $(E,\overline E,D_A,O)$ that induces $A$, all in the sense of ([Local period rings and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)). Let $\pi_v$ be a local component of $\pi$ at $v$. With respect to these data:

- (a) If $\pi_v$ has a nonzero vector fixed by $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathcal O_k)$, where $\mathcal O_k$ is the valuation ring of $k$, then $\rho|_{D_A}$ is crystalline and de Rham, and it has Hodge–Tate weights $J_\tau$ at every embedding $\tau:E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ of the form $\tau=\tau'\circ j|_E$ with $\tau':K\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ a $\mathbb Q_\ell$-embedding.
- (b) Let $\operatorname{rec}_v$ be a map as in assertion 2 and put $y_v=\operatorname{rec}_v\bigl(\pi_v\otimes|\det|^{(1-a)/2}\bigr)$. If the hypotheses of comparison 2 or the hypotheses of comparison 3 hold for $k$, $a$ and $y_v$, then $\rho|_{D_A}$ is de Rham and potentially semistable, it has Hodge–Tate weights $J_\tau$ at every embedding $\tau$ as in (a), and comparisons 1, 2 and 3 hold for $y_v$ and $x_v=\operatorname{WD}_{v,\iota}(\rho|_{D_A})$.
