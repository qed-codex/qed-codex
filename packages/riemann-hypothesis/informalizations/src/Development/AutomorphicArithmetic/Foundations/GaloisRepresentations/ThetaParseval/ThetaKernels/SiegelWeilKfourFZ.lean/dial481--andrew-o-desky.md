## Vanishing of all Siegel Fourier coefficients forces vanishing

[Theorem THETA.1193](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.SiegelWeilFormula.kfour.fourierZero_k/)

Fix the source's anisotropic Siegel–Weil datum $\mathcal D$ over number fields $E/F$, with involution $\sigma$, doubled isometry group $G$, additive character $\psi$, and Siegel frame $b$. In these coordinates let $N$ be the closed additive subgroup of matrices parametrizing the Siegel unipotent radical, and let $N_F$ be the closed subgroup generated by its rational matrices. Choose a parametrization $n:N\to G$ whose framed matrix is $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&\beta\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ at $\beta\in N$, and additive Haar measure $\lambda$ with a fundamental domain $\mathcal F$ for $N_F$ of volume one. For continuous $f:G\to\mathbb C$, define $$c_T(f;g)=\int_{\mathcal F}f(n(\beta)g)\psi\!\left(-\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb A_E/\mathbb A_F}\operatorname{tr}(T\beta)\right)\,d\lambda(\beta)\qquad(T\in N_F).$$ If $f(n(\gamma)g)=f(g)$ for every $\gamma\in N_F$, and $c_T(f;g)=0$ for every $T\in N_F$ and $g\in G$, then $f=0$.
