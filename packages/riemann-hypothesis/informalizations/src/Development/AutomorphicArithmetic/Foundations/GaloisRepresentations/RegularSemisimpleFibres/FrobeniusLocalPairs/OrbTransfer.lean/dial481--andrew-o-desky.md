## Transfer of a twisted orbital sum along a closed embedding

[Theorem REP.1150](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.InertialTypesAlt.L.orbTransfer/)

Let $G,H$ be Hausdorff topological groups and $j:H\to G$ a closed embedding that is a group homomorphism. Let $\theta:G\to G$ be a group homomorphism, $K\subseteq G$ and $K_0\subseteq H$ subgroups with $j^{-1}(K)=K_0$, and let $\delta\in G$, $\gamma\in H$. Put $T_{\theta,\delta}=\{x:x\delta=\delta\theta(x)\}$ and assume $$T_{\theta,\delta}=j(Z_H(\gamma)),\qquad \theta(jh)=jh\quad(h\in H).$$ Let $\varphi:G\to\mathbb C$ and $f:H\to\mathbb C$ satisfy $$\varphi(\kappa^{-1}y\theta(\kappa))=\varphi(y)\quad(\kappa\in K,\ y\in G),$$ $$\varphi(x^{-1}\delta\theta(x))\ne0\ \Longrightarrow\ x\in j(H)K,\qquad \varphi((jh)^{-1}\delta\theta(jh))=f(h^{-1}\gamma h).$$ For any topological group $A$, subgroup $L$, homomorphism $\alpha:A\to A$, element $a\in A$ and function $u:A\to\mathbb C$, define $T=T_{\alpha,a}$ and let $T_c$ be the subgroup generated by those $t\in T$ for which $\overline{\langle t\rangle}$ is compact. Define $$O_{\alpha,L}(a,u)=\sum_{x\in T\backslash A/L}^{\mathrm{fin}}[T_c:T_c\cap xLx^{-1}]\operatorname{av}_{k\in L}u((xk)^{-1}a\alpha(xk)).$$ Here the average of a function $v$ on $L$ is $[L:R_v]^{-1}\sum_{L/R_v}^{\mathrm{fin}}v$, where $R_v=\{k:v(yk)=v(y)\ \forall y\}$; infinite indices have natural-number value zero, division by zero is zero, and $\sum^{\mathrm{fin}}$ is zero for infinite support. Then $$O_{\theta,K}(\delta,\varphi)=O_{1,K_0}(\gamma,f).$$
