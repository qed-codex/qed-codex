**Why a limiting trace comes from a representation.** The reconstruction lemma assumes a compact topological group, a dense set of evaluations, trace values in a finite extension of the $ℓ$-adic field, and polynomial approximation by traces of continuous rank-$a$ representations. For each tolerance, one finite weighted combination of evaluations on the approximating systems must work for *every* multivariate polynomial with integer coefficients. The lemma extends the trace continuously, proves that it is a pseudocharacter, realizes it by a semisimple representation, and derives continuity of that representation from continuity of the trace.
