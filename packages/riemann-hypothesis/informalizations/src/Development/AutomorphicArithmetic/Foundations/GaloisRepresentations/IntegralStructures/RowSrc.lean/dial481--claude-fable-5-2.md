`AwayB x y` has three clauses.

1. `dim`: $x$ and $y$ have the same dimension.
2. `tor`: suppose every commutator of two elements of the Weil group acts on $y$ with characteristic polynomial $(X-1)^{\dim(y)}$, and $y$ has zero monodromy. Then $x = y$. [Tor](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjAlt.lean#L27) · [Triv](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/WeilBound/CoreStatements/AutomorphicKernels/LocalGlobalCompatibility/TemperedLocalGlobal/LocalGlobalOfIwahori.lean#L21)
3. `tame0`: suppose four things. The completion $E_w$ is $\mathbb{Q}_g$ for a prime $g$, with $\dim(y) = 1$ or $g > 16 \dim(y)^2$ (`IsQpBig`). The elements $\sigma$ of the Galois group with $|\sigma z / z - 1| < 1$ for every nonzero $z$, which form the wild inertia group at $A$, act on $y$ with characteristic polynomial $(X-1)^{\dim(y)}$ (`TmW`). The class $y$ satisfies `Sqf`: for every $\gamma$ in inertia that topologically generates inertia together with wild inertia, each repeated root $z$ of the characteristic polynomial of $\gamma$ on $y$ satisfies $z^{q-1} = 1$, where $q$ is the size of the residue field at $w$. And $y$ has zero monodromy. Then $x$ has zero monodromy, and $x$ and $y$ have the same characteristic polynomial at every element of the Weil group of degree $0$. [IsQpBig](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean#L15-L16) · [TmW](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConj.lean#L18-L19) · [Sqf](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjAlt.lean#L29-L33)

## Conditions above $l$

A bridge embeds $\Omega$ in an algebraic closure of $\mathbb{Q}_l$ and identifies the decomposition group at $A$ with the Galois group of that closure over a finite extension $K_w$ of $\mathbb{Q}_l$, in which the image of $E$ is dense. ## The reciprocity notion

A reciprocity notion is a predicate on maps `rc` as above, one for each place. `recT` is the predicate `IsRecT`: there is a local Artin map `a` for which `rc` obeys the twelve laws of `LawsT`. [RecNotion](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjAlt.lean#L16-L18) · [recT](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/Development.lean#L18) · [IsRecT](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/IsRecTOfIsRec.lean#L76)

The twelve laws are properties of a local Langlands correspondence. [LawsT, lines 55–72](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/IsRecTOfIsRec.lean#L55-L72)

- For every representation: its dimension (`dim`); the value on characters, through `a` (`one`); the Langlands classification by segments (`langlands`); cuspidal representations go to irreducible classes (`cusp`); twists by powers of the absolute value (`nu`); and the absolute value of the central character (`absCentral`).
- Only for representations in the class `Good`: twists by characters (`twist`), the central character itself (`central`), duals (`dual`), and that a representation with nonzero vectors fixed by the subgroup `KOne` goes to a tame class (`depth`). [Good](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/IsRecTOfIsRec.lean#L20-L24)
- Injectivity at `Good` representations (`inj`): if `rc` takes the same value at $x$ and at a `Good` $y$, then $x = y$. Here $x$ is arbitrary, so nothing outside the class shares a value with a member of it.
- `type`, a law on the characteristic polynomials of inertia for representations built from cuspidal characters of finite general linear groups (`TypeLaw`). [TypeLaw](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/IsRecTOfIsRec.lean#L35-L53)

The statement quantifies over every map with these properties. It does not single out one correspondence.
