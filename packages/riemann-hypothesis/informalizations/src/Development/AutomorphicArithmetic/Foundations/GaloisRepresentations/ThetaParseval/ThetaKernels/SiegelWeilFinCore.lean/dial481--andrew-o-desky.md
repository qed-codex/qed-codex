## Fourier coefficients of a definite theta integral

[Theorem THETA.1148](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.SiegelWeilFormula.Fin.thetaCoef_k/)

Let $F,E$ be number fields with an $F$-algebra structure on $E$ and $\sigma\in\operatorname{Aut}_F(E)$. Choose a doubled Weil-model datum in the source's sense, as specified in the conventions below. Its two spaces have ranks $m,n\ge1$, its first unitary group is $U(A)$, its doubled second group is $G$, its commuting Weil actions are $S$ and $\omega$, its additive character is $\psi$, and its Darboux identification and evaluation functional give a Schrödinger transform $\mathcal T$ on Schwartz functions. Let $D\subset U(A)(\mathbb A_F)$ be the datum's rational fundamental domain with Haar measure $\mu$. Write $$I_\Phi(x)=\frac{1}{\mu(D)}\int_D\sum_{u\in F^{\kappa}}(\omega(x)S(g)\Phi)(u)\,d\mu(g).$$ The finite set $\kappa$ indexes the chosen Lagrangian coordinates. In the datum's doubled frame, let $N$ be the additive group of upper unipotent matrix coordinates and $N_F$ the subgroup generated by its rational matrices. Choose $\beta\mapsto n(\beta)\in G$ with frame matrix $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&\beta\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, an additive Haar measure $\lambda$ on $N$, and a fundamental domain $\mathcal F$ for $N_F$ with $\lambda(\mathcal F)=1$. For every Schwartz function $\Phi$, every $T\in N_F$, and every $x\in G$, $$\int_{\mathcal F}I_\Phi(n(\beta)x)\psi(-\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb A_E/\mathbb A_F}\operatorname{tr}(T\beta))\,d\lambda(\beta) =\frac{1}{\mu(D)}\int_D\sum_{\substack{X\in M_{m,n}(E)\\X^tJ_A\sigma(X)=-2T}}\mathcal T(\omega(x)\Phi)(g^{-1}X)\,d\mu(g).$$ Here $J_A$ is the first space's matrix and all rational matrices are embedded adelically.
