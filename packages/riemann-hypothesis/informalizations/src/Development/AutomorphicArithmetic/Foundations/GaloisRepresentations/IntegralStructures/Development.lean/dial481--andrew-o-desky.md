## Weak attachment and spherical comparison for polarized cusp forms

[Theorem AGR.0163](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Int.coreAll_of/)

Let $E$ be a CM field, $E^+$ its maximal totally real subfield, $a\ge1$, $\ell$ prime, and $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C$. Let $\pi$ be a regular algebraic cuspidal representation of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb A_E)$ polarized by a Hecke character of $E^+$.

There is a continuous semisimple representation $r:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ weakly attached to $\pi$. Here weak attachment means that, for all but finitely many finite places $v$, the local component has a Satake multiset $B_v$, the representation $r$ is unramified at $v$, and $$\iota\bigl(\det(T-r(\operatorname{Frob}_v^{-1}))\bigr)=\prod_{\beta\in B_v}(T-q_v^{(a-1)/2}\beta),$$ where $q_v$ is the residue-field cardinality and $\operatorname{Frob}_v$ is arithmetic Frobenius.

Moreover, every continuous semisimple representation weakly attached in this sense has the following properties at every place $v$ where $\pi_v$ is spherical. If $v\nmid\ell$, it is unramified and satisfies the displayed polynomial identity. If $v\mid\ell$, it is crystalline and de Rham, and its jumps at every induced embedding $\tau:E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ are any integer multiset $J$ satisfying $$J=\{(a-1)/2-z:z\in\operatorname{Inf}_{\iota\tau}(\pi)\}$$ after embedding the integers in $\mathbb C$. Its associated Weil–Deligne parameter has the same characteristic polynomial on every Weil element as the expected unramified parameter of the algebraically normalized $\pi_v$.

## Galois representations of polarized cusp forms, given Galois representations for definite unitary groups and automorphic representations in large dimensions

[Theorem AGR.1.1762](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Int.galoisLocalGlobal_of_geometry_lift/)

Assume the following two hypotheses.

- **Hypothesis G.** The statement of ([Galois representations with local conditions, given a fixed-point trace formula](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Int.Geo.geometric_realization_of_weight_one/)) holds without its assumption of ([A fixed-point trace formula for finite étale correspondences](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.LocalSmoothExtension.HolomorphicLefschetzFormula/)). That is, for all $E$, $\bar E$, $\ell$, $\iota$, $a$, $d$, $x$, $U$, $w_0$ and $v'$ that satisfy the conditions of that theorem, including its conditions (a) to (d), its assertions 1, 2 and 3 hold.
- **Hypothesis T.** ([Hypothesis: automorphic representations in large dimensions with prescribed weights and split local pairings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.TP.TLiftK/)).

Let $E$ be a CM field with complex conjugation $c$ and maximal totally real subfield $E^+$, let $\overline E$ be an algebraic closure of $E$, and put $G_E=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline E/E)$. Let $a\ge1$ be an integer, $\ell$ a prime and $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\to\mathbb C$ an isomorphism of fields. Let $\pi$ be a cuspidal automorphic representation of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb A_E)$ that is regular algebraic, and let $\chi$ be a Hecke character of $E^+$ such that the pair $(\pi,\chi)$ is polarized.

Then there exist a continuous semisimple homomorphism $$\rho:G_E\longrightarrow\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell),$$ an integer $w$ and, for every embedding $\tau:E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, a multiset $J_\tau$ of integers, for which assertion 1 (weights), assertion 2 (finite places not above $\ell$) and assertion 3 (finite places above $\ell$) of ([Galois representations of polarized cusp forms](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Gal.Int.galoisLocalGlobalStrong_recParameter/)) hold with comparison 3 replaced by the following comparison 3′. The replacement is made in assertion 2 and in part (b) of assertion 3, both in the hypotheses and in the conclusion of that part.

*Comparison 3′.* Suppose that there is a continuous isomorphism of fields $\mathbb Q_p\to k$ for a prime $p$. Suppose that $N_{y_v}=0$ and that $R_{y_v}$ is trivial on $P_k$. Suppose also that, for every $\gamma\in I_k$ such that the subgroup generated by $\gamma$ and $P_k$ is dense in $I_k$, every root of $\det(T-R_{y_v}(\gamma))$ of multiplicity at least two is a $(q_v-1)$-th root of unity. Then $N_{x_v}=0$ and $$\det(T-R_{x_v}(g))=\det(T-R_{y_v}(g))\qquad(g\in I_k).$$
