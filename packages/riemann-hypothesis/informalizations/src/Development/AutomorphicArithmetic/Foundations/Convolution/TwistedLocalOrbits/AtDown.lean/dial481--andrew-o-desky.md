## Transfer near the identity at a nonsplit cyclic place

[Theorem ANT.0388](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.ConvolutionTheory.Loc.atDown/)

Let $E/F$ be a finite Galois extension of number fields whose Galois group is generated by $\sigma$. Let $v$ be a finite place of $F$ with exactly one place $w$ of $E$ above it, put $k=F_v$, $l=E_w$, and let $m=[E:F]$. For every $n\ge0$ there is a positive value-group radius $\rho$ such that every locally constant compactly supported $\phi:\mathrm{GL}_n(l)\to\mathbb C$ with $$\operatorname{supp}\phi\subseteq\{g:\operatorname{val}_l(g_{ij}-\delta_{ij})\le\rho\text{ for all }i,j\}$$ has a locally constant compactly supported transfer $f:\mathrm{GL}_n(k)\to\mathbb C$. Precisely, for every $\gamma\in\mathrm{GL}_n(k)$ with separable characteristic polynomial, every Haar measure $\nu$ on its centralizer, and every $\delta\in\mathrm{GL}_n(l)$ with $$\delta\sigma(\delta)\cdots\sigma^{m-1}(\delta)=\gamma,$$ the $\sigma$-twisted orbital integral of $\phi$ at $\delta$, with centralizer embedded from $k$, equals the ordinary orbital integral of $f$ at $\gamma$. If there is no such $\delta$, the latter integral is zero.
