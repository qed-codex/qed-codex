## Cyclic base change transfer near the identity at a nonsplit place

[Theorem REP.1098](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.ConvolutionTheory.Loc.placeUp/)

Let $E/F$ be a finite Galois extension of number fields with cyclic Galois group generated by $\sigma$. Let $v$ be a finite place of $F$ with exactly one place $w$ of $E$ above it. Fix $n\ge0$ and Haar measures $\mu_E,\mu_F$ on $\operatorname{GL}_n(E_w)$ and $\operatorname{GL}_n(F_v)$, with their Borel sigma-algebras. Put $d=[E:F]$ and $$N_\sigma(\delta)=\delta\,\sigma(\delta)\cdots\sigma^{d-1}(\delta).$$ There exists $\rho>0$ in the value group of $F_v$, with zero adjoined, such that every locally constant compactly supported $f:\operatorname{GL}_n(F_v)\to\mathbb C$ supported in $$\{g:|g_{ij}-\delta_{ij}|_v\le\rho\text{ for every }i,j\}$$ has a locally constant compactly supported transfer $\varphi:\operatorname{GL}_n(E_w)\to\mathbb C$ satisfying the following conditions. For every $\gamma\in\operatorname{GL}_n(F_v)$ with separable characteristic polynomial, every Haar measure $\nu$ on its centralizer $T_\gamma$, and every $\delta$ with $N_\sigma(\delta)=\gamma$, $$O_{\sigma,\delta}(\varphi;\mu_E,\nu)=O_\gamma(f;\mu_F,\nu).$$ If $\gamma$ is not such a twisted norm, then $O_\gamma(f;\mu_F,\nu)=0$.
