## A finite spectral expansion for the unitary trace identity

[Theorem AGR.0074](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.BaseChangeIdentities.uSide/)

Let $E$ be a CM number field, $F=E^+$, and assume $E/F$ is unramified at every finite place. Let $n\ge1$ and choose a set $S$ containing exactly one place of $E$ above each finite place of $F$. Let $U_w=\operatorname{GL}_n(E_w)$ at a split place, and otherwise let $U_w$ be the unitary group of the identity Hermitian form. Let $K_w$ be its standard integral compact subgroup. Choose open subgroups $K'_w\subseteq K_w$, equal to $K_w$ at all but finitely many places, and dominant integral weights $\lambda_v$ for all infinite places of $E$.

There exist a finite index set $I$, automorphic representations $\pi_i$ of the definite unitary group of the identity Hermitian form on $E^n$, and smooth irreducible representations $\sigma_{i,w}$ of $U_w$, such that each $\pi_i$ has all the weights $\lambda_v$ and a nonzero vector fixed by the specified local levels at every $w\in S$. For $w\in S$, $\sigma_{i,w}$ is a local component of $\pi_i$, has a nonzero $K'_w$-fixed vector, and its fixed vectors under every open subgroup are finite-dimensional. Every automorphic representation with these weights and a nonzero vector fixed at the specified levels has, at every $w\in S$, the same local components as some $\pi_i$.

For compactly supported bi-$K'_w$-invariant functions $f_w:U_w\to\mathbb C$, equal to $1_{K_w}$ at almost every place, assume some $w\in S$ has the property that every matrix in the nonzero support of $f_w$ has separable characteristic polynomial. Define $$T_{\sigma,w}(f_w)=\operatorname{tr}\left(\frac{1}{[K_w:K'_w]}\sum_{g\in U_w/K'_w}f_w(g)\sigma(g)\ \Bigm|\ \sigma^{K'_w}\right).$$ Then $$\sum_{i\in I}\prod_{w\in S}^{\mathrm{fin}}T_{\sigma_{i,w},w}(f_w) =\sum_{a=0}^n\ \sum_{\substack{P_1\in\mathcal E_a,\ P_2\in\mathcal E_{n-a}\\(P_1,P_2)=1}}^{\mathrm{fin}} \kappa(P_1P_2)\,c_\lambda(P_1P_2)\prod_{w\in S}^{\mathrm{fin}}O_w(P_1,P_2;f_w).$$ Here $\mathcal E_a$ consists of the monic separable degree-$a$ polynomials over $E$ whose roots have absolute value one at every complex embedding. The factor $c_\lambda(P)$ is the product, over infinite places, of the complex conjugates of the algebraic characters of highest weights $\lambda_v$ evaluated on a matrix with characteristic polynomial $P$ at that embedding.

The remaining factors use the cardinality normalization specified below. For $P$ of degree $n$, $\kappa(P)=0$ unless $P$ is the characteristic polynomial of $\delta\vartheta(\delta)$ for a rational $\delta\in\operatorname{GL}_n(E)$, where $\vartheta$ is conjugate inverse transpose followed by conjugation by the matrix $J$ with $J_{ij}=(-1)^i$ when $i+j=n-1$ and $J_{ij}=0$ otherwise, for $0\le i,j<n$. Otherwise, $\kappa(P)$ is the twisted-centralizer mass of the chosen $\delta$ divided by $2^{\omega(P)}$, where $\omega(P)$ is the number of irreducible factors. If $T$ is this centralizer in the finite adelic general linear group, $T_c$ is its subgroup generated by elements with compact cyclic closure, and $\Gamma$ is the rational general linear subgroup, this mass is $$\frac{\operatorname{card}_{\mathbb N}((\Gamma\cap T)\backslash T/T_c)}{\operatorname{card}_{\mathbb N}(\Gamma\cap T_c)}.$$

The term $O_w(P_1,P_2;f_w)$ sums orbital sums over conjugacy classes of matrices $\gamma\in U_w$ with characteristic polynomial $P_1P_2$. Its sign is $1$ at split places and otherwise $(-1)^{\operatorname{ord}_w\det H_1}$, with $H_1$ the standard Hermitian Gram matrix on $\ker P_1(\gamma)$. For the centralizer $T$ of $\gamma$, its orbital sum is the sum over $x\in T\backslash U_w/K_w$ of the normalized average of $f_w((xk)^{-1}\gamma(xk))$ over $k\in K_w$, weighted by $$[T_c:T_c\cap xK_wx^{-1}].$$ Normalized averaging means averaging over the quotient by the right stabilizer of the function. All cardinalities and indices are natural numbers, with zero assigned to infinite quotients, and division by zero gives zero; superscript $\mathrm{fin}$ denotes a finitely supported sum or product, with value zero or one respectively if the required support is infinite.
