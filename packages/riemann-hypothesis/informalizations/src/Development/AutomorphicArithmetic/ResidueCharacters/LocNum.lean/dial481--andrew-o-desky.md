## A controlled local extension containing its cyclotomic part

[Theorem PHT.1206](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.N.LocNum.exists_up/)

Let $\ell$ be prime, let $N>0$, and let $K,L\subset\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ be finite extensions of $\mathbb Q_\ell$. Assume $4\mid N$ if $\ell=2$, that $K$ lies in the field generated by the $\operatorname{lcm}(\ell,N)$th roots of unity, and that $L$ contains all those roots. Put $s=\max\{1,v_\ell(N)\}$ and let $\mathbb Q_\ell^{\mathrm{nr}}$ be the maximal unramified extension in the fixed closure.

There is a finite extension $K'\supset K$ such that $K'/\mathbb Q_\ell$ is Galois, $s\ge1$ and $s\ge2$ if $\ell=2$, and $$K'=(K'\cap\mathbb Q_\ell^{\mathrm{nr}})(\mu_{\ell^s}),\qquad v_\ell([K':K])\le\lfloor\log_\ell N\rfloor+1.$$ Every $\mathbb Q_\ell$-embedding of $(K'\cap\mathbb Q_\ell^{\mathrm{nr}})(\mu_{\ell^s})$ into the fixed closure has image in $L$.
