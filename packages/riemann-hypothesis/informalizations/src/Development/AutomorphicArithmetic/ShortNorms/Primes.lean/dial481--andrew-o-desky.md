## A polynomial bound for a prime congruent to one

[Theorem ANT.1589](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.T3.smallOne/)

There is an absolute real constant $A\ge1$ such that for every integer $M\ge2$ there is a prime $p$ with $$p\equiv1\pmod M,\qquad p\le M^A.$$
