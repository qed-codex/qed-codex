## Fourteen statements from the literature on representations, automorphic forms and theta series, taken as hypotheses

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D035](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.PrimIn.PublishedThetaConditionTheorems/)

The following fourteen statements are assumed together. Each is stated in the formalization's own definitions of the notions involved (automorphic function, cusp form, Harish-Chandra module, Weil representation and so on); those definitions have not been expanded here, so each item says what its statement asserts in terms of them. $D$ denotes a datum (see [Datum of the construction and objects over it](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.EX.OB.Obj/)), with CM field $E$ over $F$; $\mathrm{U}(V)(\mathbb{A})$ denotes the adelic unitary group of a Hermitian space $V$ over $E$.

1. **Unramified Howe duality.** Let $L$ be a complete discretely valued field of characteristic $0$ and $\mathrm{U}(J_S)$, $\mathrm{U}(J_T)$ the unitary groups of two invertible matrices of sizes $n$ and $n'$. Let $M$ be a complex vector space with operators $\rho(x)$, $x\in M_{n'\times n}(L)$, and a homomorphism $\omega\colon \mathrm{U}(J_S)\times\mathrm{U}(J_T)\to\mathrm{End}(M)$ forming a local pair in the formalization's sense (relative to a uniformiser, an endomorphism of $L$ and an additive character), and let $s\ne0$ be a vacuum vector whose images $\rho(x)s$ span $M$. Then every vector fixed by the integral points of $\mathrm{U}(J_S)$ lies in the span of $\omega(1,h)s$, $h\in \mathrm{U}(J_T)$, and symmetrically with the two groups exchanged.
2. **Rapid decay of cusp forms.** A function on $\mathrm{U}(V)(\mathbb{A})$ that is automorphic, smooth at the infinite places, of uniformly moderate growth and cuspidal is rapidly decreasing.
3. **Finiteness of spaces of automorphic forms.** For an open subgroup $U$ of the finite-adelic unitary group of $V$, finite-dimensional continuous representations of the maximal compact subgroups at the infinite places and prescribed scalars $c_{\tau,n}$, the automorphic forms of level $U$ with these data span a finite-dimensional space.
4. **Harish-Chandra modules of $\mathrm{U}(p,q)$.** For $p+q\ge1$: in an irreducible unitary representation the $K$-finite vectors are smooth and form an admissible irreducible $(\mathfrak{g},K)$-module; two irreducible unitary representations with isomorphic $(\mathfrak{g},K)$-modules are unitarily equivalent; a unitary representation whose $(\mathfrak{g},K)$-module is admissible and irreducible is irreducible. In addition, for an irreducible unitary representation $\sigma$ and a $K$-finite vector $v$, the function $g\mapsto\sigma(g)v$ is analytic.
5. **Exhaustion of the discrete series of $\mathrm{U}(p,q)$.** For $p+q\ge1$ there is a family of discrete series representations indexed by the rational parameters $\lambda$ satisfying a stated condition, each with prescribed $K$-types, such that every discrete series representation is unitarily equivalent to a member of the family.
6. **Extension of $(\mathfrak{g},K)$-maps (Casselman–Wallach).** Let $\sigma$ be an irreducible unitary representation of $\mathrm{U}(p,q)$ on a Hilbert space $H$ and $\pi$ a smooth representation of moderate growth on a complete Hausdorff space $E$ with a countable family of seminorms. Every $(\mathfrak{g},K)$-map from the $K$-finite vectors of $H$ to $E$ extends to a linear map $T$ on $H$ that is a continuous intertwining map on smooth vectors; $T$ is unique on smooth vectors, the image of the smooth vectors is closed, and if $T\ne0$ on $K$-finite vectors, every Sobolev norm of a smooth vector $w$ is bounded by a continuous seminorm of $Tw$.
7. **Convergence of theta integrals and the Siegel–Weil formula in the convergent range (Weil).** Let $A,B$ be Hermitian spaces over $E$ with $\operatorname{rank}A\ge1$ such that $B$ is anisotropic or $\operatorname{rank}A+\operatorname{Witt index}(B)<\operatorname{rank}B$. Then the integral of the theta kernel over $\mathrm{U}(B)(F)\backslash\mathrm{U}(B)(\mathbb{A})$ converges absolutely, with a bound by a continuous seminorm of the Schwartz function; and if $2\operatorname{rank}A<\operatorname{rank}B$ the Siegel Eisenstein series on the unitary group of the doubled space of $A$ attached to the Schwartz function converges and equals the average of the theta kernel.
8. **Siegel–Weil formula for $\operatorname{rank}A<\operatorname{rank}B\le2\operatorname{rank}A$.** Under $\operatorname{rank}A+\operatorname{Witt index}(B)<\operatorname{rank}B$, for a Schwartz function that is a Fock vector at the infinite places for compatible Cartan involutions, the theta kernel is integrable and the value at the point $s_0(A,B)$ of the Siegel Eisenstein series attached to it equals the average of the theta kernel.
9. **Doubling method.** For a Hermitian space $V$ of positive rank, a Hecke character $\mu$ of $E$ satisfying a restriction condition with some exponent $b$, and a section $\varphi$ of the degenerate principal series: the Siegel Eisenstein series of $\varphi$ continues in $s$ with poles of finite order; and for two cusp forms $f_1,f_2$ on $\mathrm{U}(V)$ the basic identity of the doubling method holds for $\operatorname{Re}s>\frac12\operatorname{rank}V$ and the global zeta integral continues. The statement consists of three clauses, of which the middle one is the trivial proposition.
10. **Unramified doubling integral for $\mathrm{GL}_n$.** For a non-archimedean local field $k$ of characteristic $0$ with residue field of $q$ elements, Haar measure giving $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O}_k)$ volume $1$, a representation with Satake parameters $\alpha$ and a zonal spherical function $\omega$ of it: for $\operatorname{Re}s$ large, the integral over $\mathrm{GL}_n(k)$ of the spherical section at $(g,1)$ times $\omega(g)$ converges and equals $L(s+\frac12,\alpha)\,L(s+\frac12,\alpha^{-1})$ times an explicit normalising factor depending on $q$, $n$, $s$.
11. **Smooth representations of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ over a local field (Bernstein–Zelevinsky).** Irreducible smooth representations are admissible; Jacquet functors and parabolic induction preserve finite length; the contragredient of a parabolically induced representation is isomorphic to the induction of the contragredient, through integration over the maximal compact subgroup.
12. **Cuspidal characters of $\mathrm{GL}_d(\mathbb{F}_g)$ (Green).** For a prime $g$, a labelling datum and $d>0$ there is an injective map from the prime labels of degree $d$ (certain polynomials over $\mathbb{F}_g$) onto the cuspidal characters, and each of these characters has degree $\prod_{i=1}^{d-1}(g^i-1)$.
13. **Existence of the Weil representation of a unitary pair.** For Hermitian spaces $S,T$ over $E$, a standard additive character and Hecke characters $\chi_S,\chi_T$ of $E$ satisfying the same restriction condition with exponents $\operatorname{rank}S$ and $\operatorname{rank}T$, there is a Weil representation of $\mathrm{U}(S)(\mathbb{A})\times\mathrm{U}(T)(\mathbb{A})$ on functions on $n_T\times n_S$ matrices with these splitting characters.
14. Assume ([Schur orthogonality for square-integrable representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Published.schurOrthogonalitySquareIntegrable'/)).
