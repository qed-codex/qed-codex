## Conditions on a family and its realisation for the period identity

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D029](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fam.AllFamilyConditions/)

These conditions concern a datum as in ([Datum of the construction and objects over it](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.EX.OB.Obj/)), together with a family indexed by tuples, spectral data, and a realisation of the family. The data specifying the family, the spectral data and the realisation are not expanded.

The conditions have six groups. The following descriptions do not specify every condition in those groups.

1. The first group includes the normalizations for the given realisation in ([Compatibility of recursive coordinate realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.IndefiniteRealisation/)), further conditions on the realisation at the base level and on its level structure (not expanded), an existence condition (not expanded), and an equality between the range of a linear map and a space of forms (the map and space are not expanded). The tuple index set is finite. If two primes of $E$ occur in one of the sets attached to tuples and have the same prime below in $F$, they are equal. The group includes a coprimality condition at primes in these sets, in another distinguished set, or containing one of the numbers attached to words (not expanded). It also requires the weight gap in item 9 of ([Conditions on linked records for the period identity](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fam.BundlePointConditions/)).
2. The second group includes conditions on the base and on the existence of compatible auxiliary data, equalities of characters, and a leading-coefficient condition. The definitions of these conditions and characters are not expanded. The leading-coefficient condition has the form: there is a group element such that, for every word, a linear map exists with specified level and coefficient identities.
3. For each tuple, a character is a Hecke character and its local values agree with those of the character attached to the corresponding tuple in the base data; the associated sets of primes agree. For each word there is a linear map satisfying the leading-coefficient condition with the identity as the group element. The characters, prime sets and leading-coefficient condition are not expanded.
4. There exist auxiliary data for which the twisted columns agree with the given ones and satisfy a standardness condition. The auxiliary data and the condition are not expanded.
5. A distinguished finite set $P$ of primes of $E$ equals a set specified by the base data; for every tuple two associated characters are equal. Moreover every prime in $P$ divides $g_0g_1$, and for each prime of $F$ dividing $g_0g_1$ exactly one prime in $P$ lies above it. The two characters and the set specified by the base data are not expanded.
6. For the same given realisation, there is a choice of the auxiliary data satisfying the recursive identifications in ([Compatibility of recursive coordinate realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.IndefiniteRealisation/)).

In group 1, the base-level conditions include the requirement that every tuple in the family is either the base tuple or the tuple attached to some finite place. They also include a spectral gap: at each place, two entries with different Boolean labels differ by at least $100r^4$ in absolute value, as in item 8 of ([Conditions on linked records for the period identity](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fam.BundlePointConditions/)). The remaining base-level conditions are not expanded.

## Compatibility of recursive coordinate realizations

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D203](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.IndefiniteRealisation/)

Use ([Linked arithmetic and theta realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.TiedDataSKAlt/)), ([Spectral column and period realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.HidaSetting.HidaSetting/)), ([Row-side families of Hermitian spaces](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Rows.RowSideFamily/)), ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)), ([Hecke characters in recursive realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Host.CharFrame/)) and ([Compatible points for arithmetic covolume bounds](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.BigTop.PtAt/)). All equalities of data below include their spaces, maps, subspaces and indexed functions, after the specified identifications of their domains.

Hypothesis (recursive arithmetic comparisons). Choose the same linked arithmetic, column, kernel and theta realization of ([Linked arithmetic and theta realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.TiedDataSKAlt/)). Require ([Coordinate contractions of lower period maps](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Rows.RowPinMinAlt/)), ([Local ratios and divisor character comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.JointsSAlt/)) and ([Ideal-class representatives and unramified auxiliary fields](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.HostDatumWithTies/)) for these choices. Impose also the equal-rank oscillator laws in ([Adelic oscillator actions and theta integrals](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Theta.WeilTied/)) on the same oscillator representation; these are additional to the unequal-rank laws already required by the linked realization.

Hypothesis (the analytic realization). Choose the original rational moduli realization with its scalar, adelic-character, frame and integral-module choices from ([Scalar normalizations and adelic period identifications](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.LawsJ/)). Its coefficient field $K_s$ acts on $K$, and use this same $K_s$ in the analytic data. Choose stage weights as in ([Weight gaps for analytic comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.HostHyp/)), smooth-vector tuples at every stage and coefficient-field embedding, and the singleton-twist one-step data of ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)), mapped to the base tuple. Require ([One-map and geometric comparison laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)) for these same choices. Choose the analytic and rational data and a conjugate-linear map from its initial common model to the common period space. Impose ([Analytic realizations of rational Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.BaseW/)), ([Spectral normalizations of analytic realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat3.IsScal/)) and ([Analytic theta intertwiners and Weil actions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.IsChainW/)). The subspaces reconstructed from the analytic data equal the prescribed right and lifted subspaces. The entire reconstructed base and sharp coordinate data equal the prescribed data in ([Row-side families of Hermitian spaces](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Rows.RowSideFamily/)). At these subspaces the chosen source-coordinate and common-period constructions equal the prescribed source and common-period data. The reconstructed column pairings, inverse source-period factors and conjugate-linear common-space map equal the prescribed column data.

For the analytic realization, impose the following conditional normalization of inert eigenvalue transport. Use the lifted class-sum forms $\mathcal F^L_{j,t_0}$ of ([Class sums and finite Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.DIV.CSD/)) and the finite-group operators $e_{\mathcal X},e_{P_0,\psi_0^{-1}}$ of ([Row-side families of Hermitian spaces](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Rows.RowSideFamily/)). A transport family consists of maps on $K$-valued functions of a place index and a finite list of natural numbers, one for each lifted stage $0\le j<m$. It realizes the analytic eigenvalues when, for every base type $p$ and spectral data $c$ carrying $p$ in ([Spectral normalizations of analytic realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat3.IsScal/)), the following holds: if a simultaneous eigenvector in $e_{\mathcal X}e_{P_0,\psi_0^{-1}}\mathcal F^L_{j,t_0}$ has a nonzero theta image in $W_{j,c}$, then its eigenvalue at every allowed lifted inert operator is the transported base inert eigenvalue of $p$ at that index. If there is a transport family realizing these eigenvalues and distinguishing, at every lifted stage, every pair of occurring base types distinguished by an allowed base inert operator, require the prescribed transport maps to realize these eigenvalues and distinguish every such pair at every lifted stage.

The chosen additive character equals that of the linked theta realization. Choose mutually inverse maps between the rational family's source labels and the member's source words. Every Poincare source map lies in the chosen spectral Hilbert space. For every rational source coordinate choose a vector in the corresponding ramified column space. The common period datum equals the Poincare period datum constructed from these vectors, their column transports and the chosen spectral convolution operator. The frame implication of ([Integral frames and Hecke operator laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.DIV.DefFam3.Ok3/)) holds. Identify the analytic generator-place product of general-linear groups with the member's finite group, and impose the rational-expansion comparisons of ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)).

At every stage the recorded $V_{i+1}$ basis is orthonormal and its $\mathcal O_E$-span is its recorded lattice; impose the same condition on the recorded $X'$ basis. Every $V_{i+1}$ is positive definite. Choose an element $t_S\in G_{Y_0}$, real scalars $c_S,\kappa_S$, and a group isomorphism $\iota_S$ from $G_{Y_0}$ to the spectral ambient adelic group. The latter ambient datum is the canonical rational embedding of $Y_0$, and $\iota_S$ is its canonical map. Choose a conjugate-linear map $s_S$ between the conjugation-adjusted initial analytic model and the corresponding spectral Hilbert space. The initial common-space map equals $s_S$ under these identifications. Choose $u_S\in\mathbb C$ with $|u_S|=1$. The additive character has the negative standard normalization of ([Adelic oscillator actions and theta integrals](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Theta.WeilTied/)). The generator-place sign identity, with its quantified idempotents and fixed residue lifts, is the identity in ([Compatible points for arithmetic covolume bounds](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.BigTop.PtAt/)). Impose ([Translated spectral evaluation and its normalization](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.IndefiniteRealisation.seeEval/)) for the chosen $t_S,c_S,\kappa_S,u_S,\iota_S,s_S$. Require all six array-gap conditions of ([Weight gaps for analytic comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.HostHyp/)). The base local error at every coefficient place and stage is exactly the displayed canonical error formula in ([Compatible points for arithmetic covolume bounds](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.BigTop.PtAt/)), for the same image lengths, correction allocation and selected twists.

Hypothesis (normalizations). The analytic character frame equals the linked arithmetic character frame, every supplied stage phase is $1$, the real field is the maximal real subfield of $E$, and the generator-place set is the image of the kernel realization's generator-place set. The auxiliary-prime and top-pairing conditions are the ones in ([Compatible points for arithmetic covolume bounds](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.BigTop.PtAt/)). Choose a bijection from the two families of generator-place labels to the analytic generator places and residue-field isomorphisms to $\mathbb F_{g_a}$. These identify the actual prime labels, every selected finite-group idempotent coefficient, the distinguished prime divisors in the character frame, and the finite-group coordinate actions. The recorded Gram matrices of $V_{i+1},W_{i+1}$ are diagonal over $F$. The finite-group isomorphism in the row family equals the analytic group isomorphism followed by the chosen generator-label isomorphism. Require $t_S$ to have trivial infinite component. If any such finite translation conjugates the geometric level and character to the spectral level and character and matches the row levels, $t_S$ does so. Every $\beta_{V,i}$ and $\beta_X$ is the identity.

For a coordinate source label $x$, let $u$ be its member word and $e_{N,u}:K^{a_x}\simeq N_{u,K}$ the specified rational source isomorphism. The chosen ramified vector at coordinate $a$ is exactly the image of $e_{N,u}(\mathbf e_a)$ under the member's rational-to-complex source map followed by its ramified-column isomorphism.

Hypothesis (recursive identifications). A used member twist means the base twist or a twist selected at a finite coefficient place. Choose a map $f:\mathcal T\to T$ and require $m>0$. For every $t$, the image of the top coefficient pairing on $H_{X,t}$ equals the full prescribed $X'$ automorphic multiplicity space. Every extended lifted rational-to-complex map is injective. The extension of a finite-component coefficient function is the function $h\mapsto\rho(h^{-1})F(h_f)$.

For each of the sharp and base sides choose a complex-linear isomorphism $J_E$ from the supplied common row space to the computed initial multiplicity space. For every used $\mu$ and $1\le j\le m-1$, choose a complex-linear isomorphism $J_{\mu,j}$ from the supplied multiplicity space to the computed selected $Y_j$ multiplicity space, and choose $J_{\mu,X}$ at the terminal $X'$ stage. Choose, for every finite type $\pi$, a subspace $A_\pi$ of the initial tensor-automorphic ambient space. At every stage choose a complex-linear isomorphism $J_i^W$ from the member's supplied theta-output space to the computed selected $W_{i+1}$ multiplicity space, and, for every used twist, an isomorphism $J_{\mu,i}^V$ from its supplied input space to the computed selected $V_{i+1}$ multiplicity space. Choose finite-type subspaces $A_{i,\pi}^W$ of the full $W$ tensor-automorphic ambient space. Choose circle-valued idele characters $\xi^C_\mu$ for all member labels.

The following comparisons hold on both row sides. The $Y_j$ parameter is the prescribed negative stage parameter for $j\le m-1$. The initial common space and all selected intermediate and terminal spaces lie in their full prescribed level-and-character multiplicity spaces for every used twist. All $J_E,J_{\mu,j},J_{\mu,X}$ preserve the specified Petersson pairings. Whenever the computed chain contractions satisfy the theta-integral relation, they agree with the supplied contractions after projection to the selected target space, in the exact pairing sense $$\langle x,T y\rangle=\operatorname{Pet}(Jx,T^{\rm an}Jy).$$ This applies to the initial step when $m\ge2$, the direct terminal-to-initial step when $m=1$, every intermediate step $1\le j$ with $j+1\le m-1$, and the terminal step when $m\ge2$. The common-space image of each recorded orthonormal basis vector belongs to $A_\pi$ for its recorded type $\pi$, and $A_\pi\cap A_{\pi'}=0$ for $\pi\ne\pi'$.

At every lifted stage the signed parameter is the holomorphic parameter of its prescribed integral weight. The maps $J_i^W,J_{\mu,i}^V$ preserve the computed Petersson pairings. Suppose a computed theta integral of an input is the restriction of a $W$ automorphic multiplicity map to the lowest maximal-compact type, and that the latter map lands in the chosen $W$ space. Then its full tensor map is the image under $J_i^W$ of the member's prescribed theta output. The supplied isotypic subspace is exactly the inverse image of $A^W_{i,\pi}$, and these latter subspaces have zero pairwise intersection. If the member's theta-adjoint composition is scalar $c$ on a nonzero isotypic component, its recorded scalar is $c$.

For every used twist, the analytic stage character is its base character multiplied by $\xi^C_\mu$, and $\xi^C_{\mu_0}=1$. If a source pairing vanishes on the kernel of the common-space map on the selected base input space, it equals the prescribed descended pairing on the common-space image.

Choose a finite coarse eigenvalue set $\Omega$ and a map from finite types to $\Omega$. Nonzero isotypic components with the same coarse label have the same recorded logarithmic theta scalar at every selected lift and every embedding of $K$. Choose a map from $\Omega$ to the rational family's spectral indices. Choose group isomorphisms from $\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb A_{E,f})$ onto the trivial-infinite-component subgroup of $G_{Y_0}$ and of every $G_{W_{i+1}}$. Choose a bijection from member finite types to the occurring principal-level initial representation types, complex Hecke-eigenvalue functions indexed by $\Omega$, finite $E$-places and integer lists, and homomorphisms $\iota_i:G_{Y_0,f}\to G_{W_{i+1},f}$. Each $\iota_i$ is bijective, is represented in the recorded finite-adelic matrices by $g\mapsto X_i gX_i^{-1}$, and identifies the integral spherical subgroups. For each $i$ there is $k_i\in\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb A_{E,f})$ such that the stage identification on $k_i^{-1}hk_i$ equals $\iota_i$ of the initial identification on $h$.

The sharp and base $A_\pi$ are exactly the tensor images of the principal-level isotypic spaces for the corresponding occurring finite representation; every $A^W_{i,\pi}$ is the analogous lifted isotypic space. For a used $\mu$, $\xi^C_\mu$ is the analytic idele character at $f(\mu)$, and the analytic Hecke character at $f(\mu)$ is $\xi_\mu$ of ([Hecke characters in recursive realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Host.CharFrame/)). The map $f$ carries every selected member label to the selected analytic label at the identified coefficient place. Its character realization is exactly the linked arithmetic realization. Mapping a used member label to $T$ and back returns that label, and mapping any $t\in T$ to the member labels and back returns $t$. The analytic selected twist equals the original base coordinate datum's selected twist at every coefficient place.

Finally the chosen rational input maps commute with $J^V$: for every used twist and selected stage, the supplied complexification of a rational input, followed by $J^V$, is its rational-coordinate identification followed by the analytic semilinear map and the coefficient-function extension. The chosen rational output maps commute with $J^W$: the image of each rational output coordinate vector is exactly the tensor lift of its rational-form realization in $H_W$, followed by the prescribed smooth-vector coordinate transformation. The general-linear identifications are standard in the following literal sense: mutually inverse finite-adelic matrices conjugate them to the standard general-linear matrices under the canonical $\mathbb A_{F,f}\otimes_FE\simeq E\otimes_{E^+}\mathbb A_{E^+,f}$ swap, and the maximal integral general-linear subgroup corresponds exactly to the spherical subgroup. The final local condition retains its existence premise: for every local Galois label, finite type, real-subfield place $v$, E-place $w$ above it, and local datum described below, if the corresponding finite representation has any spherical Satake tuple, it has one whose multiset is the prescribed arithmetic Satake multiset. A spherical Satake tuple has $\lfloor r/2\rfloor$ entries and means that some nonzero vector is fixed by a subgroup whose part supported at $v$ is the maximal integral subgroup and is an eigenvector for every spherical Hecke operator, with eigenvalue the normalized Satake transform at that tuple; this is also the convention in ([Automorphic realizations and normalized Hecke operators](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.NGalTiedAlt/)).

The quantified local datum consists of an isomorphism $E\otimes_{E^+}E_v^+\simeq E_w$, an involution $\sigma_w$ of $E_w$, a uniformizer $\varpi$ fixed by $\sigma_w$, and $P\in\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathcal O_{E_w})$ with $\sigma_w(P)^TP$ equal to the anti-diagonal identity matrix. The isomorphism identifies conjugation and the integral tensor module with $\sigma_w$ and $\mathcal O_{E_w}$. The involution preserves the valuation, some integral $x$ has $\sigma_w(x)-x$ a unit, $q_v\ge2$, and the residue field of $E_w$ has cardinality $q_v^2$.
