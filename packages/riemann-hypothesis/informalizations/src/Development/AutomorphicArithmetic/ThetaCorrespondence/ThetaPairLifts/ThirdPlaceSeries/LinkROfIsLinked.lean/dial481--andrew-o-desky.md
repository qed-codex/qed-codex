## Transfer of twenty-six conditions for families from stage-zero assumptions

[Theorem THETA.0012](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.AllAtMemberStage0.allRows_of_open0_alt/)

Assume the following statements.

1. For every number field $E$ and algebraic closure $\Omega$, there is a homomorphism from the ideles of $E$ to the topological abelianisation of $\operatorname{Gal}(\Omega/E)$. It is an open quotient map; its kernel is the closure of the subgroup generated by the diagonal image of $E^\times$ and the connected component of the identity at the infinite places; and it identifies local units, uniformisers and the closure of the local idele group with inertia, inverse Frobenius and decomposition, respectively, in the abelianised Galois group.
2. ([Hypothesis: the Siegel-Weil formula for unitary groups over a CM extension, for nondegenerate Fourier coefficients in the range M \> 2n](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/HeckeCharacterZeroLocus.SiegelWeilU.SiegelWeilUnitaryConvergent/)) holds.
3. Items (35), (36), (9) and (37), in that order, of ([Hypothesis list A: sixty assumed statements on representations of p-adic and real groups, automorphic forms, Galois representations and class field theory](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.UnitaryGroupThirdPlaceHypotheses/)) hold.
4. Item (40) of ([Hypothesis list A: sixty assumed statements on representations of p-adic and real groups, automorphic forms, Galois representations and class field theory](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.UnitaryGroupThirdPlaceHypotheses/)) holds.
5. Both numerical statements in ([Transfer conditions and numerical bounds for the stage-zero construction](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.OpenParameterStatements/)) hold.
6. ([Hypothesis: existence of a reciprocity map for GL\_n over the completions of number fields, with a restricted list of properties](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.RecTExists/)) holds.

Choose an assignment of a type of additional data to each permitted choice of context, base, frame, places, rows, norm choice and other construction data; its inputs include a record depending on hypothesis 6. Choose also a second assignment of a type of additional data to the construction's column data, and a property of a datum together with two further geometric objects. These arbitrary choices determine built and good points and a link, in the sense of ([Transfer conditions and numerical bounds for the stage-zero construction](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.OpenParameterStatements/)). Let $N$ be any property of classes of families.

Assume the twelve open conditions and the transfer of the following three properties, as specified in ([Transfer conditions and numerical bounds for the stage-zero construction](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.OpenParameterStatements/)), for these choices:

- the covolume property $H_{\mathrm{cov}}$ of ([A class of families satisfying the input conditions of the final argument exists, given hypothesis list A, the rationality test, the norm bound at 2 and 3, a maximal slope bound and a covolume bound](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.StageU.hypotheses_nonempty_of_unramified_exponents_alt/));
- the maximal slope property $H_{\mathrm{slope}}$ of ([A class of families satisfying the input conditions of the final argument exists, given hypothesis list A, the rationality test, the norm bound at 2 and 3, a maximal slope bound and a covolume bound](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.StageU.hypotheses_nonempty_of_unramified_exponents_alt/)), including its divisibility premise;
- the property that $N$ holds for the second refinement of the class.

Then all 26 transferred conditions of ([Transfer conditions and numerical bounds for the stage-zero construction](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.OpenParameterStatements/)) hold for these choices and $N$.
