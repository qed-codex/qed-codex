## Global hypotheses for cusp ratios

[Definition / hypothesis AV.D239](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CovTop.Hypotheses''/)

The two hypotheses below specify the seventeen arithmetic-geometric assertions and the eight Hilbert–Samuel assertions assumed in ([Cusp ratios generate the generic flag field](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.DegVal.birOn_of_weight_gaps_large/)). Each assertion is quantified over all the objects in its statement, independently of a chosen arithmetic datum.

Hypothesis (arithmetic geometry). Assume assertions 1–9 and 11–16 of ([Global hypotheses for Hodge-bundle estimates](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/k2-hodge-bundle-hypotheses.html)), together with the proportionality and marked-lattice finiteness assertions below. These are seventeen assertions: the good-metric and flag identities in assertion 10 of ([Global hypotheses for Hodge-bundle estimates](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/k2-hodge-bundle-hypotheses.html)) are replaced here by proportionality, and marked-lattice finiteness is added.

Hypothesis (Hilbert–Samuel inputs). Assume assertions 19, 21, 25, 20 and 23 of ([Global hypotheses for Hodge-bundle estimates](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/k2-hodge-bundle-hypotheses.html)), namely the fully stated vanishing, logarithmic extension, algebraization, nef numerics and geometrically integral Chow-form assertions, together with the three Chow-form and intersection assertions below. These are eight assertions.
