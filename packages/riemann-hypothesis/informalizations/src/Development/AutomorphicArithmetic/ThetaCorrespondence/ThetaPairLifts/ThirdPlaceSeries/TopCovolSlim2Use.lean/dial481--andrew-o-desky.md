## The covolume bound, given hypothesis list C

[Theorem ARAK.0008](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/SelfIntersectionVolumeEstimates.topCovolSlim2_use_alt_alt/)

Assume the hypotheses of ([Hypothesis list C: sixty assumed statements on resolution of singularities, arithmetic intersection theory, positivity of line bundles, toroidal compactifications, abelian schemes and p-divisible groups](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CompanionTheory.Hypotheses%E2%82%82.ResolutionCharZero/)). Then the individual cusp-lattice inequality in ([Covolume of integral cusp forms](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CovTop.CovolumeBoundOfX/)) holds, with the uniform quantifiers stated there.
