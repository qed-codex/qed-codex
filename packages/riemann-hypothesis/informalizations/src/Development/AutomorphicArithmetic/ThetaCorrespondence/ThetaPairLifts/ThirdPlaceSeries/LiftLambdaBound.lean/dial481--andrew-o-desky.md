## Covolume of integral cusp forms

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D001](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CovTop.CovolumeBoundOfX/)

Fix $0<\varepsilon<1/52$. Assume ([Arithmetic families with local and theta data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/family-arithmetic-data.html)), using its common interval parameter $J\ge1$, starting scale $x_1$, members $I_x$, source indices, Hermitian spaces and integral lattices. Put $L=x^\varepsilon$, $b=e^L$, $X'=e^{x^{\varepsilon/3}}$, $b'=xb/X'$, $\delta_1=x^{-\varepsilon/8}$ and $\Gamma=\exp(C_\Gamma m^5fL)$ for $x>x_1$. All formulas using $x$ as the internal scale, and conditions 1–3 below, are imposed only for $x>x_1$.

The additional family assumptions are the following fourteen conditions:

1. The fixed normalizations are as follows: the parameter constants are $C_p=1$, $e_p=12$, $c_T=28e_p+41$, $C_t=c_T+24$, $C_\eta=6$, $C_c=2$, $c_w=9/10$, $C_{YL}=9$; the designated coefficient ideal is the unit ideal, the source weight vector is independent of the embedding, the level primes are at most $e^{9L}$, the two distinguished excluded primes are below $X'$, the truncation index is $\lceil\log_5(nN_m/m)\rceil_+$, the word length is $10\lceil(C_t+4)\log x\rceil_++2\lceil30/\varepsilon\rceil_++22$, and the auxiliary order cutoff is $\lceil x^{c_T}\rceil_+$. Here $p_1,p_2$ are the two excluded primes; $\lceil\cdot\rceil_+$ means nonnegative ceiling.
2. The rank windows $x^{2+8\varepsilon}\le n\le2x^{2+8\varepsilon}$, $\sqrt{x\delta_1}\le m\le2\sqrt{x\delta_1}$, and $x^{9/2+10\varepsilon}\le k\le2x^{9/2+10\varepsilon}$, where $\delta_1$ is the selected weight-density parameter.
3. The prime $q_0$ is below $X'$, where $r_0$ is the least prime not dividing $Q_Sqd(p_1-1)(p_2-1)\operatorname{Norm}\mathfrak c$ and $q_0$ is the least prime congruent to $1$ modulo $r_0Q_Sqd$ not dividing $\operatorname{Norm}\mathfrak c$. Here $p_1,p_2$ are the two distinguished excluded primes and $\mathfrak c$ is the coefficient ideal of the datum.
4. Eventually, the tame eigenvalue lists, including $1$ and the eigenvalues and their inverses, are distinct, remain distinct after taking prime-to-$\ell$ parts outside $\mathcal B(r,g)=\{g\}\cup\{\ell:\ell\text{ prime},\ \exists1\le h\le r,\ g^h<\ell^{4v_\ell(g^h-1)}\}$ at each level prime $g$, and the selected eigenvalues are squares.
5. Eventually, the selected-prime error sum $\sum_p(\log p/p)[2\mathbf1_{\sigma_p>x^{-\varepsilon}}+4\sigma_p]$ is at most $x^{-9}$; $p$ ranges over the set $\mathcal P$ defined below and $\sigma_p$ is the orbit-loss sum defined below.
6. Eventually, every finite stabilizer denominator $D_u$ divides $M(2fN_m)$, where the particular integer required here is $$M(h)=\prod_{\substack{p\le h+1\\p\ {\rm prime}}}p^{\sum_{i=0}^{h}\left\lfloor h/(p^i(p-1))\right\rfloor}.$$
7. Eventually, at some $Y\in[x^{1/J},x]$, the characters of $-q,2(-q),-d,2(-d),(-d)(-q),2(-d)(-q)$ satisfy the following zero-free condition. Its bounds are $Q=8X_1$ for the first two and $Q=8X_1X_2$ for the last four, with $X_1,X_2$ as in condition 8; it requires each primitive inducing character to be real, have conductor at most $Q$ and be nonzero for $\Re s>1-1/400$, $|\Im s|\le Q$. Every primitive real character of $E$ whose conductor is divisible by $d$ satisfies the same condition with $Q=8Q_{S,\mathrm{odd}}X_1X_2$; if its conductor is divisible by $q$ but not by $d$, use $Q=8Q_{S,\mathrm{odd}}X_1$, where $Q_{S,\mathrm{odd}}$ is the odd part of $Q_S$.
8. Eventually, at some such $Y$, the auxiliary-prime conditions hold: $q\equiv3\pmod8$, $d\equiv7\pmod{16}$, $\sqrt{X_1}<q\le X_1$, $\sqrt{X_2}<d\le X_2$, with $X_1=e^{Y^{\varepsilon/5}}$, $X_2=e^{Y^{\varepsilon/5}(\log Y)^2}$; the quadratic character of $-d$ equals one at $q$ and at every prime at most $Y^{\varepsilon/5}$; and $\log(Q_Sqd)\le2Y^{\varepsilon/5}(\log Y)^2$. Here $J,Q_S$ are the interval and modulus parameters of the family.
9. The family constant $C_{YL}$ is at most $9$.
10. Eventually, every prime divisor of $D_u$ is at most $2fN_m+1$.
11. ([Integral twisting conditions for arithmetic families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TwIn/)) holds.
12. For some exponent $C_{\mathrm{sel}}$ and uniform positive constants $C_b,C_5,c$, there is a threshold $x_{\rm sel}$ such that, for every $x\ge x_{\rm sel}$ and every member, the following selection conditions hold with constant $3$: the second selected set has cardinality at least $n_0\exp(-C_2\sqrt{\log n_0\log L_w})/L_w$, where $n_0,L_w,C_2$ are its cyclic-group size, word length and fixed density constant; the first selected set has cardinality between $\delta_1n/2$ and $2\delta_1n$; for some uniform $C_b>0$, the weighted number of selected labels whose represented orbit fails the orbit test is at most $$\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_{\rm ord}}\frac{\log p}{p}\left|\{u\in T:\text{the orbit represented by }\beta(h_p,u)\text{ fails the orbit test}\}\right| \le4C_bx^{-2}\delta_1n\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_{\rm ord}}\frac{\log p}{p},$$ where $T$ is the first selected subset, $\mathcal P_{\rm ord}=\{p<X':p\text{ prime},\ p\in\mathcal I,\ \operatorname{ord}(h_p)\ge x^{\varepsilon/4}\}$, and the prime class $\mathcal I$, representatives $\beta$ and orbit test are specified below; the three quadratic smoothed sums satisfy $|g_\chi(x)|\le x^{1/2+\varepsilon}$ and their logarithmic derivatives at one have absolute value at most $C_5\log x$ for some uniform $C_5>0$; for some uniform $c>0$, every primitive Dirichlet character $\chi$ obtained from a character of $\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb Q)$ through the specified Artin map satisfies $|g_\chi(y)|\le y e^{-c x^{\varepsilon/8}}$ for every $y\ge e^{x^{\varepsilon/3}}/3$; $1/3\le d_u\Gamma^2/D_*\le3$; and $3\Gamma^2x^{C_{\mathrm{sel}}}$ is at most the number of sources. All constants in these bounds are uniform in the family; $d_u$ is the dimension of the initial source representation, $\Gamma$ is the period normalization, and $$D_*=\operatorname{vol}(Y_0)\prod_{v=0}^1[\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb F_{g_v}):R_{k,m}(\mathbb F_{g_v})]^f\prod_{\rho\in\Sigma}d_\rho.$$ Here $Y_0$ is the base Hermitian lattice, with its designated mass-volume measure; $d_\rho$ is the discrete-series formal degree with the measure of ([Formal degree with compact-dual normalization](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.RgFd.B.formalDegreeCompactDual/)). Put $s_{\rm lev}=m/2-1$ and define $B(i)=i$ for $i<s_{\rm lev}$, $B(i)=s_{\rm lev}$ for $s_{\rm lev}\le i<s_{\rm lev}+k+1$, and $B(i)=i-k$ otherwise. The set $R_{k,m}\subset\mathrm{GL}_r$ consists exactly of the matrices $A$ with $A_{ii}=1$, $A_{ij}=0$ whenever $B(j)<B(i)$, $A_{ij}=0$ whenever $B(i)=B(j)$ and $j<i$, and $A_{i,s_{\rm lev}+k}=0$ whenever $i\ne s_{\rm lev}+k$ and $B(i)=s_{\rm lev}$. Indices start at zero.
13. Eventually, each source prime $T_u$ satisfies $b^{m/2}\le T_u$ and $\log T_u\le C_TmL$ for the fixed family constant $C_T$.
14. For some fixed $C$, eventually $\log D_u\le CfN_m\log(fN_m)$ for every source.

Use the family and point compatibility hypotheses in the conventions below. At such a point let $M_j$ be the integral lattice of the selected cusp forms at stage $1\le j\le m$, let $D$ be the dimension of the distinguished theta-image space, and let $H_K(M_j)$ be its arithmetic covolume. In a real realization of rank $r_j$ over $K$, this normalization is $$H_K(M_j)=2^{s_Kr_j}|\operatorname{disc}K|^{-r_j/2}\operatorname{covol}_{\mathbb R}(M_j),\qquad s_K=|\Sigma_\infty(K)|.$$ Put $f=[E:\mathbb Q]/2$, $L=x^\varepsilon$, $b=e^L$, $B_0=fmbL$, and $$\log\Omega=\gamma+\log(2\pi/\mathrm{rd}_E)-\Re\frac{L'}L(1,\chi_C),\qquad P_j=\sum_\rho(k+j-1-(m-j))w_j(\rho)\log\Omega,$$ where $\rho$ runs through the CM type, $\chi_C$ is the quadratic character of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})$, and $\gamma$ is Euler's constant.

The primary assertion is uniform with the following quantifier order. There is $\varepsilon_0>0$ such that, for each $0<\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_0$, there are threshold rules for the family constants. For every pair consisting of a theta family and a value of $C_H$ satisfying conditions 1–14 and those threshold rules, there are $C_0\ge0$ and $x_0$ such that, for all $x\ge x_0$ above the family's starting scale, all $\iota\in I_x$, every compatible point and every $C\ge C_0$, $$\frac{2\log H_K(M_j)}{[K:\mathbb Q]D}\le P_j+C B_0\log\log x\qquad(1\le j\le m).$$ The threshold rules are the inequalities on the four ordered lists defined in the conventions below. This states the individual cusp-lattice bound; the total conventions $a/0=0$ and $\log0=0$ apply.
