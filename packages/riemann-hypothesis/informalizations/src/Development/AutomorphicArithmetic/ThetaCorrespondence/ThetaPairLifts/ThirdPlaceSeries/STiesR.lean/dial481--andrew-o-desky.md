## Spectral column and period realizations

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D209](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.HidaSetting.HidaSetting/)

Use ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), ([Hermitian spaces in the theta constructions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.CMData/)), ([Moment data for the family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.S.Draft.SData/)), ([Integral forms on column representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.MemberForms/)) and ([Geometric moment terms for spectral realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.TermDef.Term/)). The following hypothesis concerns the initial Hermitian space $Y_0$; write $s=r_S$, $g=g_0g_1$, and $\mathfrak d$ for the different of $F$, extended to $E$. All infinite unitary groups carry the fixed Haar measures of ([Moment data for the family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.S.Draft.SData/)).

Hypothesis (spectral unitary setting). Choose number fields $F',E'$ with involution making $E'/F'$ a CM extension in the precise sense of ([Hermitian spaces in the theta constructions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.CMData/)), an integer $n$, a nonsingular Hermitian matrix $J\in M_n(E')$, and a full self-dual $\mathcal O_{E'}$-lattice $L\subset(E')^n$. At every infinite place $v$ of $F'$ choose an embedding $\phi_v:E'\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ above $v$, a sign array $\epsilon_v$ of length $n$, and an invertible matrix $P_v$ with $P_v^*\phi_v(J)P_v=J_{\epsilon_v}$. Equip the finite-adelic unitary group $G_f$ with a group topology in which the positive integral principal-congruence subgroups of the completed lattice $\widehat L$ are open and form a neighborhood basis of one. Let $G_\infty=\prod_vU(J_{\epsilon_v})$, $G_A=G_\infty\times G_f$, and $\Gamma$ be the diagonally embedded rational unitary group, expressed at infinity using $P_v$.

Choose an isomorphism $a:E'\simeq E$ commuting with conjugation and carrying $F'$ onto $F$, a basis $b_1,\ldots,b_n$ of $Y_0$, and a bijection $v\leftrightarrow\tau$ with the member's CM labels. Under the coordinate map $x\mapsto\sum_ja(x_j)b_j$, $J$ is the Gram matrix, $L$ is exactly the recorded lattice of $Y_0$, and $\phi_v=\tau\circ a$. Require $n=r$, the sign arrays to be the prescribed initial signs, the real Harish-Chandra arrays to be the prescribed initial arrays, $[F':\mathbb Q]=f$, and the spectral root discriminant to equal the fixed root discriminant of $E$.

Choose a sigma-finite Haar measure $\mu_f$ on $G_f$ and a measurable rational fundamental set $\mathcal F\subset G_A$: every $\Gamma$-orbit meets $\mathcal F$, and a nonidentity rational translate intersects it in measure zero. Put $\mu_A=(\prod_v\mu_v)\mu_f$ and $\mathscr H=L^2(\mathcal F,\mu_A)$. Choose local complete complex inner-product spaces $H_v$, continuous linear matrix operators $\sigma_v$ and unit lowest-type vectors $w_v$, satisfying the full discrete-series conditions of ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)). Choose a complete complex inner-product space $H_\otimes$, operators $\sigma_\otimes$ and a map $\otimes:\prod_vH_v\to H_\otimes$ whose inner products are products, whose image has dense linear span, and which intertwines the local actions with $\sigma_\otimes$. Choose continuous linear operators $R(h)$ on $\mathscr H$ such that $R(h)f(x)=\widetilde f(xh)$ almost everywhere on $\mathcal F$, where $\widetilde f$ extends $f$ using a chosen representative in $\mathcal F$ of each rational orbit.

Choose a finite test function $e:G_f\to\mathbb C$. It is locally constant, convolution-idempotent, satisfies $e(x^{-1})=\overline{e(x)}$, is of positive type, is supported in the lattice stabilizer, is bi-invariant under the level-$g$ principal-congruence subgroup, and its support consists of elements with $(x-1)^n\widehat L\subset g_0\widehat L$. Positive type means $\sum_{i,j}\bar c_i c_je(x_i^{-1}x_j)\ge0$ for every finite tuple. Choose operators $R_e,R_\varphi$ on $\mathscr H$ satisfying the weak operator-integral identities for convolution against $e$ on $G_f$ and against $$\varphi(h)=\left(\prod_vd_v\right)\overline{\prod_v\langle w_v,\sigma_v(h_v)w_v\rangle}$$ on $G_\infty$, respectively; both scalar kernels are integrable. Here $d_v=\|w_v\|^4/\int|\langle w_v,\sigma_v(h)w_v\rangle|^2d\mu_v(h)$. The spectral common space is $\mathscr H_0=\operatorname{im}(R_eR_\varphi)$. The member's $D_{\rm all}$ is the dimension of the image of $R_e$ on the space of continuous intertwining maps $H_\otimes\to\mathscr H$ for the infinite action. Its $D_\star$ is $\prod_vd_v$ times $\mu_A(\mathcal F)\operatorname{Re}e(1)$.

Hypothesis (flags and finite test vectors). Choose isotropic vectors $e_a,f_a\in Y_0$, $0\le a<s$, with $h(e_a,f_b)=\delta_{ab}$, and a perpendicular $\mathcal O_E$-submodule $L_b$ such that $$L_0=\sum_a\mathfrak d^{-(s-a)}e_a+L_b+\sum_a\mathfrak d^{s-a}f_a.$$ For every source $u$ choose $z_u\in L_b$ of norm $-T_u$, perpendicular to all $e_a,f_a$. The flag consists of the independent $e_a$, their successive spans, and $z_u$. Require the initial lattice to be full and self-dual, $z_u$ to be integral, and, at every prime $P$ of $E$, the localized intersection with $\operatorname{span}_E(e_a)$ to have a basis $c_ae_a$ with $c_a\ne0$ and $c_a=1$ when $P\mid g$. Put $$L_{\perp}=L_0\cap X^\perp,\quad L_{\rm sub}=(L_{\perp}+X)\cap X^\perp,\quad X=\operatorname{span}_E(e_a).$$ Require $cz_u\in L_{\rm sub}$ to imply $c\in\mathcal O_E$.

Choose a multiplicative additive-character function $\psi_f:E'\otimes_{F'}\mathbb A_{F',f}\to\mathbb C$, equal to $1$ on the integral finite adeles, whose value on a diagonal $x\in E'$ times $\exp(2\pi\mathrm i\sum_v(\phi_v(x)+\overline{\phi_v(x)}))$ is $1$. In $\widehat L$ choose vectors $e_a^0,f_a^0,z^0,b_i,b_i'$ and an integral idempotent $\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon\bar\varepsilon=0$. Write $x\equiv y\pmod g$ for $x-y\in g\widehat L$, and use the same notation for divisibility in the integral finite-adelic scalar ring. Require the finite $e_a^0,f_a^0$ to agree with the recorded coordinates of $e_a,f_a$ modulo $g$ after an integer multiplier coprime to $g$. Require $$h(e_a^0,e_b^0)\equiv h(f_a^0,f_b^0)\equiv0,\quad h(e_a^0,f_b^0)\equiv\delta_{ab},\quad h(z^0,z^0)\equiv-1,$$ and all pairings of $z^0$ with $e_a^0,f_a^0$, and of $z^0,e_a^0,f_a^0$ with $b_i,b_i'$, to vanish modulo $g$. Require $(1-\varepsilon-\bar\varepsilon)\widehat L\subset g\widehat L$, $b_i=\varepsilon b_i$, $b_i'=\bar\varepsilon b_i'$, $h(b_i',b_j)\equiv\delta_{ij}\varepsilon\pmod g$, and these vectors to generate $\widehat L$ modulo $g$.

The finite level $K_2$ consists of lattice-stabilizing elements $x$ such that $(x-1)e_a^0$ lies modulo $g\widehat L$ in the integral span of earlier $e_b^0$, $(x-1)z^0$ lies in the span of all $e_a^0$, and $(x-1)b_i$ lies in the span of all $e_a^0$ and earlier $b_j$. Its character is $$\chi_2(x)=\psi_f\left(g^{-1}\left(\sum_ah(f_a^0,x\,\operatorname{next}(e^0,z^0)_a)+\sum_{i<k-1}h(b_i',xb_{i+1})\right)\right),$$ where the next vector after $e_a^0$ is $e_{a+1}^0$ or, at the last index, $z^0$. Require $K_2$ to be a subgroup and $\chi_2$ to be a unitary character, and require $e(x)=\overline{\chi_2(x)}/\mu_f(K_2)$ there and $e(x)=0$ elsewhere.

For every source choose $x_u\in G_f$ preserving $\widehat L$ and fixing the finite coordinates of every $e_a,f_a$. Its inverse carries the coordinates of $z_u$ to $z^0$ modulo $g$ after a multiplier coprime to $g$. For every natural $q$ coprime to $g$ and every $y\in\widehat L$, some natural multiplier coprime to $q$ carries $(x_u-1)y$ into $q\widehat L$.

Hypothesis (compact source models). Let $S_u$ be the infinite unitary subgroup carrying $e_a$ to $e_a$ plus earlier $e_b$ and $z_u$ to $z_u$ plus their span. Let $N_u$ be the subgroup additionally satisfying $(h-1)X^\perp\subset X$, and $H_u$ the subgroup fixing all $e_a,f_a,z_u$ pointwise. Choose a finite-dimensional complex inner-product space $V_u$ with a strongly continuous unitary representation of $S_u$ trivial on $N_u$. At every infinite place choose a finite-dimensional inner-product space $V_{u,v}$ with the prescribed nonzero irreducible $U(k)$ highest-weight representation, and $k$ orthonormal vectors in the signed Hermitian coordinates perpendicular to $e_a,f_a,z_u$. Choose a map $\otimes_u:\prod_vV_{u,v}\to V_u$ preserving products of inner products and with spanning image. On $H_u$ it intertwines the global action with the local actions through the matrices of pairings with those $k$ vectors.

Choose nonzero sigma-finite measures $\mu_{N,u},\mu_{H,u}$ supported on $N_u,H_u$, invariant under their left translations, and a quotient measure $\nu_u$ with $$\int_{G_\infty}f(x)\,dx=\int\int f(nhx)\,d\mu_{N,u}(n)d\mu_{H,u}(h)d\nu_u(x)$$ for every nonnegative measurable $f$. Choose continuous $W_u:G_\infty\to V_u$ that transforms under $S_u$ by its unitary action times the character $$s\longmapsto\prod_v\exp\left(\frac{2\pi\mathrm i}{g}\sum_a\bigl(h(f_a,s\operatorname{next}(e,z_u)_a)+\overline{h(f_a,s\operatorname{next}(e,z_u)_a)}\bigr)\right).$$ It is reproduced by right convolution with $\varphi$, with the defining integrals absolutely integrable, is integrable for $\nu_u$, and has squared $L^2(\nu_u)$ norm $1$. Choose continuous $V_{u,v}$-valued local functions whose tensor is $W_u$.

Hypothesis (measures). Choose Haar measures on the full adelic unitary group, its infinite and finite factors, each finite local unitary group, and every pointwise stabilizer of a rational $(2s+2)$-tuple. Choose Haar measures on every source's adelic pointwise stabilizer and unipotent subgroup and their infinite and finite factors, and on its finite triangular stabilizer and every finite local triangular stabilizer. Splitting infinite and finite adeles sends each full group, pointwise-stabilizer and unipotent measure to the product of its two factor measures. Multiplication sends the product of the finite unipotent and pointwise-stabilizer measures to the finite triangular-stabilizer measure.

There is a topological group isomorphism from $G_\infty\times G_f$ to the unitary group over $\mathbb A_{F'}\otimes_{F'}E'$ whose infinite matrix is obtained by $P_v$-conjugation and whose finite matrix is the recorded finite matrix. Under this map the full Haar measure is $\mu_A$, the infinite and finite measures are the fixed infinite measure and $\mu_f$, and each source's infinite unipotent and stabilizer measures are $\mu_{N,u},\mu_{H,u}$.

Choose a continuous, nontrivial, unitary additive character $\psi$ of $\mathbb A_{F'}$, trivial on $F'$, whose infinite restriction is $\exp(2\pi\mathrm i\sum_v\operatorname{Re}x_v)$. For each source's rational frame, and each nonsingular rational $(2s+2)$-tuple, choose a measure on its adelic Gram fibre that is supported on that fibre and has the product integral of its infinite and local fibre measures on every compactly supported continuous infinite test function times a pure finite test function. The local fibre measures disintegrate the Gram map on independent tuples with respect to self-dual additive Haar measures on tuples and Hermitian matrices. Self-dual means Fourier inversion $\widehat{\widehat f}(z)=f(-z)$ whenever the iterated transform is integrable. The tuple Fourier pairing is the trace Hermitian pairing, and the Hermitian-matrix pairing is the trace product. The global group Haar measure disintegrates over this fibre measure against the chosen pointwise-stabilizer measure, for every measurable nonnegative function and every orbit section. Every source's rational unipotent quotient has covolume $1$.

At each finite place, the local group measure likewise disintegrates over the local Gram-fibre measure against a Haar measure on the pointwise frame stabilizer; the triangular-stabilizer measure is the product of that measure and the self-dual additive measure in the strictly upper-triangular frame coordinates, for every section realizing those coordinates. Finally, for each nonzero prime $\mathfrak p$ of $\mathcal O_F$, with residue characteristic $\ell$, put $F^c_{\mathfrak p}=\widehat F_{\mathfrak p}$ and $E^c_{\mathfrak p}=F^c_{\mathfrak p}\otimes_F E$. The first has its completion valuation and its continuous $\mathbb Q_\ell$-embedding extending the rational inclusion. The second has its finite-dimensional vector-space topology and Borel measurable structure, involution $a\otimes b\mapsto a\otimes\bar b$, and integral subring the integral closure of $\mathcal O_{F^c_{\mathfrak p}}$. For $t\in\mathbb Q_\ell$ choose $a\in\mathbb Q$ with denominator a power of $\ell$ and $|t-a|_\ell\le1$, and define $\psi_\ell(t)=\exp(2\pi i a)$. The standard character on $E^c_{\mathfrak p}$ is $y\mapsto\psi_\ell(\operatorname{Tr}_{E^c_{\mathfrak p}/\mathbb Q_\ell}y)$.

Identify the member's local moment field and quadratic algebra in ([Moment data for the family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.S.Draft.SData/)) with $F^c_{\mathfrak p},E^c_{\mathfrak p}$ by field and ring isomorphisms. Both maps and their inverses are continuous. The field map commutes with $F$ and $\mathbb Q_\ell$ and preserves and reflects every valuation comparison. The algebra map commutes with $E$ and with scalar extension along the field map; it carries the involution, integral subring and chosen additive character to the specified involution, integral subring and standard character above. These requirements do not compare the optional split identifications in ([Moment data for the family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.S.Draft.SData/)).

Hypothesis (finite rows, columns and moments). For every source choose finitely many row lattices. Each is in the genus of $L_0$, satisfies the fixed-lattice flag conditions above, and intersects $X$ in exactly $\sum_a\mathfrak d^{-(s-a)}e_a$. They represent, uniquely up to the rational isometry subgroup preserving the flag and fixing $z_u$, every lattice with these properties. Each admits a decomposition $$\Lambda=\sum_a\mathfrak d^{-(s-a)}e_a+L'+\sum_a\mathfrak d^{s-a}f'_a,$$ where the $f'_a$ are isotropic dual vectors, $L'$ is perpendicular to all $e_a,f'_a$, and $z_u\in L'$.

Choose a finite class set for every source, an assigned chosen row for each class, finite pointwise-stabilizer representatives $h_c$, and a class above every chosen row. The row assigned to $c$ consists exactly of those rational vectors made integral by $(h_cx_u)^{-1}$ in the recorded finite coordinates. Each element of the finite pointwise stabilizer has a unique class in its rational-stabilizer/level double-coset decomposition. Choose a right transversal in the rational unitary group for its source triangular stabilizer and a measurable fundamental set for the source rational unipotent subgroup at infinity.

Choose finitely many finite fields of cardinality $g_0$ or $g_1$, jointly surjective reductions from the integral finite-adelic scalar ring to their product, and nontrivial complex additive characters of those fields. Their joint kernel consists exactly of elements whose $\varepsilon$ component is divisible by $g$, reduction is unchanged by multiplication by $\varepsilon$, and on that component the product of residue characters equals $\psi_f(x/g)$. Choose local Hermitian lattice models as in ([Moment data for the family](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.S.Draft.SData/)) and nonzero local scalars $\delta_P$. Their conjugations and integral rings equal the prescribed ones, their characters are $y\mapsto\psi_P(-y/\delta_P)$, their split/non-split choices agree including the splitting map and residue size, and at a place over $g_a$ require $\delta_P=g/g_a$.

The geometric terms, finite lift sets and lattice/flag predicates are those of ([Geometric moment terms for spectral realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.TermDef.Term/)). Their term operators, pair values, masses, counts and Poincare normalization are exactly ([Operators and coefficients of geometric moment terms](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.SG.TermOp.aTerm/)). Whenever the following finiteness conditions hold, choose an isomorphism of the entire member moment datum with this geometric moment datum. The conditions are: the Riesz evaluation map from each source's equivariant column space to all tuples of $V_u$ indexed by its finite classes is bijective; the normalized Poincare maps are complex-linear and land in $\mathscr H_0$; $\mathscr H_0$ is finite-dimensional; all term-lift fibres are finite; the scalar ratio and scalar-pair predicate are independent of lifts with equal assigned rows; every term's Gram scalar is in $F$; every signed unitary Haar measure admits a positive scale realizing the member's recorded unitary Haar measure; and all source unipotent and stabilizer volumes are positive.

The chosen moment isomorphism is isometric on the common and source spaces and commutes with source period maps and term operators. It preserves Haar scales, spacing, integer labels, both volume functions, identity/source-relation/relevance/equality-of-flags/generic predicates, term sizes, local data and exceptional-place labels, pair labels and their two projections, pair scalars, scalar-pair predicates, pair values, term points and coefficients, pair masses and mean counts.

Hypothesis (transport to member columns). For each source choose a Hermitian isometry $w$ of $Y_0$ sending its spectral $e_a,f_a,z_u$ to the prescribed member vectors, an isomorphism of its finite-adelic scalar algebra with the member's commuting with $E$ and preserving the integral rings, an initial finite-adelic unitary element $h_0$, and spectral finite representatives indexed by the member's initial classes. In the basis $w^{-1}i_0(\operatorname{frame}_{X_0})$, these representatives have matrix equal to the member representatives times $h_0^{-1}$. Choose a conjugate-linear map from $V_u$ to the prescribed initial polynomial coefficient functions. It intertwines the compact action with that frame action and, after each member class's frame map, takes the coefficient inner product of the two image functions to $\langle\eta,\xi\rangle$ for input vectors $\xi,\eta$.

Conjugation by $h_0$ identifies the spectral and member finite levels and their characters. The $h_0^{-1}$-translate of the member level lattice is exactly the spectral lattice expressed in this basis. Evaluating a column map at its finite representative, taking its Riesz vector, and applying the conjugate-linear coefficient map gives a bijection onto the member's selected initial column functions. Whenever the geometric moment data are finite, this column bijection carries the chosen source moment isometry to the member's prescribed source-to-column map.

Choose a continuous operator $R_{\rm good}$ on $\mathscr H$ given by convolution against the member's finite-group idempotent coefficient, divided by the principal-level volume, evaluated at a compatible matrix reduction of the finite adele; set the function to zero off the lattice stabilizer or when no such reduction exists. A compatible reduction is computed in a lattice basis generating every residue quotient. Let $\mathscr E$ be the conjugate of $\mathscr H_0\cap\ker(R_{\rm good}-1)$. Whenever the geometric moment spaces are finite, there is an isometric linear isomorphism from the member's common row space onto $\mathscr E$ whose conjugate, in $\mathscr H_0$, is the specified common period map followed by the moment isometry. The spectral ambient arithmetic-group datum is exactly the canonical datum attached to the member.
