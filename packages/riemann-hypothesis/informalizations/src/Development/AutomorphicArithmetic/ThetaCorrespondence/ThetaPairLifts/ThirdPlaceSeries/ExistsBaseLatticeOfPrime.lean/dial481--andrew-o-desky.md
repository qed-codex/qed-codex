## Mass ratio of the standard Hermitian lattices over a CM field, and existence of built points and of their attached theta-lift records carrying a prescribed eventual property

[Theorem THETA.0009](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.StageOneU.member_bundles_large_unramified_exponents_alt/)

**Setting.** The terms "context", "base", "frame", "twist data", "eventually" and "for large bases" are as in ([Contexts, and statements that hold eventually in the parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.EvG/)); "datum" and "object" as in ([Datum of the construction and objects over it](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.EX.OB.Obj/)); "point", "property of points", "built by the recipe", "choice of extensions", "attached record" and "link" as in ([Points of the construction, properties of points, attached records and statements over families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.Row/)); "rational family", "realisation", "geometric core" and "rationality test for the rows" as in ([Rational families of forms, their realisations by theta maps, and the rationality test](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Rat.RowTest/)).

Two conditions on a base $B$ with frame $f$ and twist data $T$ at a context with scale $(x,\varepsilon,J)$ recur below. The pair $(B,f)$ is *exact* if, for some choice of auxiliary field data, the image of $K_s$ in $\mathbb{C}$ equals the field generated by an explicit list of numbers attached to $(B,f)$ and that choice, and the chosen basis of $X'$ is carried by the embedding $X'\to X_m$ to the first basis vectors of $X_m$. The twist data are *saturated* if their set $\Pi$ contains every prime $p$ with $p\in\{g_0,g_1,d\}$ or $p\le n\,x\exp(x^{\varepsilon/3})$.

Assume the following.

1. ([Hypothesis list A: sixty assumed statements on representations of p-adic and real groups, automorphic forms, Galois representations and class field theory](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.UnitaryGroupThirdPlaceHypotheses/)).
2. *Rationality of the row theta maps throughout the construction.* Eventually, for large bases: if $(B,f)$ is exact and $T$ is saturated, then for every column input for $(B,f,T)$ that satisfies the starting conditions, every completion of it to stages $X$, every set $W$ of finite places of $\mathbb{L}$ and every realisation record over $D(X)$ subject to the nine conditions listed under "Conventions", the rationality test for the rows holds for the realisation and face of that record. (A realisation record is only defined when the fields of the base satisfy a standing condition of the formalization, not expanded here; the statement is for bases satisfying it.) Here a *realisation record* over $D(X)$ consists of a rational family over $\mathbb{L}$ determined by the column input, the twist data and $W$, a geometric core, a realisation of the family over the coefficient field of the core, a face, and compatibility conditions among these.
3. ([Hypothesis: existence of a reciprocity map for GL\_n over the completions of number fields, with a restricted list of properties](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.RecTExists/)).
4. $c\in\mathbb{R}$, and $A$ is a property of a context together with a base, a frame and twist data that holds eventually for large bases.

Then the following three statements hold.

**(i) Mass ratio.** Let $K$ be a number field with a ring involution $a\mapsto\bar a$ such that $K$ is totally complex, $\varphi(\bar a)=\overline{\varphi(a)}$ for every embedding $\varphi\colon K\to\mathbb{C}$, and for every maximal ideal $P$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$ there is $\theta\in\mathcal{O}_K$ with $\theta-\bar\theta\notin P$. (Thus $K$ is a CM field, and the last condition says that no finite prime of $K$ is ramified over the fixed field $K^+$ of the involution.) For $M\ge1$ let $h_M(x,y)=\sum_{i=1}^M\bar x_iy_i$ on $K^M$, and let $\operatorname{mass}(M)$ be the mass of the genus of the lattice $\mathcal{O}_K^M$ in $(K^M,h_M)$. Then for every $M\ge3$ $$\frac{\operatorname{mass}(M)}{\operatorname{mass}(M-1)}=\frac{\bigl((M-1)!\,(v/\pi)^M\bigr)^{g}}{\sqrt{(2v)^{g}}}\ \prod_{P}e_P(M),$$ where $g$ is the number of infinite places of $K$, $v=\tfrac12|\operatorname{disc}K|^{1/(2g)}$, the product is over all maximal ideals $P$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$, and $$e_P(M)=\begin{cases}\bigl(1-(-1)^M\,\mathrm{N}(P)^{-M/2}\bigr)^{-1}&\text{if }\bar P=P,\\[2pt]\bigl(1-\mathrm{N}(P)^{-M}\bigr)^{-1/2}&\text{if }\bar P\ne P.\end{cases}$$ The right-hand side equals $((M-1)!)^{g}\,(2\pi)^{-Mg}\,|\operatorname{disc}K|^{(2M-1)/4}\prod_Pe_P(M)$. For $K$ as above the primes with $\bar P=P$ lie over the primes of $K^+$ inert in $K$ and have norm the square of the norm of the prime below, and the primes with $\bar P\ne P$ come in conjugate pairs over the split primes; hence $\prod_Pe_P(M)=L(M,\eta^M)$, the value at $M$ of the Hecke $L$-function of the $M$-th power of the quadratic character $\eta$ of $K/K^+$.

**(ii) Existence of built points.** Let $\mathcal{S}$ be the choice of extensions determined by hypothesis 3, the number $c$ and the property $A$ as described under "Conventions"; a core built with extensions $\mathcal{S}$ carries in particular a proof of $A$ for its context, base, frame and twist data. Call a core *canonically built* if it is built with extensions $\mathcal{S}$ and its kernel entries are those produced by the fixed construction of kernel entries, and *good* if in addition its twisting choices satisfy two further conditions (existence of an admissible system of twists, and disjointness of the prime sets $Q(w)$, $Q(w')$ at places with different twists). Then:

- (a) Eventually there are preliminary data over the context and a frame such that, for any rules assigning finite sets $S_1$, $S_2$ of algebraic numbers to subfields of $\mathbb{C}$, there is a base $B$ with these preliminary data and this frame for which $(B,f)$ is exact, the image of $\mathbb{L}_0$ contains $S_1$ and the image of $\mathbb{L}$ contains $S_2$. Moreover exactness is preserved when $\mathbb{L}$ is enlarged with $K_s$ and $\mathbb{L}_0$ unchanged as subfields of $\mathbb{C}$.
- (b) Eventually, for large bases: if $(B,f)$ is exact and $T$ is saturated and untwisted, then there are later stages and a core over $(B,f,T)$ that is canonically built.
- (c) Eventually, for large bases: if $(B,f)$ is exact and $T$ is untwisted, then every canonically built core over $(B,f,T)$ satisfies the export condition (a condition on the twisting data of its datum, not expanded here).
- (d) Eventually, for large bases: if $(B,f)$ is exact and $T$ is untwisted, then for every canonically built core over $(B,f,T)$ satisfying the export condition, every rule $\ell\mapsto A'(\ell)$ of finite sets of natural numbers and every rule giving a finite set $S_2$ of algebraic numbers, there are a base $B'$ enlarging $B$ as in (a), twist data $T'$ with the same set $\Pi$ in which no prime of $Q(w)$ has norm in $A'(\ell)$, with $S_2$ contained in the image of $\mathbb{L}'$, and later stages with a core over $(B',f,T')$ that is canonically built, satisfies the full twisting condition (not expanded here) and is good.

**(iii) Existence of attached records.** There is a property $R$ of points such that:

- eventually for all contexts and for large bases, if $(B,f)$ is exact then every canonically built core over $(B,f,T)$ has property $R$; and
- records exist at all points that are good and have property $R$, for the following type of attached records and link. An attached record over a datum $D$, an object $O$ and a real number $c'$ has seven components: (1) a record of data tied to $O$, with four conditions on it; (2) maps between spaces of forms contained in the first component and spaces of forms of the datum, required to be bijective and to preserve levels, with further conditions; (3) the row-side coordinate data of ([Row-side families of Hermitian spaces](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Rows.RowSideFamily/)) for one choice of its row and lifted spectral subspaces; (4) six conditions tying the third component to the first; (5) two natural numbers $p_1,p_2$ such that one parameter of $D$ equals $p_1$ and one size parameter of $O$ equals $mp_2$; (6) a column record (number fields with embeddings into $\mathbb{L}$ and column data) tied to the first and third components; (7) a geometric core over $D$, a realisation of the family of the third component, weights, vectors at the infinite places, and eight further items of data and compatibility conditions between the core and the realisation, depending on $c'$ through a scale. The record is linked to a point if the datum and object of the point are those of a point built with extensions $\mathcal{S}$ and there is a witness, with sixteen components (auxiliary data and identifications), that the components of the record are the ones determined by the data from which the point was built.

The components of the attached records, the witness and the conditions named in (ii) have not been expanded beyond the lists just given. **Alternate criterion from multiplicity and origin hypotheses.**

Assume the first list, the second list, the multiplicity and cover assumptions, and the state-with-zero-data assumption of ([Hypotheses for the existence of built points with origin data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.HypAAlt/)). Assume also ([Hypothesis: existence of a reciprocity map for GL\_n over the completions of number fields, with a restricted list of properties](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.RecTExists/)) and, relative to these reciprocity maps, the six further assumptions of ([Hypotheses for the existence of built points with origin data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.HypAAlt/)). Let $c\in\mathbb R$ and assume the multiplicity-at-constructed-points hypothesis of ([Hypotheses for the existence of built points with origin data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.HypAAlt/)) for $c$.

Use the choice of extensions with origin data for $c$ and the reciprocity maps, taking the additional eventual property to be the condition that always holds. In particular the extension data include an *origin*: preliminary data $P$, a state $x$ and a level record over $x$ such that $x$ satisfies a list condition depending on $c$, the set of places of $P$ satisfies the closure condition of the state-with-zero-data assumption, this set is the prescribed set of places, and the level record retains the specified Galois, twist, form and character data.

Then conclusions (ii)(a)–(d) and (iii) above hold for this choice of extensions.
