## The norm bound at the primes 2 and 3 for the families produced by the construction with a given parameter

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D059](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.RL.LinkedAt/)

This entry states a condition $\mathrm{NB}(c)$ depending on a real number $c$ and on the hypothesis ([Hypothesis: existence of a reciprocity map for GL\_n over the completions of number fields, with a restricted list of properties](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.RecTExists/)), which must hold for the condition to be formed. It uses the vocabulary of ([Data, families of data, classes of families, and properties of all sufficiently constrained families of a class](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.Closed/)). The condition has two layers: an inequality at the primes 2 and 3 for the members of a family, and a transfer mechanism that says for which families the inequality is required.

**The inequality.** Let $D$ be a datum with size data $L$ and scale $(x,\varepsilon,J)$. The datum contains a set of *local indices*; each local index $i$ has a prime $\ell_i$, a finite set $X_i$ with a subset $Z^{\mathrm{good}}_i$, and a rational-valued function $v_{cc}(i,\cdot)$ on $X_i$. Let $\rho$ be a record of a certain kind attached to the datum, with a component $\rho_i$ for each local index $i$ (not expanded). For a real number $C$, the *norm bound at 2 and 3 for $(L,\rho)$ with constant $C$* says: for every local index $i$ that satisfies a condition (not expanded) and has $\ell_i=2$, or $\ell_i=3$ with $3\nmid Q_S$, and for every $\pi\in Z^{\mathrm{good}}_i$, $$v_{cc}(i,\pi)\ \le\ s(i,\rho_i,\pi)+C\,f\,m\,b\log x+C\,x^{C}\,\lambda(L)^2+V(\ell_i)+h(\ell_i)+\epsilon(\ell_i),$$ where $s$, $\lambda(L)$, $V$, $h$ and $\epsilon$ are quantities of the datum and of the size data that were not expanded, and $f,m,b$ are as in the cited entry.

A family $\mathrm{Fm}$ *satisfies the norm bound at 2 and 3* if there are real numbers $C$ and $x_0$ such that at every member with $x\ge x_0$ and $x>x_1$ the following holds. For every choice of three records at every prime, of a record $X$ with 14 components, one of which is a record $\rho$ as above, and of a real number $C_2$, if eight set-up conditions hold, then the norm bound at 2 and 3 holds for $(L,\rho)$ with constant $C$. Three of the set-up conditions were read: $x^{\varepsilon}\ge 8$; a natural number of the datum is at least $60$; and for every prime $p$ and every integer $e$ with $0<e<t$ the $\mathbb{Z}_p$-module $\mathrm{Ш}(p,e)$ has finite length. Here let $S_p$ be the set of places of $E$ above $p$, let $G_{E,S_p}$ be the quotient of the absolute Galois group of $E$ by the closed normal subgroup generated by the inertia groups at the finite places outside $S_p$, and let $\mathrm{Ш}(p,e)$ be the intersection of the kernels of the restriction maps from $H^1\bigl(G_{E,S_p},(\mathbb{Q}_p/\mathbb{Z}_p)(-e)\bigr)$ to a family of subgroups attached to the places in $S_p$, where $(-e)$ denotes the twist by the $(-e)$-th power of the $p$-adic cyclotomic character (the cohomology group and the family of subgroups were not expanded). The other five were not expanded.

**Attached records and selections.** An *object* $O$ over a datum $D$ consists of size data for $D$ and seven further records. A member $(x,i)$ of a family with $x>x_1$ gives a datum $D(x,i)$ and an object $O(x,i)$ over it. For a datum $D$, an object $O$ over it and a real number $c$, an *attached record over $(D,O,c)$* is a record with seven components; one is a pair of natural numbers $(p_1,p_2)$ such that $p_1$ equals a rational component of $D$ and $m\,p_2$ equals a real component of the size data, and the other six were not expanded. A *selection* on a class of families $\mathcal{A}$ assigns to every family $\mathrm{Fm}$ in $\mathcal{A}$ and every member $(x,i)$ with $x>x_1$ an attached record over $(D(x,i),O(x,i),C_{sz})$.

**Contexts and points.** A *context* $K$ is a record with 15 components, among them a scale, a table $\mathbf{C}$ of constants and two real numbers $c_D$, $c_h$. A *point* $\beta$ at a context $K$ is a record with 16 components. It contains a datum assembled in seven stages, a record $T$ of choices at places (a finite set of primes and, for each place in a set of places of the first stage, a finite set of primes of $E$, a natural number and an element of a set attached to $E$ that was not expanded), and a record with 21 components; it determines a datum $D_\beta$ and an object $O_\beta$. A *property of points* is a property of the context and of these components of a point. A *link* is a relation between a context $K$, a point $\beta$ at $K$, a real number $c$ and an attached record over $(D_\beta,O_\beta,c)$. None of these records was expanded beyond its list of components.

A *parameter tuple* $\kappa$ consists of a real number $\varepsilon$, a table of constants $\mathbf{C}$ and real numbers $c_D,c_h,C_5,c_6,C_b$. A context $K$ *has parameters $\kappa$* if its $\varepsilon$, $\mathbf{C}$, $c_D$, $c_h$ are those of $\kappa$ and a condition involving $C_5,c_6,C_b$ holds (not expanded). A family *matches $\kappa$* if its parameter is the $\varepsilon$ of $\kappa$, the numbers $C_5,c_6,C_b$ are positive, $\mathbf{C}$ satisfies a normalisation (not expanded), and the four lists of constants of the family agree with those formed from $\mathbf{C}$ (for the list $c_{\mathrm{out}}$, after one constant of the family is replaced by that of $\mathbf{C}$).

A property $p(\kappa,K)$ of parameter tuples and contexts holds *eventually* if there is $\varepsilon_0>0$ such that for all real $\varepsilon,C_8,C_5,c_6,C_b$ with $0<\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_0$, $\varepsilon<1/52$ and $C_8\ge 1$ there is a threshold $K_0$ such that for every normalised table of constants $\mathbf{C}$ with a prescribed entry equal to $C_8$ that is large for $K_0$ with another of its entries in the place of $C_H$ there is $c_1$ such that for every $c_D\ge c_1$ there is $c_2$ such that for every $c_h\ge c_2$: if $C_5,c_6,C_b>0$ then, writing $\kappa=(\varepsilon,\mathbf{C},c_D,c_h,C_5,c_6,C_b)$, there is $x_0$ such that $p(\kappa,K)$ holds for every context $K$ that has parameters $\kappa$ and scale $x\ge x_0$.

**Transfer.** Let $Q$ be a property of points, $\mathrm{Lk}$ a link, and $H(\mathcal{A},\sigma)$ a property of a class of families with a selection on it. We say that *$H$ holds for the families produced through $(Q,\mathrm{Lk})$* if there is a property $\mathcal{E}$ of attached records, depending on a parameter tuple, such that:

1. eventually, for every point $\beta$ at $K$ that satisfies $Q$, every real $c$ and every attached record $b$ over $(D_\beta,O_\beta,c)$ linked to $\beta$, the record $b$ has the property $\mathcal{E}$ for $\kappa$;
2. $H(\mathcal{A},\sigma)$ holds for every class $\mathcal{A}$ and every selection $\sigma$ on $\mathcal{A}$ with the following property: for every family $\mathrm{Fm}$ in $\mathcal{A}$ there is a parameter tuple $\kappa$ that $\mathrm{Fm}$ matches such that $\mathrm{Fm}$ satisfies a further condition (not expanded) and, for every member $(x,i)$ with $x>x_1$, the selected record $\sigma(\mathrm{Fm},x,i)$ has the property $\mathcal{E}$ for $\kappa$ and equals an attached record linked to some point $\beta$ that satisfies $Q$ in some context with parameters $\kappa$, with $D(x,i)=D_\beta$ and $O(x,i)=O_\beta$.

**The construction with parameter $c$.** The real number $c$ and the hypothesis ([Hypothesis: existence of a reciprocity map for GL\_n over the completions of number fields, with a restricted list of properties](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.RecTExists/)) determine a record of four construction parameters. Three of them are fixed choices. The fourth assigns to the components of a point, and to a record whose type depends on the hypothesis, a type of additional data (not expanded); this data includes the requirement that there be a record with parameters $(1,c,b)$, where $b=\exp(x^{\varepsilon})$ for the scale of the context, satisfying two conditions (not expanded). From this record the formalization defines two properties of points $Q^{\mathrm{built}}_c$ and $Q^{\mathrm{good}}_c$, the second implying the first, and a link $\mathrm{Lk}_c$; under $\mathrm{Lk}_c$ an attached record is linked to a point when the datum and object of the point are those of a point of the construction for which there is a witness record with 16 components, most of which are equations identifying components of the attached record with components of that point. These definitions were not expanded further.

**The condition $\mathrm{NB}(c)$.** There is a property of points $R$ such that:

- (A) for all contexts $K$ in the sense of the "eventually" above (without $C_5,c_6,C_b$), and for every first-stage record $B$ over $K$ with a record $T$ of choices at places that satisfies three largeness requirements and a base condition (none expanded), every completion of $B$ to the components of a point that satisfies $Q^{\mathrm{built}}_c$ satisfies $R$;
- (B) the following property $H$ holds for the families produced through $(Q^{\mathrm{good}}_c\text{ and }R,\ \mathrm{Lk}_c)$: for all sufficiently constrained families of the second refinement $\mathcal{A}''$ of $\mathcal{A}$, the family satisfies the norm bound at 2 and 3.
