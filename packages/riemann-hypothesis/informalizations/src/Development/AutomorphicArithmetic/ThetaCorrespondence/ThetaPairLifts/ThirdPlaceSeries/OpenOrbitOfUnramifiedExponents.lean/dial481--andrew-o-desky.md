## Twelve transfer conditions for the construction with a real parameter, given hypothesis list A and local reciprocity

[Theorem THETA.0016](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.Open0ProofU.open_orbit_of_unramified_exponents_alt/)

Assume the sixty statements of ([Hypothesis list A: sixty assumed statements on representations of p-adic and real groups, automorphic forms, Galois representations and class field theory](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.UnitaryGroupThirdPlaceHypotheses/)) and ([Hypothesis: existence of a reciprocity map for GL\_n over the completions of number fields, with a restricted list of properties](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.RecTExists/)). Then, for every real number $c$, all twelve open conditions in ([Transfer conditions and numerical bounds for the stage-zero construction](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.OpenParameterStatements/)) hold for the construction with parameter $c$ of ([The norm bound at the primes 2 and 3 for the families produced by the construction with a given parameter](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.RL.LinkedAt/)).
