## Hypotheses for the existence of built points with origin data

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D009](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.HypAAlt/)

The following are the hypotheses of the alternate criterion from multiplicity and origin hypotheses in ([Mass ratio of the standard Hermitian lattices over a CM field, and existence of built points and of their attached theta-lift records carrying a prescribed eventual property](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.StageOneU.member_bundles_large_unramified_exponents_alt/)). The vocabulary of contexts, bases, frames, twist data and eventuality is from ([Contexts, and statements that hold eventually in the parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.EvG/)); points and their attached records are from ([Points of the construction, properties of points, attached records and statements over families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.Row/)). A condition called *not expanded* below has not been translated into ordinary mathematics.

**Equality of multiplicities.** A realisation record determines, for each $i<m$, two finite-adelic groups attached to Hermitian spaces $Y_0$ and $W_{i+1}$ and a model at infinity for the latter. For every model at infinity for $Y_0$, every admitted isomorphism $\Phi$ of the finite-adelic groups, and every irreducible admissible representation $\sigma$ of the first group satisfying a fixed-vector and idempotent condition (not expanded), the hypothesis is $$m_{Y_0}(\sigma)=m_{W_{i+1}}(\sigma\circ\Phi^{-1}).$$ The multiplicity is the complex dimension, possibly an infinite cardinal, of the space of equivariant maps into the finite-adelic representation on a specified space of automorphic maps. An admitted $\Phi$ must be induced by a finite-adelic isometry $j$ of the Hermitian spaces, compatible with the specified frames up to an integral change of frame that is the identity at the distinguished finite set of places. The spaces of automorphic maps and the remaining conditions on $\sigma$ are not expanded. This is the equality in hypothesis 4 of ([Properties of the theta maps of a realised rational family: Hecke equivariance, inner product identities, rationality, and separation of Hecke types](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Closing3.primRows3_typesSep3_alt/)).

**First list.** There are 21 assumed propositions. Among them are the mass-ratio assertion in conclusion (i) of ([Mass ratio of the standard Hermitian lattices over a CM field, and existence of built points and of their attached theta-lift records carrying a prescribed eventual property](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.StageOneU.member_bundles_large_unramified_exponents_alt/)), the existence and uniqueness assertions below, and the following two statements:

- For every number field $K$ and nonzero ideal $\mathfrak q$, there is a finite abelian extension $L/K$, unramified away from $\mathfrak q$, whose Frobenius elements away from $\mathfrak q$ generate its Galois group, and for which an ideal $I$ coprime to $\mathfrak q$ is annihilated by all associated characters if and only if $I(\beta)=(\alpha)$ for a pair $(\alpha,\beta)$ satisfying a ray condition (not expanded).
- If $L/K$ is a ray field for the unit ideal and $K$ is totally complex, every ideal of $\mathcal O_K$ becomes principal in $\mathcal O_L$; the ray-field condition is not expanded.

For every size $n$, sign pattern and admissible real parameter, the existence assertion gives a representation satisfying the following condition: it acts on a nonzero complete complex Hilbert space, is unitary, irreducible and square-integrable, has a specified lowest type, and no type has smaller value of a specified norm. The uniqueness assertion says two such representations for the same data are unitarily intertwined on the corresponding unitary group. This uniqueness statement occurs twice in the list. Another assertion gives such representations an infinitesimal character, and another asserts finite dimensionality of a subspace cut out by two operators under stated hypotheses; the operators and those hypotheses are not expanded. The remaining individual propositions of this list are not expanded.

**Second list.** There are 25 assumed propositions. These include items (4), (34), (58), (59) and (60) of ([Hypothesis list A: sixty assumed statements on representations of p-adic and real groups, automorphic forms, Galois representations and class field theory](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.UnitaryGroupThirdPlaceHypotheses/)). They also include the assertion that, for a totally complex number field, an open subgroup of the finite adelic general linear group and a finite-dimensional continuous representation of the maximal compact group at infinity, the span of a specified set of automorphic forms is finite-dimensional; the definition of that set is not expanded. Another assertion says that a continuous de Rham representation over a finite extension of a $p$-adic field is potentially semistable, subject to the existence of a specified tied ring datum; those conditions are not expanded. Five of the 25 propositions recur as the first five conditions of the six-condition list below. The other propositions in this list are not expanded.

**Multiplicity and cover assumptions.** First, for every group datum in positive total rank, there is a family of representations indexed by admissible parameters, each satisfying a specified type condition, such that every representation in the stated class is unitarily equivalent to one of them; the representation class and type condition are not expanded. Assume also the fourteen statements of ([Fourteen statements from the literature on representations, automorphic forms and theta series, taken as hypotheses](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.PrimIn.PublishedThetaConditionTheorems/)) and the partial Hecke $L$-function statement (L2) in ([Properties of the theta maps of a realised rational family: Hecke equivariance, inner product identities, rationality, and separation of Hecke types](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Closing3.primRows3_typesSep3_alt/)).

Two further assumptions assert the equality of multiplicities above eventually for large bases, under exactness and the forced condition on the context, base and twist data. In the first, the column input must be pinned, and the realisation record must satisfy four conditions; in the second it must satisfy the starting conditions of ([Mass ratio of the standard Hermitian lattices over a CM field, and existence of built points and of their attached theta-lift records carrying a prescribed eventual property](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.StageOneU.member_bundles_large_unramified_exponents_alt/)) and the nine conditions in that entry, and the rationality test for the rows holds as well as the multiplicity equality. The four conditions in the first assertion are conditions (1), (2), (4), (5) of those nine; their contents are not expanded beyond that entry.

**A state with zero data.** Eventually for large exact bases satisfying the forced condition, every column input satisfying the starting conditions has preliminary data $P$ and a state $x$ over $P$ with an empty specified localisation index type and numbers $\eta_{j,v}=0$ for every $j,v$. If the twist data are untwisted, the set of places in $P$ is closed under the relation that two places lie over one place (the relation was not expanded). The preliminary data, state, localisation index and numbers are not expanded.

**Six further assumptions, relative to the reciprocity maps.** These are: (1) fourteen propositions, each assumed, whose individual statements are not expanded; (2) six propositions about the construction's realisations, of which the first five are also in the second list; their internal predicates are not expanded; (3) three comparisons, one asserting a continuity condition and the other two asserting existence of specified records, the latter one under an additional condition; their internal predicates are not expanded; (4) at every state and for every specified level record, every nonzero eligible row component at a good embedding admits a cuspidal parameter satisfying a condition not expanded; (5) at every state and for every specified level record, a property forced by automorphic semisimple data holds, with a consequent that is the conjunction of two conditions and an implication to four further conditions, all not expanded; (6) eventually for large exact bases, for every column input and compatible layer data for which two specified extension records exist and the input is pinned, every row with the prescribed local row data has the cuspidal-parameter property of (4). The quantification over states in (4) and (5), the level records and the compatibility conditions in (6) are not expanded.

**Multiplicity at constructed points.** Fix the reciprocity maps and a real parameter $c$ and form the construction with origin data for the alternate criterion from multiplicity and origin hypotheses in ([Mass ratio of the standard Hermitian lattices over a CM field, and existence of built points and of their attached theta-lift records carrying a prescribed eventual property](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.StageOneU.member_bundles_large_unramified_exponents_alt/)). The final hypothesis says that, eventually in the family parameters and for all sufficiently large compatible contexts, every point carrying a witness for this construction satisfies the equality of multiplicities above. The order of eventuality and compatibility is that of ([Points of the construction, properties of points, attached records and statements over families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.Row/)); the witness itself is not expanded.
