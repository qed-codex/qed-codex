## Linked arithmetic and theta realizations

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D205](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.TiedDataSKAlt/)

Use ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), ([Hecke characters in recursive realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Host.CharFrame/)), ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)), ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)) and ([Ideal-class representatives and unramified auxiliary fields](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.HostDatumWithTies/)). Write $K$ for the coefficient field and $E/F$ for the CM extension. Fix an algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb Q}$.

Hypothesis (linked choices). Choose the columns of ([Symmetric finite levels and column identifications](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Forms.FormsTiedSym/)), their full determinant and local comparison datum in ([Determinants, volumes and local column compatibility](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Herm.SymmetricHermitianColumns/)), and all twisted column conditions through ([Twisted terminal lattices and period values](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Forms.SymmetricTwistedTiltedData/)). The Hermitian data underlying the full datum are exactly the Hermitian data of these columns. Choose the underlying integral theta-input datum, natural bounds $N_0(i)$ and finite expansion-input subsets used in ([Integral twisting conditions for arithmetic families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.TwIn/)), and impose ([Compatibility of normalized Galois and theta data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.NTiedAllAlt/)) for these same choices. Impose the abstract oscillator and theta comparison of ([Abstract oscillator representations and theta maps](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Theta.AllTied/)) for the member's chosen theta datum and the chosen provision data.

Choose a predicate $C(\lambda,M)$ on the divisor realization's level labels $\lambda$ and natural congruence moduli $M$. Require ([Geometric realization and level compatibility](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Geo.GeoTiedP/)) for this predicate and the same divisor realization. Choose ([Group and rational comparison compatibility](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.G.GroupCompatibilityAll/)). Its initial Hermitian level and every local completion comparison are exactly those obtained from the full column datum. At every source its entire stage-zero coordinate datum is exactly the restriction of the symmetric coordinate datum: the prime-$T$ choices, initial class and value maps, split datum, its two quadratic-subfield generators, initial finite level, reductions and additive equivalences, raising/lowering laws, majorant norm and distinguished integral-subspace criterion all agree. These are equalities of the actual chosen data.

Hypothesis (common field embeddings). Choose embeddings $j_E:E\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathbb Q}$, $j_K:K\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathbb Q}$ and $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ with $\iota j_E$ and $\iota j_K$ equal to the prescribed embeddings. The embedding of $E$ in the normalized Galois frame of ([Compatibility of normalized Galois and theta data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.NTiedAllAlt/)) is exactly $j_E$. The finite set of level rational primes is exactly $$\{g_0,g_1,p_\delta,p'_\delta\}\cup\{T_u:u\text{ a source}\}\cup\{q_0\},$$ where, writing $M_E=Q_Sqd$, $r_0$ is the least rational prime not dividing $M_E(p_\delta-1)(p'_\delta-1)N(\mathfrak c)$ and $q_0$ is the least rational prime congruent to $1$ modulo $r_0M_E$ and not dividing $N(\mathfrak c)$. Here $\mathfrak c$ is the prescribed quadratic ideal of ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)).

Hypothesis (determinants and Hecke characters). Choose a surjective homomorphism $d:\mathbb A_E^\times\to D$ to the member's determinant group with open kernel. It is trivial on every single infinite-place idele. At every finite $F$-place and $a\in F^\times$, its value on the product of the single-place ideles of $a$ over all $E$-places above that place is $1$. If $d(x)=1$, the valuations of $x$ at every conjugate pair of finite $E$-places agree. If $a\in E^\times$, $z\bar z=1$ and $z\bar a=a$, then $d(a)$ is the member's prescribed determinant image of $z$. The subgroup $U_O\subset D$ is exactly the image of ideles that are units at every finite place. For every finite idele $x$ and $0\le j<m$, $$\widetilde\mu_{j+1}(d(x))=\mu_{0,j}(x),\qquad\chi_D(d(x))=\chi_\Psi(x).$$

The unramified values of $\mu_{0,j},\chi_\Psi,\delta,\delta'$ lie in the specified complex image of $K_0$. For $\eta$, its unramified value divided by the positive square root of the prime norm lies in that image.

Choose a bijection $c$ from the member's one-dimensional automorphic labels to all unitary Hecke characters of $E$. A local label is unramified precisely when its character is unramified, and at every such place its Frobenius eigenvalue multiset is the singleton inverse Hecke value. Twisting corresponds to multiplication under $c$. If the character's rational infinity type is $a_\tau$, its automorphic infinite eigenvalue multisets at $\tau$ and $\bar\tau$ are $\{a_\tau\}$ and $\{-a_\tau\}$. Conjugate self-duality is equivalent to $$\chi_{\bar P}(\bar a)=\chi_P(a)^{-1},\qquad\chi_\infty(\bar a)=\chi_\infty(a)^{-1}\quad(a\in E^\times).$$ At each local label's designated stage $j$, its specified one-dimensional character corresponds to $\mu_{0,j-1}\xi_\mu$. The designated $\eta$ and inverse-$\eta$ labels correspond to $\eta$ and $\eta^{-1}$. Every generator place lies above the chosen quadratic prime of its character frame.

Choose an injective homomorphism from the theta-character group to the Hecke characters of $E$, and a homomorphism from that group to circle-valued characters of the adelic E-algebra. Its image is exactly the unitary characters of rational infinity type, with infinity type equal to the theta-character's supplied function. The character at twist $\mu$ and stage $j$ maps to $\mu_{0,j-1}\xi_\mu$, and the theta $\eta$ character maps to $\eta$.

For every finite $E$-place $P$ choose an adelic idempotent $e_P$ supported at its underlying $F$-place, either the full place idempotent or one of two conjugate orthogonal idempotents summing to it. For every nonzero $a\in\mathcal O_E$, require $$a\in P\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\bigl(e_Pa^{-1}\bigr)_f\text{ is not integral}.$$ The theta idele character and the corresponding Hecke character agree on $1+e_P(a-1)$ for every $a\in E^\times$. They also agree on each infinite local idele: its finite part is $1$, every other designated infinite embedding is $1$, and its value at the selected embedding is that of $a$. This condition holds for every allowed infinite embedding family.

Hypothesis (Galois character comparisons). At every local Galois label $i$, its Hecke character $\psi_{w(i)}$ has the full tame, prime-power-order and exact-ramification conditions in ([Hecke characters in recursive realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Host.CharFrame/)), for the prescribed $\ell_i,Q_i$. At every finite $E$-place $P$ away from $\ell_i$ where this character is unramified, and every valuation of the chosen algebraic closure above $P$ and arithmetic Frobenius $\sigma$ at that valuation, require $$\iota_i(\psi_i^{\rm Gal}(\sigma))=\psi_{w(i)}(P)^{-1},$$ where $\iota_i$ is the specified embedding of its coefficient ring in $\mathbb C$. For every twist index $s$, $P\nmid\ell_s$ where $\delta_{\ell_s}$ is unramified, and every such Frobenius, there exists $a\in K_0$ such that $$\jmath(a)=\delta_{\ell_s}(P)^{-1},\qquad \delta_s^{\rm Gal}(\sigma)=\operatorname{emb}_{0,s}(a)$$ in the specified coefficient fraction field. For a nonbase coefficient-place twist $w$, every $P\notin Q_w$ away from $\ell_w$ and such Frobenius satisfy the selected-character comparison: if $\jmath(a)=\psi_w(P)^{-1}$ for $a\in K$, then the embedding of $a$ into the chosen extended coefficient fraction field equals the image of $\psi'_w(\operatorname{Art}_{Q_w}(\sigma))$ there. All coefficient-ring maps in these equations are the maps of ([Galois representations and coefficient twists](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NData/)) and ([Twist period lattices and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NChoice/)).

Hypothesis (coefficient ring and row weights). Use the same five chosen auxiliary fields and their specified composite $K_s^{\rm alg}$ in ([Ideal-class representatives and unramified auxiliary fields](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.HostDatumWithTies/)). The coefficient-ring map $O\to K$ is injective, and the image of $O$ under $j_K$ is exactly the elements of $K_s^{\rm alg}$ integral over $\mathbb Z$.

For $1\le j\le m$, write $p_j=k+j-1$, $q_j=m-j$, $n_V=r+t$, and use zero-based array indices. Put $e=e_{j-1}(\tau)$ and $$s_\tau(i)=\begin{cases}v_\tau(i),&i<k-1,\\-b_\tau(i-k+1),&k-1\le i<k+m-1,\\0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}$$ The recorded integral row weights are exactly $$\kappa^+_{j,\tau,i}=s_\tau(i)+e-\frac{n_V-1-2i}{2}-\frac{p_j-q_j}{2}\quad(0\le i<p_j),$$ $$\kappa^-_{j,\tau,c}=-b_\tau(j+c)+e+\frac{t+1+2c}{2}\quad(0\le c<q_j).$$ For every source $u$ and twist $\mu$, if a scalar $c$ satisfies $$\operatorname{per}_{u,\mu}(\phi,F)=cB_u\bigl(\phi,\iota_\mu(\operatorname{pr}_{Q_\mu}F)\bigr)$$ for every source vector and every selected input $F$, and the left side is nonzero for some such pair, then the recorded source-period factor is $c$. Here $Q_\mu$ is the orthogonally projected subspace in ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)).

Hypothesis (top-period metrics). Choose conjugate-linear maps from each base or twisted top input space to its recorded space of coefficient-valued row functions, as in ([Column chains and period pairings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Member/)) and ([Integral forms on column representations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.MemberForms/)). At every source the base top-period embedding has inner product equal to the prescribed top coefficient pairing after its coordinate embedding, with the two arguments reversed. The same identity holds at every nonbase twist for its twisted top-period embedding and twisted coordinate coefficient pairing.

Hypothesis (kernel frames in self-dual coordinates). Choose ([Compatible kernel realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Ker.KerEntTied/)) with these same arithmetic, twist and theta data. For every source $u$ and $i\le m$ choose $C_{u,i}\in\mathrm{GL}_{N_i}(E)$ such that $$C_{u,i}^*C_{u,i}=G_i,\qquad x\in L_{0,i}\iff C_{u,i}x\in\mathcal O_E^{N_i}.$$ Here $L_{0,i}$ is the fixed reference lattice in the recorded frame. Every local kernel constant uses its sharp choice. If $eY_{u,\mu,i}$ is the twisted frame identification of ([Twisted coordinate values and local operators](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Forms.TwAtT/)), require the kernel realization's frame at every $u,\mu,i,y,\tau$ to be $$\operatorname{frm}_{u,\mu,i}(y)_\tau=\tau(C_{u,i})\,eY_{u,\mu,i}(y)_\tau.$$
