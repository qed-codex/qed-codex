## Placewise inequalities for sufficiently constrained families, given twelve assumptions

[Theorem ARAK.0018](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.BigOn.ontoX_of_hypotheses_alt_alt/)

Assume items (1)–(9), (35) and (37) of ([Hypothesis list A: sixty assumed statements on representations of p-adic and real groups, automorphic forms, Galois representations and class field theory](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.UnitaryGroupThirdPlaceHypotheses/)), and assume ([Hypothesis: existence of a reciprocity map for GL\_n over the completions of number fields, with a restricted list of properties](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.RecTExists/)).

Then the first numerical statement under **Two numerical statements** in ([Transfer conditions and numerical bounds for the stage-zero construction](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.S0.OpenParameterStatements/)) holds.
