## Dirichlet nonvanishing on and to the right of one

[Theorem ANT.1744](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Deduction.charFamily_LFunction_ne_zero_of_one_le_re/)

Let $\chi$ be a Dirichlet character of positive integer modulus $N$, and write $L(s,\chi)$ for its Dirichlet $L$-function. Assume that $\chi$ is nonprincipal or that $N=1$. Then the meromorphic function $L(s,\chi)$ has no zeros in the closed half-plane $\Re s\ge1$.
