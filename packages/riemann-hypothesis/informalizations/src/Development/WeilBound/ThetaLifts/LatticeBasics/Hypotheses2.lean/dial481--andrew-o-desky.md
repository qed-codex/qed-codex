## Siegel Eisenstein sums on the doubled group of a Hermitian datum: frames, standard compact subgroups, height functions, sections

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D023](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.ThK.Dbl.G2/)

This page defines the Eisenstein sums attached to the Siegel parabolic subgroup of the unitary group of a doubled space, and the objects from which they are formed. The number fields $F$ and $E$, the homomorphism $\sigma\colon E\to E$, the adele ring $\mathbb A=F_\infty\times\mathbb A_f$ of $F$, the ring $\mathbb A_E=\mathbb A\otimes_FE$, base data with their ring homomorphisms $\iota_w\colon\mathbb A_E\to\mathbb C$, and Hermitian data are those of ([Setting for theta lifts between unitary groups of a CM extension: base data, Hermitian data, Weil representations, theta integrals](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.WeilTensorModels.Base.IsGood/)). Throughout, base data are fixed and $Y$ is a Hermitian datum of dimension $n$; of $Y$, the definitions use only its matrix $J_Y\in M_n(E)$.

**Characters and absolute values.** A *Hecke character* is a continuous homomorphism $\chi\colon\mathbb A_E^\times\to S^1$ that is trivial on $E^\times$. The function $\eta\colon\mathbb A^\times\to\{\pm1\}$ takes the value $1$ on $F^\times N_{\mathbb A_E/\mathbb A}(\mathbb A_E^\times)$ and the value $-1$ on every other idele of $F$. For $z\in\mathbb A_E^\times$ put $$|z|_E=\prod_w\big\|N_{E_w/F_w}(z_w)\big\|_w\cdot\prod_v\big|N_{E_v/F_v}(z_v)\big|_v .$$ Here $w$ runs over the infinite places and $v$ over the finite places of $F$; $E_w=F_w\otimes_FE$ and $E_v=F_v\otimes_FE$, and $z_w$, $z_v$ are the components of $z$ in these rings; $\|\cdot\|_w$ is the absolute value of $F_w$ if $F_w=\mathbb R$ and its square if $F_w=\mathbb C$; and $|\cdot|_v$ is the absolute value of $F_v$ normalized by $|\varpi_v|_v=N(v)^{-1}$ for a uniformizer $\varpi_v$. All but finitely many factors of the second product are $1$.

**The doubled group and its Siegel parabolic subgroup.** Put $\tilde J_Y=\mathrm{diag}(J_Y,-J_Y)\in M_{2n}(E)$. The *doubled group* of $Y$ is $$G=\{g\in GL_{2n}(\mathbb A_E):\ g^{\mathsf T}\,\tilde J_Y\,\sigma(g)=\tilde J_Y\},$$ and $G(F)$ is its subgroup of the elements with entries in $E$. Let $\Delta$ be the $2n\times n$ matrix that consists of two blocks $1_n$, one above the other; it satisfies $\Delta^{\mathsf T}\tilde J_Y\,\sigma(\Delta)=0$. The *Siegel parabolic subgroup* is $$\mathcal P=\{p\in G:\ p\Delta=\Delta t\ \text{for some }t\in GL_n(\mathbb A_E)\}.$$ The matrix $t$ is determined by $p$ and is written $t_p$. Put $\mathcal P(F)=\mathcal P\cap G(F)$.

**Frames and standard compact subgroups.** A *frame* for $Y$ is a matrix $b\in GL_{2n}(E)$ with the following two properties:

- $b^{\mathsf T}\,\tilde J_Y\,\sigma(b)=\begin{pmatrix}0&1_n\\ \varepsilon 1_n&0\end{pmatrix}$ for a sign $\varepsilon\in\{1,-1\}$;
- the matrix formed by the first $n$ columns of $b$ is $\Delta t_0$ for some $t_0\in GL_n(E)$.

Let $\widehat{\mathcal O}_E\subset\mathbb A_E$ be the additive group generated by the products $a\,o$, where $o\in\mathcal O_E$ and $a\in\mathbb A$ has component $0$ in $F_\infty$ and component in $\mathcal O_v$ at every finite place $v$. The *finite part* of $z\in\mathbb A_E$ is the product of $z$ with the adele $(0,1)\in F_\infty\times\mathbb A_f$. The *standard compact subgroup for a frame $b$* is the set $K$ of all $k\in G$ such that

- for every infinite place $w$ of $F$, the complex matrix $\iota_w(b^{-1}kb)$ is unitary;
- the finite parts of all entries of $b^{-1}kb$ and of $b^{-1}k^{-1}b$ lie in $\widehat{\mathcal O}_E$.

It is a subgroup of $G$.

**Height functions.** Let $K$ be a subgroup of $G$. A *height function for $K$* is a function $a\colon G\to\mathbb R$ such that every element of $G$ is a product $pk$ with $p\in\mathcal P$ and $k\in K$, and $$a(pk)=|\det t_p|_E\qquad(p\in\mathcal P,\ k\in K).$$

**Sections.** Let $\chi\colon\mathbb A_E^\times\to S^1$ be a homomorphism, $K$ a subgroup of $G$ and $s_1\in\mathbb C$. A *section at $s_1$ for $\chi$ and $K$* is a continuous function $f\colon G\to\mathbb C$ such that $$f(px)=\chi(\det t_p)\,|\det t_p|_E^{\,s_1+n/2}\,f(x)\qquad(p\in\mathcal P,\ x\in G),$$ and such that the functions $x\mapsto f(xk)$, $k\in K$, span a finite-dimensional complex vector space. For a height function $a$ for $K$ and $s\in\mathbb C$ put $$f_s(x)=a(x)^{s-s_1}f(x)\qquad(x\in G).$$

**Eisenstein sums.** Fix a set of representatives of the cosets $\mathcal P(F)\gamma$, $\gamma\in G(F)$. For a function $\phi\colon G\to\mathbb C$ and $x\in G$, the *Eisenstein sum of $\phi$ at $x$* is $$\sum_{\gamma\in\mathcal P(F)\backslash G(F)}\phi(\gamma x),$$ the sum over these representatives.
