## The Siegel-Weil formula for a definite Hermitian space, the continuation of Siegel Eisenstein series and a modulus identity imply the agreement of theta integrals and local matching

[Theorem THETA.1.0025](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.ThKR.theoremK_head2_of_mod/)

Assume the following three hypotheses.

1. ([Hypothesis: an index identity for the stabilizer of a Lagrangian subspace in a unitary group at an inert place](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.Sph.ModSt/)).
2. ([Hypothesis: meromorphic continuation of Siegel Eisenstein sums on the doubled group, with values in functions of moderate growth](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.ThK.Dbl.EisBL/)).
3. ([Hypothesis: the Siegel-Weil formula on the doubled group for a definite space, with constant 1 or 2](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.ThK.Dbl.SiegelWeilStd/)).

Then the following two statements hold.

(a) ([Theta integrals agree: for a matching datum the two theta integrals are proportional on matching functions, with a unique nonzero constant, and the constant changes by an L-value when a hyperbolic plane is removed](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.WeilTensorModels.ThetaIntegralsAgree/)).

(b) ([Local matching: a matching datum for two Hermitian spaces of complementary dimensions admits matched quotient maps](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.WeilTensorModels.LocalMatching/)).

Each of the five is the statement of its page in full: it is asserted for all number fields $F$ and $E$ and for all the data that the page names.
