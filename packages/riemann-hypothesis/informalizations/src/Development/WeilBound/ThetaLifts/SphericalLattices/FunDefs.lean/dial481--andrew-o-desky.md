## Hypothesis: an index identity for the stabilizer of a Lagrangian subspace in a unitary group at an inert place

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D050](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.Sph.ModSt/)

The hypothesis of this page is an identity between two indices of subgroups of a unitary group at one finite place. The adele ring $\mathbb A$ of $F$, the ring $\mathbb A_E=\mathbb A\otimes_FE$, elements supported at a finite place, places inert in $E$, admissible base data, skew-Hermitian data $W$ with their matrix $J_W$ and lattice $L_W$, and the sets $U(W)_v\subset U(W)(\mathbb A)$ are those of ([Setting for theta lifts between unitary groups of a CM extension: base data, Hermitian data, Weil representations, theta integrals](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.WeilTensorModels.Base.IsGood/)).

**Setting.** Let $F$ and $E$ be number fields, $E$ an $F$-algebra, and $\sigma\colon E\to E$ a homomorphism of $F$-algebras, and let base data admissible for $\sigma$ be given. Let $W$ be a Hermitian datum of dimension $d$ that is skew-Hermitian, of any signature, and let $v$ be a finite place of $F$ that is inert in $E$. Put $E_v=F_v\otimes_FE$; for a matrix $t$ over $\mathbb A_E$, $t_v$ denotes its image over $E_v$.

Let $L_v\subset\mathbb A_E^d$ be the additive group generated by the vectors $a\,y$, where $y\in L_W$ and $a\in\mathbb A$ is supported at $v$ with $a_v\in\mathcal O_v$.

For an integer $m\ge0$, a matrix $C\in M_{d\times m}(\mathbb A_E)$ is *Lagrangian at $v$* if

- $d=2m$;
- $C$ is supported at $v$;
- $C^{\mathsf T}J_W\,\sigma(C)=0$;
- $C\xi\ne0$ for every nonzero $\xi\in\mathbb A_E^m$ that is supported at $v$.

**Hypothesis (index identity).** In this setting, let $C\in M_{d\times m}(\mathbb A_E)$ be Lagrangian at $v$, and suppose that there is a matrix $C'\in M_{d\times m}(\mathbb A_E)$, also Lagrangian at $v$, such that every element of $L_v$ is a sum $C\xi+C'\xi'$ with $\xi,\xi'\in\mathbb A_E^m$, $C\xi\in L_v$ and $C'\xi'\in L_v$. Let $$P=\{p\in U(W)_v:\ pC=Ct\ \text{and}\ p^{-1}C=Ct'\ \text{for some }t,t'\in M_m(\mathbb A_E)\},$$ $$K=\{k\in U(W)_v:\ kL_v\subset L_v\ \text{and}\ k^{-1}L_v\subset L_v\},$$ which are subgroups of $U(W)(\mathbb A)$, and put $P_K=P\cap K$. Let $p\in P$, let $t\in M_m(\mathbb A_E)$ be a matrix with $pC=Ct$, and suppose that $u=N_{E_v/F_v}(\det t_v)$ is not $0$. Then $$[P_K:P_K\cap p^{-1}P_Kp]=|u|_v^{\,m}\,[P_K:P_K\cap pP_Kp^{-1}].$$
