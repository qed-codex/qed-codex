## Ampleness after adding a positive diagonal twist

[Theorem AG.1721](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.KAmp.kAmp/)

Let $F$ be a field, $K$ a finite linearly ordered set, and $(I_k)_{k\in K}$ finite linearly ordered coordinate sets. Let $V$ be a relevant multihomogeneous prime ideal in $F[x_{k,i}]$ such that $$\dim F[x_{k,i}]/V=d+|K|.$$ Fix an integer-valued intersection family $I$ satisfying the field-intersection axioms: on zero-dimensional projective varieties it gives the degree of an affine chart, and intersection with a rational section is computed by its divisor cycle.

For $\alpha\in\mathbb Z^K$, assume $I(W;\alpha)\ge0$ for every projective integral curve defined by a relevant multihomogeneous prime $W\supseteq V$. For integers $b,a\ge1$, put $N=b\alpha+a\mathbf1$, where $\mathbf1_k=1$. Then there exists an integer $q\ge1$ such that $\mathcal O(qN)$ on the multiprojective scheme defined by $V$ is generated by global sections and, for every $e\in\mathbb Z^K$, $$H^1(\mathcal O(e+cqN))=0\qquad\text{for all sufficiently large integers }c.$$ Here generation and cohomology use the standard multihomogeneous coordinate charts and their Čech complex.
