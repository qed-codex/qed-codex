## Vanishing from all unipotent Fourier coefficients

[Theorem THETA.1152](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.SiegelWeilFormula.kfour.fourierZero/)

Let $F,E$ be number fields with $E$ an $F$-algebra and $\sigma\in\operatorname{Aut}_F(E)$. Choose one of the source's doubled Weil-model data, with second group $G$, additive character $\psi$, and doubled frame. Let $N$ be the additive subgroup of adelic matrices occurring as upper blocks in $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&\beta\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ in this frame, and let $N_F$ be its subgroup generated by rational matrices. Choose a map $n:N\to G$ with precisely these frame matrices, an additive Haar measure $\lambda$ on $N$, and a fundamental domain $\mathcal F$ for $N_F$ of measure one. Let $f:G\to\mathbb C$ be continuous and satisfy $f(n(\beta)g)=f(g)$ for $\beta\in N_F$. If $$\int_{\mathcal F}f(n(\beta)g)\psi(-\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb A_E/\mathbb A_F}\operatorname{tr}(T\beta))\,d\lambda(\beta)=0\qquad(T\in N_F,\ g\in G),$$ then $f=0$.
