## Resolution in characteristic zero

[Theorem AG.0628](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.Resplug.ResolutionBlowup.resolutionCharZero/)

Let $X$ be a reduced separated scheme of finite type over a field $k$ of characteristic zero. There is a finite composition of blow-ups $\pi:X'\to X$ such that $X'$ is smooth over $k$. If $X_{\mathrm{sm}}$ is the smooth locus, then $\pi$ restricts to an isomorphism $$\pi^{-1}(X_{\mathrm{sm}})\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }X_{\mathrm{sm}}.$$ The reduced inverse image of $X\setminus X_{\mathrm{sm}}$ is a simple normal-crossing divisor.
