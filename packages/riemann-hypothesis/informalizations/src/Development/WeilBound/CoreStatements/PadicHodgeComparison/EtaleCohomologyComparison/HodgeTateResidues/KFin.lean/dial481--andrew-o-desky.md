## Finiteness of graded infinitesimal cohomology

[Theorem AG.1216](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/CrystallineSemistableComparison.HT.kFin/)

Let $K$ be a field of characteristic zero and let $X$ be a smooth proper scheme over $K$. For all integers $a,n\ge0$, the $K$-vector space $H^n_a(X/K)$ of ([Graded infinitesimal cohomology](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/CrystallineSemistableComparison.HT.Hdg/)) is finite-dimensional, and $H^n_a(X/K)=0$ when $n<a$.
