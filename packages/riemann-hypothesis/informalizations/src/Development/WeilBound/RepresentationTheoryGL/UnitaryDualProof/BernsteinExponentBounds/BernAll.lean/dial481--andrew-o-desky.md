## Irreducibility of unitary parabolic induction

[Theorem REP.1333](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.RgUd.All.bernsteinUnitaryProductIrreducible/)

Let $k$ be a nonarchimedean local field. Let $\pi_1,\pi_2$ be smooth irreducible unitarizable complex representations of $\mathrm{GL}_r(k)$ and $\mathrm{GL}_t(k)$. Their normalized parabolic induction $$\pi_1\times\pi_2=\operatorname{Ind}_{P_{r,t}}^{\mathrm{GL}_{r+t}(k)}(\pi_1\boxtimes\pi_2)$$ is irreducible, where $P_{r,t}$ is the standard parabolic subgroup with Levi $\mathrm{GL}_r\times\mathrm{GL}_t$, and the induction includes the square root of its modulus character.
