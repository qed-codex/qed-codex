## The double commutant of an adjoint-closed family

[Theorem REP.0375](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.Rg.Sw.LC.bicomm/)

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional complex vector space with a positive definite Hermitian form $h$, linear in its second argument. Let $(T_i)_{i\in I}$ be a family of endomorphisms such that for every $i$ there is $j$ with $$h(T_iv,w)=h(v,T_jw)\qquad(v,w\in V).$$ If $x\in\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb C}(V)$ commutes with every endomorphism that commutes with all the $T_i$, then $x$ belongs to the unital complex algebra generated by the $T_i$.
