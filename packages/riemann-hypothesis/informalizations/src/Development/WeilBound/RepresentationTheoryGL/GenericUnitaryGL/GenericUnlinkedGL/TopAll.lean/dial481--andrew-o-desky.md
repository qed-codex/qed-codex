## A generic representation is determined by its cuspidal support

[Theorem REP.1422](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.RgGu.genericUnique/)

Let $k$ be a nonarchimedean local field with its valuation and topology, let $N\ge0$, and let $\mathfrak s$ be a finite multiset of smooth irreducible complex representations of general linear groups over $k$. Let $\pi$ and $\sigma$ be complex representations of $\operatorname{GL}_N(k)$. Suppose both have cuspidal support $\mathfrak s$ in the following sense: they are smooth irreducible subquotients of normalized parabolic inductions of the cuspidal representations in this multiset. If both admit nonzero Whittaker functionals for nondegenerate characters of the standard maximal unipotent subgroup, then $$\pi\simeq\sigma.$$
