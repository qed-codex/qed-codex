## A reducible symmetric twist of a cuspidal representation

[Theorem REP.1408](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.RgGu.NR.someRed_of/)

Let $k$ be a nonarchimedean local field. Assume the source's five complementary-series input assertions for $k$. If $\pi$ is a smooth irreducible cuspidal complex representation of $\mathrm{GL}_d(k)$ which is unitarisable, there is a real $s\ge0$ such that $$\bigl(\pi\otimes|\det|^{-s}\bigr)\times\bigl(\pi\otimes|\det|^s\bigr)$$ is reducible, where $\times$ is normalized parabolic induction to $\mathrm{GL}_{2d}(k)$.
