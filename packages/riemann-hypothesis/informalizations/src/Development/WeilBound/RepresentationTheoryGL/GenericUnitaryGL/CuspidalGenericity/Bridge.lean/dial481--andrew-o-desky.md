## Genericity of irreducible cuspidal representations

[Theorem REP.1534](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.BZSeg.Cusp.cuspGeneric/)

Let $k$ be a nonarchimedean local field, $N>0$, and $\pi$ a smooth irreducible cuspidal complex representation of $\operatorname{GL}_N(k)$. Then $\pi$ is generic: it admits a nonzero Whittaker functional for a continuous nontrivial additive character of $k$.
