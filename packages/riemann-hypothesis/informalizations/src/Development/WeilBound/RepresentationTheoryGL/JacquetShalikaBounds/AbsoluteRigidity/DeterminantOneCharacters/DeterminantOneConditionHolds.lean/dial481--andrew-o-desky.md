## Absolute value of the product of unitary Satake parameters

[Theorem AGR.1369](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.RgJS.determinant_one_condition_holds/)

Let $E$ be a totally complex number field and let $\pi$ be a cuspidal automorphic representation of $\operatorname{GL}_m(\mathbb A_E)$, $m\ge0$. Suppose $\pi$ is unitary and has unramified Satake multiset $B_w\subset\mathbb C^\times$ at a finite place $w$. Then $$\left|\prod_{\alpha\in B_w}\alpha\right|=1.$$
