## A finite-chain and maximal-subspace condition for real theta representations

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D076](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Published.HoweDualityReal/)

Let $E/F$ be a CM extension of number fields with conjugation $c$, and put $A=\mathbb A_F$, $A_\infty=F\otimes_{\mathbb Q}\mathbb R$ and $B=A\otimes_FE$. Extend $c$ to $B$ by $1\otimes c$. Let $n,d\in\mathbb N$. Choose $X,W$ with Gram matrices $J_X\in M_n(E)$ and $J_W\in M_d(E)$ satisfying $$c((J_X)_{ji})=(J_X)_{ij},\qquad c((J_W)_{ji})=-(J_W)_{ij},\qquad \det J_X\ne0,\quad\det J_W\ne0.$$ Their auxiliary data consist of arbitrary $\mathcal O_E$-submodules of $E^n,E^d$, respectively, complex matrices $\Theta_{X,v},\Theta_{W,v}$ at every infinite place $v$ of $F$, and invertible matrices of sizes $n,d$ over $F_v$ at every finite place $v$. Write $U_X(A)$ and $U_W(A)$ for the groups of invertible matrices over $B$ satisfying $g^{\mathsf T}J\,c(g)=J$, with $J=J_X,J_W$ respectively, embedded entrywise in $B$.

A Schrödinger model $\mathcal M$ for this pair includes an $A$-linear isomorphism $$M_{n,d}(B)\longrightarrow A^{n\times d}\oplus A^{n\times d},\qquad u\longmapsto(q(u),p(u)),$$ satisfying, for every $u,v\in M_{n,d}(B)$, $$\operatorname{Tr}_{B/A}\!\left(-\sum_{i,j,k,l}u_{ij}(J_X)_{ik}(J_W)_{jl}c(v_{kl})\right)=q(u)\cdot p(v)-q(v)\cdot p(u).$$ It also includes a function $(F_v)^{n\times d}\to\mathbb C$ at every finite place, a measure on $U_X(A)$ and a subset of $U_X(A)$.

Let $\psi:(A,+)\to\mathbb C^\times$ be a continuous group homomorphism trivial on $F$, with infinite restriction $$\psi(a_\infty,0)=\exp\!\left(-2\pi\mathrm i\,\operatorname{Tr}_{A_\infty/\mathbb R}(a_\infty)\right)\qquad(a_\infty\in A_\infty).$$ Let $\chi_X,\chi_W:B^\times\to\mathbb S^1$ be continuous group homomorphisms trivial on $E^\times$. Let $\iota:A^\times\to B^\times$ be induced by $A\to B$, let $\operatorname{Nm}:B^\times\to A^\times$ be the norm homomorphism, and let $H\subseteq A^\times$ be the subgroup generated by the diagonally embedded $F^\times$ and $\operatorname{Nm}(B^\times)$. Require, for every $a\in A^\times$, $$\chi_X(\iota a)=\begin{cases}1,&a\in H,\\(-1)^n,&a\notin H,\end{cases}\qquad \chi_W(\iota a)=\begin{cases}1,&a\in H,\\(-1)^d,&a\notin H.\end{cases}$$ Let $\omega$ be a Weil action in $\mathcal M$ for $(\psi,\chi_X,\chi_W)$ on the complex functions on $A^{n\times d}$.

For each infinite place $v$ choose an embedding $\tau_v:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ extending its fixed embedding of $F$. Require $\Theta_{X,v}$ and $\Theta_{W,v}$ to be Cartan involutions for $\tau_v(J_X)$ and $\mathrm i\tau_v(J_W)$, respectively. For a matrix $Q$, this means $$\Theta^2=1,\qquad\Theta^{\mathsf T}Q\bar\Theta=Q,\qquad \operatorname{Re}\bigl((\Theta z)^{\mathsf T}Q\bar z\bigr)>0\quad(z\ne0).$$

Let $\mathcal P$ be the associated polynomial subspace of the archimedean Schwartz space on $(F\otimes_{\mathbb Q}\mathbb R)^{n\times d}$. Consider the complex subspaces $N\subseteq\mathcal P$ which contain every infinitesimal image of $\mathcal P$ under the first group and every difference $kf-f$ with $f\in\mathcal P$ and $k$ in the first group’s compact subgroup, and which are stable under the infinitesimal and compact actions of the second group.

The finite-chain and maximal-subspace condition is the following assertion for every choice of the data above, including the embeddings $\tau_v$. There is $L\in\mathbb N$ such that, for every $m\in\mathbb N$ and every strictly increasing chain $N_0\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq N_{m-1}$ of these subspaces, $m\le L$. For any two such subspaces $N,N'$ with $N\ne\mathcal P$ and $N'\ne\mathcal P$, one has $N+N'\ne\mathcal P$.
