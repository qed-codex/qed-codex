## Extension of semiabelian homomorphisms over a discrete valuation ring

[Theorem AV.0665](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.HomExtExamples.homExtendsOverDVR_holds/)

Let $O$ be a discrete valuation domain with fraction field $K$, and let $G,G'$ be semiabelian group schemes over $\operatorname{Spec}O$. Every homomorphism of group schemes $f:G_K\to G'_K$ extends uniquely to a homomorphism $F:G\to G'$ over $O$.
