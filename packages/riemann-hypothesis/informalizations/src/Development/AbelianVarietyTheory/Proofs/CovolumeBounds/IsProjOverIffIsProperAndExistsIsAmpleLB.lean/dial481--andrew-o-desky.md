## Projectivity over an affine base and an ample line bundle

[Theorem AG.1442](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.isProjOver_iff_isProper_and_exists_isAmpleLB/)

Let $R$ be any commutative ring and let $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ be a scheme morphism. Then $$f\text{ is projective}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f\text{ is proper and }X\text{ admits an ample line bundle}.$$
