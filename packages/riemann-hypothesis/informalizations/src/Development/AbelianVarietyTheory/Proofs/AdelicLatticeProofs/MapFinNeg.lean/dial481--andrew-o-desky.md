## Existence of a nonzero adelic lattice and Fock vacuum

[Theorem THETA.1567](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.VacuumO.exists_ne_zero/)

Fix the source's complete global theta datum, with CM fields $E/F$, a distinguished element $\xi\in E$, a multiplier set $A\subset E$, and the prescribed archimedean embeddings. Let $S,T$ be Hermitian spaces in this datum, of ranks $s,t$. Choose self-dual $\mathcal O_E$-lattices $L_S\subset E^s$, $L_T\subset E^t$, where self-duality means $y\in L_U$ if and only if $\bar x^tJ_Uy\in\mathcal O_E$ for every $x\in L_U$, for $U=S,T$.

Put $W=M_{t,s}(\mathbb A_F\otimes_FE)$ and $\Gamma=M_{t,s}(E)$. Use the alternating form $$B(w,w')=\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb A_F\otimes E/\mathbb A_F}\left(\xi\sum_{a,b,c,d}(J_S^{-1})_{ab}\overline{w'_{cb}}(J_T)_{cd}w_{da}\right).$$ Let $\psi$ be a continuous unitary nontrivial character of $\mathbb A_F/F$ whose infinite part is $\exp(-2\pi i\sum_\tau\tau(x))$, and choose a compatible family of infinite embeddings. Define $$(R_uf)(w)=\psi(B(w,u)/2)f(w+u).$$ Let $\Lambda\subset W$ be the additive subgroup generated by finite-adelic matrices $$\alpha\,a\,y\bar x^tJ_S,\qquad\alpha\in\widehat{\mathcal O}_F,\quad a\in A,\quad x\in L_S,\quad y\in L_T,$$ with zero infinite component. Choose Cartan involutions $s_{S,\tau},s_{T,\tau}$ with $s_{U,\tau}^2=1$, $s_{U,\tau}^*J_{U,\tau}s_{U,\tau}=J_{U,\tau}$ and $J_{U,\tau}s_{U,\tau}$ positive definite.

There is a nonzero function $f:W\to\mathbb C$ such that every scalar multiple of $f$ has all the following properties. It is continuous, smooth along every finite-dimensional real archimedean translation, and satisfies $$f(\gamma+w)=\psi(-B(\gamma,w)/2)f(w)\quad(\gamma\in\Gamma).$$ There is an open additive subgroup $U\subset W$ such that $R_uf=f$ for all $u\in U$ with zero infinite component. Also $R_{2u}f=f$ for all $u\in\Lambda$, and $R_uf=f$ whenever $u\in\Lambda$ and $eu=u$, where $e^2=e$, $e\bar e=0$ and $e+\bar e$ is the idempotent supported at one finite place of $F$. Finally, at every infinite embedding $\tau$, $$\bigl(dR_\tau(z)+i\,dR_\tau(i s_{T,\tau}zs_{S,\tau})\bigr)f=0\qquad(z\in M_{t,s}(\mathbb C)),$$ where $dR_\tau(z)$ is the derivative at zero of the Heisenberg translation in direction $z$ at $\tau$.
