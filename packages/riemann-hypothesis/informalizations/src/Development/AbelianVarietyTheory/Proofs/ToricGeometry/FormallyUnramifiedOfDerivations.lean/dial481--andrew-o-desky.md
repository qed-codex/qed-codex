## Very ample powers from theta uniformization and algebraic charts

[Theorem AV.1518](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.thetaBundle_veryAmpleAt/)

Let $g\ge0$, $\tau=\tau^t\in M_g(\mathbb C)$ with $\operatorname{Im}\tau>0$, and $\Lambda=\tau\mathbb Z^g+\mathbb Z^g$. Write $e(x)=e^{2\pi ix}$ and $$\theta[a,b](z,\tau)=\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z^g}e\!\left(\tfrac12(m+a)^t\tau(m+a)+(m+a)^t(z+b)\right).$$ Fix an even integer $N\ge3$ and put $\Theta_c(z)=\theta[c/N,0](Nz,N\tau)$ for $c\in\{0,\ldots,N-1\}^g$. Let $X/\mathbb C$ be proper and integral, with a surjection $q:\mathbb C^g\to X(\mathbb C)$ satisfying $q(z)=q(z')$ exactly when $z-z'\in\Lambda$. Require the following algebraic-chart conditions. Pullback of every regular function along $q$ is holomorphic on its open inverse image. There are affine opens $D_c$ covering $X$, of coordinate-ring Krull dimension at most $g$, whose inverse images are $\{\Theta_c\ne0\}$, and regular functions $r_{c,c'}$ on $D_c$ pulling back to $\Theta_{c'}/\Theta_c$ and generating its coordinate ring as a $\mathbb C$-algebra.

For $l\ge0$, let $\mathcal T_l$ be the entire functions satisfying $$f(z+\tau n+n')=e\!\left(-l(\tfrac12 n^t\tau n+n^tz)\right)f(z)\qquad(n,n'\in\mathbb Z^g).$$ Assume that whenever $P,Q\in\mathcal T_{Nd}$ for $d\ge0$, there is an open subset of $X$ with inverse image $\{Q\ne0\}$ on which $P/Q$ is a regular function. Equip $X$ with an algebraic group structure for which $q$ is a homomorphism, and assume it is an abelian variety of dimension $g$.

Let $a,b\in\{0,1/2\}^g$ and set $$j_{a,b}(n,n';z)=e\!\left(a^tn'-b^tn-\tfrac12 n^t\tau n-n^tz\right).$$ Let $L$ be an algebraic line bundle on $X$ with a holomorphic uniformization having map $q$ and lattice $\Lambda$. In its algebraic trivializations on opens $U_i$, let its frame functions $f_i$ satisfy $$f_i(z)=j_{a,b}(n,n';z)f_i(z+\tau n+n')\qquad(q(z)\in U_i).$$ Assume that for every $i$ there are entire $h_i,F_i$ with $h_i\in\mathcal T_N$ and $$F_i(z+\tau n+n')=j_{a,b}(n,n';z)e\!\left(-N(\tfrac12 n^t\tau n+n^tz)\right)F_i(z),$$ $$q(z)\in U_i\ \Longleftrightarrow\ h_i(z)F_i(z)\ne0,\qquad f_i(z)=h_i(z)/F_i(z).$$ For every $l\ge0$, assume the usual frame formula gives a bijection between $H^0(X,L^{\otimes l})$ and the entire functions $f$ satisfying $f(z+\tau n+n')=j_{a,b}(n,n';z)^l f(z)$.

Then for every $M\ge3$ there are finitely many sections $s_0,\ldots,s_r$ of $L^{\otimes M}$ with no common zero, with each $\{s_i\ne0\}$ affine and its coordinate ring generated by the ratios $s_j/s_i$. Thus $L^{\otimes M}$ is very ample in this chart sense.

## Very ampleness from analytic separation by lifted sections

[Theorem AV.1557](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.ThetaVeryAmple.veryAmpleAt_of_separation/)

Let $X/\mathbb C$ be a group scheme that is proper and smooth of relative dimension $g\ge0$, and let $L$ be a line bundle. Fix the following holomorphic uniformization data: a surjective holomorphic group map $u:\mathbb C^g\to X(\mathbb C)$ with full discrete period lattice, and compatible nowhere-zero holomorphic frame functions trivializing $u^*L$. Let $M,K\ge0$ and let $s_0,\ldots,s_K$ be sections of $L^{\otimes M}$ represented in these frames by entire functions $\theta_0,\ldots,\theta_K$. Put $V=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{\theta_0,\ldots,\theta_K\}$. Assume $$\forall z\ \exists f\in V:\ f(z)\ne0,$$ $$\forall z,z',\kappa:\ (\forall f\in V,\ f(z)=\kappa f(z'))\ \Longrightarrow\ u(z)=u(z'),$$ $$\forall z,\xi,\kappa:\ (\forall f\in V,\ df_z(\xi)=\kappa f(z))\ \Longrightarrow\ \xi=0.$$ Then $L^{\otimes M}$ satisfies the following very-ampleness condition: it has generating sections with affine nonvanishing charts whose coordinate rings are generated by section ratios.
