## Finite type descent of a polarized abelian variety with level and endomorphisms

[Theorem AV.0825](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.thetaLevel_object_descends_fg_subfield/)

Let $o$ be a commutative ring, $g,k\ge0$, and $J$ a finite set. Fix $\zeta\in o^\times$ such that $k$ is a unit in $o$ and the image of $\zeta$ under every map from $o$ to a field is a primitive $k$th root of unity. Let $K$ be a field and $\beta:o\to K$ a ring homomorphism.

Let $A/K$ be an abelian variety of dimension $g$ with a rigidified symmetric ample line bundle $L$ whose translation stabilizer is trivial. Choose $P_1,\ldots,P_{2g}\in A(K)$, killed by $k$, which form a basis of $A[k]$ on every algebraically closed extension and whose Weil pairings for $L$ are the standard symplectic pairings given by $\beta(\zeta)$. Also choose group endomorphisms $u_j$ of $A$ for $j\in J$.

There are a subfield $K_0\subseteq K$, a map $\beta_0:o\to K_0$ inducing $\beta$, and data $(A_0,L_0,(P_{0i}),(u_{0j}))$ over $K_0$ satisfying the same conditions, such that their scalar extensions identify with $A,L,(P_i),(u_j)$. Moreover, for some finite $S\subset K$, $$K_0=\text{the subfield generated by }\beta(o)\cup S,$$ and $K_0$ is countable whenever $o$ is countable.
