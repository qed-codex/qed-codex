## Small multiples of norm-one elements

[Theorem ANT.0297](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.normOne_small_multiples_by_cocompact_subgroup/)

Let $X$ be a noncompact locally compact second-countable Hausdorff commutative topological ring. Let $\tau:X\to X$ be a continuous ring homomorphism with $\tau^2=\mathrm{id}$. Let $\Lambda\subset X$ be a countable additive subgroup such that there is a compact set $C$ with $$X=\Lambda+C.$$ There is a compact set $D\subset X$ such that every $u\in X$ with $u\tau(u)=1$ admits $\beta\in\Lambda\setminus\{0\}$ satisfying $u\beta\in D$.
