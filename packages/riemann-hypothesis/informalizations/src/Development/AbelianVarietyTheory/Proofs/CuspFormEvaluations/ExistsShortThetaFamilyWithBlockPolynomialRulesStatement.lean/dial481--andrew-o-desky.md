## Short integral cusp generators containing prescribed theta sections

[Theorem ARAK.1227](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.exists_short_thetaFamily_with_block_polynomial_rules/)

Assume chart independence for the source's integral theta-moduli construction. Fix its arithmetic CM datum and a compatible moduli system satisfying the full laws, including the prescribed integral-level model. At an allowed stage $j$, let $w$ be an even-column weight with $n$ columns, positive last positive-sign entries, and let $f,r$ be the field and rank parameters. Let $L$ be the size parameter. Assume the source's complete logarithmic parameter bounds, $L\ge2$, $r\le n$ and $fr(r+1)\le2n$.

Let $K$ be the coefficient field, $d=[K:\mathbb Q]$, $g_0,g_1$ the two level primes, and $D_w$ the degree of the determinant polynomial associated with $w$. Write $p,q$ for the stage signature, $a^+_{\tau,i},a^-_{\tau,i}$ for its nonnegative weight entries, and put $$s_w=\sum_\tau\left(\sum_i a^+_{\tau,i}+\sum_i a^-_{\tau,i}\right)+f\max(q,1)rn+1.$$ There are cusp forms $v_1,\ldots,v_N$ whose $\mathcal O_K$-span is the lattice generated by all the designated theta sections of weight $w$, and a harmonic polynomial $P$ of the corresponding determinant weight that is a product of positive powers of minors on disjoint column sets, such that the family contains all theta sections obtained from $P$ and all $n$-tuples of level points, with distinct indices for distinct tuples. Moreover, $$N\le2(g_0g_1)^{frn}d(1+D_w)^{frn},$$ $$\log\|v_i\|_{\sup,\tau}\le400frL\,s_w+3frnL+5\log d+\frac{\log|\operatorname{disc}K|}{d}+2$$ for every $i$ and coefficient-field embedding $\tau$.
