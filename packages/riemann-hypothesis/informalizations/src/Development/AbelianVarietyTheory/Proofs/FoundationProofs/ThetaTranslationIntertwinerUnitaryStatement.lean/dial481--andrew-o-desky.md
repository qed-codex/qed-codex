## Isometric intertwiners between theta lattices

[Theorem THETA.1699](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.intertwinerUnitary_all/)

Fix the source's full arithmetic datum, a nonzero ideal $\mathfrak c$ of its imaginary quadratic field with $\gcd(N\mathfrak c,2d)=1$, where $d$ is the positive integer defining that field, and a twelfth root $\delta$ of the discriminant of the associated ideal lattice. Let $T$ be the resulting elliptic torus, $N,N'\ge0$, and let $V$ be the complex matrix space indexed by the CM type and matrices of size $N'\times N$. Write $$H(z,w)=\frac{\sum_{\tau,i,j}z_{\tau ij}\overline{w_{\tau ij}}}{\operatorname{area}(T)},\quad U_xf(z)=f(z+x)e^{-\pi H(z,x)-\pi H(x,x)/2},$$ $$\alpha(x)=(-1)^{\operatorname{round}((\sqrt d/2)\Re H(x,x))},$$ where $d$ is the positive integer defining the datum's imaginary quadratic field. A theta function for a lattice $A\subset V$ is an entire $f$ satisfying $\alpha(x)U_xf=f$ for all $x\in A$; for the prescribed lattice fundamental domain $\mathcal F_A$ put $$\|f\|_A^2=\operatorname{Avg}_{z\in\mathcal F_A}\bigl(|f(z)|^2e^{-\pi\Re H(z,z)}\bigr).$$ Let $A_1,A_2$ each be one of the following allowed lattices: either generated by the elliptic lattice times an orthonormal basis spanning the matrix space over the imaginary quadratic field, or obtained from a pair of self-dual Hermitian lattices. Define $$I_{12}f=[A_2:A_1\cap A_2]^{-1/2}\sum_{q\in A_2/(A_1\cap A_2)}\alpha(q)U_qf,$$ using the prescribed representatives. For every theta function $f$ for $A_1$, $$\|U_xf\|_{A_1}^2=\|f\|_{A_1}^2\quad(x\in V),\qquad I_{12}f\in\Theta(A_2),\quad\|I_{12}f\|_{A_2}^2=\|f\|_{A_1}^2,\quad I_{21}I_{12}f=f,$$ where $\Theta(A)$ denotes the theta functions just defined.
