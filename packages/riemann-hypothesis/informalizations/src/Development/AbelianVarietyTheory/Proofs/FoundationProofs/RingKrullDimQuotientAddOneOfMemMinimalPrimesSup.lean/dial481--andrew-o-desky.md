## Dimension of a component cut out by one equation

[Theorem AG.1059](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.ringKrullDim_quotient_add_one_of_mem_minimalPrimes_sup/)

Let $A$ be a finitely generated commutative algebra over a field $k$, let $P\subset A$ be prime, and let $f\notin P$. If $Q$ is a prime ideal minimal over $P+(f)$, then $$\dim(A/Q)+1=\dim(A/P).$$
