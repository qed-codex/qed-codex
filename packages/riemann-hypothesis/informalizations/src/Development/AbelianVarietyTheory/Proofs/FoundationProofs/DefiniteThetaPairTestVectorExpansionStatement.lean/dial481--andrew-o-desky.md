## A finite translated-theta expansion with coefficient bounds

[Theorem THETA.1131](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.definiteThetaPair_testVector_expansion/)

Fix the source's full arithmetic theta datum and its norm-sum input package, described in the conventions. Write $E$ for its CM field, $K$ for its imaginary quadratic subfield, $d$ for its discriminant parameter, $\Sigma$ for its chosen complex embeddings, and $A_0\subset E$ for its fixed coefficient $\mathcal O_E$-submodule. Let $0\ne\mathfrak c\subset\mathcal O_K$ satisfy $\gcd(N\mathfrak c,2d)=1$, and choose $\delta\in\mathbb C$ with $\delta^{12}$ equal to the discriminant of its unscaled complex ideal lattice; let $\Lambda_K\subset\mathbb C$ be the resulting elliptic period lattice. Choose $t_0$ with $$2t_0\in\Lambda_K,\qquad\frac{1\pm i\sqrt d}{2}t_0\notin\Lambda_K.$$ For an integer $Q\ge0$, choose a pair of theta ranks $a\le b$ with $b$ even, its normalized vacuum $e_0$, compatible Weil action $\omega$, integral weights, and test vector $f$ satisfying the pair package: its prescribed algebraic finite-translation presentation and coefficient bounds, invariance under the first integral unitary group, and unit-modulus eigenvector behavior for a subgroup of the second integral unitary group of nonzero index at most $Q^{b^2+4}$. Let $g,g'$ lie in the finite adelic unitary groups of the identity forms of ranks $a,b$, and let $L,L'$ be the integral lattices they carry from the standard lattices. Put $V=\prod_{\tau\in\Sigma}M_{b,a}(\mathbb C)$ and let $\Lambda(L,L')$ be the additive group generated by $w(\tau(B))_\tau$, for $w\in\Lambda_K$ and $B\in A_0\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O_E}(L,L')$; put $\Lambda_0=\Lambda(\mathcal O_E^a,\mathcal O_E^b)$. With $A$ the area of $\Lambda_K$, define $$H(z,y)=A^{-1}\sum_{\tau,i,j}z_{\tau,i,j}\overline{y_{\tau,i,j}},\qquad U(y)F(z)=F(z+y)e^{-\pi H(z,y)-\pi H(y,y)/2}.$$ Let $I_{\Lambda_0,\Lambda}$ be the comparison operator defined as the sum of $\alpha(y)U(y)$ over $\Lambda/(\Lambda_0\cap\Lambda)$ divided by the square root of this quotient's index, where $\alpha(y)=(-1)^{\operatorname{round}((\sqrt d/2)\operatorname{Re}H(y,y))}$ and $\Lambda=\Lambda(L,L')$. There exist $c\in\mathbb C$, finitely many $r_i\in\overline{\mathbb Q}$ and $y_i\in V$ such that, whenever $Q>0$, $c$ is algebraic with every complex conjugate of absolute value one, $$Qy_i\in\Lambda,\qquad Q^{ab}r_i\text{ is integral over }\mathbb Z,\qquad\left(\sum_i|\rho(r_i)|\right)^2\le Q^{ab}\quad(\rho:\overline{\mathbb Q}\hookrightarrow\mathbb C),$$ and $$\omega(g,g')f=c\sum_ir_i\,U(y_i)I_{\Lambda_0,\Lambda}e_0.$$ When $Q>0$, for every prime $p$, every $\lambda:\overline{\mathbb Q}\hookrightarrow\mathbb C_p$, and every integral unitary $k'$ in the second group, if $g$ and $g'k'$ are the identity at $p$, then $|\lambda(c)|_p=1$.
