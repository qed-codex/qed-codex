## Siegel compactification with polarized boundary charts

[Theorem AV.1.0022](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.SiegJoin.siegelToroidalPolCharts/)

For every genus $g\ge1$, odd integer $n\ge3$ and invariant function $\phi$ satisfying ([Polarizing fan conventions for toroidal compactifications](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.LocM.InvFun/)) over $\mathbb Z$ in ranks at most $g$, there is a Siegel family $A\to U$ over $$R_n=\mathbb Z[\zeta_n,1/n]\subset\mathbb C,\qquad \zeta_n=e^{2\pi i/n},$$ and an open immersion $j:U\hookrightarrow S$ over $R_n$ with the following properties. The morphism $s:S\to\operatorname{Spec}R_n$ is projective and smooth of relative dimension $g(g+1)/2$. For every $x\in S$, $$x\in\overline{j(U)\cap s^{-1}(s(x))}.$$ There is a semiabelian group scheme $G/S$, smooth of relative dimension $g$, whose pullback to $U$ is $A$ and whose abelian locus is exactly $j(U)$.

There are an effective Cartier divisor $B$ on $S$ and integers $d,N\ge1$ such that every point of $S$ has a polarized chart for $(\phi,B,d)$ as defined below. Write $\lambda=\det e_G^*\Omega^1_{G/S}$ for the Hodge line. Then $\lambda^{\otimes N}$ is generated by global sections, and for some integer $k\ge0$, $$\lambda^{\otimes Nk}\otimes\mathcal O_S(-B)\quad\hbox{is ample}.$$
