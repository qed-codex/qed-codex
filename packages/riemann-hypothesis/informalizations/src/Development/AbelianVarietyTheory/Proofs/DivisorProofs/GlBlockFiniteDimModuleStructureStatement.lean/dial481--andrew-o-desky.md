## Structure of a finite block Lie algebra module with rational weights

[Theorem REP.0366](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.glBlock_finiteDim_module_structure/)

Let $\mathfrak k=\mathfrak{gl}_p(\mathbb C)\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_q(\mathbb C)$ be the block diagonal matrices in $\mathfrak{gl}_{p+q}(\mathbb C)$. Let $V$ be a complex vector space and $A:\operatorname{Mat}_{p+q}(\mathbb C)\to\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb C}(V)$ a complex-linear map respecting brackets on $\mathfrak k$. Let $W\subseteq V$ be finite dimensional, invariant under $\mathfrak k$, and contained in the sum of the simultaneous diagonal weight spaces with rational weights. Then:

- Every $\mathfrak k$-invariant $U\subseteq W$ has a $\mathfrak k$-invariant complement in $W$.
- If $W\ne0$, it contains a highest-weight vector.
- Every vector of $W$ lies in the sum, over rational weights $\mu$, of the spans of vectors in $W$ of type $\mu$.
- If $v\in W$ is a highest-weight vector of weight $\mu$, then $\mu_a-\mu_b$ is a nonnegative integer whenever $a<b$ are in the same block; $v$ has type $\mu$; and every invariant subspace of the invariant subspace generated by $v$ is either zero or contains $v$.
