## Ampleness and trivial translation kernel at the same base point

[Theorem AV.0942](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.relAmple_kernelTrivial_of_same_image_point/)

Let $S$ be a scheme, $G\to S$ a smooth separated quasi-compact group scheme, and $L$ a line bundle on $G$. Let $z:\operatorname{Spec}K_1\to S$ and $x:\operatorname{Spec}K\to S$ be points defined over fields, with the same image in the underlying topological space of $S$. Suppose $G_z$ is proper, $L_z$ is relatively ample, and $L_z$ has trivial translation kernel. Then $L_x$ is relatively ample and has trivial translation kernel.
