## Local field and additive-character data at an inert place

[Theorem ANT.1621](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.inert_place_local_field_data_exists/)

Let $E/F$ be number fields with involution $\sigma$ and the good Weil base datum specified in ([A hyperbolic plane adapted to lattices and split bases](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/PositivityTheory.ThKG.Tow.Pln.exists_plane/)). In particular $E/F$ is CM, $E/\mathbb Q$ is Galois, $\mathcal O_E/\mathcal O_F$ is unramified, and the datum includes a standard additive character $\psi$ of $\mathbb A_F/F$ and its prescribed finite-place unramified conditions. If $v$ is a finite place of $F$ and $w$ a finite place of $E$ with $\mathfrak p_v\mathcal O_E=\mathfrak p_w$, there are a complete characteristic-zero discretely valued field $L$, a uniformizer $\varpi$, an involution $\tau:L\to L$, an odd integer $q\ge2$, a unit $\delta\in\mathcal O_L$, an additive character $\psi_L:L\to\mathbb C$, and an injective additive map $$j:L\to\mathbb A_F\otimes_F E.$$ The involution preserves valuation, fixes $\varpi$, has $\tau(x)-x$ a unit for some $x\in\mathcal O_L$, the residue field of $L$ has cardinality $q^2$, and $\tau(\delta)=-\delta$. The map $j$ is multiplicative, has image exactly the elements supported at $v$, and satisfies $j(\tau x)=(1\otimes\sigma)j(x)$. Moreover $$j(\mathcal O_L)=\left\langle\{a\otimes o:a\in\mathbb A_F\text{ supported at }v,\ a_v\in\mathcal O_{F_v},\ o\in\mathcal O_E\}\right\rangle_{\mathbb Z},$$ where the brackets mean the additive subgroup generated by the displayed set. The character is trivial on $\mathcal O_L^\tau$ and nontrivial on $\varpi^{-1}\mathcal O_L^\tau$, and $$\psi\!\left(\operatorname{Tr}_{(\mathbb A_F\otimes E)/\mathbb A_F}(j x)\right)=\psi_L(x+\tau x)\qquad(x\in L).$$
