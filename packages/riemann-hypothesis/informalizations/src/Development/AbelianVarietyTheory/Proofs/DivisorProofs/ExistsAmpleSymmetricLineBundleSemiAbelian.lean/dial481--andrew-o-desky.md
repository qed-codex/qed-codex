## A symmetric ample line bundle on a semiabelian scheme

[Theorem AV.1107](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.SymAmple.exists_ample_symmetric/)

Let $R$ be a regular local domain and let $G$ be a semiabelian group scheme over $R$ whose generic fibre over $\operatorname{Frac}R$ is an abelian scheme. There is an ample line bundle $M$ on $G$ such that $$[-1]^*M\simeq M.$$
