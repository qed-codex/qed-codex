## Theta sections span a holomorphic cusp space

[Theorem THETA.1272](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.RawSpan.holSub0_le_span_raw/)

Let $E$ be a CM field, and fix the polarized unitary moduli datum of ([Powers of a theta-coordinate bundle and the Hodge bundle](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.theta_map_pull_O1_power_hodge/)), level $(M,A)$, moduli scheme $X$, and coefficient field $K\subseteq\mathbb C$. Choose an embedding $\sigma:K\to\mathbb C$ satisfying the distinguished-root condition and a rank-$r$ complex uniformization satisfying the source's full uniformization package. Assume $r\ge1$, $A=1$, and that all components have the same Hermitian Gram matrix and polarizing scalar. For each component, suppose its arithmetic group contains every lattice-preserving unitary transformation congruent to one modulo $M$. Choose a star-ring structure on $\mathcal O_E$ and a differential evaluation system satisfying the source's coordinate-germ compatibility package.

Let $b\ge1$, let $n\ge4[E:\mathbb Q]r+4$ be even, and choose nonnegative integers $a_{\tau,j}$, for every embedding $\tau:E\to\mathbb C$ and $j\ge0$, nonincreasing in $j$. On each component of signature $(p,q)$ with $p+q=r$, transport the embedding indices through $\sigma$ and define the weight coordinates, indexed by $0\le j<r$, by $$\lambda_{\tau,j}=\begin{cases}-a_{\overline\tau,q-1-j}-n/2,&j<q,\\ a_{\tau,j-q}+n/2,&q\le j<r.\end{cases}$$ Assume the arithmetic-group stabilizer character of this weight is trivial on every component. Let $\mathscr H_0(\lambda)$ be the complex span of the holomorphic cusp forms of weight $\lambda$, restricted to functions which are zero outside the complex uniformization domains. Then $$\mathscr H_0(\lambda)\subseteq\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{\operatorname{res}(f): f\in\mathscr T\}.$$ Here $\mathscr T$ consists exactly of sections of the source's algebraic weight $(a,n)$ for which there exist: an $n$-tuple $x_0$ generating the full finite-level quotient as an $\mathcal O_E$-module; a genus lattice $\Lambda$ of rank $n$ which is unimodular and satisfies the source's triviality conditions at both $b$ and $M$; an integer $N\ge0$ satisfying its level compatibility condition at $M$; and a polynomial $P$ in the source's matrix-coordinate letters over $K$ satisfying its pluriharmonic weight-$a$ condition and the following integral coefficient condition: $P$ belongs to the subring generated by constants in $\mathcal O_K$ and the linear forms $\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\tau_K(v_j)X_{\tau,i,j}$ with $v\in\Lambda$, $0\le i<\operatorname{mult}(\tau)$, where $\tau_K:E\to K$ is the specified coefficient-field embedding and $X_{\tau,i,j}$ are the matrix-coordinate letters. Require, at every framed point over every commutative $K$-algebra, the source's short theta-value condition for $f$, the differential evaluation system, $\Lambda,N,P$, and the finite-level weight determined by $x_0$.
