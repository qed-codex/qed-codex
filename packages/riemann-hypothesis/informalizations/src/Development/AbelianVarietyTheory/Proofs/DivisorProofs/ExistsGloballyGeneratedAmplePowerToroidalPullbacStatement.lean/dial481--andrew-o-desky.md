## A generated power of a line bundle root

[Theorem AG.1421](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.exists_globallyGenerated_ample_power_toroidalPullback/)

Let $o$ be a commutative Noetherian ring, let $Z,S$ be schemes, and let $z:Z\to\operatorname{Spec}o$ and $\pi:S\to Z$ be morphisms. Assume $z$ is projective. Let $H$ be an ample line bundle on $Z$, let $W,M$ be line bundles on $S$, and let $a,N\ge1$ be integers such that $$W^{\otimes N}\simeq(\pi^*H)^{\otimes a}.$$ Assume $(\pi^*H)^{\otimes k}\otimes M$ is ample for every sufficiently large integer $k\ge0$. There exists an integer $N'\ge1$ such that $W^{\otimes N'}$ is generated by global sections, and there is an integer $k\ge0$ for which $W^{\otimes(N'k)}\otimes M$ is ample.
