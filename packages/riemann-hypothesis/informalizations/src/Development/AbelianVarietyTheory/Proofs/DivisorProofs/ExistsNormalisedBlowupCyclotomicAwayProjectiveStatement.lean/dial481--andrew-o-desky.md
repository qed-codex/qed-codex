## A projective normalized blowup away from a dense open

[Theorem AG.1352](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.exists_normalised_blowup_cyclotomicAway_projective/)

Let $n\ge0$ and let $R_n\subseteq\mathbb C$ be the subring generated by $\zeta_n$ and $n^{-1}$, where $\zeta_n=\exp(2\pi i/n)$ with division by zero assigned zero. Let $Z$ be a normal projective $R_n$-scheme, $\mathcal J$ an ideal sheaf on $Z$, and $W\subseteq Z$ a dense open set disjoint from $\operatorname{Supp}(\mathcal O_Z/\mathcal J)$.

There are a scheme $S$, a morphism $\pi:S\to Z$ and an effective Cartier divisor $B$ on $S$ such that $S$ is normal, $S$ is projective over $R_n$, $$\mathcal I_B=\mathcal J\mathcal O_S,\qquad \pi^{-1}(W)\xrightarrow{\sim}W,\qquad \overline{\pi^{-1}(W)}=S.$$ Moreover $\pi$ factors through the blowup $Z_0=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal J}Z$ as a finite morphism $\nu:S\to Z_0$ followed by the blowup map, and $\nu$ is an isomorphism over a dense open subset of $Z_0$ with dense inverse image in $S$.
