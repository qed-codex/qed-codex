## A Frobenius lift on the completed unramified field

[Theorem PHT.0506](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CurveDictionary.frobExists/)

Let $\ell\ge5$ be prime. Inside $\mathbb C_\ell$, let $K$ be the topological closure of the field generated by all roots of unity of order prime to $\ell$. There is a ring homomorphism $\sigma:K\to K$ such that $$|\ell|_\ell=\ell^{-1},\qquad |K^\times|_\ell\subseteq\ell^{\mathbb Z},$$ and $$|\sigma(a)-a^\ell|_\ell\le\ell^{-1}\qquad(a\in K,\ |a|_\ell\le1).$$
