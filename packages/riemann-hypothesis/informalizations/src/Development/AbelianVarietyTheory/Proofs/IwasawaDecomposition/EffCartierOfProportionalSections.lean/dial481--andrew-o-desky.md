## Proportional projective coordinates give a Cartier ideal

[Theorem AG.0399](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.ProjBlowup.effCartier_of_proportional/)

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $X,W,T$ schemes, $L$ a line-bundle datum on $X$, $\sigma_0,\ldots,\sigma_m$ sections, $g:W\to X$, and $\psi:W\to\mathbb P_R^m$. Let $I$ be an ideal sheaf on $W$. Assume that the inverse images of the trivializing opens of $L$ cover $W$, and on each such trivialization $I$ is generated by the local functions of $g^*\sigma_0,\ldots,g^*\sigma_m$. Assume these functions are proportional to the projective coordinates of $\psi$: on its $i$th chart, $$g^*\sigma_j=(\psi^*(x_j/x_i))g^*\sigma_i\qquad(0\le j\le m).$$ If there is a quasi-compact schematically dominant morphism $d:T\to W$ whose image avoids the support of $I$, then $I$ is an effective Cartier ideal: around every point of $W$, on an affine open, it is generated by a non-zero-divisor.
