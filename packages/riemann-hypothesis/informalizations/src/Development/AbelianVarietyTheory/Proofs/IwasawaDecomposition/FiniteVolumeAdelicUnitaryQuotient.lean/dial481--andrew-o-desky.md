## Finite volume of an adelic unitary quotient

[Theorem AGR.1450](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Mem.VolFin.finVol/)

Let $E/F$ be a CM extension of number fields, with involution $\sigma$, and let $J\in M_M(E)$ be a nondegenerate $\sigma$-Hermitian matrix, where $M\ge0$. For any Haar measure $\mu$ on $U(J)(\mathbb A_F)$ and any measurable fundamental domain $\mathcal F$ for the left action of $U(J)(F)$, $$\mu(\mathcal F)<\infty.$$
