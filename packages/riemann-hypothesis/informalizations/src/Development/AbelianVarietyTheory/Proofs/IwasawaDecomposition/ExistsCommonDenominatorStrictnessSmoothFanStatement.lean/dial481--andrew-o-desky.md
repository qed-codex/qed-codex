## A common denominator for strict cone support functionals

[Theorem AG.1485](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.exists_common_denominator_strictness_smooth_fan/)

Let $X$ be a finitely generated abelian group. Let $\phi$ be an invariant function on real-valued functions on finite free $\mathbb Z$-modules, compatible with surjective pullbacks and zero in rank zero; evaluate it on $X$ through a finite free presentation. Let $C(X)$ be the real symmetric forms pulled back from positive-definite forms on free quotients of $X$. Assume $\phi$ satisfies the polarization-fan conditions on $X$ stated in the conventions.

There is an integer $N>0$ such that every $B\in C(X)$ lies in a cone generated by integral positive-semidefinite symmetric forms $u_1,\ldots,u_m$ forming part of an integral basis of the symmetric forms on $X$, with $\phi$ linear on this cone. There is a finitely supported $w:X\times X\to\mathbb Z$ such that, for $\ell(\beta)=\sum_{x,y}w(x,y)\beta(x,y)$, $$\ell(u_i)=N\phi(u_i),\qquad \ell(\beta)\ge N\phi(\beta)$$ for every integral positive-semidefinite symmetric $\beta$. Equality in the second relation implies $$k\beta=\sum_i c_i u_i\quad\text{for some }k>0\text{ and }c_i\in\mathbb N.$$
