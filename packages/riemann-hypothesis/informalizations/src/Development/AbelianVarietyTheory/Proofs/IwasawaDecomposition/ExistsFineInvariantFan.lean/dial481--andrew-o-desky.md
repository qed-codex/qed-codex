## Invariant fan refinement with simultaneous congruence minima

[Theorem AG.0052](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.PolFan.exists_fine_invariant_fan/)

Fix a CM number field $E$ unramified over its maximal real subfield at finite places, with integral conjugation $a\mapsto\bar a$. Fix a prime $d\equiv7\pmod{16}$, elements $\alpha,w_0\in\mathcal O_E$ with $\alpha^2=-d$ and $2w_0=1+\alpha$, and $\alpha_0\in\mathbb C$ with $\alpha_0^2=-d$. For a commutative ring $O$ with a chosen involution, an invariant function $\phi$ assigns a real value to every function $B:O^n\times O^n\to\mathbb R$, for every $n\ge0$, compatibly with pullback along surjective $O$-linear maps, and has value zero in rank zero. It therefore evaluates such functions on finite $O$-modules by a free presentation.

For a finite projective $O$-module $X$, call a form balanced if it is symmetric and $B(ax,y)=B(x,\bar a y)$. Let $C(X)$ consist of real balanced forms pulled back from positive-definite balanced forms on projective quotients of $X$. For integral balanced positive-semidefinite forms $\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_m$ forming part of an integral basis of the module of balanced forms, call their cone a cone of $\phi$ when $\phi$ is linear on it and has a strict integral supporting functional: some integer $N>0$ and an integer-valued linear functional $\ell$ on all integral bilinear forms satisfy $\ell(\beta)\ge N\phi(\beta)$ for every integral balanced positive-semidefinite $\beta$, equality holds for each $\gamma_i$, and equality for any $\beta$ implies $k\beta=\sum_i c_i\gamma_i$ for some $k>0$, $c_i\in\mathbb N$.

Say that $\phi$ satisfies the polarization-fan conditions through rank $r$ if, on every such $X$ of rank at most $r$, it is positively homogeneous and superadditive on $C(X)$, takes integer values on integral balanced positive-semidefinite forms and is strictly positive on their nonzero members, its cones are closed under faces and cover $C(X)$, and there is one finite set of integral forms such that the generators of every cone can all be carried into it by a single $O$-linear automorphism of $X$.

Let $g,q\in\mathbb N$. Suppose $\phi_0$ is an invariant function over $\mathbb Z$ with its identity involution satisfying these conditions through rank $g$, and $[E:\mathbb Q]q\le g$. For any finite set $\mathcal N\subseteq\mathbb N_{\ge1}$, there is an invariant function $\phi'$ over $\mathcal O_E$ satisfying the polarization-fan conditions through rank $q$, with the following additional properties.

Distinct rays of any one cone are never carried to each other by an $\mathcal O_E$-linear automorphism. The function is invariant under every ring automorphism of $\mathcal O_E$ fixing $w_0$, and is positive on every positive-definite integral balanced form on every nonzero finite $\mathcal O_E$-module. For every function $B$ on a finite free module and integer $k\ge0$, $\phi'(kB)=k\phi'(B)$.

For every cone generated by $\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_m$ on a finite projective $X$ of rank at most $q$, the restriction of $\phi_0$ to the underlying $\mathbb Z$-module is linear on the cone. For every $N\in\mathcal N$ and $y\in X$, there exists $\mu_0\in X$ such that, simultaneously for every $i$ and every $\mu\in X$, $$\gamma_i(y+N\mu_0,y+N\mu_0)\le\gamma_i(y+N\mu,y+N\mu).$$
