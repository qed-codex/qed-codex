## Setting for theta lifts between unitary groups of a CM extension: base data, Hermitian data, Weil representations, theta integrals

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D008](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.WeilTensorModels.Base.IsGood/)

**Fields and adeles.** $F$ and $E$ are number fields, $E$ is an $F$-algebra, and $\sigma\colon E\to E$ is a homomorphism of $F$-algebras. $\mathbb A$ is the adele ring of $F$, $\mathbb A_f$ its finite part, $F_\infty=F\otimes_{\mathbb Q}\mathbb R$, and $\mathbb A_E=\mathbb A\otimes_F E$; $\sigma$ is extended $\mathbb A$-linearly to $\mathbb A_E$. For a finite place $v$ of $F$, $F_v$ is the completion, $\mathcal O_v$ its ring of integers, $E_v=F_v\otimes_F E$. For $u\in\mathbb A^\times$, $|u|_{\mathbb A}$ is the factor by which multiplication by $u$ scales a Haar measure of $\mathbb A$. A place $v$ is inert in $E$ when $v\mathcal O_E$ is a prime ideal.

An element $z$ of an $\mathbb A$-module (an adele, an element of $\mathbb A_E$, a vector or a matrix with such entries) is *supported at $v$* if $uz=0$ for every $u\in\mathbb A$ with $u_v=0$, and *supported at infinity* if $uz=0$ for every $u$ whose component in $F_\infty$ is $0$.

**Base data.** *Base data* for $(F,E)$ consist of - a homomorphism $\psi$ from the additive group of $\mathbb A$ to $\mathbb C^\times$; $\psi_v$ denotes its restriction to $F_v\subset\mathbb A$; - for each infinite place $w$ of $F$, an embedding $\iota_w\colon E\to\mathbb C$ extending the embedding of $F$ attached to $w$; the induced ring homomorphism $\mathbb A_E\to\mathbb C$ is also written $\iota_w$; - two families of integers $(m^\alpha_w)_w$ and $(m^\beta_w)_w$ indexed by the infinite places of $F$; - a finite set $P$ of finite places of $F$ and, for each $v\in P$, an $F$-algebra embedding $\tau_v\colon E\to F_v$; - a finite place $v_1$ of $F$ and an $F$-algebra embedding $\tau_1\colon E\to F_{v_1}$.

The base data are *admissible* for $\sigma$ if 1. $F$ is totally real, $E$ is totally complex, $[E:F]=2$, and $\tau(\sigma x)=\overline{\tau(x)}$ for every embedding $\tau\colon E\to\mathbb C$ and $x\in E$ (so $E/F$ is a CM extension with complex conjugation $\sigma$); 2. $E/\mathbb Q$ is Galois; 3. $\mathcal O_E$ is unramified over $\mathcal O_F$; 4. $\psi$ is continuous, trivial on $F$, and $\psi(x)=\exp(-2\pi i\,\mathrm{Tr}_{F_\infty/\mathbb R}(x_\infty))$ for $x$ supported at infinity; 5. for every finite place $v$ of $F$ inert in $E$: $2\notin v$, $N(v)>3$, and $\psi_v$ has conductor $\mathcal O_v$, that is, for $x\in F_v$, $\psi_v(x\mathcal O_v)=1$ if and only if $x\in\mathcal O_v$; 6. $\psi_v$ has conductor $\mathcal O_v$ for every $v\in P$.

**Hermitian data.** A *Hermitian datum of dimension $n$* is a quadruple $X=(J,L,\theta,b)$: a matrix $J\in M_n(E)$, an $\mathcal O_E$-submodule $L\subset E^n$, a matrix $\theta_w\in M_n(\mathbb C)$ for each infinite place $w$ of $F$, and a matrix $b_v\in GL_n(F_v)$ for each finite place $v$. Its form is $\langle x,y\rangle=x^{\mathsf T}J\,\sigma(y)$ on $E^n$. For a complex matrix $M$ of size $n$, say that $M$ has signature $(r,s)$ if $r+s=n$ and $A^{\mathsf T}M\overline A=\mathrm{diag}(1_r,-1_s)$ for some $A\in M_n(\mathbb C)$, and that $\theta$ is a Cartan involution for $M$ if $\theta^2=1$, $\theta^{\mathsf T}M\overline\theta=M$, and $\mathrm{Re}\big((\theta x)^{\mathsf T}M\overline x\big)>0$ for all nonzero $x\in\mathbb C^n$.

Relative to base data, $X$ is *Hermitian of signature $(r,s)$* if - $\sigma(J_{ji})=J_{ij}$ for all $i,j$; - $\iota_w(J)$ has signature $(r,s)$ and $\theta_w$ is a Cartan involution for $\iota_w(J)$, for every infinite place $w$; - $L$ is a lattice (finitely generated, spanning $E^n$ over $E$) and $L=\{y\in E^n:\langle y,x\rangle\in\mathcal O_E\text{ for all }x\in L\}$; - for every $v\in P$, the columns of $b_v$ form an $\mathcal O_v$-basis of the image of $\mathcal O_v\otimes L$ under the map $E_v^n\to F_v^n$ induced by $\tau_v$.

$X$ is *skew-Hermitian of signature $(r,s)$* if the same holds with $\sigma(J_{ji})=-J_{ij}$, with $i\,\iota_w(J)$ in place of $\iota_w(J)$ in the second condition, and with $L=\{y:\mathrm{Tr}_{E/\mathbb Q}(o\langle y,x\rangle)\in\mathbb Z\text{ for all }x\in L,\ o\in\mathcal O_E\}$ in the third.

**Groups and heights.** $U(X)(\mathbb A)=\{g\in GL_n(\mathbb A_E): g^{\mathsf T}J\,\sigma(g)=J\}$, and $U(X)(F)$ is its subgroup of elements with entries in $E$. $U(X)_v$ (resp. $U(X)_\infty$) is the set of $g\in U(X)(\mathbb A)$ with $g-1$ supported at $v$ (resp. at infinity), and $K_{X,\infty}$ is the set of $k\in U(X)_\infty$ such that $\iota_w(k)$ commutes with $\theta_w$ for every $w$.

For $g\in GL_N(\mathbb A_E)$ the height is $\mathrm{ht}(g)=\prod_w\max(1,\|g_w\|,\|g_w^{-1}\|)\cdot\prod_v\max(1,\|g_v\|,\|g_v^{-1}\|)$, over the infinite places $w$ and finite places $v$ of $F$; at $w$, $\|\cdot\|$ is the largest Euclidean operator norm on $\mathbb C^N$ of the images under the embeddings of $E$ above $w$; at $v$ it is the largest absolute value of the images of the entries under the $F$-embeddings of $E$ into an algebraic closure of $F_v$. The absolute value on $F_v$ is normalized by $|\varpi_v|_v=N(v)^{-1}$ for a uniformizer $\varpi_v$; its extension to an algebraic closure is used in the finite-place norms.

**Schwartz functions and local action.** For a finite set $\kappa$, $\mathcal S(\mathbb A^\kappa)$ is the span of the functions $x\mapsto f(x_\infty)g(x_f)$ with $f$ a Schwartz function on $F_\infty^\kappa$ and $g$ locally constant of compact support on $\mathbb A_f^\kappa$. A linear functional $l$ on the space of all functions $\mathbb A^\kappa\to\mathbb C$ is *tempered* if for each such $g$ the map $f\mapsto l(f\otimes g)$ is given by a continuous linear functional on the Schwartz space of $F_\infty^\kappa$.

Let $T$ be an operator on functions on $\mathbb A^\kappa$ and $\varphi,\varphi_1$ functions on $F_v^\kappa$. We say *$T$ sends $\varphi$ to $\varphi_1$ at $v$* if $T(\varphi\otimes r)=\varphi_1\otimes r$ for every function $r$ on $\mathbb A^\kappa$ that is invariant under translation by elements supported at $v$ and for which $(\varphi\otimes r)(x)=\varphi(x_v)r(x)$ lies in $\mathcal S(\mathbb A^\kappa)$. For a family $(T_t)_{t\in\mathbb R}$ and functions $f,f_1$ on $F_\infty^\kappa$, we say *$(T_t)$ has derivative $f\mapsto f_1$ at infinity* if for every $r$ invariant under translation by elements supported at infinity with $f\otimes r\in\mathcal S(\mathbb A^\kappa)$, and every $x$, the function $t\mapsto T_t(f\otimes r)(x)$ has derivative $(f_1\otimes r)(x)$ at $t=0$. "Sends $f$ to $f_1$ at infinity" is defined like "at $v$".

**Heisenberg operators.** Let $X$, $W$ be Hermitian data of dimensions $n$, $d$, and $\kappa=\{1,\dots,n\}\times\{1,\dots,d\}$. On $\mathbb W=M_{n\times d}(\mathbb A_E)$ put $h(x,y)=\sum_{i,j,k,l}x_{ij}J^X_{ik}J^W_{jl}\sigma(y_{kl})$ and $\langle\!\langle x,y\rangle\!\rangle=-\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathbb A_E/\mathbb A}h(x,y)$. *Coordinates* on $\mathbb W$ are an $\mathbb A$-linear isomorphism $e=(e_1,e_2)\colon\mathbb W\to\mathbb A^\kappa\times\mathbb A^\kappa$ with $\langle\!\langle x,y\rangle\!\rangle=e_1(x)\cdot e_2(y)-e_1(y)\cdot e_2(x)$. The Heisenberg operator of $w\in\mathbb W$ acts on functions on $\mathbb A^\kappa$ by $$(\rho_\psi(w)\Phi)(x)=\psi\big(x\cdot e_2(w)+\tfrac12 e_1(w)\cdot e_2(w)\big)\,\Phi(x+e_1(w)).$$

**Model data.** A *model datum* for $(X,W)$ consists of coordinates $e$ on $\mathbb W$, a function $\varphi^0_v$ on $F_v^\kappa$ for each finite place $v$, a measure $\mu$ on $U(X)(\mathbb A)$ and a subset $D\subset U(X)(\mathbb A)$. It is *admissible* for $\psi$ if - $e$ maps $M_{n\times d}(E)$ into $F^\kappa\times F^\kappa$; - for every $v$, $\varphi^0_v$ is nonzero, locally constant of compact support, and there is $\vartheta\in\mathcal O_E$ with $\vartheta+\sigma(\vartheta)=1$ such that for every $\gamma$ in the additive group generated by the matrices $a\,xy^{\mathsf T}$ ($x\in L_X$, $y\in L_W$, $a\in\mathbb A$ supported at $v$ with $a_v\in\mathcal O_v$), $\rho_\psi(\gamma)$ sends $\varphi^0_v$ to $\psi\big(\tfrac12\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathbb A_E/\mathbb A}(\vartheta\,h(\gamma,\gamma))\big)\varphi^0_v$ at $v$; - $\varphi^0_v$ is the indicator function of $\mathcal O_v^\kappa$ for all but finitely many $v$; - $\mu$ is a Haar measure and $D$ is a fundamental domain for left multiplication by $U(X)(F)$ with respect to $\mu$.

**Geometric theta data.** Fix base data and integers $a,a'\ge0$. Geometric theta data of dimensions $(a,a')$ consist of integers $p,q\ge0$, Hermitian data $V$, $V'$, $W$ of dimensions $a$, $a'$, $a+a'$, a model datum $M$ for $(V,W)$, and a model datum $M'$ for $(V',W)$. The geometric theta data are *admissible* relative to the base data if the following conditions hold.

$a'<a$; $a+a'$ is even; $a+a'<m^\alpha_w-m^\beta_w$ for every infinite place $w$; $2(p+q)+1<(a-a')/2$ and $2(p+q)+1<a'$. $V$ is Hermitian of signature $(a-p,p)$, $V'$ is Hermitian of signature $(q,a'-q)$, and $W$ is skew-Hermitian of signature $(a+a'-(p+q),\,p+q)$. $M$ and $M'$ are admissible for $\psi$.

The quotient $(a-a')/2$ in these conditions is an integer.

**Weil representations.** Let $J_A$, $J_B$ be the matrices of Hermitian data of dimensions $n,d\ge0$, put $\kappa=\{1,\ldots,n\}\times\{1,\ldots,d\}$, and let $e$ be coordinates on $M_{n\times d}(\mathbb A_E)$ for this pair. Let $\chi\colon\mathbb A_E^\times\to S^1$ be a homomorphism. A homomorphism $\varpi$ from $U(J_B)(\mathbb A)$ to the linear endomorphisms of the space of functions on $\mathbb A^\kappa$ is *regular* for $e$ if, for every $h\in U(J_B)(\mathbb A)$, $\Phi\in\mathcal S(\mathbb A^\kappa)$, tempered linear functional $\ell$ and $w\in M_{n\times d}(\mathbb A_E)$,

- $\varpi(h)\Phi\in\mathcal S(\mathbb A^\kappa)$ and $\ell\circ\varpi(h)$ is tempered;
- $\varpi(h)\rho_\psi(w)\Phi=\rho_\psi(wh^{\mathsf T})\varpi(h)\Phi$;
- $\int|\varpi(h)\Phi|^2\,d\lambda=\int|\Phi|^2\,d\lambda$ for every additive Haar measure $\lambda$ on $\mathbb A^\kappa$;
- the map $h\mapsto\ell(\varpi(h)\Phi)$ is continuous.

It is a *Weil representation of $U(J_B)(\mathbb A)$ for $(J_A,e,\chi)$* if it is regular and there exist coordinates $\tilde e$ on $M_{n\times 2d}(\mathbb A_E)$ for $J_A$ and $\tilde J_B=\mathrm{diag}(J_B,-J_B)$, and a regular homomorphism $\tilde\varpi$ of $U(\tilde J_B)(\mathbb A)$ on functions on $\mathbb A^{\kappa\sqcup\kappa}$, satisfying the following conditions. Write $\tilde\rho_\psi$ for the Heisenberg operators defined by $\tilde e$.

- For all $w_1,w_2\in M_{n\times d}(\mathbb A_E)$, $$\tilde e(w_1\,|\,w_2)=\big((e_1(w_1),e_1(w_2)),(e_2(w_1),-e_2(w_2))\big).$$
- For every $h\in U(J_B)(\mathbb A)$ and $\Phi_1,\Phi_2\in\mathcal S(\mathbb A^\kappa)$, $$\tilde\varpi(\mathrm{diag}(h,1))(\Phi_1\otimes\Phi_2)=\varpi(h)\Phi_1\otimes\Phi_2,$$ where $(\Phi_1\otimes\Phi_2)(x_1,x_2)=\Phi_1(x_1)\Phi_2(x_2)$.
- Put $\Delta=\binom{I_d}{I_d}$. Require $\Delta^{\mathsf T}\tilde J_B\sigma(\Delta)=0$ and the existence of $C\in M_{2d\times d}(\mathbb A_E)$ such that $$C^{\mathsf T}\tilde J_B\sigma(C)=0,\qquad (\Delta\,|\,C)\in GL_{2d}(\mathbb A_E).$$
- Let $\ell$ be a tempered linear functional on the space of all functions on $\mathbb A^{\kappa\sqcup\kappa}$ such that $$\ell(\tilde\rho_\psi(z\Delta^{\mathsf T})\Phi)=\ell(\Phi)\quad\bigl(z\in M_{n\times d}(\mathbb A_E),\ \Phi\in\mathcal S(\mathbb A^{\kappa\sqcup\kappa})\bigr).$$ For every $p\in U(\tilde J_B)(\mathbb A)$, $t\in GL_d(\mathbb A_E)$ with $p\Delta=\Delta t$, and $\Phi\in\mathcal S(\mathbb A^{\kappa\sqcup\kappa})$, require $$\ell(\tilde\varpi(p)\Phi)=\chi(\det t)\,\big|N_{\mathbb A_E/\mathbb A}\det t\big|_{\mathbb A}^{n/2}\,\ell(\Phi).$$

For Hermitian data $X$, $W$ of dimensions $n,d$, coordinates $e$ and homomorphisms $\chi_1,\chi_2\colon\mathbb A_E^\times\to S^1$, a homomorphism $\omega$ from $U(X)(\mathbb A)\times U(W)(\mathbb A)$ to the endomorphisms of the space of functions on $\mathbb A^\kappa$ is a *Weil representation for $(X,W)$ with characters $(\chi_1,\chi_2)$* if $h\mapsto\omega(1,h)$ is a Weil representation of $U(W)(\mathbb A)$ for $(J_X,e,\chi_1)$; $g\mapsto\omega(g,1)$ is a Weil representation of $U(X)(\mathbb A)$ for $(J_W,e',\chi_2)$, for some coordinates $e'$ on $M_{d\times n}(\mathbb A_E)$ with $e'(x^{\mathsf T})=e(x)$; and $(g,h)\mapsto\ell(\omega(g,h)\Phi)$ is continuous for tempered $\ell$ and Schwartz $\Phi$.

**Theta functions and theta integrals.** For a function $\Phi$ on $\mathbb A^\kappa$, $\theta(\Phi)=\sum_{\xi\in F^\kappa}\Phi(\xi)$. For a model datum $(e,\varphi^0,\mu,D)$ and $\omega$ as above, the theta function is $\theta(g,h;\Phi)=\theta(\omega(g,h)\Phi)$ and the theta integral is the function on $U(W)(\mathbb A)$ $$I(\Phi)(h)=\int_D\theta(g,h;\Phi)\,d\mu(g).$$

## Local matching: a matching datum for two Hermitian spaces of complementary dimensions admits matched quotient maps

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D055](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.WeilTensorModels.LocalMatching/)

Notation and terms (base data, Hermitian data, model data, Weil representations, "sends $\varphi$ to $\varphi_1$ at $v$", "has derivative $f\mapsto f_1$ at infinity") are those of ([Setting for theta lifts between unitary groups of a CM extension: base data, Hermitian data, Weil representations, theta integrals](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.WeilTensorModels.Base.IsGood/)).

**Statement (local matching).** Every matching datum admits matched quotient maps.

The two terms are defined as follows.

**Matching datum.** A *matching datum* consists of number fields $F\subset E$ with an $F$-algebra homomorphism $\sigma\colon E\to E$ and

- base data $(\psi,(\iota_w),(m^\alpha_w),(m^\beta_w),P,(\tau_v),v_1,\tau_1)$, admissible for $\sigma$;
- geometric theta data of dimensions $(a,a')$, with integers $p,q$, Hermitian data $V,V',W$ and model data $M=(e,\varphi^0,\mu,D)$ and $M'=(e',\varphi'^0,\mu',D')$;
- homomorphisms $\alpha,\beta\colon\mathbb A_E^\times\to S^1$ and finite sets $S_0$, $Q$ of finite places of $F$; put $\nu=\alpha/\beta$ and $T=S_0\cup Q$;
- homomorphisms $\omega$ and $\omega'$ from $U(V)(\mathbb A)\times U(W)(\mathbb A)$ and $U(V')(\mathbb A)\times U(W)(\mathbb A)$ to the endomorphisms of the spaces of functions on $\mathbb A^\kappa$ and $\mathbb A^{\kappa'}$, where $\kappa=\{1..a\}\times\{1..a+a'\}$ and $\kappa'=\{1..a'\}\times\{1..a+a'\}$;

subject to the following conditions.

1. (Geometry.) The geometric theta data are admissible.
2. (Characters.) $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are continuous and trivial on $E^\times$. Let $\eta(x)=1$ for $x\in F^\times N_{E/F}(\mathbb A_E^\times)$ and $\eta(x)=-1$ for all other $x\in\mathbb A^\times$; then $\alpha(x)=\eta(x)^{a'}$ and $\beta(x)=\eta(x)^{a}$ for $x\in\mathbb A^\times$. For $z\in\mathbb A_E^\times$ with $z-1$ supported at infinity, $\alpha(z)=\prod_w\big(\iota_w(z)/|\iota_w(z)|\big)^{m^\alpha_w}$ and $\beta(z)=\prod_w\big(\iota_w(z)/|\iota_w(z)|\big)^{m^\beta_w}$.
3. (Ramification.) $S_0\cap Q=\emptyset$ and $T\subset P$. At every prime of $E$ above a place of $T$, $\nu$ is ramified (its local component is nontrivial on the local units); at every prime of $E$ not above $T$, $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are unramified. $v_1\notin T$, and the rational prime below $v_1$ has $[E:\mathbb Q]$ primes of $E$ above it.
4. (Weil representations.) $\omega$ is a Weil representation for $(V,W)$ with characters $(\beta,\beta/\alpha)$, and $\omega'$ is a Weil representation for $(V',W)$ with characters $(\alpha,\alpha/\beta)$, both for $\psi$ and the coordinates of $M$ and $M'$.

**Auxiliary notions.** Fix a matching datum.

*Polynomial Fock space.* A Schwartz function $f_0$ on $F_\infty^\kappa$ is a *Gaussian* for $M$ if there are signs $\varepsilon_w=\pm1$ such that, whenever $u,u'\in M_{a\times(a+a')}(\mathbb A_E)$ are supported at infinity with $\iota_w(u')=\varepsilon_w i\,\theta^V_w\,\iota_w(u)\,(\theta^W_w)^{\mathsf T}$ for all $w$, and $(\rho_\psi(tu))_t$, $(\rho_\psi(tu'))_t$ have derivatives $f_0\mapsto f$, $f_0\mapsto f'$ at infinity with $f,f'$ Schwartz, then $f'=if$. Here $tu$, for real $t$, is the product of $u$ with the adele whose component in $F_\infty$ is $t$ and whose finite part is $0$. The space $\mathcal P(M)$ is the smallest subspace of the Schwartz space of $F_\infty^\kappa$ that contains the Gaussians and contains $f_1$ whenever it contains $f$ and $(\rho_\psi(tu))_t$ has derivative $f\mapsto f_1$ at infinity for some $u$ supported at infinity. $\mathcal P(M')$ is defined in the same way from $M'$, $V'$.

*Split models.* Let $v$ be a finite place, $t\colon E\to F_v$ an $F$-embedding, $\pi,\bar\pi\colon\mathbb A_E\to F_v$ the ring homomorphisms induced by $t$ and $t\circ\sigma$ on the component at $v$, and $b\in GL_a(F_v)$, $c\in GL_{a+a'}(F_v)$. Write $\mathcal S(Z)$ for the locally constant functions of compact support on $Z$. A *split model of $M$ at $v$ of the first kind* (for $t,b,c$) is a linear map $\iota$ from functions on $F_v^\kappa$ to functions on $M_{a\times(a+a')}(F_v)$, bijective from $\mathcal S(F_v^\kappa)$ onto $\mathcal S(M_{a\times(a+a')}(F_v))$, such that for $\varphi,\varphi_1\in\mathcal S(F_v^\kappa)$ and $u$ supported at $v$ with $\rho_\psi(u)$ sending $\varphi$ to $\varphi_1$ at $v$: - if $\bar\pi(u)=0$, then $(\iota\varphi_1)(Y)=(\iota\varphi)(Y+\mathrm{co}(u))$ for all $Y$; - if $\pi(u)=0$, then $(\iota\varphi_1)(\mathrm{co}(x))=\psi(\langle\!\langle x,u\rangle\!\rangle)\,(\iota\varphi)(\mathrm{co}(x))$ for all $x$ supported at $v$ with $\bar\pi(x)=0$;

where $\mathrm{co}(u)=b^{-1}\pi(u)\,c^{-\mathsf T}$. A split model *of the second kind* is defined in the same way with $\pi$ and $\bar\pi$ exchanged and $\mathrm{co}(u)=-\,b^{\mathsf T}\,t(J_V)\,\bar\pi(u)\,t(J_W)^{\mathsf T}c$. The same definitions apply to $M'$, with $V'$, $a'$ in place of $V$, $a$.

*Local characters.* For $v\in P$ and a homomorphism $\chi\colon\mathbb A_E^\times\to S^1$, $\chi_v$ is the character of $F_v^\times$ obtained from the component of $\chi$ at the prime $\{x\in\mathcal O_E:\tau_v(x)\in\mathfrak m_v\}$ of $E$ through the identification of its completion with $F_v$. $|\cdot|_v$ is the normalized absolute value of $F_v$ and $\varpi_v$ a fixed uniformizer.

**Matched quotient maps.** For a matching datum, *matched quotient maps* consist of a complex vector space $\Pi_\infty$ with linear maps $p_\infty$, $p'_\infty$ to $\Pi_\infty$ from the Schwartz spaces of $F_\infty^\kappa$ and $F_\infty^{\kappa'}$, and for every finite place $v$ a complex vector space $\Pi_v$ with linear maps $p_v$, $p'_v$ to $\Pi_v$ from the spaces of functions on $F_v^\kappa$ and $F_v^{\kappa'}$, such that:

*At infinity.* Below, $\gamma$ denotes a continuous homomorphism from $\mathbb R$ to the adelic unitary group in question with values in the indicated set $U(\cdot)_\infty$, $f\in\mathcal P(M)$, $f'\in\mathcal P(M')$, and $f_1$, $f'_1$ are Schwartz functions. 1. $p_\infty(\mathcal P(M))=\Pi_\infty=p'_\infty(\mathcal P(M'))$, and $\Pi_\infty\ne0$. 2. If $(\omega(\gamma(t),1))_t$ has derivative $f\mapsto f_1$ at infinity, with $\gamma$ in $U(V)_\infty$, then $p_\infty(f_1)=0$. If $k\in K_{V,\infty}$ and $\omega(k,1)$ sends $f$ to $f_1$ at infinity, then $p_\infty(f_1)=p_\infty(f)$. The same holds for $p'_\infty$, $\omega'$, $V'$. 3. Suppose $p_\infty(f)=p'_\infty(f')$. If $(\omega(1,\gamma(t)))_t$ and $(\omega'(1,\gamma(t)))_t$ have derivatives $f\mapsto f_1$ and $f'\mapsto f'_1$ at infinity, with $\gamma$ in $U(W)_\infty$, then $p_\infty(f_1)=p'_\infty(f'_1)$. If $k\in K_{W,\infty}$ and $\omega(1,k)$, $\omega'(1,k)$ send $f$ to $f_1$ and $f'$ to $f'_1$ at infinity, then $p_\infty(f_1)=p'_\infty(f'_1)$. 4. A subspace $N\subset\Pi_\infty$ is $0$ or $\Pi_\infty$ as soon as $p_\infty(f)\in N$ implies $p_\infty(f_1)\in N$ whenever $(\omega(1,\gamma(t)))_t$ has derivative $f\mapsto f_1$ at infinity for some $\gamma$ in $U(W)_\infty$, and whenever $\omega(1,k)$ sends $f$ to $f_1$ at infinity for some $k\in K_{W,\infty}$.

*At a finite place $v$.* Below $\varphi,\varphi_1\in\mathcal S(F_v^\kappa)$ and $\varphi',\varphi'_1\in\mathcal S(F_v^{\kappa'})$. 1. $p_v(\mathcal S(F_v^\kappa))=\Pi_v=p'_v(\mathcal S(F_v^{\kappa'}))$, and $\Pi_v\ne0$. 2. $p_v(\varphi_1-\varphi)=0$ whenever $\omega(g,1)$ sends $\varphi$ to $\varphi_1$ at $v$ for some $g\in U(V)_v$; likewise for $p'_v$, $\omega'$, $V'$. 3. If $h\in U(W)_v$, $\omega(1,h)$ sends $\varphi$ to $\varphi_1$ at $v$, $\omega'(1,h)$ sends $\varphi'$ to $\varphi'_1$ at $v$, and $p_v(\varphi)=p'_v(\varphi')$, then $p_v(\varphi_1)=p'_v(\varphi'_1)$. 4. A subspace $N\subset\Pi_v$ is $0$ or $\Pi_v$ as soon as $p_v(\varphi)\in N$ implies $p_v(\varphi_1)\in N$ whenever $\omega(1,h)$ sends $\varphi$ to $\varphi_1$ at $v$ for some $h\in U(W)_v$. 5. If $v\notin T$: $p_v(\varphi^0_v)=p'_v(\varphi'^0_v)\neq0$. 6. If $v\in T$ (so $v\in P$): there are a split model $\iota$ of $M$ at $v$ of the first kind for $(\tau_v,b^V_v,b^W_v)$ and a split model $\iota'$ of $M'$ at $v$ of the second kind for $(\tau_v,b^{V'}_v,b^W_v)$, both sending the distinguished function ($\varphi^0_v$, $\varphi'^0_v$) to the indicator function of the matrices with entries in $\mathcal O_v$, such that for all $\varphi,\varphi'$ $$p_v(\varphi)=p'_v(\varphi')\iff Z(\iota\varphi)=Z'(\iota'\varphi')\ \text{as functions on } GL_{a+a'}(F_v),$$ where, with $K=GL_a(\mathcal O_v)$, Haar measures on $GL_a(F_v)$, $GL_{a'}(F_v)$ giving the integral points measure $1$, and $n$ running over the non-increasing $a$-tuples of integers, $$Z(\Phi)(h)=\beta_v(\det h)\,|\det h|_v^{-a/2}\sum_{n}\int_{K\varpi_v^{n}K}\Phi\big(g\,(1_a\,|\,0)\,h^{-\mathsf T}\big)\,\nu_v(\det g)\,|\det g|_v^{(a+a')/2}\,dg,$$ $$Z'(\Psi)(h)=\alpha_v(\det h)\,|\det h|_v^{a'/2}\int_{GL_{a'}(F_v)}\Psi\big(g\,(0\,|\,1_{a'})\,h\big)\,\nu_v(\det g)\,|\det g|_v^{(a+a')/2}\,dg.$$

## Theta integrals agree: for a matching datum the two theta integrals are proportional on matching functions, with a unique nonzero constant, and the constant changes by an L-value when a hyperbolic plane is removed

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D093](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.WeilTensorModels.ThetaIntegralsAgree/)

Notation is that of ([Setting for theta lifts between unitary groups of a CM extension: base data, Hermitian data, Weil representations, theta integrals](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.WeilTensorModels.Base.IsGood/)); matching data, matched quotient maps, split models and the local characters $\chi_v$ are defined in ([Local matching: a matching datum for two Hermitian spaces of complementary dimensions admits matched quotient maps](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke.WeilTensorModels.LocalMatching/)). For a matching datum, $I(\Phi)$ and $I'(\Phi')$ denote the theta integrals on $U(W)(\mathbb A)$ formed with $(M,\omega)$ and $(M',\omega')$, and $\theta'(g,h;\Phi')=\theta(\omega'(g,h)\Phi')$.

The statement has three parts. Part 1 concerns one matching datum; parts 2 and 3 compare two.

**Definitions for part 1.** Fix a matching datum with matched quotient maps $(\Pi_\infty,p_\infty,p'_\infty,(\Pi_v,p_v,p'_v)_v)$.

*Full-rank support.* For a finite place $v$ and an $F$-embedding $t\colon E\to F_v$, a function $\varphi\in\mathcal S(F_v^{\kappa'})$ has *full-rank support for $t$* if there is a split model $\iota$ of $M'$ at $v$ of the second kind for $(t,1,1)$ with $(\iota\varphi)(Y)=0$ for every $Y\in M_{a'\times(a+a')}(F_v)$ of rank different from $a'$. $\mathcal S^\circ$ is the span of the functions $x\mapsto f(x_\infty)\prod_v\varphi_v(x_v)$ on $\mathbb A^{\kappa'}$ with $f$ Schwartz, each $\varphi_v$ in $\mathcal S(F_v^{\kappa'})$, $\varphi_v=\varphi'^0_v$ for all but finitely many $v$, and $\varphi_{v_1}$ of full-rank support for $\tau_1$.

*Matching functions.* Functions $\Phi$ on $\mathbb A^\kappa$ and $\Phi'$ on $\mathbb A^{\kappa'}$ *match algebraically* if there are a finite set $T^+\supset T$ of finite places and finite decompositions $$\Phi=\sum_i f_i\otimes\textstyle\bigotimes_v\varphi_{i,v},\qquad \Phi'=\sum_j f'_j\otimes\bigotimes_v\varphi'_{j,v},$$ with $f_i\in\mathcal P(M)$, $f'_j\in\mathcal P(M')$, all $\varphi_{i,v}$, $\varphi'_{j,v}$ locally constant of compact support and equal to $\varphi^0_v$, $\varphi'^0_v$ for $v\notin T^+$, such that $$\sum_i p_\infty(f_i)\otimes\bigotimes_{v\in T^+}p_v(\varphi_{i,v})=\sum_j p'_\infty(f'_j)\otimes\bigotimes_{v\in T^+}p'_v(\varphi'_{j,v})\quad\text{in }\ \Pi_\infty\otimes\bigotimes_{v\in T^+}\Pi_v.$$ They *match* if $\Phi'\in\mathcal S^\circ$ and there are finite decompositions $\Phi=\sum_i f_i\otimes g_i$, $\Phi'=\sum_j f'_j\otimes g'_j$ ($f_i,f'_j$ Schwartz at infinity; $g_i,g'_j$ locally constant of compact support on the finite adeles) and sequences of Schwartz functions $f_i^{(k)}\to f_i$, $f_j'^{(k)}\to f'_j$ in the Schwartz topology such that, for every $k$, $\sum_j f_j'^{(k)}\otimes g'_j\in\mathcal S^\circ$ and $\sum_i f_i^{(k)}\otimes g_i$, $\sum_j f_j'^{(k)}\otimes g'_j$ match algebraically.

*Proportionality constant.* A number $c\in\mathbb C$ is a *proportionality constant* if $I(\Phi)(h)=c\,I'(\Phi')(h)$ for all matching $\Phi,\Phi'$ and all $h\in U(W)(\mathbb A)$.

**Part 1.** For every matching datum:

(a) For every $\Phi\in\mathcal S(\mathbb A^\kappa)$ and every $h$, $g\mapsto\theta(g,h;\Phi)$ is integrable on $D$. For every $\Phi'\in\mathcal S^\circ$: for each $N$ there are $C$, $k$ with $|\theta'(g,h;\Phi')|\cdot\big(\inf_{\gamma\in U(V')(F)}\mathrm{ht}(\gamma g)\big)^N\le C\,\mathrm{ht}(h)^k$ for all $g,h$; $g\mapsto\theta'(g,h;\Phi')$ is integrable on $D'$ for every $h$; and $|I'(\Phi')(h)|\le C\,\mathrm{ht}(h)^N$ for some $C,N$ and all $h$.

(b) For all matched quotient maps: $I'(\Phi')\neq0$ for some $\Phi'\in\mathcal S^\circ$; there is exactly one proportionality constant; and it is nonzero.

(c) For all matched quotient maps: $\mathcal S^\circ$ is stable under $\omega'(g,h)$ for all $(g,h)$; and for every finite place $v\notin T$ such that the rational prime below $v$ has $[E:\mathbb Q]$ primes of $E$ above it, - for $\varphi\in\mathcal S(F_v^\kappa)$, $p_v(\varphi)=0$ if and only if $\varphi$ lies in the span of the differences $\varphi_1-\varphi_0$ with $\varphi_0,\varphi_1\in\mathcal S(F_v^\kappa)$ and $\omega(g,1)$ sending $\varphi_0$ to $\varphi_1$ at $v$ for some $g\in U(V)_v$; the same holds for $p'_v$, $\omega'$, $V'$; - for every $F$-embedding $t\colon E\to F_v$, every element of $\Pi_v$ is $p'_v(\varphi)$ for some $\varphi$ of full-rank support for $t$; - every linear functional on the space of functions on $F_v^{\kappa'}$ that vanishes on the span just described (for $\omega'$, $V'$) equals $l_0\circ p'_v$ on $\mathcal S(F_v^{\kappa'})$ for some linear functional $l_0$ on $\Pi_v$.

**Definitions for parts 2 and 3.** *Removing a hyperbolic plane.* Let $Y$, $Y_1$ be Hermitian data of dimensions $n$, $n_1$, with forms $\langle\,,\rangle$, and $1\le i_0<i_1\le n$. A *reduction from $Y$ to $Y_1$ at positions $(i_0,i_1)$* consists of $x,y\in E^n$, $\iota\in M_{n\times n_1}(E)$ and a fractional ideal $\mathfrak b$ of $E$ with: $n=n_1+2$; $\langle x,x\rangle=\langle y,y\rangle=0$, $\langle x,y\rangle=1$; $\langle\iota u,x\rangle=\langle\iota u,y\rangle=0$ for all $u\in E^{n_1}$; $\iota^{\mathsf T}J_Y\sigma(\iota)=J_{Y_1}$; $u\in L_{Y_1}$ if and only if $\iota u\in L_Y$; every $z\in L_Y$ is $sx+\iota u+ty$ with $sx,ty\in L_Y$ and $u\in L_{Y_1}$; $\mathfrak b=\{s\in E:sx\in L_Y\}$; for every infinite place $w$, $\theta^Y_w\,\iota_w(\iota)=\iota_w(\iota)\,\theta^{Y_1}_w$ and $\iota_w(y)$ is a nonzero multiple of $\theta^Y_w\iota_w(x)$; and for every $v\in P$, columns $i_0$ and $i_1$ of $b^Y_v$ are $\tau_v(x)$ and $\tau_v(y)$ and the other columns, in order, are the columns of $\tau_v(\iota)\,b^{Y_1}_v$.
