## Realisation records over the stages of the construction, and properties that hold at all admissible realisation records

[Definition / hypothesis THETA.D254](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.Bl.AtDat/)

Several theorems of the construction assert a property of every realisation of a rational family that the construction can produce, for all sufficiently large parameters. This page defines the objects that such a statement quantifies over, the *realisation records*, and the phrase "*holds at all admissible realisation records*".

Contexts, bases, frames, twist data and "eventually for large bases" are as in ([Contexts, and statements that hold eventually in the parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Mem.Top.EvG/)). Stages, the datum $D(X)$ assembled from stages $X$, and column inputs are as in ([Points of the construction, properties of points, attached records and statements over families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Fc.Row/)). Rational families, realisations with polynomial and inert Hecke labels, faces and geometric cores are as in ([Rational families of forms, their realisations by theta maps, and the rationality test](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Rat.RowTest/)).

**Notation.** Throughout, $\mathcal K$ is a context with scale $(x,\varepsilon,J)$, $B$ is a base over $\mathcal K$, $f$ is a frame for $B$ and $T$ is twist data for $B$. We write

- $E$ for the CM field of $B$, $F$ for its maximal totally real subfield and $K\subseteq E$ for the subfield of $E$ that belongs to the host field;
- $m$ and $n$ for the ranks so named, and $r$ for the row rank;
- $g_0,g_1,p_\delta,p_\delta'$ for the level primes so named, and $d$ for the prime $d$ of the host field;
- $\mathbb L$ for the coefficient field of $B$, $K_s\subseteq\mathbb L_0\subseteq\mathbb L$ for its two subfields and $\jmath\colon\mathbb L\to\mathbb C$ for its embedding;
- $\delta,\delta'$ for the two characters of $f$ so named; $\mathfrak p_0,\mathfrak p_1$ for the primes of $K$ above $g_0,g_1$ that $f$ contains; and $\omega_0,\omega_1$ for the injective characters $\omega_k\colon(\mathbb Z/g_k\mathbb Z)^\times\to\mathbb C^\times$ that $f$ contains;
- $\Pi$ for the finite set of primes of $T$ and $\psi(w)$ for its character at a finite place $w$ of $\mathbb L$.

For a finite place $w$ of $\mathbb L$ with residue characteristic $\ell$, the *twisted character at $w$* is the Hecke character $$\xi^{\rm tw}_w=\begin{cases}\delta'\,\psi(w)&\text{if }\ell=p_\delta,\\ \delta\,\psi(w)&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases}$$

**Exact pairs and saturated twist data.** The pair $(B,f)$ is *exact* if the following two conditions hold.

1. There are a ray class field $R_E\subseteq\mathbb C$ of $E$ of modulus $g_0g_1p_\delta p_\delta'$, a Weierstrass curve over a subfield of $\mathbb C$ satisfying a condition that depends on $d$, and a basis attached to the ideal classes of $E$, such that $\jmath(K_s)$ is the compositum of the following subfields of $\mathbb C$: the image of $E$ under the embedding $E\to\mathbb C$ of the host field; a field $M'$ attached to the curve and to $d$; the field $R_E$; the cyclotomic fields of orders $N_G$, $g_0(g_0-1)$, $g_1(g_1-1)$, $3^4$ and $5^4$, where $N_G$ is a natural number of the preliminary data; the fields generated by the values of the characters $\mu_{0,j},\eta,\delta,\delta'$ of $f$ and by the values of $\omega_0,\omega_1$; and $m$ fields $K_{\Psi,1},\dots,K_{\Psi,m}$ determined by the basis and the preliminary data.
2. The preliminary data contain Hermitian spaces $X'$ and $X_m$ with chosen bases and an embedding $X'\to X_m$. For every $a$ less than the rank of $X'$, the embedding carries the $a$-th basis vector of $X'$ to the $a$-th basis vector of $X_m$.

The twist data $T$ are *saturated* if $\Pi$ contains every prime number $p$ such that $p\in\{g_0,g_1,d\}$ or $p\le n\,x\exp(x^{\varepsilon/3})$.

**Column inputs and completions.** A column input $c$ for $(B,f,T)$ contains, among its components, a finite index set $\iota$ with a distinguished element $\mu_0$; a Hecke character $\xi_a$ of $E$ for every $a\in\iota$, with $\xi_{\mu_0}=1$; a finite set $Q(a)$ of primes of $E$ for every $a\in\iota$; a map $\mathrm{sel}$ from the finite places of $F$ to the finite places of $E$; and a finite set $P_c$ of finite places of $E$, the *level places*, with a finite group $G_c$ attached to them and to $r$. It determines two subgroups $P_0,P_1\subseteq G_c$ with characters $\psi_i\colon P_i\to\mathbb L^\times$. The set of characters $\{\xi_a:a\in\iota\}$ is $$\{1,\delta,\delta'\}\cup\{\xi^{\rm tw}_w: w\text{ a finite place of }\mathbb L\}.$$ A column input determines the first of the six stages that follow $B$. A *completion* $Z$ of $c$ is a choice of the other five; we write $X(c,Z)$ for the resulting seven stages and $N_Z$ for the fifth of the six.

**Type data at $g_0$ and $g_1$.** The stage $N_Z$, and through it the datum $D(X(c,Z))$, contains the following: for $k\in\{0,1\}$, a finite set $\mathcal V_k$; an injective map $v\mapsto w_{k,v}$ from $\mathcal V_k$ to the places of $E$ above $g_k$, no two of whose values are complex conjugate; elements $\chi_{k,v,j}\in(\mathbb Z/g_k\mathbb Z)^\times$ for $v\in\mathcal V_k$ and $0\le j<m$; a multiplicative map $t_k\colon\overline{\mathbb F}_{g_k}\to\mathbb C$; and an element $\epsilon_k(p)\in\mathbb C[\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb F_{g_k})]$ for every polynomial $p\in\mathbb F_{g_k}[T]$. It also contains a finite group $G_D$, whose elements have a component at every pair $(k,v)$ with $v\in\mathcal V_k$, and an element $e_0$ of the group algebra $\mathbb C[G_D]$; we write $e_0(h)$ for the coefficient of $e_0$ at $h\in G_D$.

**The field condition.** The base contains, besides $F\subseteq E$, a number field $M$ with an involution and a finite family of embeddings $\iota_\tau\colon E\to M$, $\tau\in\mathcal T_E$; the field $E$ carries an involution $x\mapsto\bar x$. The *field condition* for $B$ is the conjunction of the following.

- $F$ is totally real, $[E:F]=2$, the involution of $E$ fixes $F$, $E$ is totally complex, and $\varphi(\bar x)=\overline{\varphi(x)}$ for every embedding $\varphi\colon E\to\mathbb C$ and every $x\in E$.
- $M$ is totally complex and normal over $\mathbb Q$, and some embedding $M\to\mathbb C$ carries the involution of $M$ to complex conjugation.
- Every $\iota_\tau$ commutes with the involutions; $|\mathcal T_E|=[F:\mathbb Q]$; the map $\tau\mapsto\iota_\tau$ is injective; no $\iota_\tau$ equals $x\mapsto\iota_{\tau'}(\bar x)$ for any $\tau'$; and every embedding $E\to M$ is $\iota_\tau$ or $x\mapsto\iota_\tau(\bar x)$ for some $\tau$.
- The subfield $\{x\in E:\iota_\tau(x)=\iota_{\tau'}(x)\text{ for all }\tau,\tau'\}$ of $E$ has degree $2$ over $\mathbb Q$.

**Pinned column inputs.** A column input $c$ for $(B,f,T)$ is *pinned* if the following eight conditions hold.

- (P1) For every $a\in\iota$, the local constants and the index data of $c$ at $a$ are the ones determined by the characters of $c$, the map $\mathrm{sel}$ and the tuple of $a$.
- (P2) The jet numbers of $c$ lie in $\jmath(\mathbb L)$. These are complex numbers attached to an index $a\in\iota$, a word, an integer $0\le i<m$, a pair of lattices, an admissible pair $(c',x)$ and a monomial exponent of prescribed degrees.
- (P3) The field condition holds for $B$.
- (P4) For every finite place $v$ of $F$, the place $\mathrm{sel}(v)$ of $E$ lies above $v$.
- (P5) If two finite places $v,v'$ of $F$ lie above the same prime number, then $\mathrm{sel}(v)$ and $\mathrm{sel}(v')$ lie above the same prime of $K$.
- (P6) There are primes $\mathfrak q,\mathfrak q'$ of $K$ above $p_\delta$ and $p_\delta'$ such that $\mathrm{sel}(v)$ lies above $\mathfrak q$ for every place $v$ of $F$ above $p_\delta$, and above $\mathfrak q'$ for every place $v$ of $F$ above $p_\delta'$.
- (P7) For every $a\in\iota$ and every prime $P$ of $E$ at which $\xi_a$ is unramified, the value of $\xi_a$ at a uniformiser of $P$ lies in $\jmath(\mathbb L)$.
- (P8) For every $a\in\iota$ and every $P\in Q(a)$, the field $\mathbb L$ contains a primitive root of unity of order $\mathrm N(P)$, the cardinality of $\mathcal O_E/P$.

**Realisation records.** Let $c$ be a column input for $(B,f,T)$ and $Z$ a completion of it; put $X=X(c,Z)$ and $D=D(X)$. Suppose that the field condition holds for $B$, and let $W$ be a set of finite places of $\mathbb L$. Put $$\iota_W=\{a\in\iota:\ \xi_a=\xi^{\rm tw}_w\text{ for some }w\in W\}.$$ A *realisation record* over $(c,Z,W)$ consists of the following data (R1)–(R5), subject to the conditions (R6)–(R10).

- (R1) A finite set $\mathfrak X$ of functions $G_c\to\mathbb L$ and, for every $0\le j<m$, a map $\mathrm{tr}_j$ of the set of $\mathbb L$-valued functions on pairs $(I,s)$ to itself, where $I$ runs over the index set of inert labels of $c$ and $s$ over the finite lists of natural numbers. Together with $c$ and $\iota_W$ these determine a rational family $\mathcal A=\mathcal A(c,\iota_W,\mathfrak X,\mathrm{tr})$ over $\mathbb L$ with finite group $G_c$, with $m$ lifts, with tuples indexed by $\{\mu_0\}\cup\iota_W$ and base tuple $\mu_0$, with character set $\mathfrak X$ and transfer maps $\mathrm{tr}_j$, and with distinguished infinite place the place of $\mathbb L$ induced by $\jmath$.
- (R2) A geometric core over $D$ with coefficient field $F_c\subseteq\mathbb C$, and a field homomorphism $F_c\to\mathbb L$ whose composite with $\jmath$ is the inclusion $F_c\subseteq\mathbb C$. The image of $F_c$ in $\mathbb L$ is contained in $\mathbb L_0$ and contains $K_s$.
- (R3) A realisation $R$ of $\mathcal A$ over $D$ and $F_c$, with polynomial and inert Hecke labels. We write $G_R$ for its finite group at its level places, $e_R\colon G_c\to G_R$ for its identification of groups, $\phi_j$ for its lift scalars, $\mu_R(w)$ for its tuple at a finite place $w$ of $\mathbb L$, and $S_R$ for its set of twisted places.
- (R4) An isomorphism of groups $c_G\colon G_R\to G_D$.
- (R5) A *geometric half*: for every $0\le j<m$ a weight $\kappa_j$ at stage $j+1$ in the sense of ([Cuspidal sections and their integral Fourier data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Geo.FormsPkg/)); for every $0\le j<m$ and every embedding $\sigma\colon\mathbb L\to\mathbb C$ a vector $v_{j,\sigma}$ in the model of $R$ at $(j,\sigma)$; and for every $0\le j<m$ one-step geometric data $O_j$ of ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)) for the core and the $j$-th lift, with auxiliary twist set a singleton mapped to the base tuple. These satisfy the hypotheses of ([One-map and geometric comparison laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)) with all phases equal to $1$, and each $O_j$ satisfies the conditions of ([Geometric theta class evaluations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.OMPins/)). The phase comparison is required only when $R$ satisfies the uniform rational-structure condition of ([Analytic theta maps and primitive row comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.ExAt/)).
- (R6) The *face of the column input* is the face of $D$ and $\mathcal A$ with subgroups $P_0,P_1$ and characters $\psi_0,\psi_1$, with source-coordinate data indexed by the empty set, with common period space $\mathbb C$ and zero period subspace, and with all numerical data equal to $0$. The realisation $R$ and this face satisfy the base conditions and the inert-label condition of ([Analytic realizations of rational Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.BaseW/)), and seven further conditions on $R$ and the face. One of the seven concerns the lift scalars. Given (R8), it says for every $j$: if for some complex number $u$ of absolute value $1$ every vector $u\,\theta_{j,\mu_0}(j_V(j,\mu_0)x)$, $x\in L_{j,\mu_0}$, is the image of a rational coordinate vector of $R$ at the $j$-th lift, under the map of $R$ at the embedding chosen for the distinguished infinite place, then so is every vector $\theta_{j,\mu_0}(j_V(j,\mu_0)x)$.
- (R7) The realisation $R$, the face of the column input and $c_G$ satisfy eleven conditions that tie $R$ to the datum $D$. Four of them are the following. The matrices $\beta_V(j)$ and $\beta_X$ of $R$ are the identity. There are a bijection $(k,v)\mapsto P_{k,v}$ from $\mathcal V_0\sqcup\mathcal V_1$ to the level places of $R$ and isomorphisms $\mathbb Z/g_k\mathbb Z\simeq\mathcal O_E/P_{k,v}$ such that $P_{k,v}=w_{k,v}$, $P_{k,v}$ lies above $\mathfrak p_k$, the component of $e_R(g)$ at $P_{k,v}$ is the image under the isomorphism of the component of $c_G(e_R(g))$ at $(k,v)$, and $\jmath$ maps the coefficient at $g$ of the idempotent $\sum_{\chi\in\mathfrak X}\frac{\chi(1)}{|G_c|}\sum_{h}\chi(h^{-1})h$ of $\mathfrak X$ to $e_0(c_G(e_R(g)))$, for every $g\in G_c$. For every $0\le j<m$ and every idele $y$ of $E$ with trivial infinite component, the value $\mu_{0,j+1}(y)$ lies in the image of $F_c$ in $\mathbb C$. The values of $\psi_0$ and the coefficients of the idempotent of $\mathfrak X$ lie in the image of $F_c$ in $\mathbb L$.
- (R8) $\phi_j=1$ for all $j$. If some maps $\mathrm{tr}'_j$, $0\le j<m$, have the two properties required of the maps in conclusion (C) of ([Properties of the theta maps of a realised rational family: Hecke equivariance, inner product identities, rationality, and separation of Hecke types](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.Closing3.primRows3_typesSep3_alt/)), then the maps $\mathrm{tr}_j$ of $\mathcal A$ have the first of these properties and the family $\mathcal A$ satisfies its own condition of separation of inert types.
- (R9) The level data of $R$ are those of $c$: its frame is $f$ placed at $X$, its level places are $P_c$, its character at a tuple $a$ is $\xi_a$, its set of primes at $a$ is $Q(a)$, its matrices $\beta$ are the identity, and the conditions of ([Finite levels and character matching](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.LevCh.Is/)) hold with base tuple $\mu_0$. The identification $e_R$ is the identity at every level place.
- (R10) Put $e=c_G\circ e_R$. The set $\mathfrak X$ is the set of functions $\chi\colon G_c\to\mathbb L$ that are characters of irreducible representations of $G_c$ over an algebraic closure of $\mathbb L$ and satisfy $\sum_{g\in G_c}e_0(e(g))\,\jmath(\chi(g))=\jmath(\chi(1))$. A finite place $w$ of $\mathbb L$ belongs to $S_R$ if and only if $w\in W$; and the character $\xi_{\mu_R(w)}$ equals $\xi^{\rm tw}_w$ if $w\in W$ and $1$ otherwise.

The *realisation of the record* is $R$, and its face is the face of the column input.

**Admissible realisation records.** A realisation record over $(c,Z,W)$ is *admissible* if it satisfies the following four conditions.

(A1) *The type data agree with the frame.* For each prime $g$ fix once and for all a pair $(\tau_g,(\gamma_{g,e})_{e\ge1})$ as in the polynomial parametrization of ([Cuspidal characters of finite general linear groups](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.greenRegularCharacters_holds/)): an injective multiplicative map $\tau_g\colon\overline{\mathbb F}_g^{\times}\to\mathbb C^{\times}$, extended by $\tau_g(0)=0$, and generators $\gamma_{g,e}$ of $\mathbb F_{g^e}^\times$ compatible under the norm maps. Let $x_k$ be the element of $(\mathbb Z/g_k\mathbb Z)^\times=\mathbb F_{g_k}^\times$ with $\tau_{g_k}(x_k)=\omega_k(\gamma_{g_k,1})$. The condition is that, for $k\in\{0,1\}$:

- every $w_{k,v}$ lies above $\mathfrak p_k$;
- $t_k=\tau_{g_k}$;
- $\chi_{k,v,j}=x_k^{\,2(j+1)}$ for all $v\in\mathcal V_k$ and $0\le j<m$;
- $\epsilon_k(p)=\sum_\chi\frac{\chi(1)}{|\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb F_{g_k})|}\sum_{y}\chi(y^{-1})\,y$ for every $p$, the outer sum over the irreducible characters $\chi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb F_{g_k})$ whose label is $p$. A character has *label* $p$ if $p$ is the product of monic irreducible polynomials $p_1,\dots,p_s$ with nonzero constant terms and $\chi$ occurs in the character obtained by parabolic induction from the cuspidal characters $\chi_{p_1},\dots,\chi_{p_s}$ of that parametrization.

(A2) *The level places.* The map $(k,v)\mapsto w_{k,v}$ is a bijection from $\mathcal V_0\sqcup\mathcal V_1$ onto the set $P_c$ of level places of $c$, and every $w_{k,v}$ lies above $\mathfrak p_k$. The element $e_0$ is a central idempotent of $\mathbb C[G_D]$.

(A3) *Existence of the analytic theta maps.* The eight existence assertions of ([Analytic theta maps and primitive row comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.ExAt/)) hold for $R$.

(A4) *Comparison with algebraic cusp forms.* Use the notation of ([One-map and geometric comparison laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)) for the core, the realisation $R$ and the geometric half of the record, with $F_c$ as the coefficient field of the original moduli: $C_j$ is the $F_c$-vector space of algebraic cusp forms of weight $\kappa_j$ at stage $j+1$; $H_{j,\sigma}$ is the complex Hilbert space of $R$ at the $j$-th lift and an embedding $\sigma\colon\mathbb L\to\mathbb C$; $\sigma_c=\sigma|_{F_c}$; $\Phi_{j,\sigma_c}(f)$ is the adelic function of $f\in C_j$; and $\mathrm{ev}_v\colon H_{j,\sigma}\to\{\text{functions on the adelic unitary group of }W_j\}$ is evaluation against a tuple $v$ of vectors of the model. A form $f\in C_j$ is *integral* if $y(f)\ge0$ for every norm-test point $y$ at every finite prime of $F_c$. The *comparison map* $J_{j,\sigma}\colon C_j\to H_{j,\sigma}$ is defined as follows. If $\sigma_c$ is allowed and the Hermitian space of the core at $(j+1,\sigma_c)$ is $W_j$, it is a $\sigma_c$-semilinear map $L$ with $\mathrm{ev}_{v_{j,\sigma}}(L(f))=\Phi_{j,\sigma_c}(f)$ for all $f$, when such a map exists. If $\sigma_c$ is not allowed, it is a $\sigma_c$-semilinear map $L$ with $\mathrm{ev}_v(L(f))=\overline{\Phi_{j,\bar\sigma_c}(f)}$ for all $f$, for some unit tuple $v$, when the Hermitian space of the core at $(j+1,\bar\sigma_c)$ is $W_j$ and such $v$ and $L$ exist. In all other cases $J_{j,\sigma}=0$. The conditions are the following seven, for every $0\le j<m$ and every embedding $\sigma\colon\mathbb L\to\mathbb C$.

1. If $\sigma_c$ is allowed and the Hermitian space of the core at $(j+1,\sigma_c)$ is $W_j$, there is a $\sigma_c$-semilinear map $L\colon C_j\to H_{j,\sigma}$ with $\mathrm{ev}_{v_{j,\sigma}}(L(f))=\Phi_{j,\sigma_c}(f)$ for all $f\in C_j$.
2. If $\sigma_c$ is not allowed and the Hermitian space of the core at $(j+1,\bar\sigma_c)$ is $W_j$, there are a unit tuple $v$ and a $\sigma_c$-semilinear map $L\colon C_j\to H_{j,\sigma}$ with $\mathrm{ev}_v(L(f))=\overline{\Phi_{j,\bar\sigma_c}(f)}$ for all $f\in C_j$.
3. If $\sigma_c$ is allowed, $\mathrm{ev}_{v_{j,\sigma}}$ is injective.
4. The map $J_{j,\sigma}$ sends every finite $F_c$-linearly independent family of $C_j$ to a $\mathbb C$-linearly independent family, and its image spans $H_{j,\sigma}$ over $\mathbb C$.
5. There is a homomorphism $\rho_j\colon G_c\to\mathrm{GL}_{F_c}(C_j)$, independent of $\sigma$, such that $\rho_j(g)$ maps integral forms to integral forms and $$J_{j,\sigma}(\rho_j(g)f)=\sigma(\chi_j(g))\;A_{j,\sigma}(g)\,J_{j,\sigma}(f)$$ for all $g\in G_c$, all $f\in C_j$ and all $\sigma$. Here both sides are read in the function realisation of $H_{j,\sigma}$, $A_{j,\sigma}(g)$ is the operator of $R$ on that realisation attached to $e_R(g)$, and $\chi_j\colon G_c\to\mathbb L^\times$ is a homomorphism such that $\jmath(\chi_j(g))=\prod_{P}\chi_{j+1,P}(\det e_R(g)_P)$, the product over the level places $P$ of $R$; if no such homomorphism exists, $\chi_j=1$.
6. The core has, for every finite prime $\lambda$ of $F_c$, a set of valuations on the ring that contains $C_j$. The forms $f\in C_j$ with $v(f)\ge0$ for every such valuation $v$ at every $\lambda$ form an $\mathcal O_{F_c}$-lattice of $C_j$: a finitely generated $\mathcal O_{F_c}$-submodule that spans $C_j$ over $F_c$.
7. The dimension $n_j$ of the rational form of $R$ at the $j$-th lift equals $\dim_{F_c}C_j$.

**Properties that hold at all admissible realisation records.** Let $\mathcal P$ be a property of a context $\mathcal K$, a base $B$, a frame $f$, twist data $T$, a column input $c$, a completion $Z$, a set $W$ of finite places of $\mathbb L$ and a realisation record $z$ over $(c,Z,W)$. We say that *$\mathcal P$ holds at all admissible realisation records* if the following holds eventually for large bases: whenever $(B,f)$ is exact and $T$ is saturated, $\mathcal P(\mathcal K,B,f,T,c,Z,W,z)$ holds for every pinned column input $c$ for $(B,f,T)$, every completion $Z$ of $c$, every set $W$ of finite places of $\mathbb L$ and every admissible realisation record $z$ over $(c,Z,W)$.
