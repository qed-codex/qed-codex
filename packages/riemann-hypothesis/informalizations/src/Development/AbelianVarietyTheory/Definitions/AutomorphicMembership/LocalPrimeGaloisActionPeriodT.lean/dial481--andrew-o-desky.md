## Local period rings and coefficient comparisons

[Definition / hypothesis PHT.D135](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.LocRings/)

This page fixes the data with respect to which the catalogue states the properties of an $\ell$-adic Galois representation at a place above $\ell$. *Local period data* for a prime $\ell$ are two rings $B$ and $\Delta$ that carry the structures of Fontaine's rings $B_{\rm cris}$ and $B_{\rm dR}$ (a Galois action, a Frobenius endomorphism, a filtration, and an element on which the Galois group acts through the cyclotomic character), together with isomorphisms $B\simeq B_{\rm cris}$ and $\Delta\simeq B_{\rm dR}$ that preserve these structures. A *coefficient comparison* identifies the completion of a number field at a place above $\ell$ with a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_\ell$ inside $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, compatibly with a decomposition group, and names a coefficient field. With respect to such data the page defines crystalline, de Rham, potentially crystalline and potentially semistable representations, their Hodge–Tate weights, and the Weil–Deligne representation associated with a potentially semistable representation.

**Notation.** Let $\ell$ be a prime and $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ an algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q_\ell$, with the absolute value $|\cdot|_\ell$ for which $|\ell|_\ell=\ell^{-1}$. Put $G_\ell=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell/\mathbb Q_\ell)$, and let $\mathbb Q_\ell^{\rm nr}\subset\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ be the maximal unramified extension of $\mathbb Q_\ell$, the field generated over $\mathbb Q_\ell$ by the roots of unity of order prime to $\ell$. A *finite subextension* is a subfield of $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ of finite degree over $\mathbb Q_\ell$. For a finite subextension $K$, put $K_0=K\cap\mathbb Q_\ell^{\rm nr}$ and $G_K=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell/K)$, and let $\mathcal O_K$ be the integral closure of $\mathbb Z_\ell$ in $K$.

**Abstract local period data.** *Abstract local period data for $\ell$* consist of the following objects.

- A commutative $\mathbb Q_\ell^{\rm nr}$-algebra $B$ and a commutative $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$-algebra $\Delta$.
- Actions of $G_\ell$ on $B$ and on $\Delta$ by automorphisms of $\mathbb Q_\ell$-algebras.
- A $\mathbb Q_\ell$-algebra endomorphism $\varphi$ of $B$, a $\mathbb Q_\ell$-algebra homomorphism $\kappa:B\to\Delta$, an element $t_B\in B$ and a homomorphism $\chi_{\rm cyc}:G_\ell\to\mathbb Q_\ell^\times$.
- For every $h\in\mathbb Z$, a $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$-subspace $\operatorname{Fil}^h\Delta$ of $\Delta$.
- For every finite subextension $K$, an automorphism $\sigma_K$ of the $\mathbb Q_\ell$-algebra $K_0$.
- Elements $\zeta_m\in\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, $m\ge0$, with $\zeta_0=1$, $\zeta_1\ne1$ and $\zeta_{m+1}^\ell=\zeta_m$.
- For every finite subextension $L$, an additive valuation $v_L:L\to\mathbb Q\cup\{\infty\}$ with $v_L(\ell)=1$ such that $v_L(x)\ge0$ exactly when $x\in\mathcal O_L$. These two conditions determine $v_L$: it is the valuation of $L$ that extends the $\ell$-adic valuation of $\mathbb Q_\ell$.

These objects are required to have the following eight properties, in which an element of $\mathbb Q_\ell^{\rm nr}$ or of $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ also denotes its image in $B$ or in $\Delta$.

1. *Scalars.* The structure maps $\mathbb Q_\ell^{\rm nr}\to B$ and $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\to\Delta$ commute with the actions of $G_\ell$. Consequently, for every finite subextension $K$, the group $G_K$ acts $K_0$-linearly on $B$ and $K$-linearly on $\Delta$.
2. *Invariants.* For every finite subextension $K$, the elements of $B$ fixed by $G_K$ are the images of the elements of $K_0$, and the elements of $\Delta$ fixed by $G_K$ are the images of the elements of $K$.
3. *Frobenius.* The endomorphism $\varphi$ is injective and commutes with the action of $G_\ell$. For every finite subextension $K$, one has $\varphi(x)=\sigma_K(x)$ in $B$ for all $x\in K_0$, and $|\sigma_K(x)-x^\ell|_\ell<1$ for all $x\in K_0$ with $|x|_\ell\le1$.
4. *The map $\kappa$.* The homomorphism $\kappa$ is injective, commutes with the actions of $G_\ell$, and is $\mathbb Q_\ell^{\rm nr}$-linear, $\Delta$ being a $\mathbb Q_\ell^{\rm nr}$-algebra through $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$.
5. *Filtration.* For all $h,h'\in\mathbb Z$, $$\operatorname{Fil}^{h+1}\Delta\subseteq\operatorname{Fil}^h\Delta,\qquad1\in\operatorname{Fil}^0\Delta,\qquad\operatorname{Fil}^h\Delta\cdot\operatorname{Fil}^{h'}\Delta\subseteq\operatorname{Fil}^{h+h'}\Delta,\qquad g\bigl(\operatorname{Fil}^h\Delta\bigr)\subseteq\operatorname{Fil}^h\Delta\quad(g\in G_\ell),$$ and $\bigcup_h\operatorname{Fil}^h\Delta=\Delta$, $\bigcap_h\operatorname{Fil}^h\Delta=0$.
6. *The element $t_B$.* The element $t_B$ is a unit of $B$, and $$g(t_B)=\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)\,t_B\quad(g\in G_\ell),\qquad\varphi(t_B)=\ell\,t_B,\qquad\kappa(t_B)\in\operatorname{Fil}^1\Delta\setminus\operatorname{Fil}^2\Delta.$$
7. *Fixed points of Frobenius.* If $x\in B$ satisfies $\varphi(x)=x$ and $\kappa(x)\in\operatorname{Fil}^0\Delta$, then $x$ is the image of an element of $\mathbb Q_\ell$.
8. *Cyclotomic character.* For every $g\in G_\ell$, every $m\ge0$ and every $\zeta\in\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ with $\zeta^{\ell^m}=1$, there is an integer $n\ge0$ with $$|\chi_{\rm cyc}(g)-n|_\ell\le\ell^{-m},\qquad g(\zeta)=\zeta^n.$$

**Integral period data and identifications with Fontaine's rings.** Let abstract local period data for $\ell$ be given. Let $\mathbb C_\ell$ be the completion of $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ and $\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell}$ its valuation ring. We use the tilt $R=\varprojlim_{x\mapsto x^\ell}\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell}/\ell$, the Teichmüller lift $[x]\in W(R)$ of $x\in R$, the ring $A_{\rm inf}=W(R)$, its homomorphism $\Theta$ and the kernel $\ker\Theta$ of the extension of $\Theta$ to $A_{\rm inf}[1/\ell]$, the rings $A_{\rm cris}^0$, $A_{\rm cris}$, $B_{\rm cris}$, $B_{\rm dR}^+$ and $B_{\rm dR}$, the residue map $\Theta_{\rm dR}:B_{\rm dR}^+\to\mathbb C_\ell$, the filtration $\operatorname{Fil}^hB_{\rm dR}$, the Frobenius endomorphism $\Phi$ of $A_{\rm inf}$ and of $B_{\rm cris}$, and the actions of $G_\ell$ on $B_{\rm cris}$ and $B_{\rm dR}$, all as defined in ([Fontaine's crystalline and de Rham period rings](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Reformulated.AutPerF.BdRPlus/)); the period rings are those of Fontaine [\[Fon94\]](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/references.html#ref-Fon94).

*Integral period data* for the given abstract data consist of the following objects.

- An action of $G_\ell$ on $\mathbb C_\ell$ by ring automorphisms that preserve the absolute value and extend the action of $G_\ell$ on $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, and an action of $G_\ell$ on $R$ by ring automorphisms such that, for every $g\in G_\ell$, every $x=(x_k)_{k\ge0}\in R$ and every $y\in\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell}$ whose class modulo $\ell$ is $x_k$, the $k$th coordinate of $g(x)$ is the class of $g(y)$. These conditions determine both actions. The group $G_\ell$ then acts on $A_{\rm inf}=W(R)$ through its action on $R$.
- The element $\epsilon=(\zeta_m\bmod\ell)_{m\ge0}$ of $R$, formed with the roots of unity $\zeta_m$ of the abstract data, and the element $$\xi=\sum_{i=0}^{\ell-1}[\epsilon^{1/\ell}]^i\in A_{\rm inf},$$ where $\epsilon^{1/\ell}$ is the $\ell$th root of $\epsilon$ in the perfect ring $R$.
- An injective ring homomorphism $\alpha:A_{\rm inf}\to B$ that commutes with the actions of $G_\ell$ and satisfies $\alpha\circ\Phi=\varphi\circ\alpha$, such that $$\kappa(\alpha(A_{\rm inf}))\subseteq\operatorname{Fil}^0\Delta,\qquad\kappa(\alpha(x))\in\operatorname{Fil}^1\Delta\iff x\in\xi A_{\rm inf}\quad(x\in A_{\rm inf}),$$ and such that $\alpha([x])=\omega$ in $B$ whenever $\omega\in\mathbb Q_\ell^{\rm nr}$, $n\ge1$ is an integer prime to $\ell$ with $\omega^n=1$, and $x=(x_k)_{k\ge0}\in R$ is an element each of whose coordinates $x_k$ is the class of some $y_k\in\mathcal O_{\mathbb C_\ell}$ with $y_k^{\ell^k}=\omega$ and $y_k^n=1$.

*Identifications with Fontaine's rings*, for abstract local period data with integral period data, are ring isomorphisms $$\beta:B\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }B_{\rm cris},\qquad\delta:\Delta\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }B_{\rm dR}$$ with the following properties.

1. $\beta$ and $\delta$ commute with the actions of $G_\ell$. They are homomorphisms of $\mathbb Z_\ell$-algebras, where $B$ and $\Delta$ are $\mathbb Z_\ell$-algebras through $\mathbb Q_\ell$, and $B_{\rm cris}$ and $B_{\rm dR}$ through the homomorphism $\mathbb Z_\ell=W(\mathbb F_\ell)\to W(R)=A_{\rm inf}$.
2. $\beta\circ\varphi=\Phi\circ\beta$, and $\beta\circ\alpha$ is the natural map $A_{\rm inf}\to A_{\rm cris}\to B_{\rm cris}$.
3. $\delta(\operatorname{Fil}^h\Delta)=\operatorname{Fil}^hB_{\rm dR}$ for every $h\in\mathbb Z$, and $\delta(\kappa(t_B))=\log[\epsilon]$, the sum in $B_{\rm dR}^+$ of the logarithm series of $[\epsilon]$ for the element $\epsilon$ of the integral period data.
4. For every $x\in\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, the image under $\delta$ of the image of $x$ in $\Delta$ lies in $B_{\rm dR}^+$, and its image under $\Theta_{\rm dR}$ is $x$.
5. Put $f=\delta\circ\kappa\circ\beta^{-1}:B_{\rm cris}\to B_{\rm dR}$. The composite of the natural map $A_{\rm cris}^0\to A_{\rm cris}\to B_{\rm cris}$ with $f$ is the natural map $A_{\rm cris}^0\subset A_{\rm inf}[1/\ell]\to B_{\rm dR}^+\to B_{\rm dR}$. Moreover, for every integer $n\ge0$ there is an integer $k\ge0$ such that, for every $x$ in the image of $A_{\rm cris}$ in $B_{\rm cris}$, the element $f(x)$ lies in $B_{\rm dR}^+$ and $\ell^kf(x)$ has the same image in $A_{\rm inf}[1/\ell]/(\ker\Theta)^n$ as some element of $A_{\rm inf}$; here $B_{\rm dR}^+\to A_{\rm inf}[1/\ell]/(\ker\Theta)^n$ is the projection of the inverse limit that defines $B_{\rm dR}^+$.

**Local period data.** *Local period data for $\ell$* consist of abstract local period data for $\ell$, integral period data for them, and identifications with Fontaine's rings for this pair.

**Coefficient comparisons.** Let $E$ be a field, $\Omega$ a field extension of $E$, $G_w$ a subgroup of the group of automorphisms of $\Omega$ over $E$, and $O$ a commutative $\mathbb Z_\ell$-algebra equipped with a topology. In the applications $E$ is a number field, $\Omega$ is an algebraic closure of $E$ and $G_w$ is a decomposition group. A *coefficient comparison* for $(E,\Omega,G_w,O)$ is a tuple $(K_w,L_w,j,e,o)$ in which

- $K_w$ and $L_w$ are finite subextensions, and the image of every $\mathbb Q_\ell$-embedding $K_w\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ lies in $L_w$;
- $j:\Omega\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ is a homomorphism of fields with $j(E)\subseteq K_w$ and $j(E)$ dense in $K_w$;
- $e:G_w\to G_{K_w}$ is an isomorphism of topological groups with $$e(g)(j(x))=j(g(x))\qquad(g\in G_w,\ x\in\Omega);$$
- $o:O\to\mathcal O_{L_w}$ is an isomorphism of $\mathbb Z_\ell$-algebras and a homeomorphism.

**Period modules of a representation of $G_K$.** Let abstract local period data for $\ell$ be given. Let $K$ and $L$ be finite subextensions and $V$ a finite-dimensional $L$-vector space with an $L$-linear action of $G_K$. The group $G_K$ acts on $V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}B$ and on $V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}\Delta$ by $g(v\otimes x)=g(v)\otimes g(x)$, and $L$ acts on both through $V$. Define the $L$-vector spaces $$D_{\rm cris}(V)=(V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}B)^{G_K},\qquad D_{\rm dR}(V)=(V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}\Delta)^{G_K}.$$ The representation $V$ is *crystalline* if $\dim_LD_{\rm cris}(V)=[K_0:\mathbb Q_\ell]\dim_LV$, and *de Rham* if $\dim_LD_{\rm dR}(V)=[K:\mathbb Q_\ell]\dim_LV$. It is *potentially crystalline* if its restriction to $G_{K'}$ is crystalline for some finite subextension $K'\supseteq K$.

For a $\mathbb Q_\ell$-embedding $\tau:K\to L$ and $h\in\mathbb Z$, put $$D_{{\rm dR},\tau}(V)=\{z\in D_{\rm dR}(V):\ (1\otimes x)z=\tau(x)z\ \text{for all }x\in K\},$$ $$F_\tau^h(V)=D_{{\rm dR},\tau}(V)\cap\operatorname{im}\bigl(V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}\operatorname{Fil}^h\Delta\longrightarrow V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}\Delta\bigr),\qquad m_\tau(h)=\dim_LF_\tau^h(V)-\dim_LF_\tau^{h+1}(V),$$ where $1\otimes x$ is multiplication by $x$ on the factor $\Delta$. The integer $h$ is a *jump* of $V$ at $\tau$ if $F_\tau^{h+1}(V)\ne F_\tau^h(V)$, and $m_\tau(h)$ is its *multiplicity*.

**Potentially semistable representations.** Fix once and for all elements $\varpi_m\in\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$, $m\ge0$, with $\varpi_0=\ell$ and $\varpi_{m+1}^\ell=\varpi_m$. The ring $B_{\rm st}$ defined next, and with it the notion of a potentially semistable representation and the class $\operatorname{WD}_{w,\eta}(r)$ defined at the end of this page, are formed with this sequence; it is not part of the local period data. For $g\in G_\ell$, let $c(g)\in\mathbb Z_\ell$ be the element determined by $$g(\varpi_m)=\zeta_m^{\,c(g)\bmod\ell^m}\,\varpi_m\qquad(m\ge0).$$ Let $B_{\rm st}=B[u]$ be the polynomial ring in one variable over $B$. Extend the action of $G_\ell$ and the endomorphism $\varphi$ from $B$ to $B_{\rm st}$ by $$g(u)=u+c(g)\,t_B,\qquad\varphi(u)=\ell u,$$ and let $N=-d/du$ be the negative of differentiation with respect to $u$. For $K$, $L$ and $V$ as above, let $G_K$ act on $V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}B_{\rm st}$ by $g(v\otimes x)=g(v)\otimes g(x)$, let $\varphi$ and $N$ act through the factor $B_{\rm st}$, and put $$D_{\rm pst}(V)=\bigcup_{K'}\,(V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}B_{\rm st})^{G_{K'}},$$ the union over the finite subextensions $K'\supseteq K$. The field $\mathbb Q_\ell^{\rm nr}$ acts on $V\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}B_{\rm st}$ by multiplication on the factor $B_{\rm st}$, and this action, the action of $G_K$ and the maps $\varphi$ and $N$ preserve $D_{\rm pst}(V)$. The representation $V$ is *potentially semistable* if $D_{\rm pst}(V)$ is finite-dimensional over $\mathbb Q_\ell^{\rm nr}$ and $$\dim_{\mathbb Q_\ell^{\rm nr}}D_{\rm pst}(V)=\dim_{\mathbb Q_\ell}V.$$

**Homomorphisms with values in $\mathrm{GL}_d(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$.** Let abstract local period data for $\ell$ and a coefficient comparison $(K_w,L_w,j,e,o)$ for $(E,\Omega,G_w,O)$ be given, and let $r:G_w\to\mathrm{GL}_d(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ be a homomorphism, $d\ge0$. A *finite coefficient model* of $r$ is a finite subextension $L'\supseteq L_w$ together with a homomorphism $r':G_w\to\mathrm{GL}_d(L')$ whose composite with the inclusion $\mathrm{GL}_d(L')\subseteq\mathrm{GL}_d(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ is $r$. It defines the representation $V(r')=(L')^d$ of $G_{K_w}$ over $L'$ on which $g$ acts by the matrix $r'(e^{-1}(g))$. The homomorphism $r$ is *crystalline*, *de Rham*, *potentially crystalline* or *potentially semistable* if it has a finite coefficient model $(L',r')$ for which $V(r')$ has that property.

Let $\tau:E\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$ be a homomorphism of fields and $J$ a multiset of integers. The homomorphism $r$ *has Hodge–Tate weights $J$ at $\tau$* if there are a finite coefficient model $(L',r')$ of $r$ and a $\mathbb Q_\ell$-embedding $\tau':K_w\to L'$ such that $V(r')$ is de Rham, $\tau=\tau'\circ j|_E$, and every integer $h$ occurs in $J$ with multiplicity $m_{\tau'}(h)$, formed for $V(r')$.

**The associated Weil–Deligne representation.** Let $E$ be a number field, $\Omega$ an algebraic closure of $E$, $w$ a finite place of $E$, $A$ a valuation ring of $\Omega$ lying over $w$, and $G_w$ the decomposition group of $A$. The Weil group $W\subseteq G_w$ of $A$, the degree $\deg:W\to\mathbb Z$ and Weil–Deligne representations of $W$ are those of ([Hypothesis: existence of a reciprocity map for GL\_n over the completions of number fields, with a restricted list of properties](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.RecTExists/)). A coefficient comparison $(K_w,L_w,j,e,o)$ for $(E,\Omega,G_w,O)$ *induces $A$* if, for every $x\in\Omega$, $$x\in A\iff|j(x)|_\ell\le1.$$ In that case $w$ lies above $\ell$, and the residue field of $w$ has $q_w=\ell^{f_w}$ elements for an integer $f_w\ge1$. Let such a comparison, abstract local period data for $\ell$ and an isomorphism of fields $\eta:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\to\mathbb C$ be given.

Let $r:G_w\to\mathrm{GL}_d(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell)$ be a homomorphism and $(L',r')$ a finite coefficient model of $r$ for which $V=V(r')$ is potentially semistable. Form the complex vector space $$\mathcal W_\eta(V)=\bigl(\mathbb C\otimes_{L',\eta}D_{\rm pst}(V)\bigr)\Big/\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\bigl\{1\otimes cx-\eta(c)\otimes x:\ c\in\mathbb Q_\ell^{\rm nr},\ x\in D_{\rm pst}(V)\bigr\},$$ and write $[x]$ for the image of $1\otimes x$. The endomorphism $\varphi$ of $D_{\rm pst}(V)$ is bijective. Define an action $R$ of $W$ and an endomorphism $N_\eta$ on $\mathcal W_\eta(V)$ by $$R(s)[x]=\bigl[e(s)\,\varphi^{-f_w\deg(s)}x\bigr],\qquad N_\eta[x]=[Nx]\qquad(s\in W,\ x\in D_{\rm pst}(V)),$$ where $e(s)\in G_{K_w}$ acts on $D_{\rm pst}(V)$. The space $\mathcal W_\eta(V)$ is finite-dimensional. There are a Weil–Deligne representation $(R^{\rm ss},N_\eta)$ of $W$ on $\mathcal W_\eta(V)$, with the operator $N_\eta$ just defined as its monodromy operator, and a unipotent operator $U$ on $\mathcal W_\eta(V)$ that commutes with $N_\eta$ and with every $R(s)$, such that $$R(s)=R^{\rm ss}(s)\,U^{\deg(s)}\qquad(s\in W).$$ These conditions determine $R^{\rm ss}$: the operator $R^{\rm ss}(s)$ is the semisimple part of $R(s)$.

If $r$ is potentially semistable, the isomorphism class of $(R^{\rm ss},N_\eta)$ is the same for all finite coefficient models $(L',r')$ of $r$ for which $V(r')$ is potentially semistable; it is denoted $\operatorname{WD}_{w,\eta}(r)$. If $r$ is not potentially semistable, $\operatorname{WD}_{w,\eta}(r)$ denotes the class of $\mathbb C^d$ with the trivial action of $W$ and zero monodromy operator.
