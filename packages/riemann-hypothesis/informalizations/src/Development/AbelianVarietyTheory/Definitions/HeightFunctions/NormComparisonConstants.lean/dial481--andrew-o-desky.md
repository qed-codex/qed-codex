## Coefficient fields for covolume comparisons

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D160](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.FinalJoin2.FieldOK0/)

Hypothesis (coefficient fields for covolume comparisons). Use the coefficient ring $O$, field $K$, embedding $\jmath$ and arithmetic data of ([Identifications for arithmetic family members](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.N.LocTie/)) and ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)). If $\jmath:O\to K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ is its coefficient map, put $F_O=\mathbb Q(\jmath(O))\subset\mathbb C$. The map $O\to K$ is injective and $\jmath(O)=\mathcal O_{F_O}$. Require $\exp(2\pi\mathrm i/(g_0g_1))\in\jmath(O)$ and $\exp(2\pi\mathrm i/p)\in F_O$ for $p=p_\Delta,p'_\Delta$. The level-prime set excludes zero, and these two excluded primes lie in the split prime class.

For each $p\in\{p_\Delta,p'_\Delta\}$ put $N=g_0g_1p$. For every automorphism $\sigma$ of $\mathbb C$ fixing $F_O$, there are a principally polarized complex elliptic curve $C$, a nonzero $c\in\mathbb C$, and a surjective additive holomorphic uniformization $u:\mathbb C\to C(\mathbb C)$ with $$\ker u=c\left(\mathbb Z+\mathbb Z\frac{1+\mathrm i\sqrt d}{2}\right).$$ Its polarization is symmetric, ample and rigidified, with trivial translation kernel. The differential of $u$ identifies $\mathbb C$ with the tangent space at zero. Choose nowhere-zero holomorphic local frames $f_i(z)$ for the pullback polarization with its transition functions, a positive Hermitian form $H$ on $\mathbb C$, and unit-modulus numbers $\chi(\lambda)$ for $\lambda\in\ker u$, satisfying $$f_i(z)=\chi(\lambda)\exp\{\pi H(z,\lambda)+\tfrac\pi2H(\lambda,\lambda)\}f_i(z+\lambda).$$ The form $\Im H$ is integer-valued and unimodular on $\ker u$. For each natural $n$, sections of the $n$th polarization power correspond bijectively, by $s(u(z))=\theta(z)f_i(z)^n$ in each local chart, to entire functions satisfying $$\theta(z+\lambda)=\left[\chi(\lambda)\exp\{\pi H(z,\lambda)+\tfrac\pi2H(\lambda,\lambda)\}\right]^n\theta(z).$$ There is a Cartesian $\sigma$-semilinear group map $\iota:C\to C$. For every $a,b\in\mathbb Z$, the point $$P_{a,b}=u\left(\frac{c\{a+b(1+\phi_0(s_d))/2\}}N\right)$$ satisfies $\iota\circ P_{a,b}=P_{a,b}\circ\operatorname{Spec}(\sigma)$, viewing $P_{a,b}$ as a morphism $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb C\to C$. The curve and these choices may depend on $p$ and $\sigma$.

For each of the two excluded primes impose ([Ideal generators in rational moduli realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.OKPsi/)), with its universal quantifier over all original rational moduli realizations. The coefficient field generated by the ring image has the two size bounds $$\log[F_O:\mathbb Q]\le P,\qquad \frac{\log|\operatorname{disc}F_O|}{[F_O:\mathbb Q]}\le P,\qquad P=19fr^2L+198f^2L.$$ Here $L$ is the member's real logarithmic scale, $f=[E:\mathbb Q]/2$ and $r=k+m-1$.
