## Finite adeles under extension of number fields

[Theorem ANT.0874](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.TwU.exists_isAdIso/)

Let $E/F$ be an extension of number fields and let $\mathbb A_{F,f}$ and $\mathbb A_{E,f}$ denote their finite adele rings. Let $B\subseteq E\otimes_F\mathbb A_{F,f}$ be the subring generated by $\mathcal O_E\otimes1$ and $1\otimes\prod_{v<\infty}\mathcal O_{F_v}$. There is a ring isomorphism $$\iota:E\otimes_F\mathbb A_{F,f}\longrightarrow\mathbb A_{E,f}$$ such that $\iota(x\otimes1)$ is the diagonal adele of $x$ for every $x\in E$, and $$y\in B\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad |\iota(y)_w|_w\le1\ \text{for every finite place }w\text{ of }E.$$
