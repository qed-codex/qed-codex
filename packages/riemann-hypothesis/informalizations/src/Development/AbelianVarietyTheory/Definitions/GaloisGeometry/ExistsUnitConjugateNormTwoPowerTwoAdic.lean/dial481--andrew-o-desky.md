## An integral root of a cubic relation over the 2-adic complex field

[Theorem PHT.0954](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.ThLoc.GoodTwo.integral_root_cubic_X_add_sixteen_two_adic/)

Let $j,r\in\mathbb C_2$, with the absolute value normalized by $|2|=1/2$. If $$|j|\le1,\qquad(r+16)^3=jr,$$ then $|r|\le1$.
