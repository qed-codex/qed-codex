## Scalar normalizations and series comparisons

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D230](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.SeriesTie.ExportTie/)

Use ([Analytic data for theta-series comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.SeriesTie.Setup/)), ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)), ([Analytic realizations of rational Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.BaseW/)), ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)), and ([One-map and geometric comparison laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)). Fix a rational cusp-form realization and its coefficient-extension isomorphisms $\mathcal E_{i,j,b}$. Write $J_j$ for its distinguished complex realization and $v_j$ for the chosen extremal tuple. For each twist index $i$, selected stage $j$ and side $b$, choose a scalar $\nu_{i,j,b}\in\mathbb C$. Define a semilinear reading $r_{i,j,b}$ of the algebraic theta-form space by $$r_{i,j,b}\bigl(\mathcal E_{i,j+1,b}^{-1}(a\otimes f)\bigr)=\nu_{i,j,b}^{-1}e_{i,b}(a)\,\operatorname{ev}_{v_j}(J_j(f)).$$ Its extension to the coefficient ring $O_{i,k}$ is denoted $r^{(k)}$, with $r^{(k)}(a\otimes f)=e_{i,b,k}(a)r(f)$.

Hypothesis (series comparison). There exist theta-series data satisfying ([Analytic data for theta-series comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.SeriesTie.Setup/)) and all the following identifications with the fixed analytic realization. In every cell the additive character, infinite embeddings, target Cartan involution, source and target orthonormal bases, source and target lattices, source and target adelic frames, vacuum and generator-prime set are exactly the fixed analytic ones. The residue-matrix group action is the fixed action after the rank identification. The base character is the fixed character at stage $j+1$ and the auxiliary character is the fixed $\eta$. The delta character is a Hecke character and its value at every finite-prime-supported idele equals the selected local delta-character value for the residue prime of $i$. If a tuple auxiliary prime lies in the delta-prime set, it belongs to the selected orientation set $C_i$. The positive and negative harmonic weights are exactly the prescribed coefficient-representation weights. Each $v_j$ is an extremal tuple with the conditional determinant-polynomial normalization in ([One-map and geometric comparison laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)).

A nonzero scalar $\nu$ is called normalized for a cell if there are an arithmetic-to-analytic family comparison as in ([Analytic realizations of rational Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.BaseW/)), a complex number $c$, and lowest-type vectors representing the chosen tuple, such that for every coefficient polynomial $F$ $$\operatorname{harm}\bigl(B_j(\bigotimes v_j,c_{0,j}(F))\bigr)=c\,\operatorname{coef}(F),\qquad \nu^n=(\epsilon_j(\mathrm{ph}_j)c)^n$$ for some integer $n\ge1$. Here $c_{0,j}$ is the coefficient map from that family comparison, $\operatorname{harm}$ is its harmonic coefficient function, $\operatorname{coef}$ is the ordinary coefficient function including its determinant shift, and $\epsilon_j$ either conjugates or fixes the phase according to the distinguished embedding's prescribed orientation.

Call a reading rational when it has all three properties below. Every admissible untwisted theta series belongs to its range. For every admissible twist on an auxiliary set whose primes lie above odd rational primes in the split prime class, all elements of each twisted generator set belong to the range of the extended reading. For every universal prime set $Q$ consisting of split CM primes above odd rational primes in the split prime class disjoint from the coefficient bad primes, every universal theta coefficient belongs to its range, provided no prime in $Q$ divides the product of the untwisted input support norms. An admissible untwisted input $(S,h,P)$ has split finite support $S$ avoiding the bad integer, $h$ has trivial infinite component and is supported at $S$, and $P$ belongs to its integral coefficient lattice. Admissibility of Galois twists is the character, auxiliary-prime and coefficient-ring condition in ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)).

Require each selected $\nu_{i,j,b}$ to be nonzero. If any normalized scalar exists for its cell, the selected scalar is normalized. If some normalized scalar yields a rational reading, the selected scalar yields a rational reading. Neither implication assumes that its antecedent always holds.

The universal coefficients in these conditions use the following support and coordinate conventions. Let $A_i(Q,M)$ consist of the $P\in Q$ whose conjugate $\bar P$ also belongs to $Q$ and whose pair is admissible. A pair is admissible when both primes are split and away from $\ell_i$ and, for each of its primes $R$, there is an open anticyclotomic character to $\mathbb Z/\ell_i^M\mathbb Z$, trivial on decomposition groups above $\ell_i$, unramified outside the pair and surjective on inertia at $R$. Put $H_i(Q,M)=A_i(Q,M)\cap C_i$. The universal analytic coefficient $\Xi_a$ is constructed by ([Analytic data for theta-series comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.SeriesTie.Setup/)) from the reciprocity pullback of the coordinates of the specified universal character on $A_i(Q,M)$, using only the residue factors in $H_i(Q,M)$. Thus $a$ is indexed by $H_i(Q,M)$, even when $Q$ is larger.

For $P\in A_i(Q,M)$ and $v\in Q$, put $u_{P,v}=1$ if $v=P$, put it equal to the fixed sign supplied by the equality of the $P$- and $v$-coordinates of the universal character on $A_i(Q,M)$ when $v$ is its distinct conjugate in that set, and put it equal to zero otherwise. Define $$\operatorname{spread}_{i,Q,M}(a)_v=\sum_{P\in H_i(Q,M)}u_{P,v}a_P.$$ The coordinate equality defining the sign is $\delta(g)_v=u_{P,v}\delta(g)_P$ for every $g$.

For every cell there are maps assigning a finite support $S_d$, finite adelic input $h_d$ and polynomial $P_d$ to every selected expansion input $d$, and semilinear readings $R_{k,Q,\chi}$ of the period-input spaces into polynomial-valued finite-adelic functions. They satisfy all of the following requirements.

The selected finite subgroup is exactly the theta-series level subgroup under the chosen generator-group isomorphism, and their characters agree through $e_{i,b}$. Each $(S_d,h_d,P_d)$ is admissible. Conversely every admissible triple whose theta series belongs to $\operatorname{im}r$ occurs exactly as such a triple for some $d$; uniqueness of $d$ is not required. The level primes of $d$ are exactly the prime divisors of $\prod_{P\in S_d}NP$. The theta vector satisfies $$r(\operatorname{th}(d))=\Theta(h_d,P_d).$$ For every admissible twist, an element $x$ belongs to the specified twisted theta set attached to $d$ exactly when $r^{(k)}(x)$ belongs to the analytic twisted generator set attached to $(h_d,P_d)$.

For every admitted universal set $Q$, exponent $M$ and family $(x_a)$ with $r(x_a)=\Xi_a(h_d,P_d)$ for every universal coefficient, require $$\operatorname{th}^U(Q,M,d)=\sum_a x_a\otimes[\operatorname{spread}_{i,Q,M}(a)].$$ The map $\operatorname{spread}$ is the map just defined, with the chosen orientation $C_i$. If $Q$ fails the split-CM, odd-split-class and bad-prime-disjoint conditions, or if some universal coefficient has no rational reading, require $\operatorname{th}^U(Q,M,d)=0$.

For every admissible twist, $R_{k,Q,\chi}$ is injective and identifies the selected integral period-input lattice exactly with the polynomial-valued integral period-input lattice of ([Analytic data for theta-series comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.SeriesTie.Setup/)). Whenever the analytic class theta sum of $R_{k,Q,\chi}f$ belongs to the range of $r^{(k)}$, require $$r^{(k)}(\operatorname{th}^P f)=\mathcal S(R_{k,Q,\chi}f).$$

At every admissible Hecke index $(k,P)$, if for some $f$ there is $g$ whose reading realizes every analytic Hecke representative sum, the selected algebraic Hecke operator realizes those sums. Explicitly, for every adelic Hecke element $\alpha$ at $P$ of index $k$, every complete double-coset representative set $\mathcal R$, and every compatible determinant-root idele $z$, $$r(H_{k,P}f)(x)=\mu(z)^{-1}(NP)^{-k\lfloor\dim V_j/2\rfloor}\sum_{\gamma\in\mathcal R}r(f)(x\gamma).$$ The antecedent requires one $g$ satisfying this equation with $r(g)$ on the left simultaneously for all such $\alpha,\mathcal R,z$.

The selected universal Galois character equals the following fixed construction. For a prime pair $T=\{P,\bar P\}$, fix once and for all a character $d_{T,M}:G_E\to\mathbb Z/\ell_i^M\mathbb Z$ with open kernel, inverted by the specified complex conjugation, trivial on decomposition groups above $\ell_i$, unramified outside $T$, and surjective on inertia at every prime in $T$, whenever such a character exists; otherwise fix the zero character. The $P$-coordinate of the universal character for $Q$ is $d_{T,M}$ if $T\subseteq Q$, and zero otherwise. Require equality with this fixed character for every $i,Q,M$.
