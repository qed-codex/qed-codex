## Unitary toroidal models and boundary charts

[Definition / hypothesis AG.D102](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.MumfordChart.HasChart/)

**Moduli data.** Use the CM-host and polarized-lattice definitions in the conventions of ([An indefinite polarized CM lattice splits by signature](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.hermitianLatticeSignatureSplit_of_indefinite/)). Write $E$ for the CM field, $(p,q)$ for the signature, and $r,w\in\mathcal O_E$ and $r_0\in\mathbb C$ for the chosen elements with $r^2=r_0^2=-d_0$ and $2w=1+r$. Put $\Psi=\{\tau:E\hookrightarrow\mathbb C:\tau(r)=r_0\}$.

A level consists of an odd integer $M\ge3$, an integer $A\ge1$ and an ideal $\mathfrak b\subset\mathcal O_E$, generated by its elements in $\mathbb Z[w]$, such that $$\mathfrak b\overline{\mathfrak b}=(M),\qquad \mathfrak b+\overline{\mathfrak b}=\mathcal O_E.$$ Put $M'=\operatorname{lcm}(M,A)$ and $K_0=\mathbb Q(r_0,e^{2\pi i/A})\subset\mathbb C$. The moduli scheme is a smooth quasiprojective $K_0$-scheme of dimension $[E:\mathbb Q]pq/2$. Use the moduli objects, both marked level maps, their determinant condition and their torsion compatibility conditions in ([Polarized modules and unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)). It represents these objects over reduced schemes and satisfies the infinitesimal uniqueness in ([Polarized modules and unitary moduli](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/ReciprocityTheory.ChartRestriction.G.Geo.Tor.Host/)). The number field $K\subset\mathbb C$ contains $K_0$, every image of $E$ in $\mathbb C$, and $e^{2\pi i/M'}$; $X$ is the base change to $K$.

**Models and the determinant Hodge presentation.** For the coefficient-field base change $X/K$ of this moduli scheme, a model is a normal projective flat $K$-scheme $Y$ with a dense open immersion $j:X\hookrightarrow Y$ and a semiabelian family $G/Y$ extending the universal family. The extension and its identification over $X$ preserve the $\mathcal O_E$-action, symmetric rigidified cubical polarization, conjugate-adjointness and compatibility with $w$ of the universal family. The abelian locus of $G$ is exactly $j(X)$. Write $D=Y\setminus j(X)$ with its reduced closed-subscheme structure. An effective Cartier divisor $B$ on $Y$ has ideal $\mathcal I_B$ and associated line bundle $\mathcal O_Y(B)$.

The determinant Hodge presentation $\lambda$ is defined as follows. Use all affine integral opens of $Y$ carrying a section of the polarization line that is nonzero somewhere on every fibre of $G$, and carrying a finite tangent basis and $\mathcal O_E$-action matrices compatible with arbitrary base change. On an overlap, if the second tangent basis is obtained from the first by a matrix $C$, the transition unit for $\lambda$ is $(\det C)^{-1}$. The condition that $\lambda$ is a line bundle requires these opens to cover $Y$; the transitions then define the dual of the determinant tangent bundle. The twist used with $B$ and an integer $k\ge0$ is $$\lambda^{\otimes k}\otimes\mathcal O_Y(-B).$$

**Boundary charts.** Boundary chart data at a point $x\in D$ consist of a field $k_x$, integers $m,e\ge0$, and $$R=k_x[[t_1,\ldots,t_m,z_1,\ldots,z_e]],\qquad \xi:\operatorname{Spec}R\longrightarrow Y$$ taking the closed point to $x$. The stalk map $u:\mathcal O_{Y,x}\to R$ is flat, satisfies $u(\mathfrak m_x)R=\mathfrak m_R$, and induces a finite residue-field extension. The boundary ideal maps to $(\prod_i t_i)$, and a prime maps to $D$ exactly when it contains this product. There is a semiabelian pullback of $G$ with a positive convergent polarized degeneration datum and a polarized uniformization, in the senses defined in ([Siegel compactification with polarized boundary charts](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.SiegJoin.siegelToroidalPolCharts/)). Write $C/R$ for its abelian part, $L$ for its polarization line, $X_0$ for its character group, $c:X_0\to C(R)$ for its additive map, and $\psi(y)\in L_{c(y)}^{-1}$ for its quadratic period section. The group $X_0$ carries an $\mathcal O_E$-module structure, and $C$ and the generic abelian scheme carry $\mathcal O_E$-actions with conjugation as Rosati adjoint. Require $c(ay)=[a]c(y)$ and balanced periods: under every normalized Rosati identification $u_a$ of the two polarization biextensions, $$\tau(ay,z)u_a(c(y),c(z))=\tau(y,\bar a z).$$ The generic-fibre identification and the formal quotient maps are $\mathcal O_E$-linear; on the torus, $[a]$ acts on characters by $y\mapsto\bar a y$. The uniformization line equals the polarization line of the pulled-back semiabelian family. With $w=(1+r)/2$, require a normalized trivialization $v$ on $C$ of $$L_{x+[w]x}\otimes L_x^{-2}\otimes L_{[w]x}^{-1}\otimes L_0^2$$ such that, for every $y\in X_0$, $\tau(y,wy)v(c(y))=\psi(y)\eta$, where $\eta$ is the rigidification at zero. There are symmetric balanced integral forms $\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_m$ on $X_0$ whose values are divisible by $M'$. For every $y\in X_0$, the quadratic period section $\psi(y)$ has coordinate, relative to a line-bundle trivialization over $R$, $$u_y\prod_i t_i^{n_i(y)},\qquad u_y\in R^\times,\quad n_i(y)\in\mathbb N,\quad 2n_i(y)=\gamma_i(y,y).$$

An ordinary boundary chart requires these data and that $\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_m$ extend to a $\mathbb Z$-basis of the group of symmetric balanced integral forms on $X_0$ divisible by $M'$.

A polarized boundary chart for $(\phi,B,d)$ requires the boundary chart data, the rank bound $\operatorname{rk}_{\mathcal O_E}X_0\le\min(p,q)$, and a cone $\delta_1,\ldots,\delta_m$ for $\phi$ in ([Polarizing fan conventions for toroidal compactifications](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.LocM.InvFun/)), with $$\gamma_i=M'\delta_i.$$ There must be integers $a_i\ge0$ satisfying $$a_i=d\phi(\gamma_i),\qquad \mathcal I_B R=\left(\prod_i t_i^{a_i}\right).$$
