## Divisor rows and additive-character comparisons

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D218](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.DIV.DivisionCompatibilityDataThreeSided/)

Use ([Arithmetic families with local and theta data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/family-arithmetic-data.html)), ([Row-side families of Hermitian spaces](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Rows.RowSideFamily/)), ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)), ([Divisor realizations in arithmetic families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DivX/)) and ([Hermitian row lattices and their rational models](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DivData/)). Write $K$ for the coefficient field, $E/F$ for the CM extension, $T$ for the tuples and $t_0$ for the distinguished tuple. Let $S_0,S_1$ be the specified coordinate realizations at the base and sharp levels. Denote their row dimensions by $d_0,d_1$, their row lattices by $R_0,R_1$, their archimedean frame matrices by $X_{E,0,v},X_{E,1,v}$, and their lifted dimensions, integral modules and theta matrices by $d_{W,j},M_j,\theta_{j,t}$. Let $L_{j,t}$ be the matrix including selected lifted coordinates into all lifted coordinates, and $\pi_{j,t}$ its specified projection back. All these are fixed data, not additional existence conclusions.

Hypothesis (divisor row comparisons). Choose a group isomorphism $e_G:G\simeq G_{\rm gen}$ to the product of generator-place matrix groups in ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)), a tuple map $\tau:T\to\mathcal T$, and $K$-linear isomorphisms $$e_R:K^{d_1}\simeq R_K,\qquad e_R^0:K^{d_0}\simeq R_K^0.$$ Here $R_K$ and $R_K^0$ are the rational sharp and base Hermitian row spaces. Choose semilinear maps from all sharp and base row coordinates to their respective complex ambient row spaces at $\tau(t)$. For every $j,t$ choose a $K$-linear isomorphism $e_{j,t}:K^{n_{j,t}}\simeq V_{\tau(t),j+1}$ to the rational theta-input space, and a semilinear map $J_j:K^{d_{W,j}}\to M_{j+1}^{\mathbb C}$ to the theta-output space. Choose a map $u\mapsto w(u)$ from family source words to coordinate-column words, and $K$-linear isomorphisms $e_{N,u}:K^{a_{w(u)}}\simeq N_{u,K}$ to the rational source spaces.

The first two isomorphisms identify the integral row lattices and every archimedean Hermitian form: $$e_R(R_1)=R,\qquad e_R^0(R_0)=R^0,$$ $$Q_v(e_Rx,e_Ry)=\langle X_{E,1,v}^{-1}x,X_{E,1,v}^{-1}y\rangle,\qquad Q_v^0(e_R^0x,e_R^0y)=\langle X_{E,0,v}^{-1}x,X_{E,0,v}^{-1}y\rangle.$$ For every $j,t$, the selected complex input space is exactly $$Z_{\tau(t),j+1}=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{J^{\rm in}_{\tau(t),j+1}(e_{j,t}(L_{j,t}\mathbf e_a)):a\},$$ and the theta maps satisfy $$\theta_{\tau(t),j+1}^{P}\bigl(J^{\rm in}_{\tau(t),j+1}(e_{j,t}x)\bigr)=J_j(\theta_{j,t}\pi_{j,t}x).$$ The source-to-row pairings satisfy $$B_u(e_{N,u}x,e_Ry)=B^{\rm col}_{w(u)}(x,y).$$

Impose the following three numerical identities for every coordinate stage $0\le i<m$, tuple $t$ and coefficient prime $w$. The family indices at stage $i+1$ equal the coordinate indices at stage $i$: $$I_{\tau(t),i+1,w}=[M_i:\theta_{i,t}(T_{i,t})]_w,\qquad J_{\tau(t),i+1,w}=[T_{i,t}:T_{i,t}^{\#}]_w,$$ where $T_{i,t}^{\#}$ is the dual for the coordinate Hermitian form. The correction is zero off the prescribed twisted set and otherwise equals $$\eta_{i+1,w}=\frac{1}{e_w}\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O_{K,w}}\bigl((T_{i,\mu(w)})_w/((T_{i,\mu(w)})_w\cap(C_{i,\mu(w)})_w)\bigr).$$ Here $T_{i,t}$ and $C_{i,t}$ are respectively the selected theta-input and cut numerator lattices obtained from the base-level row coordinates. The supplied family indices are those in ([Local error functions and weight normalizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.ErrC/)). Relative local index means $$[A:B]_w=\operatorname{length}(A_w/(A_w\cap B_w))-\operatorname{length}(B_w/(A_w\cap B_w)).$$ In these numerical identities transport $w$ through the specified identification of coefficient primes before evaluating the coordinate data.

Hypothesis (additive characters and finite levels). There exist a finite set $P_g$ of primes of $E$, a bijection $\lambda:\coprod_{a=0,1}\mathcal P_a\simeq P_g$ from the generator-place labels, field isomorphisms $r_v:k_{\lambda(v)}\simeq\mathbb F_{g_a}$ for $v\in\mathcal P_a$, additive characters $\psi_P:k_P\to K^\times$ for all primes $P$ of $E$, and a group isomorphism $$e_C:G\simeq\prod_{P\in P_g}\mathrm{GL}_r(k_P).$$ The prime $\lambda(v)$ is the prescribed prime of the family label $v$. Applying $r_v$ entrywise to $e_C(g)_{\lambda(v)}$ gives $e_G(g)_v$. Every $\psi_P$ for $P\in P_g$ is nontrivial. If $\bar\xi_0$ denotes the reduction of the fixed integral scalar $\xi_0$, then $$\jmath(\psi_{\lambda(v)}(c))=\exp(2\pi\mathrm i b/g_a)\quad\text{whenever }\bar\xi_0c=b\text{ in }k_{\lambda(v)},\ b\in\mathbb Z.$$

For a residue field $k$, let $U_r(k)$ be the upper unitriangular group, with character $u\mapsto\psi(\sum_{i=0}^{r-2}u_{i,i+1})$. Let $U_{r,\rho,k}^{\sharp}(k_P)$ consist of those $u\in U_r(k_P)$ with $u_{i,\rho+k}=0$ for $\rho\le i<\rho+k$. Its character is $u\mapsto\psi_P(\sum_{(i,l)\in A}u_{i,l})$, where $$A=\{(i,i+1):i+1\ne\rho,\ i+1\ne\rho+k\}\cup\{(\rho-1,\rho+k)\}$$ with only indices in $\{0,\ldots,r-1\}$ retained. Require that $e_C$ identifies $P_0,\psi_0$ with the product of the groups $U_r(k_P)$ and these characters. Define a permutation $s$ of the indices by $s(i)=k+i$ for $i<\rho$, $s(i)=i-\rho$ for $\rho\le i<\rho+k$, and $s(i)=i$ otherwise, where $\rho=r_S$. Reindexing $e_C(g)_{s(i),s(l)}$ identifies $P_1,\psi_1$ with the product of the groups $U_{r,\rho,k}^{\sharp}(k_P)$ and their displayed characters.

Hypothesis (rational theta output). Write $\mathcal H_j$ for the rational Hermitian theta-output space in ([Divisor realizations in arithmetic families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.DivX/)) and $W_j^K$ for the rational cusp-form realization in ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)). There are $K$-linear maps $d_j:\mathcal H_j\to W_j^K$ for every natural $j$. For each selected $i$, if $y$ is the coordinate vector obtained from $x\in\mathcal H_{i+1}$ by the inverse of the specified output-coordinate isomorphism, then $$f_i(d_{i+1}x)=\sum_a y_a b_{i,a},$$ where $f_i$ is the fixed comparison with the rational realization and $b_{i,a}$ is its specified base-level rational basis.

For every coefficient prime $w$ and natural $j$ there is a semilinear map $$E_{w,j}:\mathcal H_j\longrightarrow K_w'\otimes_{O_{i(w)}}\mathcal F_{i(w),j,\epsilon(w)}.$$ Write $O'_w$ for the coefficient DVR with fraction field $K'_w$. For $1\le j\le m$ and $\mu_w\ne\mu_0$, the images under $E_{w,j}$ of the specified rational basis are independent over $K_w'$. The $O'_w$-span of the images of the supplied integral lattice in $\mathcal H_j$ is finitely generated, is contained in the scalar extension of the selected finite-group idempotent image of the $\psi_U$-eigenspace, and spans that image over $K'_w$. Under these same restrictions on $j,w$, if $x$ belongs to the selected rational theta-input subspace at $\mu_w^{\rm tw}$ and $J_j^{\mathcal H}y=\theta_{\mu_w^{\rm tw},j}^{P}(J_{\mu_w^{\rm tw},j}^{\rm in}x)$, then $$E_{w,j}y=\theta_{w,j}^{P}(e_{w,j}^{\rm in}x).$$ The rational cusp-form structure has a $K_s$-basis which remains a $K$-basis after scalar extension at every selected stage. For $1\le j\le m$, if $d_jx=\sum_{a=1}^n c_a f_a$ with $c_a\in K$ and $f_a$ in its $K_s$-form, require $$E_{w,j}x=\sum_{a=1}^n\iota_w(c_a)\otimes\mathcal E_{i(w),j,\epsilon(w)}^{-1}(1\otimes f_a).$$ For every $a\in K_s$ and theta form $f$, require also $$\iota_w(a)\otimes f=1\otimes(\iota_{i(w),\epsilon(w)}(a)f).$$ The coefficient rings, embeddings, twist index $i(w)$, side $\epsilon(w)$, input comparisons and theta maps are those of ([Integral theta expansions and local parameters](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NExp/)), ([Twist period lattices and coefficient comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.NChoice/)), and ([Integral and theta compatibility for families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Extra/)). The identities just stated apply to every natural $j$ where their quantifier does not explicitly restrict $j$ to the selected range.

## Admissible points over the members of a family, the row lattice of a point and its maximal slope

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D271](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.BigTop.FamHyp/)

Several theorems of the proof assert a property of the points over the members of a family, for all members beyond a bound. This page defines the class of families $\mathcal C$, points and admissible points, the phrase "eventually at the admissible points", the row lattice of a point and its maximal slope, the divisibility bound for a family, and the lower bound for the scalars.

**Families.** Datum, size data, family with parameter $\varepsilon$, member, starting scale $x_1$, class of families, and "holds for all sufficiently constrained families of a class" are as in ([Data, families of data, classes of families, and properties of all sufficiently constrained families of a class](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.MainNotions.Closed/)). At a member $(x,i)$ we write $k,m,n,f$ for the numbers of its datum, $K$ for its coefficient field (written $\Lambda$ on that page), $s_K$ for the number of infinite places of $K$, and $b=\exp(x^{\varepsilon})$. Throughout, $\mathcal C$ is the class of the families that satisfy the fourteen standard conditions (N1)–(N4) and (a)–(j) of that page. These are the fourteen conditions on a family in ([Covolume of integral cusp forms](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CovTop.CovolumeBoundOfX/)).

**Points.** Let $(x,i)$ be a member of a family with $x>x_1$. A *point over $(x,i)$* consists of the choices of ([Compatible points for arithmetic covolume bounds](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.BigTop.PtAt/)), subject to the conditions of that page, for the arithmetic member $(x,i)$ with its local and theta data and for the height constant $C_{\rm size}$ of the family. A point contains a row-side family in the sense of ([Row-side families of Hermitian spaces](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Rows.RowSideFamily/)), with its tuple set $\mathcal T$ and its base tuple, written $\mu_0$ here; analytic realisation data; and the coordinate data of the base level and of the sharp level that are computed from the analytic realisation.

**Notation at a point.** For the coordinate data we use the notation of ([Row-side families of Hermitian spaces](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Rows.RowSideFamily/)) with the following abbreviations. At the base level $b=0$: $d=d_0$ is the common dimension; $X_v=X_{E,0,v}$ is the frame matrix at an infinite place $v$ of $K$; $\mu(w)\in\mathcal T$ is the tuple assigned to a finite place $w$ of $K$; for $t\in\mathcal T$, $P_t=P_{0,t}$ is the projection, $M_t=M_{0,t}$ the integral module and $\iota_t=\iota_{0,t}$ the matrix with values in $K^{d}$; and $\kappa_v=\kappa_{0,v}$. At the sharp level $b=1$: $\lambda_{j,t,\pi,v}=\lambda_{1,j,t,\pi,v}$ for a stage $j$, a tuple $t$, a full label $\pi$ and an infinite place $v$. For real numbers $C$ and $C_H$, the *numerical data of the point with constants $(C,C_H)$* are those of that page; among them are $$h=x^{C_H}\,b,\qquad l_6(j)=l_{6,m}(j)-C\,l_{6,u}.$$

**The row lattice.** For an $\mathcal O_K$-submodule $L$ of $K^{d}$ and a finite place $w$ of $K$ with prime ideal $\mathfrak p_w$, put $$L_{(w)}=\{\,y\in K^{d}\ :\ s\,y\in L\ \text{for some } s\in\mathcal O_K\smallsetminus\mathfrak p_w\,\}.$$ The *row lattice* of a point is the $\mathcal O_K$-submodule $$R_0=\bigcap_{w}\bigl(\iota_{\mu(w)}(P_{\mu(w)}M_{\mu(w)})\bigr)_{(w)}\ \subseteq\ K^{d},$$ where $w$ runs over the finite places of $K$. It is the module $R_0$ of ([Base target slope from determinant and local index bounds](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Indef.IndefSide.boundsMu/)), formed from the coordinate data of the base level.

**Maximal slope.** For $y\in K^{d}$ and an infinite place $v$ of $K$, let $y_v\in\mathbb C^{d}$ be the image of $y$ under the embedding chosen for $v$, and let $\phi(y)=(X_v^{-1}y_v)_v$, an element of the real Euclidean space $\prod_v\mathbb C^{d}$ with its standard inner product. For a nonzero $\mathcal O_K$-submodule $N$ of $K^{d}$ put $$\sigma(N)=-\frac{\log\operatorname{covol}\phi(N)}{\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}\phi(N)},$$ where $\operatorname{covol}$ is the square root of the Gram determinant of a $\mathbb Z$-basis. The *maximal slope* of the row lattice is $$\mu_{\max}(R_0)=2\,\sup\{\sigma(N)\ :\ N\subseteq R_0\text{ a nonzero }\mathcal O_K\text{-submodule}\}-\log 2+\frac{\log|\operatorname{disc}K|}{[K:\mathbb Q]} .$$ When $K$ is totally complex and $N$ is a full lattice in a $K$-subspace, the number $2\sigma(N)-\log2+\log|\operatorname{disc}K|/[K:\mathbb Q]$ is the slope $\mu_X(N)$ defined in the conventions of ([Base target slope from determinant and local index bounds](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Indef.IndefSide.boundsMu/)).

**Admissible points.** A point is *admissible* if its analytic realisation data, with the further choices of the point, satisfy the following seven conditions. The objects named in them are those of the pages cited.

1. Hypothesis (existence of analytic theta maps) of ([Analytic theta maps and primitive row comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.ExAt/)).
2. At every stage and at every embedding $K\to\mathbb C$ that is not good, some unit vector of the discrete-series model evaluates to the complex conjugate automorphic form.
3. At every stage there is an action of the finite group on the cusp forms over the field of the original moduli that preserves the integral forms and that, at every embedding $K\to\mathbb C$, corresponds to the complex action multiplied by the designated character.
4. At every stage and at every good embedding $K\to\mathbb C$, evaluation against the chosen extremal vector is injective.
5. At every stage there is a tuple of unit lowest-type vectors of the discrete-series model at the distinguished embedding, each of the prescribed extremal weight, that pairs with a coefficient vector of norm $1$ to the determinant polynomial of ([Analytic theta intertwiners and Weil actions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.IsChainW/)).
6. At every stage, Hypothesis (one-map rows) of ([Geometric theta class evaluations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.OMPins/)).
7. Hypothesis (primitive row comparisons) of ([Analytic theta maps and primitive row comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.ExAt/)).

An embedding $\sigma\colon K\to\mathbb C$ is *good* if its restriction to the coefficient field $K_s$ of the original moduli is allowed in the sense of ([One-map and geometric comparison laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)), that is, if it sends the designated square root in $K_s$ to the fixed complex square root. Conditions 2 to 5 are four of the conditions of ([Compatibility conditions for covolume points](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.BigTop.PtAt.CompatibilityConditions/)), which imposes the seven conditions together with further ones; the objects of these four conditions are those of that page.

**Eventually at the admissible points.** Let $\mathrm{Fm}$ be a family and $Q$ a property of points. We say that *$Q$ holds eventually at the admissible points of $\mathrm{Fm}$* if there is a real number $x_0$ such that $Q$ holds for every admissible point over every member $(x,i)$ of $\mathrm{Fm}$ with $x>x_1$ and $x\ge x_0$.

**The divisibility bound.** A family *satisfies the divisibility bound* if there are real numbers $C$ and $x_0$ such that at every member with $x\ge x_0$ and for every $j\in\{1,\dots,m\}$, $$\sum_{w}\frac{\log\mathrm N\mathfrak p_w}{[K:\mathbb Q]}\,e_w\,\eta_j(w)\ \le\ D_0\cdot C\,x^{\varepsilon}\bigl(f\,m\,b\,x^{\varepsilon}+N_c\bigr),\qquad N_c=\frac{|T_2|\,k\,b\,x^{2\varepsilon}}{n^{2}} .$$ Here $w$ runs over the finite places of $K$ in the finite set $\mathcal S$ of ([Local size and support data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.SizeData%E2%82%82/)) to which the datum assigns a tuple different from its base tuple; $e_w$ is the ramification index of $w$ over $\mathbb Q$; $\eta_j(w)$ is the non-negative rational number $\eta_{j,w}$ of ([Complex vector spaces and integral spans](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.BasicNotions.Rows.Data/)), and $D_0$ is the dimension of the space $H_0$ of that page; $T_2$ is the finite set of ([Family fields and Galois data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.HC.Frame/)).

**The lower bound for the scalars.** This is the following statement. For all sufficiently constrained families $\mathrm{Fm}$ of $\mathcal C$, with the real number $C_H$ of that definition, the following holds eventually at the admissible points of $\mathrm{Fm}$: for every real number $C$, the numerical data of the point with constants $(C,C_H)$ satisfy $$l_6(j)\ \le\ \log\lambda_{j,\mu_0,\pi,v}$$ for every stage $j$, every full label $\pi$ and every infinite place $v$ of $K$.

## Compatible points for arithmetic covolume bounds

[Definition / hypothesis ARAK.D172](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.BigTop.PtAt/)

Hypothesis (compatible points for arithmetic covolume bounds). Fix one arithmetic member, its local and theta data, and a real height constant $C_{\rm size}$. Use a row family, selected rational subspace $Z$, column datum at $Z$, and its two indefinite Hermitian realizations $S_0,S_1$ as in ([Row-side families of Hermitian spaces](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Rows.RowSideFamily/)). For every selected subspace of the ambient row family choose a column datum and a period datum. At $Z$ this chosen column datum equals the row family's designated one. Every stabilizer order on every class of every base or lifted form space divides the distinguished source denominator $D_{u_0}$.

Choose $\Delta,h_0\in\mathbb N$ with the member's rational spacing scalar equal to $\Delta$ and its tolerance equal to $mh_0$. Use original rational moduli data with its scalar choices, coefficient embeddings and stagewise integral-module maps as in ([Scalar normalizations and adelic period identifications](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Pj.LawsJ/)). The additive character is the one chosen in ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)). Require this character to satisfy the negative standard additive-character normalization of ([Adelic oscillator actions and theta integrals](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Theta.WeilTied/)), including existence of compatible infinite embedding families and the identity for every such family. Its number field acts on the coefficient field $K$ of the arithmetic member. Choose ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)) over this field. Impose ([Analytic realizations of rational Hecke families](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.BaseW/)), ([Analytic theta intertwiners and Weil actions](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat.IsChainW/)), ([Weil actions and prescribed theta test vectors](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.WeilFull.IsWeilFull/)), and ([Spectral normalizations of analytic realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat3.IsScal/)), using the face determined by the selected columns, periods, $\Delta,h_0,C_{\rm size}$. The selected subspace computed from the analytic realization is $Z$, and its two computed indefinite Hermitian realizations are $S_0,S_1$.

For each input space $V_j$ and for the terminal definite space $X$, require the designated frame to be orthonormal for its Hermitian form and its $\mathcal O_E$-span to be the designated integral lattice. Every $V_j$ is positive definite. Impose ([Weight gaps for analytic comparisons](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.HostHyp/)), including its chosen weights at every stage. Choose a vector in the target discrete-series model at every stage and every embedding $K\to\mathbb C$. Use the rational expansion data and its compatibility with the analytic expansion specified in ([Analytic and rational realization data](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.Links.RealDat/)).

For each coefficient place $w$, write $e_w$ for its ramification index and $p_w$ for its residue characteristic. Write $I_j(t,w)$ for the chosen natural local image length, $D$ for the distinguished theta-image dimension, $F_0(w,j)$ for the chosen local error allocation and $\mu(w)$ for the chosen tuple. Require, for every $w,j$, the base error to equal $$\frac{\max\left(0,\ D^{-1}\sum_a\{I_a(\mu(w),w)+I_a(\mu(w),\bar w)\}-2e_w\sum_aF_0(w,a)-2e_wp_w^{-2}\right)}{e_wm}.$$ The sums run over all stages $0\le a<m$. The fractions use total division, so a zero denominator gives zero.

Use ([One-map and geometric comparison laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)) for the chosen extremal vectors, their conjugate realizations, the geometric group action, the rational cusp-form realization, the period scalar and the one-step theta comparison. At every stage choose the integral-moduli realization there with a singleton tuple set, mapped to the base tuple. Its coefficient field as a subfield of $\mathbb C$ is exactly the image of $K$ under the designated embedding. Impose the integral-moduli conditions in ([One-map and geometric comparison laws](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.IndefR.FWT.FWTie/)) for this realization, using the chosen stage weight, extremal vector, integral-module map and basepoint matrices. Its phase implication is required whenever all real uniformization data of the analytic realization satisfy their stated uniformization conditions; the phase used is the designated phase, conjugated when the distinguished-place scalar convention requires it.

The real base field is the maximal totally real subfield of $E$. All designated phases are $1$. The finite adelic frame factors on the input spaces and on $X$ are $1$. At every stage both the input and target Gram matrices are diagonal with entries in the real base field.

The initial coefficient pairing is an isometry in the following literal sense. For every choice of finite orthonormal basis $b_{\tau,a}$ of every smooth factor of its discrete-series model and all coefficient vectors $w,w'$, require $$\langle w,w'\rangle=\sum_f\overline{\operatorname{pr}\!\left(w,\bigotimes_\tau b_{\tau,f(\tau)}\right)}\operatorname{pr}\!\left(w',\bigotimes_\tau b_{\tau,f(\tau)}\right).$$ The sum runs over all choices of one basis index at each $\tau$.

Let $\mathcal P_g$ be the generator-place set and $G\simeq\prod_{P\in\mathcal P_g}\mathrm{GL}_r(k_P)$ its chosen finite group realization. Choose an isomorphism from that product to the designated finite group of the arithmetic member. The following stronger description also holds. There is a bijection from the disjoint union of the member's two generator-place index sets to $\mathcal P_g$, with field isomorphisms $\mathbb F_{g_a}\simeq k_P$. Under it the actual places equal the prescribed generator places, the two chosen quadratic-prime orientations agree, the embedded central-idempotent coefficients agree, and the finite group actions agree after base change by the field isomorphisms. The finite group identification of the rational row realization is the composite just specified.

Here is the sign convention on the principal level subgroup $P_0$ with character $\psi_0$. For every choice of adelic place idempotents $e_P$ and every $u\in P_0$, let $s_P(u)$ be the sum of the superdiagonal entries of the residue matrix of $u$ at $P$. Choose the source's fixed integral lift $\widetilde s_P(u)$ of this residue. Require $$\jmath(\psi_0(u))=\prod_{P\in\mathcal P_g}\psi\left(\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb A_F\otimes_FE/\mathbb A_F}\left(e_P\xi_0\frac{\widetilde s_P(u)}{NP}\right)\right).$$ The source fixes a right inverse of each residue map to choose these lifts; the equality is required for every family of the place idempotents.

For every tuple $t$, any two places in its finite auxiliary set which lie above the same rational prime have equal contractions to the chosen quadratic subfield. None of its auxiliary places divides $Q_Sq\operatorname{Norm}\mathfrak c$.

Finally $m>0$. Use the tied tuple-character data of ([Hecke characters in recursive realizations](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/Formalism.L2.Host.CharFrame/)). Choose a map $t(\mu)$ from the member's tuple set to the real realization's tuple set. On every used tuple, meaning the base tuple or one attached to a coefficient place, its realized level character equals that tuple's designated Hecke character. For every coefficient place, $t(\mu_w)$ is its selected real tuple. The rational row tuple map sends $t(\mu)$ to $\mu$ on used tuples, while $t$ applied after the rational row tuple map is the identity on every real tuple. The real tuple assignment at coefficient places equals the assignment in $S_0$.
