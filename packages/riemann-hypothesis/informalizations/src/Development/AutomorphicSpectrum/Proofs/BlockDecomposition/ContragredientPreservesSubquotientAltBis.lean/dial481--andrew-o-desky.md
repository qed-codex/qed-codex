## Smooth contragredients preserve subquotients

[Theorem REP.1067](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.isSubquotient_contra_of_isSubquotient/)

Let $G$ be a topological group for which every neighborhood of the identity contains a compact open subgroup. Let $\rho$ and $\tau$ be complex representations of $G$. If $\rho$ is smooth and $\tau$ is a subquotient of $\rho$, then $\tau^\vee$ is a subquotient of $\rho^\vee$.
