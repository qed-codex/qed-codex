## Finite-dimensional cusp spaces at fixed level and type

[Theorem AGR.1463](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CompanionTheory.CuspInvariant.cuspInvFin/)

Assume the boundedness and uniform convolution-reproduction properties specified below for every totally complex number field and every rank. Let $E$ be totally complex, $a\ge0$, $L\subseteq\operatorname{GL}_a(\mathbb A_{E,f})$ an open subgroup, and $\tau$ a continuous finite-dimensional complex representation of the standard infinite maximal compact subgroup $K_\infty$. Let $J$ be a set in the algebra $\mathcal Z$ of central differential operators such that the ideal generated by $J$ has finite codimension in $\mathcal Z$.

The complex span of smooth automorphic functions $f$ on $\operatorname{GL}_a(\mathbb A_E)$ satisfying all the following conditions is finite-dimensional: $f$ is right $L$-invariant, its right $K_\infty$-translates at each argument lie in the matrix-coefficient span of $\tau$, every element of $J$ annihilates $f$, $f$ is cuspidal, and $f$ is invariant under the real split central subgroup.
