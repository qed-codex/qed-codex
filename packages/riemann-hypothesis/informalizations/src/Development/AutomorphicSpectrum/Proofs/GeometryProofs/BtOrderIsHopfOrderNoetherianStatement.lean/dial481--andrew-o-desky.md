## A Hopf order from regular functions on a split generic group

[Theorem AG.0260](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.btOrder_isHopfOrder_noetherian/)

Let $R$ be a Noetherian integrally closed domain, $K=\operatorname{Frac}R$, and $T$ a finite abelian group. Let $G/R$ be a commutative group scheme that is flat, locally quasi-finite, separated, quasi-compact, and locally of finite presentation. Suppose its generic fiber is reduced and has specified $K$-points $p_t$ indexed by $T$, compatible with multiplication, whose images are pairwise disjoint and exhaust the points of the generic fiber.

Let $A\subseteq R^T$ be the $R$-subalgebra generated by all functions $f:T\to R$ with the following property: for every $t\in T$ and every $g\in G$, there are an open neighborhood $U$ of $g$ and $s\in\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_G)$ such that, whenever $p_h$ factors through $U$, its value on $s$ is $f(th)\in K$.

Then $A$ is a finite projective $R$-module, and there exists $d\in R\setminus\{0\}$ with $dR^T\subseteq A$. For every $f\in A$, $$\bigl((t,h)\mapsto f(th)\bigr)\in\operatorname{span}_R\{(t,h)\mapsto a(t)b(h):a,b\in A\},\qquad (t\mapsto f(t^{-1}))\in A.$$
