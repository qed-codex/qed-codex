## A valuation bound for coefficients of a norm form

[Theorem ARAK.1224](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.T.norm_form_gauss_bound_holds/)

Let $\Omega$ be a field of characteristic zero, $D\ge0$, and $l_0,\ldots,l_D\in\Omega$. Let $P\subset\Omega$ be the subfield generated over $\mathbb Q$ by the ratios $l_i/l_k$ with $l_k\ne0$, and suppose $\Omega/P$ is finite, of degree $s$. Assume that a homogeneous polynomial over $\mathbb Q$ vanishing at $(l_0,\ldots,l_D)$ is always zero.

Let $p$ be prime, $0\le j\le D$, $K,d\ge0$, $x\in\Omega$, and let $c_i\in\mathbb Q[X_0,\ldots,X_D]$ for every $i\ge0$. Suppose $c_s$ is homogeneous of degree $d$ and the characteristic polynomial of multiplication by $x$ on $\Omega$ over $P$, viewed over $\Omega$, is $$\sum_{i=0}^{s}c_i(l_0/l_j,\ldots,l_D/l_j)T^{s-i}.$$ Suppose $p^Kx$ is integral over the subring of $\Omega$ generated by all ratios $l_i/l_k$ and all $1/r$ with $r\in\mathbb N$ and $p\nmid r$. Then every coefficient satisfies $$v_p([X^\mu]c_s)\ge-sK\qquad(\mu\in\mathbb N^{D+1}).$$ Ratios and the valuation at zero use the total conventions: $a/0=0$ and $v_p(0)=0$.
