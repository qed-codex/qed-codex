## Algebraic independence after imposing generic linear incidences

[Theorem ALG.0561](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CompanionTheory.Ar.Short.ChowClosing.Auto.ChartFreeParAlgIndep/)

Let $F\subseteq K\subseteq L$ be fields, let $n,e\ge0$, and let $u_{im}\in K$ for $1\le i\le e$, $1\le m\le n$, be algebraically independent over $F$, with $K=F(u_{im})$. Let $\eta_m\in L$ and $t_m\in F$ satisfy $$L=K(\eta_1,\ldots,\eta_n),\qquad\sum_m u_{im}\eta_m=0\quad(1\le i\le e),\qquad\sum_m t_m\eta_m=1.$$ Fix $j_0$ with $\eta_{j_0}\ne0$ and put $E=F(\eta_m/\eta_{j_0}:1\le m\le n)$. If $$\operatorname{trdeg}_F F[\eta_m/\eta_{j_0}:1\le m\le n]\le e,$$ then $(u_{im})_{i,\,m\ne j_0}$ is algebraically independent over $E$, and $L/K$ is algebraic.

## A Chow form as a determinant on an incidence chart

[Theorem AG.1161](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CompanionTheory.Ar.Short.ChowClosing.Auto.ChartNormAlgClosed/)

Let $F$ be algebraically closed, $n,e\ge0$, and $I\subset F[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ a homogeneous prime ideal not containing all coordinate variables. Let $f(u_0,\ldots,u_e)$ be a Chow form for $I$: it is nonzero and generates the ideal of polynomials vanishing on tuples of linear forms with a common nonzero zero on the affine cone of $I$. Write $f_t=f(t,u_1,\ldots,u_e)$ for $t\in F^n$, and put $$R=F[u_{ij}:1\le i\le e,\ 1\le j\le n],\qquad B=R[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/\left(I,\ \sum_jt_jx_j-1,\ \sum_ju_{ij}x_j\ (1\le i\le e)\right).$$ Let $\mathfrak p\subset R$ be prime and either zero or generated by a prime element, and assume $f_t\notin\mathfrak p$. Set $B_{\mathfrak p}=B\otimes_RR_{\mathfrak p}$. For every $u\in F^n$, $$f_u=f_t\det_{R_{\mathfrak p}}\!\left(m_{\sum_ju_jx_j}:B_{\mathfrak p}\to B_{\mathfrak p}\right)$$ in $R_{\mathfrak p}$, where $m_b$ denotes multiplication by $b$.

## A prime polynomial divisible by a linear form is a norm

[Theorem ALG.1189](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CompanionTheory.Ar.Short.ChowClosing.Auto.NormOfDividedFormSep/)

Let $\mathcal O$ be an infinite commutative domain and $B$ a commutative domain, finite free as an $\mathcal O$-algebra. Assume $\operatorname{Frac}(B)/\operatorname{Frac}(\mathcal O)$ is separable. Let $\eta_1,\ldots,\eta_n$ generate $B$ as an $\mathcal O$-algebra, and choose $t\in\mathcal O^n$ with $\sum_it_i\eta_i=1$. Let $P\in\mathcal O[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$ be a prime element. If $$\sum_i\eta_iX_i\ \mid\ P\quad\text{in }B[X_1,\ldots,X_n],$$ then for every $u\in\mathcal O^n$, $$P(u)=P(t)\det_{\mathcal O}\!\left(m_{\sum_i u_i\eta_i}:B\to B\right).$$

## Chow degree of a closed projective point

[Theorem AG.1378](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/GlobalTheory.CompanionTheory.Ar.Short.ChowClosing.Auto.ObjectsChow_ChowDegreeClosedPoint/)

Let $F$ be a perfect field, $n\ge0$, and $I\subset F[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$ a homogeneous prime ideal not containing every coordinate variable. Assume $$\dim F[X_1,\ldots,X_n]/I=1.$$ For any $i$ with $X_i\notin I$, the Chow degree in one hyperplane block, defined below, satisfies $$\deg_{\mathrm{Chow}}(I;1)=\dim_F F[X_1,\ldots,X_n]/\bigl(I+(X_i-1)\bigr).$$
