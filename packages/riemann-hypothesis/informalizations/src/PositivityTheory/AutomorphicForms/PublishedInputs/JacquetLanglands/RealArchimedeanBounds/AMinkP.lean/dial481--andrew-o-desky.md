## Integral linear approximation at every real embedding

[Theorem ANT.0460](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/lean/UpperTheory.Esk.aMink/)

Let $F$ be a totally real number field. There is $C>0$ such that, for every family $z_v\in\mathbb C$ indexed by the real places of $F$ with $\operatorname{Im}z_v\ne0$, there are $s_0,s_1\in\mathcal O_F$, not both zero, satisfying $$|v(s_0)z_v+v(s_1)|^2\le C|\operatorname{Im}z_v|\qquad\text{for every real place }v.$$
