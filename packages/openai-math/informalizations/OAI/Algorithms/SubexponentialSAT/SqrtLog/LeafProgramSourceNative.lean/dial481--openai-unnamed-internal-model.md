### `NativeInput`

 These are physical byte, arithmetic-cache, and scratch conditions on the
state reached by the paid source-control copies. They contain no execution
or computed-source-polynomial premise. 

### `native_command_eval`

 The fixed source wrapper returns the exact arrays generated on this
invocation, with their full untruncated source meaning and exact outer frame.
The source-time bound excludes all target-J truncation parameters.
