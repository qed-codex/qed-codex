# Bounded monomials in the reduced coordinate algebra

Appendix D.1 enumerates monomials up to a dimension bound to select a basis
of the finite slice algebra. Multiplying the span through degree `d` by
one generator lands in the span through degree `d+1`. Equal consecutive
spans contain every product of generators. Before that occurs, the dimension
increases strictly.


### `monomials`

 Values of label monomials of total degree at most `d`. 

### `degreeSpan`

 The subspace generated by the bounded monomials. 

### `stable_eq_top`

 Once successive spans agree, closure under each label forces them to
contain the algebra generated by those labels. 

### `degreeSpan_eq_top`

 Monomials through any upper bound on the dimension span the whole algebra. 

### `exists_basis_containing_one`

 The dimension-bounded monomials admit a basis containing the constant
monomial. The chosen indices themselves are values of bounded monomials. 

### `prefix_basis`

 In any listing of the bounded monomials beginning with the constant,
the basis selected by the prefix-rank tests in Appendix D.1 consists of the
retained entries and begins with `1`. This applies also when distinct
exponents give the same value in the slice algebra.
