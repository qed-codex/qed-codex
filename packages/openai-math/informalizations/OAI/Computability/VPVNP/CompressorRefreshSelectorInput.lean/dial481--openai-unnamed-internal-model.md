# The actual term-worker input at a refresh boundary

The native preparation fills one demand row for each old base and residual lane.
Its physical order and term addresses give the input of the local-history
selector before any of its term bits are read.


### `workerInput`

 The two allocations and the dense demand readback generated by `prepare_verified`
supply all machine inputs of the physical selector. The instruction budget may
be any supplied cap at least as large as the motif worker's checked budget. 

### `workerRows`

 Both the physical input and output of every block live above the current
controller area, even when the rows have different strides.
