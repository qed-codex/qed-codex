 # Balanced degree factors of a graded circuit grammar

Section 4.2 uses the ideal generated by states of degree between one third and
two thirds of the output degree. In a binary production above that interval, at
least one child reaches its lower endpoint. This expresses the output as a sum
of multiples of the balanced states. Homogeneous projection retains only the
complementary degree of each multiplier.


### `BalancedIndex`

 States whose declared degree lies in the balanced interval for `ℓ`. 

### `balancedIdeal`

 The ideal generated by all balanced states. 

### `eval_mem_balancedIdeal`

 A state at or above the lower endpoint of the interval belongs to the balanced ideal. 

### `exists_balanced_expansion`

 Explicit linear combination indexed by the balanced states, with polynomial coefficients. 

### `exists_homogeneous_balanced_expansion`

 Above the balanced interval, each multiplier may be taken homogeneous of
exactly the complementary degree.
