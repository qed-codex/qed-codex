# Exact call sequences of the terminal controller

The same list of private branch strings is used by the terminal image and
the individual workers. Each continuing report determines the stage and local
state of the next genuine call. After the last stage accepts the sequence
cannot contain any further calls.


### `Log`

 The worker entry together with its original report. 

### `reports`

 The chronological physical reports generated by the logged worker calls. 

### `hostState`

 Persistent host marker for an entry, or termination after the final worker. 

### `StateValid`

 Every pending entry is consistent with the two controller cells. 

### `rawFuel`

 Fuel remaining for the worker and its exit after the entry instructions. 

### `Visits`

 One derivation step is a successful call of the original component.
The source clock includes the space needed for its selected native exit. 

### `recover_visits`

 Inverse at every finite prefix, recovering exactly the worker call and
its actual branch stream. In particular an additional call after final
acceptance has no derivation. 

### `finish`

 A closed visit sequence ending at a worker halt has exactly the host
terminal marker specified by that final call. 

### `actual_iff`

 Exactly the complete genuine call strings, preserving any caller cap
on physical reports. The proof works in both directions on the same input.
