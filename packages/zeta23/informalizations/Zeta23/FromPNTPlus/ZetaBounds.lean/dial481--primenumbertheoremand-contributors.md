### `riemannzeta0`

 We use `ζ` to denote the Rieman zeta function and `ζ₀` to denote the alternative Rieman zeta
function. 

### `norm_zeta_product_ge_one`

 For positive `x` and nonzero `y` we have that
$|\zeta(x)^3 \cdot \zeta(x+iy)^4 \cdot \zeta(x+2iy)| \ge 1$.
