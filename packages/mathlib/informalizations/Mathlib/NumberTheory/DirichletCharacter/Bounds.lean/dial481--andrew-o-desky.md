## Absolute values of Dirichlet characters

[Theorem ANT.1742](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/z2-character-absolute-values.html)

Let $F$ be a normed field, $N\in\mathbb N$, and $\chi:\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z\to F$ a Dirichlet character. Then $$\|\chi(a)\|\le1\qquad(a\in\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z).$$
