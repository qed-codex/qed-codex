## Gamma nonvanishing and cosine zeros

[Theorem ANT.1735](https://andrewodesky.com/rh/s/z1-gamma-cosine.html)

Let $\Gamma$ denote Euler's gamma function. For every $w\in\mathbb C$, $$\Re w>0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Gamma(w)\ne0.$$ For every $z\in\mathbb C$, $$\cos z=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad z=\frac{(2k+1)\pi}{2}\ \text{for some }k\in\mathbb Z.$$
