# The period rings A, B⁺, B and B_dR⁺: θ, Frobenius, domains and contraction

`New.Period.Ainf` is `A_inf`, for the prime `p` supplied by the class `ThePrime p`. In it, `ξ`
(`New.Period.xi`) generates the kernel of Fontaine's `θ`, `μ = [ε] - 1 = ξ τ` with
`τ = [ε^(1/p)] - 1`, where `ε ∈ O^♭` is a compatible system `(1, ζ_p, ζ_{p²}, …)` of `p`-power roots
of unity and `[·]` is the Teichmüller lift, and `φ` is the Frobenius. The map `θ` is defined on `A`,
`B⁺` and `B_dR⁺` (`New.Period.thetaBdR`). The file studies `A` modulo `p` and its divisibility by
powers of `p`. It shows that `θ` has kernel `ξ B⁺` on `B⁺` and that `B_dR⁺` is a discrete valuation
ring. It deduces that `A`, `B⁺` and `B` are domains.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.Period.Amax`: `A`, the `p`-adic completion of `A_inf[X] / (p X - ξ)`, with the existence half
  of its universal property, `New.Period.Amax.exists_lift`.
* `New.Period.BPlus`: `B⁺ = A[1/p]`; `New.Period.Bmax`: `B = B⁺[1/μ]`; `New.Period.BdRPlus`: the
  `ξ`-adic completion `B_dR⁺` of `B⁺`.
* `New.Period.actBmax`: the monoid `New.Period.FrobGal = ℕ × G_p`, with `G_p` the absolute Galois
  group of `ℚ_p`, in which `(n, g)` stands for `φ^n g`, acts on `A`, `B⁺` and `B`. On `B` this needs
  `New.Period.isUnit_smul_of_dvd_mu`, which rests on `New.Period.associated_p_frobEnd_xi`: `φ ξ` is
  `p` times a unit.
* `New.Period.thetaBPlus`: `θ` extends to `A`, with `X ↦ 0`, and to a map from `B⁺` onto `ℂ_[p]`
  (`New.Period.thetaBPlus_surjective`).
* `New.Period.BdRPlus.prime_xi`: `ξ` is prime in `B_dR⁺`, so `B_dR⁺` is a discrete valuation ring
  with uniformiser `ξ`.
* `New.Period.exists_Ainf_eq_of_contraction`: let `V ⊆ B⁺` (`New.Period.BPlus`) lie in the image
  `New.Period.amaxImage` of the period ring `A`, and let every `v ∈ V` satisfy `ξ ^ n v = u φ(w)`
  for some `w ∈ V`, with `u ∈ A_inf` fixed and `φ` the Frobenius. Then `p ^ (n + 1) V` lies in the
  image of `A_inf`: each application of `φ` improves an approximation by `A_inf` by one power of
  `p`.

Notions from other files:
* `New.Period.Ainf`: the ring `A_inf` of `p`-typical Witt vectors over the tilt `O^♭` of the ring of
  integers of `ℂ_p`.
* `New.Period.xi`: the element `ξ = ∑_{i < p} [ε^{1/p}]^i` of `A_inf`, so that `ξ τ = μ`.
* `New.Period.theta`: Fontaine's ring homomorphism `θ` from `A_inf` onto the ring `New.OC p` of
  integers of `ℂ_p`, sending the Teichmüller lift `[x]` to `x^♯`.

## Relation to the literature

Known; see `Lib.PadicHodge.PeriodRings.DeRhamPeriods` for sources.


 ### The rings 

### `Amax`

 The period ring A (Colmez's A_max): the p-adic completion of A_inf[X]/(pX − ξ). 

### `BPlus`

 The period ring B^+ = A[1/p], where A is `New.Period.Amax`. 

### `Bmax`

 The period ring B = B^+[1/μ] (Colmez's B_max), over which crystalline representations are
defined here. It is used in place of Fontaine's B_cris. The two rings are known to give the same
crystalline representations; that comparison is neither proved nor used here. 

 ### The ring `B_dR⁺` 

### `BdRPlus`

 The ring B_dR^+, defined as the completion of B^+ with respect to the ideal generated by the
image of ξ. 

### `instance [Algebra R Amax] : IsAdicComplete (span {(p : R)}) Amax`

 The period ring A is `p`-adically complete, for the ideal generated by `p` in any ring `R` over
which it is an algebra. 

### `amaxImage`

 The image of the period ring A in B^+ = A[1/p], as an A_inf-subalgebra. 

### `Amax.xiDivP`

 The element X = ξ/p of the period ring A: the image of the variable of A_inf[X]/(pX − ξ). 

### `isLocalizationAway_p_bPlus`

 B⁺ is the localization of the period ring A at the powers of p. 

### `instance : IsLocalization.Away (mu : BPlus) Bmax`

 B is the localization of B⁺ at the powers of μ. 

### `OC.toPadicIntAlgHom`

 For a commutative Z_p-algebra R and a ring homomorphism f: R → O_C, the composite of f with
the inclusion O_C ⊂ C_p, as a Z_p-algebra homomorphism R → C_p. 

### `Amax.p_mem_nonZeroDivisors`

 The prime p is not a zero divisor in the period ring A, so A is p-torsion free. 

### `Amax.exists_lift`

 The universal property of `A`: a ring map `f` from `A_inf` to a `p`-adically complete ring, with
an `x` such that `p x = f ξ`, extends to `A` with `X ↦ x`. 

### `Amax.p_pow_dvd_of_mem_pow`

 The image in `A` of an element of `(p, ξ) ^ n` is divisible by `p ^ n`. 

### `Amax.exists_p_pow_dvd_sub_aeval`

 Every element of `A` is congruent modulo `p ^ n` to a polynomial in `X = ξ / p` with
coefficients in `A_inf`. 

### `Amax.exists_algebraMap_eq_p_pow_mul_sub`

 For every `y` in the period ring A and every `n`, some `p ^ d (y - p ^ n w)`, with `w` in A, is
the image of an element of `A_inf`. 

 ### The action of Frobenius and of the Galois group 

### `actAmax`

 Frobenius and `G_p` act on `A`. 

### `instance : SMulDistribClass FrobGal Ainf Amax`

 The action on A extends the action on `A_inf`. 

### `actBPlus`

 Frobenius and `G_p` act on `B⁺`. 

### `instance : SMulDistribClass FrobGal Amax BPlus`

 The action on B⁺ extends the action on A. 

### `instance : SMulDistribClass FrobGal Ainf BPlus`

 The action on B⁺ extends the action on `A_inf`. 

### `instance : Algebra ℚ_[p] BPlus`

 B⁺ is a `ℚ_p`-algebra, since `p` is a unit of B⁺. 

### `instance : IsScalarTower ℤ_[p] ℚ_[p] BPlus`

 The `ℤ_p`-algebra structure of B⁺ factors through `ℚ_p`. 

### `associated_p_frobEnd_xi`

 The image of φ(ξ) in the period ring A equals p up to a unit of A. 

### `isUnit_smul_of_dvd_mu`

 If `a ∈ A_inf` divides `μ`, then for every `m = (n, g)` in `FrobGal = ℕ × G_p` the image of
`m • a = φ^n (g a)` in B = B^+[1/μ] is a unit. 

### `actBmax`

 Frobenius and `G_p` act on `B`. 

### `instance [SMul FrobGal R] [Algebra R BPlus] [SMulDistribClass FrobGal R BPlus] : SMulDistribClass FrobGal R Bmax`

 If the action on B⁺ extends an action on a ring `R` below it, so does the action on B. 

 ### The map `θ` 

### `thetaAmax`

 The ring homomorphism from the period ring A to the integers of C_p that extends Fontaine's
θ on A_inf and sends X = ξ/p to 0. 

### `thetaBPlus`

 The ring homomorphism θ: B^+ = A[1/p] → C_p extending `New.Period.thetaAmax`. 

### `thetaAmax_algebraMap`

 On `A_inf`, `θ` of the period ring A is Fontaine's `θ`. 

### `thetaBPlus_algebraMap_Amax`

 On A, `θ` of B⁺ is `θ` of A followed by the inclusion of `O_C` into `ℂ_p`. 

### `thetaBPlus_ainf`

 On `A_inf`, `θ` of B⁺ is Fontaine's `θ` followed by the inclusion of `O_C` into `ℂ_p`. 

### `thetaBPlus_surjective`

 The map `thetaBPlus` : B⁺ → ℂ_p is onto. It is Fontaine's θ on the period ring B⁺ = A[1/p],
where A is `Amax`, and it extends θ on `Amax`. 


## Structure of A: reduction modulo p and divisibility by powers of p

* `New.Period.Amax.exists_reduction_mod_p`: modulo `p` the ring A is the polynomial ring over
  `A_inf / (p, ξ)`.
* `New.Period.Amax.mem_span_pow_of_p_pow_dvd`: divisibility by `p ^ m` in A, of an element of
  `A_inf`, means membership in `(p, ξ) ^ m`.


### `exists_reduction_mod_p`

 Let A (`Amax`) be the p-adic completion of A_inf[X]/(pX − ξ), and write `X` also for the image
of the variable in A, so that p·X = ξ. There is a ring homomorphism from A to (A_inf/(p, ξ))[X] with
kernel pA that sends P(X) to the reduction of P for every polynomial P over A_inf; so
A/p = (A_inf/(p, ξ))[X]. 

### `p_pow_dvd_of_dvd_aeval_mul`

 Let `f` be a monic polynomial over `A_inf` and `X = ξ / p` (`xiDivP`). If `p ^ m` divides
`f(X) y` in the period ring A, then it divides `y`. 

### `exists_p_pow_mul_eq_add`

 Let `Amax` be the period ring A, the p-adic completion of A_inf[X]/(pX − ξ), and let `ν ∈ A_inf`
with `ξ` in the ideal `(p, ν)`, that is, `ν ∣ ξ - p b` for some `b ∈ A_inf`. For every `n` and every
`y` in A there are `a ∈ A_inf` and `z` in A with `p ^ n y = a + ν ^ n z`. 

### `mem_span_pow_of_p_pow_dvd`

 If the image in the period ring A of an element `a` of `A_inf` is divisible by `p ^ m`, then `a`
lies in the ideal `(p, ξ) ^ m` of `A_inf`. 


## The kernel of θ on B⁺ and B_dR⁺; B_dR⁺ is a discrete valuation ring

* `New.Period.thetaBPlus_eq_zero_iff`, `New.Period.thetaBdR_eq_zero_iff`: on `B⁺` and on
  `B_dR⁺` the kernel of `θ` is generated by `ξ`.
* `New.Period.BdRPlus.isUnit_iff`: the units of `B_dR⁺` are the `x` with `θ x ≠ 0`.
* `New.Period.BdRPlus.prime_xi`: `ξ` is prime in `B_dR⁺`, which is a discrete valuation ring;
  `A`, `B⁺` and `B` are domains.


### `injective_Amax_BPlus`

 The natural map from the period ring `Amax` (Colmez's `A_max`, the p-adic completion of
`A_inf[X]/(pX − ξ)`) to `B⁺ = Amax[1/p]` is injective, since `p` is not a zero divisor in `Amax`. 

 ### The ring `B⁺` 

### `BPlus.exists_p_pow_mul_eq_amax`

 For every `y` in `B⁺ = A[1/p]` there is a power `p ^ m` such that `p ^ m * y` is the image of
an element `b` of the period ring `A`. 

### `tau_dvd_xi_sub_p`

 `τ = [ε^(1/p)] - 1` divides `ξ - p`: each of the `p` terms `[ε^(1/p)] ^ i` of `ξ` is congruent
to `1` modulo `τ`. 

### `exists_tau_dvd_xi_sub_p_mul`

 `τ` divides `ξ - p * b` for some `b` in `A_inf` (namely `b = 1`): `ξ` lies in the ideal
`(p, τ)`. 

 ### `A_inf ∩ y B⁺ = y A_inf`: reduction modulo `(y, p ^ n)` extends to `Amax` 

### `isAdicComplete_of_p_pow_eq_zero`

 A commutative ring in which a power of `p` vanishes is `p`-adically complete. 

### `Amax.exists_lift_quotient`

 Let `ξ ≡ p b` modulo `y` in `A_inf`. Then for every `n` the reduction of `A_inf` modulo the
ideal `(y, p ^ n)` extends to a ring homomorphism on the period ring `Amax`. 

### `dvd_of_dvd_prime_pow_mul`

 Powers of a prime `q` of `A_inf` that does not divide `y` cancel: if `y` divides `q ^ k * a`,
then `y` divides `a`. 

### `dvd_of_algebraMap_BPlus_dvd`

 `A_inf ∩ y B⁺ = y A_inf`. Let `y` in `A_inf` not be divisible by `p`, and let `ξ` lie in the
ideal `(p, y)`, that is, `y ∣ ξ - p b` for some `b`. If `a` is in `A_inf` and `y` divides `a` in
`B⁺ = Amax[1/p]`, then `y` divides `a` in `A_inf`. 

 ### Approximation by `A_inf` and the kernel of `θ` on `B⁺` 

### `amaxImage.p_pow_mul_mem_of_dvd_mu`

 Let `p` be a prime (`ThePrime p`), let `e` in `A_inf` divide `μ = [ε] - 1`, and let `y` be in
`B⁺ = A[1/p]`, where `A` is the period ring `Amax`. If `e ^ n • y` lies in the image `amaxImage` of
`A` in `B⁺`, then so does `p ^ (2 * n) * y`. 

### `BPlus.exists_Ainf_approx`

 Let `p` be a prime (`ThePrime p`) and `B⁺ = A[1/p]`, where `A` is the period ring `Amax`. For
`y` in `B⁺` and natural numbers `k`, `n` there are `m`, an element `a` of `A_inf` and an element `w`
of the image `amaxImage` of `A` in `B⁺` with `p ^ m * y = a + p ^ (m + k) * μ ^ n * w`, where
`μ = [ε] - 1`. 

### `BPlus.iterate_frobEnd_algebraMap`

 For `a` in `A_inf` and every `j`, applying the Frobenius `φ` (`frobEnd`) `j` times to the image
of `a` in the period ring `B⁺ = Amax[1/p]` gives the image of `φ^j a`. Here `Amax` is Colmez's
`A_max`, the p-adic completion of `A_inf[X]/(pX − ξ)`, and `A_inf` maps to `B⁺` through `Amax`. 

### `BPlus.dvd_of_thetaBPlus_frobEnd_eq_zero`

 Let `ν` be in `A_inf` and `s : ℕ → ℕ`, and suppose `ν` divides every `a` in `A_inf` with
`θ (φ^[s j] a) = 0` for all `j`, where `φ` is the Frobenius. Then `ν` divides in `B⁺` every `y`
with `θ (φ^[s j] y) = 0` for all `j`. 

### `thetaBPlus_eq_zero_iff`

 For `y` in `B⁺`, `θ y = 0` if and only if `ξ` divides `y` in `B⁺`; that is, the kernel of
`θ : B⁺ → ℂ_p` is `ξ B⁺`. 

### `BPlus.eq_zero_of_forall_pow_dvd`

 Let `a ∈ A_inf`. An element `y` of `B⁺` divisible by `(ξ - p a) ^ n` for every `n` is zero. 

### `thetaEquiv`

 The isomorphism of `ℚ_p`-algebras `B⁺ ⧸ span {ξ} ≃ₐ[ℚ_[p]] ℂ_[p]` induced by the surjective
map `θ : B⁺ → ℂ_p`, whose kernel is generated by `ξ`. 

### `thetaBdR`

 The ring homomorphism `θ` from `B_dR⁺` to `ℂ_p`: the projection to `B⁺ / ξ B⁺` followed by
the isomorphism `thetaEquiv` of that quotient with `ℂ_p`. 

### `thetaBdR_algebraMap_BPlus`

 On `B⁺`, `θ` of `B_dR⁺` is `θ` of `B⁺`. 

### `thetaBdR_ainf`

 On `A_inf`, `θ` of `B_dR⁺` is Fontaine's `θ` followed by the inclusion of `O_C` into `ℂ_p`. 

### `BdRPlus.xi_pow_dvd_iff`

 For `y` in `B⁺` and a natural number `n`, `ξ ^ n` divides the image of `y` in `B_dR⁺` if and
only if `ξ ^ n` divides `y` in `B⁺`. 

### `BdRPlus.exists_xi_pow_dvd_sub`

 Every element of `B_dR⁺` is congruent modulo `ξ ^ n` to an element of `B⁺`. 

### `BdRPlus.exists_Ainf_approx`

 For every `x` in `B_dR⁺` and every natural number `n` there are a natural number `m` and `r`
in `A_inf` such that `ξ ^ n` divides `p ^ m * x - r` in `B_dR⁺`. 

### `thetaBdR_eq_zero_iff`

 For `x` in `B_dR⁺`, `θ x = 0` if and only if `ξ` divides `x`: the kernel of
`θ : B_dR⁺ → ℂ_p` is `ξ B_dR⁺`. 

### `BdRPlus.isUnit_iff`

 An element `x` of `B_dR⁺` is a unit if and only if `θ x ≠ 0`. 

### `BdRPlus.exists_associated_xi_pow`

 Every nonzero element of `B_dR⁺` is a power of `ξ` times a unit. 

 ### `B_dR⁺` is a discrete valuation ring; the period rings are domains 

### `BdRPlus.prime_xi`

 The element `ξ` is prime in `B_dR⁺`. 

### `injective_BPlus_Bmax`

 The natural map from `B⁺ = Amax[1/p]` to Colmez's period ring `B = B⁺[1/μ]` (`Bmax`) is
injective, since `B⁺` is a domain and `μ ≠ 0`. Here `Amax` is the p-adic completion of
`A_inf[X]/(pX − ξ)`, and `μ = [ε] − 1` (`mu`), where `[ε]` is a Teichmüller lift. 

### `Amax.nonempty_adicBasis`

 Let `Λ` be a ring that is complete for a maximal ideal `I = (p)`, and let the period ring `A`
be a `Λ`-algebra. Then `A` has an `I`-adic topological basis over `Λ` (`New.Lift.AdicBasis`):
vectors whose finite combinations are dense, with coordinate forms. 

 ## The contraction lemma 

### `New.Period.xi_pow_dvd_of_algebraMap_BPlus_dvd`

 Divisibility by `ξ ^ n` in `B⁺` of an element `a` of `A_inf` descends to `A_inf`, since in both
rings the multiples of `ξ` are the kernel of `θ`. 

### `New.Period.exists_Ainf_eq_of_contraction`

 Contraction: let `B⁺ = A[1/p]`, where `A` is the period ring `Amax`, and let `V ⊆ B⁺` lie in
`amaxImage`, the image of `A` in `B⁺`. Let `u` be in `A_inf`, and suppose every `v` in `V` satisfies
`ξ ^ n • v = u • φ w` for some `w` in `V`. Here `ξ = ∑_{i<p} [ε^{1/p}]^i ∈ A_inf`, and `φ`
(`frobEnd`) is the Frobenius of `B⁺` given by the generator of `ℕ` in its `ℕ × G_p` action. Then for
every `v` in `V`, `p ^ (n + 1) * v` is the image in `B⁺` of an element of `A_inf`.
