# Mayer–Vietoris for two charts of a line modulo an ideal

This file gives the commutative algebra behind a Mayer–Vietoris sequence of locally constant
`ZMod ℓ`-valued functions for two charts of a line. The setting is two charts `Spec B`, `Spec C`
over `A` with overlap `Spec D`, where `D = B[1 / t] = C[1 / s]`, `t * s = 1` in `D`, and `D` is
integral over `A[t, s]`. Everything is read modulo an ideal `I` of `A`. Under further hypotheses,
compatible clopens of the charts come from the base, and every locally constant function on the
overlap is a difference of functions from the charts.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.Variety.exists_eq_basicOpen_of_isClopen_of_preimage_eq`: compatible clopens of the charts
  come from `A`, assuming every pair in `B × C` agreeing in `D` comes from `A` (`hA_onto`) and `hI`.
* `New.Variety.exists_eq_locConstSpec_map_sub_map`: under further hypotheses (`A`, `D` domains; `ℓ`
  prime and a unit in `A`; `A`, `B`, `C` absolutely integrally closed; `hI`; `h_cases`), every
  locally constant `ZMod ℓ`-valued function on the overlap is a difference of functions from the
  charts.

Notions from other files:
* `New.Variety.IsAbsIntegrallyClosed`: A is absolutely integrally closed: every monic polynomial
  over A of positive degree has a root in A.
* `New.ThePrime`: `ThePrime ℓ` fixes the prime `ℓ` (it extends `Fact ℓ.Prime`).
* `New.Variety.locConstSpec`: For an abelian group Λ, the functor on commutative rings sending A to
  the group of locally constant functions Spec A → Λ; it sends a ring map to pull-back along the map
  of spectra.

## Relation to the literature

Known; see `Lib.Cohomology.ProperBaseChange.Theorem` for sources.


### `eventually_mem_span_closure`

 Approximation by finitely generated subrings: if `z` lies in the `A`-span of a family
`f : ι → D`, then for all large finite `T ⊆ ι` and `S ⊆ A`, `z` lies in the span of `f(T)` over
the subring of `A` generated by `S`. 

### `eventually_zpow_mul_mem_range`

 If `t` is the unit `τ` in `D = B[1 / t]`, then for every `x ∈ D` and all large enough integers
`j`, the element `τ ^ j * x` is the image of an element of `B`. 

### `mem_span_range_zpowers_of_mem_adjoin`

 For a unit `τ` of `D`, every element of the algebra `A[τ, τ⁻¹]` is an `A`-combination of
integer powers of `τ`. 

### `exists_finset_subset_span_of_isIntegral`

 If `D` is integral over a subalgebra `L` and `G ⊆ D` is finite, then there is a finite `S ⊆ D`
such that `1`, the elements of `G` and the products of two elements of `S` lie in the `L`-span
of `S`. 

### `exists_pow_le_of_mul_le`

 Let `M a b` be submodules of an `S`-algebra `R`, indexed by pairs of integers, with
`M a b * M c d ≤ M (a + c - k) (b + d + k)` for all `a, b, c, d`, and `1 ∈ M (-k) k`. Then every
power of `M (-k) k` lies in some `M i j`. 

### `exists_pow_smul_mem_range_of_pow_succ_smul_mem_range`

 Let `F` be a Noetherian `R`-submodule of `D`, for a subring `R` of `A`, let `φ : B × C → D` be
`A`-linear and `ϖ ∈ A`. Then for some `k`, every `z ∈ F` with `ϖ ^ (k + 1) z` in the image of `φ`
has `ϖ ^ k z` in the image of `φ`. 

### `exists_isNoetherian_pow_le_fg`

 Let `D = B[1 / t] = C[1 / s]` with `t * s = 1` (`hts`) be integral over `A[t, s]` (`hD`), and
let `G ⊆ D` be finite. Then there are a subring `R` of `A` and an `R`-submodule `Y` of `D` such
that `Y` contains `1` and `G`, `Y` is Noetherian over `R`, and for each `n` there are
`R`-submodules `P`, `Q`, `M` of `D` with `P` in the image of `B`, `Q` in the image of `C`,
`M` finitely generated, `Y ^ n ≤ M ≤ P ⊔ Q ⊔ Y` (membership in `Icc`) and `P * Q ≤ M`. 

### `exists_common_lift_of_isIdempotentElem`

 Let `D = B[1 / t] = C[1 / s]` with `t * s = 1` be integral over `A[t, s]`, and let `A` map onto
the fibre product `B ×_D C` (`hA`). Let `x ∈ B` and `y ∈ C` have idempotent classes in `B / IB`
and `C / IC`, and let `x (1 - y) = ϖ α` and `y (1 - x) = ϖ β` in `D` for one `ϖ ∈ I`. Then one
`k ∈ A` has the class of `x` in `B / IB` and the class of `y` in `C / IC`. 

### `exists_pow_eq_mul_of_local`

 Let `D = B[1 / t] = C[1 / s]` with `t * s = 1` be integral over `A[t, s]`, where `A` is an
absolutely integrally closed local domain in which `ℓ` is a unit. Let `ϖ` be a non-unit of `A` and
`w ^ ℓ = 1 + ϖ G` in `D`. Then there are `a ∈ B`, `b ∈ C` and `c ∈ D` such that
`(w (1 + ϖ c)) ^ ℓ = (1 + ϖ a) (1 + ϖ b)` in `D`. 

### `isPrimitiveRoot_map_of_isRoot_cyclotomic`

 Let `ℓ` be a unit of `A` and `ζ ∈ A` a root of the `ℓ`-th cyclotomic polynomial. Then the image
of `ζ` under any ring map `ψ` from `A` to a domain is a primitive `ℓ`-th root of unity. 

### `exists_locallyConstant_sub_pow_mem_of_pow_eq_one`

 Logarithms of roots of unity on a spectrum. Let `ℓ` be a unit of `A` and `ζ ∈ A` a root of the
`ℓ`-th cyclotomic polynomial. If `y ^ ℓ = 1` in an `A`-algebra `S`, then there is a locally constant
`e : Spec S → ℕ` such that `y - ζ ^ e p` lies in each prime `p` of `S`. 

### `exists_locallyConstant_eq_sub_of_pow_eq_mul`

 Kummer theory. Let `A`, `D` be domains, `A`, `B`, `C` absolutely integrally closed, `ℓ` a prime
invertible in `A`, and `J`, `L`, `K` ideals of `B`, `C`, `D` with `J`, `L` mapping into `K`. If
every `ℓ`-th root of `1` in `D ⧸ K` lifts to some `W` with `W ^ ℓ = a * b`, `a ≡ 1` in `B ⧸ J` and
`b ≡ 1` in `C ⧸ L` (`h_factor`), then every locally constant `ZMod ℓ`-valued function on
`Spec (D ⧸ K)` is a difference of pull-backs from `Spec (B ⧸ J)` and `Spec (C ⧸ L)`. 

### `IsLineChartCase`

 The condition on the chart `R[1 / a]` of a line modulo the ideal `I` of `R`: `a * (a - 1) ∈ I`,
or the equation `ε + r * ε ^ 2 = 1` has a solution `ε ∈ R` for every `r ∈ I`. 

### `exists_pow_eq_mul_of_away`

 The case of a chart `B = A[1 / a]` in `exists_eq_locConstSpec_map_sub_map`. Let `1 - t = 1 / a`
in `B` and `s - 1 = 1 / (a - 1)` in `C`, let no element of the ideal `I` be a unit in `D`, and let
`a (a - 1) ∈ I` or `ε + r ε ^ 2 = 1` be solvable in `A` for every `r ∈ I`. If `w ^ ℓ - 1 = ϖ G` in
`D` with `ϖ ∈ I`, then `w ^ ℓ = (1 + ϖ e) (1 + ϖ f)` in `D` for some `e ∈ B` and `f ∈ C`. 

### `exists_eq_basicOpen_of_isClopen_of_preimage_eq`

 Two charts of a line modulo `I`: clopens of `Spec (B ⧸ J)` and `Spec (C ⧸ L)` with the same
preimage in `Spec (D ⧸ K)` are the basic opens of one element of `A`. Here `D = B[1 / t] = C[1 / s]`
with `t * s = 1` in `D` (`hts`), `D` is integral over `A[t, s]` (`hD`), `A` maps onto the
fibre product `B ×_D C` (`hA_onto`), and every element of an extension of `I` is a multiple of
the image of one element of `I` (`hI`). The ideals `J`, `L`, `K` are the extensions of `I` to
`B`, `C`, `D`. 

### `exists_eq_locConstSpec_map_sub_map`

 Two charts of a line modulo `I`: every locally constant function `Spec (D ⧸ K) → ZMod ℓ` is the
difference of the pull-backs of locally constant functions on `Spec (B ⧸ J)` and `Spec (C ⧸ L)`.
Here `D = B[1 / t] = C[1 / s]` with `t * s = 1` in `D` (`hts`), `D` is integral over `A[t, s]`
(`hD`), `A` and `D` are domains, `A`, `B`, `C` are absolutely integrally closed, and the prime `ℓ`
is a unit of `A`. Either `A` is local (`IsAbsIntegrallyClosedLocal A`: absolutely integrally closed,
and if `a + b = 1` then `a` or `b` is a unit), or `B = A[1 / a]` with `1 - t = 1 / a` in `B`,
`s - 1 = 1 / (a - 1)` in `C`, and `a (a - 1) ∈ I` or `ε + r ε ^ 2 = 1` solvable in `A` for every
`r ∈ I` (`h_cases`, with `IsLineChartCase I a`). Every element of an extension of `I` is a multiple
of the image of one element of `I` (`hI`). The ideals `J`, `L`, `K` are the extensions of `I` to
`B`, `C`, `D`.
