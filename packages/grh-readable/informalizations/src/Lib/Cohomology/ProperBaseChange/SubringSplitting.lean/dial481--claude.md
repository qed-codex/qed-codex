# Splitting absolutely integrally closed subrings with a Mayer–Vietoris sequence

This file gives a splitting step in an induction over the absolutely integrally closed subrings of
an algebraically closed field `K` that contain the image of `γ : D →+* K`. A subring `B` of this
kind that is not a valuation ring is replaced by the integral closures of `B[t]` and `B[1 / t]`,
as in the cover of a projective line by two charts. These two larger rings fit into a short exact
Mayer–Vietoris sequence of locally constant `ZMod ℓ`-valued functions on the spectra modulo `J`.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.Variety.absIntegrallyClosedSubrings`: the absolutely integrally closed subrings of `K`
  containing the image of `γ`.
* `New.Variety.absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.quotientFunctor`: the functor `B ↦ B / J B` on these
  subrings.
* `New.Variety.absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.exists_isSplitting_isMayerVietorisExact`: the two larger
  rings and a short exact Mayer–Vietoris sequence of locally constant `ZMod ℓ`-valued functions on
  the spectra modulo `J`.

Notions from other files:
* `New.Variety.IsAbsIntegrallyClosed`: A is absolutely integrally closed: every monic polynomial
  over A of positive degree has a root in A.
* `New.ThePrime`: `ThePrime ℓ` fixes the prime `ℓ` (it extends `Fact ℓ.Prime`).
* `New.Variety.locConstSpec`: For an abelian group Λ, the functor on commutative rings sending A to
  the group of locally constant functions Spec A → Λ; it sends a ring map to pull-back along the map
  of spectra.
* `New.Variety.IsLineChartCase` (in `Lib.Cohomology.ProperBaseChange.LineCharts`): the condition on
  the chart `R[1 / a]` of a line modulo an ideal `I` of `R`.

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings`

 The absolutely integrally closed subrings of `K` that contain the image of `γ`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.toRingHom`

 For `B` in `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings γ`, the map `γ : D → K` with its values in `B`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.extendedIdeal`

 The ideal `J B` of `B` generated by the image of the ideal `J` of `D` under `γ`, which lands in
`B`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.extendedIdeal_eq_map_toRingHom`

 `J B` is the extension of `J` along `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.toRingHom B : D → B`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.quotientFunctor`

 The functor `B ↦ B / J B` on `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings γ`, ordered by inclusion. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.quotientFunctor_map_mk`

 On classes, the map of `quotientFunctor J γ` along `B ≤ B'` sends the class of `x ∈ B` to the
class of `x` in `B'`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.map_extendedIdeal`

 For `B ≤ B'` in `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings γ`, the ideal `J B` extends along the inclusion
`B → B'` to `J B'`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.exists_mem_extendedIdeal_dvd`

 Let divisibility in `D` be total (`PreValuationRing D`), and let `ψ : B → E` be a ring map. If
`x` lies in the extension of `J B` along `ψ`, then `ψ i` divides `x` for one `i ∈ J B`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.isUnit_natCast_of_isUnit`

 If `ℓ` is invertible in `D`, then it is invertible in every member of
`absIntegrallyClosedSubrings γ`. 

### `instance : Algebra S T`

 For subrings `S ≤ T` of `K` (the instance `Fact (S ≤ T)`), `T` is an `S`-algebra through the
inclusion. 

### `instance [Fact (T ≤ U)] [Fact (S ≤ U)] : IsScalarTower S T U`

 The inclusions of subrings `S ≤ T ≤ U` of `K` form a scalar tower. 

### `local instance [a : IsAbsIntegrallyClosed S] : IsIntegrallyClosedIn S K`

 An absolutely integrally closed subring of `K` is integrally closed in `K`. 

### `IsAbsIntegrallyClosed.isLocalization_away`

 Let `S ≤ T` be subrings of `K` with `S` absolutely integrally closed, and let `y ∈ T` be the
inverse of `x ∈ S`. If `T` lies in every absolutely integrally closed subring that contains `S` and
`y`, then `T` is the localisation of `S` away from `x`. Here `T` is an `S`-algebra through the
inclusion. 

### `_root_.Subring.isUnit_of_inv_mem`

 An element `a` of a subring `S` of a field `F` with `a ≠ 0` and `a⁻¹ ∈ S` is a unit of `S`. 

### `_root_.Subring.exists_notMem_and_inv_notMem_of_not_isUnit`

 Let `b` be an element of a subring `S` of a field `F` such that neither `b` nor `1 - b` is a
unit of `S`. Then `t = 1 - b⁻¹` satisfies `t ∉ S`, `t⁻¹ ∉ S` and `b * (1 - t) = 1`. 

### `IsAbsIntegrallyClosed.exists_notMem_and_inv_notMem`

 Let `K` be an algebraically closed field, `S` an absolutely integrally closed subring of `K`
that is not a valuation ring of `K`, and `I` an ideal of `S`. Then there is `t ∈ K` with `t ∉ S` and
`t⁻¹ ∉ S` such that either `1 - t` is the inverse of some `b ∈ S`, and `b * (b - 1) ∈ I` or the
equation `ε + r * ε ^ 2 = 1` has a solution `ε ∈ S` for every `r ∈ I` (`IsLineChartCase I b`); or
`S` is local (`IsAbsIntegrallyClosedLocal S`).

These are the two cases that `exists_eq_locConstSpec_map_sub_map` allows. 

### `integralClosureAdjoin`

 The integral closure in `K` of the subring `S[s]` generated by `S` and `s`. 

### `integralClosureAdjoin_le_iff`

 For a subring `U` of `K` that is integrally closed in `K`, the integral closure of `S[s]` lies
in `U` if and only if `S ≤ U` and `s ∈ U`. 

### `isAbsIntegrallyClosed_integralClosureAdjoin`

 The integral closure of `S[s]` in the algebraically closed field `K` is absolutely integrally
closed. 

### `le_integralClosureAdjoin`

 The integral closure of `S[s]` in `K` contains `S`. 

### `mem_integralClosureAdjoin`

 The integral closure of `S[s]` in `K` contains `s`. 

### `IsAbsIntegrallyClosed.isLocalization_away_integralClosureAdjoin`

 Let `S` be an absolutely integrally closed subring of `K`, and `t ∈ K` with `b * (1 - t) = 1`
for some `b ∈ S`. Then the integral closure of `S[t]` is the localisation of `S` away from `b`; it
is an `S`-algebra through the inclusion. 

### `isIntegral_adjoin_pair_of_le_sup`

 Let `S ≤ T` be subrings of `K`, so that `T` is an `S`-algebra, and let `s, t ∈ T`. If `T` lies
in the subring generated by the integral closures of `S[s]` and `S[t]`, then every `x ∈ T` is
integral over the `S`-subalgebra `S[s, t]` of `T`. 

### `mem_range_algebraMap_prod_of_inf_eq`

 Let `S ≤ S₁, S₂ ≤ T` be subrings of `K`, each an algebra over the smaller ones through the
inclusion, with `S₁ ⊓ S₂ = S`. If `x ∈ S₁` and `y ∈ S₂` have the same image in `T`, then `(x, y)` is
the image of one element of `S`. 

### `exists_locallyConstant_of_fibers`

 Let the images of `f₁ : X₁ → X` and `f₂ : X₂ → X` cover `X`. Locally constant functions `x` on
`X₁` and `y` on `X₂` are pull-backs of one locally constant function on `X` if, for each value
`c`, their fibres over `c` are the preimages of one open subset of `X`. 

### `IsMayerVietorisExact`

 Let `F` be a functor from a preorder to abelian groups, and `a ≤ b₁ ≤ c`, `a ≤ b₂ ≤ c`. The
property that the sequence `0 → F a → F b₁ × F b₂ → F c → 0` is exact, where the first map is the
pair of the maps induced by `F` and the second is their difference. 

### `IsMayerVietorisExact.eq_zero`

 Exactness on the left: an element that vanishes in `F b₁` and in `F b₂` is zero. 

### `IsMayerVietorisExact.exists_glue`

 Exactness in the middle: two elements with the same image in `F c` come from `F a`. 

### `IsMayerVietorisExact.exists_eq_sub`

 Exactness on the right: every element of `F c` is a difference. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.exists_valuationSubring_mem_range_comap`

 For every prime `p` of `B / J B` there is a valuation ring `V` of `K` containing `B` such that,
for every `B' ∈ absIntegrallyClosedSubrings γ` with `B ≤ B' ≤ V`, `p` is the contraction of a prime
of `B' / J B'`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.IsSplitting`

 `B` splits into the strictly larger rings `B₁` and `B₂`, which generate `B₃`: every valuation
ring of `K` over `B` contains one of the two. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.IsSplitting.lt_left`

 `B₁` is strictly larger than `B`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.IsSplitting.lt_right`

 `B₂` is strictly larger than `B`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.IsSplitting.eq_sup`

 `B₃` is the subring of `K` generated by `B₁` and `B₂`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.IsSplitting.le_or_le`

 A valuation ring of `K` that contains `B` contains `B₁` or `B₂`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.IsSplitting.inf_eq`

 If `B` splits into `B₁` and `B₂` (`IsSplitting`), then `B₁ ⊓ B₂ = B`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.IsSplitting.mem_range_comap_or`

 The spectra of `B₁ / J B₁` and `B₂ / J B₂` cover that of `B / J B`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.isSplitting_of_integralClosureAdjoin`

 Let `t ∈ K` with `t ∉ B` and `t⁻¹ ∉ B`. Let `B₁`, `B₂` be the integral closures of `B[t]` and
`B[t⁻¹]`, and `B₃` the subring that they generate. Then `B` splits into `B₁` and `B₂`, generating
`B₃`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.IsSplitting.eq_zero_of_map_eq_zero`

 Let `B` split into `B₁` and `B₂`, generating `B₃` (`IsSplitting`). A locally constant
`ZMod ℓ`-valued function on `Spec (B / J B)` that vanishes after pull-back to `Spec (B₁ / J B₁)` and
to `Spec (B₂ / J B₂)` is zero, because these two spectra cover `Spec (B / J B)`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.IsSplitting.exists_map_eq_and_map_eq`

 Let `B` split into `B₁` and `B₂`, generating `B₃` (`IsSplitting`). If compatible clopens of
`Spec (B₁ / J B₁)` and `Spec (B₂ / J B₂)` come from one open of `Spec (B / J B)` (`h_clopen`), then
locally constant `ZMod ℓ`-valued `x` on `Spec (B₁ / J B₁)` and `y` on `Spec (B₂ / J B₂)` with
the same pull-back to `Spec (B₃ / J B₃)` are the pull-backs of one such function on
`Spec (B / J B)`. 

### `absIntegrallyClosedSubrings.exists_isSplitting_isMayerVietorisExact`

 Let `K` be algebraically closed, `γ : D →+* K` a ring map, `J` an ideal of `D`, `ℓ` a prime
invertible in `D`, and divisibility in `D` total (`PreValuationRing D`). Let `B` be an absolutely
integrally closed subring of `K` containing the image of `γ` (`absIntegrallyClosedSubrings γ`) that
is not a valuation ring of `K`. Then there are such rings `B₁`, `B₂`, strictly larger than `B` and
generating `B₃`, with every valuation ring of `K` over `B` containing one of them (`IsSplitting`),
such that `0 → C B → C B₁ × C B₂ → C B₃ → 0` is exact. Here `C S` is the group of locally constant
functions `Spec (S / J S) → ZMod ℓ`, the first map is the pair of pull-backs and the second is their
difference.
