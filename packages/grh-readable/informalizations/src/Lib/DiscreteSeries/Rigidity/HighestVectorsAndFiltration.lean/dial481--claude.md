# K-highest vectors, norm bounds, Casimir growth on the filtration, and analytic vectors

The setting is a `gl_n`-module with an invariant inner product and a lower bound, depending on `l`,
for the Casimir operator (`New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe l ρ`). It is studied through the increasing
filtration `M_s = New.GlModule.filtration ρ ε x` generated by a `K`-highest vector `x`. Signs
`ε_a = ±1` define `𝔨`, spanned by the `E_ab` with `ε_a = ε_b`. `Ω_K = ρ.casimirK ε` is the Casimir
operator of `K`, and `c_K = New.HCParam.casimirKValue l` is its value on a highest vector. Take a
parameter with a large gap (`New.HCParam.HasLargeGap`) and `x` of weight `Λ`
(`New.HCParam.lowestKType`). Then the file shows that `Ω_K` grows along the filtration. With norm
bounds on the `M_s` it also shows that a bounded coefficient map is determined on `x` by its values
at `1` on the `M_s`.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.HCParam.exists_highest_vector`: a nonzero finite-dimensional 𝔨-stable subspace contains a
  highest weight vector.
* `New.HCParam.IsKHighest.finite_filtration`: for an invariant inner product, M_s has finite
  dimension.
* `New.HCParam.IsKHighest.norm_apply_le_of_mem_filtration`: a matrix `X` maps `M_s` into `M_{s+1}`
  (`New.GlModule.apply_mem_filtration_succ`), with an explicit bound for the norm of this action
  (`New.HCParam.IsKHighest.norm_apply_le_mul_norm`).
* `New.mem_orthogonal_filtration_of_casimirK`: an eigenvector of `Ω_K` of eigenvalue at most
  `c_K(Λ) + s` that is orthogonal to `M_s` is orthogonal to every `M_t`.
* `New.HCParam.IsKHighest.coeffMap_eq_zero`: let `l` be a parameter with a gap, and let `ρ` satisfy
  `New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe l ρ`. Then a bounded coefficient map that vanishes at `1` on the module
  generated by a `K`-highest vector `x` vanishes on `x` at every `g`.

Notions from other files:
* `New.HCParam.IsKHighest`: x has weight ν and is killed by the compact raising operators.
* `New.GlModule.filtration`: M_0 = U(𝔨)x and M_{s+1} = M_s + Σ E_ab M_s.
* `New.HCParam.casimirKValue`: the integer |ν + ρ_c|² − |ρ_c|², the value of the Casimir operator of
  𝔨.
* `New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe`: the inner product of the gl_n-module H satisfies ⟨E_ab v, w⟩ = ε_aε_b⟨v,
  E_ba w⟩, and Re⟨y, Ωy⟩ ≥ κ_λ‖y‖² for the full Casimir operator Ω, with
  κ_λ = `New.HCParam.casimirValue l`.
* `New.HCParam.HasGap`: the entries of l = 2λ are distinct, have the parity of n - 1, and those with
  different signs ε are at least twice the gap apart; `New.HCParam.HasLargeGap A` asks for a gap of
  at least `gapThreshold n A`, which puts such entries at least `1000 (card n)³` apart.

## Relation to the literature

Known result: Lowest K-type and Casimir value determine a discrete series coefficient, via analytic
vectors. Here the setting is U(p,q), and no irreducibility is assumed. Proved here are two parts: a
growth estimate for the compact Casimir operator along the filtration, for parameters with large
gaps, and a vanishing criterion for coefficient maps on a highest vector by explicit norm bounds in
place of analyticity.

Source: R. Parthasarathy, Classification of discrete series by minimal K-type, Represent. Theory 19
(2015) 167-185, Thm 1.1; earlier Hotta-Parthasarathy, Invent. Math. 26 (1974) 133-178; Schmid, Ann.
of Math. 102 (1975) 535-564; Vogan-Zuckerman, Compositio 53 (1984) Prop 6.1; Nelson, Analytic
vectors, Ann. of Math. 70 (1959) 572-615.



## Highest vectors, weights of words and finiteness of M_s

An integer vector `l` orders the indices, so that `E_ab` is raising if `l_b < l_a` and lowering if
`l_a < l_b`.


### `wordWeight`

 For a list of index pairs (a, b), the vector ν + Σ (e_a − e_b): the weight of the product of
the E_ab applied to a vector of weight ν. 

### `GlModule.E_self_E_apply_eq_smul`

 If E_cc acts on v by the scalar m, then it acts on E_ab v by m + (e_a − e_b)_c. 

### `HCParam.exists_highest_vector`

 A nonzero finite-dimensional subspace stable under 𝔨 contains a nonzero weight vector that is
killed by the compact raising operators. 

### `abs_wordWeight_sub_le`

 The coordinates of `wordWeight ν w` differ from those of ν by at most the length of w. 

### `HCParam.IsKHighest.E_self_word_eq_smul`

 Let x be a K-highest vector of weight ν. The word w, a product of operators E_ab, sends x to a
vector of weight `wordWeight ν w`. 

### `HCParam.IsKHighest.filtration_le_of_word_mem`

 Let l be a parameter of some gap (`New.HCParam.HasGap`) and x K-highest of weight ν. Let the
subspace T be stable under the compact lowering operators (E_ab with ε_a = ε_b and l_a < l_b) and
contain wx for all words w of length ≤ s in the E_ab. Then T contains the step M_s of
`New.GlModule.filtration`. 

### `HCParam.IsKHighest.finite_filtration`

 Assume `New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe l ρ` (invariant inner product, Casimir lower bound). Let `l` be a
parameter of some gap `g` (`|λ i - λ j| ≥ g` when `ε i ≠ ε j`, `l = 2λ`) and `x` a `K`-highest
vector of any weight `ν`. Then each `M_s = ρ.filtration ε x s` is finite-dimensional. Here `M_0` is
the least subspace containing `x` stable under the `E a b` with `ε a = ε b`, and
`M_{s+1} = M_s + Σ E a b M_s`. 


## Norm bounds for the action of gl_n on M_s

* `New.IsKInvariantForm`: the form `∑ ⟪J_i u, J_i v⟫` is given by an operator that commutes with
  `𝔨`.


### `IsKInvariantForm`

 A finite family of endomorphisms J_i of H has this property if Σ_i⟨J_i u, J_i v⟩ = ⟨u, Dv⟩ for
all u, v, for some operator D that commutes with every E_cd with ε_c = ε_d. 

### `IsKInvariantForm.mul_E`

 Under `New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe l ρ` (of which only the invariance of the inner product is used),
`New.IsKInvariantForm`, that is, `∑ ⟪J_i u, J_i v⟫ = ⟪u, D v⟫` with `D` commuting with `𝔨`, passes
from the family `J_i` to the family of all products `J_i E_qp`. 

### `IsKInvariantForm.sum_inner_casimirK`

 Assume `New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe l ρ`, which gives `E_ab` the adjoint `ε_a ε_b E_ba`, and let `J`
satisfy `New.IsKInvariantForm`: its form is given by an operator commuting with `𝔨`. Then the
compact Casimir operator `Ω_K = ∑ E_cd E_dc` splits between the two arguments of the form
`∑ ⟪J_i u, J_i v⟫`. 

### `HCParam.IsKHighest.casimirK_bound_of_highest`

 A bound for `Ω_K` on the highest vectors holds on all vectors. Let the inner product be
invariant under 𝔲(diag ε), with Casimir Ω ≥ κ_λ (`New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe l ρ`; only the invariance
is used). Let `l` be a parameter of some gap and `x` be `K`-highest of weight `ν`. Let `U ≤ M_s` be
finite-dimensional and stable under `𝔨`, where `M_0 = U(𝔨)x` and `M_{s+1} = M_s + ∑ E_ab M_s`. Let
`J` be a family with `∑ ⟪J_i u, J_i v⟫ = ⟪u, D v⟫` for some `D` commuting with `𝔨`
(`New.IsKInvariantForm`). Write `c_K(μ) = New.HCParam.casimirKValue l μ`. Suppose that
`β ≤ α c_K(μ)` for every word `w` of length at most `s` such that some `K`-highest `z ∈ U` of weight
`μ` is not orthogonal to `w x`, where `μ` is the weight of `w x`. Then
`β ∑ ‖J_i y‖² ≤ α Re ∑ ⟪J_i y, J_i Ω_K y⟫` for `y ∈ U`. 

### `GlModule.apply_mem_filtration_succ`

 Every matrix `X` maps step `M_s` of the filtration generated by `x` into step `M_{s+1}`. 

### `HCParam.IsKHighest.norm_apply_le_mul_norm`

 Let the inner product of `H` be invariant under 𝔲(diag ε) and satisfy Re⟨y, Ωy⟩ ≥ κ_λ‖y‖² for
all y, with Ω = `ρ.casimirK 1` and κ_λ = `l.casimirValue` (`New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe`). Let `l` be a
parameter of some gap `A`, and let `x` be a `K`-highest vector of weight `ν` with `|ν_a| ≤ L`. Let
`M_s` be the filtration generated by `x`, where `M_0` is the least subspace containing `x` stable
under the `E a b` with `ε a = ε b`, and `M_{s+1} = M_s + Σ_{a,b} E_ab M_s`; then for `y ∈ M_s` and
every matrix `X`, `‖ρ X y‖ ≤ (card n + 1)⁴ (L + 1 + s) ‖X‖ ‖y‖`, with `‖X‖` the `L²` operator
norm. 

### `HCParam.IsKHighest.norm_apply_le_of_mem_filtration`

 Let the inner product be invariant and satisfy the Casimir lower bound
(`New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe`), `l` be a parameter of some gap, and `x` a `K`-highest vector of weight
`ν` with `|ν_a| ≤ L`. A matrix `X` sends a vector `y` of step `M_s` of the filtration to a vector of
`M_{s+1}` of norm at most `(card n + 1)⁴ (L + 1 + s) ‖X‖ ‖y‖`, with `‖X‖` the `L²` operator norm. 


## Growth of Ω_K along the filtration

A large gap of the parameter (`New.HCParam.HasLargeGap`: the gap is at least `gapThreshold n A`) is
assumed where the growth is proved.

* `New.IsDetectingFamily`: a finite family of operators that kills `M_kᗮ`, detects step `k` of the
  filtration, and bounds `Ω_K` from below by `c_K(Λ) + 2 k`.


### `New.casimirKValue_lowestKType_add_le`

 Let `ρ` be a gl_n-module structure on the inner product space `H`, let `l` have gap
`500 n³ + A`, and let `Λ = l.lowestKType`. Let the inner product be `𝔲(diag ε)`-invariant with
Casimir operator `ρ.casimirK 1 ≥ l.casimirValue`, and let `w` be a word of length at most one.
Suppose a `K`-highest vector `z` of weight `wordWeight Λ w` (`Λ` plus `Σ (e_a − e_b)` over `w`) is
orthogonal to step 0, `U(𝔨) x`, of the filtration generated by `x ∈ H`, but not to `ρ.word w x`.
Then the value `casimirKValue` of the compact Casimir operator at the weight of `z` exceeds its
value at `Λ` by at least `4 card n + 2`. 

### `New.map_mem_orthogonal_filtration`

 Under `New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe l ρ` (only the invariance of the inner product is used), every
operator `ρ X` maps the orthogonal complement of step `k + 1` of the filtration into that of step
`k`. 

### `New.sum_norm_sq_le_sum_re_inner_lie`

 Let `l` have a large gap, the inner product be invariant with the lower bound for the Casimir
operator (`New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe l ρ`), and `x` be `K`-highest of weight
`Λ = l.lowestKType`. Let `P` commute with the compact `ρ.E c d`, have kernel `M_0ᗮ`, where
`M_0 = ρ.filtration ε x 0`, and satisfy `IsKInvariantForm` (that is, `⟪P u, P v⟫ = ⟪u, D v⟫` for an
operator `D` that commutes with the compact `ρ.E c d`). Suppose that the form of `Ω_K` at every
`P a` is `casimirKValue Λ * ‖P a‖²`. Then for `a ∈ M_0ᗮ` the sum of the `‖P (ρ.E q p a)‖²` over all
`(p, q)` is at most the sum of the real parts of the cross terms of `ρ.E d c (P (ρ.E q p a))` with
`P (⁅ρ.E q p, ρ.E d c⁆ a)`, over the compact pairs `(c, d)` and all `(p, q)`; both have a factor
2 in the statement. 

### `New.IsDetectingFamily`

 A finite family `J` of operators detects step `k` of the filtration
`M_s = ρ.filtration ε x s`. Write `c_K(Λ)` for `l.casimirKValue l.lowestKType`. 

### `New.IsDetectingFamily.isKInvariantForm`

 The form `∑ ⟪J_i u, J_i v⟫` is given by an operator that commutes with `𝔨`. 

### `New.IsDetectingFamily.apply_eq_zero`

 Every `J_i` kills the orthogonal complement of `M_k`. 

### `New.IsDetectingFamily.cross_term_bound`

 On the orthogonal complement of `M_k`, the cross term between `J_i` and one further factor
  `E_qp` is at least `∑ ‖J_i E_qp a‖²`. 

### `New.IsDetectingFamily.mem_orthogonal`

 A vector orthogonal to the earlier steps and killed by every `J_i` is orthogonal to `M_k`. 

### `New.IsDetectingFamily.casimirK_bound`

 On vectors orthogonal to the earlier steps, `c_K(Λ) + 2 k` bounds the form of `J` at `Ω_K`
  from below, even after the squares of the commutator terms are subtracted. 

### `New.IsDetectingFamily.mul_E`

 Assume the inner product on `H` is invariant under 𝔲(diag ε) and the Casimir operator
`ρ.casimirK 1` is bounded below by `l.casimirValue` (`New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe l ρ`). If a finite
family `J i` detects step `k` of the filtration `ρ.filtration ε x` generated by `x`, in the sense of
`New.IsDetectingFamily`, then the family of all products `J i * ρ.E q p`, indexed by `(p, q, i)`,
detects step `k + 1`. 

### `New.exists_detecting_family`

 Let `l` be a parameter for the signs `ε` with gap `500 n³ + A` (`l.HasLargeGap A`). Assume the
inner product is invariant under 𝔲(diag ε) and the Casimir operator is bounded below by
`l.casimirValue` (`New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe l ρ`), and let `x` be `K`-highest of weight
`Λ = l.lowestKType`. Then for each `k` some finite family of operators on `H` detects step `k` of
the filtration `ρ.filtration ε x` generated by `x`, in the sense of `New.IsDetectingFamily`. 

### `New.mem_orthogonal_filtration_of_casimirK`

 `Ω_K` grows along the filtration `M_s = New.GlModule.filtration`. Let `l` have a large gap
(`New.HCParam.HasLargeGap`), the inner product be invariant with the lower bound for the Casimir
operator, and `x` be `K`-highest of weight `Λ`. An eigenvector `a` of `Ω_K` with real eigenvalue
`θ ≤ c_K(Λ) + s` that is orthogonal to `M_s` is orthogonal to every `M_t`. 


## Analytic vectors and adjoints for invariant forms

The vanishing of coefficient maps on highest vectors, and links between a representation `ϖ` of
`New.USign ε`, the unitary group of `diag ε`, and the `gl n`-module `ρ` that is its derivative
along `ι` (`New.IsDerivedAction`).

* `New.IsDerivedAction.isCoeffMap`: `g ↦ φ ∘ ϖ g ∘ ι` is a coefficient map for each continuous
  linear `φ`.


### `HCParam.IsKHighest.coeffMap_eq_zero`

 A coefficient map that vanishes at `1` on the filtration of a `K`-highest vector vanishes on
that vector everywhere. Let `x` be `K`-highest of weight `ν` in a `gl n`-module whose inner product
is invariant under `𝔲(ε)` and satisfies the Casimir lower bound for the parameter `l` of gap `gap`
(`New.IsUnitaryCasimirGe l ρ`). Let `c` be a scalar coefficient map (the derivative of
`t ↦ c (g exp tY) v` is `c (g exp tY) (ρ Y v)`) with `‖c g v‖ ≤ D ‖v‖`. If `c 1` vanishes on every
step of the filtration generated by `x`, then `c g x = 0` for all `g`. 

### `IsDerivedAction.isCoeffMap`

 If `ρ` is the derivative of `ϖ` along `ι` (at `1`), then `v ↦ φ (ϖ g (ι v))` is a coefficient
map for `ρ` (at every point of the group), for each continuous linear map `φ`. 

### `IsUnitaryRep.exists_innerProductSpace`

 The invariant form `B` of a unitary representation `ϖ` (`New.IsUnitaryRep A ϖ B`: `B` is
conjugate symmetric, positive definite and `ϖ`-invariant; `A` is not used here) is the inner product
of an inner product space `H`, in the universe of `X` and linearly isomorphic to `X`, and the group
acts by isometries of its norm. `H` need not be complete, and `j` need not be a homeomorphism. Here
`type_of% X` is the type universe in which `X` lives. 

### `IsUnitaryRep.form_apply_eq_form_inv_apply`

 For a form `B` invariant under `ϖ` (`New.IsUnitaryRep A ϖ B`; only the invariance is used), the
adjoint of `ϖ g` is `ϖ g⁻¹`. 

### `IsDerivedAction.form_E_eq_sign_mul`

 For the invariant form `B` (`New.IsUnitaryRep A ϖ B`: conjugate symmetric, positive definite,
`ϖ`-invariant) of a representation `ϖ` with derivative `ρ` along `ι`, the adjoint of `E a b` is
`ε a ε b E b a`.
