# The real unitary group G₁, unipotent coordinates and Bessel functions

The form `(𝔰.middleGram N).splitGram` on the space Y₀ of column vectors indexed by `𝔰.BesselIndex`
is the sum of ϱ hyperbolic planes, the identity form on X₀ = A^κ and a line of norm −N. Its
archimedean version is the complex form `New.Shape.J₁`, whose space has basis `e_a`, `x_i`, `z`,
`e*_a` with `h(z, z) = -1` for the hermitian form `h`, and frame `e_0, …, e_{ϱ-1}, z`. The file
embeds the unitary group of X₀ in U(Y₀) and in `G₁`. It gives explicit elements of the unipotent
groups 𝖴⁰ = `New.Shape.Form.besselU0` and 𝖴 = `New.Shape.Form.besselU`, and measures on them. It
then defines Bessel functions on `G₁`.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.exists_besselU_mul_toBesselH`: for N a unit, a coefficient ring with an element of trace one
  and an invertible hermitian form, every element of `besselS` is a product `u h` with `u` in
  `besselU` and `h` in the image of `New.toBesselH`.
* `New.Shape.G₁`, `New.Shape.J₁`: the group and its form.
* `New.RealPlace.unipotentOf`, `New.RealPlace.mem_besselU0_iff_unipotentOf`: a parametrization of
  the unipotent group `New.Shape.Form.besselU0` that fixes the frame.
* `New.RealPlace.embedH`: the unitary group of the `x_i` inside `G₁`.
* `New.Shape.besselHaar₀`, `New.Shape.besselHaar`: measures on 𝖴⁰ and 𝖴, pushed forward from
  Lebesgue measure on the parameters and, for 𝖴, from its product with the measure on 𝖴⁰.
* `New.IsBesselFunction`: bounded measurable functions that transform by a character under the
  unipotent group `New.Shape.Form.besselU` and by `rep` under `U(κ)`.

Notions from other files:
* `New.Shape.Form.splitGram T`: the Gram matrix of ϱ hyperbolic planes and a middle space with Gram
  matrix T. Identity blocks pair the first and last ϱ indices, T is the middle block, and all other
  entries are zero.
* `New.inclMatrix`: for a map u : m → n of index sets, the n×m matrix whose j-th column is the
  standard basis vector e_{u(j)}.
* `New.frameMatrix`: the square submatrix on the rows and columns of the frame.
* `New.Shape.Form.besselS`: the Bessel subgroup, generated by the unipotent group 𝖴 =
  `New.Shape.Form.besselU` and the fixer H = `New.Shape.Form.besselH` of the basis vectors that
  are not indexed by κ.

## Relation to the literature

Known; see `Lib.DiscreteSeries.Bruhat.FloorGroups`, `Lib.DiscreteSeries.Bessel.UnipotentHaarMeasure`
and `Lib.DiscreteSeries.Bruhat.ComplexPlace` for sources.



## The embedding of U(X₀) in U(Y₀) and its image H

The form `(𝔰.middleGram N).splitGram` on Y₀ is ϱ hyperbolic planes plus the identity form on
X₀ = A^κ plus a line of norm −N. H is the group `New.Shape.Form.besselH` of isometries that fix the
basis vectors not indexed by κ.

* `New.toBesselH`: the extension of a unitary κ × κ matrix by the identity.


### `toBesselH`

 The embedding of the unitary group of the identity form on X₀ = A^κ into the unitary group
`(𝔰.middleGram N).G`, acting on X₀ and fixing the other basis vectors. 

### `exists_eq_toBesselH`

 Let `(𝔰.middleGram N).G` be the unitary group of the form made of ϱ hyperbolic planes, the
identity form on X₀ = A^κ and a line of norm −N. If N is a unit, every element of
H = `New.Shape.Form.besselH`, the fixer of the basis vectors outside X₀, is the extension
`New.toBesselH` of a unitary κ × κ matrix by the identity. 

### `exists_besselU_mul_toBesselH`

 Assume that A has a chosen element of trace one, that the form `(𝔰.middleGram N).splitGram` (ϱ
hyperbolic planes, the identity form on X₀ = A^κ and a line of norm −N) is hermitian and invertible,
and that N is a unit. In its unitary group, the Bessel subgroup `besselS` is generated by the
unipotent group `besselU` and by the fixer of the basis vectors outside X₀. Every element of it is
a product `u * New.toBesselH 𝔰 N g`, with `u` in `besselU` and `g` a unitary κ × κ matrix, which
`New.toBesselH` extends by the identity. 

### `exists_mem_besselU_frameMatrix_eq_one_add_row`

 Assume that A has a chosen element of trace one, and that N is a unit. Let `𝔰.middleGram N` be
the diagonal matrix with entries 1 on κ and −N at the extra point, and assume its split form
`(𝔰.middleGram N).splitGram` is hermitian and invertible; this split form is the Gram matrix of ϱ
hyperbolic planes plus that middle block. For `a < ϱ` and a row `r` that vanishes at the columns
`c ≤ a`, some `u` in the Bessel unipotent group `besselU` has `frameMatrix u` equal to the identity
matrix plus `r` in row `a`; here `besselU` consists of the isometries of the split form that are
block upper unitriangular for the Bessel levels, and `frameMatrix u` is the submatrix of `u` on the
frame (e_0, …, e_{ϱ-1}, z). 

### `exists_mem_besselU_frameMatrix_eq`

 Make three assumptions. First, `A` is a commutative ring with involution and a chosen `τ` with
`τ + τ̄ = 1`. Second, the form `(𝔰.middleGram N).splitGram` is hermitian and invertible; this form
consists of ϱ hyperbolic planes, the identity form on X₀ = A^κ and a line of norm −N. Third, `N` is
a unit. Then every upper unitriangular matrix `M` of size ϱ + 1 equals `frameMatrix u` for some `u`
in `besselU`. Here `besselU` is the group of isometries of this form that are block upper
unitriangular for the levels `New.BesselIndex.level`, so their middle diagonal block is the
identity. `frameMatrix u` is the square submatrix of `u` on the rows and columns of the frame
(e_0, …, e_{ϱ-1}, z). 


## The real group G₁, frame and column matrices, free-entry coordinates

Definitions for the archimedean group `New.Shape.G₁`, the unitary group of the complex form
`New.Shape.J₁`. Its basis is `e_a`, `x_i`, `z`, `e*_a`, indexed by `New.Shape.BesselIndex`, also
written `SplitIndex 𝔰.ϱ 𝔰.MiddleIndex` or `Fin 𝔰.ϱ ⊕ 𝔰.MiddleIndex ⊕ Fin 𝔰.ϱ`, as `inl a`,
`inr (inl (inl i))`, `inr (inl (inr ()))`, `inr (inr a)`. The frame `New.Shape.besselFrame` is
`(f_0, …, f_ϱ) = (e_0, …, e_{ϱ-1}, z)`.


 ### The group 

### `G₁`

 The unitary group of the complex hermitian form made of ϱ hyperbolic planes, the standard
positive form on κ, and one vector z with h(z, z) = −1. 

### `FrameMatrix`

 The space of (ϱ+1)×(ϱ+1) complex matrices. 

 ### The form, the column matrices of the basis vectors, parameters of unipotents 

### `J₁`

 The Gram matrix of the hermitian form defining G₁: ϱ hyperbolic pairs e_a, e*_a, the
identity on X₀, and h(z, z) = −1. 

### `frameCols`

 The matrix whose ϱ+1 columns are the basis vectors of the frame (e_0, …, e_{ϱ-1}, z). 

### `dualFrameCols`

 The matrix with ϱ+1 columns, the basis vectors e*_0, …, e*_{ϱ-1} and x_{k₀}, where k₀ = `𝔰.k₀`
is the distinguished index in κ. 

### `isotropicCols`

 The matrix with ϱ columns, the basis vectors e_0, …, e_{ϱ-1}. 

### `definiteCols`

 The matrix whose columns, indexed by κ, are the basis vectors x_i of X₀. 

### `dualCols`

 The matrix with ϱ columns, the basis vectors e*_0, …, e*_{ϱ-1}. 

### `zCol`

 The one-column matrix whose column is the basis vector z. 

### `UnipotentParams`

 The space of pairs consisting of a complex ϱ×κ matrix and a real ϱ×ϱ matrix. 

### `frameVector`

 For y in G₁, the a-th vector y⁻¹f_a of the frame moved by y⁻¹, as a vector of the standard
Euclidean space. 

### `realFrameGram`

 For y∈G₁, the (ϱ+1)×(ϱ+1) complex matrix Z(y) of products h(f_j, y f_l) of the frame with
its translate. 

### `freeEntries`

 Coordinates on matrices, as a continuous `ℝ`-linear map. For a set `p` of positions `(a, i)`,
a matrix `M` goes to its complex entries in row `e_a` and column `i` for `(a, i)` in `p`,
together with the imaginary parts of its entries in row `e_a` and column `e*_a`. 

### `IsFreeEntry₀`

 The positions (a, i), meaning row `e_a` and column i, of the complex coordinates of the
frame-fixing unipotent group. The predicate is false for the columns `e_b` and `z` of the frame. It
is true for the columns indexed by `κ`. For the column `e*_b` it says `a < b`. It differs from
`New.RealPlace.IsFreeEntry` by leaving out the columns of the frame. 

### `IsFreeEntry`

 The positions (a, i) with row e_a and column e_b or e*_b for b > a, or any column of the middle
block. 

### `actOnCols`

 The matrix 1 − CCᴴ + ChCᴴ; for orthonormal columns C it acts by h on their span and fixes
the orthogonal complement. 

 ### Upper unitriangular matrices, and properties of `J₁` 

### `UpperEntries`

 The space of complex families indexed by the pairs a < b among j indices: the entries of a
j×j matrix strictly above the diagonal. 

### `unitriangularMatrix`

 The upper unitriangular j×j complex matrix whose entries above the diagonal are the given
family x. 

### `isHermitianInvolution_J₁`

 The Gram matrix `New.Shape.J₁` is a hermitian involution. 

### `instance : Floors.IsHermitianUnit 𝔰.J₁`

 The Gram matrix `New.Shape.J₁` is hermitian and invertible. 


## Explicit elements of 𝖴⁰, H and 𝖴 in G₁

Explicit matrices for subgroups of the unitary group `New.Shape.G₁` of the form `New.Shape.J₁`, with
basis `e_a`, `x_i`, `z`, `e*_a`.

* `New.RealPlace.liftUnitriangular`: an element of `New.Shape.Form.besselU` with a given
  unitriangular block on the frame.


 ### Matrices of basis columns 

### `inclMatrix_gram_eq_zero`

 Two families of basis columns with no common index are orthogonal. 

### `inclMatrix_gram_eq_one`

 The basis columns selected by an injective map are orthonormal. 

### `mul_mul_eq_of_mul_eq`

 A known product `A * B = C` can be rewritten inside the right-associated product
`A * (B * M)`. 

### `inclMatrix_gram_mul_eq_zero`

 A product with basis columns `e` is orthogonal to basis columns `e'` with no common index. 

### `inclMatrix_gram_mul_eq_self`

 If the index map `e` is injective, the conjugate transpose of `inclMatrix ℂ e`, the matrix whose
`j`-th column is the standard basis vector at `e j`, cancels it on the left. 

### `norm_inclMatrix_le_one`

 The inclusion matrix of an injective map of indices has operator norm at most one. 

### `J₁_eq`

 The Gram matrix in terms of basis columns: `e_a` pairs with `e*_a`, the `x_i` are orthonormal
and `z` has square `-1`. 

 ### The explicit elements 

### `unipotentOf`

 For q = (a, b) with a complex ϱ×κ and b real ϱ×ϱ, the isometry 1 + XaWᴴ − WaᴴDᴴ + X(sk
b − ½aaᴴ)Dᴴ. Here X, W, D have columns e_i, x_i, e*_i, and sk is `New.skewOfReal`, which makes a
skew-hermitian matrix from a real one. 

### `embedH`

 For h in the unitary group U(κ), the element of G₁ acting as h on X₀ and as the identity on
the other basis vectors. 

### `norm_embedH_mul`

 Left multiplication by `embedH h`, the unitary matrix that acts by h on the span of the `x_i`
and fixes the other basis vectors, preserves the L² operator norm. 

### `liftUnitriangular`

 The lift to G₁ of the upper unitriangular matrix `New.RealPlace.unitriangularMatrix` x of size
ϱ+1: an explicit isometry acting by this matrix on the frame e_0, …, e_{ϱ-1}, z and fixing X₀. 

 ### Membership in the subgroups 

### `coe_embedH`

 `embedH h` acts as `h` on the span of the `x_i` and as the identity on its orthogonal
complement. 

### `unipotentOf_mem_besselU0`

 Each explicit isometry `unipotentOf q` lies in 𝖴⁰ = `besselU0`: it is block upper unitriangular
for the levels `New.BesselIndex.level` and fixes the frame e_0, …, e_{ϱ-1}, z. 

### `mul_embedH_mem_besselFixer`

 For `u` in 𝖴⁰ and `h` in U(κ), the product `u * embedH h` lies in the fixer of the frame. 

### `frameMatrix_liftUnitriangular`

 The block of `liftUnitriangular x` on the rows and columns of the frame (e_0, …, e_{ϱ-1}, z) is
`unitriangularMatrix x`, the upper unitriangular matrix with entries x above the diagonal. 

### `liftUnitriangular_mem_besselU`

 For a family x of entries above the diagonal of a matrix of size `𝔰.n` = ϱ+1, the isometry
`liftUnitriangular x`, which acts on the frame by the unitriangular matrix with entries x, lies in
𝖴 = `besselU`: it is block upper unitriangular for the levels `New.BesselIndex.level`. 

### `exists_mul_unipotentOf_dualCols_eq`

 Let s be in `G₁`, let h be the form `J₁`, and let the ϱ columns B_b of B span an isotropic space
and be dual to the frame (`frameCols`) moved by s: h(s e_a, B_b) = δ_ab and h(s z, B_b) = 0, which
are the entries of `inclMatrix ℂ castSucc`. Then B = s u (e*_0, …, e*_{ϱ-1}) for some
u = `New.RealPlace.unipotentOf` q in the frame-fixing unipotent group 𝖴⁰. 

### `exists_embedH_eq_of_fix_frameCols`

 If `s` in `G₁` fixes the frame e_0, …, e_{ϱ-1}, z (the columns of `frameCols`), then
`s * unipotentOf q = embedH h` for some `q` and some `h` in U(κ): after a correction by an element
of the frame-fixing unipotent group 𝖴⁰, `s` only acts on the span of the `x_i`. 

### `embedH_eq_one_of_mem_besselU0`

 If `embedH h`, which acts by the unitary matrix h on the span of the `x_i`, lies in the
frame-fixing unipotent group 𝖴⁰ = `besselU0`, then it is trivial. 

### `mem_besselU0_iff_unipotentOf`

 The group 𝖴⁰ = `besselU0` of block unitriangular elements of `G₁` that fix the frame consists
exactly of the explicit isometries `unipotentOf q`. 

### `_root_.New.Shape.besselHaar₀`

 The measure on the frame-fixing unipotent group 𝖴⁰ of G₁. It is the pushforward of Lebesgue
measure on the parameters q under q ↦ `unipotentOf q`. 

### `_root_.New.Shape.besselHaar`

 The measure on the unipotent group 𝖴 of G₁. It is the pushforward of the product of Lebesgue
measure on `UpperEntries 𝔰.n` (the entries above the diagonal of a matrix of size 𝔰.n = ϱ+1) with
`New.Shape.besselHaar₀`, under (x, u) ↦ u · `liftUnitriangular x`. 

### `_root_.New.besselQuotMeasure`

 The measure on G₁ that computes integrals over 𝖴\G₁: Haar measure weighted by a cutoff for the
measure `New.Shape.besselHaar` on 𝖴. 


## Bessel functions on G₁ and right convolution

* `New.convolve`: the convolution of a vector-valued function with a complex function on a group.


### `convolve`

 The convolution `y ↦ ∫ x (a⁻¹) F (y a) da` of a vector-valued function `F` with a complex
function `x` on `G`. 

### `IsBesselFunction`

 A Bessel function on `G₁` with values in `ℂ^I` is a bounded, strongly measurable `F` with two
transformation laws. For `n` in the unipotent group `New.Shape.Form.besselU`, `F (n y) = ψ(n) F y`.
For `h` in `U(κ)` = `U (1 : Matrix 𝔰.κ 𝔰.κ ℂ)` (written `U 1` below), embedded by
`New.RealPlace.embedH`, `F (h y) = rep h (F y)`.

Here `ψ(n)` is `New.wave` of the sum of the entries just above the diagonal of `n` on the frame
`New.Shape.besselFrame`. 

### `IsBesselFunction.map_unipotent_mul`

 `F` transforms by the fixed character `n ↦ wave (simpleRootSum n)` under the unipotent
  group. 

### `IsBesselFunction.map_unitary_mul`

 `F` transforms by `rep` under `U(κ)`. 

### `DiscreteSeries.besselValueRep`

 The unitary matrix representation `rep` of `U(κ)`, acting by continuous linear operators on
Euclidean space.
