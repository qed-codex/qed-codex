# Isogenies: factorisation of homomorphisms, finiteness and flatness

Two facts on isogenies. Over an Artinian ring, a homomorphism that kills the kernel of an isogeny
factors through it. Over a local ring, a quasi-finite surjective homomorphism of abelian schemes is
an isogeny.

* `New.Variety.exists_isAdditive_comp_eq_of_isIsogeny`: over an Artinian ring, a homomorphism that
  kills the kernel of an isogeny `f`, tested on finite local algebras, factors through `f`.
* `New.Variety.isIsogeny_of_locallyQuasiFinite`: over a local ring, a locally quasi-finite
  surjective homomorphism of abelian schemes is finite and flat.

Notions from other files:
* `New.specOver`: The spectrum of an R-algebra B, as a scheme over Spec R.
* `New.IsAbelianScheme`: A commutative group scheme that is smooth and proper over the base, and on
  which multiplication by every m ≠ 0 is surjective.
* `New.IsIsogeny`: A homomorphism that is finite, flat and surjective.

## Relation to the literature

Known result: Factorisation through an isogeny, and quasi-finite surjective homomorphisms of abelian
schemes are finite flat. Here an abelian scheme means a smooth and proper commutative group scheme
on which multiplication by every m ≠ 0 is surjective; the base is an Artinian ring for the first
result and a local ring for the second. Source: B. Edixhoven, G. van der Geer, B. Moonen, Abelian
Varieties (draft), ch. IV §3; The Stacks project, Section 0BF9; EGA IV and S. Bosch, W.
Lütkebohmert, M. Raynaud, Néron Models (1990) 7.3, 8.1.


### `Ideal.map_maximalIdeal_eq_maximalIdeal_of_mem_minimalPrimes`

 Let R be a local ring with maximal ideal 𝔪, and L an R-algebra such that L ⧸ 𝔪L is reduced.
For a minimal prime q over 𝔪L, the ideal 𝔪 generates the maximal ideal of the localisation of L
at q. 

### `rTensor_subtype_maximalIdeal_injective_of_map_eq_of_flat`

 Let D be a local R-algebra whose maximal ideal 𝔪_D is generated by the maximal ideal 𝔪_R of R,
and let P be a D-algebra that is flat over R. Then 𝔪_D ⊗[D] P → P is injective. 

### `exists_isAdditive_comp_eq_of_isIsogeny`

 Let `A` be a commutative group scheme and `B`, `C` abelian schemes over an Artinian ring `k`.
Let `f : A ⟶ B` be an isogeny and `g : A ⟶ C` a homomorphism that kills every point of the
kernel of `f` with values in a finite local Noetherian `k`-algebra `S`. Then `g` factors through `f`
by a homomorphism. 

### `fst_comp_add_snd_comp_left_mem`

 For a commutative group scheme B over S, with x : T → B and y : Y → B, the sum map T × Y → B,
`(a, t) ↦ x a + y t`, inherits from `x` and from the structure map of `Y` every multiplicative
property that is stable under base change. 

### `isPullback_fst_comp_add`

 Translation by the first factor identifies the pull-back of a homomorphism f : A → B along
v : A ⊗ Z → B with its pull-back along the map m (a, z) = f a + v (a, z). 

### `flat_descendsAlong_fpqc`

 Flatness of morphisms of schemes descends along fpqc covers, that is, along surjective, flat
and quasi-compact morphisms. 

### `flat_left_of_isPullback_flat`

 Let f : A → B be a surjective, quasi-compact homomorphism of abelian schemes over Spec R, and
u : Spec D → B a flat map over Spec R, where D is an R-algebra and Spec D → Spec R is surjective.
If the pull-back g : X → Spec D of f along u is flat, then f is flat, by descent along the fpqc
cover m (a, d) = f a + u d of B. 

### `flat_of_isPullback_of_map_maximalIdeal_eq`

 Let f : A → B be a finite morphism of abelian schemes over the local ring R, and u : Spec D → B
a flat map, where D is a local R-algebra whose maximal ideal is generated by that of R. If
Spec P → Spec D, given by φ : D → P, is the pull-back of f along u, then φ is flat. 

### `isIsogeny_of_locallyQuasiFinite`

 A locally quasi-finite surjective homomorphism of abelian schemes over a local ring is finite
and flat, that is, an isogeny.
