# Principal ideal powers, S-units and a class number bound

For every ideal `I` of a ring of integers of a number field some power `I ^ n` with `n ≠ 0` is
principal. For ideals of norm greater than 1 the generators can be chosen large at every complex
embedding.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.ThetaTori.exists_pow_eq_span_inv_lt`: for an ideal of norm greater than 1, some power is
  principal with a non-unit generator of arbitrarily large absolute value at every complex
  embedding.
* `New.SUnit.dirichlet_unit_theorem`: for a finite set `S` of finite places of a number field `K`,
  the `S`-units are finitely generated of rank `#S + #(infinite places) - 1`. This follows from
  Mathlib's unit theorem and the finiteness of the class number, in the form
  `New.exists_valuation_eq_exp_mul`. Valuations are multiplicative, with values in `ℤᵐ⁰`, and a
  uniformiser has valuation `exp (-1)`.
* `New.eq_zero_of_forall_norm_lt_one`: an algebraic integer of absolute value below `1` at every
  infinite place is `0`. Here `Φ v`, from an instance of `New.PlaceEmbedding`, is a chosen complex
  embedding that induces the infinite place `v`.
* `New.classNumber_le`: a number field of degree `n` and discriminant `d` has class number at most
  `(2 ^ n * |d|) ^ (n + 1)`.

Minkowski's bound is at most `2 ^ n * |d|` (`New.minkowski_bound_le_two_pow_mul_discr`), so each
class has an integral ideal of norm `k ≤ 2 ^ n * |d|` as a member. Such an ideal is generated by
`k` and one more element, and the proof counts the possible pairs.

* `New.conj_mem_quadInt`: for a prime `d ≡ 7` mod `8`, the ring `New.quadInt d`, the image of the
  ring of integers of ℚ(√−d) in ℂ, is stable under complex conjugation.

## Relation to the literature

Dirichlet's `S`-unit theorem is deduced from the unit theorem in Mathlib, and the class number bound
is a crude explicit form of the finiteness of the class number, deduced from Minkowski's bound in
Mathlib.


 ## Principal powers of ideals of a ring of integers 

### `New.exists_ideal_pow_eq_span_singleton`

 For every ideal `I` of the ring of integers of a number field, some power `I ^ n` with
`n ≠ 0` is principal. 

 ## Generators of ideal powers large at every complex embedding 

### `New.ThetaTori.exists_pow_eq_span_inv_lt`

 If `I` is an ideal of norm greater than 1 in the ring of integers of a number field `K` and
`r > 0`, some power of `I` is generated by a non-unit `a` with `‖σ a‖⁻¹ < r` for every complex
embedding `σ` of `K`. 


## The ring of integers of an imaginary quadratic field in ℂ

The ring `New.quadInt d` (the integers of K = ℚ(√−d), inside ℂ) contains
ω = `New.quadGen d` = (1 + i√d)/2 and is stable under complex conjugation; ω + ω̄ = 1. For
membership in `quadInt d`, d is assumed to be a prime ≡ 7 mod 8 (`New.PrimeSevenModEight`).


### `CMLattice.quadGen_mem_quadInt`

 For a prime d ≡ 7 mod 8, ω = (1 + i√d)/2 lies in the image of the ring of integers of ℚ(√−d)
in ℂ: it is the image of the algebraic integer `New.QuadField.betaK d` of ℚ(√−d). 

### `conj_mem_quadInt`

 For a prime d ≡ 7 mod 8 (`PrimeSevenModEight d`), the image `quadInt d` in ℂ of the ring of
integers of ℚ(√−d) is stable under complex conjugation. 

 ## Dirichlet's S-unit theorem 

### `exists_valuation_eq_exp_mul`

 Let `S` be a finite set of finite places of `K`. There is `n ≠ 0` such that for every family of
integers `e v`, `v ∈ S`, some `S`-unit has valuation `exp (n * e v)` at each `v ∈ S`. 

### `SUnit.dirichlet_unit_theorem`

 Dirichlet's `S`-unit theorem: for a finite set `S` of finite places (nonzero primes of `𝓞 K`),
the group of `S`-units is finitely generated, and its rank plus one is `#S` plus the number of
infinite places. 

 ## Nonzero algebraic integers are large at some infinite place 

### `New.eq_zero_of_forall_norm_lt_one`

 Let `x` be an algebraic integer in a number field `E`, each infinite place `v` of which comes
with a chosen complex embedding `Φ v` inducing it (`PlaceEmbedding E`). If `‖Φ v x‖ < 1` for every
infinite place `v`, then `x = 0`. 

 ## A crude upper bound for the class number 

### `New.minkowski_bound_le_two_pow_mul_discr`

 For a number field of degree `n` and discriminant `d`, Minkowski's bound on the norm of an ideal
in a given class is at most `2 ^ n * |d|`. 

### `New.classNumber_le`

 A number field of degree `n` and discriminant `d` has class number at most
`(2 ^ n * |d|) ^ (n + 1)`.

This is a crude consequence of Minkowski's bound on the smallest norm of an integral ideal in each
ideal class.
