# Holomorphic data, functions of a level, and theta kernels modulo 𝔪 as level multipliers

This file sets up vocabulary for holomorphic automorphic functions on a unitary group over the CM
field E. It covers holomorphic data in the Fock space, adelic holomorphy, rapidly decreasing
functions of a given level, and additive characters of matrices modulo 𝔪. It also proves that
multiplication by a theta kernel `New.modKernel` preserves level N. Here the kernel's finite datum
is a function on matrices modulo 𝔪, and 𝔪 is generated by N. This needs the character `b` to have a
tame part that is trivial off the places of 𝔪.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.Rows.holomorphicData`: Fock vectors all of whose matrix coefficients on the first group are
  holomorphic of weight u. `New.Rows.prod_mem_holomorphicData` and
  `New.Rows.mem_holomorphicData_of_highestVector` give such vectors.
* `New.Rows.IsAdelicHol`: adelic functions holomorphic in the archimedean variable.
  `New.Rows.isAdelicHol_pairTheta` passes from a holomorphic vector to its theta kernel.
* `New.Rows.rapidOfLevel`: rapidly decreasing automorphic functions (members of `V.rapidFunctions`)
  of a given level. The set is closed under conjugation, and `New.Rows.mul_sum_mem_rapidOfLevel`
  treats products with linear combinations.
* `New.Rows.mul_modKernel_mem_rapidFunctions`, `New.Rows.mul_modKernel_mem_rapidOfLevel`:
  multiplying by the kernel in the first variable keeps rapid decay, and keeps the level `N` if `N`
  generates the modulus.

Notions from other files:
* `New.PairFock`: The Fock space for the signs s′_i s_j of the pair, at all infinite places.
* `New.Space`: A hermitian space over E; its automorphic functions live on the adelic group `New.G`,
  and `New.Space.IsRapid` is decay faster than every power of the height.
* `New.pairTheta`: The theta kernel θ(ω_{a,b}(g,h)f) of two hermitian spaces; `New.modKernel` is
  this kernel for the datum made of a function on matrices modulo 𝔪 and the Fock vector of a
  polynomial.
* `New.AutChar.HasWeight b e ψ`: The character b has weight e at the infinite places and tame part
  ψ, given by a character of each residue field.

## Relation to the literature

Known result: Harmonic highest vectors for (U(p,q), U(n)) give holomorphic vectors. Proves a special
case: highest vectors, such as products of leading minors, that are killed by each mixed
second derivative ∂²/∂x_{ca}∂x_{cb} with a, b of opposite signs have matrix coefficients that extend
holomorphically with an explicit weight. This hypothesis is stronger than pluriharmonic in the sense
of Kashiwara–Vergne, where only the sums over c must vanish. Source: M. Kashiwara, M. Vergne, On the
Segal–Shale–Weil representations and harmonic polynomials, Invent. Math. 44 (1978) 1–48, III 6.3,
7.2; M. G. Davidson, The harmonic representation of U(p,q) and its connection with the generalized
unit disk, Pacific J. Math. 129 (1987) 33–55.

See also `Lib.Weil.DualPair.ThetaLiftConvergence`.



## Holomorphic data, adelic holomorphy, functions of a level, characters

Conventions: `New.IsHolOfWeight s o u` is holomorphy of weight u for the Borel subgroup of the order
o. `New.IsPosFirstOrder s o` says that o is injective and puts the indices of sign +1 first.
`New.realPairWeil γ e` is the real Weil representation of the pair for characters of weights γ and
e.


### `traceChar`

 The additive character y ↦ ψ(tr(yu)) of matrices modulo 𝔪 attached to the matrix u, with ψ
the chosen primitive additive character of O_E/𝔪. 

### `polyMatrixCoeff`

 The coefficient `c_P(h, h') = ⟨v_P, ω(h, h') v_P⟩` of the Fock vector of a polynomial `P`, for
real points of the two groups and characters of weights `γ`, `e`. 

### `holomorphicData`

 The set of Fock vectors v such that x ↦ ℓ(Ω(x,1)v) is holomorphic of weight u for the
order o, for every bounded linear functional ℓ. Here Ω is `New.realPairWeil γ e`. 

### `IsAdelicHol`

 For every adelic g, the function h ↦ F(g·(h,1)) on the archimedean group is holomorphic of
weight u for the order o (`New.IsHolOfWeight`). 

### `rapidOfLevel`

 The functions F that are continuous, left invariant under the rational points of V and right
invariant under the principal level K(a), and that decay rapidly (members of `V.rapidFunctions`). 

### `prod_mem_holomorphicData`

 Let the second group be definite, let o put positive indices first, and let each parameter l w
order indices of equal sign as o does. Suppose that at each infinite place w the polynomial P w is a
joint highest vector (`IsJointHighest`) for the standard dual pair data `std 1 s (γ w) (-e w)`,
whose actions involve the integers γ w and −e w. That is, it is nonzero, K-highest of weight ν w on
the rows for the parameter l' w and of weight u w on the columns for −l w, and killed by each
∂²/∂x_{ca}∂x_{cb} for columns a, b of opposite signs. Then the product Q of the P w, in disjoint
sets of variables, is nonzero and its Fock vector v_Q lies in `holomorphicData s 1 o u γ e`. That
is, for every bounded linear functional ℓ, x ↦ ℓ(Ω(x,1)v_Q) is holomorphic of weight u for o, where
Ω = `realPairWeil γ e`. 

### `mem_holomorphicData_of_highestVector`

 Let the linear map f to the Fock space intertwine the conjugate of the representation
`conjWeylRep κ e'` (on `WeylIndex n' κ → ℂ`, of highest weight given by κ, twisted by powers e′ of
the determinant; `highestVector κ l` is its highest vector for l) of the definite second group with
its Weil action, and let each l w be injective. If f of the highest vector for l is holomorphic of
weight u (in `holomorphicData`), so is every f(ξ). 

### `isAdelicHol_pairTheta`

 For automorphic characters `a`, `b` of weights γ, e (tame parts φ, ψ), a Fock vector `v` in
`holomorphicData s s' o u γ e` (so x ↦ ℓ(Ω(x,1)v) is holomorphic of weight `u` for the order
`o : n → ℤ`, for every bounded linear functional ℓ), f ∈ 𝒮(Hom(S,T)) (type `𝒮 (S.hom T)`), whose
joint datum v ⊗ f is `integrated (S.hom T) v f`, and a fixed k in the second group: the function
g ↦ `pairTheta S T a b (v ⊗ f) (g, k)` satisfies `IsAdelicHol o u`. That is, for every adelic g, the
function h ↦ θ(g·(h,1), k) on the archimedean group is holomorphic of weight `u` for the order
`o`. 

### `maxOpNorm`

 For an element u of the archimedean group, the maximum over infinite places of the operator
norm of the matrix of u at that place. 

### `mem_rapidOfLevel_iff`

 A function has level a exactly when it is a rapidly decreasing automorphic function of V that
does not change under right translation by the principal level subgroup K(a). 

### `apply_mul_of_mem_rapidOfLevel`

 A function of level a (a member of `rapidOfLevel V a`) does not change under right translation
by an element of the principal level subgroup K(a). 

### `star_mem_rapidOfLevel`

 The complex conjugate of a rapidly decreasing automorphic function of level a is again one. 

### `mul_sum_mem_rapidOfLevel`

 If each F · fᵢ is a rapidly decreasing automorphic function of level a, then so is the product
of F with the linear combination ∑ cᵢ fᵢ. 

### `integral_mul_sum_const_mul`

 If each F · fᵢ is a rapidly decreasing automorphic function of level a, then the integral of
F · ∑ cᵢ fᵢ over the automorphic quotient is ∑ cᵢ ∫ F · fᵢ. 


## Theta kernels modulo 𝔪 as level multipliers

`modKernel S T a b 𝔪 Ψ Q` is the theta kernel of the spaces `S`, `T` and the characters `a`, `b` for
the function `Ψ` on matrices modulo `𝔪` and the Fock vector of the polynomial `Q`.


### `modKernel_mul_of_reductionGL_eq_one`

 Let the character `b` have weight `e` and tame part `ψ`, trivial at the places outside `𝔪`. Then
the theta kernel `modKernel` does not change under right translation of the first variable by an
integral finite point `k` that reduces to `1` modulo `𝔪`. 

### `mul_modKernel_mem_rapidFunctions`

 Let the character `b` have weight `e` and tame part `ψ`, trivial at the places outside `𝔪`. If
`F` is a rapidly decreasing automorphic function, then so is the product of `F` with the theta
kernel `g ↦ modKernel S T a b 𝔪 Ψ Q g h`. 

### `mul_modKernel_mem_rapidOfLevel`

 Let `𝔪` be the modulus generated by `N`, and let the character `b` have weight `e` and tame part
`ψ`, trivial at the places outside `𝔪`. If `F` is a rapidly decreasing automorphic function of
level `N`, then so is the product of `F` with the theta kernel `g ↦ modKernel S T a b 𝔪 Ψ Q g h`.
