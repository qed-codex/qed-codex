# p-adic tests: tilts, Fontaine's map, covers by tests and flat base change

A cell is a p-complete, absolutely integrally closed valuation ring in which p is neither zero nor a
unit. A p-adic test c is a non-empty product S = `c.ProdRing` of cells over O_C. Its tilt is
S♭ = `c.TiltRing`. ξ generates the kernel of Fontaine's map θ : W(S♭) → S, and
π = `New.Variety.pFlat` is ξ mod p. The file computes the tilt and the kernel of θ for tests. For
perfect b, it identifies algebra maps b → S♭ with algebra maps O_C ⊗ W(b) → S (`wittFunctor` sends b
to W(b) over A_inf, and `algBaseChange OC` is O_C ⊗_{A_inf} –), so tilting is fully faithful on
tests. It also produces cells over primes and covers by tests, and proves flat base change for
ℤ[Y∘ι], for ι full, faithful and with covering points, when Y is affinely covered.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.Variety.PadicTest.tiltPiEquiv`, `New.Variety.PadicTest.isPerfectTorsionFree_tilt`: S♭ is the
  product of the tilts of the cells; it is perfect and has no π-torsion.
* `New.Variety.PadicTest.ker_theta`: the kernel of Fontaine's θ : W(S♭) → S is generated by ξ.
* `New.Variety.PadicTest.thetaIso`: O_C ⊗_{A_inf} W(S♭) ≅ S, naturally in the test.
* `New.Variety.homTiltIso`: for perfect `b`, algebra maps `b → c.TiltRing` correspond to algebra
  maps `O_C ⊗ W(b) → c.ProdRing` by `f ↦ θ ∘ W(f)`, naturally in the test `c`.
* `New.Variety.hasCoveringPoints_padic`: if an algebra has a point in some test, then all its points
  in tests factor through a single point.
* `New.Variety.IsAffinelyCovered.hasFlatBaseChange`, `New.Variety.hasFlatBaseChange_of_iso_reprBy`:
  ℤ[Y∘ι] has flat base change (`New.Variety.HasFlatBaseChange`: its base change maps are bijective
  in all degrees) for affinely covered Y, when ι is full, faithful and has covering points. ℤ[Y] has
  flat base change for Y isomorphic to the points of an O_C-algebra.

Notions from other files:
* `New.OC`: The ring of integers O_C of the field C_p of p-adic complex numbers.
* `New.Variety.PadicTest.algFunctor`: The functor from p-adic tests to commutative O_C-algebras
  sending a test to its product ring S with its structure map; `reprBy B` is the functor of points
  R ↦ (algebra maps B → R), and `Sigma F` is the type of pairs (c, point of `F c`).
* `New.Variety.freeAb`: The functor sending a set-valued functor Y to Z[Y], whose value at A is the
  free abelian group on Y(A).
* `New.Variety.IsAffinelyCovered`: `X` is separated and, after restriction along `ι`, covered by one
  affine: there are a `k`-algebra `B` and a point `y` of `X(B)` such that, for every object `c`,
  each point of `X(ι c)` is the image of `y` under some map `B → ι c`.

## Relation to the literature

Known result: Tilting and untilting for products of perfectoid valuation rings over O_C. Only
products of p-complete absolutely integrally closed valuation rings over O_C are treated, and such a
ring is recovered from the Witt vectors of its tilt. Tilting is fully faithful, and maps from a
perfect algebra to the tilt are maps from its untilt. Source: B. Bhatt, M. Morrow, P. Scholze,
Integral p-adic Hodge theory, Publ. Math. IHES 128 (2018), Lemma 3.10; P. Scholze, Perfectoid
spaces, Publ. Math. IHES 116 (2012), Theorem 5.2.

See also `Lib.PadicComparison.Descent.AlmostDescent` and `Lib.Cohomology.Etale.FilteredLimits`.



## Tilts of tests and the kernel of Fontaine's map

* `New.Variety.PadicTest.exists_isQuotientByElem`: the quotient of S by an idempotent e ≠ 1 is again
  a test.


### `cellPoint`

 For ring maps y_i from an O_C-algebra B to a non-empty family of cells W_i, this is the p-adic
test ∏ W_i with structure map O_C → B → ∏ W_i, together with the point (y_i) : B → ∏ W_i. 

### `isTest`

 The product ring of a p-adic test c is a test ring: reduced, absolutely integrally closed,
and any a, b admit an idempotent e with a ∣ eb and b ∣ (1 − e)a. 

### `isLeftRegular_p`

 Multiplication by `p` is injective on the ring of a p-adic test: each cell is a domain in which
`p ≠ 0`. 

### `tiltPiEquiv`

 For a p-adic test with cells W_i, the ring isomorphism from the tilt of ∏ W_i to the product
of the tilts W_i^♭ induced by the projections. 

### `dvd_of_untilt_dvd`

 Divisibility in the tilt of a test can be checked after untilting. 

### `isPerfectTorsionFree_tilt`

 The tilt of a p-adic test c is a perfect O^♭-algebra without `pFlat`-torsion, in the sense of
`New.Variety.IsPerfectTorsionFree`. 

### `ker_theta`

 Let S be the ring of a p-adic test, that is, the product of its family of valuation rings, with
its ring map from O_C. Then the kernel of Fontaine's map θ: W(S^♭) → S is the principal ideal
generated by the image of ξ = ∑_{0 ≤ i < p} [ε^{1/p}]^i ∈ A_inf = W(O^♭) under the map
W(O^♭) → W(S^♭) induced by the algebra map O^♭ → S^♭, where O^♭ is the tilt of O_C. 

### `exists_isQuotientByElem`

 For an idempotent e ≠ 1 in the ring of a p-adic test c, there is a map of tests f: c → d
that is universal among maps killing e and is the quotient by the ideal (e). 

### `thetaIso`

 The natural isomorphism O_C ⊗_{A_inf} W(S^♭) ≅ S of O_C-algebras on p-adic tests S, induced
by Fontaine's map θ. 

### `thetaIso_hom_one_tmul`

 The isomorphism `thetaIso` sends `1 ⊗ x` to `θ x`. 

 ## Maps into the tilt and full faithfulness of tilting 

### `theta_comp_bijective`

 For a perfect ring `R` of characteristic `p` and a p-adic test `c` (a non-empty family of cells
with a ring map from O_C to their product `c.ProdRing`), ring maps from `R` to the tilt of
`c.ProdRing` correspond bijectively to ring maps `W(R) → c.ProdRing`, by `f ↦ θ ∘ W(f)`, where θ
is Fontaine's map. 

### `homTiltIso`

 For a perfect algebra `b` over the tilt `OFlat` of `OC`: algebra maps `b → c.TiltRing` over
`OFlat` correspond to algebra maps `OC ⊗ W(b) → c.ProdRing` over `OC` (tensor product over
`W(OFlat)`), naturally in the `p`-adic test `c`. The map `f` goes to the map induced by
`θ ∘ W(f)`. 

### `instance : tilt.Full`

 Tilting is full: every map between the tilts of two tests is the tilt of a map of tests. 


## Cells over primes, covers by tests and flat base change

* `New.Variety.IsAffinelyCovered.devissage_bijective`: dévissage from representable to affinely
  covered functors.


### `preValuationRing_hyperfilter_germ`

 An ultrapower of a ring in which divisibility is total, along the hyperfilter on `ℕ`, is again
such a ring (`PreValuationRing`): of two germs, one divides the other at almost all indices. 

### `surjective_shiftDiff_hyperfilter_germ`

 In an ultrapower along the hyperfilter on `ℕ`, every system `x j - a * x (j + 1) = c j` is
solvable (`shiftDiff`), for a natural number `a`. 

 ### Cells and universal covers 

### `exists_cell_over`

 If B is p-torsion-free and m is a prime ideal of B containing p, there are a cell W
(`New.Variety.IsCell`) and a ring map B → W sending every element of m to a non-unit. 

### `isCell_of_isSolutionClosed`

 A subring `g : W → V` of a cell is again a cell if every equation `IsSolution` with coefficients
in `W` that is solvable in `V` is solvable in `W` (`New.Variety.Envelope.IsSolutionClosed`). 

### `hasCoveringPoints_padic`

 Universal cover by a test: `New.Variety.PadicTest.algFunctor` has covering points
(`New.Variety.HasCoveringPoints`). If the O_C-algebra B has a point (an O_C-algebra map to the ring
of the test) in some p-adic test, then there are a test c and a point y of B in c through which
every point of B in any test d factors by a map of tests c → d. 

### `instance : algFunctor.Full`

 The functor `New.Variety.PadicTest.algFunctor` is full: maps of tests are by definition the
`O_C`-algebra maps between their product rings. 

 ### Flat base change for affinely covered functors 

### `HasFlatBaseChange.isClosedClass`

 Flat base change (`New.Variety.HasFlatBaseChange`) defines a closed class of functors from C to
abelian groups: it contains 0, and for M' ⊂ M with M' in the class, M is in it exactly when M/M'
is. 

### `isAffinelyCovered_reprBy`

 The functor of points of an A-algebra B in the algebras ι(c) is affinely covered. 

### `isAffinelyCovered_cech`

 Let `ι` be any functor from `C` to `A`-algebras, and let `X` be a separated functor on
`A`-algebras, meaning that for any two points of X(B) there is an ideal J of B such that a map φ :
B → B' makes them equal exactly when φ kills J. Let `y` be a point of `X` over `B` and `η : B ⟶ T`.
Then the fibre powers (`New.Variety.cech`, `m + 1` factors) of the composite h_T → h_B → X, given by
`η` and `y`, are affinely covered relative to `ι`: each is separated and has a single point over
some `A`-algebra from which every point over every `ι c` is obtained by some map
(`New.Variety.IsAffinelyCovered`). 

### `IsAffinelyCovered.devissage_bijective`

 Let ι from C to A-algebras be fully faithful, and suppose that each A-algebra B with a point in
some ι(c) has one point through which all its points factor (`New.Variety.HasCoveringPoints`). Let
`f` be a family of maps `M N' n → N N' n`, indexed by functors `N'` from C to abelian groups and
degrees n. Suppose it satisfies the five and four lemmas like a map of contravariant δ-functors
(`New.Variety.Devissage`). If f is bijective in all degrees at ℤ[h_c] for every object c of C, then
it is so at ℤ[Y∘ι] for every affinely covered Y. Here Y is separated and has one point y ∈ Y(B) of
which every point of each Y(ι c) is an image (`New.Variety.IsAffinelyCovered`). 

### `IsAffinelyCovered.hasFlatBaseChange`

 Let ι from C to A-algebras be fully faithful, and suppose each A-algebra B with a point in some
ι(c) has one point through which all its points factor (`New.Variety.HasCoveringPoints`). If Y is
affinely covered (`New.Variety.IsAffinelyCovered`), then ℤ[Y∘ι] has flat base change. 

### `hasFlatBaseChange_of_iso_reprBy`

 If a functor Y on p-adic tests is isomorphic to the points of an O_C-algebra B, then ℤ[Y] has
flat base change.
