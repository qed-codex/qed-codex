# Good pairs: excision, Thom retraction and Alexander duality for principal opens

Spaces are set-valued functors on the tests over `A` (`New.Variety.TestOver`, the full subcategory
of `A`-algebras satisfying `IsTest`). A pair `(Y, U)` is the functor
`New.Variety.relFreeAb U = ℤ[Y] / ℤ[U]`. The pair is good,
`New.Variety.ComparedTheory.IsFiniteLerayHirsch D`, if its cohomology with locally constant
`ℤ/ℓ`-coefficients is finite and compares bijectively with the theory `D`. Here
`New.Variety.offClosedFibre Y` is the part of `Y` on tests where the maximal ideal of `A` generates
the unit ideal.

* `New.Variety.ComparedTheory.isFiniteLerayHirsch_iff_of_locally_bijective`: excision along a map
  that is Zariski-locally bijective on pairs.
* `New.Variety.ComparedTheory.isFiniteLerayHirsch_of_prod_projLine`: goodness descends from the
  product with `(ℙ¹, ℙ¹ ∖ (0 : 1))`.
* `New.Variety.ComparedTheory.isFiniteLerayHirsch_principal_open`: principal opens of affine space
  inside `(ℙ¹)^d`, by Alexander duality.
* `New.Variety.ComparedTheory.isFiniteLerayHirsch_standard_etale`: standard étale charts, by two
  excisions.

Notions from other files:
* `New.Variety.projLine.unimodularRow`: homogeneous coordinates (1 + uv, v) of a point of P¹(B) with
  chosen representative (u, v); `New.Variety.projLine.affinePt x` is the point (x : 1).
* `New.Variety.ComparedTheory`: a theory along g with morphisms to its coefficient from the locally
  constant ℤ/ℓ-valued functions, such that it and `New.Variety.Theory.locallyConstant` satisfy the
  projective line formula.
* `New.Variety.IsZariskiLocallyTop ι U`: every point of `Y` lies in `U` after inverting some element
  outside any given maximal ideal, that is, `U` is all of `Y` Zariski-locally.

## Relation to the literature

Known result: Comparison of cohomology theories by duality, via a diagonal kernel on products of P¹.
This step is common to several comparison theorems. It concerns torsion coefficient theories in
which the projective line is computed. In these theories, a class supported on the diagonal of
(P¹)^d carries finiteness and comparison from the point to standard étale charts over affine space.
Sources for the theorems: SGA 4 XVI, SGA 4½ Finitude/Dualité; Faltings, Scholze primitive
comparison. The proof is in the spirit of Dold–Puppe dualisability.


 ### Points of the line, principal open sets and the ideal of their complement 

### `multihomogeneousIdeal`

 The ideal of `B` generated by the image of `J` under `φ` and by the multihomogeneous form of
`Φ` (of degree `Φ.degreeOf i` in the `i`-th pair of coordinates) evaluated at the rows `r i`,
times the product of the second entries of the rows. 

### `map_multihomogeneousIdeal`

 The ideals `multihomogeneousIdeal` are compatible with ring maps that carry the rows `r i` to
unit multiples of the rows `r' i`. 

### `testSpec`

 For an A-algebra T, its functor of points c ↦ Hom_A(T, c) on tests over A. 

### `testSpec.asAlgHom`

 A point ψ of Spec T with values in a test A-algebra c, viewed as an A-algebra homomorphism
T → c. 

### `testUnitLocus`

 For a in T, the subfunctor of Spec T (on tests over A) of the points ψ at which ψ(a) is a
unit. 

### `testUnitLocus.nonempty_testSpec_iso_of_isLocalization_away`

 Let `L` be a localization of `T` away from `a`. Then the functor of points of `L` on tests over
`A` is isomorphic to the unit locus `testUnitLocus a` of `a` in Spec `T`. 

### `testUnitLocus.nonempty_iso_away`

 `nonempty_testSpec_iso_of_isLocalization_away` with `L := Localization.Away a` unless `L` is
given: the functor of points of `L` is isomorphic to the unit locus `testUnitLocus a`. 

### `affineToLines`

 For e: A[X_1,…,X_d] ≅ T, the map from Spec T to (P¹)^d on tests over A sending ψ to the
points (ψ(e X_i) : 1). 

### `instance : Mono (affineToLines e)`

 `affineToLines e` is a monomorphism: a point of Spec `T` is determined by its values at the
generators `e (X i)`. 

### `multihomogeneousIdeal_eq_top_iff`

 For `a : T` and a point `q` of `(ℙ¹)^d` over the test `c`, consider the ideal of `c` generated
by the multihomogeneous form of `e.symm a`, evaluated at the rows `unimodularRow (q i)` and
multiplied by the product of their second entries (the `⊥` adds no other generators). This ideal is
the unit ideal exactly when `q` is the image under `affineToLines e` of a point of
`testUnitLocus a`, i.e. when `q` lies in the affine space and `a` is a unit there. 

### `multihomFamily`

 At a point `(q, φ)` of `(ℙ¹)^d × Spec A`, the ideal `multihomogeneousIdeal Φ J` for the ring map
`φ` and the unimodular rows of `q`. It is an `IdealFamily` on the elements `(B, q, φ)` over rings
`B`: one ideal of `B` for each element, compatible with ring maps (`map_eq`). 

### `offClosedFibre`

 The part of `Y` on the tests in which the maximal ideal of `A` generates the unit ideal, that
is the part away from the special fibre. 

### `sameClassLocus`

 The pairs of points of `E` with the same image under `u` that have the same class in
`ℤ E / ℤ (u⁻¹ U)`. 

### `isZariskiLocallyTop_sameClassLocus_of_injective`

 If `u` is injective on every test, then two points of `E` with the same image under `u` are
equal, so they have the same class and the locus `sameClassLocus u U` is all of `fibreProd u u`;
in particular it is everything Zariski-locally. 

 ### Excision and the Thom retraction 

### `isFiniteLerayHirsch_iff_of_locally_bijective`

 Excision along a locally bijective map of pairs. Locally every point of `Y` lies in the image
of `u` or in `U` (`h_surj`), and locally two points of `E` with the same image have the same class
modulo the preimage of `U` (`h_inj`). Then the pair `(E, u⁻¹ U)` is good for the compared theory
`D` iff `(Y, U)` is. Here `E` and `Y` are set-valued functors on the tests over `A`, `u : E ⟶ Y`
and `U` is a subfunctor of `Y`. 

### `mulClassOp`

 For a flat functor `N`, a class `ξ` in the total Ext from `P` to the Tate module, a map
`p : N' ⟶ N ⊗ P` and a map `π : N ⟶ K`: the operation `y ↦ p^*((π^* y) · ξ)` from the total Ext of
`K ∘ g` to that of `N' ∘ g`, with coefficients in a theory `Θ` along `g`, natural in `Θ`. 

### `isFiniteLerayHirsch_of_prod_projLine`

 A pair is a retract of its product with `(ℙ¹, S)`, where `S ≅ 𝔸¹` is the complement
`affineChart` of `(0 : 1)`. Let `V` be a subfunctor of `Y`. If the product pair
`(Y × ℙ¹, V × ℙ¹ ∪ Y × S)` is good for the compared theory `D` (`h_prod`), so is `(Y, V)`. 

 ### Standard étale charts 

### `rootPairs`

 For a standard étale pair `P = (f, g)` over `R`, the pairs `(w, ψ)` of a point `w` of
`Spec (R[X] / f)[1 / g]` and a point `ψ` of `Spec R[X][1 / g]` that agree on `R` and such that
`f x` is an associate of `x - y`, where `y = w (root f)` and `x = ψ X`. So `y` is a root of `f`,
and `g` is a unit at `y` and at `x`. 

### `exists_eq_add_mul_and_sub_derivative_mem`

 Taylor expansion at a root of `P.f`, to first order. Let `w` be a root of `P.f` and `ψ` a
point of `Spec R[X]` with values in the same ring that agree on `R` (`hC`), with
`ψ X = w (root P.f) + t` (`hX`) and `t ∈ m`. Then every `ψ p` is `w p + s * t` for some `s`
that is congruent to `ψ p'` modulo `m`. 

### `exists_notMem_of_rootPairs`

 Near the prime `m` of the test ring, the pair `(w, ψ)` lies in `rootPairs P`: `w` is a root of
`P.f`, `ψ` a point of `Spec R[X]`, they agree on `R` (`hC`), `ψ X = w (root P.f) + t` with
`t ∈ m` (`hX`), and `P.g` is a unit at `w` or at `ψ`. So if a property `S` of maps `f` out of
`c` holds wherever `ε ∉ m` is a unit and `f` sends `(w, ψ)` into `rootPairs P` (`hS`), then there
is `a ∉ m` such that `S` holds along every `f` that makes `a` a unit. 

### `rootPairsToProdLine`

 For a standard étale pair `P = (f, g)` over `R`, the map from `rootPairs P` to `Y × ℙ¹`, where
`Y = Spec (R[X] / f)[1 / g]`, that sends a pair `(y, x)` to `(y, (x - y : 1))`. 

### `rootPairsToProdLine_app_injective`

 The map `(y, x) ↦ (y, (x - y : 1))` from `rootPairs P` to `Y × ℙ¹` is injective on every test:
`y` and `x - y` determine `x`. 

### `isZariskiLocallyTop_range_rootPairsToProdLine_sup`

 For a standard étale pair `P = (f, g)` over `R`, locally every point `(y, q)` of `Y × ℙ¹`,
`Y = Spec (R[X] / f)[1 / g]`, is the image `(y, (x - y : 1))` of a pair of `rootPairs P`, or lies in
`offClosedFibre Y × ℙ¹` or in `Y × (ℙ¹ ∖ (0 : 1))`. 

 ### Principal open sets of affine space and standard étale charts 

### `isFiniteLerayHirsch_principal_open`

 Alexander duality for a principal open set of `Spec T ⊆ (ℙ¹)^d`, embedded by the presentation
`e` of `T` as a polynomial ring. `D.IsFiniteLerayHirsch N` requires, among other things, that
`w ↦ ∑ i, (w i restricted along g) · D.comparison i` be a bijection from families in total
Ext(`N`, locally constant `ℤ/ℓ`) onto total Ext(`g ⋙ N`, coefficient of `D`). If this holds for
ℤ[pt]/ℤ[pt away from the special fibre] (`L`), it holds for ℤ[(ℙ¹)^d]/ℤ[`Spec T[1/a]` ∪ complement
of the special fibre]. 

### `isFiniteLerayHirsch_offClosedFibre_sup_testUnitLocus_preimage`

 Assume `L`: the pair of the point and its part away from the special fibre is good for `D`.
Let `e` present `R[X]` as the polynomial ring in `d` variables over `A`, and let `P = (f, g)` be
a standard étale pair over `R`. In `Spec R[X][1 / g]`, let `U` be the union of the part where `f`
is a unit and the part `offClosedFibre` away from the special fibre. Then the pair of
`Spec R[X][1 / g]` and `U` is good for `D`. 

### `isFiniteLerayHirsch_standard_etale`

 Standard étale charts are good. A pair `U ⊆ Y` is good for the compared theory `D` if
`D.IsFiniteLerayHirsch` holds for `ℤ[Y]/ℤ[U]`: in particular
`w ↦ ∑ i, (w i restricted along g) · D.comparison i` is bijective on total Ext. Let `e` present
`R[X]` as the polynomial ring in `d` variables over `A`, and let `P = (f, g)` be a standard étale
pair over `R`. Assume `L`: the pair of the point and its part `offClosedFibre` away from the special
fibre is good for `D`. Then the pair of `Y = Spec (R[X] / f)[1 / g]` and `offClosedFibre Y` is
good.
