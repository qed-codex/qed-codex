# Integration over ℙ¹, compared cohomology theories, and moving kernel classes in (ℙ¹)^d

The file gives the geometric step of the duality for a zero locus in (ℙ¹)^d: a kernel class is moved
between points one coordinate at a time. In the first section, g is a functor from C to tests over
A, ℓ is invertible in A, and I is a family of ideals on the points of a functor X on tests. η
denotes the class `New.Variety.etaClass`; `M.HasLineFormula` (the projective line formula) says
that, for every S, each class from S over the points of ℙ¹ is a·1 + b·η for unique a, b restricted
from the base. The file then sets up the formal comparison of two cohomology theories on tests over
`A`, using integration over `ℙ¹`.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.Variety.IdealFamily.exists_move_kernel`: a class at the point t(q) is moved to t(q') by
  multiplying with the classes η(q_j) + η(q'_j).
* `New.Variety.TorsionCoeff.HasLineFormula.integrate`: integration `a + η b ↦ b` over `ℙ¹`, given
  `New.Variety.TorsionCoeff.HasLineFormula`.
* `New.Variety.ComparedTheory`, `New.Variety.ComparedTheory.IsFiniteLerayHirsch`: a theory with maps
  from the locally constant `ZMod ℓ` coefficients, and the property that these maps compute its
  cohomology from finite groups.
* `New.Variety.NaturalOp`: operations on cohomology, natural in the theory, with parameters in `ℙ¹`.
* `New.Variety.ComparedTheory.isClosedClass_isFiniteLerayHirsch`,
  `New.Variety.ComparedTheory.isFiniteLerayHirsch_of_shortExact`,
  `New.Variety.ComparedTheory.isFiniteLerayHirsch_iff_of_isCohomEquiv`,
  `New.Variety.ComparedTheory.isFiniteLerayHirsch_of_retract`: the property passes along short exact
  sequences, equivalences and retracts by operations.
* `New.Variety.ComparedTheory.isFiniteLerayHirsch_of_integrate_params`,
  `New.Variety.ComparedTheory.isFiniteLerayHirsch_topPoints_of_surjective`: it passes to pairs in
  `(ℙ¹)^m`, by self-duality.

Notions from other files:
* `New.Variety.TestOver`: The category of tests over A: the full subcategory of commutative
  A-algebras whose underlying rings are test rings.
* `New.Variety.TorsionCoeff`: a functor F on C to abelian groups, killed by ℓ, with Zariski descent
  for the rings g(c). A `New.Variety.Theory` is one that also behaves well on closed loci.
* `New.Variety.IdealFamily`: For a functor ι from C to commutative rings, ideals I_c of ι(c) such
  that I_b is generated by the image of I_a for every morphism a → b.
* `New.Variety.IdealFamily.topLocus`: the subfunctor of the one-point functor that is non-empty
  exactly where I_c is the unit ideal.

## Relation to the literature

Known; see `Lib.PadicComparison.Duality.GoodPairs` for sources.



## Moving a kernel class between points of (ℙ¹)^d

`New.Variety.TotalH` N F is the total Ext group and `New.Variety.TotalH.comp` the Yoneda product.
`New.Variety.relFreeAb` U is ℤ[V]/ℤ[U] for U ⊆ V. `New.Variety.TorsionCoeff.etaAt` b is the product
with the class η = `New.Variety.etaClass` pulled back along b, as a class in Ext²(F, F).


### `projLinePow.component`

 For a section q of (P¹)^ι along g and i in ι, its i-th component, a section of P¹ along g. 

### `topPoints`

 For a compatible family I of ideals on the elements of X, the subfunctor of X of points x in
X(c) at which I is the unit ideal. 

### `atSection`

 The morphism ℤ[pt]/ℤ[O] → (ℤ[X]/ℤ[U])∘g induced by the section q, where U ⊆ X and O ⊆ pt
are the loci where I and its pull-back along q are the unit ideal. 

### `atSectionMod`

 The reduction modulo ℓ of `New.Variety.IdealFamily.atSection`: the morphism
(ℤ[pt]/ℤ[O])/ℓ → ((ℤ[X]/ℤ[U])∘g)/ℓ induced by the section q, where U ⊆ X and O ⊆ pt are the loci
where I and its pull-back along q are the unit ideal. 

### `zerosEquiv`

 The isomorphism of total Ext groups into Θ given by pull-back along
`New.Variety.IdealFamily.constToZeros` (constants mod ℓ to functions on the zeros of I), restricted
along the section q. 

### `extendToZeros`

 From classes on (ℤ[X]/ℤ[`New.Variety.IdealFamily.topPoints`])/ℓ along g to classes on the
locally constant ℤ/ℓ-valued functions modulo those dying where I = 0
(`New.Variety.IdealFamily.zerosCoeff`), taken along q: pull back along q, then invert
`New.Variety.IdealFamily.zerosEquiv`. 

### `zerosEquiv_extendToZeros`

 The defining property of `I.extendToZeros Θ q x`: its image under `I.zerosEquiv Θ q` is the
pull-back of `x` along the section `q`. 

### `extendToZeros_naturality`

 Let `I` be a family of ideals on the points of a functor `X` on tests, `q` a section of `X`
along `g`, `Θ` a theory along `g`, `φ : C' ⥤ C` a functor, `K` a theory along `φ ⋙ g`, and `b` a
class in the total Ext group from `φ ⋙ Θ.functor` to `K.functor`. Write `R` for the map
`y ↦ (y restricted along φ) · b` from classes with coefficients in `Θ.functor` to classes with
coefficients in `K.functor`. Then `R` commutes with `extendToZeros`: for a class `x` on
`(ℤ[X]/ℤ[I.topPoints])/ℓ` along `g`, the image under `R` of the extension of `x` for `Θ` and `q` is
the extension of `R x` for `K` and the restriction of `q` along `φ`. 

### `isZariskiLocallyTop_preimage_topLocus_inf_sup`

 Let every maximal ideal `m` of a ring `g(c)` be the kernel of `g(f)` for some `f : c ⟶ c'`
(`h_max`), and let the families `L` and `L'` of ideals agree at every object where the family `J` is
zero. Then, inside the locus where `L'` is the unit ideal, the locus where `L` or `J` is the unit
ideal is locally everything. 

### `exists_move_kernel_of_eq_imp_eq`

 The one-coordinate case of `exists_move_kernel`. Let `K` be an `ℓ`-torsion coefficient along
`g`, and let the sections `s` and `s'` of `X` agree wherever the sections `x` and `x'` of `ℙ¹` do.
Let `h_max` say that each maximal ideal of a ring `g(c)` is the kernel of some `g(f)`. Then for
every total Ext class `δ` into `K` from ℤ[pt]/ℤ[O], with O the locus where the pull-back of `I`
along `s` is the unit ideal, there is a class `δ'` of the same kind for `s'` with the following
property. For every `φ : C' ⥤ C`, theory `Θ` along `φ ⋙ g` (a coefficient that behaves well on
closed loci), class `T` from `K` restricted along `φ` to `Θ`, and class `v` from
`ℤ[X]/ℤ[I.topPoints]` along `φ ⋙ g` to `Θ`: if the Yoneda product `δ · T` (of `δ` restricted along
`φ` with `T`) is the pull-back of `v` along `I.atSection s`, then `δ' · T` is that of
`v · (η(x) + η(x'))` along `I.atSection s'`. 

### `exists_move_kernel`

 Let `K` be a torsion coefficient along `g`. Suppose every maximal ideal of each ring `g c` is
the kernel of the ring map along some `c ⟶ c'` (`h_max`). Let `t` send `d` sections of `ℙ¹` to a
section of `g ⋙ X`, pointwise (`t_congr`). Let `δ` be a class in the total Ext group from
`ℤ[pt]/ℤ[O_q]` to `K.functor`, where `O_q` is where the pull-back of `I` along `t q` is the unit
ideal. Then some `δ'` (for `q'`) satisfies the following. Take any `φ : C' ⥤ C`, theory `Θ` along
`φ ⋙ g`, class `T` from `K.functor` restricted along `φ` to `Θ.functor`, and class `w` from
`ℤ[X]/ℤ[I.topPoints]` along `φ ⋙ g` to `Θ.functor`. If `δ · T` is the pull-back of `w` along `t q`,
then `δ' · T` is the pull-back along `t q'` of `w` followed by the products with `η` at `q_j + q'_j`
for all `j`. 


## Integration over the line and compared cohomology theories

`New.Variety.lerayHirsch θ` is the map `w ↦ ∑ Φ (w i) · θ i` for an exact functor `Φ`, here a
restriction, where x · y = `New.Variety.TotalH.comp x y` is the Yoneda product (x followed by y).


 ### Integration over the projective line 

### `map_foldr_etaAt`

 Restriction to the points of `X` passes through a product of classes `M.etaAt (b j)` factor by
factor. 

### `lineBasis_naturality`

 Restriction along `φ` followed by the product with a class `T` from `M.functor` restricted along
`φ` to `M'.functor` commutes with the two maps `x ↦ x · 1` and `x ↦ x · η` to the cohomology over
the points of the line. 

### `bijective_iff_of_hasUniversalPoints`

 If the comparison `(φ, β)` has universal points along `u : Y' ⟶ φ ⋙ Y`, the projective line
formula holds for `M` at `ℤ[Y]` (each class from ℤ[Y] over the points of ℙ¹ is a·1 + b·η for unique
a, b from the base) if and only if it holds for `M'` at `ℤ[Y']`. 

### `onElements`

 The projective line formula passes to categories of elements: if it holds for `M`, it holds
for the restriction of `M` to the category of elements of any set-valued functor `X` on `C`. 

### `integrate`

 Integration over the projective line: given the projective line formula for M, the map
sending a class a + ηb over the points of P¹ to b. 

### `integrate_naturality`

 If additive maps `f` on the base and `f'` over the points of the line are compatible with the
products with `1` and with `η`, they are compatible with integration over the line. 

### `integrate_mul_cupClass`

 The integral over the line of `y · (η + ξ)`, with η here the class `M.cupClass` placed in degree
2 (η in `M.lineBasis`), is `y`, for `y` and `ξ` pulled back from the base. 

 ### Compared theories and natural operations 

### `ComparedTheory`

 A theory along g with morphisms b i (the field `comparison`, indexed by `Index`) to its
coefficient F (the field `functor`) from the locally constant ℤ/ℓ-valued functions, such that it and
`New.Variety.Theory.locallyConstant` satisfy the projective line formula. 

### `ComparedTheory.Index`

 The index set of the comparison morphisms. 

### `ComparedTheory.comparison`

 The morphisms from the locally constant `ℤ/ℓ`-valued functions along `g` to `functor`. 

### `ComparedTheory.line_formula`

 The coefficient `functor` satisfies the projective line formula. 

### `ComparedTheory.line_formula_const`

 The theory of locally constant functions satisfies the projective line formula. 

### `LineStage`

 A functor g (the field `toTestOver`) from a category C (the field `Base`) to tests over A, a
theory Θ (the field `theory`) along g satisfying the projective line formula, and d compatible
points of P¹ over the rings g(c). 

### `LineStage.Base`

 The category on which the stage lives. 

### `LineStage.category`

 The category structure on `Base`. 

### `LineStage.toTestOver`

 The functor from `Base` to tests over `A`. 

### `LineStage.theory`

 A theory along `toTestOver`. 

### `LineStage.line_formula`

 The theory satisfies the projective line formula. 

### `LineStage.points`

 A compatible family of points of `(ℙ¹)^d` over the rings of the tests `toTestOver.obj c`. 

### `NaturalOp`

 Operations with d parameters: additive maps from total Ext(N∘g, Θ) to total Ext(N'∘g, Θ),
for every g, theory Θ with the projective line formula and section of (P¹)^d, commuting with
comparisons along functors. 

### `NaturalOp.app`

 For a stage `t`, with theory `Θ = t.theory` along `g = t.toTestOver`, the projective line
  formula and the section `t.points` of `(ℙ¹)^d`, an additive map from the total cohomology of
  `g ⋙ N` to that of `g ⋙ N'`, coefficients in `Θ.functor`. 

### `NaturalOp.naturality`

 The maps `app` commute with restriction along a functor `φ`, followed by the product with a
  class `b` from `φ ⋙ t.theory.functor` to `Θ.functor`. 

### `LineStage.consTautologicalPoint`

 The restriction of t to the category of pairs (c, x) with x ∈ P¹(t.toTestOver(c)), with the
tautological point x placed before the d points of t. 

### `NaturalOp.integrate`

 Integrating out the first parameter of r: pull back to the points of P¹, apply r with the
tautological point as first parameter, and integrate over the line. 

### `finite_of_exact`

 If `K → I → P` is exact at `I`, with `f` any map and `g` additive, and `K` and `P` are finite,
then `I` is finite. 

 ### The comparison property and its permanence 

### `IsFiniteLerayHirsch`

 `D.IsFiniteLerayHirsch N`, for a functor `N` from tests over `A` to abelian groups. Write `L`
for the locally constant `ℤ/ℓ`-valued functions and `F` for the coefficient of `D`. Then (1) the
Leray–Hirsch map of the classes `D.comparison i`, which sends a family `(w i)` of classes in the
total Ext group of `N` into `L` to `∑ i, (w i restricted along g) · D.comparison i`, is a bijection
onto the total Ext group of `g ⋙ N` into `F`, and (2) the total Ext group of `N` into `L` is
finite. 

### `isClosedClass_isFiniteLerayHirsch`

 `D.IsFiniteLerayHirsch` is a closed class (`IsClosedClass`): it holds for zero functors, and in
a short exact sequence `0 → X₁ → X₂ → X₃ → 0` with `D.IsFiniteLerayHirsch X₁` it holds for `X₂` if
and only if for `X₃`. 

### `isFiniteLerayHirsch_of_shortExact`

 In a short exact sequence `0 → X₁ → X₂ → X₃ → 0` of functors on tests over `A`, if
`D.IsFiniteLerayHirsch` holds for `X₂` and `X₃`, it holds for `X₁`. 

### `isFiniteLerayHirsch_iff_of_isCohomEquiv`

 Let `α : N ⟶ N'` be a map of functors on tests over `A` such that, for every functor `g` from a
category `C` to tests over `A` and every functor `F` on `C` with Zariski descent along `g`,
pull-back along `α` (restricted along `g`) is a bijection of total Ext groups into `F`
(`IsCohomEquiv`). Then `D.IsFiniteLerayHirsch N` holds if and only if `D.IsFiniteLerayHirsch N'`
holds. 

### `isFiniteLerayHirsch_of_retract`

 `D.IsFiniteLerayHirsch` passes to retracts by natural operations. Let `N` have flat values,
write `N / ℓ` for `abCoker (ℓ • 𝟙 N)`, and let `r i : N / ℓ → N'` and `ρ i : N' → N` be operations
without parameters (`NaturalOp 0`). If `∑ i, ρ i (r i x)` is always the pull-back of `x` along
`N → N / ℓ`, then `D.IsFiniteLerayHirsch N'` implies `D.IsFiniteLerayHirsch N`. 

 ### Pairs in a power of the projective line 

### `isFiniteLerayHirsch_of_integrate_params`

 `D.IsFiniteLerayHirsch` passes from `N` to `ℤ[(ℙ¹)^m]/ℤ[U]`, `U = I.topPoints`, given classes
`β i` from it to `N`, operations `T i` with `d` parameters in `ℙ¹` from its quotient by `ℓ` to `N`,
and `σ` sending parameters to coordinates, such that (`h_sum_eq_foldr`) for every stage `t` with
points `t.points`, section `q'` and class `w`, `∑ i, β i · T i (w)` and
`w · ∏ j, (η(t.points j) + η(q' (σ j)))` agree after pull-back along `I.atSection q'`. 

### `isFiniteLerayHirsch_topPoints_of_surjective`

 Let I be a compatible family of ideals on points of (P¹)^m, U the locus where I is the unit
ideal, and Q = ℤ[(P¹)^m]/ℤ[U]. Let `θ i` be classes of degree `d i`, over the points of (P¹)^m, from
N to the locally constant ℤ/ℓ-valued functions modulo those dying where I = 0 (`I.zerosCoeff`).
Suppose their Leray–Hirsch map, sending families `(w i)` of total Ext classes from Q to N to
`∑ i, θ i ∘ w i` (with `w i` restricted to points), is surjective. Then `D.IsFiniteLerayHirsch`
passes from N to Q; this property includes bijectivity of the analogous map built from the classes
`D.comparison i`.
