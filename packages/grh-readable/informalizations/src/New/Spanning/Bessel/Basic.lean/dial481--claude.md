# Bessel systems at a real place: length and norm bounds, lattice basis, discriminant

The file sets up the objects that connect the analysis at one real place with the filling argument.
`New.Shape.G₁` is the unitary group of a hermitian form made of ϱ hyperbolic planes, a positive
space X₀ with basis κ and a vector z with h(z, z) = −1. The frame is (e₁, …, e_ϱ, z). The Bessel
subgroup `New.Shape.Form.besselS` is generated by the unipotent group 𝖴 = `New.Shape.Form.besselU`
and by `New.Shape.Form.besselH` = U(X₀). `New.frameVector y a` is the image under `y⁻¹` of the
`a`-th frame vector. The file first proves elementary bounds on the length `New.besselLength y`, the
infimum of `‖s * y‖ + 1` over `s` in `besselS`. It then defines the lattice basis, the discriminant
𝗀 and the link, and the structure `New.BesselSystem` listing what is used of a Bessel function W and
its cut W♭. Norms on G₁ are those of the Euclidean norm for which the basis e_a, x_i, z, e*_a is
orthonormal. The frame has n = ϱ + 1 vectors.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.norm_add_one_le_of_mem_frameBox`: `‖y‖` is bounded by the lengths of `y⁻¹ e_a`, `y⁻¹ z` and
  `y⁻¹ e*_a`.
* `New.orthoFrame`, `New.gramSchmidtMatrix`: Gram–Schmidt for the frame. The orthogonal vectors are
  left invariant under `besselS` (`New.orthoFrame_besselS_mul`), and the Bessel length bounds their
  lengths on both sides (`New.norm_orthoFrame_mem_Icc`). The triangular matrix depends continuously
  on the point (`New.continuous_gramSchmidtMatrix`).
* `New.besselLength_le_of_norm_orthoFrame`: conversely, these lengths bound `besselLength y`; the
  proof corrects `y` on the left until the first bound applies. `New.besselLength_le_of_dual` is the
  case of a frame with a bounded triangular dual system.
* `New.besselLatticeBasis`: the basis of the lattice, in which `x_{k₀}` is replaced by
  `v = x_{k₀} / p̄ + z / N`. `New.gram_besselLatticeBasis` gives its Gram matrix, with middle block
  `New.latticeMiddleGram`.
* `New.frameDisc`: the quantity `|D_{ϱ+1}|² - N² |D_ϱ|²` in the leading minors `New.leadingMinor`,
  written `𝗀`. Up to sign it is the Gram determinant of the frame and its image under an isometry
  (`New.det_gram_framePair`).
* `New.IsCutPair`: the L² bounds of W and W♭, the support of W♭, and decay below a common integrable
  majorant (`New.IsDecayMajorant`).

Notions from other files:
* `New.Shape.besselFrame`: The indices of the frame (e₁, …, e_ϱ, z) among the basis indices.
* `New.Shape.middleGram`: for a scalar N, the diagonal matrix on κ plus one point, with entries 1 on
  κ and −N at the extra point.

## Frame vectors and the Bessel length

Norms of matrices are `L²` operator norms.


### `besselLength`

 For y in G₁, the infimum of ‖sy‖ + 1 (operator norm) over s in the Bessel subgroup S. 

### `frameBox`

 For real ρ, the set of y in G₁ such that every vector y⁻¹f_a of the moved frame has Euclidean
norm at most ρ. 

### `frameVector_mul_of_mem_besselFixer`

 The frame vectors at `y` do not change when `y` is multiplied on the left by an element of
`besselFixer`, the group of the elements that fix the frame. 

### `frameVector_eq_sum`

 For `s` in the unipotent group `New.Shape.Form.besselU`, the frame at `y` is the frame at
`s * y` times the matrix `frameMatrix s` of the action of `s` on the frame. 

### `norm_frameVector_le`

 The vector `frameVector y a` = y⁻¹ f_a, where f_a is the `a`-th vector of the frame (e₁, …, e_ϱ,
z), is not longer than the operator norm of `y`. 

### `norm_sum_le_mul_norm_sum_frameVector`

 The norm of the `i`-th entry of `s` applied to `∑ c a • Pi.single (𝔰.besselFrame (e a)) 1` is at
most `‖s * y‖` times the norm of `∑ c a • frameVector y (e a)`. 

### `RealPlace.exists_norm_le_of_besselLength_le`

 If `besselLength y ≤ T`, then some `u` in the unipotent group `besselU` gives `‖u * y‖ ≤ T` in
operator norm. 

### `besselLength_le_norm_inv_mul_mul_besselLength`

 The length `besselLength` changes by at most the factor `‖x⁻¹ * y‖` from `x` to `y`. 

### `continuous_besselLength`

 The length `besselLength y`, the infimum of `‖s * y‖ + 1` (operator norm) over `s` in the Bessel
subgroup `besselS`, is a continuous function of `y` in the unitary group `𝔰.G₁`. 


## Norm bounds from a frame and its dual frame

* `New.realFrameGram_eq_inner`, `New.realFrameGram_mul_of_mem_besselFixer`,
  `New.realFrameGram_eq_frameMatrix_mul`, `New.norm_realFrameGram_le_mul`: the Gram matrix
  `New.realFrameGram` as products of moved frames, its behaviour under `New.Shape.Form.besselU`
  and `New.Shape.Form.besselFixer`, and a bound on its entries.


### `norm_J₁`

 The Gram matrix `J₁` of the form has operator norm one. 

### `norm_sum_rankOne_col`

 The operator norm of a matrix is the norm of the sum of the rank-one operators given by its
columns. 

### `norm_add_one_le_of_mem_frameBox`

 Let `1 ≤ M` and let `y` in `𝔰.G₁` be such that (a) `y` lies in `frameBox M`, that is, the
vectors y⁻¹ e_a and y⁻¹ z have length at most `M`, and (b) the vectors y⁻¹ e*_a, the columns of y⁻¹
at the indices `eStar a`, have length at most `M`. Then `‖y‖ + 1 ≤ 8 (ϱ + 1) M`. 

### `norm_inner_J₁_le`

 Cauchy–Schwarz for the form `h` with matrix `J₁`: `|h(v, w)| ≤ ‖v‖ * ‖w‖`. 

### `realFrameGram_eq_inner`

 Let h(v, w) = ⟪v, J₁ w⟫ be the hermitian form with Gram matrix `𝔰.J₁`, `𝔰.G₁` its unitary group
and f_a the `a`-th vector of the frame (e₁, …, e_ϱ, z); `frameVector y a` is y⁻¹ f_a. For `γ` and
`y` in `𝔰.G₁`, the entry `realFrameGram γ a b` = h(f_a, γ f_b), a complex number, equals h(y⁻¹ f_a,
y⁻¹ γ f_b). 

### `realFrameGram_mul_of_mem_besselFixer`

 For `s` in `besselFixer`, the matrix `New.realFrameGram` is unchanged when `γ` is multiplied by
`s` on the left or on the right. 

### `realFrameGram_eq_frameMatrix_mul`

 Let `s` lie in the unipotent subgroup `besselU` of G₁ and act on the frame by the matrix
`m = frameMatrix s`. Then the matrix `Z(y) = realFrameGram y` of the products `h(f_a, y f_b)` is
`mᴴ Z(s * y)`. 

### `norm_realFrameGram_le_mul`

 The entry `realFrameGram γ a b` = h(f_a, γ f_b) has norm at most the product of the lengths of
`frameVector y a` = y⁻¹ f_a and `frameVector (γ⁻¹ * y) b` = y⁻¹ γ f_b. 

 ### Gram–Schmidt for the frame 

### `gramSchmidt_eq_of_sub_mem_span`

 Gram–Schmidt gives the same vectors for `v` and `w` if each `v a - w a` is a combination of the
`w b` with `b < a`. 

### `orthoFrame`

 The vectors obtained from the frame `y⁻¹ e₁, …, y⁻¹ e_ϱ, y⁻¹ z` by the Gram–Schmidt process for
the Euclidean product; they are pairwise orthogonal and are not normalised. 

### `gramSchmidtMatrix`

 The upper unitriangular matrix of the coefficients of the frame at `y` in the orthogonal
vectors `orthoFrame y`. 

### `frameVector_eq_sum_orthoFrame`

 The frame at `y` is `orthoFrame y` times the matrix `gramSchmidtMatrix y`. 

### `exists_frameVector_mul_eq_orthoFrame`

 There is a `u` in the Bessel unipotent group `besselU` whose submatrix `frameMatrix u` on the
rows and columns of the frame is `gramSchmidtMatrix y`, and for which `frameVector (u * y)` is
`orthoFrame y`. 

### `orthoFrame_besselS_mul`

 For `y` in the unitary group `𝔰.G₁`, let `orthoFrame y` be the Gram–Schmidt vectors of the moved
frame. They do not change when `y` is multiplied on the left by an element `s` of the Bessel
subgroup `besselS`, the group of the elements of `𝔰.G₁` that map each vector of the frame (e₁, …,
e_ϱ, z) to itself plus a combination of the earlier ones. 

### `norm_orthoFrame_le`

 A Gram–Schmidt vector `orthoFrame y a` is not longer than the vector `frameVector y a` of the
moved frame (y⁻¹ e₁, …, y⁻¹ e_ϱ, y⁻¹ z) from which it comes. 

### `one_le_norm_mul_norm_orthoFrame`

 The Gram–Schmidt vectors `orthoFrame y a` of the moved frame (y⁻¹ e₁, …, y⁻¹ e_ϱ, y⁻¹ z) have
length at least `‖y‖⁻¹`, with the operator norm of `y`. 

### `norm_orthoFrame_mem_Icc`

 If `besselLength y`, the infimum of ‖s y‖ + 1 over `s` in the Bessel subgroup `besselS`, is at
most `M`, the Gram–Schmidt vectors `orthoFrame y a` of the moved frame (y⁻¹ e₁, …, y⁻¹ e_ϱ, y⁻¹ z)
have length between `M⁻¹` and `M`. 

### `gramSchmidtMatrix_besselS_mul`

 For `y` in the unitary group `𝔰.G₁`, let `gramSchmidtMatrix y` be the unitriangular matrix of
the moved frame in its Gram–Schmidt vectors. Let `s` lie in the Bessel subgroup `besselS`, the group
of the elements of `𝔰.G₁` that map each vector of the frame (e₁, …, e_ϱ, z) to itself plus a
combination of the earlier ones, and let `frameMatrix s` be the submatrix of `s` on the rows and
columns of the frame. Then `gramSchmidtMatrix y` is `gramSchmidtMatrix (s * y)` times
`frameMatrix s`. 

### `continuous_orthoFrame`

 The Gram–Schmidt vectors `orthoFrame y a` of the moved frame (y⁻¹ e₁, …, y⁻¹ e_ϱ, y⁻¹ z) depend
continuously on `y` in the unitary group `𝔰.G₁`. 

### `besselLength_le_of_norm_orthoFrame`

 If `1 ≤ M` and the vectors `orthoFrame y a` have Euclidean length between `M⁻¹` and `M`, then
`besselLength y ≤ 8 (ϱ + 1) M`. 

### `besselLength_le_of_dual`

 A bounded frame with a bounded triangular dual has bounded length. Let h(v, w) = ⟪v, J₁ w⟫ be
the hermitian form with Gram matrix `𝔰.J₁`, made of ϱ hyperbolic planes (e_a, e*_a), a positive
space and a vector z with h(z, z) = −1, and let `y` lie in its unitary group `𝔰.G₁`. Let `1 ≤ M`
and let `V` be a sequence of vectors with: (a) `V 0, …, V ϱ` is the moved frame
`frameVector y` = (y⁻¹ e₁, …, y⁻¹ e_ϱ, y⁻¹ z); (b) `‖V a‖ ≤ M` for `a ≤ 2 ϱ`; (c) `h(V a, V b)` is
`-1` if `a + b = 2 ϱ` and `0` if `a + b < 2 ϱ`. Then `besselLength y`, the infimum of ‖s y‖ + 1
(operator norm) over `s` in the Bessel subgroup `besselS`, is at most `8 (ϱ + 1) M`. 


## Lattice basis, Gram matrix, link and the discriminant 𝗀

The space has the basis `e_a`; `x_i`, `z`; `e*_a` (indices `𝔰.BesselIndex`) and the Gram matrix
`T.splitGram`, which pairs `e_a` with `e*_a` and is `T` on the middle block.

* `New.link`: the relation on `X^⊥/X` defined by an isometry, built from the matrix
  `New.linkMatrix`.


### `latticeMiddleGram`

 For a scalar N, the matrix with diagonal 1 at the x_i (i ≠ k₀), 0 at v and −N at z, and entries
−1 at (v, z) and (z, v). If N = p p̄, it is the Gram matrix of X₀ ⊕ Ez in the basis x_i (i ≠ k₀), v,
z, where v = x_{k₀}/p̄ + z/N (`New.gram_besselLatticeBasis`). 

### `middleInclusionMatrix`

 The matrix whose columns are the orthonormal basis vectors x_i of X₀ in the lattice basis (x_i
for i ≠ k₀, v, z), where v = x_{k₀}/p̄ + z/(p p̄). 

### `fromCols_middleInclusionMatrix`

 With the column of z appended, the inclusion matrix `middleInclusionMatrix` from the x_i to the
lattice basis is block lower triangular: `scaleAt p̄ k₀` (diagonal, p̄ at k₀ and 1 elsewhere), `1`,
and the single entry `-p⁻¹` below the diagonal. 

### `fromBlocks_scaleAt_mul_eq_one`

 Two block lower triangular matrices that scale the distinguished coordinate `k₀` of κ by `a`,
`a'` and carry it to the last coordinate by `c`, `c'` multiply to `1` if `a * a' = 1` and
`c * a' + c' = 0`. 

### `fromBlocks_one_mul_eq_one`

 Two matrices that are the identity outside the middle block, where they are inverse to each
other, multiply to `1`. 

### `besselLatticeBasis`

 The invertible matrix whose columns are the lattice basis (e_a; x_i for i ≠ k₀, v, z; e*_a),
v = x_{k₀}/p̄ + z/(p p̄), in the standard basis. 

### `frameDisc`

 For Z of size ϱ+1 with leading minors D_l, the quantity |D_{ϱ+1}|² − N²|D_ϱ|², where |x|²
means x x̄. 

### `gram_besselLatticeBasis`

 For a matrix T on the middle indices (x_i; z), `T.splitGram` is the Gram matrix in the basis
(e_a; x_i, z; e*_a) of the form that is T on the middle block and pairs e_a with e*_a as hyperbolic
planes. Let p be a unit and N = p p̄, and take T = `𝔰.middleGram N` = diag(1, …, 1, −N). The columns
of `besselLatticeBasis 𝔰 p` are the basis in which x_{k₀} is replaced by v = x_{k₀}/p̄ + z/N. Its
Gram matrix has the same shape, with middle block `latticeMiddleGram 𝔰 N`: diagonal 1 at the x_i
(i ≠ k₀), 0 at v and −N at z, entries −1 at (v, z) and (z, v), and zero elsewhere. 

### `framePair`

 For γ in the unitary group, the matrix (f | γf) of the frame f and its image under γ, side by
side. 

### `isotropicFramePair`

 The matrix with columns e₁, …, e_ϱ, γe₁, …, γe_ϱ. 

 ### The Gram determinant of two frames 

### `det_eq_add_of_row_eq_add`

 The determinant of a square matrix whose row `i` is the sum of the rows `i` of two matrices
that agree with it in all other rows is the sum of their determinants. 

### `det_fromBlocks_single`

 For square matrices `Z`, `W` and a scalar `c`, the block matrix
`fromBlocks Z (single i i c) (single i i c) W` (off-diagonal blocks have the single entry `c` at
`(i, i)`) has determinant `Z.det * W.det - c ^ 2 * (Z.adjugate i i * W.adjugate i i)`. 

### `gram_framePair`

 Let `N` be self-adjoint in the commutative star ring `R`, let h be the form of ϱ hyperbolic
planes and the middle block diag(1, …, 1, −N), and let `γ` be an isometry of h. The Gram matrix of
the frame f = (e₁, …, e_ϱ, z) and its image γf, side by side in `framePair γ`, has both diagonal
blocks zero except for the entry −N at `𝔰.top`, the position of z. The other two blocks are the
matrix `frameGram γ` of the products h(f_a, γ f_b) and its adjoint. 

### `det_gram_framePair`

 Let `N` be self-adjoint in the commutative star ring `R`, let h be the form of ϱ hyperbolic
planes and the middle block diag(1, …, 1, −N), and let `γ` be an isometry of h. The columns of
`framePair γ` are the frame f = (e₁, …, e_ϱ, z), which has `𝔰.n` = ϱ + 1 vectors, and its image γf.
Let Z be the matrix `frameGram γ` of the products h(f_a, γ f_b), and D_ϱ its leading minor of order
ϱ. The Gram determinant of `framePair γ` is `(-1) ^ 𝔰.n` times `frameDisc N Z` = |det
Z|² − N²|D_ϱ|². 

 ### The map to pairs of middle coordinates, and the link 

### `linkMatrix`

 The matrix of the map t ↦ ([t], [γ⁻¹t]) from the space to two copies of X₀⊕Ez, where [·] takes
the middle coordinates. 

### `link`

 The link of `γ`. Let `X` be the span of `e₁, …, e_ϱ`. A vector is orthogonal to `X` if its last
block of coordinates vanishes, and then its middle block is its class in `X^⊥/X`. The link is the
module of the pairs (class of `t`, class of `γ⁻¹ t`) for `t` orthogonal to `X` and to `γ X`. A pair
is written as one function on `𝔰.MiddleIndex ⊕ 𝔰.MiddleIndex`. 


## Bessel systems: cut pairs, Schur bounds and local bounds

* `New.HasLocalBounds`: local bounds, a system of forms (`New.SieveSystem`) whose top form comes
  from `New.besselOrbitalIntegral`.


 ### Constants 

### `Shape.openCellConst`

 The constant c_G = vol U(k) · (2π^k/(k−1)!) / vol U(k+2ϱ+1), with the volumes `New.volU`. For
k ≥ 1 the middle factor is vol U(k)/vol U(k−1), so that c_G = vol U(k)² / (vol U(k+2ϱ+1) · vol
U(k−1)). 

### `Shape.scaledVolumeRatio`

 The constant Θ = π^{(2ϱ+1)k}·vol U(k)/vol U(r), with k = |κ| and r the rank of G₁. 

 ### The scaled length and the bounds at a real place 

### `scaledBesselLength`

 The length `besselLength y` in units of `b ^ (6 n)`, and at least one; here n = ϱ + 1. 

### `le_scaledBesselLength`

 If `2 ≤ b` and the length `besselLength y` exceeds half of `b ^ (6 n + 2)`, with n = ϱ + 1, then
the scaled length `max 1 (besselLength y / b ^ (6 n))` is at least `b`. 

### `InCut`

 The condition that the length `New.besselLength` of y is at most b^(6(ϱ+1)+2). 

### `RealPlace.gramDiscReal`

 For a (ϱ+1)×(ϱ+1) complex matrix Z, the real number 𝗀(Z) = |det Z|² − |D_ϱ|², D_ϱ the
leading principal minor of order ϱ. 

### `pairingBoundSq`

 The number (ρ^(9n)/𝗀)^(10⁵n⁴)·d²/(deg·c_G·(𝗀/2)^(k−1)), with n = ϱ+1, k = |κ|,
c_G = `New.Shape.openCellConst` and 𝗀 = `New.RealPlace.gramDiscReal (New.realFrameGram y)`, taken
at the matrix of the products h(f_a, y f_b) of the frame with its image under y. It bounds a mass in
`New.BesselSystem.IsOpenCellBound`, where deg is the degree of a Bessel system, d the dimension of
its value space and ρ a bound on the lengths of frame vectors. 

### `besselOrbitalIntegral`

 The tensor J(γ) = ∫ W(y) ⊗ W(γ⁻¹y)^* dμ(y), with coordinates ∫ W_i(y) conj(W_j(γ⁻¹y)) dμ(y),
for a vector-valued function W on a group G. 

### `SchurLe`

 Schur's bound: for every integrable φ on G₁, the function γ ↦ ∫ φ(y)W(γy) dy has squared L²
norm on 𝖴∖G₁ (for the measure `New.besselQuotMeasure`) at most D·∫|φ|². 

### `RealPlace.HasSchurBound`

 There is a family `V w` of continuous vector-valued functions on `G₁`, `w` a word in pairs of
indices, with `V [] = W`, such that (a) each `V w` is a Bessel function of type `rep`: it is
bounded and transforms on the left by a fixed character under 𝖴 and by `rep` under U(κ); (b)
the `V ((a, b) :: w)` are the coefficients of the right derivative of `V w`: if a curve has
`γ' t = γ t * X`, then `(V w ∘ γ)' t = ∑ a b, X a b • V ((a, b) :: w) (γ t)`; (c) if `w` has length
at most `2 (ϱ + 1) ^ 2`, then `V w` satisfies `New.SchurLe` with constant
`B ^ (2 * w.length) * D`. 

### `normalizedOrbitalIntegral`

 The tensor J(w_p y) of `New.besselOrbitalIntegral` for W, times
√c_G · (|p_ϱ|² − 1)^((k−1)/2) · |I|⁻¹ · ∏_{a<ϱ} |p_a|^(k+1+2(ϱ−a−1)), where k = |κ| and
c_G = `New.Shape.openCellConst`. The Weyl element w_p = `weylElem ϱ p` maps e_a to p_a e*_a and e*_a
to e_a/p̄_a for a < ϱ, and fixes the other basis vectors. 

### `HasLocalBounds`

 Local bounds for given forms J_i(p, q) on tuples of nonzero complex numbers, with n = ϱ + 1 and
k = |κ|: (a) J is a system `New.SieveSystem`, in which each J_i (i < n) is the integral of J_(i+1)
over a common entry and the integrals twisted by the character e^(c − c̄) are bounded, with
|J_0| ≤ (1 + b^(−10))/deg, weights `New.headWeight` with exponent n⁶ that vanish unless the products
of the first entries have modulus at most b^(60n²), and decay Θ b^(2000n⁹) ℓ_{k−n⁷}(1/|ξ|), where
Θ = `New.Shape.scaledVolumeRatio` and ℓ_j(r) = r^j min(1, r)⁶ (`New.dampedPower`); (b) if σ fixes
the hyperbolic basis vectors (σ in `floorGroup ϱ`) and the last entries p_ϱ, q_ϱ both equal h(z,
σz), then J_n(p, q) is the inner product of the tensors `New.normalizedOrbitalIntegral` of W and σ
at p and at q. 

### `FullOrCut`

 The two functions of a Bessel system: the Bessel function W (`full`) and its cut W♭ (`cut`). 

### `FullOrCut.full`

 The Bessel function W itself. 

### `FullOrCut.cut`

 Its cut W♭. 

### `FullOrCut.select`

 The family that is `W` on the full side and `W'` on the cut side. 

### `IsDecayMajorant`

 `A` bounds the norms of both functions `W t` times a power, growing with `f`, of their scaled
length; the number `a` bounds `A`; and `a` times the integral of `A` over 𝖴∖G₁ is at most
`b ^ (400 r ^ 2)` times the dimension. Here r is the rank of G₁. 

### `IsDecayMajorant.measurable`

 The majorant is measurable. 

### `IsDecayMajorant.fixesLeft`

 The majorant is left invariant under the Bessel subgroup. 

### `IsDecayMajorant.pow_mul_enorm_le`

 `‖W t y‖` times the power `3 r ^ 3 f` of the scaled length of `y` is at most `A y`. 

### `IsDecayMajorant.le_bound`

 The majorant is bounded by `a`. 

### `IsDecayMajorant.bound_ne_top`

 The bound is finite. 

### `IsDecayMajorant.bound_mul_lintegral_le`

 The bound times the integral of the majorant over 𝖴∖G₁ is at most `b ^ (400 r ^ 2)` times
  the dimension. 

### `IsCutPair`

 A normalised Bessel function and its cut, for the type `rep`: they differ only where the length
exceeds half of `b ^ (6 n + 2)`, are close in L², and decay in the length at a rate given by `f`.
Here n = ϱ + 1 and r is the rank of G₁. 

### `IsCutPair.isBesselFunction`

 W and W♭ are Bessel functions. 

### `IsCutPair.integrable`

 W is integrable on 𝖴∖G₁. 

### `IsCutPair.lintegral_sq_full_eq`

 The squared L²(𝖴∖G₁) norm of W is the dimension of the value space. 

### `IsCutPair.lintegral_sq_cut_le`

 The squared L²(𝖴∖G₁) norm of W♭ is at most that dimension. 

### `IsCutPair.inCut_of_ne_zero`

 W♭ vanishes off `InCut`. 

### `IsCutPair.lintegral_sq_sub_le`

 The squared L²(𝖴∖G₁) distance of W♭ from W is at most `b ^ (-2 r ^ 3 f)` times the
  dimension. 

### `IsCutPair.lt_besselLength_of_ne`

 The cut changes W only where the length exceeds half the radius of `InCut`. 

### `IsCutPair.exists_majorant`

 W and W♭ decay in the length, below a common integrable majorant. 

 ### Bessel systems 

### `BesselSystem`

 A Bessel system: two continuous functions W (full) and W♭ (cut), given by the field `besselFn`,
from G₁ to π = ℂ^Index, where `Index` is a field, Bessel functions of an irreducible type rep. W
obeys the Schur bounds; W♭ has bounded support modulo the Bessel subgroup `besselS` and local
bounds; together they form a cut pair; and b is at least 8r. The size parameter b sets the radius of
the cut and the constants; f sets the rate of decay in `New.IsCutPair`; r is the rank of G₁. 

### `BesselSystem.Index`

 The index set of a basis of the value space π. 

### `BesselSystem.fintype`

 π is finite-dimensional. 

### `BesselSystem.nonempty`

 π is not zero. 

### `BesselSystem.besselFn`

 The Bessel function W (`.full`) and its cut W♭ (`.cut`), from G₁ to π. 

### `BesselSystem.deg`

 The degree; its inverse scales the constant in `New.RealPlace.HasSchurBound` and the bound of
  J_0 in `New.HasLocalBounds`. 

### `BesselSystem.deg_pos`

 The degree is positive. 

### `BesselSystem.rep`

 The type of the Bessel functions W and W♭. 

### `BesselSystem.isCutPair`

 W and W♭ form a cut pair: L² bounds, the support of W♭ and decay below a common majorant. 

### `BesselSystem.irreducible`

 Every matrix that commutes with all rep(h) is scalar. 

### `BesselSystem.hasSchurBound`

 For every P ≥ b^5, W satisfies `New.RealPlace.HasSchurBound` with constants P and
  |Index|/deg. 

### `BesselSystem.eight_mul_rank_le`

 The parameter b is at least 8r, with r the rank of G₁. 

### `BesselSystem.bandLimit`

 The forms J of the local bounds of W♭. 

### `BesselSystem.hasLocalBounds`

 W♭ has local bounds.
