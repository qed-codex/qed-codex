# An average on polynomials in the entries of a matrix

Let `L` be a Levi subgroup of `GL_k`, `W` a local ring with large finite residue field, and
`ι : W → O` a ring map to a valuation domain of characteristic `0` in which the prime `p` is not a
unit. Let `ρ ∈ ℤ ^ k` weigh the indices, and call a monomial in the matrix entries *bad* if its row
weight exceeds a threshold. The file constructs an `O`-linear form `I` on polynomials in the matrix
entries (`New.Average.exists_isAverage`) that is invariant under conjugation by `L(W)`, vanishes
on bad monomials, and agrees modulo an ideal `J` with a power of `p` times evaluation at the matrix
`1` on every polynomial of small degree whose conjugates have no bad monomial modulo `J`
(`New.Average.IsAverage`, `New.Average.evalAtOne`).

* `New.Average.exists_isAdjointWeight`: the multinomial coefficients of the monomials of
  degree `< p ^ f` in `m` variables divide `p ^ (m f)` in `O`, and the quotients make the
  substitutions by a matrix and by its transpose adjoint.
* `New.Average.eq_top_of_schurMap_eq_leviMonomialValues`: the points of `L(W)` span the Schur
  algebra of `L` of degree `d`, which acts on `U = (O ^ k)^{⊗ d}`.
* `New.Average.dualOperator`: with the quotients above, a polynomial `ψ` gives an element `ψ♯`
  of the Schur algebra. For `d < p ^ f` its trace on `U` is the scaled value at `1` of the part of
  degree `d` of `ψ`, and conjugation of `ψ` becomes conjugation of `ψ♯`.
* `New.Average.averagePart`: in degree `d ≤ D` the form `I` is the trace of `ψ♯` on `U` minus its
  trace on the submodule `U₊` (`New.Average.largeWeightSubmodule`) generated by the basis tensors of
  large weight.

All `k ^ 2` entries are variables; a monomial with an entry outside the blocks has `ψ♯ = 0`.
The row weight of a monomial `α` is `∑ α (i, j) • ρ i`; the column weight has `ρ j` in place of
`ρ i`. The Schur algebra is built on the transpose of the matrix of variables, so the row indices
of a monomial are the column indices of `ψ♯`, and no transpose appears in the rule for conjugation.
Transposing the variables gives the same statements with column weights, since `L(W)` is closed
under transposition.

Notions from other files:
* `New.linSubst z`: for an `m × σ` matrix `z` over `R`, the `R`-algebra map from polynomials in
  variables indexed by `σ` to those indexed by `m` that sends `X b` to `∑ t, z t b • X t`.
* `New.levi W τ`: the Levi subgroup of a labelling `τ` of the indices, made of the matrices in
  `GL_k(W)` whose entry `(i, j)` vanishes whenever `τ i ≠ τ j`.
* `New.kroneckerPi A`: the Kronecker product of a family of matrices `A s`; its entry at `(β, α)`
  is `∏ s, A s (β s) (α s)`.
* `New.fockPairing`: the bilinear form on polynomials for which monomials are orthogonal and
  `⟨x ^ α, x ^ α⟩ = α!`.

The average serves the map `Av` of `New.UpperBound.ElementaryDivisors.AveragingMap`.

## Relation to the literature

Known result: the Schur algebra, and that group elements span it in degrees below the size of the
field. Here this is proved for Levi subgroups over local rings, in degrees `d` with `d + k` at most
the size of the residue field. Source: Green, Polynomial representations of GL_n, 1980; Benson–Doty,
Schur–Weyl duality over finite fields, 2009.


 ### Multinomial coefficients and adjoint substitutions 

### `cast_choose_dvd_pow`

 Let the prime `p` be a non-unit of the valuation domain `O`. A binomial coefficient with upper
index `< p ^ f` divides `p ^ f` in `O`. 

### `cast_multinomial_dvd_pow`

 Let the prime `p` be a non-unit of the valuation domain `O`. A multinomial coefficient over `s`
of total degree `< p ^ f` divides `p ^ (#s * f)` in `O`. 

### `IsAdjointWeight`

 Give the polynomials in the variables `σ` the bilinear form for which monomials are orthogonal
and `⟨x ^ α, x ^ α⟩ = η α`. The weights `η` are adjoint weights if, for every matrix `A`, the linear
substitutions of the variables (`linSubst`) by `A` and by `Aᵀ` are adjoint for this form. 

### `prod_factorial_mul_coeff_linSubst`

 The linear substitutions (`linSubst`) of the variables `σ` by a matrix `A` and by `Aᵀ` have
matrices in the monomial basis that are transposes of each other up to the factors
`α! = ∏ i, (α i)!`. This is their adjointness for `fockPairing`, the bilinear form with
`⟨x ^ α, x ^ α⟩ = α!`, written out for two monomials. 

### `coeff_linSubst_monomial_eq_zero`

 A linear substitution of the variables preserves the degree: the image of the monomial `δ` has
no monomial `γ` of another degree. 

### `exists_isAdjointWeight`

 Let `O` be a valuation domain of characteristic `0` in which the prime `p` is not a unit. There
are weights `η γ ∈ O` of the monomials in the variables `σ`, with
`p ^ (#σ * f) = multinomial γ * η γ` in degree `< p ^ f`, such that the symmetric bilinear form with
the diagonal values `η γ` is invariant in the sense that linear substitutions by a matrix and by its
transpose are adjoint for it (`IsAdjointWeight`). 

 ### Conjugation and averages 

### `conjSubst`

 Conjugation of the generic matrix: the `O`-algebra map that substitutes `ι(a⁻¹) * X * ι(a)` for
`X`, so `X (i, j) ↦ ∑ s t, ι (a⁻¹ i s) * ι (a t j) • X (s, t)`. It is written as the linear
substitution `New.linSubst` by the Kronecker product of `ι(a⁻¹)ᵀ` and `ι(a)`, whose entry at
`((s, t), (i, j))` is that coefficient. 

### `conjSubst_diagGL_X`

 Conjugation (`conjSubst`) by the diagonal matrix `diagGL u` with unit entries `u i` scales the
variable `X (i, j)` by `ι ((u i)⁻¹ * u j)`. 

### `evalAtOne`

 The form `ψ ↦ c * ψ(1)`: evaluation at the identity matrix, times `c`. 

### `evalAtOne_apply`

 The form `evalAtOne c` sends `ψ` to `c` times the value of `ψ` at the identity matrix. 

### `evalAtOne_conjSubst`

 Let `ι : W → O` be a map of commutative rings and `a ∈ GL_k(W)`. The substitution
`conjSubst ι a : X ↦ ι(a⁻¹) * X * ι(a)` of the matrix of variables does not change
`evalAtOne c ψ = c * ψ(1)`, because conjugation fixes the identity matrix. 

### `prod_zpow_eq_zpow_sum`

 A product of integer powers of one element of a commutative group adds the exponents. 

### `conjSubst_diagGL_zpow_monomial`

 Let `ι : W → O` be a map of commutative rings, `u` a unit of `W`, and `a` the diagonal matrix
with entries `u ^ ρ i`. The substitution `conjSubst ι a : X ↦ ι(a⁻¹) * X * ι(a)` of the matrix of
variables multiplies the monomial `α` by `ι u ^ e`, where `e` is the column weight
`∑ α (i, j) • ρ j` less the row weight `∑ α (i, j) • ρ i`. 

### `apply_monomial_eq_zero_of_weight_ne`

 Let `ι : W → O` be a ring map from a local ring to a domain of characteristic `0` in which
`p > 0` is not a unit. Let `levi W τ` be the group of the `a ∈ GL_k(W)` with `a i j = 0` whenever
the labels `τ i` and `τ j` differ, and let the linear form `I` on polynomials in the entries of a
matrix `X` be invariant under the substitutions `conjSubst ι a : X ↦ ι(a⁻¹) * X * ι(a)` for `a` in
this group. Then `I` vanishes on the monomials `α` whose row weight `∑ α (i, j) • ρ i` and column
weight `∑ α (i, j) • ρ j` differ. 

### `IsAverage`

 The linear form `I` on polynomials over `O` in the entries of a `k × k` matrix `X` is an average
for the Levi subgroup `L(W) = levi W τ` of the `a ∈ GL_k(W)` with `a i j = 0` whenever `τ i ≠ τ j`,
which acts by the substitutions `conjSubst ι a : X ↦ ι(a⁻¹) * X * ι(a)`. A monomial `α` is bad if
its row weight `∑ α (i, j) • ρ i` exceeds `t`; `c` scales the evaluation at the matrix `1`, and `D`
bounds the degree. If `W` is local and `O` is a domain of characteristic `0` in which some `p > 0`
is not a unit, then by invariance `I` also vanishes on the monomials whose row weight and column
weight differ (`apply_monomial_eq_zero_of_weight_ne`). 

### `IsAverage.map_conjSubst`

 `I` is invariant under conjugation by `L(W)`. 

### `IsAverage.map_monomial_eq_zero`

 `I` vanishes on bad monomials. 

### `IsAverage.sub_evalAtOne_mem`

 If `ψ` has degree at most `D` and the bad monomials of all its conjugates have coefficients
  in the ideal `J`, then `I ψ ≡ c * ψ(1)` modulo `J`. 

### `map_C_mul_mul_C`

 An `O`-linear form `l` on polynomials, applied entry by entry to a matrix `X`, commutes with
multiplication by constant matrices `A` and `B`. 

### `eq_top_of_forall_linearMap_residueField_eq_zero`

 A form of Nakayama's lemma: a submodule `V` of a finite module `M` over a local ring `W` is
everything if every linear map from `M ⧸ V` to the residue field is zero. 

### `eq_top_of_eval_monomial`

 Zariski density over local rings. Let the polynomial `g` over the local ring `W` be homogeneous
of degree `e` and take a unit value at some point `b₀`, and let `n + e ≤ Nat.card` of the residue
field of `W`. Let `γ` run over `Monomials σ n`, the exponent vectors of degree `n`, and let
`ι : W → O` be a ring map. Then the vectors `γ ↦ ι (b ^ γ)`, at the points `b` where `g (b)` is a
unit, span `Monomials σ n → O`: a submodule `V` that contains them is everything. 

### `leviGeneric`

 The generic block matrix of the labelling `τ` of the indices, transposed: the entry at `(i, j)`
is the variable `X (j, i)` if `τ i = τ j` and `0` otherwise. This is the index order of
`Matrix.vec`. 

### `leviTensor`

 The `d`-th tensor power of the generic block matrix `leviGeneric O τ`. 

### `schurMap`

 The map onto the Schur algebra of degree `d`: a linear form, given by its values on the
monomials of degree `d`, is applied to the entries of `leviTensor O τ d`. 

### `leviMonomialValues`

 The values of the monomials of degree `d` at the point `a` of the Levi subgroup, mapped to `O`
by `ι`. The variable `X (j, i)` takes the value `a i j`, as in `Matrix.vec`. 

### `scaledCoeff`

 The coefficients of degree `d` of a polynomial, scaled by `η`: `ψ ↦ (γ ↦ coeff γ ψ * η γ)`. 

### `dualOperator`

 Polynomials as operators: the element `ψ♯` of the Schur algebra of degree `d` dual to the
polynomial `ψ`, a matrix that acts on `(O ^ k)^{⊗ d}`. For multi-indices `i, j : Fin d → Fin k` its
entry at `(i, j)` is `η α` times the coefficient in `ψ` of the monomial `α = ∏ l, X (j l, i l)` if
`τ (i l) = τ (j l)` for all `l`, and `0` otherwise. Only the part of degree `d` of `ψ` matters. 

### `tensorOrbit`

 The map that sends `c` to the image of the basis tensor with indices `j` under the element
`schurMap O τ d c` of the Schur algebra. 

### `largeWeightSubmodule`

 The submodule `U₊` of the `d`-th tensor power of `O ^ k` that is generated under the Schur
algebra by the basis tensors `j` whose weight `∑ l, ρ (j l)` exceeds `t`. 

### `finite_largeWeightSubmodule`

 `largeWeightSubmodule` is a finite sum of images of finite modules. 

### `schurMap_mulVec_single_mem_largeWeightSubmodule`

 An element `schurMap O τ d c` of the Schur algebra maps a basis tensor `j` of weight
`∑ l, ρ (j l) > t` into `largeWeightSubmodule O τ ρ t d`, which such images generate. 

### `leviTensor_apply`

 The entry `(i, j)` of `leviTensor O τ d`, the `d`-th tensor power of the generic block matrix,
is the monomial `∏ l, X (j l, i l)` if `τ (i l) = τ (j l)` for all `l`, and zero otherwise. 

### `weight_eq_of_coeff_leviTensor_ne_zero`

 If `α` has a nonzero coefficient in the entry `(i, j)` of the tensor power `leviTensor O τ d` of
the generic block matrix, then the weight of `α` under `w` is `∑ l, w (j l, i l)`. 

### `schurMap_apply_monomial`

 Let `T = leviTensor O τ d` be the `d`-th tensor power of the generic block matrix of `τ`, a
matrix of polynomials. The linear map `schurMap O τ d` sends the basis vector at a monomial `γ` of
degree `d` to the matrix of the coefficients of `γ` in the entries of `T`. It sends the vector of
values of a linear form `l` on these monomials to `T` with `l` applied to each entry. 

### `schurMap_aeval`

 Evaluation at `ι ∘ b` goes, in the Schur algebra, to the `d`-th tensor power of the generic
block matrix `leviGeneric W τ` evaluated at `b` and mapped to `O` by `ι`. 

### `leviGeneric_map_eval`

 The generic block matrix evaluated at a point `a` of the Levi subgroup is `a`. 

### `schurMap_leviMonomialValues`

 The image in the Schur algebra of a point `a` of the Levi subgroup (`leviMonomialValues` lists
the values of the monomials at `a`) is the `d`-th tensor power of `ι(a)`. 

### `dualOperator_monomial`

 The element `dualOperator τ η d (monomial α 1)` of the Schur algebra dual to the monomial `α`
has as entries the coefficients of `α` in the entries of the tensor power `leviTensor O τ d` of the
generic block matrix, times `η α`. 

### `dualOperator_monomial_mulVec_single_eq_zero`

 The operator `dualOperator τ η d (monomial α 1)` dual to the monomial `α` kills the basis tensor
`Pi.single j 1` unless the weight `∑ l, ρ (j l)` of `j` equals the weight of `α`. Here the weight of
`α` is `weight (fun v ↦ ρ v.1) α`. 

### `trace_leviTensor`

 The trace of the `d`-th tensor power of the generic block matrix is the `d`-th power of the sum
of the diagonal variables, written here as the linear form with coefficients the matrix `1`. 

### `trace_dualOperator_monomial`

 The matrix `dualOperator τ η d (monomial α 1)`, indexed by multi-indices `i, j : Fin d → Fin k`,
has the entry `η α` where `α` is the monomial `∏ l, X (j l, i l)` and `τ (i l) = τ (j l)` for all
`l`, and `0` elsewhere. If `α` has degree `d`, its trace is the multinomial coefficient of `α` times
`η α` times the value of the monomial at the matrix `1`. Otherwise it is zero. 

### `ite_inv_mul_eq_of_mem_levi`

 For `a` in the Levi subgroup, the entries `a⁻¹ s j` and `a i t` vanish off the blocks, so on
their product the conditions `τ i = τ j` and `τ t = τ s` agree. 

### `leviGeneric_map_linSubst_transpose`

 Let `a ∈ GL_k(W)` lie in the Levi subgroup `levi W τ`. Let `G = leviGeneric O τ` be the matrix
with the variable `X (j, i)` at `(i, j)` if `τ i = τ j` and `0` otherwise. The linear substitution
of the variables (`linSubst`) by the transpose of the Kronecker product of `ι(a⁻¹)ᵀ` and `ι(a)`
takes `G` to `ι(a) * G * ι(a⁻¹)`. 

### `dualOperator_conjSubst`

 Let `ψ♯ = dualOperator τ η d ψ` be the matrix on `(O ^ k)^{⊗ d}` whose entry at the
multi-indices `(i, j)` is `η α` times the coefficient in `ψ` of `α = ∏ l, X (j l, i l)` if
`τ (i l) = τ (j l)` for all `l`, and `0` otherwise. Assume `IsAdjointWeight η`: the linear
substitutions by a matrix and by its transpose are adjoint for the diagonal form with
`⟨x ^ α, x ^ α⟩ = η α`. Let `a ∈ GL_k(W)` have `a i j = 0` whenever `τ i ≠ τ j`. Then the
substitution `conjSubst ι a : X ↦ ι(a⁻¹) * X * ι(a)` in `ψ` conjugates `ψ♯` by
`schurMap O τ d (leviMonomialValues τ ι d a)`, which is the tensor power `ι(a)^{⊗ d}`. 

### `schurMap_leviMonomialValues_mul`

 The product of two points of the Levi subgroup goes to the product of their images in the
Schur algebra of degree `d`, which are the tensor powers of their images under `ι`. 

### `schurMap_leviMonomialValues_inv_mul`

 The images of `a⁻¹` and `a` in the Schur algebra of degree `d` are inverse matrices. 

### `isHomogeneous_det_leviGeneric`

 The determinant of the generic block matrix is homogeneous of degree `k`. 

### `eq_top_of_schurMap_eq_leviMonomialValues`

 The points of the Levi subgroup `levi W τ` span the Schur algebra of degree `d`, if the residue
field of `W` is finite with at least `d + k` elements. A submodule `V` is `⊤` as soon as it contains
every `c` with the same `schurMap O τ d` image as some `leviMonomialValues τ ι d a`. 

### `largeWeightSubmodule_le_of_forall_leviMonomialValues`

 Let `levi W τ` be the group of the `a ∈ GL_k(W)` with `a i j = 0` whenever `τ i ≠ τ j`; the
matrix `schurMap O τ d (leviMonomialValues τ ι d a)` is the tensor power `ι(a)^{⊗ d}`. Let
`U₊ = largeWeightSubmodule O τ ρ t d ⊆ (O ^ k)^{⊗ d}` be generated, under the Schur algebra of this
group, by the basis tensors `j` with `∑ l, ρ (j l) > t`. If the residue field of `W` is finite with
at least `d + k` elements, the images of these tensors under the `ι(a)^{⊗ d}` already span `U₊`: it
lies in every submodule `N` that contains them. 

### `schurMap_mem_largeWeightSubmodule`

 The matrices `schurMap O τ d c`, for vectors `c` indexed by the monomials of degree `d` in the
entries of a `k × k` matrix, form the Schur algebra of the Levi subgroup whose blocks are the fibres
of `τ`. The submodule `largeWeightSubmodule O τ ρ t d` of `(O ^ k)^{⊗ d}` is spanned by the images
under these matrices of the basis tensors `j` with `∑ l, ρ (j l) > t`. If the residue field of `W`
is finite with at least `d + k` elements, each of these matrices keeps the submodule. 

### `largeWeightRep`

 The action of the Schur algebra on `U₊ = largeWeightSubmodule O τ ρ t d`, the submodule of the
`d`-th tensor power of `O ^ k` that it generates from the basis tensors `j` of weight
`∑ l, ρ (j l) > t`. The vector `c` acts by the restriction of the matrix `schurMap O τ d c`; the
ring map `ι` and the bound `hW` serve only to show that this matrix keeps `U₊`. 

### `trace_mem_of_forall_mem_smul_top`

 An endomorphism of a finite free module with values in `J` times the module has its trace in
the ideal `J`. 

### `largeWeightTrace`

 The trace on `U₊ = largeWeightSubmodule O τ ρ t d` of the operator `ψ♯ = dualOperator τ η d ψ`,
which keeps `U₊` because it lies in the Schur algebra (`schurMap_mem_largeWeightSubmodule`). 

### `largeWeightTrace_apply`

 `largeWeightTrace` at `ψ` is the trace on `U₊ = largeWeightSubmodule O τ ρ t d` of the action
(`largeWeightRep`) of the vector `scaledCoeff η d ψ`, whose entries are the coefficients of degree
`d` of `ψ` times the weights `η`. 

### `averagePart`

 The part of degree `d` of the average: the trace of `ψ♯ = dualOperator τ η d ψ` on the whole
tensor power `(O ^ k)^{⊗ d}` minus its trace on `U₊ = largeWeightSubmodule O τ ρ t d`. 

### `averagePart_conjSubst`

 Let the residue field of `W` be finite with at least `d + k` elements, and let the substitutions
by a matrix and by its transpose be adjoint for the diagonal form `⟨x ^ α, x ^ α⟩ = η α`
(`IsAdjointWeight`). Then `averagePart` does not change under the substitution
`conjSubst ι a : X ↦ ι(a⁻¹) * X * ι(a)`, for `a ∈ GL_k(W)` with `a i j = 0` whenever `τ i ≠ τ j`. 

### `range_dualOperator_monomial_le`

 If the row weight `∑ α (r, s) • ρ r` of the monomial `α` exceeds `t`, then the operator
`dualOperator τ η d (monomial α 1)` dual to `α` maps the whole `d`-th tensor power of `O ^ k` into
`largeWeightSubmodule O τ ρ t d`. 

### `dualOperator_mulVec_single_mem_smul`

 Let `ψ♯ = dualOperator τ η d ψ` be the matrix on `U = (O ^ k)^{⊗ d}` whose entry at `(i, j)` is
`η α` times the coefficient in `ψ` of `α = ∏ l, X (j l, i l)` if `τ (i l) = τ (j l)` for all `l`,
and `0` otherwise. Let `U₊ = largeWeightSubmodule O τ ρ t d ⊆ U` be generated under the Schur
algebra by the basis tensors `j` of weight `∑ l, ρ (j l) > t`. If the coefficients in `ψ` of the
monomials `α` of row weight `∑ α (r, s) • ρ r > t` lie in the ideal `J`, then `ψ♯` maps every basis
tensor of weight `> t` into `J U₊`. 

### `dualOperator_mulVec_mem_smul`

 Let `ψ♯ = dualOperator τ η d ψ` be the matrix on `U = (O ^ k)^{⊗ d}` whose entry at `(i, j)` is
`η α` times the coefficient in `ψ` of `α = ∏ l, X (j l, i l)` if `τ (i l) = τ (j l)` for all `l`,
and `0` otherwise. Let `U₊ = largeWeightSubmodule O τ ρ t d ⊆ U` be generated under the Schur
algebra by the basis tensors `j` with `∑ l, ρ (j l) > t`. Let the residue field of `W` be finite
with at least `d + k` elements, and let the substitutions by a matrix and by its transpose be
adjoint for the diagonal form `⟨x ^ α, x ^ α⟩ = η α` (`IsAdjointWeight`). Suppose that in all
conjugates `conjSubst ι a ψ = ψ(ι(a⁻¹) * X * ι(a))`, for `a ∈ GL_k(W)` with `a i j = 0` whenever
`τ i ≠ τ j`, the monomials `α` of row weight `∑ α (r, s) • ρ r > t` have their coefficients in the
ideal `J`. Then `ψ♯` maps `U₊` into `J U₊`. 

### `free_largeWeightSubmodule`

 Let `O` be a valuation domain. The submodule `largeWeightSubmodule O τ ρ t d` of the `d`-th
tensor power of `O ^ k`, generated under the Schur algebra by the basis tensors `j` of weight
`∑ l, ρ (j l) > t`, is finitely generated and torsion-free, hence free. 

### `averagePart_monomial_eq_zero_of_lt`

 Let `ψ♯ = dualOperator τ η d ψ` be the matrix on `U = (O ^ k)^{⊗ d}` whose entry at `(i, j)` is
`η α` times the coefficient in `ψ` of `α = ∏ l, X (j l, i l)` if `τ (i l) = τ (j l)` for all `l`,
and `0` otherwise. Let `U₊ ⊆ U` be generated under the Schur algebra by the basis tensors `j` with
`∑ l, ρ (j l) > t`; `averagePart` sends `ψ` to the trace of `ψ♯` on `U` minus its trace on `U₊`.
Let `O` be a valuation domain and let the residue field of `W` be finite with at least `d + k`
elements. Then `averagePart` vanishes on a monomial `α` of row weight `∑ α (r, s) • ρ r > t`,
because `α♯` has its image in `U₊`. 

### `largeWeightTrace_mem`

 Let `ψ♯` be the matrix on `U = (O ^ k)^{⊗ d}` whose entry at `(i, j)` is `η α` times the
coefficient in `ψ` of `α = ∏ l, X (j l, i l)` if `τ (i l) = τ (j l)` for all `l`, and `0` otherwise.
Let `U₊ ⊆ U` be generated under the Schur algebra by the basis tensors `j` with `∑ l, ρ (j l) > t`.
Let the residue field of `W` be finite with at least `d + k` elements; then `ψ♯` keeps `U₊`, and
`largeWeightTrace` sends `ψ` to its trace there. Let `O` be a valuation domain, and let the
substitutions by a matrix and by its transpose be adjoint for the diagonal form
`⟨x ^ α, x ^ α⟩ = η α` (`IsAdjointWeight`). Suppose that in all conjugates
`conjSubst ι a ψ = ψ(ι(a⁻¹) * X * ι(a))`, for `a ∈ GL_k(W)` with `a i j = 0` whenever `τ i ≠ τ j`,
the monomials `α` of row weight `∑ α (r, s) • ρ r > t` have their coefficients in the ideal `J`.
Then this trace lies in `J`. 

### `sum_trace_dualOperator`

 The matrix `dualOperator τ η d ψ` on `(O ^ k)^{⊗ d}` has at `(i, j)` the entry `η α` times the
coefficient in `ψ` of `α = ∏ l, X (j l, i l)` if `τ (i l) = τ (j l)` for all `l`, and `0` otherwise.
If `c = multinomial α * η α` for all monomials `α` of degree at most `D` and `ψ` has degree at most
`D`, then the traces of these matrices for `d ≤ D` add up to `evalAtOne c ψ`, which is `c` times the
value of `ψ` at the matrix `1`. 

### `exists_isAverage`

 Let `O` be a valuation domain of characteristic `0` in which the prime `p` is not a unit, let
`ι : W → O` be a ring map from a local ring whose residue field is finite with at least `D + k`
elements, and let `D < p ^ f`. The matrices `a ∈ GL_k(W)` with `a i j = 0` whenever `τ i ≠ τ j` act
on polynomials in the entries of a `k × k` matrix `X` by the conjugation `X ↦ ι(a⁻¹) * X * ι(a)`.
For all weights `ρ` of the indices and every threshold `t` there is a linear form `I` on these
polynomials that is invariant under conjugation, vanishes on the monomials `α` of row weight
`∑ α (i, j) • ρ i > t`, and agrees modulo an ideal `J` with `p ^ (k² f) ψ(1)` on every `ψ` of degree
at most `D` whose conjugates have the coefficients of such monomials in `J` (`IsAverage`).
