# Integrality of jets of theta products and stability of standard data

This file proves two facts. Let `μ_i` be linear forms that are `d` times integral forms, and let `y`
be the parameter described below. Then the derivatives at `0` of a product of series `θ(μ_i z)`,
times an integral series in the `y(μ_i z)`, are integral.
Standard data of level `δ` are stable under the Heisenberg operators of finite points that `δ`
makes integral above `p`, and under the Weil operators of finite points that are integral above
`p`. An element of `ℂ_[p]` is integral if its norm is at most `1`.

* `New.jet`: the pairing `(Q(∂) F)(0)` of a polynomial `Q` with a power series `F`.
* `New.taylorVia ιₚ C.θ`: the Taylor series at `0` of the function `θ` of the CM lattice `C`, with
  coefficients sent to `ℂ_[p]` by `ιₚ`; and `New.linearSeries c`, the linear form `∑ c_x z_x`.
* `New.CMLattice.multiSeriesRing`: the integral power series in the `y(μ_i z)`, where the parameter
  `y` is the Taylor series of `C.translatedY`.
* `New.Parameters.integralAfter δ S`: the finite adelic vectors `x` with `δ x` integral at the
  places in `S`.
* `New.Parameters.standardData V δ`: the module over `New.embValRing` spanned by the translates of
  the integral vacuum by the points that `δ` makes integral above `p`.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.CMLattice.jetRing`: the ring generated by the integral series in the `y(μ_i z)` and by the
  `θ(μ_i z)` and `(-log θ)'(μ_i z)`.
* `New.Parameters.finHeisenbergOp_mem_standardData`: standard data of level `δ` are stable under the
  Heisenberg operators of finite points that `δ` makes integral above `p`.
* `New.Parameters.omega_mem_standardData`: they are stable under the Weil operators of finite
  points integral above `p`.



## Integrality of the derivatives of products of copies of `θ`

The series of `New.CMLattice.norm_jet_thetaProd_pow_mul_le_one` lies in the ring
`New.CMLattice.jetRing`.


### `norm_jet_le_one_of_pderiv_mem`

 Let `S` be a ring of power series that is preserved by partial derivatives and whose elements
have integral constant terms, and let `φ : R →+* ℂ_[p]` have integral values. Then the jet
`(φ(Q)(∂) F)(0)` is integral for `F` in `S` and every polynomial `Q` over `R`. 

### `smul_derivative_derivative_negLogTheta_mem`

 Let `C` be a CM lattice for a prime `d ≡ 7` modulo `8`, with `2`-torsion point `t`, and fix a
`p`-adic place `ιₚ` of `ℂ`. Let `θ` be the twisted translate of the sigma function of `C` by `t`,
normalised to `θ(0) = 1`, and let `g = -log θ` (`negLogTheta`), a power series over `ℂ_[p]`. Let `y`
be the Taylor series at `0`, read in `ℂ_[p]` through `ιₚ`, of the function `C.translatedY`. Then
`d g''` lies in `integralSeriesRing`, the ring of power series in `y` with coefficients of norm at
most `1`. 

### `jetRing`

 For a family of linear forms `μ_i`, the ring generated by `multiSeriesRing`, the integral power
series in the `y(μ_i z)`, and by the `θ(μ_i z)` and `g'(μ_i z)`, where `g = -log θ`. Here `y` is the
Taylor series of `C.translatedY`, the function `(℘'(z + t) - a₁ (℘(z + t) - ℘(t))) / 2` for the
`2`-torsion point `t` of `C` and the square root `a₁` of `12 ℘(t)`. 

### `mem_jetRing_of_mem_multiSeriesRing`

 The integral series in the `y(μ_i z)` lie in `jetRing`. 

### `theta_mem_jetRing`

 The series `θ(μ_i z)` lies in `jetRing`. 

### `derivative_negLogTheta_mem_jetRing`

 The series `g'(μ_i z)`, where `g = -log θ`, lies in `jetRing`. 

### `smul_mem_jetRing`

 The ring `jetRing` is stable under multiplication by integral constants. 

### `thetaProd_mem_jetRing`

 The product `∏ θ(μ_i z) ^ m i` lies in `jetRing`. 

### `norm_constantCoeff_le_of_mem_jetRing`

 Let `C` be a CM lattice, and let `θ` and `y` be the Taylor series at `0` of `C.θ` and of
`C.translatedY`, read in `ℂ_[p]` through `ιₚ`. Let `μ_i z = ∑ μ i x * z_x` be linear forms, for
`i : I` and `x : X`. The ring `C.jetRing μ` is generated by the integral power series in the
`y(μ_i z)` and by the `θ(μ_i z)` and `(-log θ)'(μ_i z)`. Its elements have constant term of norm at
most `1`, because `y(0) = 0`, `θ(0) = 1` and `(-log θ)'(0) = 0`. 

### `pderiv_mem_jetRing`

 Let `C` be a CM lattice for a prime `d ≡ 7` modulo `8`, with `2`-torsion point `t`. Let the
linear forms `μ_i = d ν_i` be `d` times forms `ν_i` with coefficients of norm at most `1`. If `F`
lies in `jetRing`, then so does each partial derivative `pderiv x F`. 

### `norm_jet_thetaProd_pow_mul_le_one`

 Let `C` be a CM lattice for a prime `d ≡ 7` modulo `8`, with `2`-torsion point `t`. Let `θ` and
`y` be the Taylor series at `0`, read in `ℂ_[p]` through `ιₚ`, of `C.θ` and of `C.translatedY`. Let
the linear forms `μ_i = d ν_i` be `d` times forms `ν_i` with integral coefficients, and let `H` be
an integral series in the `y(μ_i z)`. If `φ : R →+* ℂ_[p]` has integral values, then the jet
`(φ(Q)(∂) F)(0)` of `F = (∏ θ(μ_i z) ^ m i) ^ N * H` is integral for every polynomial `Q` over
`R`. 

 ## Stability of standard data 

### `integralAfter.stdLattice_le`

 The standard lattice `Ôⁿ` is contained in `integralAfter δ S`: its vectors stay integral at the
places of `S` after multiplication by the integer `δ`. 

### `smul_mem_standardData_of_norm_le_one`

 Standard data are a submodule of the space `𝒮 V` of the Weil representation of the hermitian
space `V` over `embValRing`, the ring of complex numbers whose image under `ιₚ` has norm at most
`1`. So they are stable under multiplication by such numbers. 

### `finHeisenbergOp_intVacuum_mem_standardData`

 Let `V` be a hermitian space over the cyclotomic extension `P.E s` of the imaginary quadratic
field `P.K`, and let `δ` be an integer of `P.K`. The integral vacuum `intVacuum V` is the vacuum
vector of `𝒮 V` times the power `vacuumScale ^ card n` of a fixed nonzero scalar. Its translate by
the Heisenberg operator of a finite adelic vector `x` with `δ x` integral above `p` is one of the
generators of the standard data of level `δ`. 

### `finHeisenbergOp_mem_standardData`

 The standard data `standardData V δ` are the span of the translates of the integral vacuum by
the points that `δ` makes integral above `p`. The integral vacuum is the vacuum of `𝒮 V` times
`vacuumScale ^ card n`. These data are stable under the Heisenberg operator `V.finHeisenbergOp x` of
a finite adelic vector `x` such that `δ x` is integral at the places above `p`. 

### `mulVec_mem_integralAfter`

 Let `g` be a finite unitary point in `integralAbove n p`, that is, integral at the places above
`p`. If `δ x` is integral above `p`, then so is `δ g x`. 

### `omega_finHeisenbergOp_intVacuum`

 The Weil operator `ω(1, g)` takes the translate by `x` of the integral vacuum `intVacuum V` to
`(finMultiplier g⁻¹)⁻¹` times the translate by `g x` of the sum of the translates of the integral
vacuum by the `g y`, where `y` runs over integral vectors modulo those that `g` keeps integral. Here
`finMultiplier` is the finite part of the scalar factor `weilMultiplier` in the definition of the
Weil operators. 

### `omega_mem_standardData`

 Standard data of level `δ` are stable under the Weil operator `ω(1, g)` of a finite unitary
point `g` in `integralAbove n p`.
