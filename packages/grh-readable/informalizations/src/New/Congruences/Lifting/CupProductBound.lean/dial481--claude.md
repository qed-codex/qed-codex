# Sides of a pairing, cokernel killers and the pairing lemma for cup products

A side `New.Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide` consists of a lattice Y, a lattice C (`paramLattice`) and
an O-linear map α (`cocycleMap`) from C to the crystalline cocycles Z_f(Y)
(`New.Period.crysCocycles`). These cocycles define an extension of the trivial lattice on
`S.paramLattice` by Y. All lattices are tensored with W(C^♭), on which ℤ × Γ acts with φ the
Frobenius, and `P` is a pairing of the lattices of two sides modulo p^m. The pairing lemma shows
that, for cocycles that pair safely, safe moves from (φx, φx') to (x, x') make a multiple `p ^ e` of
their cup product, read in ℚ̄_p^×, a coboundary.

On a Kummer chart let z be the coordinate, E a generator of ker θ and N the Kisin module. The ideal
J_l(i) is generated by a modulus r and by the multipliers s ∈ A_inf for which s times each constant
(the letter-i part of 1 ⊗ c, c ∈ C) lifts to the extension inside A_inf·φ^{l*}N. Elements of the
form p^κ E^s and z^a are shown to lie in it.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.Lift.Pairing.IsSafe`: a pair is safe if `p ^ e` times its pairing is small, that is, lies in
  the image `New.Lift.smallPart` of W(𝔪^♭).
* `New.Lift.Pairing.IsScaledCupCoboundary`: the cup product of the cocycles `S.cocycleMap c` and
  `p ^ e • S'.cocycleMap c'` is a coboundary.
* `New.Lift.Pairing.IsScaledCupCoboundary.of_isSafePath`: the pairing lemma, which derives this from
  a path of safe moves.
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.liftIdeal`, `New.Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.KillsCokernel`:
  the ideal J_l(i); p^κ E^s lies in J_0(i) with r = 0.
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.exists_z_pow_mem_liftIdeal`: z^a ∈ J_0(i) with r = p^{κ+1} and
  (p − 1)a ≤ e(ω + κ), where the jumps are ≤ ω and e is the ramification index.
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.killsCokernel_of_isUnramified`: on an unramified chart with p ≥
  5, p^s E^t ∈ J_0(i), r = 0, when the jumps are ≤ (p − 3)(s + t).

Notions from other files:
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.Coeffs`: a ring of coefficients O, a finite free Z_p-algebra with an element
  ζ such that ζ^{p^f−1} = 1; ζ cuts out the letters.
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice`: a finite free O-module with an action of Γ, crystalline over
  the subgroup Γ' of the chart, with a finite set `jumpSet i` of integers holding its jumps at each
  letter i. Its `Ambient` is its tensor product with W(C^♭).
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.Letter`: the letters of a Kummer chart: the multiplicative group of the
  field with p^f elements inside C^♭, where f is the number `k.f` of the chart.
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.stdOrders`: the exponents (a, u) ↦ 0 for a < l and −u for a ≥ l, prescribed
  at φ^a E for the jump u.
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.stdOrdersPart`: the elements of 𝒩, the Kisin module with all φ^a
  E inverted, that lie at the letter i and have the standard exponents of level l.


 ## The pairing lemma for scaled cup products 

### `KummerChart.PairingSide`

 One side of a pairing: a lattice Y (`kerLattice`), a lattice C (`paramLattice`), an O-linear map
α (`cocycleMap`) from C to the crystalline cocycles Z_f(Y), and for each letter a finite set of
integers that contains 0. 

### `KummerChart.PairingSide.paramLattice`

 The lattice C that parametrises the cocycles. 

### `KummerChart.PairingSide.cocycleMap`

 An O-linear map from C to the crystalline cocycles Z_f(Y). 

### `KummerChart.PairingSide.zero_mem`

 Each jump set of the side contains 0. 

### `KummerChart.PairingSide.extensionLattice`

 For a side S, the lattice datum formed by the extension of the trivial lattice on
`S.paramLattice` by Y given by the cocycles of S. At each letter it has the jump set of S; at each
embedding of O into ℂ_p its set `embJumps` is that of S with 0 added. The field `inClass` says that
the extension is crystalline over Γ' with its jumps in these sets. 

### `KummerChart.PairingSide.trivialLattice`

 For a side S, the lattice datum formed by C with trivial action of Γ, with jump set {0} at
every letter and at every embedding of O into ℂ_p. The latter is written {−0}, the case c = 0 of
the jump −c of the c-th power of the cyclotomic character. 

### `KummerChart.PairingSide.projTrivial`

 The projection from the extension of C by Y onto the trivial lattice C, as an equivariant
map of lattice data. 

### `KummerChart.PairingSide.wittFst`

 For a side S, the W(C^♭)-linear extension of the projection of Y × C onto Y, from the ambient
module of the extension lattice to that of Y. 

### `KummerChart.PairingSide.tensorLattice`

 For two sides S, S', the lattice datum Y ⊗_O Y' with, at each letter i, the sumset U(i) + U'(i)
of their integer sets. 

### `Pairing.tateDualSide`

 The third side of a pairing P. Its lattice is the Tate dual Hom(Y ⊗ Y', O(1)), and
`paramLattice` = O maps 1 to `P.cocycle`, the crystalline cocycle c with gψ − ψ = p^m c(g) for the
form ψ of P. The jump sets are {0} ∪ {−u − u' − 1 : u ∈ `S.jumpSet i`, u' ∈ `S'.jumpSet i`} at the
letters i; at the embeddings of O into ℂ_p (`embJumps`) they are formed in the same way, without
adding 0. 

### `Pairing.wittPairing`

 The W(C^♭)-bilinear extension of the pairing Y × Y' → ℤ/p^m(1) of P to a pairing of
W(C^♭) ⊗ Y and W(C^♭) ⊗ Y' with values in W(C^♭) ⊗ ℤ/p^m(1). Here `DiagTensor T` is W(C^♭) ⊗ T
over ℤ_p, `overZp` reads a representation over O as one over ℤ_p, and `charRep M χ` is M with Γ
acting through the cyclotomic character χ. 

### `Pairing.IsSafe`

 The pair (w, y) of elements of the ambient modules of the two extension lattices is safe: p^e
times the pairing of their components in W(C^♭) ⊗ Y and W(C^♭) ⊗ Y' lies in `New.Lift.smallPart`,
the image of W(𝔪^♭). 

### `Pairing.IsSafeMoveFst`

 The move of the first side by `y` is safe while `x'` is at rest: for every `g` in `k.galMonoid`
(the elements (0, g) with g in Γ'), the pair `(y, g • x' - x')` is safe. 

### `Pairing.IsSafeMoveSnd`

 The move of the second side by `y'` is safe while `x` is at rest: for every `g` in
`k.galMonoid`, the pair `(g • x - x, g • y')` is safe. 

### `Pairing.IsSafePath`

 Four safe moves lead from `(y, y')` by `(w, y')`, `(w, w')` and `(x, w')` to `(x, x')`. 

### `Pairing.IsSafePath.isSafeMoveFst_start`

 The first side moves between `y` and `w` with `y'` at rest. 

### `Pairing.IsSafePath.isSafeMoveSnd_start`

 The second side moves between `y'` and `w'` with `w` at rest. 

### `Pairing.IsSafePath.isSafeMoveFst_end`

 The first side moves between `w` and `x` with `w'` at rest. 

### `Pairing.IsSafePath.isSafeMoveSnd_end`

 The second side moves between `w'` and `x'` with `x` at rest. 

### `Pairing.IsScaledCupCoboundary`

 The conclusion of the pairing lemma. For every topology on Γ for which the map to G_p is an open
embedding, the cup product of the cocycles `S.cocycleMap c` and `p ^ e • S'.cocycleMap c'`, read in
ℚ̄_p^×, is the coboundary of a continuous cochain (`New.Lift.Pairing.IsCupCoboundary`). 

### `wittFst_smul`

 For `n` in ℤ × Γ and `x` in the extension lattice tensored with W(C^♭), the Y-component of
`n • x` is `n` applied to the Y-component of `x`, plus the cocycle of the Γ-component of `n` at
the C-component of `n • x`. 

### `Pairing.smul_wittPairing`

 The extended pairing of a pairing P commutes with the action of ℤ × Γ. 

### `Pairing.isCupCoboundary_of_eq_coboundary`

 Let `Γ` map to G_p by an open embedding (`hj`), and let `a` and `a'` be crystalline cocycles of
the lattices Y and Y' of the sides `S` and `S'`. If on `k.subgroup` their cup product under `P` is
the coboundary of a function `κ` that vanishes near `1`, then `P.IsCupCoboundary a a'`: on all of
`Γ` the image of the cup product in ℚ̄_p^× is the coboundary of a continuous cochain. 

### `smul_smul_frobGroup_comm`

 `smul_comm` oriented for `simp`: an element of `FrobGroup Γ` moves inside any commuting
scalar `a`. 

### `eventually_smul_sub_mem_smallPart`

 Near `1` (the filter `nearOne Γ`) the group fixes each element of W(C^♭) ⊗ ℤ/p^m(1) up to an
element of `smallPart m`, the image of W(𝔪^♭). 

### `Pairing.phi_smul_sub_mem_smallPart`

 A step of the pairing lemma. Let `x`, `w` lie over `p ^ e₁ • c` and `x'`, `w'` over
`p ^ e₂ • c'`, let `B z` be `p ^ e` times the pairing with the component of `z` in Y, and let `y`
solve `(φ - 1) y = B w (φ x' - w') + B x (w' - x')`. If the moves are safe (`h_path`), then for `g`
in `k.subgroup` the value `f g` of the cochain `f` of `hf`, which is corrected by the coboundary
`n • y - y`, is fixed by `φ` up to an element of `smallPart P.exponent`, the image of `W(𝔪^♭)`. 

### `Pairing.nsmul_modPairing_eq_coboundary`

 A step of the pairing lemma. Let `x`, `x'` lie over `p ^ e₁ • c`, `p ^ e₂ • c'`, and suppose
that for all `g`, `h` in `Γ'` `p ^ e₃` times the pairing of `g x - x` and `g (h x' - x')` is small
(`IsSafeMoveSnd`). Here `wittFst` takes the component in the lattice Y and `wittPairing` is the
`W(C^♭)`-bilinear extension of `P.modPairing`. Let `B z` be `p ^ e₃` times the pairing with that
component of `z`, let `f` be the corrected cochain of `hf` and `p ^ e = p ^ (e₁ + e₂ + e₃) * r`. If
on `k.subgroup` each `f g` is the constant `1 ⊗ κ g` up to a small element, then on `k.subgroup` the
cup product of `S.cocycleMap c` and `S'.cocycleMap c'`, times `p ^ e`, is `r` times the coboundary
of `κ`. 

### `Pairing.IsScaledCupCoboundary.of_isSafePath`

 The pairing lemma. Let `x`, `w` lie over `p ^ e₁ • c` and `x'`, `w'` over `p ^ e₂ • c'`. Call a
move of one component safe if, with the other at rest, `p ^ e₃` times the pairing of the moved part
with the change of the other under each `g` in `Γ'` lies in the image of `W(𝔪^♭)`. Assume that the
moves by `h x' - x'`, `h` in `Γ'`, are safe with `x` at rest, and that four safe moves lead from
`(φ x, φ x')` by `w` and `w'` to `(x, x')` (`IsSafePath`). Then, for `e ≥ e₁ + e₂ + e₃` and any
topology on `Γ` for which `Γ → G_p` is an open embedding, the cup product of the cocycles
`S.cocycleMap c` and `p ^ e • S'.cocycleMap c'`, read in ℚ̄_p^×, is a coboundary. 


## Elements p^κ E^s killing the cokernel on Kisin modules

The tool is that the operators α (g − 1)/zτ, with g ∈ Γ' and τ the element `tau` of A_inf, preserve
the ideals J_l(i) (`New.Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.mem_liftIdeal_of_eq_zTau_mul_smul_sub`), and
that at level one they may also be divided by E
(`New.Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.mem_liftIdeal_of_eq_mul_smul_sub`). One works modulo p^{κ+1} and
drops the modulus by Nakayama's lemma
(`New.Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.liftIdeal_le_of_p_pow_mem`). Here `k.letterFrob` is i ↦ i^p, and
`L.withOrders i (stdOrders l)` is the letter-i part of A_inf·φ^{l*}N
(`New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.mem_withOrders_stdOrders_iff`).


### `PairingSide.add_add_padicValNat_factorial_le`

 The count of factors `p` in the cokernel bound with weights, for a prime `p`. Let
`c ≤ J (d ω' + v_p(ω'!))`, `n ≤ a / p ^ J + ω' / (p - 1)`, `(p - 1) a ≤ e (ω + κ)` and
`8 e ≤ p ^ (J + 1)`. If `κ ≥ 2 J d ω' + (2 J + 4) ω' / (p - 1) + 8 e ω / p ^ (J + 1)`, then
`c + n + v_p(n!) ≤ κ`. 

### `JumpLattice.tau_pow_smul_mem_ainfLattice_of_eq_chi_pow_smul`

 Let `Γ` act on the lattice `L` through the power `χ ^ c` of the cyclotomic character, and let
`i` be a letter, a unit of the field with `p ^ k.f` elements. Let `x` have the standard orders of
level `0` at `i`; equivalently, `x` lies in the letter-`i` part of `W(C^♭) ⊗ L` and in
`L.kisinAinf`, the `y` in `A_inf[1/τ] ⊗ L` with `μ (φ ^ n • y)` in `B_dR^+` for all `n ≥ 1` and all
`Γ'`-equivariant forms `μ` (`L.forms`). Then `tau ^ c • x` lies in `L.ainfLattice`, the lattice
`A_inf ⊗ L`. Here `τ = tau` is the element `[ε^{1/p}] − 1` of `A_inf`. 

### `JumpLattice.kisinModule_eq_span_of_trivial`

 On a Kummer chart `k` of `Γ` with ring `𝔖 = k.S ⊂ A_inf`, let `L` be a lattice over the
coefficient ring `O` on which all of `Γ` acts trivially. Its Kisin module is `𝔖 ⊗ L`: the
`k.S`-span of the vectors `1 ⊗ t`. 

### `mul_add_pow_mem_of_mul_pow_mem`

 In a commutative ring, let `s * E ^ n` and `q ^ (κ + 1)` lie in an ideal `I`. Then
`s * (u * E + v * q) ^ (n + κ)` lies in `I`: each monomial `E ^ a * q ^ b` with
`a + b = n + κ` has `a ≥ n` or `b ≥ κ + 1`. 

### `constantAt`

 For c in the lattice C and a letter i, the image of 1⊗c, in the ambient module of the trivial
lattice, under the projector to the letter i. 

### `liftIdeal`

 The ideal J_l(i) of A_inf generated by r and the elements a of A_inf that multiply the letter-i
part of A_inf ⊗ C into the image of the extension's module of standard exponents of level l. 

### `KillsCokernel`

 p^κE^s kills the cokernel at the letter i: it multiplies the letter-i part of A_inf ⊗ C into
the image of the extension's module of standard exponents of level 0. 

### `mem_liftIdeal_iff`

 s lies in `New.Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.liftIdeal` iff for some t in A_inf and every c in C,
(s − rt) times the letter-i part of 1⊗c lifts to an element with the standard exponents of level l
at i of the extension `S.extensionLattice` of C by Y, along the projection `S.projTrivial.wittMap`
from W(C^♭) ⊗ (Y × C) to W(C^♭) ⊗ C. 

### `p_pow_mem_liftIdeal_self`

 The ideal `S.liftIdeal r l i` contains its modulus `r`; this is the case `r = p ^ κ`. 

### `phi_pow_smul_constantAt`

 The `l`-th power of Frobenius sends the constant of `c ∈ C` at the letter `i` to its constant at
the letter `σ^l(i) = i ^ (p ^ l)`, where σ is `k.letterFrob`. 

### `mul_E_pow_mem_liftIdeal_one`

 Going up a level: suppose `s` lies in `S.liftIdeal r 0 i` and all elements of `S.jumpSet i` are
at most `n`. Then `s * E ^ n` lies in `S.liftIdeal r 1 i`. 

### `liftIdeal_le_of_p_pow_mem`

 Nakayama step: if p^c lies in the ideal J_l(i) with modulus p^{c+1}
(`New.Lift.KummerChart.PairingSide.liftIdeal`), then this ideal is contained in J_l(i) with modulus
0, the ideal of multipliers that lift constants exactly. 

### `killsCokernel_of_p_pow_mem_liftIdeal`

 Nakayama at one letter: if `p ^ κ` lies in `S.liftIdeal (p ^ (κ + 1)) 0 i`, then `p ^ κ` kills
the cokernel at `i`. 

 ### Frobenius and the ideals 

### `constantAt_mem_stdOrdersPart`

 The constant of c ∈ C at the letter i lies in the Kisin module of the trivial lattice C with all
φ^a E inverted, and has exponents `New.Lift.KummerChart.stdOrders` l at i. 

### `phi_pow_smul_mem_liftIdeal_iff`

 For every modulus `r`, the element `φ ^ l • s` lies in `S.liftIdeal r l` at the letter
`(k.letterFrob ^ l) i = i ^ p ^ l` iff `s` lies in `S.liftIdeal r 0 i`. 

### `p_pow_mul_z_pow_mem_liftIdeal_iff`

 For the letters `i` and `σ(i) = k.letterFrob i` and the coordinate `z = k.z`, the element
`p ^ c * z ^ (p * a)` lies in the ideal `S.liftIdeal r 1 σ(i)` iff `p ^ c * z ^ a` lies in
`S.liftIdeal r 0 i`. 

### `exists_lift_of_mem_liftIdeal`

 Let S be a side with parameter lattice C, and let f in the ring `k.S` of the Kummer chart lie in
the ideal J_l(i) with modulus r. For some t in `k.S`, (f − rt)y lifts along `S.projTrivial.wittMap`,
for every y in `S.trivialLattice.stdOrdersPart l i` (the elements of `kisinLoc`, the Kisin module
with all φ^a E inverted, with the standard exponents of level l at i), to an element of
`S.extensionLattice.stdOrdersPart l i`. 

### `smul_sub_mem_withOrders_of_stdOrders_one`

 Let `x` lie in the Kisin module of the extension with all `φ^a E` inverted and have the standard
orders of level one at the letter `i`, that is at least `0` at `a = 0` and `-u` at `a ≥ 1`. Then for
`g` in `Γ'` the difference `g x - x` has exponent at least `1` at `a = 0` and at least `-u` at
`a ≥ 1`. An order is prescribed at `φ^a E` for each jump `u` in the jump set
`S.jumpSet (i.stepBack a)` at the letter whose `p ^ a`-th power is `i`. 

### `stdOrders_one_le_add`

 At level one the standard exponents allow one division by `E`: `stdOrders 1 a u` is at most
`(if a < 1 then 1 else -u)` lowered by `1` at `a = 0`. That is, the exponents raised by one at
`a = 0` and then lowered by one there are at least the standard exponents. 

### `mem_liftIdeal_of_mul_eq_mul_smul_sub`

 An operator `α (g - 1) / d` preserves the ideals. Let `g` lie in `Γ'`, the subgroup of the
Kummer chart `k`, and let multiplication by `d` in `Ainf` be injective on `W(C^♭) ⊗ S.paramLattice`,
the ambient module of `S.trivialLattice`. Let `d` divide `g t - t` for all `t` in the ring `k.S`.
For all `x` in `S.extensionLattice.stdOrdersPart l i` let `g x - x` be `d` times an element of
`withOrders i (stdOrders l)`: this is the `Ainf`-module of elements with the standard orders of
level `l` at the letter `i`, and `stdOrdersPart l i` its `k.S`-submodule in the Kisin module with
all `φ^a E` inverted. If `f` in `k.S` lies in `S.liftIdeal r l i` and `d s = α (g f - f)`, then `s`
lies in the same ideal. 

### `mem_liftIdeal_of_eq_zTau_mul_smul_sub`

 Operators preserve the ideals at every level. Let `f` in `k.S` lie in `S.liftIdeal r l i`, the
ideal generated by `r` and by the elements that multiply the constants at the letter `i` into the
image of the extension of C by Y at level `l`. If `zτ (s + β f) = α (g f - f)` with `g` in `Γ'` and
`s`, `α`, `β` in `Ainf`, then `s` lies in the same ideal. Here `zτ = k.zTau` is the product of the
coordinate `z = k.z` and `τ = [ε^{1/p}] − 1`, ε a system of p-power roots of 1. 

### `mem_liftIdeal_of_eq_mul_smul_sub`

 The derivation step. Let `f` in `k.S` lie in `S.liftIdeal r 1 i`, the ideal generated by `r` and
by the elements that multiply the constants at the letter `i` into the image of the extension of C
by Y at level one. If `zτE (s + β f) = α (g f - f)` with `g` in `Γ'` and `s`, `α`, `β` in `Ainf`,
then `s` lies in the same ideal. Here `zτ = k.zTau` is the product of the coordinate `z = k.z` and
`τ = [ε^{1/p}] − 1`, ε a compatible system of p-power roots of unity, and `E = k.E` is the generator
of ker θ given by the chart. 

 ### Monomials in p, z and E that lie in the ideals 

### `mem_liftIdeal_zero_of_mem_liftIdeal_one_letterFrob`

 Going down a level, on a Kummer chart `k` with ring `k.S ⊂ A_inf` and coordinate `z = k.z`: if
`p^c z^n` lies in `S.liftIdeal r 1 (σ i)`, where `σ i = i^p` is `k.letterFrob i` for the letter `i`
(a unit of the field with `p ^ k.f` elements), if `p a ≤ n` and `c + v_p(n!) ≤ κ + v_p((p a)!)`,
then `p^κ z^a` lies in `S.liftIdeal r 0 i`. 

### `mem_liftIdeal_of_mul_E_pow`

 Trading `E` for `z` at level one, on a Kummer chart `k` with ring `k.S ⊂ A_inf`, coordinate
`z = k.z` and generator `E = k.E` of ker θ: if `p^c z^a E^n` lies in `S.liftIdeal r 1 i`, if
`c + d n + v_p(n!) ≤ b` with `d = k.differentBound` and `a + n ≤ κ`, then `p^b z^κ` lies in the same
ideal. `d` is `0` if the ramification index `e` of the chart is `1`, else `v_p(e) + 1`. 

### `p_pow_mul_z_pow_pred_mem_liftIdeal`

 Lowering by one the power of the coordinate `z = k.z` of the Kummer chart `k`: for a side `S`
and a letter `i`, if `p ^ c * z ^ n` lies in `S.liftIdeal r 0 i` and `1 ≤ n`, then so does
`p ^ (c + v_p(n) + 1) * z ^ (n - 1)`. 

### `p_pow_mul_z_pow_mem_liftIdeal_of_le`

 Let `S : PairingSide O` be a pairing side and `i` a letter. This lemma repeatedly lowers the
power of the coordinate `z = k.z` of the Kummer chart `k`. If `p ^ c * z ^ n` lies in
`S.liftIdeal r 0 i`, `m ≤ n` and `c + n + v_p(n!) ≤ κ + m + v_p(m!)`, then `p ^ κ * z ^ m` lies in
the same ideal. 

### `p_pow_mul_z_pow_div_mem_liftIdeal`

 Passing to the letter before, on a Kummer chart `k` with coordinate `z = k.z` in `Ainf`, the
ring A_inf: for a pairing side `S`, let every element of `S.jumpSet (k.letterFrob j)` be at most
`ω`. If `p ^ c * z ^ n` lies in `S.liftIdeal r 0 (k.letterFrob j)`, then
`p ^ (c + d * ω + v_p(ω!)) * z ^ ((n + ω) / p)` lies in `S.liftIdeal r 0 j`. Here `d` is
`k.differentBound`: `0` if the ramification index `e` is `1`, else `v_p(e) + 1`. 

### `exists_forall_z_pow_mem_liftIdeal`

 Let `k` be a Kummer chart with coordinate `z = k.z`, an element of `Ainf`, the ring A_inf. Let
`S` be a pairing side (`PairingSide O`). For each `κ`, some power of `z` lies in
`S.liftIdeal (p ^ κ) 0 i` for every letter `i`, a unit of the field with p^f elements. 

### `exists_z_pow_mem_liftIdeal`

 Let `S` be a side, and for every letter `i` let every element of `S.jumpSet i`, the jump set of
`S` at `i`, be at most `ω`. Then some power `z ^ a` of the coordinate `z = k.z` of the Kummer chart
`k` with `(p - 1) * a ≤ e * (ω + κ)`, where `e = k.e` is the ramification index, lies in
`S.liftIdeal (p ^ (κ + 1)) 0 i` for every letter `i`. 

### `killsCokernel_zero_zero_of_small_jumps`

 Let `S` be a pairing side. Let every element of `S.jumpSet i` be at most `ω`, for every letter
`i`, and let `e * ω ≤ p - 2`, where `e = k.e` is the ramification index of the Kummer chart `k`.
Then `S.KillsCokernel i 0 0` holds at each letter `i`. That is, `1 = p ^ 0 * E ^ 0`, with `E = k.E`
the generator of the kernel of θ, multiplies the letter-`i` part of `Ainf ⊗ C` (C the parameter
lattice of `S`) into the image of the extension's module of standard exponents of level `0`. So the
cokernel vanishes. 

### `killsCokernel_of_isUnramified`

 Let the Kummer chart `k` be unramified (`k.E = k.z + p` and `k.e = 1`) and `p ≥ 5`. If at every
letter `j` all elements of `S.jumpSet j`, the jump set of the lattice Y of `S`, are at most
`(p - 3) (s + t)`, then `p ^ s * k.E ^ t` kills the cokernel at the letter `i`: it multiplies the
constants at `i` into the image of the elements of the extension with the standard exponents of
level `0`. 

### `exists_p_pow_mul_z_pow_mem_liftIdeal_and_le`

 Coming back through `J` letters, for a side `S` on a Kummer chart `k` of a group `Γ` over G_p,
with coordinate `z = k.z`. Let `z ^ a` lie in `S.liftIdeal r 0 j` for every letter `j`. Let the jump
sets `S.jumpSet j` be bounded by `ω` at the letters `j = (k.letterFrob ^ t) i = i ^ p ^ t` with
`1 ≤ t ≤ J`. Then some `p ^ c * z ^ n` with `c ≤ J (d ω + v_p(ω!))` and
`n ≤ a / p ^ J + ω / (p - 1)` lies in `S.liftIdeal r 0 i`, where `d = k.differentBound`. 

### `killsCokernel_zero_of_jumps_le`

 Let the jump sets `S.jumpSet j` be bounded by `ω` at every letter `j` and by `ω'` at the `J`
letters `(k.letterFrob ^ t) i = i ^ p ^ t` with `1 ≤ t ≤ J`, and let `8 e ≤ p ^ (J + 1)` for the
ramification index `e = k.e`. If `κ ≥ 2 J d ω' + (2 J + 4) ω' / (p - 1) + 8 e ω / p ^ (J + 1)` with
`d = k.differentBound`, then `p ^ κ` kills the cokernel at `i`: it multiplies the constants at `i`
into the image of the elements of the extension of C by Y with the standard exponents of level
`0`.
