# Lattices with prescribed orders, depth steps and the depth lemma

This file studies lattices with prescribed orders in W(C^♭) ⊗ L, for a lattice datum L over a
Kummer chart k with ring S = Λ⟦z⟧ ⊆ A_inf, coordinate z, p_a = φ^a(E), δ = zτ and Galois group Γ'.
Here τ = `tau` is the element [ε^{1/p}] − 1 of A_inf, `valueAt a μ` is μ ∘ φ^{−a} with values in
B_dR, and `hodgeFil n` is tⁿB_dR^+. `L.withOrders i c` consists of the x in the A_inf-span
`L.kisinLocAinf` at the letter i with `valueAt a μ x ∈ hodgeFil (c a u)` for all a, all u in
`L.jumpSet (i.stepBack a)` and all forms μ of type u at the letter `i.stepBack a`; such x are said
to have orders c at i. An element x of the Kisin module `L.kisinLoc` (all φ^a E inverted) has
depth n at p_a for a form μ if `valueAt a μ x` is a constant modulo `hodgeFil n`. Such an x at the
letter i has depth o a u at p_a for all a, all u in `L.jumpSet (i.stepBack a)` and all forms of
type u at `i.stepBack a` if and only if gx − x has orders o at i for every g in Γ'
(`New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.mem_invariantsMod_withOrders_iff`). The depth lemma gives an
operator p^c + υ ∑ (t_l − 1)/δ, with t_l in Γ', that sends elements of depth n to elements whose
value is a constant modulo `hodgeFil (n + 1)`.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.withOrders`: the elements with orders at least c(a, u) at p_a
  on the jumps u.
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.valueAt_mem_of_hodgeFil_le`: the gap rule, bounds for forms
  whose type need not be a jump.
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.invariantsMod`: the x with gx − x in Q for all g in Γ'.
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.mem_withOrders_improvedOrders`: an element with orders lo
  that is invariant modulo the elements with orders hi has the orders
  `New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.improvedOrders lo hi`.
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.exists_valueAt_sub_mem_hodgeFil_succ`: the depth lemma, with c =
  `k.cost a n`.

Notions from other files:
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.galMonoid`: the group Γ' as a submonoid of `FrobGroup Γ` = ℤ × Γ, whose
  first factor is generated by the Frobenius `φ`.
* `New.Lift.thetaPhiInv`: the ring map θ ∘ φ^{−a} from A_inf to O_C, with kernel generated by p_a.
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.kisinLocAinf`, `New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.kisinLoc`: the
  A_inf-module A_inf·𝒩 and the S-module 𝒩 in `L.Ambient` = W(C^♭) ⊗ L, where 𝒩 is the Kisin module
  with all p_a inverted (`New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.span_kisinLoc` shows that the first,
  defined as a saturation, is the span of the second).
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.formsOfType`: the A_inf-linear maps `L.Ambient → B_dR` that are
  Γ'-equivariant on 1 ⊗ L and send its projection to the letter i into t^{−u}B_dR^+.
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.valueField`: the subfield `𝒦_a` of `B_dR` generated over
  `localFieldOf k.subgroup` by the images of `S_B = k.bicommutant ⊆ B⁺` and of `φ^{-a} S`.
* `New.Lift.KummerChart.Letter`: the units of the field with p^f elements. The letter `i.stepBack a`
  is the one whose p^a-th power is i, `L.letterPart i` is the part of `L.Ambient` on which the root
  of unity ζ of the coefficient ring acts as the Teichmüller lift of i, and `L.jumpSet i` is a
  finite set of integers, called the jumps at i, that contains the Hodge–Tate jumps of L above i.

 ### Steps in the Galois group of the chart and their costs 

### `cost`

 The cost c_a(n) of the step n at p_a = φ^a(E): c_a(n) = v_p(n) + d + 3a, with v_p(0) = 0. Here
d = `k.differentBound` is 0 if the ramification index e of the chart is 1, and v_p(e) + 1
otherwise. 

### `associated_pow_natAbs_sub_one`

 For a unit `ω` and an integer `n`, the elements `ω ^ |n| - 1` and `ω ^ n - 1` are associated:
for negative `n` they differ by the unit `-ω ^ n`. 

### `zTau_dvd_sub_one_of_mul_eq_smul_E`

 If `g` in the Galois group `Γ'` of the chart multiplies `E` by `ω`, then `ω - 1` is divisible
by the product `k.zTau` of the coordinate `z` with the element `τ = [ε^{1/p}] - 1` of `A_inf`. 

### `theta_sub_one_dvd_pow_mul_theta_zTau`

 Let `g` in the Galois group `Γ'` of the Kummer chart multiply the coordinate `z` by
`[ε] ^ p ^ a` and `E` by `ω`. Then `θ (ω - 1)` divides `p ^ (d + a) θ(δ)` in `O_C`. Here `θ` is
Fontaine's map `A_inf → O_C`, `δ = k.zTau` is the product of `z` with the element
`τ = [ε^{1/p}] - 1` of `A_inf`, and `d = differentBound` bounds the different of the chart. 

### `p_mul_theta_mul_theta_z_dvd_pow`

 Let `r` in `A_inf` be divisible by `k.zTau`, the product of the coordinate `z` of the Kummer
chart with the element `τ = [ε^{1/p}] - 1` of `A_inf`. Then `p θ(r) θ(z)` divides `θ(r) ^ p` in
`O_C`, for Fontaine's map `θ : A_inf → O_C`. 

### `theta_zTau_dvd_pow_mul_thetaPhiInv`

 For the product `k.zTau` of the coordinate `z` of the chart with `τ = [ε^{1/p}] - 1` in `A_inf`,
the image under `θ` divides `p ^ (2 a)` times the image under `thetaPhiInv a = θ ∘ φ^{-a}`. 

### `IsDepthStep`

 The data for the step n ≠ 0 of the depth lemma at p_a = φ^a(E) (`k.frobE a`): an element g of
the Galois group Γ' of the chart (`k.galMonoid`) that multiplies p_a by a unit ω and acts trivially
on the ring S of the chart modulo p_a, the quotient X of ω^n − 1 by δ = zτ (`k.zTau`), and an
inverse υ of X modulo p_a up to the factor p^c, c = `cost a n`. 

### `IsDepthStep.smul_frobE`

 g multiplies p_a by ω. 

### `IsDepthStep.frobE_dvd_smul_sub`

 g acts trivially on S modulo p_a. 

### `IsDepthStep.frobE_dvd_mul_sub_pow`

 υX ≡ p^c modulo p_a. 

### `IsDepthStep.zTau_mul_eq_pow_sub_one`

 δX = ω^n − 1. 

### `exists_isDepthStep`

 For an integer n ≠ 0 there are g in the Galois group Γ' of the Kummer chart (`k.galMonoid`), a
unit ω of A_inf and υ, X in A_inf that form a depth step at p_a = φ^a(E): g p_a = ω p_a, g acts
trivially on the ring S = Λ⟦z⟧ of the chart modulo p_a, υX ≡ p^c modulo p_a, and δX = ω^n − 1. Here
δ = `k.zTau` is the product of the coordinate z with the element τ = [ε^{1/p}] − 1 of A_inf. The
exponent c = `cost a n` is v_p(n) + d + 3a, where d = `k.differentBound` is 0 if the ramification
index e of the chart is 1, and v_p(e) + 1 otherwise. 

### `exists_smul_frobE_eq_mul_and_zTau_mul_eq`

 The statement of `exists_isDepthStep` with the four fields of `IsDepthStep` written out: for
n ≠ 0 there are g in Γ' (`k.galMonoid`), a unit ω and υ, X in A_inf such that g p_a = ω p_a, g acts
trivially on the ring S of the chart modulo p_a = φ^a(E) (`k.frobE a`), υX ≡ p^c modulo p_a, and
zτX = ω^n − 1, where τ = [ε^{1/p}] − 1. The exponent c = `cost a n` is v_p(n) + d + 3a, where
d = `k.differentBound` is 0 if the ramification index e of the chart is 1, and v_p(e) + 1
otherwise. 

### `isPrimitiveRoot_thetaPhiInv_teichmuller_epsTilt`

 The image under `thetaPhiInv a = θ ∘ φ^{-a}` of the Teichmüller lift `[ε]` of
`epsTilt = (1, ζ_p, ζ_{p²}, …)` is a primitive `p ^ a`-th root of unity. 

### `sum_pow_eq_zero_of_isPrimitiveRoot`

 If `ζ` is a primitive `q`-th root of unity in a domain, `q ∣ N` and `q ∤ m`, then the sum of
`(ζ ^ m) ^ l` over `l < N` vanishes. 

### `exists_frobE_dvd_smul_sum_sub_sum`

 The Galois elements for the step n = 0 of the depth lemma. There are p^c elements t_l of the
Galois group Γ' of the chart (`k.galMonoid`) such that for every s in the ring S of the chart and
every g in Γ' one has g(∑_l t_l s) ≡ ∑_l t_l s modulo p_a = φ^a(E) (`k.frobE a`). The exponent
c = `cost a 0` is d + 3a, where d = `k.differentBound` is 0 if the ramification index e of the chart
is 1, and v_p(e) + 1 otherwise. 

 ### Lattices with given orders 

### `withOrders`

 The submodule of the x in A_inf·𝒩 (`L.kisinLocAinf`, for 𝒩 the Kisin module with all φ^a E
inverted) at the letter i with μ(φ^{−a}x) ∈ t^{c(a,u)}B_dR^+ for all a, all jumps u at the letter
`i.stepBack a` and all forms μ of type u at that letter. Such x are said to have orders c at i.
A letter is a unit of the field with p^f elements, and `i.stepBack a` is the letter whose p^a-th
power is i. The jumps at a letter j are the elements of `L.jumpSet j`, a finite set of integers that
contains the Hodge–Tate jumps of L above j. 

### `stdOrdersPart`

 The elements of `L.kisinLoc`, the Kisin module with all `φ^a E` inverted, that lie at the letter
`i` and have the orders `stdOrders l`, which are `0` at `p_a = φ^a E` for `a < l` and `-u` on
the jump `u` for `a ≥ l`. 

### `mem_kisinLocAinf_of_smul_of_dvd_frobE`

 If `r` divides `k.frobE a` = φ^a E and `r • x` lies in `L.kisinLocAinf`, then so does `x`. So a
divisor of φ^a E can be cancelled in `L.kisinLocAinf`, where φ^a E is inverted. 

### `frobE_smul_mem_kisinLocAinf_iff`

 `(k.frobE a).val • x` lies in `L.kisinLocAinf` if and only if `x` does. So the element
`k.frobE a` = φ^a E can be cancelled in `L.kisinLocAinf`, where it is inverted. 

### `kisinLoc_induction`

 Induction on `L.kisinLoc`, the Kisin module with all `p_a = φ^a E` inverted: a property that
holds on the Kisin module `L.kisinModule` and passes from `p_a • y` to `y` holds on `L.kisinLoc`. 

### `frobEUnit_zpow_smul_mem_kisinLoc_iff`

 The Kisin module `L.kisinLoc` with all `φ^a E` inverted is stable under multiplication and
division by the integer powers of `k.frobEUnit a`, which is `φ^a E` as a unit of `W(C^♭)`. 

### `Hom.wittMap_mem_kisinLocAinf`

 The scalar extension `F.wittMap` to W(C^♭) of a map F: L → L′ of lattice data sends
`L.kisinLocAinf`, the A_inf-span of the Kisin module with all φ^a E inverted, into
`L'.kisinLocAinf`. 

### `Hom.wittMap_mem_kisinLoc`

 The scalar extension `F.wittMap` to W(C^♭) of a map F: L → L′ of lattice data sends
`L.kisinLoc`, the Kisin module with all φ^a E inverted, into `L'.kisinLoc`. 

### `galMonoid_smul_mem_kisinAinf`

 The A_inf-span `L.kisinAinf` of the Kisin module is stable under the Galois group Γ' of the
chart (`k.galMonoid`). 

### `galMonoid_smul_mem_kisinLocAinf`

 The A_inf-span `L.kisinLocAinf` of the Kisin module with all φ^a E inverted is stable under the
Galois group Γ' of the chart (`k.galMonoid`). 

### `mem_withOrders_iff`

 `x` has orders `c` at the letter `i` if and only if it lies in `L.kisinLocAinf` and in the
part at `i`, and `L.valueAt a μ x` lies in `hodgeFil (c a u)` for all `a`, all jumps `u` at the
letter `i.stepBack a` and all forms `μ` of type `u` at that letter. 

### `valueAt_mem_hodgeFil_of_mem_withOrders`

 If `x` has orders `c` at the letter `i`, `u` is a jump at the letter `i.stepBack a` and `μ`
is a form of type `u` at that letter, then `L.valueAt a μ x` lies in `hodgeFil (c a u)`. 

### `mem_withOrders_of_mem_hodgeFil`

 To raise orders it is enough to check the values where they go up. Let L be a lattice with
Galois action over the Kummer chart, and let i be a letter. Let x in `L.Ambient` = W(C^♭) ⊗ L have
orders c at i. This means three things. First, x lies in `L.kisinLocAinf`, the A_inf-span of the
Kisin module with all φ^a E inverted. Second, x lies in the part at i. Third,
`valueAt a μ x` = μ(φ^{−a}x) lies in t^{c(a,u)}B_dR^+ for all a, all jumps u at the letter
j = `i.stepBack a` and all forms μ of type u at j. Suppose μ(φ^{−a}x) lies in t^{c'(a,u)}B_dR^+ for
all such a, u and μ with c(a, u) < c'(a, u). Then x has orders c' at i. 

### `mem_withOrders_of_le`

 An element with orders `c` also has all smaller orders `c'`; only the values at the
jumps are compared. 

### `valueAt_mem_hodgeFil_of_mem_ainfLattice`

 Let x lie in the A_inf-span `L.ainfLattice` of 1 ⊗ L, at the letter i, and let μ be a form of
type u at the letter `i.stepBack a`, a steps before i. Then μ(φ^{−a}x) lies in t^{−u}B_dR^+. 

### `Hom.wittMap_mem_withOrders`

 Let F: L → L' be an equivariant map of lattice data, and let the jump set `L'.jumpSet j` lie in
`L.jumpSet j` for every letter j. Then the scalar extension `F.wittMap` of F to W(C^♭) sends
elements with orders c at the letter i to elements with orders c at i. 

### `mem_withOrders_of_smul`

 Saturation: let r in A_inf be divisible by no φ^a E, and let x lie in `L.kisinLocAinf`, the
A_inf-span of the Kisin module with all φ^a E inverted. If rx has orders c at the letter i, then
so does x. 

### `frobEUnit_zpow_smul_mem_withOrders_add`

 Let i be a letter and let x in `L.Ambient` = W(C^♭) ⊗ L have orders c at i: x lies in the
A_inf-span `L.kisinLocAinf` of the Kisin module `L.kisinLoc` with all φ^a E inverted, and in the
part at i, and its values μ(φ^{−a}x) lie in t^{c(a,u)}B_dR^+ for all a, all jumps u at the letter
`i.stepBack a` and all forms μ of type u there. Let p_b = φ^b E, as the unit `k.frobEUnit b` of
W(C^♭). Then p_b^m x has at i the orders c raised by m at p_b, that is, for a = b, and unchanged for
a ≠ b. 

### `frobEUnit_zpow_smul_mem_withOrders`

 Let `x` have orders `c` at the letter `i`, and let `p_b = φ^b E` be the unit `k.frobEUnit b`.
Then `p_b ^ m • x` has orders `c'` if, at the jumps `u`, `c' b u ≤ c b u + m` and
`c' a u ≤ c a u` for `a ≠ b`. 

### `frobEUnit_zpow_smul_mem_withOrders_add_iff`

 Let `p_b = φ^b E` be the unit `k.frobEUnit b`. Then `p_b ^ m • x` has orders at the letter `i`
given by c(a,u) + m for a = b and c(a,u) for a ≠ b if and only if `x` has the orders `c` at `i`. 

### `prod_frobEUnit_zpow_smul_mem_withOrders_add_iff`

 Let `F = ∏_{b ∈ s} p_b ^ r b` be a product of integer powers of the units `p_b = φ^b E`
(`k.frobEUnit b`). Then `F • x` has the orders `c` raised by `r a` at `p_a` for `a ∈ s` (and
unchanged at the other `p_a`) at the letter `i` if and only if `x` has the orders `c` at `i`. 

### `prod_frobEUnit_zpow_smul_mem_kisinLoc_iff`

 The Kisin module `L.kisinLoc` with all `φ^a E` inverted is stable under multiplication and
division by products of integer powers of the units `k.frobEUnit b = φ^b E`. 

### `kisinLocAinf_le_fracSpan`

 Every element of `L.kisinLocAinf` has a multiple by a non-zero element of A_inf in the
A_inf-span of 1 ⊗ L, that is, lies in `L.fracSpan`. 

### `valueAt_smul_sub`

 For x in `L.kisinLocAinf`, a form μ (Γ'-equivariant on 1 ⊗ L) and g in Γ', the map
`L.valueAt a μ` = μ ∘ φ^{−a} sends gx − x to gv − v, where v is its value on x. 

### `smul_sub_mem_withOrders`

 If x has orders c at the letter i and g lies in the Galois group Γ' of the chart, then gx − x
has orders c at i. 

 ### Divisibility of `g x - x` by `zTau` 

### `galMonoid_smul_eq_toGp_smul`

 An element `g` of `k.galMonoid` (the image of `Γ'` under `inr`) that comes from
`γ` in `Γ'` acts on `B_dR` through the image of `γ` in `G_p`. 

### `exists_zTau_smul_eq_smul_sub`

 Let `x` in `L.Ambient = W(C^♭) ⊗ L` lie in the Kisin module `L.kisinModule` of the Galois
lattice `L`, and let `g` be an element of the Galois group `Γ'` of the Kummer chart `k`. Then
`g x - x` is `δ = k.zTau` times a vector of `L.kisinAinf`, the `A_inf`-span of the Kisin module. 

### `zTauUnit_inv_smul_sub_mem_kisinLocAinf`

 Let L be a Galois lattice over the Kummer chart k, inside `L.Ambient` = W(C^♭) ⊗ L, let x lie in
its Kisin module `L.kisinLoc`, in which φ^a E is inverted for every a ≥ 0, and let g lie in the
Galois group Γ' of k. Then (zτ)^{−1}(gx − x) lies in `L.kisinLocAinf`, the A_inf-span of
`L.kisinLoc`.

This is a form of the known triviality of the Galois action on Kisin modules modulo the variable. 

 ### Frobenius, values and retractions 

### `frobEUnit_pow_smul_phi_smul_mem_kisinModule`

 The map `E ^ h • φ` preserves the Kisin module `L.kisinModule`. Here `h = L.jumpBound` is the
least natural number with all Hodge–Tate jumps of `L` over `Γ'` in `[-h, h]`, and `k.frobEUnit 0` is
`E` as a unit of `W(C^♭)`. This is a part of `frame_theorem`. 

### `phi_smul_mem_kisinLoc`

 Frobenius φ maps `L.kisinLoc`, the Kisin module of L with all φ^a E inverted, into itself. 

### `phi_pow_smul_mem_kisinLoc`

 Every power φ^l of Frobenius maps `L.kisinLoc`, the Kisin module of L with all φ^a E inverted,
into itself. 

### `span_kisinModule`

 The A_inf-module `L.kisinAinf` is spanned by the Kisin module `L.kisinModule`. This is a part of
`frame_theorem`. 

### `span_kisinLoc`

 The A_inf-span of the Kisin module `L.kisinLoc` with all φ^a E inverted is `L.kisinLocAinf`,
which is defined as the set of x that some product of the φ^a E sends into `L.kisinAinf`, the
A_inf-span of the Kisin module `L.kisinModule`. 

### `sum_map_smul_eq_zero_of_mem_valueFieldPoints`

 An S-linear map f: A_inf → S preserves linear relations among elements v_i of `L.fracSpan` on
which all Γ'-equivariant forms take values in the field 𝒦₀ = `valueField 0`. This is a part of
`frame_theorem`. 

### `sum_map_smul_eq_zero_of_mem_kisinLoc`

 An S-linear map f: A_inf → S preserves linear relations among elements v_i of `L.kisinLoc`, the
Kisin module with all φ^a E inverted: if ∑ b_i v_i = 0 with b_i in A_inf, then ∑ f(b_i) v_i = 0. 

### `tau_pow_smul_mem_kisinAinf`

 The element τ^h multiplies the A_inf-span `L.ainfLattice` of 1 ⊗ L into `L.kisinAinf`, the
A_inf-span of the Kisin module. Here τ = `tau` is [ε^{1/p}] − 1, and h = `L.jumpBound` is the least
natural number with all Hodge–Tate jumps of L over Γ' in [−h, h]. This is a part of
`frame_theorem`. 

### `valueAt_mem_valueField`

 Let L be a Galois lattice over the Kummer chart and a ≥ 0. Let μ be a form, that is, an
A_inf-linear map `L.Ambient` = W(C^♭) ⊗ L → B_dR that is Γ'-equivariant on 1 ⊗ L, and let x lie in
the Kisin module `L.kisinLoc` with all φ^b E inverted. Then the value `valueAt a μ x` = μ(φ^{−a}x)
lies in `valueField a`, the subfield of B_dR generated over the fixed field of Γ' by the images of
the ring `k.bicommutant` and of φ^{−a}S. 

### `exists_coordRetract`

 The chart comes with a chosen topological S-basis of A_inf, and λ₀ = `k.coord₀`: A_inf → S is
the coordinate in this basis that restricts to the identity on S. For a Galois lattice L there is an
S-linear map r from `L.kisinLocAinf`, the A_inf-span in W(C^♭) ⊗ L of the Kisin module `L.kisinLoc`,
to `L.kisinLoc` with r(ax) = λ₀(a)x for a in A_inf and x in `L.kisinLoc`. 

### `coordRetract`

 A chosen S-linear map from the A_inf-span `L.kisinLocAinf` to `L.kisinLoc` sending a·x to
λ₀(a)·x for a in A_inf and x in `L.kisinLoc`. Here λ₀ = `k.coord₀`: A_inf → S is the coordinate that
is the identity on S. This property is `coordRetract_smul`. 

### `coordRetract_smul`

 The retraction `coordRetract` sends a·x to λ₀(a)·x for a in A_inf and x in `L.kisinLoc`, where
λ₀ = `k.coord₀`: A_inf → S is the coordinate that is the identity on S. 

### `coordRetract_sum`

 Let v_i be finitely many elements of `L.kisinLoc` and a_i elements of A_inf, and let
y = ∑ a_i v_i in the A_inf-span `L.kisinLocAinf`. Then the retraction `coordRetract` sends y to
∑ λ₀(a_i) v_i, where λ₀ = `k.coord₀`: A_inf → S is the coordinate that is the identity on S. 

### `coordRetract_of_mem_kisinLoc`

 The retraction `coordRetract` from the A_inf-span `L.kisinLocAinf` to `L.kisinLoc` is the
identity on `L.kisinLoc`. 

### `Hom.wittMap_coordRetract`

 The retractions `coordRetract` commute with a map F: L → L′ of lattice data: if the scalar
extension `F.wittMap` of F to W(C^♭) sends y to y′, then it sends the retraction of y to the
retraction of y′. 

 ### The gap rule and depth 

### `valueAt_mem_of_hodgeFil_le`

 The gap rule. Let `i` be a letter, let `j = i.stepBack a` be the letter with `j ^ p ^ a = i`,
and let `c : ℕ → ℤ → ℤ`. Let `x` have orders `c` at `i`: it lies in `L.kisinLocAinf` and in the part
at `i`, and for every `b` and every jump `v` at `i.stepBack b` the forms of type `v` there send
`φ^{-b} x` into `hodgeFil (c b v) = t^(c b v)B_dR⁺`. A form of type `u` at `j` is an `A_inf`-linear
map `μ` to `B_dR` that is `Γ'`-equivariant on `1 ⊗ L` and maps the projection of `1 ⊗ L` to `j` into
`hodgeFil (-u)`. If `W` contains `hodgeFil (c a v)` for every jump `v ≤ u` at `j`, then
`valueAt a μ x = μ (φ^{-a} x)` lies in `W` for each such form `μ`. 

### `zTauUnit_inv_smul_sub_mem_withOrders`

 Let x lie in the Kisin module `L.kisinLoc` (all φ^a E inverted) and g in the Galois group Γ' of
the chart. If gx − x has orders c at the letter i (`L.withOrders i c`), then so does
(gx − x)/(zτ). 

 ## Invariants modulo a submodule and the depth lemma 

### `locKisinPart`

 For an A_inf-submodule Q of W(C^♭)⊗L, the Z_p-submodule of
`New.Lift.KummerChart.JumpLattice.kisinLoc` of the elements that lie in Q. 

### `invariantsMod`

 The Z_p-module of the x in W(C^♭) ⊗ L that are invariant modulo Q: gx − x lies in Q for all g in
Γ'. Take Q = `L.withOrders i o` and x in `L.kisinLocAinf` at the letter i. Then this holds if and
only if, for every a, every jump u at `i.stepBack a` and every form μ of type u there,
`valueAt a μ x` is a constant in `localFieldOf k.subgroup` modulo `hodgeFil (o a u)`. This is the
notion of depth in the module header (`mem_invariantsMod_withOrders_iff`). (The module is the
intersection of the preimages of Q under the maps g − 1; `copy` only makes membership unfold to the
displayed condition.) 

### `cost_le_of_isUnramified`

 Let the Kummer chart be unramified: `E = z + p` and the ramification index is `1`, so that the
number `d = k.differentBound` is `0`. Then the cost `k.cost a n = v_p(n) + d + 3a` is at most `3a`
if `n = 0` or `p` does not divide `n`. 

### `improvedOrders`

 The order `hi a u` where `0 < lo a u` and `min (hi a u) 0` elsewhere. An element with
orders `lo` that is invariant modulo the elements with orders `hi` has these orders
(`mem_withOrders_improvedOrders`). 

### `exists_const_sub_mem_hodgeFil_of_smul_sub_mem`

 If `y` in `B_dR` is fixed modulo `hodgeFil n` by the Galois group `k.galMonoid` of the chart,
then `y` is congruent modulo `hodgeFil n` to a constant in the fixed field of `Γ'`. 

### `exists_valueAt_sub_mem_hodgeFil`

 Let `x` lie in `L.kisinLocAinf` and let `μ` be a `Γ'`-equivariant form. If the value
`valueAt a μ` on `g • x - x` lies in `hodgeFil n` for all `g` in `k.galMonoid`, then the value on
`x` is congruent modulo `hodgeFil n` to a constant in the fixed field of `Γ'`. 

### `galMonoid_smul_localFieldOf`

 The Galois group `k.galMonoid` of the chart fixes the image in `B_dR` of its fixed field
`localFieldOf k.subgroup`. 

### `valueAt_smul_sub_mem_hodgeFil_of_sub_const_mem`

 Let `x` lie in `L.kisinLocAinf` and let `μ` be a `Γ'`-equivariant form. If the value
`valueAt a μ x` is congruent modulo `hodgeFil n` to a constant `c` in the fixed field of `Γ'`, then
the value on `g • x - x` lies in `hodgeFil n` for every `g` in `k.galMonoid`. 

### `mem_invariantsMod_withOrders_iff`

 Let `i` be a letter and let `x` lie in `L.kisinLocAinf`, the `A_inf`-span of the Kisin module
`L.kisinLoc` (all `φ^a E` inverted) in `W(C^♭) ⊗ L`, and in `L.letterPart i`. Then `x` is in
`invariantsMod (L.withOrders i o)`, that is, `g x - x` has orders `o` at `i` for every `g` in the
Galois group `Γ'` of the chart, if and only if the following holds for every `a`, every `u` in the
finite set `L.jumpSet j` of jumps at the letter `j = i.stepBack a` (the one with `j ^ p ^ a = i`)
and every form `μ` of type `u` at `j` (a linear map to `B_dR`, `Γ'`-equivariant on `1 ⊗ L`, sending
its projection to `j` into `hodgeFil (-u)`): the value `valueAt a μ x = μ (φ^{-a} x)` is congruent
modulo `hodgeFil (o a u) = t^(o a u)B_dR⁺` to a constant in the fixed field of `Γ'`. 

### `mem_withOrders_improvedOrders`

 Let `i` be a letter and `x` an element of `L.Ambient = W(C^♭) ⊗ L` with orders `lo` at `i`: it
lies in `L.kisinLocAinf` and in the part at `i`, and for all natural numbers `a`, all jumps `u` at
the letter `i.stepBack a` and all forms `μ` of type `u` there, `μ (φ^{-a} x)` lies in
`t^(lo a u)B_dR⁺`. Let `x` be invariant modulo the elements with orders `hi`: `g • x - x` has orders
`hi` at `i` for all `g` in the Galois group `Γ'` of the chart. Then `x` has the orders
`improvedOrders lo hi`. 

### `smul_sub_mem_hodgeFil_one_of_mem_valueField`

 If `t` in `k.galMonoid` (an element of `Γ'`) acts trivially on the ring `S` of the chart modulo
`p_a = k.frobE a`, then it fixes modulo `hodgeFil 1` every `y` of `hodgeFil 0 = B_dR⁺` in the
subfield `valueField a`. 

### `smul_sub_phiInvToBdR_mul_mem_hodgeFil_succ`

 Let `t` in the Galois group `Γ'` multiply `p_a = k.frobE a = φ^a E` by a unit `ω` and act
trivially modulo `p_a` on the ring `k.S = Λ⟦z⟧ ⊆ A_inf` of the Kummer chart. Let `y` lie in the
step `hodgeFil n = Filⁿ B_dR` and in `k.valueField a`, the subfield of `B_dR` generated over the
fixed field of `Γ'` by the images of `k.bicommutant` and of `φ^{-a} S`. Then `t • y` is congruent
modulo `hodgeFil (n + 1)` to `y` times the image `phiInvToBdR a (ω ^ n)` of `φ^{-a} (ω ^ n)`. 

### `depthStepOperator`

 The operator `p ^ e + υ ∑ l, (t l - 1) / (τ z)` on `L.Ambient = W(C^♭) ⊗ L`, for `υ` in `A_inf`
and finitely many elements `t l` of the Galois group `Γ'` of the chart. Here `k.zTauUnit` is `τ z`
as a unit of `W(C^♭)`. 

### `exists_valueAt_depthStepOperator_sub_smul_mem`

 One step of depth at `p_a = φ^a E` for `n ≠ 0`. There are `t` in the Galois group `Γ'` of the
Kummer chart and `υ` in `A_inf` such that the operator
`L.depthStepOperator e υ ![t] = p ^ e + υ (t - 1) / δ` on `W(C^♭) ⊗ L` has the property below. Here
`δ = k.zTau` is the product of the coordinate `z` of the chart with `τ = [ε^{1/p}] - 1`, and
`e = cost a n = v_p(n) + d + 3a`, where `d = k.differentBound` is `0` or `v_p(e_k) + 1` as the
ramification index `e_k` of the chart is `1` or not. Let `x` lie in the Kisin module `L.kisinLoc`
with all `φ^b E` inverted, and let `μ` in `L.forms` be an `A_inf`-linear map from `W(C^♭) ⊗ L` to
`B_dR` that is `Γ'`-equivariant on `1 ⊗ L`. If its value `valueAt a μ x = μ (φ^{-a} x)` is congruent
modulo `hodgeFil n = Filⁿ B_dR` to a constant `c` in the fixed field `localFieldOf k.subgroup`,
then its value on the image of `x` is congruent to `p ^ e • c` modulo `hodgeFil (n + 1)`. 

### `exists_valueAt_sub_mem_hodgeFil_succ`

 Raising depth by one step at `p_a = φ^a E`. There are finitely many elements `t l` of the Galois
group `Γ'` of the Kummer chart and `υ` in `A_inf` such that the operator `L.depthStepOperator e υ
t = p ^ e + υ ∑ l, (t l - 1) / δ` on `W(C^♭) ⊗ L` has the property below. Here `δ = k.zTau` is the
product of the coordinate `z` of the chart with `τ = [ε^{1/p}] - 1`, and
`e = cost a n = v_p(n) + d + 3a`, where `d = k.differentBound` is `0` or `v_p(e_k) + 1` as the
ramification index `e_k` of the chart is `1` or not. Let `x` lie in the Kisin module `L.kisinLoc`
with all `φ^b E` inverted, and let `μ` in `L.forms` be an `A_inf`-linear map from `W(C^♭) ⊗ L` to
`B_dR` that is `Γ'`-equivariant on `1 ⊗ L`. Suppose `valueAt a μ x = μ (φ^{-a} x)` is a constant (in
the fixed field of `Γ'`) modulo `hodgeFil n = Filⁿ B_dR`. Then
`valueAt a μ (L.depthStepOperator e υ t x)` is a constant modulo `hodgeFil (n + 1)`.
