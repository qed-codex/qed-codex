# Modules of functions, Fitting ideals and trace ideals for Ribet's method

This file bounds Fitting ideals of modules `R^s / N` over `R = O[X j]`, `O` a discrete valuation
ring, through modules of functions, and states the conditions on traces and on common Ribet
elements. Each index `j : J` carries two pairs `τ j`, `τ' j` and `φ j`, `φ' j` of elements of `G`.
In the application `τ' j` is the inverse of the conjugate of `τ j`, so that `c (τ j) - c (τ' j)`
is the value at `τ j` of the sum of a cocycle `c` and its conjugate, an even class; likewise for
`φ`. The file also gives the linear algebra for several characters and for the main summand.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.Selmer.HasTriangleCost`, `New.Selmer.exists_mul_prod_X_mem_fittingIdeal`: if the module of
  functions `New.Selmer.formsMod` attached to `N ⊆ R^s`, `R = O[X j]`, satisfies a triangular
  condition along a finite set `I` of indices and a bound on lengths, then the Fitting ideal of
  `R^s / N` contains `∏ j ∈ I, X j` times a polynomial whose constant coefficient has bounded
  valuation.
* `New.Selmer.IsTraceIdeal`: an algebra `S` of families of polynomials in variables `X j` holds the
  characteristic polynomials, and an `S`-submodule `𝒥` holds the traces of given relations.

Notions from other files:
* `New.Selmer.fittingIdeal N`: the ideal of `R` generated by the determinants of square matrices
  with all rows in `N ⊆ R^ι`, the zeroth Fitting ideal of `R^ι / N`.
* `New.Selmer.formsMod Φ z N p`: the functions `g ↦ Ψ (z g)` on `G`, where `Ψ : O^n → O / p` runs
  over the `O`-linear maps that vanish on `Φ⁻¹ N`.
* `New.Selmer.ribetCocyclesIn u ρ B`: the cocycles `G → B^d` for `ρ` that obey the linear relations
  among the families `(u x g - 1)_x`.


### `HasTriangleCost`

 There is a set `L ⊆ G` with two properties, where `Z₀` denotes the functions of `Z` that vanish
on `L`. (a) Let `j ∈ I` and `c ∈ Z₀`. If `c (τ i) = c (τ' i)` for all `i < j`, and
`c (φ i) = c (φ' i)` for those `i < j` in `I`, then `C • c (τ j) = C • c (τ' j)`. (b) Let `S ≤ Z₀`
be a submodule whose members vanish at all `τ j` and `τ' j` and have `c (φ j) = c (φ' j)` for
`j ∈ I`. Then the length of `Z / Z₀` plus the length of `S` modulo its `C`-torsion is at most
`b`. Here `relLength S T` is the length of `S / (S ⊓ T)`. 

### `exists_mem_fittingIdeal_addVal_constantCoeff_le`

 Let `R` be the polynomial ring over `O` in the variables `X j` and `Φ : O^n → R^s`. Suppose
`p ≠ 0`, `Φ (p • O^n) ⊆ T`, and that modulo `T` the basis vector of `R^s` at each `g ∈ s` is
`Φ (z g)`. Let `T₀` be `T` plus all vectors of polynomials without constant term. Then the Fitting
ideal of `R^s / T` contains an element whose constant coefficient has valuation at most
`#s * addVal O C` plus the length, modulo `C`-torsion, of the module `formsMod Φ z T₀ p` of the
functions `Ψ ∘ z` for linear `Ψ : O^n → O / p` vanishing on `Φ⁻¹ T₀`. 

### `smul_X_smul_mem_of_forall_form`

 Let `Φ : O^n → R^s` with `R = O[X j]`, `p ≠ 0`, `Φ (p • O^n) ⊆ T`, and put `y g = Φ (z g)`.
Suppose that `y (τ i) - y (τ' i) = X i • (y (φ i) - y (φ' i))` modulo `T` for all `i`, and that
`X i • (y (φ i) - y (φ' i))` lies in `T` for `i < j`. Suppose that every linear `Ψ : O^n → O / p`
that vanishes on `Φ⁻¹ T` and has `Ψ (z (τ i)) = Ψ (z (τ' i))` for `i < j` has
`C • Ψ (z (τ j)) = C • Ψ (z (τ' j))`. Then `C X j • (y (φ j) - y (φ' j))` lies in `T`. 

### `exists_mul_prod_X_mem_fittingIdeal`

 Let `O` be a discrete valuation ring, `R` the polynomial ring over `O` in the variables `X j`,
let `Φ : O^n → R^s` and put `y g = Φ (z g)`. Suppose `p ≠ 0` and `Φ (p • O^n) ⊆ N`. Suppose that
modulo `N`, the basis vector of `R^s` at each `g ∈ s` is `y g`, and `y (τ j) = X j • y (φ j)` and
`y (τ' j) = X j • y (φ' j)` for every `j`. Suppose that every `i` below a member of `I` lies in `I`
or has `X i • R^s ⊆ N`. Let `Z = formsMod Φ z N p` be the module of the functions `Ψ ∘ z` on `G`,
for linear `Ψ : O^n → O / p` vanishing on `Φ⁻¹ N`, and suppose `HasTriangleCost` for `Z`: there is a
set `A ⊆ G` with two properties, where `Z₀` is the part of `Z` that vanishes on `A`. (a) For `j ∈ I`
and `c ∈ Z₀`, if `c (τ i) = c (τ' i)` for all `i < j` and `c (φ i) = c (φ' i)` for those `i < j` in
`I`, then `C • c (τ j) = C • c (τ' j)`. (b) The length of `Z / Z₀` plus the length of `Z₁` modulo
its `C`-torsion is at most `b`, for every submodule `Z₁ ≤ Z₀` whose members `c` vanish at all `τ j`,
`τ' j` and have `c (φ j) = c (φ' j)` for `j ∈ I`. Then the Fitting ideal of `R^s / N` contains
`F * ∏ j ∈ I, X j` for some polynomial `F` whose constant coefficient has valuation at most
`b + (#I + #s) * addVal O C`. 

### `IsTraceIdeal`

 Elements of `S` and `𝒥` are families, indexed by `x : Y`, of polynomials over `L` in the
variables `X j`. The algebra `S` contains the variables and the coefficients of the characteristic
polynomials of `u`. The `S`-module `𝒥` contains `p` times the traces of the `u x g` and, for each
relation that ties `τ i` to `φ i`, the traces of its products with the `u x g` on the right. 

### `IsTraceIdeal.X_mem`

 The algebra `S` contains the constant families `X j`. 

### `IsTraceIdeal.charpoly_coeff_mem`

 The algebra `S` contains the coefficients of the characteristic polynomials of the `u x g`,
  as families of constants. 

### `IsTraceIdeal.smul_trace_mem`

 The `S`-module `𝒥` contains `p` times the trace of `u x g`. 

### `IsTraceIdeal.trace_relation_mem`

 The `S`-module `𝒥` contains the trace of
  `(τ i - 1 - X (k i) * (φ i - 1) * ε (k i) (φ i)) * g` under `u x`. 

### `ribetCocyclesVanishingOn`

 The cocycles `c : G → B ^ r` for `ρ` that obey the linear relations among the families
`(u x g - 1)_x` (`ribetCocyclesIn u ρ B`) and vanish on the set `T` and at `γ`. 

### `mem_ribetCocyclesVanishingOn`

 Membership in `ribetCocyclesVanishingOn`, clause by clause: `c` lies in
`ribetCocyclesVanishingOn u ρ B T γ` iff `c` lies in `ribetCocyclesIn u ρ B`, `c g = 0` for all
`g ∈ T`, and `c γ = 0`. 

### `rankOneRep`

 A character `χ` as a representation by `1 × 1` matrices. 

### `IsCharacterCocycle`

 The function `c : G → O / p` is a cocycle for the character `χ` that obeys the linear relations
among the families `(u x g - 1)_x` and vanishes at the `δ v`; if `c γ = 0`, then every `χ g - 1`
kills `c a`. 

### `IsDetectedBy`

 The finite set `s` detects the module `Z` of functions: an element of `Z` that vanishes on `s`
is zero. 

### `IsCommonRibetElement`

 One Ribet element for a family `β` of functions `β i : Y → L`. Here `S` is an algebra of
families, indexed by `x : Y`, of polynomials over `L` in the variables `X j`, and `𝒥` is an
`S`-module of such families. Every family of constant polynomials `x ↦ C (β i x)` lies in `S` and
vanishes on `𝓧`. One `f : S` multiplies all of them into `𝒥`; for `x ∈ 𝓧`, `f x` has no monomial
properly dividing `∏ X j`; and the coefficient of `∏ X j` in `f π` is `c`. 

### `exists_common_unit_row`

 Let `N` be a module of vectors over `A`, let `η e` be finitely many characters of `A`, and let
`c j` be finitely many coordinates. If for each `e` and each `i` some `w ∈ N` has coordinates
`w (c j)` that `η e` sends to the unit vector `i`, then for each `i` one `w ∈ N` does so for all `e`
at once. 

### `exists_rows_det_eq_pow_mul_det`

 Let `R` be a `Λ`-algebra with characters `χ e : R →ₐ[Λ] Λ`, and let `π e` have `χ e (π e) = μ e`
and `χ e' (π e) = 0` for `e' ≠ e`. Let `N` be a module of relations among generators indexed by
`ι₀ ⊕ ι`. Suppose that the generators in `ι` suffice modulo the kernel of each `χ e`: for each `e`
and `i : ι₀` some `w ∈ N` is, under `χ e`, the unit vector `i` on `ι₀`. Let each row of `B e` be
`χ e` of the `ι`-part of some `w ∈ N` whose `ι₀`-part `χ e` kills. Then there is a square matrix `M`
with rows in `N` such that `det (χ e M) = μ e ^ #ι det (B e)` for all `e`. 

### `exists_relations_det_eq_pow_mul_det`

 Let `R` be a `Λ`-algebra with characters `χ e : R →ₐ[Λ] Λ`, of kernels `𝔨 e`, let `π e` have
`χ e (π e) = μ e` and `χ e' (π e) = 0` for `e' ≠ e`, and let `y` span a left `R`-module `C`. If the
`y (inr i)` generate `C` modulo `𝔨 e • C` for every `e`, and each row of `B e` is a relation among
them modulo `𝔨 e • C`, then there is a square matrix `M` over `R`, whose rows are relations among
the `y k`, such that `det (χ e M) = μ e ^ #ι det (B e)` for all `e`. 

### `exists_forall_eq_one_and_forall_eq_zero`

 If each of finitely many `ψ x` kills an element on which all characters `η e` are `1`, then one
such element is killed by all `ψ x`. 

### `exists_apply_eq_ite_of_prod_dvd`

 Let `η k` be finitely many characters of `A` and `a : A`. If `c` is a multiple of the product of
the differences `η e a - η k a` over `k ≠ e`, then some element of `A` is `c` on the character `η e`
and `0` on the other characters. 

### `exists_sum_mul_det_of_mem_fittingIdeal_map`

 An element of the Fitting ideal of the image `f N` is a linear combination of determinants of
matrices `B i` whose rows are the images of elements `B' i j` of `N`. 

### `nonDivisorIdeal`

 The ideal spanned by the monomials that do not divide `∏ X j`. 

### `mem_nonDivisorIdeal`

 A polynomial lies in `nonDivisorIdeal O J` if and only if none of its monomials divides
`∏ X j`. 

### `X_mem_colon_nonDivisorIdeal`

 Let `X j` (`j : J`) be finitely many variables over the commutative ring `O`, each with a type
`ty j`. If `ty i < e`, the product of `X i` with `∏ j with ty j < e, X j` is divisible by `X i ^ 2`,
so it lies in `nonDivisorIdeal O J`, the ideal spanned by the monomials that do not divide
`∏ X j`. 

### `coeff_mul_prod_of_mem_colon`

 Let the variables `X j` of polynomials over the commutative ring `O` be indexed by a finite
linear order `J`, and let `ty : J →o Fin t` be a monotone map that gives each variable a type.
Suppose that `d e` is `Γ e * ∏ j with ty j = e, X j` up to an error `ζ e` that
`∏ j with ty j < e, X j` multiplies into `nonDivisorIdeal O J`, the ideal spanned by the monomials
that do not divide `∏ X j`. Then `F * ∏ d e` has no monomial properly dividing `∏ X j`, and the
coefficient of `∏ X j` is the constant term of `F * ∏ Γ e`. 

### `exists_mem_span_add_mul_eq_add_mul`

 Let `χ x` (`x : Y`) be representations of the group algebra `Λ[G]` by `σ × σ` matrices and `η₀`
the augmentation, and let every `g - 1` be, up to elements that every `χ x` kills, in the left ideal
spanned by the `y i`. Further let `S` be an algebra of families `Y → L[X j]`, where `L[X j]` is
`MvPolynomial J L`, and `𝒥` an `S`-module of such families, and let `b` lie in the left ideal
spanned by the `w` with `x ↦ tr (χ x (w g))` in `𝒥` for every `g`. If `η₀ m = 0`, then
`b + m ρ₀ = b' + m' ρ₀` with `b'` in that left ideal and `m'` in the left ideal spanned by the
`y i`. 

### `exists_block_rows`

 Rows with a determinant of bounded valuation under a representation `ρ` whose matrices span. Let
`O` be a discrete valuation ring and `u x` (`x : Y`) matrix representations of `G` over `O`. Let
`ψ₀` be a representation of `A` by matrices over `O` that is `ρ` on the group elements `ofGroup g`,
and let `v` send vectors over `O` to rows over `A`, with `ψ₀ (v m j) = m j • 1`. Let `N` be a module
of rows that holds, for the vectors `z g`, the cocycle rule, the equations `z (gen i) = eᵢ`, the
linear relations among the families `(u x g - 1)_x`, and, through `ψ₀`, the equations `z (τ k) = 0`
and `z γ = 0`. If the cocycles of `ρ` with values in `DivisibleQuot O = Frac(O) / O` that obey those
linear relations and vanish at the `τ k` and at `γ` have length at most `b₀`, then for some square
matrix `B₀` with rows in `N` the determinant of the block matrix `ψ₀(B₀)` has valuation at most
`b₀`. 

### `isCharacterCocycle_of_mem_formsMod`

 The character step. Let `u x` (`x : Y`) be matrix representations of `G` over `O`. Let `Φ` send
vectors over `O` to rows, and let `N` hold the images under `Φ` of the cocycle rule of the vectors
`z g` for the character `ξ`, of the `z (δ w)`, of the linear relations among the families
`(u x g - 1)_x`, and of the multiples `(ξ g - 1) (c₁ z γ + z a)`. Then every `c` in
`formsMod Φ z N p`, that is, `c = Ψ ∘ z` for a linear form `Ψ` modulo `p` that vanishes on `Φ⁻¹ N`,
is a cocycle for `ξ` that obeys the linear relations and vanishes at the `δ w`; and if `c γ = 0`,
then every `ξ g - 1` kills `c a`. 

### `exists_unit_row`

 Let `η` be a character of `A` and `p ≠ 0`. Let `N` hold `p` times every row and, for each
generator `gen i`, the row `v (z (gen i)) - eᵢ`, where `gen` is the identity on `s`. Let `E` read
rows under `η` on the `S₀`-coordinates only, and take the linear forms `Ψ` modulo `p` that kill
every `m` with `E (v m)` in the image of `N` under `E`. Suppose that every function `Ψ ∘ z` that
vanishes on `s` is zero. Then for each `i : S₀` some row in `N` is, under `η`, the unit vector `i`
on the `S₀`-coordinates. 

### `exists_row_apply_eq_zero`

 Clearing the `S₀`-coordinates. Let the rows `ν i ∈ N` be, under the character `η`, the unit
vectors on the `S₀`-coordinates. Subtracting multiples of them turns a row `w ∈ N` into a row of `N`
that is zero under `η` on the `S₀`-coordinates; on the `S`-coordinates it is the result of
eliminating the `S₀`-coordinates of `w` by the `ν i`.
