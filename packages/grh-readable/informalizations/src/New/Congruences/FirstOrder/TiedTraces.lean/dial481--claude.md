# Tied traces and the dictionary at one auxiliary place

Forms of the base data `D` are taken at principal level at the auxiliary places `Q.place j`. Each
isotypic space `x` has a Galois representation `N x`. This is the twist of `x.galRep` by a character
`a` that is trivial at the tame generators. This file shows that the coefficients of the
characteristic polynomials of the `N x` lie in the Hecke algebra, and so does the indicator function
of the systems congruent to a given one. It defines traces as members of the Hecke algebra, and the
ideal of the tied relations. At one place it gives the dictionary between Galois elements and
operators on forms: the tame generator acts as a diamond operator, and the Frobenius element acts
through the polynomial `p(Ũ)` in a normalised Hecke operator.

## Main definitions and results

* `New.Patching.BaseLevel.charpoly_coeff_mem_heckeAlgebra`,
  `New.Patching.BaseLevel.exists_indicator_isCongruent`: the coefficients of characteristic
  polynomials, and the indicator function of the systems congruent to a given one, lie in the Hecke
  algebra.
* `New.Patching.BaseLevel.traceElement`, `New.Patching.BaseLevel.tiedIdeal`: traces as members of
  the Hecke algebra, and the ideal generated by the traces of the tied relations.
* `New.Patching.BaseLevel.tameTrace_smul_eq_diamond_sub_one`,
  `New.Patching.BaseLevel.tameTrace_smul_eq_aevalHecke`: operators on forms for the tame generator
  and for Frobenius.

Notions from other files:
* `New.Patching.BaseLevel.formsOfLevel`: the forms of the type fixed by `D` that have principal
  level at the places `L j` and maximal level at other places off `D.places` and its conjugate. The
  empty family `L = ![]` gives the base level.
* `New.Patching.BaseLevel.Eigensystem`, `New.Patching.BaseLevel.IsOffLevel`: the systems of level
  `L`, that is, the isotypic spaces (`New.Patching.Isotypic`, spaces of forms with given Hecke
  eigenvalues at almost all places) that contain a nonzero form of level `L`. For such an `x`,
  `(x : Isotypic V κ).val` is the space of forms. `w` is off level `L` if it lies off `D.places`,
  its conjugate and `ℓ`, and the forms of level `L` in each system are Hecke eigenforms at `w`.
* `New.Patching.BaseLevel.integralEnd`, `New.Patching.BaseLevel.localOperators`: the endomorphisms
  of the forms of level `L` that preserve the integral forms, and those among them that come from
  integral combinations of translations supported at `Q.place j` and its conjugate.
* `New.Patching.BaseLevel.heckeAlgebra`, `New.Patching.BaseLevel.heckeAction`: the `ℓ`-adic
  functions `t` on systems such that scaling each system `x` by `ιℓ.symm (t x)` preserves the
  integral forms, and the operator that such a function defines.
* `New.Patching.weightedSum`: `weightedSum ρ ind g = ∑ γ, g (ind γ) • ρ γ` for `g : ZMod m → ℤ`.

## Relation to the literature

### `LocalChoice`

 A local choice at one auxiliary place `w = Q.place j`: a uniformiser `ϖ`, a unit `tameUnit` and
a logarithm `ind` on the units of the residue field, fitted to the Frobenius element `φ` and the
tame generator `γ` of the Frobenius datum `Q.frobData j`. Here `Q` is a family of places, no two
equal or conjugate, with `m ∣ q - 1` for the order `q` of each residue field. 

### `LocalChoice.ϖ`

 A uniformiser that the local Artin map sends to the class of `φ`. 

### `LocalChoice.tameUnit`

 A unit that the local Artin map sends to the class of `γ`. 

### `LocalChoice.ind`

 The logarithm modulo `m` on the units of the residue field, to the base the residue of
  `tameUnit`. 

### `LocalChoice.v_ϖ`

 `ϖ` is a uniformiser. 

### `LocalChoice.v_tameUnit`

 `tameUnit` is a unit of the ring of integers. 

### `LocalChoice.localArtin_ϖ`

 The Artin symbol of `ϖ` is the class of `φ`. 

### `LocalChoice.localArtin_tameUnit`

 The Artin symbol of `tameUnit` is the class of `γ`. 

### `LocalChoice.ind_tameUnit`

 The logarithm of the residue of `tameUnit` is `1`. 

### `nonempty_localChoice`

 Let `Q` be a family of auxiliary places: Frobenius data at finite places of `E`, no two places
equal or conjugate, with `m ∣ q - 1` for the order `q` of each residue field. Each place
`Q.place j` admits a local choice: a uniformiser and a unit whose Artin symbols are the classes of
the Frobenius element and of the tame generator, and a logarithm modulo `m` on the units of the
residue field, to the base the residue of that unit. 

### `indLine`

 The logarithm `ind₁ g = ind g₁₁` of the first entry, on the stabiliser in `GL_N(𝔽_w)` of the
line of the first basis vector. 

### `indDet`

 The logarithm `indDet = ind ∘ det` on `GL_N(𝔽_w)`. 

### `diamond`

 The element `⟨γ⟩ = diag (z, 1, …, 1)` of `GL_N(𝔽_w)`, where `z` is the residue of
`P.tameUnit`. 

### `transfer_indLine`

 The transfer of `ind₁` from the stabiliser of the line to `GL_N(𝔽_w)` is `ind ∘ det`. 

### `placeRep`

 The action of the group `GL_n(𝔽_w)` at the auxiliary place `w = Q.place j` on the forms of
principal level. 

### `lineRep`

 The action of the stabiliser of a line in `GL_n(𝔽_w)` at the auxiliary place `w = Q.place j`. 

### `commute_heckeAction_residueRep`

 If the auxiliary places `w j = Q.place j` are off the base level, the Hecke algebra commutes
with `D.residueRep Q`, the action of `∏ GL_n(𝔽_{w j})` on the forms of principal level. 

### `localFormRep_apply_eq_lineRep`

 Let the auxiliary places be off the base level. An element `k` of the parahoric subgroup
of the line in `GL_n(E_w)`, `w = Q.place j` (blocks of sizes `1` and `n - 1`), acts on forms of
principal level through its reduction, which stabilises the line. On the left, `localFormRep` is
the action of `GL_n(E_w)` on all forms; on the right, `lineRep` is that of the stabiliser. 

### `mem_levelSpace_proIwahori`

 Let `V` be the standard hermitian space (`IsStd`), let `E` carry Weil representation data
(`WeilData`), and let the auxiliary places be off the base level. If the elements of the stabiliser
of the line in `GL_n(𝔽_w)`, `w = Q.place j`, with first entry and determinant `1` fix a form `y` of
weight `κ`, then the pro-unipotent Iwahori subgroup `proIwahori` of `GL_n(E_w)` fixes it: `y` lies
in the space `levelSpace` of its fixed vectors for the action `localFormRep` of `GL_n(E_w)` on
forms. 

### `mem_parahoricEigenspace_line`

 Let `V` be the standard hermitian space, let `E` carry Weil representation data, let the
auxiliary places be off the base level, and let `P` be a local choice at `w = Q.place j`: it has a
logarithm `P.ind` modulo `ℓ ^ ν` on `𝔽_wˣ`, and `P.indLine g = P.ind g₁₁`. A form `y` of weight `κ`
on which the stabiliser of the line in `GL_n(𝔽_w)` acts by `ψ ∘ P.indLine` is fixed by `proIwahori`,
and the parahoric subgroup of the line in `GL_n(E_w)` acts on it by `k ↦ ψ (P.ind k₁₁)`. 

### `mem_parahoricEigenspace_det`

 Let `V` be the standard hermitian space, let `E` carry Weil representation data, let the
auxiliary places be off the base level, and let `P` be a local choice at `w = Q.place j`: it has a
logarithm `P.ind` modulo `ℓ ^ ν` on `𝔽_wˣ`, and `P.indDet = P.ind ∘ det`. A form `y` of weight `κ`
on which `GL_n(𝔽_w)` acts by `ψ ∘ P.indDet` is fixed by `proIwahori`, and the maximal compact
subgroup of `GL_n(E_w)` (level function `0`) acts on it by `k ↦ ψ (P.ind (det k))`. 

 ### The Hecke algebra: characteristic polynomials, traces and the tied relations 

### `heckeEigenvalues_mem_heckeAlgebra`

 Let `w.place` be a place of prime norm that is off the level `L` of the base data `D`. The
function that sends a system of level `L` to its `j`-th Hecke eigenvalue at `w.place`, a complex
number moved to `PadicAlgCl ℓ` by the fixed isomorphism `ιℓ`, lies in the Hecke algebra, which is
a ring of such `ℓ`-adic functions on systems. 

### `IsGalRepTwist`

 For every isotypic space `x`, the representation `N x` is the twist of the Galois representation
`x.galRep` by the character `a` with values in the ℓ-adic integers. 

### `charpoly_coeff_mem_heckeAlgebra`

 Let `N x` be the twist of `x.galRep` by a character `a` with values in the ℓ-adic integers. Then
for every `g` and `i` the function sending a system `x` of level `L` to the `i`-th coefficient of
the characteristic polynomial of `N x g` lies in the Hecke algebra. 

### `IsCongruent`

 Two systems are congruent if the characteristic polynomials of their representations `N` agree
modulo the maximal ideal at every element of the Galois group. 

### `exists_indicator_isCongruent`

 If `N` is the twist of the Galois representations by `a` (`IsGalRepTwist`) and the
characteristic polynomials of `N x` are integral for all systems `x` of level `L`, then the
indicator function of the systems congruent to `π` lies in the Hecke algebra. A case of the
decomposition of a finite algebra over a henselian local ring as a product of local rings (Stacks,
Tag 04GG), in which the idempotent of one congruence class is written down. 

### `traceElement`

 The trace `tr[P(h) g]`, the function `x ↦ tr (P(N x h) N x g)` on systems of level `L`, as a
member of the Hecke algebra. Here `N x` is the twist of `x.galRep` by the character `a`. 

### `tameTrace`

 The trace `tr[(γ - 1) g P(φ)]` for the tame generator `γ` and the Frobenius element `φ` at
`Q.place j`, as a member of the Hecke algebra. 

### `tameTrace_one`

 `tameTrace Q hN j 1 g` equals `traceElement hN (X - 1) γ g`: both are the trace `tr[(γ - 1) g]`
as a member of the Hecke algebra, where `γ` is the tame generator at `Q.place j`. 

### `tiedIdeal`

 The ideal `𝔞` of `𝕋[α]` generated by the `ℓ ^ ν tr[g]` and the traces `tr[r j g]` of the tied
relations `r j = γ j - 1 - α j P j (φ j)`, where `𝕋` is the Hecke algebra, `P j = (X - 1) H j` and
`α j` are polynomial variables. Reading only the coefficients of the square-free monomials
`α_S = ∏_{j ∈ S} α j` has the effect of `α j ^ 2 = 0`. 

 ### One place: the dictionary between Galois elements and operators on forms 

### `weightedSum_mul_heckeAction_eq_of_eigen`

 Let a finite group `Γ` act through `ρ` by operators supported at the place `Q.place j`, let
`W = ∑ γ, weight (χ γ) • ρ γ` and `BW = B ∘ W`, and let `r`, `s` lie in the Hecke algebra. Suppose
that the weight is `f (· - shift) - root f`, and that `r x • z = s x • B z` for every form `z` of a
system `x` on which `Γ` acts by `ψ ∘ χ` for a character `ψ` with `ψ shift ≠ root`. Then
`W r = BW s`. 

### `tameTrace_smul_eq_diamond_sub_one`

 The tame generator `γ` at `w = Q.place j` acts as a diamond operator. Let the auxiliary places
be off the base level, let `N x` be the twist of `x.galRep` by a character `a` trivial at the tame
generators, and let `P` be a local choice at `w`: a unit `P.tameUnit` of residue `z̄` and a
logarithm `P.ind` modulo `ℓ ^ ν` on `𝔽_wˣ` with `P.ind z̄ = 1`; put `P.indDet = P.ind ∘ det` and
`⟨γ⟩ = P.diamond = diag (z̄, 1, …, 1)`. On a form `y` of the system `x` on which `GL_n(𝔽_w)` acts by
`ψ ∘ P.indDet`, we have `tr[(γ - 1) g p(φ)] = (⟨γ⟩ - 1) tr[g p(φ)]`, where `φ` is the Frobenius
element and the traces are those of `N x`. 

### `aevalHecke`

 The polynomial `p(Ũ)` in the normalised operator `Ũ = a(φ) U`, where `U` is the Hecke operator
of `diag (ϖ, 1, …, 1)` for `proIwahori` at `Q.place j`, `ϖ = P.ϖ`, and `φ` is the Frobenius
element. 

### `tameTrace_smul_eq_aevalHecke`

 Dictionary for the Frobenius element `φ`. Let the auxiliary places be off the base level (`hQ`),
let `N x` be the twist of `x.galRep` by `a` (`hN`), let `a` be `1` at the tame generators (`ha`),
and let `P` be a local choice at `w = Q.place j`, with uniformiser `ϖ` and logarithm `P.ind` modulo
`ℓ ^ ν` on `𝔽_wˣ`; put `P.indLine g = P.ind g₁₁`. If the stabiliser of a line in `GL_n(𝔽_w)` acts on
a form `y` of the system `x` through `ψ ∘ P.indLine`, where `ψ ((card n)!) ≠ 1`, then
`tr[(γ - 1) g p(φ)] = p(Ũ) tr[(γ - 1) g]` on `y`. Here `γ` is the tame generator at `w`,
`tr[(γ - 1) g p(φ)]` is `tameTrace`, and `p(Ũ)` is `aevalHecke`, for `Ũ = a(φ) U` with `U` the Hecke
operator of `diag (ϖ, 1, …, 1)` for the pro-unipotent Iwahori subgroup. 

### `weightedSum_indDet_mul_tameTrace`

 The dictionary for the tame generator `γ` at `w = Q.place j` on weighted norms. Let the
auxiliary places be off the base level (`hQ`), let `N x` be the twist of `x.galRep` by `a` (`hN`),
let `a` be `1` at the tame generators (`ha`), and let `P` be a local choice at `w`, whose
logarithm `P.ind` on `𝔽_wˣ` gives `P.indDet = P.ind ∘ det` with values modulo `ℓ ^ ν`. If `L` is
the norm over `GL_n(𝔽_w)` with weight `f ∘ P.indDet`, then
`L tr[(γ - 1) g p(φ)] = (⟨γ⟩ - 1) L tr[g p(φ)]`, where `φ` is the Frobenius element at `w`,
`⟨γ⟩ = P.diamond`, and the traces are `tameTrace` and `traceElement`. 

### `norm_mul_traceElement_tameGenerator_eq_zero`

 Let the auxiliary places be off the base level (`hQ`), and let `N x` be the twist of `x.galRep`
by `a`, which is `1` at the tame generators (`hN`, `ha`). The norm over `GL_n(𝔽_w)` at
`w = Q.place i` kills `tr[(γ - 1) g]`, where `γ` is the tame generator at `w` and `traceElement` is
the trace of the `N x` as an element of the Hecke algebra. 

### `weightedSum_indLine_mem_levelSpace`

 Let the auxiliary places be off the base level and `w = Q.place j`. The norm over the stabiliser
of a line in `GL_n(𝔽_w)` (acting by `lineRep`) with weight `f ∘ P.indLine` has values fixed by the
pro-unipotent Iwahori subgroup of `GL_n(E_w)`. 

### `exists_aevalHecke_weightedSum`

 Let the auxiliary places be off the base level and let `P` be a local choice at
`w = Q.place j`, with uniformiser `P.ϖ` and a logarithm modulo `ℓ ^ ν` on `𝔽_wˣ`, read on the first
entry as `P.indLine`. Let `L f` be the norm over the stabiliser of a line in `GL_n(𝔽_w)` with weight
`f ∘ P.indLine`, and let `a` be a character of the Galois group with values in the ℓ-adic integers.
There is an additive map `B` from weights to integral operators made of translates at `w`
(`localOperators`) with `B f = p(Ũ) L f`. Here `p(Ũ)` is `aevalHecke`, with `Ũ = a(φ) U` for the
Frobenius element `φ` and the Hecke operator `U` of `diag (P.ϖ, 1, …, 1)`. 

### `map_aevalHecke_valMinAbs_mul_tameTrace`

 Let the auxiliary places be off the base level (`hQ`), and let `N x` be the twist of `x.galRep`
by `a`, which is `1` at the tame generators (`hN`, `ha`). Fix an index `j` and a local choice `P`
at `Q.place j`, with tame generator `γ` and Frobenius element `φ`. Let `B f` be `p(Ũ)`
(`aevalHecke V κ P a p`: forms on `V` of weight `κ`, `Ũ = a(φ) U`, `U` the Hecke operator of
`diag (P.ϖ, 1, …, 1)`) after the norm over the stabiliser of the line with weight `f ∘ P.indLine`
(`hB`). Then `p(Ũ) L tr[(γ - 1) g]` and `L tr[(γ - 1) g p(φ)]` have the same image under a ring map
`σ` on `𝒜 = D.integralEnd Q.place` that kills each `T` with `2 (card n)! T ∈ ℓ ^ ν 𝒜` (`hσ`).
The traces are `tameTrace`, and `L` is the norm whose weight is the logarithm `P.indLine` lifted to
`ℤ` by `valMinAbs`. 

### `lineRep_mul_eq_self`

 Let the auxiliary places be off the base level (`hQ`), let `P` be a local choice at
`w = Q.place j` for an index `j`, `p` a polynomial and `a` a character. Let `B f` be
`p(Ũ) = aevalHecke V κ P a p` after the norm over the stabiliser of the line in `GL_n(𝔽_w)` with
weight `f ∘ P.indLine` (`hB`). Then the stabiliser fixes the values of `B 1`. 

### `heckeAction_mul_norm_mul_eq_zero`

 Let the auxiliary places `w i = Q.place i` be off the base level (`hQ`), and let `P` be a local
choice at `w j` for an index `j`, with uniformiser `ϖ` and Frobenius element `φ`, and let `q` be the
order of the residue field at `w j`. Let `U' = B 1` be `p(Ũ)` after the norm over the stabiliser of
the line (`hB`), where `p = (X - q ^ t) H`, `Ũ = a(φ) U` and `U` is the Hecke operator of
`diag (ϖ, 1, …, 1)`, and let `U'` be made of translates at `w j` (`hB_mem`). Let `δ` be in the Hecke
algebra, and suppose that the Hecke polynomial at `w j` of every base system where `δ` does not
vanish is, after scaling by `1 / a(φ)`, a chain of `t + 1` roots `1, q, …, q ^ t` times a divisor of
`H` (`HasRootChain`). Then `δ Nm U' = 0`, where `Nm` is the sum over `∏ GL_n(𝔽_{w i})`.
