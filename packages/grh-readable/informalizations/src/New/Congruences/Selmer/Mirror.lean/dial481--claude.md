# Reciprocity at conjugate primes

Given a tied class `z` of full order whose Frobenius values at all new auxiliary primes are tame
values, one attaches to each member `y` of the dual group a `G`-map `η : T / a → (T / a)^{*c}` with
`η(z(h)) = y(h^c)` on `H`, which determines `y`. Here `G = Gal(Ω/E)`, `T / a` is the lattice modulo
`a`, `g^c = New.conjGal g` is the conjugate of `g` by complex conjugation, and `(T / a)^{*c}` is
the Cartier dual of `T / a` with `g` acting through `g^c`. The proof shows that the form
`Ψ(h, h') = ⟨z(h), y(h')⟩ - ⟨z(h'^c), y(h^c)⟩` vanishes on `H × H`.

* `New.Selmer.SelmerDatum.exists_isNewAuxiliaryPrime`: there are new auxiliary primes whose
  Frobenius element is near any element of `γ₀ H`, where `γ₀` is the tame generator at the first
  prime.
* `New.Selmer.SelmerDatum.mirrorForm_self_eq_zero`: first step, `Ψ(h, h) = 0` by reciprocity.
* `New.Selmer.SelmerDatum.mirrorForm_eq_zero`: second step, `Ψ = 0` by conjugation with `γ_*`.
* `New.Selmer.SelmerDatum.exists_linearMap_apply_eq_dualSelmer_conj`: third step, the map `η`.
* `New.Selmer.SelmerDatum.length_dualSelmer_le`: the dual group has length at most `globalTerm(T)`.

Notions from other files:
* `New.Selmer.SelmerDatum`: a lattice `T = O ^ n` with Galois action `T.ρ`, local conditions and an
  inertial element `γ_* = T.γ` that acts by a scalar; `T.tiedSelmer` is its tied Selmer group.
* `New.Selmer.SelmerDatum.reductionKer`, `New.Selmer.SelmerDatum.sahGroup`: the kernel `U` of the
  action on `T / ℓ ^ M` and `μ_{ℓ ^ N}`, and the group `H` generated by all `[g, γ_*]` and
  `[g, γ_*^c]`.
* `New.Selmer.SelmerDatum.dualSelmer`: the dual of the tied Selmer group, formed for a finite set
  `R` of primes. Its members are equivariant homomorphisms from `U` to the characters
  `T / a → ℚ/ℤ`, extended by zero to `Gal(Ω/E)`, that are unramified outside `R \ {P₀, P̄₀}` and
  satisfy reciprocity at split primes outside that set.
* `New.Selmer.IsTiedPrimes`: says that `J` is a set of auxiliary primes with data `x` (place,
  Frobenius element, tame generator, character, slopes). It has a first prime `P₀`, whose Frobenius
  element lies in `W` and whose slopes are `0`, so that tied classes are unramified there.


### `isTameGeneratorFor_conjGal_inv`

 Let the place `B` of `Ω` lie over the prime `P` of `E`, and let `δ` be a normalised tame
generator at `B`: it is inertial at `B` and multiplies an `m`-th root of a uniformiser by the fixed
primitive `m`-th root of unity. Then `δ^{-c} = (conjGal δ)⁻¹` is a normalised tame generator at the
conjugate place. 

### `characterModule_eq_zero_of_span`

 A character `f : X → ℚ/ℤ` of an `O`-module `X` that kills all scalar multiples of the members of
a spanning set is zero. 

 ### New auxiliary primes 

### `reductionKerInfConj`

 The group `U ∩ U^c` of the elements `g` such that `g` and `g^c = conjGal g` lie in the kernel
`U = T.reductionKer M N` of the action on `T / ℓ ^ M` and on the `ℓ ^ N`-th roots of unity. It
contains `H = T.sahGroup` and the inertia groups at auxiliary primes. 

### `isOpen_reductionKerInfConj`

 The group `T.reductionKerInfConj M N = U ∩ U^c` is open. It consists of the `g` such that `g`
and `g^c = conjGal g` lie in `U = T.reductionKer M N`. It is open because `U` is open and
conjugation is continuous. 

### `mem_reductionKerInfConj_of_mem_inertiaGroup`

 Let the prime `P` and its conjugate lie outside `S₀`, and let the place `B` of `Ω` lie over `P`.
Then the inertia group of `B` lies in `U ∩ U^c`, where `U = T.reductionKer M N`. 

### `γ₀_mem_reductionKerInfConj`

 Let `γ₀ = (x P₀).1.tameGenerator` be the tame generator at the first prime of `J`. Then `γ₀` and
its conjugate lie in `U = T.reductionKer M N`, the kernel of the action on `T / ℓ ^ M` and on the
`ℓ ^ N`-th roots of unity. 

### `γ₀_mul_mem_normalClosure_inertiaAbove`

 Let `T` be a Selmer datum: a lattice `O ^ n` over an `ℓ`-adic ring with Galois action `T.ρ`,
unramified outside `T.places ⊆ S₀`, with an inertial element `γ_* = T.γ` that acts by a scalar. Let
`H = T.sahGroup` be generated by all commutators with `γ_*` and with its complex conjugate, let `R`
contain `P₀`, `S₀` and `S̄₀`, and let `γ₀ = (x P₀).1.tameGenerator`. For `h ∈ H` the element `γ₀ h`
lies in the normal closure of the inertia groups at the places of `R` that are prime to `ℓ` and are
not among the primes of `J` other than `P₀` or their conjugates.

Here `J` is a set of auxiliary primes tied to one another (`IsTiedPrimes`): it lies outside `S₀`,
has a first prime `P₀`, whose Frobenius element lies in `W`, and each `v ∈ J` has data `(x v).1` and
a slope `(x v).2 i` for each datum `i`; `V i` is a condition on the Frobenius elements at primes of
nonzero slope. 

### `exists_isNewAuxiliaryPrime`

 Let `T` be a Selmer datum, let `V i g` say that `g` acts trivially on `T / ℓ ^ M`, and let
`γ₀ = (x P₀).1.tameGenerator`. For `h` in the group `H = T.sahGroup` generated by all commutators
with `T.γ` and with its complex conjugate, a finite set `R` and a neighbourhood `X` of `γ₀ h`, some
prime `P` with data `l` is a new auxiliary prime for datum `i`, lies outside `R ∪ R̄`, and has
`l.frob ∈ X`.

So `P` may be added to `J` with a nonzero slope for datum `i`. 

 ### The form `Ψ` and the dual group 

### `exists_apply_pair_eq_nsmul`

 Let `T` be a Selmer datum: a lattice `O ^ n` over an `ℓ`-adic ring, with action `T.ρ` of
`Gal(Ω/E)` that is unramified outside a subset of the finite set `S₀` of primes, which contains
those above `ℓ`. Let `a ∣ ℓ ^ M` and `M < N`, let the place `B` lie over a prime `P` with
`P, P̄ ∉ S₀`, let `τ` be inertial at `B`, and let `δ` be a normalised tame generator at `B` for
`ℓ ^ N`. The values of a locally constant cocycle `z` with coefficients in `T / a` at `τ, τ^{-c}`
are the same multiple of its values at `δ, δ^{-c}`. 

### `mirrorForm`

 The form `Ψ(h, h') = ⟨z(h), y(h')⟩ - ⟨z(h'^c), y(h^c)⟩` attached to functions `z` and `y` on the
Galois group with values in `(O / a) ^ n` and in its character module. The pairing `⟨u, f⟩` is the
character `t ↦ f (t • u)` of `O`; this is the value of `Ψ(h, h')` at `t`. 

### `frobInTameImage`

 The functions `z` with `ev_φ z ∈ ev_γ (Sel^𝔮(J))` modulo `a`. Here `𝔮 = P` is a new prime with
Frobenius element `φ = l.frob` and tame generator `γ = l.tameGenerator`, `ev_g` (`evalConjPair g`)
takes the values at `g` and `g^{-c}`, and `Sel^𝔮(J)` is the tied Selmer group with ramification also
allowed at `𝔮, 𝔮̄`. 

### `apply_conj_of_mem_dualSelmer`

 A member `y` of the dual Selmer group is equivariant on `U`: `y (g u g⁻¹) = cyc(g) · y(u) ∘ g⁻¹`
for `u ∈ U`, where `g⁻¹` acts on `T / a` by `T.ρ` and `cyc` is the `ℓ`-adic cyclotomic character
with values in `O`, the coefficient ring of the lattice `T = O ^ n`. 

### `apply_first_tameGenerator_eq_zero_of_mem_dualSelmer`

 A member `y` of the dual Selmer group is unramified at the first prime `P₀` of `J` and at its
conjugate, so it vanishes at the tame generator `γ₀ = (x P₀).1.tameGenerator` and at
`γ₀^c = conjGal γ₀`. 

### `eq_zero_of_mem_dualSelmer_of_forall_sahGroup`

 Sah's argument for the dual module: a member `y` of the dual Selmer group that vanishes on `H`
is zero everywhere. 

 ### First step: `Ψ(h, h) = 0` 

### `dualSelmer_frob_add_conj_frob_eq_zero`

 Let `y` lie in the dual Selmer group. Let `P` be a new auxiliary prime for datum `i`, with
`P, P̄ ∉ R`: it has prime norm, lies with `P̄` outside `J ∪ S₀`, and its data `l` (tame generator
`γ`, Frobenius element `φ`, character) meet the conditions `IsNewAuxiliaryPrime` for being added to
`J` with a nonzero slope, among them `V i` at `φ` and `φ^{-c}`. Suppose `φ, φ^c ∈ U`, where
`g^c = conjGal g` is the conjugate of `g` by complex conjugation and `g^{-c} = (g^c)⁻¹`. Every `z`
in the tied Selmer group in which ramification is also allowed at `P, P̄` (with no tie there) has
`y(φ)(t z(γ)) + y(φ^c)(t z(γ^{-c})) = 0` for all `t ∈ O`. 

### `mirrorForm_self_eq_zero`

 Let `y` lie in the dual Selmer group and `z` in the tied Selmer group. Suppose that at every new
auxiliary prime `P` for datum `i` the values of `z` at `φ, φ^{-c}` are the values at `γ, γ^{-c}` of
a member of the tied Selmer group with ramification also allowed at `P, P̄` (`frobInTameImage`);
here `φ` is the Frobenius element and `γ` the tame generator at `P`. Then the form
`Ψ = mirrorForm z y t`, given by `Ψ(h, h') = y(h')(t z(h)) - y(h^c)(t z(h'^c))`, has `Ψ(h, h) = 0`
for `h ∈ H`. 

 ### Second step: `Ψ = 0` 

### `mirrorForm_add_swap_eq_zero`

 Let `y` lie in the dual Selmer group and `z` in the tied Selmer group. Suppose that at every new
auxiliary prime `P` for datum `i` the values of `z` at `φ, φ^{-c}` are the values at `γ, γ^{-c}` of
a member of the tied Selmer group with ramification also allowed at `P, P̄` (`frobInTameImage`);
here `φ` is the Frobenius element and `γ` the tame generator at `P`. Then the form
`Ψ(h, h') = y(h')(t z(h)) - y(h^c)(t z(h'^c))` has `Ψ(h, h') + Ψ(h', h) = 0` on `H`. 

### `mirrorForm_conj_γ_left`

 Let `y` lie in the dual Selmer group, let `z` be a cocycle with values in `T / a`, and let
`h, h^c` lie in `U`. In the form `Ψ_t(h, h') = y(h')(t z(h)) - y(h^c)(t z(h'^c))`, conjugating the
first argument `h` by `γ_* = T.γ` multiplies the parameter `t` by `ζ_* = T.ζ`. The element `T.γ` is
the inertial element of the datum: both `T.γ` and `(conjGal T.γ)⁻¹` act on `T` by the scalar
`T.ζ`. 

### `mirrorForm_conj_γ_right`

 Let `y` lie in the dual Selmer group, let `z` be a cocycle with values in `T / a`, and let
`h, h^c` lie in `U = T.reductionKer M N`. In the form
`Ψ_t(h', h) = y(h)(t z(h')) - y(h'^c)(t z(h^c))`, conjugating the
second argument `h` by `γ_* = T.γ` removes a factor `ζ_* = T.ζ` from the parameter. 

### `mirrorForm_eq_zero`

 Let `y` lie in the dual Selmer group and `z` in the tied Selmer group. Suppose that at every new
auxiliary prime `P` for datum `i` the values of `z` at `φ, φ^{-c}` are the values at `γ, γ^{-c}` of
a member of the tied Selmer group with ramification also allowed at `P, P̄` (`frobInTameImage`);
here `φ` is the Frobenius element and `γ` the tame generator at `P`. Then the form
`Ψ(h, h') = y(h')(t z(h)) - y(h^c)(t z(h'^c))` vanishes on `H × H`. 

 ### Third step: the map `η` 

### `exists_linearMap_apply_eq_dualSelmer_conj`

 Let `y` lie in the dual Selmer group and `z` in the tied Selmer group. Suppose that at every new
auxiliary prime `P` for datum `i` the values of `z` at `φ, φ^{-c}` are the values at `γ, γ^{-c}` of
a member of the tied Selmer group with ramification also allowed at `P, P̄` (`frobInTameImage`);
here `φ` is the Frobenius element and `γ` the tame generator at `P`. Suppose also that `z(H)` spans
`(O / a) ^ n`. Then there is a linear map `η` from `(O / a) ^ n` to its character module with
`η(z(h)) = y(h^c)` for `h ∈ H`. 

### `mem_conjDualHom_of_apply_eq`

 Let `y` lie in the dual Selmer group, let `z` be a cocycle such that `z(H)` spans `(O / a) ^ n`,
and let `η` be linear with `η(z(h)) = y(h^c)` on `H`. Then `η` lies in `conjDualHom ℓ T.ρ a`: it
satisfies `η(g u)(m) = η(u)(cyc(g^c) • (g^c)⁻¹ m)` for all `g, u, m`, where `g` acts by `T.ρ` and
`cyc` is the `ℓ`-adic cyclotomic character. 

### `length_dualSelmer_le`

 The dual Selmer group has length at most the global term of the Selmer datum `T`, the supremum
over `b ≠ 0` of the lengths of the modules of Galois equivariant maps from `(O / b) ^ n` to its dual
twisted by complex conjugation (`globalTerm`). The hypotheses concern a member `z` of the tied
Selmer group, some value of which has a unit coordinate, and ask that at every new auxiliary prime
`P` for datum `i` the values of `z` at `φ, φ^{-c}` are the values at `γ, γ^{-c}` of a member of the
tied Selmer group with ramification also allowed at `P, P̄` (`frobInTameImage`); here `φ` is the
Frobenius element and `γ` the tame generator at `P`, and `g^{-c} = (conjGal g)⁻¹` is the inverse of
the conjugate of `g` by complex conjugation.
